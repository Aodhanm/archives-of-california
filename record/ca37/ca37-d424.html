  • Previously read Dated 1837-04-07.. Full-resolution crop (blind sample audit #110/200, 2026-07-18): the block header reads "1839. Mont." — and the content (Peña taking possession of the Prefectura, Francisco Castillo named its secretary) belongs to the 1839 prefecture rollout; prefectures did not operate in California in 1837.

Oct 13 'Su nombram.ᵗᵒ á Comis.º — El patronato'

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 885

Oct 13 'Su nombram.ᵗᵒ á Comis.º — El patronato' — if he's arrived as commissioner, welcome, but must have full conformity re the office; 'hace media D.N. su suj.ᵗᵒ q. he de relevar al P. Calvo…' re formality of the patronato; ⭐ Oct 16 'Contribuciones á guerra' — transcribes viceroy Branciforte's circular asking contributions to sustain the war against the French; the fathers to administer it in the Indians' name

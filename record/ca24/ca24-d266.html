the Indians who killed the S. Francisco neophytes have given their statements — punish them 'con arreglo á sus delitos' (the judicial close of the 1795 Mission Dolores massacre arc; cf. Doc 208's recovery); a soldier's son permitted 'á la otra banda' on the *Concepción*; Francisco Peña promoted t.te of the Loreto Co.; Manuel Rodríguez recognized as Alférez; some gentiles presented themselves; P. Espinosa's letter

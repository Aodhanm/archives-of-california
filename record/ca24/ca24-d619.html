Founding of Sta Catalina Martir on Nov.re 12 by P. José Loriente, comisionado of P

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 619

Founding of Sta Catalina Martir on Nov.re 12 by P. José Loriente, comisionado of P. Pres.te Cayetano Pallás, with sarg.to Man.l Ruiz. Buildings described (church/granary 12 varas, oven, corral, guard-house, a small bulwark for three canoncitos). On Indian policy: «y qe hará castigar á los qe cometan alguna falta con ellos, adelante de los mismos Naturales agraviados» — offenders against Indians to be punished in front of the aggrieved natives.

Heading 'Espedicion de Fages al Rio Colorado'

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 537

Heading 'Espedicion de Fages al Rio Colorado'. Asks that ex-governor Pedro Fages be ordered to send the government of the Californias the account of his own expedition reconnoitring the mouth of the Colorado, 'hecho en 86 (o) 87'. Adds that the sargento distinguido invalido Manuel de Vargas, who had worked in Fages' office, told him 'qe Fages se habia llevado todos papeles concernientes a esta a Mex.o y por eso no habia constancia ninguna en su archivo (de Monterrey)' [that Fages had carried off to Mexico all the papers concerning this, and that is why there was no record whatever in the Monterey archive]. A direct contemporary statement of loss from the provincial archive.

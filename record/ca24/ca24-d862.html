Julio 27: remits list of voluntary contributions of all inhabitants of Nueva Calif

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 862

Julio 27: remits list of voluntary contributions of all inhabitants of Nueva Calif. to sustain the current war with England = 1853 pesos; Julio 31: the 28th 'La Concepción' left w/o being pursued by the English — ran 3 of the S. Fernandino [friars]: Lorenzo Merelo, Jacinto López, José Ureas, + cadets Ign. Martínez de la Vega, Fernando Tovar, & poblador Luis de las Cuevas; Agto 20: women employed in labors proper to their sex, baja del cadete Estenaga(?); accounts; Agto 27: artillery in the batteries

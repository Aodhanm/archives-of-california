“Mandará articular[?] la orden conv.te p.a reconocer al P. Pdte

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 438

“Mandará articular[?] la orden conv.te p.a reconocer al P. Pdte. como vicario foraneo y castrense.” = will issue the appropriate order for the Father President to be recognized as vicario foráneo and castrense. The verb reads articular; circular is the likelier intent but is not what is written.

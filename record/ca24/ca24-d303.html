Alberni's SF-defense & settlement reconnaissance

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 303

Alberni's SF-defense & settlement reconnaissance: Borica celebrates Alberni's arrival 'á buen tiempo para tranquilizar… la frontera'; the Engineer must arrange the puntos de defensa at San Francisco & examine the terrain 'para hacer el mapa lo menos mal que pueda'; ⭐ Alberni reconnoitered 'los terrenos… buenos para población de Españoles' (lands fit for Spanish settlement); + the Viceroy's order re a settlement promoted by tte Beltran; [top: the S. Pedro Mártir Indians, 30 azotes + 12/Sunday]

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 630

«sobre Comunicacion entre Cal.a y las Prov.as de Sonora - por el Rio Colorado.» Forwards Arrillaga's oficio of Oct. 26 on the Colorado river nations, «su caracter activo e interesado», their lagoon-and-carrizal country where they could «guarecerse y armarnos emboscadas». Two schemes: (1) a presidio at the laguna de Sta Olalla near «las mis.s destruidas en 81 por los Yumas», covering the ford called ‘bicuñe’ [?], 100 men + 20 at S.n Felipe + 20 at Gonotaic [?] Sonora, as Fages proposed; (2) Arrillaga's, a presidio at the river mouth on the Antigua Cal. side, 20-25 leagues from Sta Catalina.

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 830

“Da licencia al ingen.o Alberto de Cordoba p.a ir a Mexico despues de 2 años 7 meses de buenos servicios en Cal.a” [leave for engineer Alberto de Cordoba to go to Mexico after 2 years 7 months of good service in California]. Ends Cordoba’s California tour, which produced the Santa Cruz report (n424) and the coast chart (n427).

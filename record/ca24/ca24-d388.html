“Queda enterado q.e se le quiere obligar [al padre] á retirarse del cargo de la mision

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 388

“Queda enterado q.e se le quiere obligar [al padre] á retirarse del cargo de la mision. Tomará medidas q.e juzgue justas.” [= notes that they wish to force the padre out of charge of the mission, and will take such measures as he judges just]; “al padre” is interlined. The governor siding, at least provisionally, with a friar against his own order.

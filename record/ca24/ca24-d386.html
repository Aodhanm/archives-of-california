Under year “1802.” and the heading “Arrillaga al P. P.te” — “Manda q.e se tomen precauc.s contra los Indios Seris ó Tiburones - p.a cuyo fin mandó tambien unos 4 soldados etc - Avisa q.e tengan cuidado en Sta Gertrudis y S.n Borja.” [= orders precautions against the Seri or Tiburón Indians, has sent four soldiers, warns the missions of Santa Gertrudis and San Borja to be on guard]. Named Indian nation, named missions, military response.

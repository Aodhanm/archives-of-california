“Manda dar gracias, á Dios, por la paz entre la corte de Esp.a y la Rusia

Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 387

“Manda dar gracias, á Dios, por la paz entre la corte de Esp.a y la Rusia. - Otro igual se pasó al […]” [= orders thanksgiving for the peace between the courts of Spain and Russia; a like order was sent to …]. The sentence breaks at the foot of n337 and completes on the ERASED top line of n338, which is illegible even enlarged, so the second addressee is not recovered. Spain-Russia relations reaching the Californias a decade before Ross.

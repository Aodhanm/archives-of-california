Provincial Records, tomo V–VII · BANC MSS C-A 24, document 441

“Arillaga avisa al P. Pdte. q.e en Marzo 25 [1804. interlined by Savage] fué nombrado por el Rey Gob.or en propiedad - politico y militar de la Alta Cal.a” = Arrillaga notifies the Father President that on 25 March 1804 he was named by the King governor in propriety, political and military. Savage writes de la Alta Cal.a; Arrillaga’s propietary commission was for the Californias.

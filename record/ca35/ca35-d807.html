the full 8-article Jones–Micheltorena Convention settling the 19 Oct 1842 Monterey seizure

Departmental State Papers, Los Angeles, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados V–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 35, document 807

the full 8-article Jones–Micheltorena Convention settling the 19 Oct 1842 Monterey seizure — indemnity & private claims reserved to both govts (art. 1–2); mutual non-aggression pledge (3); the Alvarado–Jones Capitulation 'nula y sin efecto' (4); U.S. ships at San Pedro to salute the flag in 10 days (5); Jones to hand over 500 infantry uniforms (6) + pay $1,500 & replace the ruined band instruments (7); quadruplicate English/Spanish (8) → E8 (doc pp.230-3)

the Graham Affair aftermath — the President's disposition on the foreigners of 'la citada revolución'

Departmental State Papers, Los Angeles, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados V–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 35, document 427

the Graham Affair aftermath — the President's disposition on the foreigners of 'la citada revolución': Isaac Graham, Alberto Morris, Guillermo Chard, Jorge [George] held abroad under that juzgado; naturalized or Mexican-married foreigners spared (on bond); the rest expelled from the Republic, never to return to Californias; + reiterates the 1 May 1828 foreigner law (doc pp.—)

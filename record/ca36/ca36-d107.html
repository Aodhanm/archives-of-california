'Reglamento para la enajenación y arriendo de las Misiones'

Departmental State Papers, Los Angeles (Decrees & Despatches), tomo Vol. IX · BANC MSS C-A 36, document 107

'Reglamento para la enajenación y arriendo de las Misiones' — the complete 1845 mission sale-and-lease scheme (23 arts, 5 chapters): SELL San Rafael/Dolores/Soledad/San Miguel/La Purísima (+ surplus buildings at SLO/Carmelo/San Juan Bautista/San Juan Capistrano); LEASE 9-yr San Buenaventura/Santa Bárbara/Santa Inés (+ later San Diego/San Luis Rey/San Gabriel/San Antonio/Santa Clara/San José); proceeds → Treasury → mission debts; ⭐⭐⭐ Cap. 4 De los Indios: neophytes freed, not bound to serve lessees, elect 4 *celadores*; ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Art. 20 — Indians may petition for TITLE to their house/garden plots (inalienable); Cap. 5 Jueces de Paz enforce & protect free use of property

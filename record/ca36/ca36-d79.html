Departmental State Papers, Los Angeles (Decrees & Despatches), tomo Vol. IX · BANC MSS C-A 36, document 79

*Bando* — penalties against aiders of the *revoltosos* (a horse-thief band 'pronounced' in the South): forfeiture; foreigners' death penalty *sin cuartel*; aiders shot/presidio; 8-day surrender amnesty. Eve of the 1844–45 anti-Micheltorena revolt.

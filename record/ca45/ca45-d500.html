Abril 6. Verbatim: 'Que con esta fha. digo al Asesor del Territorio D. Rafael Gomez no se admite su renuncia y q.e continue en su destino hasta q.e el Gobierno nombre otro en su lugar.' [= the Territorial Asesor Rafael Gomez has his resignation refused and is to stay in post until the Government names a successor.] The territorys only trained legal officer kept in place by refusal of resignation.

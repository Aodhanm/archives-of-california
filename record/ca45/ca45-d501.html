Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 501

Abril 12. Verbatim: 'Enterado de la ocurrencia con los cuatro gentiles siempre q.e haya espediciones de esa clase, es preciso q.e los q.e vayan en ella, no procedan a matar a Gentiles sin q.e primero esten seguros de q.e son los q.e bienen a robar.' [= informed of the incident with the four gentiles; whenever there are expeditions of that kind those who go must not proceed to kill gentiles without first being sure they are the ones who come to steal.] A restraint-of-force order on anti-raiding expeditions.

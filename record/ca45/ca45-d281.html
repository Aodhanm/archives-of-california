Desertion of foreign seamen in San Francisco bay. Savage

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 281

Desertion of foreign seamen in San Francisco bay. Savage: 'Anuncia que han desertado cinco marineros de un buque americano ballenero.' [= announces that five sailors have deserted from an American whaling vessel]. Notice circulated inland to the San José alcalde, i.e. an attempt to catch deserters heading for the pueblo.

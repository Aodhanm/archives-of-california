a 1824 Diputación session — makes the ayuntamientos responsible for pueblo *policía*

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 11

a 1824 Diputación session — makes the ayuntamientos responsible for pueblo *policía*; ⭐ debates founding a 'Hospicio de Estudios' (a provincial school for youth) with a recorded VOTE — José Antº Carrillo & Cristóbal Palomares against (fiscal grounds, troops unpaid) vs. the Castros & José Cruz for ('por su grande utilidad á toda la Provincia') — deliberative self-rule + education policy

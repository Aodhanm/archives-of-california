Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 355

Restoration of the San Francisco juzgado. Verbatim: 'Ordena q. por disposicion del Gob. Mich. vuelva á encargarse inmediatamte del Juzgº de S. Francº.' Gloss: Francisco Sanchez is ordered, by Micheltorena's disposition, to take immediate charge again of the San Francisco juzgado. Part of the governor's wholesale reversal of Suñol's 1843 appointments (see the Nota on n379).

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 276

Mission Indian as plaintiff before the San Francisco juzgado. Savage: 'Que se ha presentado el neófito Celso demandando á Francisco Sibrian [?] por un caballo extraído de su propiedad.' [= the neophyte Celso has appeared suing Francisco Sibrian over a horse taken from his property]. Surname Sibrian is an uncertain reading (could be Silvian).

Micheltorena's INAUGURAL circular as Governor

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 164

Micheltorena's INAUGURAL circular as Governor — announces that on 31 Dec 1842 he was 'solemne y legalmente entregado el mando político de Ambas Californias,' sworn before interim gov Manuel Jimeno Casarín; his program to attend to men 'no… á su origen sino á sus virtudes personales' (explicitly welcoming *nacional ó extranjero*) → / foreigners

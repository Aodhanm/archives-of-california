Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 521

Verbatim: '1835. Nov. 10. Mont. / Jose Castro al diputado Antonino Buelna / Le intimida 2.a citacion p.a asistir a las sesiones estraordinarias (de la Diputacion Territorial.) p.p. 99-100.' [= serves him with a second summons to attend the extraordinary sessions of the Territorial Diputacion.] The parenthesis is Savages own gloss. Evidence of attendance/quorum trouble in the Diputacion in late 1835.

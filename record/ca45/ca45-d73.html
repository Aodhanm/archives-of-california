Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 73

a mounted-police bando against raiding: 'frecuentes los robos por indios bárbaros y otros malhechores en el 1er distrito,' so the govt organizes a 20-man force divided into two *partidas* to patrol constantly, funded at 254 pesos/month, subsisting one month then reviewed; San Juan & San José authorities to name the men, govt to supply arms — a standing anti-raiding patrol for the San José district (1840)

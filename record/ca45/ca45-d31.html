Victoria's inaugural *Manifestación* (1 Feb 1831) on taking the mando

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 31

Victoria's inaugural *Manifestación* (1 Feb 1831) on taking the mando — his governing program ('las leyes han de ser cumplidas, el gobierno obedecido… la delicada [tarea]… favorecida la honradez y castigada la perversidad'); + Echeandía's 5 Jan 1832 proclama from San Luis Rey (the interregnum) — the governor's-side bookend to the anti-Victoria manifesto (Doc 30)

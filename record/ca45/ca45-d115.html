'Le avisa q[u]e los P.P. de S[an]ta Clara y de S. José nombraron apoderado suyo a Fr[ancis]co Arce p[ar]a hacer arreglos con los deudores á d[ic]has mis[ione]s.' Gloss: the padres of Santa Clara and San José have named Francisco Arce their attorney to settle with debtors of those missions. Doc 4 of 4 under Savage's single 1844 Monterey heading.

Same standing heading. Entry verbatim: 'Ag.to 27. (2 Dcts.) pp 430. 32.' Doc 1: 'Que se aprueva el nombram.to p.a secret.o del C. Salvio Pacheco.' Doc 2: 'Que se remita el padron.' [= the appointment of citizen Salvio Pacheco as secretary is approved; and that the padrón be remitted.] Savage writes 'aprueva'. Person-layer value: dates Salvio Pacheco in the San José secretaryship.

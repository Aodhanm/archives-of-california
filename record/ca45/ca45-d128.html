Bando to the juez 1o of San Jose publishing the Asamblea Departamental decree of that day by which Pico takes the governorship. Verbatim: 'resultando serlo el Sr Dn Pio Pico se encargara este Sor y desde esta misma fha del gobno departl interinamte, conforme al artc. 138 de las bases organicas de la Nacion' [= Pico, as senior vocal, assumes the interim departmental government under art. 138 of the Bases Organicas, the post vacant 'por estar desconocida esta autoridad' in Gen. Manuel Micheltorena]. spans n271-n272.

Proof of the governor's orders. Verbatim: 'Remitiéndole originales las órdenes del Gral

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 358

Proof of the governor's orders. Verbatim: 'Remitiéndole originales las órdenes del Gral. Micheltª para que no dudare de su autenticidad... las hiciese por escrito pª remitirselas al Gobº.' Gloss: sends Guillen the original orders of General Micheltorena so he may not doubt their authenticity, warning that any objections he has about receiving the judgeship again entrusted to him must be made in writing for transmission to the Governor. Doc 2 of 2 under the Abril 29 heading.

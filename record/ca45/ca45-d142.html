GRAHAM-AFFAIR AFTERMATH ('Estrangeros revolucionados')

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 142

GRAHAM-AFFAIR AFTERMATH ('Estrangeros revolucionados'): transcribes the President's disposition on the prisoners held at Tepic for the attempted uprising — named: Isaac Graham, Alberto (Albert) Morris, Guillermo (William) Chard[?] y Jorge José Bonito[/Bonilla] — plus the order on admission of foreigners in California: admit no foreigner lacking the documents required by law. The 1841 resolution of the 1840 Graham arrests (pairs w/ Doc 59) → /foreigners

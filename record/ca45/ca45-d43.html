Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 43

Indian-labor-bando tail (each Indian one month in the works, then back to his mission); a *policía*-bando model for the pueblo; ⭐ Salvio Pacheco assigned a corral site near the San José *ejidos* (per art. 6 of the pueblos' policía bando); ⭐⭐ the Diputación's 'rodeos de comunidad' reglamento — regulating the communal cattle/horse roundups (breeders with property 'en los contornos… del [pueblo]') — Californio ranching-economy/land regulation

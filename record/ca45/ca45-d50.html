judicial-costs parecer + the reo Juan Higuera's flight; ⭐⭐ Figueroa's illness/handover circular: his health 'quebrantada desde el año de 1833' → hands the political command interimly to the 1st Vocal of the Diputación, will retire once 'ya habré acabado las expediciones militares en que ahora me ocupo' (the anti-raiding expeditions — ties the campaign to his final months; †29 Sep 1835); the 1st Vocal accepts the military command

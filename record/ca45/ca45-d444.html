DATING PROBLEM, stated as such: the month is Savage's looped '?bre' glyph, and a same-scale comparison shows it is the SAME letterform he uses for 'Sbre' on n94. But the entry sits at pp.348-49, between Febrero 5 1830 (pp.344-47) and Febrero 10 1830 (pp.350-51) in a strictly page-ordered run. The year therefore cannot be fixed from this leaf and is NOT asserted here.

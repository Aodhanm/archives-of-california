A whole American-era juzgado book dismissed in one line. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 361

A whole American-era juzgado book dismissed in one line. Verbatim: 'Libro de varios documentos judiciales correspondte al Juzgº de San José; escritos en Ingles bajo la Admnorª ya de los Americanos. Sin interes.' Gloss: a book of assorted judicial documents of the San José court, written in English under the administration now of the Americans; Savage judges it of no interest. It occupies pp. 76-315 of the original bound volume, i.e. some 240 pages of 1847 San José judicial record passed over unabstracted.

Thanks to the outgoing jueces de paz. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 350

Thanks to the outgoing jueces de paz. Verbatim: 'Dandoles las gracias en nombre de la Prefª por el tpo[?] que desempeñaron su cargo que finalizará el 2 de Enº del pte año.' Gloss: the Sub-Prefect thanks Francisco Sanchez and Guillermo Castro in the Prefecture's name for their service, their term closing 2 January. Immediately follows (on n375-n376 top) the circular announcing the newly approved jueces de paz for 1843.

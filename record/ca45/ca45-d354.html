Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 354

Restoration of the Contra Costa juzgado to its former holder; spans n377-n378. Verbatim: 'Ordena q. por [disposicion] del Gob. Micheltª vuelva á hacerse cargo del Juzgo de Contra Costa q. entregó á D. Joaquin Estudillo.' Gloss: Guillermo Castro is ordered, on Micheltorena's disposition, to resume charge of the Contra Costa juzgado which he had handed to Joaquin Estudillo (Estudillo had been named propietario for Contra Costa in the 2 Jan circular, n375-n376).

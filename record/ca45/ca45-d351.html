Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 351

Exile of a woman for concubinage; spans n376-n377. Verbatim: 'Avisa el destierro de Miliana Bernal; ordenandole, no la deje regresar á S. José, pª evitar la discordia en el matrimonio de Fco Palomares quien tiene mucha familia.' Gloss: notifies the San Francisco judge of Miliana Bernal's banishment and orders him not to let her return to San José, to avoid discord in the marriage of Francisco Palomares, who has a large family. Backstory in the 6 Dec doc on n375 (complaint of Margarita Pacheco, Palomares's wife).

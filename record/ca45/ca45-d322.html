'Que notifique por órden del Superior Tribunal á Mariano Castro y acuse á Tomas Pacheco bienes ó raices…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 322

'Que notifique por órden del Superior Tribunal á Mariano Castro y acuse á Tomas Pacheco bienes ó raices de la clase que pueda poner libre á Pacheco y disponga de ellos.' (that he notify Mariano Castro by order of the Superior Tribunal and make over to Tomás Pacheco goods or real property of such kind as may free Pacheco, who is to dispose of them). The verb acuse is clear on the leaf but does not parse; possibly a slip for asegure/adjudique [?]. Execution of a Superior Tribunal judgment on property.

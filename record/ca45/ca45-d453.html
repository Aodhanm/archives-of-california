Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 453

Heading verbatim: '1831. D.bre 21, Pueblo de N.a S.a de los Angeles. / Jose M.a de Echeandia al Ilustre Ayuntam.to del Pueblo de San Jose'. 'Que el S.or D. Manuel Victoria no se halla en aptitud p.a gobernar, y ya camina a embarcarse.' [= that Sr. D. Manuel Victoria is not in a condition to govern, and is already on his way to embark.] Echeandia, writing from Los Angeles a fortnight after the Cahuenga fight, notifies the San Jose ayuntamiento of Victoria's removal and imminent departure.

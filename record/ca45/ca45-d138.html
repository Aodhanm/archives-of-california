Bando publishing the Asamblea's 17-article law dividing California into districts and partidos. Art 1

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 138

Bando publishing the Asamblea's 17-article law dividing California into districts and partidos. Art 1: two districts, Los Angeles (from mision de S. Luis Obispo inclusive to the Baja California frontier) and Monterey (from the northern limit of S. Luis to 'los confines del territorio Mejicano hacia el Norte'). Art 3 gives Monterey two partidos: Monterey (with Sta Cruz and Villa de Branciforte) and 'la Yerba buena desde San Jose Guadalupe hasta los confines del Territorio Mej.co pr. el Norte y el Rio Sacramto.' Prefect 1500 pesos, subprefects 500. spans n278-n281.

Under block heading '1836. Sonoma / Vallejo al alc.de de S. José'. Entry: 'Set. 13.'... 'Causa contra un estrangero por robo de caballo' [= case against a foreigner for horse theft]. Savage gives no further detail and does not name the foreigner. One of the few foreigner items in this stretch.

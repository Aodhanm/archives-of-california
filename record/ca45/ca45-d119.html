Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 119

'Le comunica la desaprobac[io]n terminante del gob[ernado]r por un decreto, de la venta de una [sitio struck through] vivienda en Santa Clara, por Jesus M[arí]a Vásquez á Diego Forbes.' Gloss: notifies the governor's flat disapproval, by decree, of the sale of a dwelling at Santa Clara by Jesús María Vásquez to Diego Forbes [= James Alexander Forbes]. Doc 3 of 3 under the same heading; Savage struck 'sitio' and wrote 'vivienda'.

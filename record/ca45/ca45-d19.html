liquor-tax reglamento (*contribución sobre licores*), full text (Caps 1–4)

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 19

liquor-tax reglamento (*contribución sobre licores*), full text (Caps 1–4): provincial duties on liquor — 5 pesos/barrel aguardiente & 20 r/barrel wine at Monterey & SF; 10 & 5 at Sta Bárbara/LA/SD; collected by the Síndicos (or the Habilitado where there's no ayunt., e.g. San Diego); *guías*/boletas procedure; the *propios y arbitrios* fund the *instituto*. Fiscal self-rule. ⚠ likely a gubernatorial circular = duplicates C-A 42/43

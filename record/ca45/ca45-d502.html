Mayo 22. Verbatim: 'Que se combenga con el M. R. P. Comicario F.r Fran.co Garcia Diego en las medidas q.e propone o en otras q.e sean mas faciles p.a los rodeos, con tal de q.e ni las Misiones ni los particulares se perjudiquen,' followed by a bracketed clause cancelled with heavy cross-hatching, only partly legible (I can read the words about vecinos going out in pursuit of ladrones de caballada carrying arms, but several words are cancelled beyond certainty and I do NOT present that clause as verbatim). [= come to terms with the Father Comisario Fr. Francisco Garcia Diego on the rodeo measures he proposes, or easier ones, so long as neither the Missions nor private parties are injured.] [orig cite: written on the leaf as: pp 509. 10-24. 25]

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 57

an anti-raiding military expedition order: raids have brought 'ruina total de varias familias establecidas en los Ranchos,' so on the 1st of next month a punitive expedition 'con objeto de escarmentar á sus tribus enemigas' marches; every citizen is obligated to serve — enlist a 30-man force of the most patriotic, joined to Capt. D. Antonio Buelna's force, to march the last day of the month; the enlisted to arm & mount themselves (merchants/others excused for personal service) — the Figueroa-era campaign continuing into the Alvarado period

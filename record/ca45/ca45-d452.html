Heading verbatim: 'En.o 31.' 'Que se ha recibido de la Gefatura Politica y Comand.a gral. de este territorio el S.r Ten.te Coronel C. Manuel Victoria, previo el juram.to de costumbre.' [= Lt. Col. Manuel Victoria has taken over the jefatura politica and comandancia general of this territory, after the customary oath.] The handover of both commands from Echeandia to Victoria; pairs with the Victoria inaugural Manifestacion already recorded at n104 (orig p.402-406).

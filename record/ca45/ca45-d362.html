Poisoning inquest over a mission Indian; spans n381-n382. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo IV+ · BANC MSS C-A 45, document 362

Poisoning inquest over a mission Indian; spans n381-n382. Verbatim: 'Causa Criminal instruida en averiguacion de la muerte de un indígena llamado Juan, perteneciente á la Mision de S. Luis Rey y encontrado á 400 vs distante de las casas del Rancho llamada Milpitas sin herida alguna y con señales de envenenamtº.' Gloss: body found 400 varas from the Milpitas ranch houses, unwounded, showing signs of poisoning; the india Susana who lived with him is supposed author, the indio Paulino her lover an accomplice. Both jailed, both protest innocence.

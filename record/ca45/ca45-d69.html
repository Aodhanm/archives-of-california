tail of the false-invasion-rumor doc (Mexico's distant tribunals, pp.221–2); ⭐⭐ 'Campaña sobre los Tulares': Alvarado acknowledges the return of the Tulares campaign — 'con incendio de rancherías' (burning of Indian villages), 1 man lost & some wounded, 'el orden público… se halla restablecido'; 'un tal Tobar que vino de Sonora se fué huyendo á su país,' artillery recovered, & he pardoned 'Sr. Dn. Carlos [Carrillo] y varios mejicanos… su crimen' — the 1838 Central-Valley punitive expedition folded into the southern-rebellion mop-up → (Tulares)

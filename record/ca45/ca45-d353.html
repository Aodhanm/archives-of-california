Handover of the San José juzgado. Verbatim: 'Ordenandole por disposicion del Gob. Micheltª entregar el Juzgado en el estado en que lo recibió á D. Isidoro Guillen.' Gloss: by order of Governor Micheltorena the sitting judge is to hand the juzgado over to Isidoro Guillen in the condition in which he received it. Doc 2 of 2 under the '(2 Docs.)' heading dated Feb 23 on this leaf; Savage dates and page-cites it separately.

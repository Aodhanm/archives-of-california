Heading: '1837. Mrzo 27. / Vallejo al capitan de milicias civicas, Salvio Pacheco. Invasion de Juan José Tobar.' Abstract: 'Le manda bajo la mas estrecha responsabilidad, reunir la guardia civica de su mando para estar pronto á resistir á la invasion de Tobar.' [= orders him under the strictest responsibility to assemble the civic guard under his command, ready to resist Tobar's invasion]. No place written on the leaf.

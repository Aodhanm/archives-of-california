'Cuestión con los P.P. sobre mal entendida conexión con Indios criminales': Gov. Gutiérrez transcribes his communication to the Santa Clara & San José friars accusing them of a 'mal entendida conexión con Indios criminales' and of being 'destructores de la autoridad de los comisarios de policía,' demanding they abstain — the state charging the missions with *harboring* the Indians it wanted punished (a friar-vs-state / mission-Indian item complicating the raiding narrative) → + secularization; + a prisoner relation

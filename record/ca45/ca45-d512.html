Verbatim heading: '1833. Sbre. 20 Los Angeles pp 553. 54. / Jose Figueroa al Alc.de de S. Jose'. Verbatim text: 'Que se lleve a efeto mi orn. y q.e el Ayuntam.to no tiene facultad de sentenciar ni destinar a los vagos a obras publicas.' ('efeto' as written.) [= my order is to be carried into effect, and the ayuntamiento has NO power to sentence vagrants or to assign them to public works.] A governors ruling stripping the municipality of penal jurisdiction over vagrancy, three months after he ordered art. 5 of the ley de vagos enforced (n120, 18 May 1833). Directly on point for the alcalde-jurisdiction question.

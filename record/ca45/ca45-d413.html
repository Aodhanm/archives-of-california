'Febrero 13 (2 docs.) Comunicandole que el Licenciado Jose Joaquin Avilez Juez de Circuito del Estado de Sonora, de Sinaloa y de ambas Californias, le ha anunciado que el Tribunal correspondte Circuital, de q. es cabeza, se instalo en el Rosario el 26 de Julio ultimo.' The federal circuit court covering Sonora, Sinaloa and both Californias installed at El Rosario, 26 July [1827]. Doc 2 (tail on n52) is the incest-militiaman sumaria plus the rule that all crimes in the pueblo be judged first by the alcalde; that second document is already in the export under the n52-n54 record, but cited there as pp. 217-233.

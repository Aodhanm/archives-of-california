Verbatim heading: '+ Julio 19 pp. 33-34' under the standing block heading '1823 - Monterey / Arguello al Alcalde de San Jose.' carried over from n7. Gloss: 19 July 1823, Monterey, Arguello to the Alcalde of San Jose. SUBSTANCE, Savage verbatim: 'Que acaba de saber que Don Antonio Sunol ha introducido en ese Pueblo cantidad de Ron y Miel pa vender al menudeo, y que compone cierto licor q. esta causando mucho dano al pueblo; que se informe de ello y se lo comunique.' [= that he has just learned that Don Antonio Sunol has brought into that Pueblo a quantity of rum and molasses to sell at retail, and that he compounds a certain liquor which is causing much harm to the pueblo; that he inform himself of it and report to him.] The surname is written with the tilde, Sunol, and the given name Antonio: the San Jose merchant Antonio Maria Sunol.

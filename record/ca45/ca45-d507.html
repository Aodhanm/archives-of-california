Mayo 18. Verbatim: 'Que se obserbe el articulo 5.o de la ley sobre bagos y viciosos.' ('obserbe' and 'bagos' as written.) [= article 5 of the law on vagrants and the vicious is to be observed.] Pairs with the 20 Sept 1833 vagrancy-jurisdiction ruling on n127.

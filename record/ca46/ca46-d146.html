Departmental Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 46, document 146

Barba. al de Sn. Diego con la mayor brevedad, sin admitir demora' - fixes the mail schedule between presidios; estafeta administrators to publish it in their districts. Ends pp.78-9 on n110.

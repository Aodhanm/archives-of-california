Departmental Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 46, document 30

'Es en mi poder el Sob.o Dec.to de fha. de 23 de Ab.l p.o p.o en el q.e se ordena q.e todos los q.e no huviesen reconocido espresam.te el actual Gob.o lo verifiquen inmediatam.te... han adherido á tan justo Gob.o y q.e dará cumplim.to á lo q.e se le órdena de q.e le cante misa Solemne Tedeum y se hagan las precas y litanias' — decree of 23 Apr requiring express recognition of the government; province has adhered; solemn Te Deum, prayers and litanies ordered (runs onto n20)

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Recipient recorded as the Minister of War.. The docket (blind sample audit #138/200, 2026-07-19) reads "Argüello al Mntro de ESTADO: Naufragio de un Buqe" — the letter goes to the Minister of State, forwarding the parte rendered to the Minister of War.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

State-of-forces report (heading on n210, tail runs through n211–212)

Departmental Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 46, document 275

State-of-forces report (heading on n210, tail runs through n211–212): 'el Estado de sus fuerzas Militar.s p.a su defenza é inutilidad de su armam.to en q.e estriba su seguridad' — 11 companies (4 presidial cavalry, 2 auxiliary infantry, 1 militia artillery, 4 newly raised national militia) 'estan sin ning.n Armam.to'; the Mazatlán/San Blas auxiliary 2.a comp.a sent 1819 now down to 56 men incl. officers; asks 25 qq. fine powder, 50 qq. ordinary, 8 cannon of 24 and 18 (at SF 'solo hay 3 cañon.s de á 24'); force 'se halla desteriorada' — asks S.M.I. (Iturbide) resolve whether the company be completed 'ó se estingue del todo'

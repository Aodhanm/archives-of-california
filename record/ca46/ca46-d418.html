Departmental Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 46, document 418

START of the á-Martínez series. Foreign brig at SF: per Martínez's oficio 'de 15 del preste. mes' [sic], anchored the day before 'el Bergn. Ingles (qe. á mi, me parece será Americano) mercante nombrado 'Guafi' procedte. de Sandwich á cargo de su Capn. Kele, con 22 hombs. de tripulacn.'; Martínez told nothing of whether it came to trade or from necessity of víveres — manifest of cargo required 'ántes de dar el permiso de tratar'; since it must soon come here, 'aqui se le ecsigirá' (ends on n295)

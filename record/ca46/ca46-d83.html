Valle violating his arrest: 'el ten.te D.n Ant.o Valle no guarda en el arresto

Departmental Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 46, document 83

Valle violating his arrest: 'el ten.te D.n Ant.o Valle no guarda en el arresto... aquella reclus.n' but 'mirando con desprecio é indiferencia mis órd.s, prueva su insubordinac.n é irrespecto, con andar paseando á varias distancias fuera de la casa'; orders him brought to the presidio and lodged 'interin dispongo lo q.e convenga'; if he resists 'inmediatam.te me dará V. aviso p.a remitirle 1 cabo, y 4 sold.s q.e hagan cumpla y obedezca mis órdenes' — gloss: Valle caught strolling outside his mission confinement; to be marched to the presidio, with a corporal and 4 soldiers authorized if he resists.

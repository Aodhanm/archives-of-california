Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read orig_page: 'p.56'. Leaf n55 (stamp 56, ink 55) of bancarchca_81_14. The Romero petition foots its tomo citation as 'pp. 373-4' (which the record itself preserves inside the summary). The recorded orig_page 56 is the leaf stamp, not the tomo page; the citable pages are 373-374.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

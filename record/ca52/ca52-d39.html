State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 52, document 39

San José 1783 possession dossier (COMPLETE roster, item-read) — Fages's framing ordinance + Moraga's *autos*; for each poblador 3 *diligencias*: (1) a 30-vara-square solar, (2) 4 *suertes de tierra* (200×200 varas, irrigated), (3) a cattle brand (fierro); dated 9 May 1783. ⭐ The 9 grantee-pobladores: Manuel González, José Tiburcio Vásquez, Manuel Amézquita, Antonio Romero, Sebastián Alvitre, Bernardo Rosales, Francisco Ábila, Claudio Alvires (Alviso), Juan Atanacio Vásquez (adjacency notes tie their solares/suertes together). Officials/witnesses: José Moraga (comisionado), Alcalde Ignacio Archuleta, Juan Pablo Grijalva, Juan José Peralta, Felipe Tapia, Felipe Saavedra, Antonio Butrón (most pobladores illiterate → signed by proxy) ⚠ SPAN NARROWED 2026-08-27 (band audit): read n240-n269 and concealed three documents. True close n266.

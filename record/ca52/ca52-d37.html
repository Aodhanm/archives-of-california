1782 PLANO/MAP of the Pueblo San José *suertes de tierra*

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 52, document 37

1782 PLANO/MAP of the Pueblo San José *suertes de tierra* — the grid of distributed farm-plots (each 200 v²) labeled with the pobladores' names (Amézquita, Alvitre, Claudio Alvires, Butrón, González, B. Rosales, Peralta, Ábila, J.T. Vásquez, Archuleta, Ortega…), showing the Río de Guadalupe + acequia; distributed & delivered to their owners 23 Apr 1782 → (a founding land-distribution map; companion to the LA 1786 plano @n302)

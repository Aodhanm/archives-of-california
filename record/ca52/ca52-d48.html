Virrey Antonio María de Bucareli y Ursúa to Gobernador de Californias, México, 1774-09-30

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 52, document 48

RECOVERED BY THE PHASE-4 AUDIT 2026-08-27 — no record existed. Heading verbatim: '1774 - Set.e 30 - Mex.o / El Virey Bucareli / (Instrucciones al Gob.r de Californias)'. Opens: 'Todo buen M.tro debe tener p.r Norte de sus operaciones el serv.o de Dios y del Rey. La Peninsula de Calif.s ha padecido confusiones q.e deben desterrarse, y necesita de un sugeto prud.te y amante del serv.o p.a establecer, mantener y radicar el buen or.n' [every good minister must take as his lodestar the service of God and the King; the Peninsula of the Californias has suffered confusions which must be banished, and needs a prudent man devoted to the service to establish, maintain and root good order]. Names the friction 'entre los Mtros. Rs. y RR. Ps. Misioneros' and 'las desavenencias entre los Religiosos Dominicos y el Gob.r D.r Felipe Barri'. Runs as numbered articles across n305-n314: art. 4 on observance by the Gobernador de Californias, the Comandante of the new establishments and the San Blas commissary; art. 5 on Felipe Barri and the Reglamento Provisional; art. 6 on harmony with the missionaries and the 6 June 1772 delivery of the missions to the Dominicans.

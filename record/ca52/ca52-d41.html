Gov. Diego de Borica (Monterey) to Fr. Francisco Miguel Sanchez; operative order directed to the engineer Alberto de Cordova, Monterey, 1797-05-11

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 52, document 41

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n240-n269, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '1797 - Mayo 11 - Monterey / Borica al P. Sanchez: Limites de la Misn. de Santa Clara y Pueblo de Sn. Jose.' [= 1797, May 11, Monterey. Borica to Father Sanchez: boundaries of Mission Santa Clara and the pueblo of San Jose. (Formally a decree of referral, endorsed on Sanchez's letter.)] Borica's decree, and the operative order behind the entire 1797 survey. 'Pase este Espediente al Ingro. extraordo. Dn. Alberto de Cordova pa. qe. a su regreso del Presidio de Sn. Franco. determine los limites entre la Misn. de Sta. Clara y el Pueblo de Sn. Jose, oyendo a los RR. PP. Mtros. de aquella, y al Alcalde, Rejidores y vecinos de este, sobre sus mutuas pretenciones, pero sirviendole de Norte pa. las medidas y linderos el ultº. auto del Tente. Difto. Dn. Jose Moraga en el cual constan y se senalan las qe. se destinaron a dho. Pueblo pa. fabricas, labores, y propios; con advertencia de qe. las medidas se han de entender desde el Antº. Pueblo de donde se echaron, no desde el terreno qe. hoy ocupa, pr. estar asi determinado pr. la superioridad.' Cordova is then to set the mojoneras the vecinos must immediately erect, and to draw up the corresponding diligencia in full clarity so that no doubt arises in future about the dividing line of Mission and Pueblo, 'firmando en ella los RR. PP. Ministros pr. sus Indios, y el Alcalde y Regidores pr. el vecindario'. Two things make this a substantive loss rather than a formality. First, it names the deceased Moraga's final auto (the very 1783 dossier that record d36 covers) as the controlling instrument, which ties the band's own contents to the 1797 case. Second, it rules that all measurement runs from the ORIGINAL pueblo site the settlers were moved off, not the ground they then occupied, 'por estar asi determinado por la superioridad' - and the superior determination it is invoking is the document sitting immediately below it on the same leaf.

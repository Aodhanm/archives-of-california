Heading: '1804 - Nov.e 28 - S.n Fran.co - Argüello á Arrillaga.' Abstract: 'Que ha recibido la R.l órden q.e le envió con oficio 20 de Oct.e sobre no admision de buques estranjeros' [= acknowledges the royal order, sent under cover of the governor's oficio of 20 October, on the non-admission of foreign vessels]. Second of two documents of the same date, place and correspondents on this leaf — a genuinely separate piece (p. 833 vs p. 834), not a duplicate. Pairs directly with the Hazard warning at n334: the exclusion policy in force in 1804.

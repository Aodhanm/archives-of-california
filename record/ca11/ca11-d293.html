'Informa qe. no cree fundada la pretension de Jose Herrera, sobre qe. se le paguen los anos qe

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVIII · BANC MSS C-A 11, document 293

'Informa qe. no cree fundada la pretension de Jose Herrera, sobre qe. se le paguen los anos qe. sirvio en la marina' [= reports that he does not consider Jose Herrera's claim well founded, that the years he served in the navy be credited/paid]. Heading reads verbatim '1804 - Mzo. 7 - Sn. Franco' (magnified and confirmed as Mzo.), although it precedes the Feb. 1 and Feb. 20 1804 entries that follow and carries a lower original page number - see issues.

'Licencia absoluta á un soldado.' 'Que en vista de su informe sobre la mala conducta del soldado Salvador Linares, le remite su licencia absoluta, recomendándole qe le aplique doble castigo si reincide.' — On de la Guerra's report of soldier Salvador Linares's bad conduct, Arrillaga sends his absolute discharge and recommends double punishment if he reoffends.

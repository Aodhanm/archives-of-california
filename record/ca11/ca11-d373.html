spans n327-n328. 'Memorial del Cirujano Benites.' 'Que ha recibido el memorial qe presentó el nuevo cirujano Benites en qe solicita trasladarse á buques del Apostadero de Sn Blas ó á su pais, el qe ha enviado al Virrey.' — Reports receipt of the memorial of the new surgeon Benites requesting transfer to the ships of the San Blas naval station or return to his own country, which he has forwarded to the Viceroy.

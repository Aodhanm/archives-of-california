Heading verbatim: '+ 1800. - Oct.e 12 - Branciforte. - / Ignacio Vallejo á Sal. Semillas cosechadas en aquella Villa en dicho año 1800.' [= Ignacio Vallejo to Sal. Seed/grain harvested in that Villa in the said year 1800.] Body in full, verbatim: 'Trigo, 264 f.s Maiz, 386; frijol, 18, garbanzo, 10. Total 678 -' [= wheat 264 fanegas, maize 386, beans 18, chickpeas 10, total 678]. INTERNAL CHECK: 264+386+18+10 = 678 exactly, which independently confirms my reading of all four numerals and the total. Entry begins and ends on this leaf.

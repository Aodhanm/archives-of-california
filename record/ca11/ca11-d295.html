Provincial State Papers, tomo XVIII · BANC MSS C-A 11, document 295

Spans n264-n265. 'Informa qe. el soldado Herrera ha cumplido, durante el largo tpo. qe. ha servido a sus ordenes con sus obligaciones, pero qe. en lo particular es de mala fe e inclinado al hurto' [= reports that soldier Herrera has fulfilled his duties throughout the long time he has served under him, but that in private life he is of bad faith and given to theft]. Blunt character verdict closing the Herrera dossier.

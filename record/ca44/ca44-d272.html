Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 272

Decree authorising the government to establish weekly mails at the points where mail was received only every fifteen days. 'Decreto autorizando al Gob.o para que pueda establecer correos semanarios en los puntos q.e no se recibian sino cada 15 dias' [= upgrade of fortnightly to weekly post routes].

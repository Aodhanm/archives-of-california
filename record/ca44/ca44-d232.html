Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 232

'Decreto ordenando que el Sup.mo Gob.o disponga se forme en todo el presente ano, el censo Gral. de los habitantes de la Federacion.' [= orders the Supreme Government to have the general census of the inhabitants of the Federation drawn up during the present year]. Relevant as the legal basis for territorial and pueblo padrones.

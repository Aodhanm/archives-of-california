Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 238

Spans n209-n210. 'Decreto previniendo que la pena de los Complices en la causa a q. se contrae el decreto de facultades extraord.s de 14 de Set. de 1829, que no esten condenados a muerte por dos sentencias conformes, no pasaran de cuatro anos de prision contados desde el dia en q. fueron condenados a ella.' [= accomplices in the cause covered by the extraordinary-faculties decree of 14 Sept 1829 who are not condemned to death by two concurring sentences shall serve no more than four years' imprisonment, counted from the day of condemnation].

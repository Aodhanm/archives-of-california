(a) 'Marzo 9 = Sobre siembras', pp. 592. 593: the wheat sowing by the vecindario is concluded

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 730

(a) 'Marzo 9 = Sobre siembras', pp. 592. 593: the wheat sowing by the vecindario is concluded; besides, other individuals of the Compañia de San Fran.co have sown, and these must pay Diezmos on all they harvest; the sorteo of the Milicianos and of mozos for soldiers noted. (b) Abril 18, pp. 594. 595: the burial certificate of the invalid Juan Amezquita received; the Subt.te de Art.a Manuel Gomez to give a list of the mozos fit for Milicianos; Sola writes to the RR. PP. Mtros. of Santa Clara about the affairs of the Miliciano Peralta; and the Indians who fled from San Francisco are to be seized and remitted to this presidio. (c) Abril 26, pp. 596. 597: the militiaman José Peralta and M.a Ant.a Altamirano to remain at absolute liberty, the woman warned with much severity and delivered to her parents.

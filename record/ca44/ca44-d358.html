Publication of decrees of particular object. Verbatim: «los decretos que tubieren objeto particular o se contraigan a determinadas personas, bastara se hicieran notorios al individuo o individuos a quienes tocase su cumplimto.» Gloss: decrees touching named persons need only be made known to those persons, their insertion in the newspapers sufficing for public notoriety. Savage copies this decree a second time at n260 marked (Dup.), cross-referring to his own extract p. 105 (= this leaf).

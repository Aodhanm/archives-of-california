'Decreto del Congº, declarando subsistente la parte segunda del Artº 1º de la ley de 23 de Junio de 1833, hasta tanto no la derogase el Congº Gral.' = the second part of Art. 1 of the law of 23 June 1833 (the ley del caso, banishment of named citizens) declared to remain in force until Congress repeals it.

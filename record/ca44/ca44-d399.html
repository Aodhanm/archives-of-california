Congress extends to all classes of duties the earlier decree exempting from import duties the arms of…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 399

Congress extends to all classes of duties the earlier decree exempting from import duties the arms of calibre, sabres and munitions brought into the Republic for the civic militia, the exemption reaching stock already detained in the ports for that purpose. Savage: 'haciendo extensivo a toda clase de dros. el Decreto espedido por el Cong. gral. q. eximir del pago de dros. de importacion, el armamento de calibre, sables y municiones q. se introduscan en la Republica p.a la milicia civica.' Distinct from the 19 Apr 1834 decree at p. 607.

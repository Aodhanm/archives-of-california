(a) Obre 23, pp. 619. 620: set at liberty Julián Cantua and Andrés García

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 736

(a) Obre 23, pp. 619. 620: set at liberty Julián Cantua and Andrés García; proceed to the construction of the room for the school and the maestro's dwelling; the solares de repartimiento assigned to the newly settled are in order, and 'de ninguna manera dará V. solar fuera del cuadro'; and remit a list of the vecinos fit for cavalry soldiers. (b) Ntre. 21, pp. 621. 622: the vecino militiamen and fit invalids to come to this presidio. (c) Dbre. 16, pp. 623. 624, the sharpest item on the leaf: 'Que José Castro, Juan Albirez y Antonino Buelna llebaron p.a el pueblo cada uno su maleta del saqueo q.e hicieron en el Presidio y entre otras cosas llebaban Paño y Pabilo, proceda con mucha brebedad y reserba al registro de sus casas y dará aviso de su resultado.' Given the date, this concerns plunder taken at Monterey; I have not verified against the Bouchard narrative and flag the connection as inference only.

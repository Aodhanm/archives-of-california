MAJOR, marked 'O'. Heading verbatim: '1822. Nbre. 23. Mont. / Pablo V.te de Sola á los Sres. del Ilustre Ayuntam.to del del pueblo de San José = Sobre Diputado al S. C. de Megico.' In the acta held that day the Exma. Diputación agreed that Sola, as Diputado named by this Province to the Soberano Congreso de Cortes de Mégico, should carry a paper from the Diputación and one from each Ayuntamiento of the province giving the parabién to the Emperor D. Agustín 1º on the proclamation by which the Soberano Congreso constituyente and its corporations made him Emperor, and that Sola is commissioned to give the enhorabuena and make the recognition of his person as Diputado. The Ayuntamiento is to remit its paper without loss of time to his successor Capt. D. Luis Antonio Argüello, who will forward it to the presidio of San Diego where the ship will call, the sailing from this port being on the 25th.

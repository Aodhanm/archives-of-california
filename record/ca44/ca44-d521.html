Ministerial change at Hacienda. Verbatim: 'Que por renuncia de S.rio de H.da Jose Ign.o Esteva, se ha nombrado p.a dicho encargo a Tomas Salgado.' [= on the resignation of Secretary of the Treasury Jose Ignacio Esteva, Tomas Salgado has been named to that charge]. Dates the Salgado signature used elsewhere in this register.

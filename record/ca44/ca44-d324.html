Abolition of the compulsory tithe: 'Decreto del Congreso, mandando cesar en toda la Republica, la obligacion civil de pagar el diezmo eclesiastico.' Gloss: Congress orders that throughout the Republic the civil obligation to pay the ecclesiastical tithe shall cease. One of the core Gomez Farias church-state reform laws; directly relevant to mission and parish finance in Alta California.

Seizure of the Duque de Monteleone estates for schools. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 357

Seizure of the Duque de Monteleone estates for schools. Verbatim: «autorizando al Gobno para entrar en posesion de los bienes nacionales situados en el Distto. y q. posiia en la actualidad un descendiente de Hernan Cortez conocido con el titulo de Duque de Monteleone». Gloss: government to take possession of the Cortes-descendant entail in the Federal District, the rents applied exclusively to the free public schools of the capital, after deducting enough to keep in the Hospital de S. Andres a number of beds equal to that of the Hospital de Jesus.

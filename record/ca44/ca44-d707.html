Three Sola orders of 1822. (a) Jan 8, pp. 484. 485: the Diezmo produce noted

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 707

Three Sola orders of 1822. (a) Jan 8, pp. 484. 485: the Diezmo produce noted; the rodeo to be held on the south side of the pueblo; the cerco round the pueblo to be made. (b) Feb 16, pp. 486. 487: name who would serve to relieve the pueblo's Alcalde Agustín Narváez. (c) Apr 27, p. 488: approves receiving the vecino Fran.co Castro as Alcalde, and says he will very soon dispatch someone to the pueblo so that the Independencia del Imperio Megicano en la Nueva España may be sworn there. The lower part of the leaf opens the Apr 30 Jura document, which is catalogued already (see interiors).

Aggregate return of the presidial companies of the Internal States of the East and West

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 449

Aggregate return of the presidial companies of the Internal States of the East and West. 'Estado de Compañias presidiales de estados internos de Oriente y Occidente.' Column heads: Compañias / Oficiales capellanes y armeros / Fuerza de Tropa / Haberes en el año. Totals: 27 companies, 157 officers-chaplains-armourers, 2.812 troops, $787,818.2.0 [= the whole northern presidial establishment costed in one line; California is not included in the series].

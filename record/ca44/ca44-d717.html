Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 717

(a) Close of Jun 27, pp. 520. 521: Robles has been punished and admonished by Sola and his predecessors for bad conduct, so keep watch on him. (b) Jun 28, pp. 522. 523: Pedro Amador to vacate the house in the coming month of July and hand it to whoever sold it to him. (c) Jun 29, p. 524: go to Pedro Amador's house with two witnesses, read him the memorial and deliver it. (d) Ag.to 11, pp. 525. 526: the release of Antonio Chavoya and Domingo Peralta noted; the little fruit some get from their work; watch Robles's conduct.

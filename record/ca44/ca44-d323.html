Spans n242-n243. Congressional decree extending emergency powers

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 323

Spans n242-n243. Congressional decree extending emergency powers: 'Decreto del Congreso prorogando por cuatro meses a contar del dia 8 del corriente, las facultades consedidas al ejecutivo en 7 de Junio.' Gloss: prolongs for four months, counting from the 8th of the current month, the faculties granted to the executive on 7 June [1833]. Heading on n242, abstract completed at the head of n243.

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 454

'Esteva Srio. de Hcda. á Comt.e de Cal. — Pide doc. estadisticos.' [= the Treasury minister requests statistical documents from the commandant of the Californias]. Savage's abstract is this bare line; the underlying request is the sort that generated the padrones and revenue returns of the mid-1820s.

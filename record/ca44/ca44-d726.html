(a) Close of Julio 2, cited by Savage 'pp. 170. 171.' though the run is in the 570s

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 726

(a) Close of Julio 2, cited by Savage 'pp. 170. 171.' though the run is in the 570s: the invalid José Aguila and Joaquín Buelna have arrived at the pueblo; the vecindario cannot gather flour for Monterey; and, on the theft of the Yegua Caponera with twelve horses belonging to the Alcalde, 'cada dia me ratifico mas en el concepto del abandono con q.e miran sus bienes los vecinos de ese pueblo', with an order that the vecindario gather the tame caballada into their corrals. (b) p. 572: noted that he has imprisoned Antonio Castro for the knife wound he gave the militiaman of the R.l Cpo. de Art.a Cruz Chaboya; form the sumario, reporting to the Tte. grad.o of that Royal Corps D. Manuel Gomez. (c) Julio 4, pp. 573. 574: form a small sumario on the wound given by the boy Castro to the other named Chaboya.

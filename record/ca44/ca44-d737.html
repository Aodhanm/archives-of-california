A paired gambling file spanning nineteen years. (a) '1817. En.o 4. Mont

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 737

A paired gambling file spanning nineteen years. (a) '1817. En.o 4. Mont. / Sola al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José', pp. 625. 626: noted the completion of the construction of the Bridge. (b) '1798. En.o 12. S. José / Los Vecinos del P. de S. José al Gob.r D. Diego de Borica', pp. 627. 628: 'Solicitan se les permita jugar Malilla los Domingos en el Cuerpo de Guardia, y Borica al margen de la solicitud les concede lo q.e piden.' (c) '1817. Dbre. 2. Mont. / Sola', p. 628: although his predecessor Coronel D. Diego de Borica saw fit to permit the game of Malilla or other card play on feast days in the Guardia of the pueblo of San José, being informed of the abuses committed in it he orders that not even on feast days is it to be permitted at that post - Sola expressly revoking Borica's marginal grant.

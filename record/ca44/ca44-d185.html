Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 185

Spans n188-n189. Guerrero, under extraordinary powers, prorogues and extends the general Congress decree of 20 August last even to those who were not called upon to march to operate against the enemy in the foreign invasion; Savage notes the said decree concerns gratuities to the army. Savage: '...aunque no les haya tocado la suerte de ponerse en marcha pª operar contra el enemigo en la invacion estrangera. Dho. decreto se refiere a las gratificaciones al ejercito.' [= Barradas invasion bounties]

Spans n370-n375. Decree of the Alta California Diputación dividing the territory into two cantons. 'El primero Se denominará de Monterrey, cuya Capital será la poblacion de este nombre, y comprenderá dicha municipalidad, las de S.n José y San Francisco, inclusa la frontera del norte' [= the first canton, Monterey, takes in the municipalities of San José and San Francisco and the northern frontier]; second canton = Los Angeles with Sta Barbara and San Diego. Each canton to have a Gefe Político (art. 4-14), elected from a terna formed by the ayuntamientos; powers include watching over mission property, granting colonizable land, and licensing marriages.

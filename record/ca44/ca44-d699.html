Four Borica orders of Oct 1797 to the comisionado of San José, abstracted by Savage. (a) Oct 3

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 699

Four Borica orders of Oct 1797 to the comisionado of San José, abstracted by Savage. (a) Oct 3: 'Que las urgencias del servicio obligan á hechar mano p.a soldados á los ocho jovenes q.e se piden.' (b) Oct 14: noted the departure of the eight youths for soldiers, and that the pueblo's caballadas y ganados are not to pass onto the lands of Mission San José. (c) Oct 23: the cattle 'aquerenciados en los terrenos de la Mision de San José' belonging to those vecinos are to be withdrawn. (d) Oct 27 (ends on n90): gather the hemp and remit it 'á jornadas dobles' so it is here Sunday, and if cabo Mateo Bello opposes the order, arrest him.

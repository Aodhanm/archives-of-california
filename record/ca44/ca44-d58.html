10–12 fanegas maize for distribution; Cabo Vallejo to keep the monthly work-diary

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 58

10–12 fanegas maize for distribution; Cabo Vallejo to keep the monthly work-diary; ⭐ 'Medidas sobre robos' — measures vs the robberies Gentile or Christian Indians commit at harvest time (guards in the Cuerpo de Guardia); apprenticeships (José de los Santos, Hermenegildo, José Gabriel to the smith's trade); ⭐ the death of vecino Justo Saer — goods to his eldest son under the widow's direction, inventory by the Alcalde per Recopilación 'art. 6 del tít. 14' (pueblo inheritance procedure)

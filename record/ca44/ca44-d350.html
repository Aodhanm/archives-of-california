Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 350

Duty rebate for goods carried in Mexican bottoms. Verbatim: «Decreto del Congreso gral. concediendo el privilegio de la 5a parte de rebaja de dros. a los efectos y mercancias trasportados del estrangero a los puertos de la Republica, en buques nacionales.» Gloss: a one-fifth reduction of import duties on foreign goods brought to Republican ports in national vessels. Relevant to the California coasting/hide trade in principle, though the decree is general.

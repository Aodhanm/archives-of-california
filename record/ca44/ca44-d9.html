1785 Oct index: refers to the late José Moraga's provision (SF)

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 9

1785 Oct index: refers to the late José Moraga's provision (SF); ⭐ the diezmo of maize & frijol from each pueblo individual → set aside for the *pósito* (granary), surplus to the presidios; Ramón Lasso to supply clothing on the pobladores' account; pursue deserters (via the SF teniente); orders to be obeyed 'al pie de la letra'; on commerce with the Indians

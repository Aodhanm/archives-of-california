(a) '1816. En.o 11 Mont. / Sola al Com.do del pueblo de S. José', pp. 651. 652

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 741

(a) '1816. En.o 11 Mont. / Sola al Com.do del pueblo de S. José', pp. 651. 652: Carlos Castro not to go to the house of his brother Mariano Castro. (b) Fbro. 10, pp. 653. 654: approves that the Alferez graduado D. Manuel Gomez had made the filiaciones of the newly enlisted for the Comp.a de milicias de Art.a, from which he may except Fran.co Valencio, José Larios, Dolores Mesa and Gregorio Aceves. (c) Fbro. 16, p. 655: Sgt. José Dolores Pico goes to Mission Sta. Clara to bring 100 fanegas of maize for this presidio, and some carretas of the vecindario are to be furnished him; Vicente Cabañas Castañeda and Pedro Arias to be despatched to this presidio. (d) 'Fbro 29. = Sobre caballada mesteña' opens, marked 'O', closing on n146.

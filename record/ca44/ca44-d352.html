Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 352

Extraordinary session of Congress convoked. Verbatim: «Decreto del Presidte Santa-Anna, convocando al Congo Gral a seciones extraordinarias pa el 1o de Junio; haciendo iniciativa sobre los asuntos q. debia tratar.» Gloss: Congress called into extraordinary session for 1 June 1833, the President himself proposing the business to be taken up.

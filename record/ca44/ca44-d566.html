Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 566

'Se autoriza al Gobo. pa. gastar las Cantidades necesarias en la Colonizacion de Territorios.' [= the government is authorized to spend the sums necessary on the colonization of the Territories]. This is the federal spending authorization standing behind the 1834 Híjar-Padrés colonizing expedition to California.

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 482

Decree that [vessels] be admitted at no port of the Republic, whatever their origin and passport, while the war with Spain lasts. Savage: 'Sobre q.e no se admitan en ningun Puerto de la República, sea cual fuere su procedencia y pasaporte mientras dure la Guerra con España.' Savage omits the noun; the object of 'no se admitan' is not stated on the leaf.

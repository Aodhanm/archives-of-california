Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 728

Two documents, both substantial. (a) 'Dbre. 21. = Sobre mozos p.a las armas', pp. 584. 585: of the fourteen mozos fit for arms in that pueblo, they are to be assembled and a sorteo held and five taken for service, but any who is the only son of a widow or of a sexagenarian father is not to be included in the sorteo; and the Diezmos of last year and the present are to be collected. (b) '1818. En.o 8. Mont. / Sola al Com.do Del Pueblo de S. José. = Sobre Ganado y escuela', pp. 586. 587: acknowledges the Padrón General of the pueblo, its estado, the inventory of pósito stocks and two lists of the ganados chichiguos y cimarrones; the tame cattle are to be conserved and the mostrenco reduced to rodeo; and the school is going to be established with all formality, for which he names as maestro the vecino Antonino Buelna, to whom every vecino of the pueblo must annually deliver one fanega of wheat or maize for his work.

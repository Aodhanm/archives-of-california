Nomination of a Mexican-born bishop for Yucatan. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 370

Nomination of a Mexican-born bishop for Yucatan. Verbatim: «previniendo que el Gobno Gral oyendo previamte a los gobiernos de Yucatan y Tabasco, presentaria a la Santa Sede para la silla episcopal de aquella Diocesis, un eclesiastico mejicano por nacimiento, de conosida virtud e ilustracion.» Gloss: after hearing the governments of Yucatan and Tabasco, the national government is to present to the Holy See a Mexican-born ecclesiastic of known virtue and learning for that see. Note the year 1834, an outlier in an otherwise 1833 run.

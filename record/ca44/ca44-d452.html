Decree that the Supreme Court of Justice shall for the present exercise in the District and Territories…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 452

Decree that the Supreme Court of Justice shall for the present exercise in the District and Territories the attributions which by the law of 9 October 1812 belonged to the audiencias de Ultramar (composed of 3 salas), so far as these do not conflict with the Constitution and laws of the Union. 'Suprma Corte de justicia ejercerá por ahora en el Distrito y Territorios las atribuciones q.e por ley de 9 de Octubre de 812 correspondian á las audiencias de Ultramar' [= the appellate jurisdiction over the Territories, California among them, is lodged in the Supreme Court].

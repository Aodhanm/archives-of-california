Spans n249-n250. Revocation of the coalition plan

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 340

Spans n249-n250. Revocation of the coalition plan: 'Decreto de Santa-Anna, mandando cesar la orden de 12 de Set. del corriente ano y 14 del mismo, q. aprobo el plan de coalicion propuesto por los Estados de Zacatecas y Jalisco.' Gloss: the orders of 12 and 14 Sept. of the current year that approved the coalition plan proposed by Zacatecas and Jalisco are to cease. Heading on n249, abstract completed at head of n250.

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 260

Decree establishing a direccion general de rentas, under whose inspection would fall all branches of the Treasury administered on account of the Federation, excepting the general administration of posts and the direction of the Mint. 'Decreto estableciendo una direccion general de rentas... con escepcion de la Admon. gral. de correos y la direccion de la Casa de moneda' [= central revenue directorate over all federal fiscal branches].

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 178

Gomez Pedraza issues regulations under the general Congress law of 23 November 1826 on the method by which mules assigned to army corps were to be used for transporting baggage. Savage: 'G. Pedraza - Reglamenta la Ley del Cong. Gral. de 23 de Nobre. de 1826, relativa al metodo con que debian de usarse las mulas designadas a los Cuerpos de ejercito pª el trasporte de los equipages.' Longest item in the run and the only 1827-dated one.

PROCLAMATION on the expulsion of the foreigners — the 1840 GRAHAM AFFAIR. Denounces 'una sórdida y venal facción… de algunos ingratos y extrangeros,' suppressed by the Guarnición; a *causa* was formed and, no Tribunal Superior being established, remitted to the Corte Suprema; fifteen foreigners proceeded against; invokes the Governor's power under Art. 12 of the Ley de 1º de Mayo de 1828. Signed Juan Bautista Alvarado → / Graham-Affair dossier

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 35

[otter tariff ends, tomo 207–210] + ⭐⭐ 'no dar hachas ú á los Indios' — don't talk to the Gentiles/rancherías; give the recruited Indians no 'hachas ni otro género de herramientas ó armas'; ⭐ the Indians & their 'capitancillo' who beat the india to be admonished (punished if repeated); Valerio Alas held a further month; Alegre's woman kept in shackles 20 more days

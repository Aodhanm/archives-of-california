HIGH VALUE. Congress secularizes the missions of the Republic. Savage, in full: 'Decreto del Cong.o Gral. secularizando las Misiones de la Republica.' This is the national secularization law of 17 [16 as dated here] April 1834, the enabling instrument behind California mission secularization; Savage gives it a single line and one original page (603).

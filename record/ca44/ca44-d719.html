(a) '1820. En.o 9. Mont. / Sola al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José Sargento Luis Peralta', p. 538

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 719

(a) '1820. En.o 9. Mont. / Sola al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José Sargento Luis Peralta', p. 538: not conforming with the re-election of Alcalde and Regidores, Sola has resolved that for the present year the Alcalde be Teodosio Flores and the Regidores Joaquín Higuera and Felipe Briones - a governor overriding a pueblo election. (b) 'Enero 11 = Nomb.to de Alcalde y Reg.s', which opens here and closes on n118: confirms the appointments, and reports that some terror may have been given the Mission Indians against continuing the robbery of caballada and ganado by the apprehension of five of those Christians, whom together with others held at this Presidio he has despatched to San Diego for punishment.

sow in the Llano (not harming the vecinos' herds)

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 16

sow in the Llano (not harming the vecinos' herds); ⭐ gambling — the bad conduct of Joaquín Valerio & Ávila, find the other 'ladroncillo,' take cabo Soto to witness any game; don't slaughter breeding animals; the regidores to finish the Casa [town hall] 'y que no esperen otra reconvención'

pueblo admin: curb waste (tithe + the pobladores 'se verán… pereciendo')

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 12

pueblo admin: curb waste (tithe + the pobladores 'se verán… pereciendo'); only surplus maize to the presidio; identify the thieves; are the Guardia & Ronda doing their duty; Castro may not sell or slaughter breeding cattle ('defrauda al Diezmo'); watch the chapel; tithe cattle to be sold (always for maize)

General Electoral Law issued by Santa Anna as interim president

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 439

General Electoral Law issued by Santa Anna as interim president: primary or compromisario elections and secondary elections; election of deputies and of the Departmental Assemblies; election of senators and of the President of the Republic; general provisions and the installation of Congress. 'Ley General para las Elecciones primarias ó de Compromisarios, y secundarias; elecciones de Diputados y Asambleas Departamentales; Eleccion de Senadores y Presid.te de la República' [= the electoral statute of the Bases Orgánicas regime, the rules under which California's departmental assembly and deputy elections were run].

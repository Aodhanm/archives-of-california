Decree on a state vice-governorship: 'Decreto del Congreso gral. declarando conforme en el art. 161 de la Constitucion fed.l el de la Legislatura del Estado de Queretaro q. mando sustituir al C. Gloss: Congress declares the Queretaro legislature's act replacing citizen Lino[?] Ramirez in the exercise of vice-governor to be conformable with art. 161 of the federal Constitution.

Two school documents of 1818. (a) 'Fbro. 17. = Sobre sorteo p.a el Servicio', pp. 588. 589

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 729

Two school documents of 1818. (a) 'Fbro. 17. = Sobre sorteo p.a el Servicio', pp. 588. 589: Tte grad.o D. Gabriel Moraga goes to the pueblo to proceed to the sorteo of the young men fit for the King's service; and all the children of that pueblo must attend school under the maestro named for their education and teaching, Antonino Buelna, and in his illness or absence Carlos Castro. (b) '1818. Fbro. 26. = Sobre la escuela y M.tro', pp. 590. 591: the maestro Antonino Buelna has presented that the fanega of wheat or maize assigned from each vecino for his annual work is not enough for his maintenance and that the house where he lives has no capacity for the children, so the vecindario must proceed without loss of time to form, adjoining that same house, a room capable and comfortable for all; and Sola will remit eighteen cartillas already being made.

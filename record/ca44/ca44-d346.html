Notice of appointment with signature: 'Jose M.a Tornel de la Sria. de Guerra y Marina

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 346

Notice of appointment with signature: 'Jose M.a Tornel de la Sria. de Guerra y Marina; comunica el nombram.to dando a reconoser su firma del Gral. de Division D. Miguel Barragan para Sria. de Estado y del Despacho universal de guerra y marina.' Gloss: Tornel announces the appointment of Division General Miguel Barragan as Secretary of State and of the universal Despacho of War and Navy, giving his signature to be recognised.

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 738

(a) Close of the preceding letter, pp. 633. 634, ending '...balidos de esta Comp.a q.e ecisten en ese pueblo.' (b) '1818. Marzo 12. Mision de la Soledad / Sola. al Com.do del pueblo de S. José', pp. 635. 636: he has received the list of those who pay the schoolmaster 'á razon de 4 y 3 p.s anuales', notes the cession the vecino Fran.co M.a García has made of his house to put the school in, and adds verbatim 'Es muy landable la caridad q.e los R. P.s M.tros. de S.ta Clara egercen con los ancianos é inhabiles de ese pueblo.' (c) '1817. Abril 14 Mont.', p. 637: Andrés Escamilla goes to establish himself in that pueblo and is to be given the land that belongs to him to cultivate. (d) '1817. Ag.to 13. San Diego.' opens, closing on n142.

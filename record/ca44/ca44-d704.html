Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 704

(a) 'Sobre juegos prohibidos', pp. 449. 450. (b) Apr 13, pp. 451. 452: José Abila to San Francisco; Rafael Gomez suspected in the almacén robbery. (c) May 3, p. 453: report how many Indians work the hemp, so that cotones y fresadas may be given them. (d) May 13, pp. 454. 455: twelve cotones and twelve fresadas for the hemp Indians. (e) Jun 3, marked '(2 Dctos)', p. 456: Albiso against Acebes over a killed horse.

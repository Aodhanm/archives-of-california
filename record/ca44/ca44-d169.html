General Congress declares Veracruz state decree no. 138 contrary to art

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 169

General Congress declares Veracruz state decree no. 138 contrary to art. 158 of the Federal Constitution, and orders the body installed with the individuals named in the final junta of 5 October last.

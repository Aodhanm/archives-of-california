(a) Close of the Apr 3 order: the chapel, the acequia and Branciforte. (b) Apr 8, pp. 445. 446, the strongest item in this gap: 'las tierras q.e poseen los gentiles deben quedarles intactas y no deben ser molestados ni en sus siembras ni ganados' [the lands the Gentiles hold must be left to them intact and they are not to be molested in their sowings or their stock].

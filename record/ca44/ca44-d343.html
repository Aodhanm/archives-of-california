Spans n250-n251. Suppression of the army medical corps

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 343

Spans n250-n251. Suppression of the army medical corps: 'Decreto estinguiendo el cuerpo de Sanidad militar, creado por el Decreto de 30 de Nob. de 1829, por no haber correspondido a los objetos de su organizacion.' Gloss: the military health corps created by the decree of 30 Nov. 1829 is extinguished for having failed to answer the purposes of its organisation. Heading on n250, abstract completed at head of n251. Second document dated 11 Nov. 1833 in this run.

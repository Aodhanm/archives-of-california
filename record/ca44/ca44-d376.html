Congress declares vacant the posts of General of Division held by three Spaniards. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o gral. - declarando vacantes los empleos de Generales de Division que obtenian los espanoles D. Pedro Celestino Negrete, D. Jose Ant.o Echavarri, D. Melchor Alvarez.' Named officers: Pedro Celestino Negrete, Jose Antonio Echavarri, Melchor Alvarez.

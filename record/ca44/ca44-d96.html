One-line order for timber: 'Que remita las 12 trozas de Roble' [= that he forward the 12 oak logs]

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 96

One-line order for timber: 'Que remita las 12 trozas de Roble' [= that he forward the 12 oak logs]. Savage gives no further detail; the logs are presumably for presidio or bridge construction, but he does not say.

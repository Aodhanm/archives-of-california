Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 716

(a) Jun 18, pp. 518. 519: Felipe García to build his house in the cuadro de la plaza, and until that square is entirely formed no house may be built outside it - a direct statement of the pueblo's building line. (b) 'Junio 27 = Sobre Zanja y cercas' (opens here, closes on n114): the delivery of goods assigned for Tiburcio Vazquez's granddaughter is concluded; before the vecindario goes out to the matanza of ganado mayor and Ciervos it must first finish the zanja and the cercas; M.a Gertrudis Higuera reports that several vecinos owe her more than six hundred pesos advanced on credit to be paid in sebos, and Sola charges that this poor woman, burdened with children and grandchildren, be paid by her debtors; and José Ant.o Robles is ordered to move with his family to live in the pueblo.

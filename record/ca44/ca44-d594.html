Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 594

Political expulsion. Verbatim: 'Que Gob.o hara salir de la Republica a puntos q.e estime convenientes por termino q.e no pase de seis años, a complices de Conspiracion de Montaño.' Gloss: government to expel from the Republic, to such places as it thinks fit and for a term not exceeding six years, the accomplices in the Montaño conspiracy [= the 1827 Plan de Montaño / Otumba rising].

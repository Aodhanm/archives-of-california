Decree requiring the state Legislatures that had not by the publication of this measure passed the law…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 276

Decree requiring the state Legislatures that had not by the publication of this measure passed the law regulating the esclusiva spoken of in art. 3 of the law of 16 May last, to pass it within the following 60 days; those failing would be held to renounce the faculty, and vacant prebends in the Cathedral churches would be filled. '...se entenderia q.e renunciaban á hacer uso de dtha. facultad, y se proveeria de las piezas vacantes en las Yglesias Catedrales' [= church patronage, presentation to cathedral stalls].

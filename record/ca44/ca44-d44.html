Borica's economic exhortation to San José — the pueblo consumes ~600 fanegas maize, 130 frijol, 180 arrobas flour/yr (excl. the ships' demand); foster the 'fértil terreno,' their produce bought first 'á dinero de contado'; take up tanning, saddle-making, boots & shoes; 'los quiere buenos Cristianos y trabajadores' (idlers to be corrected)

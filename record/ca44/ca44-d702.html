Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 702

Run of 1799 orders. (a) p. 440: twelve individuals 'de costumbres relajadas' are to sleep at the cuerpo de guardia. (b) Feb 25, p. 441: on the robbery of over four hundred pesos from the presidio almacén, with a charge to watch the veteran troops. (c) Mzo 28, p. 442: the soldier Mendoza alias Choquet. (d) 'Abril 3. = Sobre la acequia, siembras, Y[ndio]s y Capilla' (continues on n94). Preceding lines on the leaf close an entry cited pp. 438. 439.

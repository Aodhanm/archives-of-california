(a) Close of Jan 11, pp. 539. 540: from the pueblo and the Ranchos of Ygn.o Ortega and Mariano Castro…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 720

(a) Close of Jan 11, pp. 539. 540: from the pueblo and the Ranchos of Ygn.o Ortega and Mariano Castro, four or six armed men are to go out weekly 'p.a el escarmiento y esterminio' of the damage the raiders do; the padrón general of the vecindario is in Sola's hands, with one of goods and harvests and the list of pósito stock; he wants to know the school's progress, and that the comisionado dedicate himself so that the moral and political customs of the pueblo become known. (b) '1820 Fbro. 2. San Carlos. / Sola al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José', p. 541: some vecinos hold suertes de tierra and do not work them but rent them out and live in idleness; Briones asks for a paraje to sow maize, and the comisionado is to advise whether it could be given without prejudice to the común of the vecindario.

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 700

(a) 'Octbre 31 = Sobre el cañamo': the sample of hemp beneficiado by cabo Mateo Bello, examined by the 'oficiales de Guerra de Marina y Pilotos', was found 'pasado y de poco servicio', and compared with the sample sent the previous year 'se halló este mucho mejor q.e aquel'; henceforth the hemp is to be worked as it was the year before, and cabo Bello is to move to his destination. (b) Nov 3, marked by Savage '(2 Dctos)': Bello presented himself at the presidio yesterday and leaves today with a sergeant and four men for his post; and before the rains he is to try to beneficiar the hemp cut this year and thereafter sow all the seed there may be.

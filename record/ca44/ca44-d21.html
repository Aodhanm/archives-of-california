the RELOCATION of Pueblo San José to its new site

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 21

the RELOCATION of Pueblo San José to its new site — 'en la primavera se cambiará el pueblo' (José Argüello to report); Pedro Bojórquez given a solar & lands; 'empezarán las obras en el nuevo sitio'; all must be moved 'al otro sitio' by March; gratifications to the pobladores for building the Guardia, Iglesia y revellines at the new site

Tte de Artillería Manuel Gómez — before Alcalde José Castro

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 89

Tte de Artillería Manuel Gómez — before Alcalde José Castro — summons Tomás Patrón & wife + Luis Chaboya for *amonestación*: Patrón 2 months prison at the SF presidio, wife 1 month at the mission; ⭐ Diezmo audit — 47 contributors gave 100½ fanegas maize, 3 frijol, 66 cattle, but 'nothing in horses'; grant Mariano Castro his late father Pedro's lands

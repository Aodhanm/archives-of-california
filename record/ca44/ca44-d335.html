Spans n247-n248. Arms-collection decree: 'Decreto autorizando al Gob.o para recojer las armas de los particulares en el dist.o y Territorios previo la indemnizacion de $5. u.f por cada fusil.' Gloss: the government is authorised to collect private persons' arms in the Federal District and the Territories, paying an indemnity of five pesos in cash per musket. Heading on n247, abstract completed at head of n248.

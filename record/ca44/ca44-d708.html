Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 708

(a) Jul 24, pp. 493. 494: 'Benancio Mogica' has wounded the pueblo's first Regidor Juan Hernandez, and Capitán D. José Ant.o Navarrete leaves today to conduct the prisoner to the presidio. (b) Jul 29, pp. 495. 496: the Milicianos de Artillería resident in the pueblo are to alternate with the vecinos in service, and the pueblo's judges are to see that the Milicianos fulfil the duties belonging to every citizen. The upper part of the leaf is the tail of the Jul 6 tallow letter already catalogued (see interiors).

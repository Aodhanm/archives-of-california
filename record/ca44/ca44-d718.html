MAJOR for rancho history. (a) 'Sbre. 1º = Sobre el parage los Tularcitos', pp. 527. 528

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 718

MAJOR for rancho history. (a) 'Sbre. 1º = Sobre el parage los Tularcitos', pp. 527. 528: the paraje called los Tularcitos was understood not to lie in the pueblo's jurisdiction, and the vecindario is to state whether the donation of it to the vecino José Higuera prejudices them, the Alcalde, Regidores and vecinos to speak through the Alcalde; the same order adds that if the Militia-of-Artillery deserter Isidro Rosas is in the pueblo he is to be secured and sent to the presidio. (b) Ottbre 4, p. 529: the invalid soldier José Higuera is to be put in possession of the land he solicits; and Capt. D. Luis Argüello is to go out on a reconocimiento of lands, the escoltas of Sta. Clara and San José to join him at the presidio when he gives notice. (c) 'Ottbre. 30' opens, running to n116: 140 fanegas of Diezmo wheat collected, 40 to be milled into flour.

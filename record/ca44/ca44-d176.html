Blanket desertion amnesty for all soldiers who deserted between 28 September 1821 and 31 July of the…

Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 176

Blanket desertion amnesty for all soldiers who deserted between 28 September 1821 and 31 July of the current year, and for officers married without licence, provided they present themselves to the comandantes generales within one month of publication. Savage: 'Decreto indultando del delito de desercion a todos los soldados que lo hubiesen cometido del 28 de Set. de 1821, hasta el 31 de Julio del pste. año, y a los oficiales q. se hallan casados sin licencia...' [= reached every comandancia incl. Alta California]

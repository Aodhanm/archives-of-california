Departmental State Papers, San José, tomo I–III · BANC MSS C-A 44, document 593

Port opening; spans n348-n349. Verbatim (heading on n348, tail on n349): 'Se habilita p.a el Comercio de cabotage / los Ptos de Tecolutla y Santecomapan - Estado de Veracruz.' Gloss: the ports of Tecolutla and Santecomapan, state of Veracruz, opened to the coasting trade. Savage repeats the heading in parentheses beneath the tail on n349 as a signature recapitulation.

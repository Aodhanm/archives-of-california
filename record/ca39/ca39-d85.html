'Que por última vez se ha permitido á Don Alejandro, Gobernador de los establecim.tos Ross y Bodega pa

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 85

'Que por última vez se ha permitido á Don Alejandro, Gobernador de los establecim.tos Ross y Bodega pa. que pueda entrar su buque á ese puerto bajo la condicion de no traer ningun comercio.' [= for the last time Don Alejandro, governor of the Ross and Bodega establishments, has been permitted to bring his vessel into San Francisco, on condition that it carry no trade goods]. Hardening of the Monterey customhouse line on Russian shipping only eight days after the more permissive order of 14 May. Spans n227-n228; the sentence completes on n228, fetched outside the assigned range solely to confirm the span.

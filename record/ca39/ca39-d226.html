Verbatim heading: '+ 1839 Julio 9 Sta Bárb.a / Raymundo Carrillo, Sub-Prefecto del 2.o distrito - Causa criminal contra Indio Juan Pablo, acusado de sedicion.' Charges by the Sta Bárbara mission administrator (interlined: Manuel Cota) against the neophyte for conato de sedición; Fr. Durán's declaration showed Cota's maltreatment; the Indians only meant to petition Visitador Gen. Guill.o E. Hartnell for Cota's removal 'y no hubo conato de sedicion'; end unknown. A 'sedition' that dissolves into a grievance petition on investigation. Recovered 2026-09-02.

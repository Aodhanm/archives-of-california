Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 224

Verbatim heading, at the foot of n233: '1839 - Dbre 31 - Mont. / Adm.or de Aduana al Comisario.' Gloss: the Administrator of Customs to the Comisario. Body runs onto n234 and reads verbatim: 'Que del importe de los derechos de la Goleta Americana 'Morse' ha tomado la cantidad necesaria p.a cubrir los gastos de la Aduana, y q.e el resto de $2609 - 8 g.r los pone a su disposicion y los entregara en plata el Capitan de d.ha Goleta.' That is: from the duties of the American schooner Morse he has taken the sum needed to cover customhouse expenses, and places the remainder of 2609 pesos 8 granos at the Comisario disposal, to be paid in silver by the captain of that schooner. Heading falls inside the n232-n233 gap, body on the covered leaf n234.

Verbatim heading: '1839 - Agosto 21 - Monterey / Adm.or al Sobrecargo del 'Corsario''. Gloss: the Administrator of Customs to the supercargo of the Corsario. Substance verbatim: 'Que el resto del importe de derechos, lo entregue al Comisario.' That is: he is to deliver the balance of the duties to the Comisario. The executing order paired with the preceding entry. NOTE a page-cite collision: an already-published ca39 row (scan n231) also carries orig_page p.7 but its summary is the Dbre 31 1839 cabotage circular, whose own cite Savage writes as pp. 10-11 on leaf n233. The p.7 on the published n231 row appears to be an error; the p.7 recorded here is the cite Savage actually wrote beside this entry.

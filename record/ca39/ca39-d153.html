A Los Angeles political-disturbance causa (the flag affair)

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 153

A Los Angeles political-disturbance causa (the flag affair) — a bandera raised at [Abel] Stearns's house; witnesses/accused Guillermo (William) Quinto[?], Sérulo/Cérulo Varelas, Antonio López; Alcalde Domínguez took declarations; referred to Alcalde 1º Tiburcio Tapia (context = the mid-1830s LA political disturbances) [Leaf-verified 2026-09-02: the bandera was the PREFECTURA's flag kept at Abel Stearns's house (Peña had no fixed prefecture office); the motín's object was to remove it from a foreigner's house; Stearns himself ordered the res tied to the flagpole (n444); Tapia as Prefecto int.o received the expediente at the end.]

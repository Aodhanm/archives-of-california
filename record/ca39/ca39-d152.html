Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 152

José Salazar's Indian expedition & skirmish — a partida de indios gentiles at a rancho disarmed (20 arcos, 20 carcajes); Salazar ordered to conduct the Indians to the capital; they resisted with mazas & cuchillos, came upon the 12-man force; forced to fire in self-defense — 18 indios killed, 1 taken prisoner, 2 wounded on Salazar's side ('uno se fué bueno y 2 heridos') [Leaf-verified 2026-09-02 (Salazar's letter n433 + Severiano declaration n435-6): 18 indios gentiles dead on the field, 1 escaped unhurt, 2 escaped WOUNDED (Indians), 7 hurt on Salazar's side, NOBODY taken prisoner (18+2+1 = all 21); weapons 'macetas, cuchillos y piedras' / 'piedras y macanas de madera de mezquite'; bodies 9 south + 9 west ~700 varas from the house, 'muertos de balas, bayonetas y armas cortantes', burned/buried. Older '1 prisoner / 2 wounded on Salazar's side' figures were wrong.]

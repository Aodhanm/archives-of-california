OUTSIDE THE ASSIGNED GAP LIST, see issues. Verbatim heading: '+ Noviembre 30 pp.77-78'. Substance: that in this oficina he has named Sub-Comisario interino at this point Don José M.a Abrego, to take charge of the distribucion de los caudales públicos entering it, with a commission of two per cent not exceeding 1,000 pesos a year, and that he give fianza of 1,000 pesos.

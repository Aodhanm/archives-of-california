Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 81

'Que no existiendo papel sellado en las poblaciones, procederá á imprimir los sellos qe. acompaña.' [= there being no stamped paper in the towns, he is to proceed to print the seals sent herewith]. First entry under the new section head 'Borrador de Oficios' (p. 1). Notable for styling José de la Rosa as government printer at Sonoma in Jan. 1839.

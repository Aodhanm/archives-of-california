OUTSIDE THE ASSIGNED GAP LIST, see issues. Verbatim heading: '+ Enero 24 pp.73-74' (year 1839 carried from the running heading on the preceding leaf; the entry itself reads only 'Enero 24'). Substance: that the Capitan Don Andrés Castillero, named Comisionado del Gob.o Nacional, has shown him in the original the ordenes y comunicaciones that must operate in ending 'las convulsiones políticas del país', and that the undersigned is interinamente in charge of the gobierno of this Departamento.

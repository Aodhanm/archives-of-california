Verbatim heading: '+ 1841. En.o 18 San Juan de Castro / José Tiburcio Castro, Prefecto del 1.o Dist.o - Causa de incesto.' The Hernández-Romero concubinage affair: Francisco Hernández petitions for the return of his sister M.a Concepción; Branciforte Juez de Paz letters; Gob. Alvarado orders diligencias (9 Jan 1841); Juez Juan Miguel Anza takes declarations (18 Jan): Juana Hernández (wife of Juan Romero) accuses Concepción of concubinage with Juan Romero and brothers-in-law (also Agustín, Claudio Vásquez, Juan Salazar); Concepción confesses only the Romero part; no end appears — Anza passed it to the Prefect for the Governor. Recovered 2026-09-02 (the old d149 row's Castro/Anza fields came from THIS causa).

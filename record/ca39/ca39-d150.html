A debt-execution / vaquillas case against a Sepúlveda

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo II (Miscellaneous) · BANC MSS C-A 39, document 150

A debt-execution / vaquillas case against a Sepúlveda — the Juez decreed payment of vaquillas/caballos/costas within 15 days on pain of ejecución sobre bienes; Sepúlveda pleaded the bienes were his father's ('él es hijo de familia'); the fiador José Mª Aguilar cited ('por haber sido fiador de un sujeto inhábil para contratos'); appeals through the Prefect; Juan Gallardo appealed on Sepúlveda's behalf [Frame added 2026-09-02, leaf-verified: a horse-race wager — 100 vaquillas + el caballo cuatralbo — that Fernando Sepúlveda lost to Andrés Pico and refused to pay; Juez Felipe Lugo; Bandini judged the race; restitution of 130 reses ('100 vaquillas, 16 vacas, y 14 novillos', n421).]

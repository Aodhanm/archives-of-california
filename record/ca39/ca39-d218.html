Verbatim heading: '1836 Oct. 4 - Pueblo de San José. / José Mª Albisu, Juez Constitucional.' English gloss: Jose Maria Albisu, constitutional judge, Pueblo de San Jose, 4 October 1836. Substance, Savage verbatim: 'Dice al Gefe Político que está ocupado en recoger las semillas que adeudan algs. por diezmos.' That is: he tells the political chief that he is occupied in collecting the grain that some parties owe in tithes. Note the spelling is Albisu on the leaf, not Albiso.

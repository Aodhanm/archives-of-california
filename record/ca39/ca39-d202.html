Verbatim heading: '+ 1843 - Enero 10. Angeles / Man.l Micheltorrena á José M.a Castañares.' Substance: that he accepts his generosa oferta, which will enable him to maintain the tropas and conserve el órden; that he has ordered an empréstito until funds arrive from Méjico, or the arrival of some buques.

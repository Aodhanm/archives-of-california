Verbatim heading: '+ 1845 - Junio 15 - Angeles / Gob.or Pío Pico al Adm.or de Aduana, Monterey'. Substance: that from the derechos caused by the Barca Joven Guipurevana [so spelled by Savage] he discount to D.n José Ant.o Aguirre the sum of 336 pesos, for an equal sum owed to that señor for a suplemento made to la lista civil.

Departmental State Papers (Juzgados / Naturalization), tomo XVII–XX · BANC MSS C-A 33, document 158

(cont. of Doc 157) the 14-article hospital reglamento fully abstracted (arts. 3–14): admit all indigents not in another's service + those who pay; junta members rotate daily vigilance; sick sailors of viruela [smallpox] treated ashore; foreign captains pay $20 for their crews' care, Mexicans pay via captain; the commission to raise the contribution = Rafael González, David Spence, Tomás O. Larkin; a medical-method commission = Mariano Silva, Dionisio González, Rafael González Frost

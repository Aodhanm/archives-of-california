Departmental State Papers (Juzgados / Naturalization), tomo XVII–XX · BANC MSS C-A 33, document 61

Death of Anthony Campbell (English-language original, verbatim): certifies that said Campbell, at 2 a.m. of Aug 23, 'was killed by an arrow which went thro' his heart' at the place of Mr. Thomas Brown; Burton heard the dogs bark, went out, returned, called his companions Campbell & Wm. Lewis — Campbell went to the door and called the dogs; it was then that the arrow struck him

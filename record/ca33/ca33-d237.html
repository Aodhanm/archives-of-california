Verbatim heading: '1842 Abl 13 Sta Barbª / Juez de paz Joaquin Carrillo al Srio de Gobno' with cite 'p.419-21'. (The month abbreviation is a superscripted contraction; I read it as Abl = Abril, matching the capital A form used in the Agtº heading lower on the same leaf, but the reading is not certain.) SUBSTANCE (verbatim): 'Que José Antº Dominguez no ha desocupado Salsipuedes porque no se le ha indemnizado el avalúo de su propiedad en dho parage y que monta á 901 p 6 rs.' GLOSS: José Antonio Dominguez has not vacated Salsipuedes because he has not been indemnified the appraisal of his property at that paraje, amounting to 901 pesos 6 reales.

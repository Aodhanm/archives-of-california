The Real Pragmática de Matrimonios applied to the military in California (R.O. 10 Jul 1781, Madrid)

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 25

The Real Pragmática de Matrimonios applied to the military in California (R.O. 10 Jul 1781, Madrid): first-instance jurisdiction (ordinary Audiencia vs. military) for the *disenso*, parental consent when distant (Pragmática arts. 5–7 for the Indies), & the crown's interest in facilitating soldiers marrying & settling — 'sumamente Conveniente para el bien del Estado'

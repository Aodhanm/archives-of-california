The 1785 San Diego presidio artillery inventory (Zúñiga + Alférez Velásquez), presidio + 3 missions (SD/San Juan Capistrano/San Gabriel): Artillery = 4 cannon (cal. 4 & 2) — 2 at the presidio, 2 on the SD coast, 1 at San Gabriel; 4 gun-carriages; cannon cartridges + grape (24 balls cal.4, 43 cal.2); 35 pairs French pistols; 1,380 fusil-cartridges; ~1,268 flints; powder/lead → artillery register

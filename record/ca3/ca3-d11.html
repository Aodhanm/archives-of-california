BOUNDED CLUSTER — the 1783 Los Ángeles pobladores muster-extracts (one *revista* per month)

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 11

BOUNDED CLUSTER — the 1783 Los Ángeles pobladores muster-extracts (one *revista* per month): the 9 founding LA settlers — Félix Villavicencio, José Vanegas, Pablo Rodríguez, Manuel Camero, Antonio Navarro, José Moreno, Basilio Rosas, Alejandro Rosas, Antonio Miranda (last paid at Loreto) — each w/ pay & ración; margin-notes on pay-cessations (Rodríguez 13 Aug, Camero 11 Aug, Moreno 2 Sep, A. Rosas & Miranda 7 Nov 1783; entry-dates back to 1780, w/ *libro de Población* folios). Pairs w/ the San José extracts (Docs 6–8) = an early settler census of both pueblos

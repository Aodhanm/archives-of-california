Private letter of advice to Gov. Fages's wife (*the Callís marital scandal, 1785*): urging her to restrain her desires, moderate her dealings with the friars (*Ministros*), suffer with patience 'lo que hicieron en la Iglesia'; notes Fages showed her the proceedings sent to the Bishop, which she refused to read

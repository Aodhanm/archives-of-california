Livestock census of the Santa Bárbara individuals (privately-held stock by year 1782–86

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 446

Livestock census of the Santa Bárbara individuals (privately-held stock by year 1782–86: cattle/swine/goats/horses/mules w/ births & deaths) + a named roster of the whole SB company by class — Tte Goycoechea, Alf. José Argüello, Sgt. Pablo Cota, retired José Ochoa, Cabo Pedro Saavedra, the soldiers (Peña, Vega, Gonzáles, Lugo, Aceves, Rubio de Sol, Lobo, Ortega, Salazar, Cuevas, Valero, Lara, Velasco, Chávez, Soto, Samaniego, Rodríguez…) + pobladores (Villavicencio, Rosas…) + carpenter — a genealogical/prosopographical snapshot

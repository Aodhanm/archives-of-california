Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read savage_page: '163'. Read leaf n166 of 168036075_79_5_2, second document. Heading verbatim: '1785 Agosto 9 Sta Barbara / Felipe de Goicoechea á Pedro Fages. / Sobre vigilancia en la Conducta moral de los Indios'. Body: 'Dice: que queda impuesto por su oficio de 2 de Junio ultimo de celar con la mayor vigilancia de los individuos que se hallan bajo sus ordenes, quedando advertido al que contravenga que se le impondrá la mas rigorosa pena. Quedando advertidos los Sarjentos y Cabos de esta vigilancia, sin el menor disimulo, por que serán comprehendidos en la misma pena.' Foot-cite reads 'p. 334 = 335.' — so the out-of-sequence orig_page 'v.334–335' is Savage's own and is recorded correctly (not the transposition it looks like). savage_page WRONG: the printed stamp at the head of n166 is 164 (pencil 163 above it, verified on a magnified crop); the record's 163 is the pencil foliation, contrary to this volume's own convention — n87 is catalogued 88 (stamp) not 87, n120 is catalogued 121 (stamp) not 120, and the very next leaf n167 (stamp 165 / pencil 164) is catalogued 165. The defect also affects d193–d195. place PARTIAL: 'Sta Barbara' is on the leaf but recorded only as '(SB)' inside the author field. Omissions: n166 carries two documents (d195, d196), both catalogued.

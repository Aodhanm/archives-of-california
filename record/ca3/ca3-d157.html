The wreck of the Macao ship 'San Francisco de Paula alias el Hércules' at the Bahía de San José del Cabo (3 Nov 1784): from Macao (East India) under the 'Conde de San Donás,' bound Macao→Callao (Lima), ran aground ('varase contra la costa á causa de no haber agarrado bien la ancla'; broken rudder, 14 in. water/hr, small crew); given Spanish aid (*socorro*) by the Juez del Partido, on the express condition of returning the men to the Peninsula; the count then armed it as a corsair for the Lima merchants, sailed the Philippine coasts → E2

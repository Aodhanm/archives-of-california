Formal mission-siting report (companion to Doc 243): Ortega, Fr

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 244

Formal mission-siting report (companion to Doc 243): Ortega, Fr. *Luis de Sales* & 4 soldiers surveyed la Grulla vs. San Francisco Solano (11 Apr) for a new mission — la Grulla rejected (only hot/salty water, salitrous land, timber 'imposible'); San Francisco Solano favored (two springs, medium-quality arable land, easy irrigation, timber; ~1-day roads San Vicente→site→Encino) and would 'abrasar á todo el Gentilismo del distrito'; asks Fages for a garrison; found a shorter San Vicente→Solano road; certified by Fages, San Gabriel 11 May 1785

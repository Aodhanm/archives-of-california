A friar withdraws pastoral care from the Santa Bárbara presidio in reprisal for the denied escort

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 335

A friar withdraws pastoral care from the Santa Bárbara presidio in reprisal for the denied escort: since the presidio 'no es de nuestra jurisdicción' and what the friars did for it 'ha sido por pura caridad,' henceforth the garrison may be deprived of Mass & the sacraments — a rare, sharp church-vs-military rupture

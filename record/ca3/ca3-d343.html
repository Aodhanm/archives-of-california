The eve of Mission Santa Bárbara's founding: awaiting the P. Presidente & P. Paterna (only Fr. Cristóbal present); the padres may wish to begin the founding before the rains, with the aid furnished by José Esteban Martínez; will fill vacant plazas w/ men of 'ambas Repúblicas' & collect the tithes (*diezmos*); a shortfall in Capt. Tovar's delivery (1 carga + 4 of soap)

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 422

Relays the R.O. (via Conde de Gálvez / Marqués de Sonora, 17 Oct) on imperial Indian-war policy for the Provincias Internas: the King, informed of the *indios bárbaros*' hostilities/killings/robberies (per Rengel's reports 30 Apr & 28 May), authorizes measures to 'escarmentar' the enemy Indians & the new Cmdt-Gen Ugarte y Loyola — with the command-reorganization (interim governor/intendancy) *(Provincias-Internas-wide, not CA-specific)*

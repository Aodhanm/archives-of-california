Various: congratulates Fages (Inspector); excuses not collecting otter pelts

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 477

Various: congratulates Fages (Inspector); excuses not collecting otter pelts — lack of cloth/goods to barter; ⭐ spoke to Padre Gardiaga about 'el encargo de Doña *Eulalia Callís*' (Fages's wife), which the padre had 'avoided,' & presumes P. Sales would too (ties E5); the SD+Rosario escort to El Encino w/ P. Sales; hunting the medicinal herb 'Lengua de Buey' (found only in southern Baja)

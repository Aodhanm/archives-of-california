Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 237

Audit of the accounts of the late Capt. *Fernando de Rivera y Moncada* (killed in the 1781 Yuma/Quechan massacre) — the fiscal settlement of a dead officer's estate: the $835 (also cited $1,730) Comisario Álvarez de Osío had advanced him, ordered discounted from his back-salary; the Tribunal (dir. Saviñón, 17–30 Sept 1784; contadores Abad/Mejía/Guerrero/Fagle; 'Méjico y Octubre 22 de 1784') requires all Baja presidios to remit full supporting *órdenes/documentos* with their accounts; Fages orders compliance at Mission San Gabriel 4 May 1785 via Arrillaga (Loreto) → habilitado Estrada. ⭐ Estrada's reply confirms Rivera y Moncada died 17 Jul 1781 'á manos de los gentiles' and reckons a 650 p 7 r balance in the dead captain's favor (Gov. Neve having responded 1 Jul 1782); certified 'pp. 424 á 429' Loreto 16 Jun 1785

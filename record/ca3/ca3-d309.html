On the shortage of recruits to fill vacant presidio *plazas*

Provincial State Papers, tomo V–VI · BANC MSS C-A 3, document 309

On the shortage of recruits to fill vacant presidio *plazas*: the lack of mounts (*bestias*) to move certain Loreto-company men is the obstacle; proposes recruits volunteer to go north & trade their beasts for horses — a presidio-manpower/logistics scheme

(sin fecha) 'Sobre el assassinato de un indio'

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 836

(sin fecha) 'Sobre el assassinato de un indio' — in the rancheria next to San Buenaventura appeared the gardener Márcos strangled/throat-cut ('degollado'); declarations taken from the hortelano who accompanied him & from Márcos's wife; remit the sumaria when ready for sentence, sent to Viceroy; great care with the accused.

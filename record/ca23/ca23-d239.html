Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 239

'Envío de un buque' — requests a ship to bring grain from the Sonora coast, not having received remesas from México nor from that department (San Blas); only the Sta Lucía available; warns Cap. Joaquín Cañete of that Presº that when Province ships need repair or crew, apply directly to him (Trillo).

  • Previously read savage_page: '118/119'. Leaf n119 (168036074_80_5_2) header: "+ 1783 - Julio 3 - Loreto. / Fages á Fran.co Trillo Bermudes. Envio de un buque." RECIPIENT PARTIAL — catalogue records "Franco Trillo", dropping the second surname "Bermudes" written on the leaf. SAVAGE_PAGE WRONG — n119's top right bears stamped 120 (the continuously running Savage volume page) and handwritten 118 (the per-tomo leaf number); the catalogue records "118/119", i.e. the per-tomo handwritten number of this leaf plus the next leaf's, not the Savage volume page. Under the catalogue's own dominant convention in this volume (61 at n60, 184 at n185, 234 at n235, 411 at n419, 414 at n422 — all stamped) "118" resolves to leaf n117. orig_page confirmed: abstract ends "pp. 233-4". Summary is faithful to "Solicita la remision de un buque, p.a la conducción de granos de la Costa de Sonora, p.r no haberse recibido las remesas de México, ni de aquel Departam.to (debe ser S.n Blas); p.r no haber lanchas propias p.a la conducción, sino la 'S.ta Lucia'..." — only embellishment is the rank "Cap." for Cañete, who the leaf calls "D.n Joaquin Cañete". Leaf also holds the tail of d238 ("pp. 222-3") and the head of d240 (Fages al Virrey); both are catalogued.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

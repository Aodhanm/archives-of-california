'Excusa de la tropa con las indias; su correccion'

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 1214

'Excusa de la tropa con las indias; su correccion' — full-text bando spanning n429-n430, on the repeated complaints of excesses company individuals commit with the Indian women, gentile & Christian: 'En lo referido se supone no haber habido violencia; porque en este caso se debe formar sumaria.' GLOSS: graduated penalties for illicit relations — sergeants/corporals 2 months' arrest (1st), stripped of ginetas & escuadras, demoted to last soldier (2nd); soldiers 1/2/4 months in irons at public works, then sumaria with due rigor; any NCO or detachment commander who consents or conceals such vice: sumaria; violence cases require a sumaria (rape treated separately); copy posted in all presidio & mission guard corps.

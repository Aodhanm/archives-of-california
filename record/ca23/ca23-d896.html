Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 896

(sin fecha) 'Sobre colonos y empeños' — convenient the son of Santiago Pico goes to the pueblo to keep & maintain his father's house, lands & goods; (cont. n345) convenient settlers increase their vineyards with plots of vines & cattle — that's the true basis of an establishment, strayed cattle not so useful; a good officer must be upright in justice, condescendence & piety to one side — not indulge a soldier viciously with 100 p. he can't repay.

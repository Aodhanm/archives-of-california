'Sentencia de los asesinos de Márcos': 'se limita la pena á cuatro años de prision y 50 azotes á cada uno, excepto á Bonifacio á quien se darán 100.' GLOSS: Viceroy, with the war-auditor, rules the gardener Márcos's murderers deserve death but — for the Indians' natural rusticity & lest capital punishment deter conversion to Catholicism — commutes to 4 yrs prison + lashes; execute it.

(sin fecha) 'Reconocimiento de una veta mineral'

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 884

(sin fecha) 'Reconocimiento de una veta mineral' — informed of the finding of a metallic vein that seems to be of silver ('de plata') in the woods at the point of the Zanja/Laguna; sent Ortega there with 8 men to make a formal recon; (cont. n339) if covered, take a load of the mineral in earth or stone, bring to presidio, examine & notify. Spans n338-n339.

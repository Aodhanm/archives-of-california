Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 819

Heading 'Prevenciones contra los extrangeros = Acuse del Gob = Donativo al Rey = Embarque de maderas.' VERBATIM: 'Que si llegase otro buque estrangero impida toda ida á bordo y no la consienta á nadie. Que es punto de delicadeza.' + 'le va de honor en este mundo, y el alma en el otro.' GLOSS: if another foreign ship arrives prevent all boarding — a point of delicacy; supposes no commerce with said Englishman (Roller), since San Blas men would reveal it plainly; glad there was no disorder at the ship's arrival; his honor at stake in this world & his soul in the next; the [military] grade not yet appeared, known only via Arrillaga; (cont. n309) timber embarked for San Diego, saved the King much.

BORICA'S DEPARTURE / handover of command: '...por Gob.[ernador] interino de la Provincia al teniente Coronel Don J. Joaq.[uín] de Arrillaga, y por Comand.[ante] de las Armas de la N.[uev]a Calif.[ornia] al de igual grado Don Pedro de Alberni'. GLOSS: departing the 12th-15th on the frigate Concepción for Mexico under the Viceroy's license; troops & Indians to recognize Arrillaga as interim Governor & Alberni as Commander of Arms. End of the letterbook proper.

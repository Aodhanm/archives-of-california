Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 240

'Pedido de un buque á Sn Blas' (spans n119–n120) — Comandᵗᵉ Genl provided 18 Octe '82 for ship-repair; occur to the Comandᵗᵉ de Sn Blas; hence asked a ship, the Balandra 'El Pilar' being useless, to bring grain from Sonora coast to the Province. (Survey row #133 reads the sloop's name as 'El Filer' — companion reads 'El Pilar'.)

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 434

'Haberse embarcado con su fam.a en Loreto; abortado su esposa en Mulegé donde demoró un mes y llegado el 6 á S.n Luis de donde se traslado á S.n Fern.do' — embarked with his family at Loreto; his wife [Eulalia de Callis] miscarried at Mulegé, where he delayed a month; reached San Luis the 6th, then moved to San Fernando.

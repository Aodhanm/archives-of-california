Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 789

Sowing measures — at LA locusts/mice ('ratas') ate much maize seed, ordered land fenced & irrigated; (cont. n294) maize & bean should be abundant; comisionado of missions to examine on transit how [Indians] work & live; no maize at San Gabriel & San Buenaventura, some at San Juan — bad crop year generally.

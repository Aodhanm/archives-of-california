'Sobre el establec.[imiento] de la escuela' — school establishment (heading n309, content n310)

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 822

'Sobre el establec.[imiento] de la escuela' — school establishment (heading n309, content n310): all soldiers & residents contribute to the schoolmaster's subsistence, 125-130 p. annual (married soldiers 1 p., unmarried 1/2 p., rest from residents); read at fiesta so pueblo & company see the Gov't's intent; teach Christian doctrine 'lo primero' & reading & writing; boys attend mornings, help parents afternoons; primers & paper at pupils'/parents' cost then via habilitado; then re pension.

'Relacion del estado del pais' — before this letter received answer of 21 Octe '84

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 105

'Relacion del estado del pais' — before this letter received answer of 21 Octe '84; King pleased with the Province's progress; accepts recommendation to keep serving; at start of '86 plans to form a circumstantial relation of the state of the country & send to Viceroy Gálvez; peninsula not subject to viceroyalty but will see good results of minister's good disposition; Escribano & pueblo of San [Blas?] extensive establishments, will send an exemplar.

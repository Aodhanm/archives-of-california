VERBATIM heading: '1.783 - Mayo 26 - Loreto. / Fages a Sold.o Pedro Amador.' | Substance, Savage verbatim: 'Que no ha creido conven.te acceder a la solicitud de retiro q.e le envio, pr q.e pasara a Sta Clara a ocupar la plaza de Cabo.' [That he has not thought it fitting to grant the retirement petition Amador sent him, because he is to go to Santa Clara to occupy the post of Cabo.]Person-layer value: fixes Pedro Amador in May 1783 as a soldado whose retirement was refused and who was posted to Santa Clara as cabo; the export already carries him as Sarg.to Amador by Feb 1789 (docs 1800-1801, C-A 23 n43).

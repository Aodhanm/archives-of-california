Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 916

'Aprendizage de Sastrería y Alfarería' — send García's sons to learn tailoring, since the armorer the Viceroy was to send hasn't come; recruit ('busque') youths 11-16; (cont. n354) youths 11-16 to learn armory; these & the first fed/clothed & maintained at Gov't cost; had 10,000 bricks (ladrillos) made for the kiln — after the potter finishes at Monterey he'll transfer. Spans n353-n354.

'Sobre el litigio de la Zanja' — resolution in the matter of Manuel Nieto's lands 'La Zanja' (Monterey, 14 May 1796): without prejudice to the right claimed in this expediente by the San Gabriel Mission fathers & Manuel Nieto; (cont. n343) so the disputed lands be cultivated in favor of the Indians & residents, Nieto keeps the ones he has cultivated & the rest reverts to San Gabriel Mission; pass a certified copy of this decree to the Comdte of Sta Bárbara to transfer to the fathers & to Nieto, who is charged to mark the measured lands to avoid differences.

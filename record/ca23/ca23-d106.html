Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 106

'Quejas contra P. Peña' — complains against P. Tomás de la Peña for refusing to send the maize he asked for supply to the Real de Sta Bárbara unless paid a higher price than the Arancel sets, on pretext the seed is scarce, & that from San Blas is inferior quality; disregarding the King's authority who sanctioned said arancel.

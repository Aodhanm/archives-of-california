'Llegada de la fragata ing.[lesa] 'Fénix' y disposiciones relativas' — FÉNIX FRIGATE full abstract (heading at n297 foot; abstract n298/n301). English merchant frigate Fénix, Capt. Roller[?], from Bengal, anchored for bad weather, firewood/provisions & crew health; lend aid per law of nations, then courteously tell her to leave; forbid all coastal exploration; demand account of all she takes; permit no one to converse with her; recall Arrillaga's orders vs selling livestock to foreigners. VERBATIM: 'Que si se niega á marcharse le manifieste su contravencion al convenio del año 90, del cual se dará aviso á la Corte de Londres.' (if she refuses to leave, cite breach of the 1790 [Nootka] Convention, to be reported to the Court of London). Spans n297-n301.

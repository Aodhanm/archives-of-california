'Sobre modo de tratar á gentiles' — VERBATIM: 'Le recomienda qe cuide no experimenten el mas leve daño los gentiles inmediatos al Presº de su mando, qe conservarlos...' [Recommends he take care the gentile Indians near his presidio suffer not the least harm; to conserve them] (cont. n92, orig p.177–8) in peace, & re the non-celebration of Masses await the superior's order, observe the greatest indifference w/ the friars w/o admitting conversation on the matter.

Provincial Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 23, document 871

'Reconocimiento de las tierras de Nieto' — transfer to the Zanja with the crops & recognize the water & lands it could irrigate for wheat & maize; Manuel Nieto sowed there in 95; (cont. n333) present the original document of the cession Gov. Fages made to Nieto in Feb 84 & the reasons he assists so as not to be removed; make it known to the San Gabriel ministers; remit stating distance from the Zanja to San Gabriel, sowing & irrigation lands, whether the mission benefits & if other useful lands near it. Spans n332-n333.

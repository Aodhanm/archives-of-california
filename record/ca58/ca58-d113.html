[= 1838 - April 24 - Mexico. Pavon, director general of revenues, to the administrator of the Monterey Customs House. He forwards documents relating to the ultimatum of the Baron de Deffaudis, for his information etc.] Transmits to the Monterey customs house the documents on the French ultimatum delivered by Baron Deffaudis, the diplomatic trigger of the Pastry War. This is the French crisis reaching the California customs administration in its own paperwork.

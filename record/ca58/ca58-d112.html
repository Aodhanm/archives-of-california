Mejº. / Pesado. / Decreto sobre division de los Deptos en dos secciones, formando Califª parte de una de estas, y nombrando sus Diputados pª el bienio proxº. en el 1º domingo de Oct. de 38. p. 290.' [= Decree dividing the departments into two sections, California forming part of one of them, with deputies to be named for the coming biennium on the first Sunday of October 1838] The companion decree of the same date. Savage's line states that the departments are divided into two sections, that California forms part of one of these, and that their deputies are to be named for the coming biennium on the first Sunday of October 1838. This bears directly on California's representation under the centralist regime. I report only what the abstract line says; the leaf does not state which section California fell into.

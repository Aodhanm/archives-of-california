Heading verbatim: '+ 1838. Mayo 2. Mej.o / Pavon al admor de la aduana marit.a de S.n Fr.co / Le comunica lo mismo que en p. 30 comunicó al admor de la aduana de S. Diego. — p.p. 333-4.' [= 1838 - May 2 - Mexico. Pavon to the administrator of the San Francisco maritime customs house. He communicates the same thing he communicated on p. 30 to the administrator of the San Diego customs house.] Circular repeated to San Francisco of an order previously sent to San Diego. Establishes that a San Francisco maritime customs house was being addressed directly from Mexico in 1838.

Heading verbatim: '+ 1838. Set. 22. Mej. inspector g.l de Guias y Tornaguias, / Mariano Gonzalez Calderon al admor de la aduana marit.a de Mont. / Sobre modo de situar el importe de los sellos de Guia y Tornaguia en la Inspeccion de Guias y Tornaguias, de Mejico. p.p. 381' [= 1838 - September 22 - Mexico. Mariano Gonzalez Calderon, inspector general of guias and tornaguias, to the administrator of the Monterey maritime customs house. On the manner of remitting the proceeds of the guia and tornaguia stamps to the Inspection of Guias and Tornaguias at Mexico.] Instructs Monterey how the proceeds of the guia and tornaguia stamps are to be placed at the disposal of the central Inspection at Mexico. Note that the 'inspector g.l de Guias y Tornaguias' clause is interlined into the date line in Savage's hand, above the sender line.

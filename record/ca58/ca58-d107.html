[= Initiative of the Constitutional Junta of the department of Guanajuato asking the chambers of the National Congress to repeal certain laws on direct taxation (two copies)] A departmental junta petitioning Congress against the direct-contribution laws of the centralist fiscal system. Notable as evidence of departmental resistance to centralist taxation circulating to California in printed form.

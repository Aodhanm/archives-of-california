Ramos Arizpe, Minister of Justice, to the Jefe Politico of Both Californias.] Abstract as written: 'Decreto, dando el tratamiento de Excelencia al Presidente de la Suprema Corte, y el de Señoría á sus ministros y Fiscales, etc.' Decree granting the style of Excelencia to the President of the Supreme Court and that of Señoria to its ministers and fiscales. Addressed to California's own jefe politico, so it is a California-received document.

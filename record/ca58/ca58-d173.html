Pavon to the administrador de la aduana maritima de Monterey, Mexico, 1841-11-22

Heading verbatim: '+ 1841. Nov. 22. Mej. / Pavon al admor de la aduana marit.a de Mont.y' [= Pavon to the administrator of the maritime customhouse of Monterey.] Transmits a 'Disposicion del presidente' on the power to confiscate silver and gold in bullion (plata y oro en pasta) moving without its corresponding guia. A specie-export control measure, directly applicable to the Monterey customhouse.

[= The law of 22 February 1832 in full vigour against those who withdraw from obedience to the government] Transcribes superior dispositions that the law of 22 February 1832 stands in full vigour, under which those who have withdrawn or shall withdraw from obedience to the government must answer with their own property for the sums involved.

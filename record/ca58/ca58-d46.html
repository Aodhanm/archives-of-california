Heading verbatim: '+ 1829. Rosario / Riesgo al comisario de la alta Calªa / Mzo 20... p.275 / Decreto dispensando á A. Salazar siete mezes de practica pª recibirse de abogado. / Junio 26. Alamos. p.276 / Decreto indultando al Tente C. Juan Brisgas y Garmendia. / Junio 4. p.277 / Decreto dispensando cursos de cañones en la universidad á unos estudiantes.' [= 1829, Rosario. Riesgo to the comisario of Alta California. Three decrees: dispensing A. Salazar seven months of practice to be admitted as advocate (p.275); pardoning Lieut. Juan Brisgas y Garmendia (p.276); dispensing university courses in canon law to some students (p.277).] The opening block of the 1829 Rosario remittance, three short decrees (a legal-practice dispensation, an officer's indulto, an academic dispensation). Catalogued here mainly to fix where the 1829 run begins. Two transcription cautions: the surname I read as 'Brisgas' is more probably Bringas, and 'cursos de cañones' is almost certainly canones, i.e. canon law, not artillery; both are flagged as uncertain readings rather than asserted.

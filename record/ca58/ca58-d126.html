Pedro Echeverría, president of the Banco Nacional, to the gefe superior de Hacienda of the Departamento de Californias, México, 1838-05-31

Superior Government State Papers, Benicia, tomo XIX–XXI · BANC MSS C-A 58, document 126

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1838. Mayo 31. Mej. / Pedro Echeverria, presidte del Banco Nacional al gefe superr de Hac.ª del Depto de Cals. / Contabilidad' [= Echeverría, president of the Banco Nacional, seeking a return of unpaid active credits] Transcribed in full. So that the Junta may reach every object the laws have committed to its care, it needs all offices and employees of the Republic to instruct it, in view of the documents and information they hold. Wishing for now to obtain what the visitadores of that departamento can supply regarding active credits caused up to the end of June 1836 and unpaid up to 17 January of last year, that have come or shall come to their knowledge, he begs the addressee to direct them to give the indicated information through the Bank's agents or directly to the Junta. Ends 'Sirvese V. S. admitir etc.'

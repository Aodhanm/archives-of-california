[= 184-. Model of a 'statement of charge and discharge of goods and funds which a principal administration remits to the general directorate of tobacco and other monopoly revenues, for the (present month)'.] A blank accounting model for the tobacco estanco and the other monopoly revenues, showing the carga y data format a principal administration was to remit monthly to the general directorate.

Heading verbatim: '√ Decreto trunco sobre espulsion de Españoles. / p.p 282-3.' [= Truncated decree on the expulsion of Spaniards.] Savage flags this one with a check mark rather than the usual '+', and calls the copy trunco (truncated/incomplete). The expulsion decrees against Spaniards are the legal backdrop to the position of the Spanish-born Franciscans in the California missions, so a copy of one reaching the California archive is substantive even in mutilated form. No record exists for it. Note the catalog does hold a separate and different item, d36 at n80, on the expulsion of foreigners prejudicial to public order; this is not that.

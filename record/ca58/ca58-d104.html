Heading verbatim: '+ 1836. Julio 19. Mej. - / J. de la Fuente al Jefe politº. de A. Cal. / Sobre modo de abonarse las donativas para los gastos de guerra contra los sublevados de Tejas. - p. 206.' [= On the manner in which the donations for the war expenses against the Texas rebels are to be credited] Addressed specifically to the Jefe Político of Alta California, on how the war donativos for the Texas campaign are to be credited. This is a California-addressed document and is uncatalogued. It complements d74 (n88), which covers the 1835 war subsidy and the April 1836 Texas liability decree.

Heading verbatim: '+ 1841. Dic. 16. Mej. / Trigueros, M.tro de Hac.a / Decreto, extinguiendo los empleos de Gefes superiores de Hac.a' [= Trigueros, Minister of the Treasury. Decree abolishing the posts of superior chiefs of the Treasury.] Decree suppressing the office of gefe superior de Hacienda. This matters for California administrative history because the immediately preceding item on n149 is addressed to precisely that officer in the Department of California, so the two entries together show the post being used and then abolished within the same year.

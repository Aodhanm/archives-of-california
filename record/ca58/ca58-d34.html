Echeandia to the Comandante Militar de Monterey, México (as written in the heading), 1828-04-25

Superior Government State Papers, Benicia, tomo XIX–XXI · BANC MSS C-A 58, document 34

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n28-n39, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1828 = Abril 25 = México. / Echeandía al Comandante Militar de Monterey. / Decreto.' [= 1828, April 25, Mexico. Echeandia to the Military Commandant of Monterey. Decree.] Abstract as written: 'Que los derechos causados por los géneros, frutos y efectos importados por Huatulco, pueden asegurarse en la Comisaría Gral. de Oaxaca.' That the duties incurred on goods, produce and effects imported through Huatulco may be secured at the Comisaria General of Oaxaca. This is the Huatulco ruling the band summary names, here with its date, parties and cite for the first time. Caveat I am not resolving: the heading gives the place as Mexico while naming Echeandia, who was in California; I report the line as written and draw no conclusion.

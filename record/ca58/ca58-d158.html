Heading verbatim: '1839. En. 17. Mej. / Mariano Calderon - Circular á los adminisres de rentas - Guias y Tornaguias.' [= Calderón's circular calling in the tornaguías of expired guías] Asks that within one month the respective tornaguías of the guías the administrators have issued, whose second term has already expired, be in his office, or failing that the due proofs that the payment of the corresponding duties has been charged to them in that office, in conformity with article 26 of the decree that established the Inspección de Rentas.

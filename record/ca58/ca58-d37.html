Superior Government State Papers, Benicia, tomo XIX–XXI · BANC MSS C-A 58, document 37

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n40-n52, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1827 = Enero 20 = Rosario. / Riesgo = Sobre bagajes.' [= 1827, January 20, Rosario. Riesgo, on baggage animals.] Riesgo certifies an Executive decree, and Savage transcribes its operative clause in quotation marks: 'A los Gefes efectivos y agregados se daran dos; a los oficiales, uno; los necesarios para las Cajas y municiones, regulando a diez arrobas por carga. Los Gefes y oficiales pagaran los bagajes al tiempo de recibirlos, segun el precio de costumbre. Las cajas de los Cuerpos abonaran en los mismos terminos las cargas que tomen para las atenciones comunes de ellos con cargo a las Tesorerias donde cobren los haberes.' Two pack animals for jefes efectivos and agregados, one for officers, as many as needed for pay-chests and munitions at ten arrobas per load; officers pay for their baggage animals at the customary price on receipt; unit chests reimburse on the same terms against the treasuries where their pay is drawn. Distinct from ca58-d28, which is a freight-rate decree of 8 Aug 1829 at p.502-6.

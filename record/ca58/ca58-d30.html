The closing parenthesis is not written on the leaf.] Abstract as written: 'Que la Suprema Corte ejerza las atribuciones de la ley de 9 de Octubre de 812 concernientes á las Audiencias de Ultramar.' That the Supreme Court exercise the powers, assigned by the law of 9 October 1812 to the overseas Audiencias, that is, the transfer of colonial-era appellate jurisdiction to the republic's Supreme Court. Distinct from the 14 February 1826 Arizpe decree on n26.

Mej. / Pavon al admor de adª de Mont. / Decision del presidte: que las bandas de burato así como todas las de otra clase de la misma materia et. - no deben reputarse como ropa hecha. / p. 462-4.' [= Presidential ruling that burato bands are not to be classed as made-up clothing] A tariff classification ruling: bands of burato and of any other class of the same material are not to be reputed made-up clothing, which governed the prohibited-goods schedule.

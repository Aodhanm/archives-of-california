Superior Government State Papers, Benicia, tomo XIX–XXI · BANC MSS C-A 58, document 39

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n40-n52, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1827 = Marzo 14 = Mexico. / Salgado, Secretario de Hacienda.' [= 1827, March 14, Mexico. Salgado, Secretary of the Treasury.] Savage abstract: 'Decreto por el que se dejan libres de derecho nacional en todo el Territorio varios articulos, como Aventadores, Bateas, Canastos, Cucharas sin tornear, etc.' A decree exempting from the national duty, throughout the territory, various humble manufactured goods, the examples given being winnowing fans, wooden trays or bateas, baskets, and unturned wooden spoons. Savage names no further articles beyond the 'etc.'

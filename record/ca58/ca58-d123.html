[= 1838 - March 21 - Mexico. Pavon to the superior treasury chief of the Department of the Californias, Monterey. Superior orders on the pay of chiefs and employees in active service of all the revenue guards.] Superior dispositions on the salaries of chiefs and employees in actual service in all the revenue guard (resguardo) establishments. Addressed to the Departmental treasury head at Monterey, so it bears on the staffing and pay of the California revenue service.

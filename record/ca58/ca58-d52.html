[= 1829, Sept. 9, Mexico. Bocanegra, secretary of state, to the jefe politico of the Californias. Orders precautions taken against some twelve or fourteen ships supposed to be at sea near Matanchel.] A direct naval-threat warning to California from the Mexican secretary of state: take precautions against a squadron of twelve or fourteen vessels believed to be at sea near Matanchel (the San Blas roadstead). I am inferring, not asserting, that this belongs to the Barradas Spanish reconquest scare of 1829, given the date and the anti-Spanish material immediately around it in the same run. Directly relevant to California coastal defense and to the 1829 independence-preservation decree already catalogued as d17.

SPANS n86-n87. Full transcript, not an abstract

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 252

SPANS n86-n87. Full transcript, not an abstract: 'Ha dispuesto el E.S Presidente que los 3000 pesos, pertenecientes al situado de Calif.ª en que ha salido descubierta la Testamentaría de D.n Federico Becher del Comercio de Mazatlan... como apoderado del S. Comand.te Gral de aquel Departam.to D.n Manuel Micheltorena' — 3,000 pesos of the California situado found owing by the estate of Federico Becher, merchant of Mazatlán, from balances he had received as agent of Comandante General Micheltorena; once recovered they are to be placed at the disposal of Micheltorena's sole agent D.n Juan N. López Portillo, to meet the Department's needs. Marked with a marginal '0' beside the usual tick.

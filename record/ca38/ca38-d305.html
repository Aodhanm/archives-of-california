Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 305

President orders the offices in his district to pay in the sums they have collected under the tax on salaries since 1 July 1842, or whatever part remains. Verbatim: 'enteren las cantidades que hayan cobrado por la Contribucion sobre sueldos desde 1º de Julio de 1842 ó la parte que resten.' Addressed explicitly to the Treasurer at Monterey.

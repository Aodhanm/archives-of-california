Because the government must know the seniority and services of employees in every branch of public…

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 301

Because the government must know the seniority and services of employees in every branch of public administration, the President orders the Administrator to draw up and forward to the Ministry the escalafón (seniority roster) of the employees dependent on that office. Verbatim: 'El Presdte dispone proceda á formar y remitir á este Ministo el escalafon pr el orden de Antiguedad de los empleados que dependen de esa oficina.' CALIFORNIA-SPECIFIC: the San Francisco maritime customhouse.

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 314

Circular giving notice that General Don Mariano Paredes y Arrillaga has been placed in the interim Presidency of the Republic by the vote of the junta of representatives assembled in the capital. Verbatim: 'queda colocado en la Presidencia interina de la República el General Dn Mariano Paredes y Arrillaga por el sufragio de la junta de representantes de la República reunida en la Capital.' The official notification to California of the fall of Herrera.

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 325

Supreme disposition on the reduction of foreign measures of length to Mexican varas. Verbatim: 'Disposicion suprema, relativa á la reduccion de medidas de longitud estrangeras á varas Mexs.' Gloss: conversion standard for foreign linear measures, of practical weight for customs valuation and for land measurement.

'Que el Presidente dispone se esceptuen del descuento del 29 1/2 por 100

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 232

'Que el Presidente dispone se esceptuen del descuento del 29 1/2 por 100. á los Generales y Coroneles que estan empleados en los Departam.[entos], ó mandando en Gefe alg.s fuerzas' — generals and colonels employed in the Departments, or commanding forces in chief, are exempted from the 29 1/2 per cent salary deduction. [Figure checked at max resolution; second digit carries a clear descender, so 29 not 20 — but cf. n83, which reads 20 1/2 for what looks like the same deduction.]

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo I (SGDD) · BANC MSS C-A 38, document 292

'Trasladada esta Comunicacion del Comisario General de guerra y Marina relativa a la orden que dio el Comte gral de Califa al Tesorero, sobre haber reorganisado las Companias permanentes que estaban Casi disueltas' [= transmitting the Comisario General of War and Marine communication on the order the Comandante General of California gave the Treasurer, concerning the reorganization of the permanent companies, which were almost dissolved]. HIGH VALUE: direct evidence of the near-collapse and reconstitution of California's presidial companies in 1845.

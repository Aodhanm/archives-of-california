Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 187

Detailed ajuste of the Santa Bárbara company for 1782: Tte. Ortega $550; Alférez Argüello $400; 2 sargtos $525; 2 cabos $450; 49 soldados $10,657; gratificación $540; adjustments $521.2; gross $13,623.6; less invalids' deduction (8 mrs/peso) $400.5½ & agency, net $13,195.7; rebajas (2 facturas de géneros $1,200.58, agencias $1,995.2, prior balance owed the company $2,353.74); $4,349.17 owed.

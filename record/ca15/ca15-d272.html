Service record (hoja de servicio) of Sargento Mariano de la Luz Verdugo, age 42

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 272

Service record (hoja de servicio) of Sargento Mariano de la Luz Verdugo, age 42. Enlisted as a soldier 15 Dec 1766; cabo 1 Dec 1774; sargento 5 May 1781; served at Loreto (7+ yrs), San Diego (7 yrs), Monterey. Campaigns: the 1769 party under Capt. Fernando de Rivera y Moncada that *sostuvieron* (withstood) the attack the gentiles made at the paraje de la Cieneguilla, in which 8 of the attackers were killed; the suppression of the 1775 San Diego revolt (sierra sweeps to seize the ringleaders 'de la sublevación de cristianos y gentiles que destruyeron la misión de San Diego en 1775'); and the 1782 Río Colorado expedition under Cmdt.-Inspector Felipe de Neve (post-Yuma campaign). Orig. p.335.

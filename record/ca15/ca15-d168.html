Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 168

Loreto presidial company & employees, pay 1784–1789 (per ajustes de cuentas): Capt. José Joaquín de Arrillaga $1,500; Tte. José Fco. Ortega $550; Alférez José Mª Estrada $400; 1 sargento $262.4; 3 cabos @ $225 = $675; 39 soldados $8,482.4; gratificación $390; invalids (Capt. José Cañete $500, Sargto José Gabriel Arce $120, soldados Diego Ochoa & Luis Góngora[?]); maritime crews — 1 carpintero de ribera $132, 1 herrero $120, patrón Diego Pérez $168, guardián $84, 6 marineros @ $72 = $576, arráez $84 + 6 marineros $444; 5 marineros added 22 Mar 1788, serving to 11 Feb 1789. Orig. pp.719–725.

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 195

Loreto presidial company, 1 Dec 1783: Capitán, Tte, Alférez, 2 sargtos, 3 cabos, 39 soldados = 47 hombres; 1 Tte/1 sargto/2 cabos/26 soldados detached on the frontier; 1 sargto + 4 at Santa Ana. Caballada 135 mules, 14 horses; arms reserve; scarcity of provisions, warehouse empty of effects. (Monthly reports by Capt. Joaquín Cañete show little change.) Orig. pp.728–740.

Santa Bárbara company, 1786: 1 Tte, 1 Alférez, 3 sargtos, 2 cabos

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 269

Santa Bárbara company, 1786: 1 Tte, 1 Alférez, 3 sargtos, 2 cabos; soldiers grew from 45 (March) to 53 (Dec), total ~60 hombres; 1 sargto + 14 soldados detached at San Buenaventura, others at missions/caballada; free for service the 2 officers, 1 sargto, 17 soldados. (Cabos named: Raymundo Carrillo &c.) Orig. pp.213–331 (scattered).

SB company arms & livestock, Mar 1783: 84 horses, 162 mules

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 203

SB company arms & livestock, Mar 1783: 84 horses, 162 mules; 14 pairs pistols, 140 cannon cartridges (4-pdr), 6,700 musket cartridges, 18 arr. powder, 14 arr. balls, 119 arr. lead, 2,860 gunflints. Mentions 2 pedreros (swivel guns) firing at the target (consuming lead/ball). Orig. p.7.

Per-recruit armament & remuda distribution roster for the new Santa Bárbara company

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 58

Per-recruit armament & remuda distribution roster for the new Santa Bárbara company — 'Nombres' table, each soldier with equipment columns (silla, adarga/flecha, caballos) and a value (~$58–68): Juan Ignacio Valencia, José Villa, Manuel Valenzuela, M. Vázquez, José Mª Velasco, José Esteban Romero, M. I. Lugo, Ignacio Rodríguez, Juan Javier Mejía, José Lorenzo Salazar, Ignacio Cota, José Patiño, Felipe Gonzáles, José Polanco, Julián Guerrero, José Samaniego, Rosalino Fernández, Juan Ignacio Martínez, Agustín Sota[?], Vitorino Félix, Guillermo Soto, Eugenio Valdés, Justo Hernández, José Soto, Juan Antonio Monreal, Hildefonso Domínguez… Summary $2,658; net charge $1,530. Genealogically central for SB's founding company. Orig. pp.4–6.

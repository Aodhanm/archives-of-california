Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 421

Hoja de servicio of José [Darío] Mariano Argüello, age 37: soldier from 20 Oct 1773, ~18 yrs; founder of the cavalry detachment at the Colorado; commissioned by Neve in 1781 to found the Pueblo de Los Ángeles; founded San Buenaventura as the presidio of the Santa Bárbara Channel. (The Argüello patriarch.) Orig. p.553.

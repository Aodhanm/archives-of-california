San Francisco presidial company distribution & defense, 1 Dec 1782. Tte. Moraga

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 188

San Francisco presidial company distribution & defense, 1 Dec 1782. Tte. Moraga; Alférez José Ramón Lasso de la Vega; Sargto Juan Pablo Grijalva; 4 cabos; 26 soldados. Postings: caballada 7; presidio guard 1 cabo + 4 sold; 5 at each of Missions San Francisco & Santa Clara; pueblo de San José 2; detached/sick/prisoners 8; free for service 2 officers + 5 sold. Caballada 73 horses, 58 mules; recua 13 mules; arms (33 pairs pistols, 7 good escopetas, 3 adargas, 11 cueras, 8 espadas, 32 lance-heads); 2 mounted cannon + 2 remounted; 90 arr. powder, 1,496 ball cartridges, 502 gunflints, 81 balls of 3½ lb & 16 of 6 lb for the cannon. 'Tropa bien vestida y bastimentada.' Orig. pp.456–459.

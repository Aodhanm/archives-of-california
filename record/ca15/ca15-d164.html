Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 164

Padrón of neophytes at Mission San Luis Obispo. Officers named: Miguel Robles (alcalde), Joaquín Murillo (alcalde), Tomás Llavería[?] (regidor); 104 more married; 1 widower; 2 single; total 107 adult males, each contributing $1 to the war donativo = $107. 528 baptized since founding; 89 dead; rest women & children. Orig. pp.658–661.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read author: 'José de Ávila'; summary: 'Invoice for the Monterey presidio incl. costs to San Blas, $1,427. Orig. pp.128–133.'. Leaf n342 (Savage stamp '336'), third and last entry, verbatim: "+1791 = Abril 15 = México / José de Arvide / — / Importa la fact.ª p.ª el Pres.o de Monterey, incluidos los gastos hasta S. Blas. $ 8427. / pp 128-33." AUTHOR WRONG: at 10x the underlined name is unambiguously "Arvide" (A-r-v-i-d-e, with a clear medial d-ascender and a terminal e), not "Ávila" — there is no l-ascender and the word does not end in a. SUMMARY WRONG on the sum: the leaf reads "$ 8427" (leading 8 formed exactly like the 8s in '128-33' and '16158' on the same leaf); the catalogue's "$1,427" is not supported — there is no 1 in the figure. Place (México), date (15 Apr 1791), orig pp.128-33 and stamp 336 are correct; no recipient named. Omissions OK: all three entries on n342 are catalogued (d489, d490, d491).

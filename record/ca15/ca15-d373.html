Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 373

Romeu's letter to Capt. Alejandro Malaspina, regretting he was not at Monterey during Malaspina's visit & wishing him a happy arrival at his destination. A documentary trace of the Malaspina scientific expedition's Sept 1791 call at Monterey — a major foreign exploration contact. Orig. p.4.

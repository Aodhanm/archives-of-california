Desertion + robbery toward the Colorado. Night of 13 Mar, soldado José Mariano Lepis[?] deserted with 3 escort arrieros + a servant of Sargto Grijalva, having robbed the King's cargo & some troop valuables and taken 25 mounts; Moraga pursued (with 'Chumbras'[?] auxiliaries) and caught them 18 Mar, 4 leagues from the Colorado River, on the road to Sonora; Indians killed 3 [animals], 2 deserters lost. Orig. pp.924–925.

Weather & health report (temperamento), 1798: wet winter, strong winds blamed for poor wheat

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo I–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 15, document 479

Weather & health report (temperamento), 1798: wet winter, strong winds blamed for poor wheat; continuous summer/autumn fogs; many fevers (atabardillados), pleurisy-like side pains, sore throats, general catarrh; Aug/Oct downpours with much thunder & lightning; from Nov. heavy rains made roads near-impassable; no frosts yet. Orig. pp.707–708.

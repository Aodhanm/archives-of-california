Diligencias re the Indian Valerio's fatal wounding of Wenceslao, a fellow Sta Bárbara mission neophyte

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 304

Diligencias re the Indian Valerio's fatal wounding of Wenceslao, a fellow Sta Bárbara mission neophyte; the reo hid in a cueva (cave), was fired on when he showed his head, fled wrapped in his blanket, was pursued, recaptured wounded, and died shortly after; escribano sarg. Dámaso Rodríguez [⚠ dated 1815 — out of sequence in an 1827 tomo]

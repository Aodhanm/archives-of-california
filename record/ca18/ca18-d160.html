Vallejo ordered to investigate the wounding of vecino José Antonio Castro at Mission San Juan Bautista…

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 160

Vallejo ordered to investigate the wounding of vecino José Antonio Castro at Mission San Juan Bautista (9 Aug — a sable cut to the left arm by Buenaventura Zúñiga 'Quinte' over a gambling quarrel: a won horse + a disputed card-deck payment); witnesses Ignacio Vallejo, Felipe García; cirujano Manuel Toca (wound not mortal); confession before alcalde Ignacio Pacheco — Zúñiga pleads self-defense

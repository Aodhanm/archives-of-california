Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 25

San Diego presidial company strength return (April): 71 men (1 capt. + 1 tte. vacant, 1 alf., 2 sgts, 7 cpls, 1 cadet, 59 soldiers) + 4 artillery + 19 invalids; 437 horses; 25 fusiles. Troop & families 'medianamente vestida,' armament 'continuamente comprometido.' Independence sworn at SD 20 Apr 1822

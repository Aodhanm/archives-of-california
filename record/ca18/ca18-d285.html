Ship log: arrival of the Pocahontas (Am., from Bodega, Capt. Bradshaw, 21 crew)

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 285

Ship log: arrival of the Pocahontas (Am., from Bodega, Capt. Bradshaw, 21 crew); the *Volunteer* sailed 9 May for San Blas carrying Comisario José Mª Herrera + 5 artillerymen + 5 cavalry + 4 civilians — the deported 1829 Solís-Revolt prisoners; the *Brookline* to Sta Bárbara; later the Mexican brig *Vitor* (Capt. Hatch) bringing 11 convicts, the *Leonor* (Capt. Henry Fitch), the brig Ayacucho (233 t, in ballast); the Globe (Capt. Moore)

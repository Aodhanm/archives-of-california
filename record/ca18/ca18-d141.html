Artillery troops' back-pay grievance: owed *alcances*

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 141

Artillery troops' back-pay grievance: owed *alcances*; promised (when the brig *Morelos* came) 2/3 in effects + 1/3 cash — unfulfilled; paid only Aug–Dec last year, nothing of the arrears, since Jan 'con la ración nada más' (reiterated 7 Jun, no resolution)

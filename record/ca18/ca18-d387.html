Causa over an accidental shooting: soldiers out hunting deserters near rancho San Ignacio

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 387

Causa over an accidental shooting: soldiers out hunting deserters near rancho San Ignacio; while remounting, Gabriel Albiso's shotgun snagged in the holster and discharged, wounding his comrade José Soto; fiscal (Sánchez) recommends acquittal — a casualty with no quarrel; passed to comte Ignacio Martínez; no final result

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LIII–LXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 18, document 22

Causa de conspiración = the 1835 Apalátegui–Torres revolt against Gov. Figueroa (first 2 folios missing). Deposition of American Mr. Job Day via interpreter John B.R. Cooper; confession of Don Miguel Hidalgo detailing meetings w/ Antonio Apalátegui & Francisco Torres, letters smuggled by brigantine to Fernando Escobosa in Guaymas (Sonora), Englishman Lewis Burton, Martin Cooper, Benito Díaz; plotters 'counted on the Monterey troops, minus their officers.'

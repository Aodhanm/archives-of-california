Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read date_text: '1810-06-07'; year: '1810'. Leaf n12 (stamp 13, ink 12) of 168036074_80_3_2, first entry, heading verbatim: '+1819 = Junio 7 = S. Blas / Antonio Quartara'. At scale 1 the year's final digit is a 9 with a closed bowl and long descender, not a 0. The next entry on the leaf is dated 1820, and the content (the Junta de Marina chartering the fragata 'Reyna de los Angeles' for $10,000 plus $4,000 monthly to carry articulos de guerra to Alta California) fits the 1819 post-Bouchard reinforcement. The date is 7 June 1819, not 1810.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

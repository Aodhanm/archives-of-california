Corte de Caja, 1 Nov 1845 (verbatim figures): 'Exista en esta fha $19,812'

Departmental State Papers (Miscellaneous), tomo IX–XVI · BANC MSS C-A 32, document 49

Corte de Caja, 1 Nov 1845 (verbatim figures): 'Exista en esta fha $19,812'; remitted to Comp.a de San Diego $2,000; 'Entregado al Comand.te Gen. Castro $13,378'; 'Enviado al Gob.r Pico p.a los empleados $4,005'; 'Quedando existentes $430'. The Pico–Castro division of the department's cash on the eve of 1846 in one entry (arithmetic checks: 19,812−2,000−13,378−4,005=429≈430). Recovered 2026-09-02 from inside d032's declared span.

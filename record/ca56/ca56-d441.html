'amenazas de invasión de los Amajavas' (Mojave) — relays Capt. José de la Guerra (SB, 27 Feb) that the invasion rumors persist; recommends a fortified casafuerte with loopholes & sentry tower at the escoltas ('2 or 3 miserable Indians can surprise the guards'); ordered vigilance at San Buenaventura, San Gabriel & San Fernando

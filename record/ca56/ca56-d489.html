Entry verbatim: Junio 2. Da Cuenta Con el parte del Cabo graduado de Sargto Jose Herrera de la salida que hiso Con la Tropa a la Ranch.a nombrada Huilantuliyami por la que vera los registros que anda haciendo Caros Smitht. Gloss: reports the despatch of cabo graduado de sargento Jose Herrera, who marched out with troops to the rancheria named Huilantuliyami, by which the Jefe will see the registros that Caros Smitht is making. The rancheria name is written clearly; the surname reading Caros Smitht is uncertain and may be intended for Carlos Smith.

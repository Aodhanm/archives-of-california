Heading verbatim: Mayo 1o. Jose de Herrera a... sin dereccion. Entry verbatim: Partida en requerimiento de Indios Cristianos. Gloss: 1 May 1823, Jose de Herrera, addressee not given; a detachment sent out in requerimiento of Christian Indians, that is in pursuit of runaway neophytes. Savage places this entry out of date order, after the Junio 2 item.

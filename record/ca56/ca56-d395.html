State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 395

observations on financing the Deputies — the Province has no wealthy men to bear the office & no public funds; the Junta reckoned $4,000/yr needed (distance to the Capital + deputies' costs); asks the mission Padres for 'un donativo gracioso' (as the vecindario will) to avoid the *contribución forzosa*

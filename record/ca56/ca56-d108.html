State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 108

Ponponio-pursuit narrative — the SF mission escolta learns from a captive India that the outlaw Ponponio & 2 companions raided from the Sierra; soldiers recognize Ponponio, fire on him as he flees into the zacatal, surround it but can't seize him (nightfall), recover 4 stolen horses; a party under cabo Reyes finds Ponponio's 'madriguera' in the Sierra (2 lances, a machete made from a barrel-hoop, 2 knives, a hammer & chisel); Ponponio ambushes & disarms Reyes's son; cabo Reyes returns with 8 bow-armed Indian auxiliaries from Mission Santa Clara but can't catch him (pp.621–628)

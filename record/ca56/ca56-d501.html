State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 501

Entry verbatim: (ditto) 16 Dice, que el sabado 13 se presento en esta guarnicion el Sarg.to de Infant.a Pedro Castillo Con 14 Sold.s a relevar a los que se hallan en este Destacam.to. Savage repeats the day with a ditto mark under Abril 16. Gloss: reports that on Saturday the 13th the sergeant of infantry Pedro Castillo presented himself at this garrison with 14 soldiers to relieve those serving in the detachment.

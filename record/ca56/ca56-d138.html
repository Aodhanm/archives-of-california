full *hoja de servicio* of Capt. Luis Antonio Argüello (first native-born gov.) — b. SF presidio, age 44, cadet 1799 → captain 1817, ~40 yrs; ⭐ his 1808 expedition up the river debouching at SF (10 men, 17 days, attacked = his Sacramento exploration), 1826 anti-gentile campaigns to recover mission fugitives, the 1834 northern reconnaissance; 2nd Jefe Político 1822–25 (the Asia/Constante surrender); ⭐⭐ Echeandía's damning note: conduct 'estraviada por el uso de los licores,' health 'quebrantada,' relieved of command (in Echeandía's own hand)

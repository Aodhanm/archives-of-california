the General Colonization Law of 18 Aug 1824 (full 17 articles) — the foundational Mexican land/colonization statute: foreigners' security; only ungranted national land colonizable; the 20-league-border / 10-league-coast restriction; the 11-league grant maximum (1 sq. league irrigated + 4 temporal + 6 grazing); preference to Mexican citizens; empresario-contract guarantees; no mortmain; no holding if domiciled abroad → see Entry 4

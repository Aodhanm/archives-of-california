'amenazas de invasión de los Indios' — a night dispatch from the SD cdr

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 448

'amenazas de invasión de los Indios' — a night dispatch from the SD cdr. reports a large armed mounted Indian force at the Río Colorado (w/ 2 rancherías of Christian [runaway] Indians); ordered Lt. Gabriel Moraga to sally with 25 men toward San Gabriel to reconnoiter; the Padre Presidente to meet Kotzebue that Sunday

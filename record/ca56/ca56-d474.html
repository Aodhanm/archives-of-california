State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 474

long omnibus report — the Territorial Diputación suspended since 1825 under Argüello & hard to reconstitute (no educated men, no funds); the Comisario Herrera's certified letter & contracts; unlicensed otter-hunting by Californios & foreigners; a Russian frigate; the mission tithe / church-state question — the missions 'are not poor' (some holding up to $100,000 in cash reserves), tithes could fund new missions; forwarded to the President

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 476

on partial contributions (direct taxes) & the presidial-company reform — proposes 3 new California companies: (1) to secure the Sonora communication, (2) ⭐ a NORTHERN company to support San Francisco & guard the region 'where the Russian establishments & Missions San Rafael & San Francisco Solano lie' (a counter-Fort-Ross garrison), (3) a Baja California company; man them with army deserters & vagrants

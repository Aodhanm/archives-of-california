dossier 'sobre pagos a individuos que trabajan en fábricas de Presidios'

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 313

dossier 'sobre pagos a individuos que trabajan en fábricas de Presidios' — a detailed fiscal reconstruction of the presidio-construction gratification (Croix's proposed 15% rebate; $8,000 to Factor Manuel Ramón de Goya; the King's approval 8 Feb 1782; Fages's 1788 memorias for $12,000; habilitado Carcaba's shortfall on $8,511; the habilitado gral to settle at $5,235 + $600 freight, incl. Sta Bárbara, paid by *bando* to the troops/servants who did the work) + a per-presidio/per-year distribution table & a 15-Aug-1792 nota

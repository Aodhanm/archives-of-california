State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 382

(draft cluster, n333–334) ⭐ no foreigner left by arriving ships; San Blas ships' pennant signals; ⭐ how to charge the English for supplies & bar trade with them (Nootka/anti-contraband); Pablo Grijalba's retirement; ⭐ proposes the spiritual conquest of the Sta Bárbara Channel Chumash; ⭐ reconnaissance for founding a Spanish villa at SF + new presidio construction; then (n334) Pedro Varela named Marine-dept. Secretary; faculty to permit missionary retirements; urgent request for armeros; ⭐⭐ engineer Alberto de Córdova's SF works (repairing the fort, 2 garitas, a double wall for the powder-magazine, mounting 3 cannon from the *San Carlos*, reconnoitring lands fit for Spanish settlements); 4 Fernandino friars retiring (Antonio Dantí, Baldomero López, José Antonio Calzada, José de Arruita)

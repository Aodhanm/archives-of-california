on the conservation & propagation of the smallpox vaccine ('fluido vacuno')

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 340

on the conservation & propagation of the smallpox vaccine ('fluido vacuno') — ayuntamientos, diputaciones & jefes políticos to promote arm-to-arm vaccination via children, extend it to haciendas/ranchos, dispel anti-vaccine 'preocupaciones y falsas ideas'; the Diputaciones to fund it; recirculate the vaccination instruction made under Viceroy Félix Calleja

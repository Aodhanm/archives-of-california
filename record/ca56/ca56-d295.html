State Papers, Sacramento, tomo X–XIX · BANC MSS C-A 56, document 295

original letter declaring the impossibility of aiding the troops — last harvest only 135 fanegas of wheat; after maize & beans <600 fanegas of seed to feed 'más de 1100 almas'; the pueblo has wheat to sell for cash but the mission has none to spare — 'no me [moleste] más'

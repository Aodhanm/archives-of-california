(2 Docs) 'Enterado de q.e las tropas del Pres.o carecen de viveres

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1062

(2 Docs) 'Enterado de q.e las tropas del Pres.o carecen de viveres; de q.e habiéndolos pedido á P.s se los han negado: remita informes p.a la averiguacion del caso' — informed that the presidio troops lack provisions and that, having requested them of the Padres/missions, they were refused: send reports for the investigation of the case. [2nd:] 'Remitir una lista de españoles q.e haya en su jurisdiccion, tanto de los q.e deban quedarse como de los q.e no' — send a list of the Spaniards in his jurisdiction, both of those who should remain and those who should not.

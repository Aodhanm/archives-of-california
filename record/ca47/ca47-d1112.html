Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1112

'Q.e ha habido graves motivos p.a q.e la causa de Alferez José M.a Ramirez no se haya concluido p.r el Tral. comun y p.r esto no se le ha consignado el reo p.a q.e lo juzgue el eclesiastico, p.r el delito de poligamia' — serious reasons have kept the common tribunal from concluding Alferez José M.a Ramírez's case, so the accused has not been handed to the ecclesiastical court for the crime of polygamy.

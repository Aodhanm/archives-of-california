'Ha ordenado á Pᵉ Narciso Duran qᵉ facilite $800. pª suministraciones del Piquete pr Octᵉ y Novᵉ'

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1233

'Ha ordenado á Pᵉ Narciso Duran qᵉ facilite $800. pª suministraciones del Piquete pr Octᵉ y Novᵉ' — has ordered Padre Narciso Durán to furnish $800 for the Picket's supplies for October and November.

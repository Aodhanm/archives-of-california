(2 docs) '...Se le enviarán de Sn Gabriel $1.000 en efectos y metálico pa suministraciones de la tropa' + 'Informe sobre Realistas qe se dice hay en Sta Barba' — $1,000 in goods and coin will be sent from San Gabriel for supplying the troops; and report on Royalists (Realistas) said to be in Santa Barbara.

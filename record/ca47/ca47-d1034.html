(2 Docs) 'Q.e linderos señalados p.r Ayuntam.to á Ranchos de Sn Pedro y Sta Gertrudis, son nulos, p.r estar ya señalados de antemano, con todas las formalidades' — that the boundaries marked by the town council for the Ranchos of San Pedro and Santa Gertrudis are null, as they had already been marked beforehand with all formalities.

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 923

(2 docs) 'Avisa haberse embarcado sin licencia Ps. de Sta Barba. y Sn Buenaventa. y consulta qe. si pa. evitar estos escandalos, se les concede á los otros la qe. solicitan' — advises that Fathers of Sta Bárbara & San Buenaventura embarked without license and consults whether to grant the others the license they seek, to avoid such scandals; and 'Acompaña docs. relativos á internacion de pescadores Americanos, y providencias tomadas pa. arrojarlos fuera' — encloses docs on the incursion of American fishermen and the measures taken to expel them.

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 950

(4 docs; entry continues on n225, beyond this range) 'Manifiesta ser imposible cumplir con circular 8 Octe. 1827 qe. previene se den noticias al Gobno. cuando sean importantes, con oportunidad' — states it is impossible to comply with the 8 Oct 1827 circular requiring timely reporting of important news; 'Acompaña copia de diligencias sobre apresamto. de Goleta Ama. Aguila, en Rada de Sta Barba. 1822; y pide se resuelva sobre el particular' — encloses copy of proceedings on the seizure of the American schooner 'Águila' in the Sta Bárbara roadstead in 1822, requesting resolution; sends index of correspondence dispatched; and argues 'qe. Comanda. Gral. de Cals. ha sido establecida pr. ley', asking that the Chambers clarify the point if his word be doubted.

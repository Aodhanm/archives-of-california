Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 928

[Savage writes 'En.o 8'; read as 8 Feb per sequence] 'Avisando qe. pa. construir la Casa-Fuerte cerca de Mision Sn Rafael, se carece de los precisos medios, pero se procurará levantar un pequeño alojamiento pa. la tropa qe. ha de custodiar dicha Mision y sus Contornos' — advises that to build the blockhouse (Casa-Fuerte) near Mission San Rafael the necessary means are lacking, but a small quarters will be raised for the troop to guard that mission and its surroundings.

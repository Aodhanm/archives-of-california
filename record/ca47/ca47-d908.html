'Acompaña acta de eleccion de Diputados Territoriales, y avisa ha remitido á Spmo. Consejo la de los Representantes del Territorio al Congreso' — encloses the record of the election of Territorial Deputies, and advises he has remitted to the Supreme Council that of the Territory's Representatives to Congress.

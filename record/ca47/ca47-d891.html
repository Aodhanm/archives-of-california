Nov.o 11: 'Haber dado pasaporte a Americanos William Pope e Isaac Slover, qe salieron de nuevo Mex pa Sonora y habiendo estraviado Camino llegaron al Territorio' — gave passports to the Americans William Pope and Isaac Slover, who left New Mexico for Sonora and, straying from the road, reached California.

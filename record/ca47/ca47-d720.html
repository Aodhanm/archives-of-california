Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 720

'Que lleva consigo al capⁿ de artª Migˡ Gonzalez por convenir así á la tranquilidad pública, y que cuide ese Ayuntamiento por su parte de evitar cualesquiera sátiras qᵉ puedan motivar odiosidades y choques con la fami[lia]…' — he is taking Capt. of artillery Miguel González away with him as it suits public tranquility, and the Ayuntamiento must for its part prevent any satires that could provoke hatreds and clashes with the family (body continues beyond range, onto next scan n166)

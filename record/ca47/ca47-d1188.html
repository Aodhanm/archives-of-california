Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1188

'Disponer q.e cada dia 15 se presenten en S.n Gabriel 1 Sarg.to y un Cabo de Confianza p.a tomar y conducir los haberes de la tropa.' Gloss: arrange that a sergeant and trusted corporal report at San Gabriel each 15th to take and convey the troops' pay.

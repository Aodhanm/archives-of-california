Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1204

'Trasladandole lo q.e le dice al anterior. Que luego q.e se reciba del mando ocurra a Mision S.n Jose donde se le daran $500.' Gloss: forwards what was told to Arguello; upon taking command, Martinez is to go to Mission San Jose where he will be given $500.

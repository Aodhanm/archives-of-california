(3 doc.) 'Sin intˢ / id / Que en el Estado de su Compᵃ se manifiesta un sacristan y como que no hay semejante plaza, se denomine encargado del cuidado de la capilla' — three offices; first two without substance; third: since a sacristán appears in the company report and there is no such post, he is to be styled caretaker of the chapel

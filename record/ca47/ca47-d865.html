En.o 22 (2 docs): 'Mala calidad de tabacos qe se remitieron pr buque N.l General Bravo'

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 865

En.o 22 (2 docs): 'Mala calidad de tabacos qe se remitieron pr buque N.l General Bravo' — poor quality of the tobacco shipped on the national vessel General Bravo; and 'Pr no haber papel sellado se esta habilitando el comun' — for want of stamped paper, plain paper is being validated.

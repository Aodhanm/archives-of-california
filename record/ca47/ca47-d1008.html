Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1008

'Detener en el Pto. a Fragata 'Franklin' cuyo Capn. ha sido acusado pr. varias personas y entre ellas el estranjero Simpson de Contrabandista, y formar diligencias del asunto' — detain the frigate Franklin in port, its captain accused (including by the foreigner Simpson) of smuggling, and institute proceedings.

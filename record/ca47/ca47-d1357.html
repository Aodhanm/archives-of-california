On investigations ordered 28 Oct against the Sandwich-Islands brig 'Karimoko' for coastal offenses

Departmental Records, tomo IV–VI (Books 16–20, 27–32) · BANC MSS C-A 47, document 1357

On investigations ordered 28 Oct against the Sandwich-Islands brig 'Karimoko' for coastal offenses: '1o Que se le aviso pr el Gobno estar prohibido qe tocasen buques estranjeros en Sta Catarina, y no solo se ha contentado con tocar, sino qe ha depositado en ella efectos y animales... 2o Que arribo pr Abril a Ensenada de Sn Pedro donde comercio sin permiso, y se dio a la vela sin pagar dros.' Orders him to go to Sta Catalina I. and transport effects/animals, secure payment of duties, leaving the females it is prohibited to export; '...pagara 17$ pr cada tonelada y $1000 pr valor de derechos.' Foreign (Hawaiian) ship, smuggling, Sta Catalina Island.

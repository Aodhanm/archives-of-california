State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 53, document 123

the companion diplomatic letter (to the Ross commander, day after Doc 112 to the Governor): even more explicit — seeks 'un convenio entre el gabinete de San Petersburgo y el de México,' friendship & commerce especially with neighboring nations; notes he has commissioned Vallejo to contract some armament & other articles at Ross, asking the commander's protection

the committee's dictamen on the Chico–Durán affair, siding with the government

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 53, document 169

the committee's dictamen on the Chico–Durán affair, siding with the government: characterizes Durán's resistance as the padres' grief at losing 'el poder temporal con que á la desgraciada California mantenían en el envilecimiento y la obscuridad'; finds Chico facultado to secularize Sta Inés & S. Buenaventura, approved 1 July 1836 (the secular-californio counter-view completing docs 157–158)

Previously catalogued only within a grouped entry; itemised by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit. ⚠ Note: the concealing band does name this document in passing, so it was not wholly invisible; what was missing is a record of its own. It lay inside the span of a grouped entry covering n148-n162, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 Mayo 9 Mot. / José Figueroa á la Diputación Territorial' [= Figueroa to the Territorial Deputation on the Branciforte ayuntamiento's lack of municipal funds] Savage's abstract states that the alcalde of Branciforte reports the ayuntamiento has no funds with which to pay the maestro de primeras letras nor the secretary of that body, and asks whether they may collect duties for the cutting of timber. The leaf says only this; it does not record any decision.

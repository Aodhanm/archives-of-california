the 'el Toro' land dispute — forwards Comandante Gral Nicolás Gutiérrez's communication + Capt. Agustín V. Zamorano's oficio asking the Diputación suspend the possession of 'el Toro' conceded to José Ramón Estrada, which Zamorano claims for his cavalry company's horse-pasture 'con equívocas líneas, argumentos y palabras ofensivas'; the Diputación rebuts, resolving lands go 'á familias honradas y laboriosas' (also the S. Francisquito paraje). The future governor Alvarado in the Diputación

Carrillo, Haro and Estudillo (Comisión de arbitrios); closed by Figueroa and J. M. Maldonado, Monterey, 1834-07-30

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 53, document 86

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no ITEM-LEVEL record existed. ⚠ Note: the concealing band does name this document in passing, so it was not wholly invisible; what was missing is a record of its own. It lay inside a band record spanning n148-n162, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1834 Julio 30 Monterey / José Antº Carrillo Franco de Haro, J. A. Estudillo' [= The 30 July continuation of the plan, articles 8 to 19, closed by Figueroa and Maldonado] Opens with Savage's note 'Este documento es la continuacion del anterior comenzando desde el artículo 8'. Art. 8: one real on each sealed piece used for weighing and measuring. Art. 9: 2 pesos per performance from impresarios of maromas, comedias and títeres. Art. 10: billiard tables 1 peso monthly. Art. 11: at Monterey, San Francisco, Santa Barbara, San Pedro and San Diego every national vessel not a warship pays medio real per bulto landed for trade, foreigners one real. Art. 12: the 2 reales per ton on foreign vessels ceded to the States by art. 4 of the 1827 maritime tariff to be sought for the Territory. Art. 13 (on n161): 4 reales per otter skin of stated size and the same for beaver. Art. 14: fines imposed by alcaldes and the Jefe Político to municipal funds. Art. 15: the municipal duty on domestic liquors reduced from publication of the acuerdo to 3 pesos per barrel of aguardiente, 2 pesos for angélica and 12 reales per barrel of wine, payable by the introducer at the point of sale. Art. 16: a voluntary contribution from national and foreign vessels anchoring in Monterey bay for the sole object of building a wharf, collected by the Captain of the Port and ceasing when the work is done. Art. 17: 3 pesos municipal duty per almoneda of trade goods sold at public auction. Art. 18 (on n162): given the urgency of funds for ordinary and preferred expenses of the pueblos, under attribution 4 of art. 335 of the constitution then in force and with the Jefe Político's prior assent, the arbitrios of articles 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 17 are to be used. Art. 19: the plan to be reported to the Supreme Government with a request for approval by the General Congress. Closing note: 'En esta fha la comicion retiro el artº 15 para nueva redaccion = Figueroa = J. M. Maldonado.'

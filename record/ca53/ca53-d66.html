State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 53, document 66

a provisional resolution fixing the northern-district jurisdictional boundaries (límites not yet divided) in 4 articles: San Francisco to the Bay + the San Joaquín & Sacramento rivers and 'el otro lado de la Bahía hasta la gentilidad' (the North-Bay/Solano frontier to unconverted Indian country); San José (Laguna→Cordillera→sierra de Sta Cruz); Branciforte (sierra de Sta Cruz to the coast, to Rancho S. Andrés); Monterey (from Branciforte's line, separated from the Valle de los Tulares), Bay-Area jurisdictional geography

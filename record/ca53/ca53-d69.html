the municipal-revenue ordinance (copy) — a 7-article tax schedule

State Papers, Missions and Colonization, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 53, document 69

the municipal-revenue ordinance (copy) — a 7-article tax schedule: 4 reales/vara on a house-solar grant (adobe 4 / palo-parado 2); ejidos sold *justipreciados* or leased; a meat-slaughter tax; a monthly shop tax; 15 pesos on foreign ships' *aguada*; medio real per hide + a tallow duty — to fund the municipal treasury & the Casas Consistoriales

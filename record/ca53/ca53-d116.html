assessment of the missions' capacity to support the frontier fort: Missions S. José & Sta Clara already bear the presidios of SF & Monterey and are strained by neophyte flight, so cannot fund a new establishment — but hopes Missions San Francisco Solano & San Rafael, built up by new missionaries & more neófitos, will grow enough to help realize the project

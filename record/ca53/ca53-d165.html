backs the Police Commissioners' complaint of the 'mal entendida compasión' with which the padres of Missions Sta Clara & S. José release criminal Indians (named Pitea, Mateo, Estanislao) on *caución juratoria* to the pueblo's Juzgado — the friars exercising arbitrary powers that 'autorizan… los repetidos delitos que… cometen los indígenas,' to the Territory's insecurity

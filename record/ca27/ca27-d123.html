Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 123

'Cuarto documento.' Heading: '1825 Marzo 14 Acapulco / J. M. E (sin direccion) (sic) / Insiste en el préstamo al Bergantin Morelos'. Encloses the original oficio and two copies sent the previous day to the Acapulco commissary 'insistiendo en el préstamo que pretende de la cantidad que conduce á Califa para la pronta salida del Bergn Morelos, sobre cuyo negocio espera le diga lo que le paresca' [= insisting on the loan he seeks out of the sum being conveyed to California for the Morelos's prompt departure, on which business he awaits his opinion]. An appended note ('A continuacion', same date) reports the commissary's refusal, continuing onto n116: 'insiste en que no está en sus facultades de prestar legalmte sin traspasar los límites que le prescribe su empleo, á pesar de que conoce la urgencia de la salida del Morelos' [= he insists it is not within his powers to lend lawfully without exceeding the limits his office prescribes, although he knows the urgency of the Morelos's departure]; he would assent only 'cuando diera una órden superior, aplicable á las circunstancias en que se halla, ú otro arbitrio equivalente, capaz de cubrir su responsabilidad' [= given a superior order applicable to the circumstances, or an equivalent expedient capable of covering his responsibility]. Ends '= J. M. E. (sic)' followed by the bundle's source cite. Spans n115-n116.

Solís's own first-person account of the revolt's military collapse (Savage: 'no tiene principio')

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 306

Solís's own first-person account of the revolt's military collapse (Savage: 'no tiene principio'): learning Capt. Portilla was sent with 130 men to surprise them, and defenseless for lack of munitions, he spiked the cannon and retreated toward Monterey; could enter neither the fortified Santa Bárbara presidio nor the loopholed mission, and realized they were losing; despite ~6 deserting to the General he claims 100+ men still

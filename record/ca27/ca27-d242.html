Order to capture the American frigate Franklin, accused of smuggling

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 242

Order to capture the American frigate Franklin, accused of smuggling: she resisted and attempted to flee; all presidio commanders, if she touches any coastal point, to seize her by any means, take exact note & report; foreign goods in deposit not to be released until further order

Reached the Río Colorado the 15th after a painful 13-day march

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 94

Reached the Río Colorado the 15th after a painful 13-day march; the beasts too worn to cross, rested 2 days, then built balsas (rafts) for the mule-packs; the river Indians offered peace/war against the Yuma and, a captain brought in, 'quedaron todos de Paz' — resolves the 1825 second phase

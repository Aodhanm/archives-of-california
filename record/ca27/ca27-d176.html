Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 176

Punitive-expedition & incident report: a man's escopeta burst (skull fractured, brain matter exposed), treated by the two surgeons of the 'Fragata Inglesa Científica' (likely HMS *Blossom*, Beechey) and given the last rites; a small cannon + 2 shotguns captured from a launch; the troop lacks cueras, so requests 300 gamuzas from the 6 missions to make 30 cueras + 30 adargas; of 44 Indian prisoners, the women to remain at Mission San José, the men brought to the Presidio ⚠ author/date lost to the n157 defect

Monterey Municipal Ordinances (Ross–RAC dossier c)

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 244

Monterey Municipal Ordinances (Ross–RAC dossier c): the ayuntamiento's 15-article police code — Art 1 religion/Mass-attendance fines; Art 2 no feast-day aguardiente sales before Mass; gaming, gatherings, arms/pistol-fire curfew; Art 14 non-workers declared vagos → public works; Art 15 bars taking pawned pledges; binds all militia/disabled save those in active service ⚠ date corrected 1826→1828 (2026-07-15 leaf re-exam, n226): the heading year-digit is ambiguous (6/8 in this hand), but the doc is filed entirely among 1828 records (docs 231/232/234/235), so 1828-12-06 is the better reading — date_confidence set to inferred.

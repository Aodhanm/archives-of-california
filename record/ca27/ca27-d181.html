Decree forming the Ayuntamiento of Santa Bárbara: its electoral procedure

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 181

Decree forming the Ayuntamiento of Santa Bárbara: its electoral procedure — 9 electors named Sun 24 Dec (per the 4 Nov Bando), who then name an Alcalde, 2 Regidores & a Síndico Procurador on 31 Dec; officeholder qualifications (citizen over 25, 5 yrs' vecindad, literate, no competing jurisdiction); elections preceded by public rogation; published as a Bando

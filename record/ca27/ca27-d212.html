Petition on Jedediah Smith's behalf (Jedediah Smith dossier, doc B): the govt has nothing to fear

Departmental State Papers, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 27, document 212

Petition on Jedediah Smith's behalf (Jedediah Smith dossier, doc B): the govt has nothing to fear; Smith is no troop captain but of his 'Compañía de Cazadores de Castor' — the firm Smith, Jackson y Sublette — holding a U.S. Government beaver-hunting license 'de suficiente garantía'; asks leave to reside in the country or return to Smith (Savage: original in English)

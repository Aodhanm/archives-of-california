Verbatim heading: '1843 En.o 4 Méx. / Director Gen. de Rentas - al Admor de la Ad.a de Mont. / Nombram.to de Vice-Cónsul de Gran Bretaña en Cal.' Gloss: appointment of a British vice-consul in California. Savage's abstract verbatim: 'Le notifica que el nombram.to hecho por el Ministro de G. Bret.a en Méx. de James Forbes p.a Vice-Cónsul de su nacion en Cal., ha obtenido la aprob.n del Sup.mo Gob.o - de cuyo hecho se ha dado conoc.to en 29 Dic. 1842, al Gob.or del Dep.to de Cal.s, p.a que se le reconozca en su carácter consular, y se le permita el ejercicio de sus funciones, guardándole las prerogativas que le corresponden.' Gloss: the appointment of James Forbes by the British Minister in Mexico as vice-consul in California has the supreme government's approval, notice having gone on 29 Dec 1842 to the governor of the Department of the Californias to recognise him in his consular character, permit the exercise of his functions and preserve his prerogatives. Fixes the Mexican approval of the Forbes vice-consulship to 29 Dec 1842 and its transmission to the Monterey custom house on 4 Jan 1843.

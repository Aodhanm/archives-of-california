A jurisdiction/punishment dispute over an Indian 'Román'

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 726

A jurisdiction/punishment dispute over an Indian 'Román': the officer Lázaro Piña (hunting deserters) had him tied to a stake and given 'cinco balazos por detrás', and the San Rafael administrator 'Timoteo' [Timothy Murphy] disclaimed military/civil authority to apprehend an Indian after the fact (p.450–1); + ⭐ Guerrero remits in 6 útiles the información sumaria against the captain & supercargo of the brig *Corsair* — a contraband prosecution begun by the Juez de Paz.

Police ordinance issued jointly with the judge of first instance. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 466

Police ordinance issued jointly with the judge of first instance. Verbatim: 'Publica por Bando y de acuerdo con el Juez de 1a Insta. una providencia sobre policia q. prohibe el Juego de naipes, y la venta de licores en dias festivos antes de misa; y matar reses dentro de la poblacion.' Gloss: bans card-gambling, liquor sales on feast days before mass, and slaughtering cattle inside the town. Marginal mark '+'.

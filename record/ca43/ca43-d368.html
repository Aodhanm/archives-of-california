Marginal mark 'v.' Heading: '1834.- Enero 25.- Mejico.' Abstract: 'Decreto del Cong.o - declarando subsistente la parte 2.a del Art. 1.o de la Ley de 23 de Junio de 1833; hasta tanto, no la derogase el Congreso Gral.' [gloss: declares the second part of art. 1 of the law of 23 June 1833 still in force until Congress should repeal it]. Foxing over the last two lines; 'Junio' verified under magnification. Cf. the 7[?] May 1834 decree on n110 which repeals that very clause.

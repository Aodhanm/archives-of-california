Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 616

spans n271-n272. Headed 'Trascribe la Comunicacion relativa á la Prision y Conducion á Méjico de unos Estrangs el año 1840' [= Graham affair]. Copies the Ministerio de lo Interior order of 23 Sept: of the prisoners at Tepic taken there by Capt. José Castro in the national bark la Guipuzcoana, 'los llamados Ysac Graham, Alberto Morris, Guillermo Chard, y Jorge José Bonill' remain jailed under that court; the rest, if unnaturalized or not married to Mexican women, are to be expelled and barred from returning to California; those married to Mexicans go free on bond, staying at Tepic.

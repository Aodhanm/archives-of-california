Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 924

Heading (marginal +): 1844 = Junio 11 = Monterrey / M.a Ant.o Albisu al Gob.r [surname reading Albisu, possibly Alviso; the abbreviation of the given name reads Ant.o though the petitioner is a woman]. Text: Solicita, separe a su hija Josefa de Santiago Stoc con quien esta amancebada desde 7 anos. Gloss: a mother petitions the Governor to separate her daughter Josefa from Santiago Stoc, with whom she has been living in concubinage for seven years. [The surname after Santiago is written with a horizontal stroke over the final letters and the reading is uncertain; identification with Santiago Stokes of Monterey would be inference, not established.] This whole leaf (hand 53) is uncatalogued.

In view of the superior resolution on yesterday's consulta about naming a sub-prefect in the Pueblo de San José de Alvarado, the Prefectura has named for the post C. Antonio Suñol, who has the necessary qualities. 'La Prefectª ha nombrado pª el Cargo indicado, al C. Antº Suñol quien reasume las Cualidades nesesarias.'

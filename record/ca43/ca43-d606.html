Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 606

spans n265-n266. Full text of the Supremo Poder Conservador's declaration of the national will, Mexico 10 Oct 1840, in 3 points: 1º judicial employees are not covered by attribution 23 of art. 17 of the 4th Constitutional Law nor may interinos replace the suspended; 2º the President's 3-day arrest power (art. 18) is not to be extended to 30 days nor to other political authorities; 3º pending the reforms the govt may give commissions to individuals otherwise barred. 'No es voluntad de la Nacion que se haga estencivo á los empleados de justicia la atribucion 23ª...'

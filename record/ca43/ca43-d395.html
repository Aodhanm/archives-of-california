'Decreto del Cong.o declarando no admitirse la renuncia q. el General D. Ant.o Lopez de Santa Anna hace la Presid.a de la Republica - permitiendosele separarse del Gob.o por el tpo. necesario p.a restablecer su salud.' = refuses Santa Anna's resignation of the presidency but grants him leave from the government for as long as needed to recover his health.

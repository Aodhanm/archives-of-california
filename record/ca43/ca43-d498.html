Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 498

'Publica la Comunicacion de Pico, en q.e le trascribe la del Sup.mo Gob.o haciendo saber su nombram.to de Gobernador Propietario del Departam.to' = Escamilla publishes Pico's communication transcribing the supreme government's notice of his appointment as full governor. Marginal '+'. Title after Escamilla's name reads as a capital with superscript 'te' - 'P.te' (Presidente of the Ayuntamiento) or 'A.te' [uncertain].

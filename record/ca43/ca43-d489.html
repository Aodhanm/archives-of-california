the anti-Micheltorena revolt — Monterey under martial law

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 489

the anti-Micheltorena revolt — Monterey under martial law: the 'ciudad… regida por la Ley marcial,' declared in 'estado de sitio por los sublevados,' Comandante Abrego charged with its defense with the battalions/troops left him, calling the vecinos to arms & evacuating them, ordering citizens to guard their homes & keep public order (per Micheltorena's delegated faculties)

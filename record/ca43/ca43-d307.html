First document of Tomo III. 'Decreto del Cong. Gral

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 307

First document of Tomo III. 'Decreto del Cong. Gral. secularizando las Misiones de la República.' [= decree of the General Congress secularizing the missions of the Republic]. Marginal mark 'v'. This is the national secularization law of 17 [16 as given] April 1834 that underlies Figueroa's California program.

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 149

Spans n36-n37. At the request of Count Markoff on behalf of the Emperor of Russia, orders 'á todos los Capitanes generales, Comtes, prefectos, admres coloniales, agentes y oficiales militares y civiles, auxilien dichos buques en todo lo que puedan, sea que se presenten juntos ó por separado' [= all French officials to assist the Russian-American Company ships under Rezanov bound for Russian North America, together or singly]. Savage notes the bound original is mis-paginated.

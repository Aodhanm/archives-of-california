'Manda publicar dec.o del Cong.o que dice:' — tobacco decree, arts. 1-9 transcribed; spans n70-n71. Art. 1: 'La siembra y espendio del tabaco será libre por parte del Gob.no G.l' [planting and sale of tobacco free of government monopoly]; art. 2 importation of foreign tobacco, leaf or manufactured, remains prohibited; arts. 3, 4 and 5 noted by Savage as 'no referentes á Calif.a'; art. 6 leaf or manufactured tobacco entering the District and Territories pays 15 per cent consumption duty on valuation at market prices; art. 7 not relating to California; art. 8 tobacco free of export duty; art. 9 leaf tobacco pays neither tithes nor primicias.

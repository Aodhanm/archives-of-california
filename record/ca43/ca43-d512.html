'Bando sobre arreglo de Rondas en dias de fiesta.' [Bando regulating the night patrols on feast days.] Preamble: the commission charged with settling 'el orden con que deben sistemarse las rondas de los vecinos de este Pueblo en dias de fiesta para evitar desordenes' found no authority but tomo 1 of Lic. Marcos Gutierrez, apend. 2 n.o 40 p. 342, and Salazar, Consejo cap. 35 p. 380. Arts.: 1, vecinos de buen porte must serve when named by the Comisarios de Policía, mustering at 8 at night at the alcalde's house, rondas of 2, 4 or more under the alcalde or regidor; 2, the alcalde to coordinate hours with the Comandante Militar for the night patrol; 3, rondas to enforce arts. 5, 6, 10, 12 of the bando de buen gobierno; 4, the Comandante to pass the santo seña to the alcalde on feast days; 5, extraordinary occurrences reported at once to alcalde and military patrols; 6, arms to be left at the casa consistorial; 7, rotation of command. Spans n227-n230.

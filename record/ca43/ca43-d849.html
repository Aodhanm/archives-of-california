Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 849

Verbatim: 'Mayo 13 Manda asegurar la perzona de Enrique Sepulveda, hasta la oportunidad de poderlo remitir a los Angeles'. Gloss: orders Enrique Sepulveda held in custody until he can be sent to Los Angeles. Continued by his own petition at n370 (p.605).

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 872

ANCHORED ON n382, TEXT RUNS ONTO n383. Heading verbatim (bottom of n382): '1827 = Setiembre 11 = Monterrey / J.é M.a Echeandia á J.é Castro'. Entry verbatim, beginning on n382 and completed on n383: 'Que mandó publicar un bando sobre que... fueron nombrados diputados provisionales á la 2.ma diputacion, Franc.o Pacheco, Juan Rocha, José Castro, Estevan Manrai y Guadalupe Vallejo.' Gloss: a bando ordered published announcing the provisional deputies named to the diputación: Francisco Pacheco, Juan Rocha, José Castro, Estevan Manrai (almost certainly Savage rendering Esteban Munrás, an inference) and Guadalupe Vallejo. The ordinal in 2.ma diputacion is written thus on the leaf.

'Publica por bando un acuerdo de la Asamblea, relativa a la rendicion de cuentas de las Misiones y concesion de terrenos contiguos a estas; carta de libertad a los neofitos &.' = Pico as interim governor publishes an Assembly accord on the rendering of mission accounts, on grants of land adjacent to the missions, and on letters of liberty for the neophytes. Marginal '+'. Secretary's surname reads 'Bal[n]dini' - probably Juan Bandini [?].

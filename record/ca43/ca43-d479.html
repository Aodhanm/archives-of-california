Verbatim: 'Circular de la Acta de la Asamblea Dptal. de 15 del corriente mes

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 479

Verbatim: 'Circular de la Acta de la Asamblea Dptal. de 15 del corriente mes. (impresa.)' — the word 'Set.' is written and struck out before 'corriente'. Gloss: printed circular of the act of the Departmental Assembly of 15 August 1844. Marked '(Cuadrup.)' and '(Vall. Docs.)'. Marginal mark 'v.' See the duplicate entry at n190 (p. 272), to which Savage himself cross-refers.

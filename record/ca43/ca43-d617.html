Second discrete document inside the same Savage abstract (no separate cite; shares pp. 187-90). Inserting the supreme order above, the prefect orders each justice of the peace to watch scrupulously, under his strictest responsibility, that no class of foreigner lacking the documents required by law enters his demarcation, reporting at once to the prefecture. 'no se introduscan en la demarcacion de su mando ninguna clase de Estrangeros que carescán de los documtos prevenidos por las Leyes.'

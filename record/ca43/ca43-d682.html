Frees the vecino Felipe Vásquez (p.340); then a 1845 cluster

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 682

Frees the vecino Felipe Vásquez (p.340); then a 1845 cluster: the robbery of soldier Manuel Soto (p.341–2); ⭐ pursuit of a fugitive Indian (p.343–4); ⭐ the Comandancia's regret at the assassination by Indians at the Rancho de las Pozas of Bernabé García (p.345–6); on soldier-committed robberies in nearby settlements, he authorizes the vecinos to apprehend the culprits (p.347–8).

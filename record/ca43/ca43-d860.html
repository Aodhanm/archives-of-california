Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 860

Heading verbatim: '1844 Nov.re 23 Monterey / Juan Malarin al Juez de 1.a Instancia'. Entry verbatim: 'Ordena se ponga en libertad absoluta a Jose M.a Bravo en atencion al tiempo que ha estado detenido'. Gloss: orders the unconditional release of Jose Maria Bravo in view of the time he has already been held. Belongs with the Tribunal run against arbitrary detention (cf. the Pablo Juan ruling, p.638-9).

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 631

Land-denouncement file rejected. Verbatim (Savage's whole abstract): 'Expediente de denuncio de terrenos no admitida'. Gloss: the government returns/refuses to admit a denuncio (claim/denunciation) file on vacant land forwarded by the interim prefect of the 2nd District; Savage gives no claimant or tract name.

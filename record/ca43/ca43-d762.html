A national judicial-oversight decree (signed Santa Anna & Justice-Minister Baranda)

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 762

A national judicial-oversight decree (signed Santa Anna & Justice-Minister Baranda): judges to render monthly account to the Superior Tribunals with the *actas* of the month's trials, faults in procedure to be examined & responsibility demanded (p.509); ⭐ the Juez leaves for the Rancho de las Calabazas to gather data on the poisoning of Juan Romero by his wife (*envenenamiento… por su mujer*) and secure the minors' interests (p.592–3).

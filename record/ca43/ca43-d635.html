Verbatim (Savage's whole abstract): 'Comunicacio [sic] relativa a la anterior'. Gloss: the prefect's reply on the same business as the preceding item, i.e. acknowledging the appointment of the interim Secretary of the Despacho. Savage gives no further text. Marginal '+'.

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 475

spans n185-n186. Verbatim opening: 'El Y. Ayuntamto. deseando el buen orden y policia de esta Capital en Secion del dia 29 del mes ppo., de acuerdo con el Exmo. Sr. Gobernador de este Departamto. ha tenido á bien disponer los arts. siguientes.' Arts.: 1o no riding horseback inside the town, 1 peso fine; 2o no driving loose slaughter cattle or lassoing them inside town, to be done before entering; 3o smaller stock to be kept one league outside; 4o at most one or two penned dogs per house, loose dogs may be killed but not with firearms; 5o mud-pits dug for masonry beside houses to be filled within 15 days. Marginal mark 'o'.

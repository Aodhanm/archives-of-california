Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 507

'Decreto prohibiendo ejersan su profesion los facultativos en Medicina, sin exivir previam.te su diploma respectivo.' Preamble: '...el abuso con q.e los facultativos en Medicina y cirujia gravan á los Ciud.s exigiendoles ecsivas y esorvitantes pagas por su asistencia y medicinas...' Art. 1 diplomas to be shown to ayuntamientos/jueces; art. 2 fee ceiling — one peso a visit for the rich, four reales for middling, nothing for the notoriously poor nor for soldiers from sergeant down where there is no military doctor; art. 3 medicines priced by agreement, disputes to the juez; art. 4 amputations/deliveries/anatomies capped at 25 pesos, 12 for middling, nothing for the poor; art. 5 penalties 10/20/30 pesos and expulsion from the pueblo for repeat offenders.

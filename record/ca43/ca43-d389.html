'Decreto del Cong.o erigiendo en Ciudad el pueblo de los Ang.s de la Alta Cal

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 389

'Decreto del Cong.o erigiendo en Ciudad el pueblo de los Ang.s de la Alta Cal. y en Capital del Territorio.' = erects the pueblo of Los Angeles into a city AND into the capital of the Territory of Alta California. The single most consequential Californian item in this run; the capital transfer was never effectively carried out, and Monterey business continues through this volume. Marginal 'v.'.

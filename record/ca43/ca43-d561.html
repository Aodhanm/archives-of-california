Land-concession communications (the paraje 'el Tucho,' between vecinos); the 16 Sept celebration; ⭐ the Governor orders a march out against Indian horse-thieves (per the auxilio requested by vecino Antonio Germán) until the stolen *caballada* is recovered (p.101–2). Genuine 1840 Monterey-district governance.

Acknowledges the note of the 21st on the ban on liquor sales and card-play

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 599

Acknowledges the note of the 21st on the ban on liquor sales and card-play; reports the governor's order published by bando. 'Acusa recibo de la nota de 21 del Corrte relativa á la prohibision de venta de licores y juego de naipes, informa que la orden del Gobernador la ha mandado publicar por Bando.'

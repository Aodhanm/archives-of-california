On sales where the buyer contracts to pay the tax

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 12

On sales where the buyer contracts to pay the tax: 'debe reputarse mayor precio de la cosa vendida, la Cantidad a que ascienda aquel real derecho.' (Savage writes sic). Worked example: 'en mil pesos, Con la citada condicion, ha de regularse y exigirse la alcabala sobre el importe de mil y sesenta.' (sic) = the alcabala itself is added into the taxable price.

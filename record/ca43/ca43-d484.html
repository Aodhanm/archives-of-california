Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 484

spans n193-n195. Verbatim heading: 'Publica por Bando las medidas tomadas para castigar á Malhechores y revolucionarios de cierta azonada, modificadas con motivo de la llegada de la Goleta 'California.'' Text notes the pretext of the asonada, which has 'no tener plan ni objeto, ni Gefe conosido', is the continual robberies and quarrels 'tanto de algunos pocos soldados como de los indios y gente de razon'. Arts.: 1o severe punishment of any theft, wounding or disorder by citizens military or civil; 2o damages to peaceful citizens to be indemnified from the property of the factious, per the supreme decree of the President; 3o from sunset to sunrise none may ride in the town, riders to be fired on and, if taken alive, fined $50 or prison; 4o all property under government guarantee; 5o supplies requisitioned to be paid at the owner's will. Marginal mark 'o'.

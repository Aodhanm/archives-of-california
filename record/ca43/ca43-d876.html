STARRED. Heading verbatim: '1831 = Diciembre 21 = Angeles / J.é M.a Echeandia al Ayunt.to de Mont.y'. Entry verbatim: 'Que Manuel Victoria ya Caminó para embarcarse.' Gloss: Echeandía notifies the Monterey ayuntamiento from Los Angeles that Manuel Victoria has already set out to take ship, that is, the deposed governor is on his way out of California after the revolt of 1831. Savage gives the piece a long page run, p. 34-40, so enclosures are likely.

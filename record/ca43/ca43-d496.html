Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 496

spans n211-n212. 'Bando: previniendo ciertas medidas de defensa, con motivo de una partida de aventureros capitaneada por Meric q.e se apodero el 14 del corriente del Comand.te Militar de Sonoma (D. Mariano G. Vallejo.)' = Bear Flag Revolt. Art. 1: jueces de campo and auxiliaries to be given certified copies of the Prefecture's communication and to publish it so citizens, aware of the danger to 'nuestras garantias sociales y libertades publicas', volunteer to defend 'nuestra cara patria.' Art. 2: every man with horses to furnish them by ten tomorrow against the judge's receipt, plus saddles, arms and ammunition. Art. 3: jueces de campo to collect the aid and list each donor. 'Meric' = Ezekiel Merritt [probable, not certain].

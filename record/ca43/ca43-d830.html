Marked. Heading: 'Sobre derecho de consumo.' Order of the Gob.o Sup.mo 'aumentando hasta 15% el derecho que antes se pagaba de 5% sobre efectos estrangeros, por derecho de consumo' [the consumption duty on foreign goods raised from 5 per cent to 15 per cent]. A threefold increase in the levy on imported goods at Monterey.

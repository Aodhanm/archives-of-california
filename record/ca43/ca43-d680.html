Undated: by order of Comte. Gral. Micheltorena, form a Compañía de auxiliares for the security of this point, recruitment under Capitán de auxiliares Santiago Estrada (p.332–3); publishes the Soberano decreto of 30 Jun on the half-renovation of the Cámara de Diputados (p.334–5); ⭐ communication prohibiting foreign ships the retail cabotage & consumption trade ('comercio por menor de cabotage y consumo,' continues n311).

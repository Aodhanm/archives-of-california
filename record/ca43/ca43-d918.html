Heading (marginal +): 1843 = Monterrey / Micheltorena al Juez Teodoro Gonsalez. Entry: Julio 6 = Angeles. Que no puede atender a la presentacion de Salvio Pacheco. Gloss: Micheltorena replies from Los Angeles that he cannot attend to the presentation (petition) of Salvio Pacheco. Absent from the existing coarse row; connects to the Salvio Pacheco judgeship approved at n430 p. 484.

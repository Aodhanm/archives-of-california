Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 609

Orders each justice of the peace to draw up the padrón (census roll) of his demarcation on the accompanying model, as exactly as possible, since it will both fulfil the supreme order inserted and supply the material for the department's statistics. 'Orden para que procedan á formar el Padron de esa demarcacion de su mando, en un Estado, por el orden del que acompaña.'

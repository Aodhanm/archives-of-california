Verbatim heading: '1833 = Enero 14 = A bordo del bergantin 'Catalina' / José Figueroa al ayuntamiento de Monterrey.' Entry verbatim in full: 'Que con fecha 9 de Mayo último, le avisáron que el vice-presidente le nombró gefe político de la alta California.' Gloss: written from on board the brig Catalina, Figueroa notifies the Monterey ayuntamiento that on 9 May last he was informed the vice-president had named him political chief of Alta California. This is the opening piece of the whole 1833 Figueroa block that runs from here to n406.

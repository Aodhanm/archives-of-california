Orders José Arana arrested 24 hrs in the Cuartel del Batallón Permanente (p.255–6)

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 652

Orders José Arana arrested 24 hrs in the Cuartel del Batallón Permanente (p.255–6); ⭐ notes that despite the 31 Dec bando, various municipalities persist in 'el pernicioso mal que llaman *cucaracha*' [reading uncertain — a popular vice/gambling] — the Governor wants this municipality to propose how to 'poner un dique al mal' (p.257).

'Decreto del Cong.o previniendo haga el Gob. que tenga efecto el decreto de 2 de Dbre. del año ant.o dado por el Gral. Presid.te para restablecer el orden publico en el Estado de Cohahuila y Tejas...' = orders enforcement of the 2 Dec 1834 decree restoring public order in Coahuila y Texas and the consequent orders of 18 Dec 1834 and 14 Jan; meanwhile, pending Congress's ruling on Saltillo's petition to join another state, the state authorities may not use arms to reduce it. Published in Cal. 13 Feb 1836 by Gutiérrez.

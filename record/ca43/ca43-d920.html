Same heading block (1843 = Angeles / Micheltorena al Prefecto). Entry: Junio 6 = Que se nombren soldados aquellos que no hagan falta a sus familias. Gloss: those men whose families can spare them are to be enrolled as soldiers. A recruitment criterion for Micheltorena military levy in California.

Prisoner-rights ruling: orders a detainee freed at once and reprimands arbitrary detention

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 764

Prisoner-rights ruling: orders a detainee freed at once and reprimands arbitrary detention — the law forbids holding anyone 'más de 3 días sin declararlo bien preso'; henceforth account of every *causa* within 3 days of its start, per art. 99 of the 10 Jun 1837 decree (p.638–9); + a demanda against the Alcalde 1º de Branciforte for *injurias* to the wife of Rafael Castro, to be tried with witnesses & Castro's apoderado (p.640–1).

3 days of mourning ordered by the Sup. Govt (25–27 Feb, p.72–73); ⭐ Congress having closed without resolving the petitions to 'variar la forma de Gobierno,' Gutiérrez invites measures, by the President's order, to avoid 'toda perturbación pública' (pre-centralism tension, p.74–75); approves $6 for the Alcalde 2º's *bastón* (p.76).

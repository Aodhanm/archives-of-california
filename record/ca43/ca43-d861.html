Heading verbatim: 'Juan Santiago Madox a Jose Z. Fernand.z Juez de Paz (Carta particular) sin f.ha'. Entry verbatim: 'Solicita su libertad.' Gloss: an undated private letter from Juan Santiago Madox, whose surname is Anglo (Maddox), to the juez de paz Jose Z. Fernandez, asking for his release. Filed by Savage as a carta particular, not an official piece.

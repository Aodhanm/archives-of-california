Early customs accounting (bound out of order)

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 687

Early customs accounting (bound out of order): the 6% export duty on the cargo the English brig *'Hevel'* [Savage's spelling] loaded for the Missions of Santa Clara & San José — tallow (*botas de sebo*) & hides (*cueros*); a detailed 'A Saber' duty ledger (Sta Clara 61 botas @614@; S. José 101 botas @1,154½@; 540 hides; Total de Derechos; líquido al fondo de arbitrios 365). Foreign-ship mission-produce export.

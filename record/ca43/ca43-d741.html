Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 741

Asks for a room of the casa principal (or the Mayordomo's aid with paid Indians) to make a secure locale for the Juzgado archive (p.492–3); ⭐ a *carta particular* begging to resign the Juzgado ('muy cansado') and reporting that Coppinger's sawmill 'no es otra cosa que un abrevadero de Extrangeros desertores de los buques' (nothing but a watering-hole of foreign ship-deserters, p.494); ⭐ remits a new list of foreigners found illegally 'internados en el paraje de la Sierra Morena.' → foreign-infiltration / Open-Door.

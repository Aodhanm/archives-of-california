Acknowledges notice that the death sentence of the reo Manuel González was carried out (p.566)

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 758

Acknowledges notice that the death sentence of the reo Manuel González was carried out (p.566); ⭐ Tribunal circular to the subordinate civil & criminal juzgados that the sentences they pronounce be read to the reos in full, 'sin omitir una sola coma,' from the first procedure to devolution — the acts bearing 'el sello de la pureza,' so the condemned are 'persuaded of the justice… on which their sentences are founded' (p.567–8). Due-process/legitimacy doctrine.

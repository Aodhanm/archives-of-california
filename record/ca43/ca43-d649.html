The Secretaría has no more documents on Amesti & Hernández (p.247)

Departmental State Papers, Monterey, tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 43, document 649

The Secretaría has no more documents on Amesti & Hernández (p.247); ⭐ against robberies in the vecindad 'que se dice hacen los militares,' the Governor orders a nightly civilian patrol — a Regidor + a vecino accompanying the Jefe del día to *rondar la Población* and pursue malefactors, vecinos rotating by seniority (p.248–49).

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 182

Alférez José Mª Aguilar, earlier ordered to remain at the Pueblo de Santiago, is recalled to report to the Comandancia: the outposts near La Paz report 'que han llegado 2 fragatas enemigas que estan desembarcando gente para batirse' [2 enemy frigates have arrived and are landing men to fight]; he marches instead on the Pueblo de Todos Santos to join Alférez Manuel Calderón, 'que se halla con 40 hombres en dicho Pueblo'.

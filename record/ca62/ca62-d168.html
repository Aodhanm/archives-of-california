Orders scrupulous watch over the jurisdiction, any disturbance remedied and offenders remitted to the Comandancia General. 'Avisa q. marcha el Alf.z Ayudante D. Manuel Calderon Comand.te de la Comp.a Presid.l de Loreto, con 40 hom.s de su Comp.a para cuidar del orden y tranquilidad Pub.a' [= Ensign-adjutant Manuel Calderón, commander of the Loreto presidial company, is marching with 40 of his men to keep public order]; the alcalde is to furnish them the supplies needed and to explain to the vecinos why the force has come.

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 226

On arriving he found the order withdrawing the officer and garrison to San Antonio and leaving him (Moreno) in their place. “al formar esta guerrilla fue Con recursos que le facilitaron los particulares que los tenian, á fin de hacer la guerra al enemigo, y no Como su subalterno” — he raised the guerrilla on private means, not as anyone's subordinate, and will not stay longer than a reply takes; urges “que se deje de tertulias, y que trabajen juntos en defenza de el Territorio”.

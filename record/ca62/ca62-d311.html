Safe-conduct for 4 Cocomaricopa (Maricopa) Indians «de la Nación Cocomaricopa» carrying an executive dispatch from Sonora to the Comte. Gen. of Alta California — indigenous couriers crossing the Colorado/Gila frontier; asks JPs en route give them free passage & lawful aid. (+ brief: 1828-06-20, José Antonio Carrillo, juez 1er juzgado, Los Ángeles.) → Indian history / cross-frontier communication.

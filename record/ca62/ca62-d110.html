spans n152-n153. Acknowledges the comandante's nota of the 29th and the Proclama of the 3rd with the partes of the fighting against the invading forces, and published them. 'Este vecindario ha visto Con jubilo el triunfo obtenido Contra el enemigo, sintiendo no tener parte en esa gloriosa jornada' [the town rejoiced at the victory, regretting it had no part in it]. The vecindario had already enrolled as a guerrilla 'bajo las ordenes de su gefe Ciudo Matias Moreno en Numd de 36. que se hallan acuartelados' [36 men quartered under Jose Matias Moreno], awaiting arms and parque, wholly lacking in these pueblos.

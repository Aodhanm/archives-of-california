Calendar entry 'Dbre 5' under a heading giving only the year and place. The enemy 'sale fuera de sus parapetos á provocar una accion'; he asks the Jefe to reinforce with the 'fuerza de Infantª Mulegina', which joined to those under his orders would form the line of defenders on this side, 'equipando á cada soldado con 3 paradas de Cartuchos', maintenance and clothing left to his discretion, and to send at the same time '10 ó 12 armas de fuego, para con ellas acabar de armar 50 hombres'.

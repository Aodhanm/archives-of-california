Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 117

spans n157-n158. Transcribes a communication showing his comandancia was deceived by Padre Sotomayor, who had assured the comandancias generales of Sonora and Sinaloa that he had 3 guerrillas ready for the defence of the Peninsula and would sacrifice his interests to keep them. Reports 'que el R. P. Soto Mayor ha servido de obstaculo a la defenza del Pais, dejandolo en desavenencias y llendose a Sonora tan solo por el terror que le han Causado los Yankes' [Sotomayor obstructed the defence and fled to Sonora out of terror of the Yankees], though his troops never took a single head of his cattle, 'por que las tropas se mantienen Con un pedazo de Carne cada 24 horas' [the troops live on one piece of meat every 24 hours].

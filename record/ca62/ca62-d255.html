First entry under the calendar heading '+1847 - Santiago / Mauricio Castro a Manl. Pineda Comandte'. Acknowledges Pineda's despatch reporting reinforcements: 'Acusa recibo la Gefatura, su comunicacion fha 27 del presente, en que le notifica la llegada de 100 hombres de infanta y 2 piezas de Artilla para la defenza del Pais' [= acknowledges receipt of his communication of the 27th notifying the arrival of 100 infantrymen and 2 artillery pieces for the defence of the country].

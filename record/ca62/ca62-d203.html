Calendar-style entry dated 'Novre 19' under one correspondent heading. 'En este momento en union de la fuerza del Sr Comandte de la guerrilla Guadalupana de Comondu esperan batirse con el enemigo que impunemente se ha apoderado de S José' [they expect to engage the enemy who has seized San José with impunity]; the same day he circularised all the authorities to stand ready and lose no time against the enemy.

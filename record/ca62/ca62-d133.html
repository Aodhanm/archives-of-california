Reports he reached Vicente Mejía at 6 in the evening

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 133

Reports he reached Vicente Mejía at 6 in the evening: 'su fuerza se compone de 100 hombres, que esperan de Todos Santos 30 ó 40, que esta tarde Comienza á hostilizar al enemigo que esta fortificado con 25 hombres y 2 piezas de Artilla en la Casa Cural' [his force is 100 men, 30 or 40 more expected from Todos Santos; this afternoon he begins to harass the enemy, fortified with 25 men and 2 guns in the parish house].

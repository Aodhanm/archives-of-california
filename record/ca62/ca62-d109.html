spans n151-n152. Strength and ammunition return. 'Remite parte de la fuerza que ecsiste dice; que el Comandta principal y 1er ge[fe] se tendra 150 hombres, con los que se halla hostilizando al enemigo' [returns the force on hand; principal comandante and first chief will have 150 men, with whom he is harassing the enemy]. Mexican losses only '3 hombres heridos levemente' against 'porcion de muertos y heridos' for the enemy; state of supply 'solo le quedan 20 tiros de Canon, y 2000 tiros de fucil' [only 20 cannon rounds and 2,000 musket cartridges left].

First entry under the calendar heading '+1848 Mulege / Raimundo Gutierrez Comte into al Comte Pral'. The Captain of the Guardia Nacional 'Vicente Mejia' has arrived saying that by verbal order of the Comandante Pral he came to take charge of the Comandancia of this pueblo; as his superior he must hand it over, regretting that the Comandancia gave no timely notice, and assuring 'no habra ninga discusion para no perturbar el orden Publico'.

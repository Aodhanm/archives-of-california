Funding California's diputado to the Mexican Congress

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 299

Funding California's diputado to the Mexican Congress: no public funds to cover the *elecciones de Diputados*; the 4,000 pesos anuales agreed «en la junta» to subsidize the deputy given «la gran distancia que tiene que viajar»; discusses how to subvene the Alta-California representation. → (Repertoire of Self-Rule — CA's federal representation).

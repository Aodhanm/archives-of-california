1830 elections — the partido-elector roster by district

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 322

1830 elections — the partido-elector roster by district: Juan Malarín (Monterey), Luis Peralta (San José Gpe.), Domingo Carrillo (Sta Bárbara), José Antonio Carrillo (Los Ángeles), Juan María Osuna (San Diego), José Peña (San Francisco); a Peralta-credential defect noted («no consta literalmente la acta… por falta de las otras actas anteriores»). Territorial Deputation (4 Oct): propietarios Mariano G. Vallejo, José Antonio Ortega, Antonio María Osio; suplentes Francisco de Haro, Tomás Yorba, Santiago Argüello..

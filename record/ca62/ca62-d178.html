Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 178

A second, independently paged copy of the same 21 Jan. 1848 order (cf. n192, p. 141-42): vigilance over the jurisdiction, accomplices secured and remitted, and 'Que marcha el Alferez Ayudante Man.l Calderon Comand.te de la Comp.a Presidial de Loreto Con 40 hombres, p.a Cuidar del orden y tranquilidad publica' [= Calderón marching with 40 men of the Loreto presidial company to keep public order]; the pueblo is to be told beforehand the troops come for no other purpose, 'para que todos esten tranquilos en sus Casas'.

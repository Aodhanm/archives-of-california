Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 128

Orders the juez to assemble the vecinos of his jurisdiction and 'forme una Compañia de guardia nacional, nombrando ellos mismos sus oficiales Sargentos y Cabos, que los deban mandar' [the men themselves naming the officers, sergeants and corporals who are to command them]; to collect all armament and munitions in the jurisdiction to arm them at once, and to march a further force to S. Antº to await orders. Spans n164-n165.

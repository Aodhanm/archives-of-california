His repeated orders and those of the Jefe Político to send maize, and the quantities carried off by…

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 196

His repeated orders and those of the Jefe Político to send maize, and the quantities carried off by various persons, 'han dejado al Pueblo sin un grano'; but 'le cabe la satisfaccion de que los Vecinos lo dejan gustosos de comer, pr dar este auxilio á los defensores del Pais' [the vecinos gladly go without food to supply the defenders of the country].

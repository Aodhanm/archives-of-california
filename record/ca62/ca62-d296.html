1809 report of a frigate anchored at the PUERTO DE BODEGA

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 296

1809 report of a frigate anchored at the PUERTO DE BODEGA: Moraga tells the governor that the SF-mission neophytes returning from «la otra banda del estero» reported «una fragata fondeada en el Puerto de Bodega», its launch working up & down the estero de San Rafael — early documentation of the Russian approach to Bodega (Kuskov era, eve of Fort Ross) → +. (+ Abella's 1809 mission Easter-communion certificate.)

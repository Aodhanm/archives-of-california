2o of the stack. 'Pide le remita lo 5 individuos estrangeros q. habian llegado de la Magdalena á aquel pueblo (T. Santos) á fin de hacer una aclaratoria de dhos. individuos por la Comand.a p.a saber á q. venian y cuales eran las minas q. traian' [= send up the 5 foreigners who had come from Magdalena to Todos Santos so the Comandancia can establish what they came for and what mines they were working]. 'estrangeros' is interlined above the line.

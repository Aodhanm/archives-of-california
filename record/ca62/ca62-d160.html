Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 160

Vargas transcribes guerrilla chief Fr. Vicente Soto Mayor's dispatch of 24 Dbre. ultimo: 'hallandose autorizado por las Comand.s Grales. de Sonora y Sinaloa p.a combatir al invasor Norte Americano, le ordenaba regresar p.a Loreto á reunirse con el Comand.te Gral.' [= being authorized by the comandancias-general of Sonora and Sinaloa to fight the North American invader, he was ordered back to Loreto to join the comandante general], there to draw the enemy's attention and harass him as convenient.

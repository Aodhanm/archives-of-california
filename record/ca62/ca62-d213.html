Replying to an order of the same date to send 6 mounted men

Archives of California, Miscellaneous, tomo — · BANC MSS C-A 62, document 213

Replying to an order of the same date to send 6 mounted men: “no apareciendo en este Juzgado ning.a ley sobre la materia, ní este pueblo sugetos que puedan empuñar, mas de solam.te los empleados” — the order should have been transcribed to him; the pueblo has no men able to bear arms beyond the officials and one or two whom “los mismos Comandantes de guerrillas” left behind in consideration of their large families, since nothing remains here for those families to live on.

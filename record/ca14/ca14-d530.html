BAJA MISSION-CHAIN REORGANIZATION. Verbatim: transmits the Virrey's 28 Dec 1793 resolution (on Arrillaga's own 17 Aug informe): missions Comondú, Purísima, Mulegé, S.n Ignacio, Sta. Gertrudis y S.n Borja continue supplying Loreto and the frontier-mission correspondence line; Santiago and Guadalupe SUPPRESSED, annexed to S.n José del Cabo and to Mulegé/Purísima.

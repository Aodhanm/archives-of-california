Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 3

Borrador. Verbatim: 'Que las cuentas q.e mando remitiese estan purificadas y p.a ello tuvo q.e recurrir a la Com.a de S.n Blas &c - p. 42-'. Gloss: the accounts the viceroy ordered remitted have been purified (audited/cleared), for which he had to apply to the Comisaria of San Blas.

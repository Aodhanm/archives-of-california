Register of official letters sent by the interim governor to the viceroy. Verbatim

Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 1

Register of official letters sent by the interim governor to the viceroy. Verbatim: 'Indice de los oficios qe el Gob. Int.o de Californias remite al Virrey de Mex, Conde de Revilla Gigedo, desde 17 de Oct.e de 1792, hasta el 9 de Set.e de 1794 - pp. 1-41-'. Gloss: index of the oficios remitted 17 Oct 1792 - 9 Sep 1794, occupying pp. 1-41 of the original tomo; Savage records only the fact of the index, not its entries. Immediately below, the section heading 'Borradores.' opens the run of draft letters that follows.

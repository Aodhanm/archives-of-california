Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 477

Heading verbatim: + 1794 - Feb.o 16 - Mont. / Arrillaga al Virrey de Mex. Body verbatim: Remitiendo ajustam.tos del Pres.o de Mont. de anos 81 a 92, un resumen gral. e indice de todos los docs. con su respectiva numeracion. - pp. 324-5. Gloss: Arrillaga forwards to the Viceroy the settlements of account of the Monterey presidio for the years 1781 to 1792, together with a general summary and an index of all the documents with their respective numbering. Value is as evidence that a numbered document index of the Monterey presidio accounts for 1781-1792 was compiled and sent in February 1794.

Mission Santa Cruz land survey: all cultivable lands irrigated

Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 355

Mission Santa Cruz land survey: all cultivable lands irrigated. Report terrain remaining for mission livestock. Viceroy may grant alternate rancho at 'Altos' [possibly Scotts Valley]. Smallpox contained — 5 sick recovered after 1 month, no spread. Land measurements forwarded to Viceroy. Report needs more thoroughness

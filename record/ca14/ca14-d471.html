VERBATIM HEADING: '+ 1793 - Setᵉ 24 - Mont. / Arrillaga al Virrey de Mex. / (2 Docs) - pp. 294-6.' GLOSS: Arrillaga to the Viceroy of Mexico, Monterey, 24 Sept 1793, two items. SUBSTANCE, verbatim: (1) 'Pide se envie una lancha esquifada, de competente arqueo, pª. trasportar maderas al Ptº. de Bodega, con el objeto de dar fin á las obras.' (2) 'Da parte, qe. en Fragata Princesa se embarcaron los 5 prisioneros ingleses, con órden de entregarlos al Com[andan]tᵉ. de Sn. Blas.' The bracketed expansion is mine: the abbreviation is written small and I could not resolve it letter by letter. Item 1 shows works at Bodega still being pushed in late Sept 1793, after Matute had refused the establishment. CROSS-REFERENCE, not a duplicate: the DB row at scan n184, p.xxii.~420, dated 1793-09-23, reports the same five English deserters shipped on the Princesa; that is a separate entry with a different page cite and date.

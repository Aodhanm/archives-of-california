Heading verbatim: + 1794 - Abril 15 - Mont. / Arrillaga a Oficiales Reales del Rosario. Body verbatim: Remite cuenta de Coleccion de diezmos de 1792, colectados en la Jurisdiccion de Sta. Barbara - p. 428. Gloss: Arrillaga to the royal officials at El Rosario, Monterey, 15 April 1794, forwarding the account of the collection of tithes for 1792 gathered in the jurisdiction of Santa Barbara. Establishes that Alta California tithe accounts were rendered to the Oficiales Reales del Rosario.

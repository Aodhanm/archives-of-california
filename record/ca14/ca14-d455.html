Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 455

«Avisando que Ignº del Corral, Capn de la Compª de Cabª de San Diego (Alta Cal.) (individuo que nunca vino á Cal. pero que muy largo tiempo apareció como Comandte de agª compª y recibiendo, probablemte, su paga en Méx.) fué ascendido á Sargto mayor del batallon lijero de infantª provincial de Méx.» [notice that Ignacio del Corral, captain of the San Diego cavalry company, was promoted to sergeant major of the Mexican provincial light infantry battalion]. The inner parenthesis is SAVAGE'S OWN editorial comment, not the document: an individual who never came to California yet long figured as commander of that company and probably drew his pay in Mexico. A struck-through parenthetical follows «de Méx.» — cancelled and illegible

SMALLPOX EPIDEMIC: Goycoechea failed to follow Viceroy's quarantine protocols

Provincial State Papers, tomo XXI–XXII · BANC MSS C-A 14, document 351

SMALLPOX EPIDEMIC: Goycoechea failed to follow Viceroy's quarantine protocols — allowed ship to dock without inspection. Viruelas on 'the other coast.' 5 passengers had smallpox; clothing/belongings burned to prevent contagion. Other precautions ordered. 'All the forms in the world wouldn't save the commandant from embarrassment' if epidemic spreads

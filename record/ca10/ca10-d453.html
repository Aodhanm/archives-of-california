Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 453

'que está entdo de la llegada á S. Francᵒ de la 'Elisa', su capⁿ Rowan, sobre-cargo J. Kendrick y de que socorrida, se despache luego, no franqueándole auxilios.' — Arrillaga acknowledges the arrival at San Francisco of the American ship Elisa [= Eliza], Capt. Rowan, supercargo J. Kendrick, and that once relieved she is to be sent off at once, no further aid granted. NB Savage spells the supercargo 'Kendrick' here (cf. 'Juan Kandrick' ~n210/n240). Spans n302–n303.

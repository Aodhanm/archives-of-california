Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 446

'que está entdo de que la 'Princesa' trae las mems pª los 4 Presᵈᵒˢ y de que se procure su pronto despacho pʳ tener comisⁿ importante.' — Goycoechea acknowledges that the supply ship Princesa is bringing the memorias (annual requisitions) for the four presidios and that she is to be dispatched promptly because she carries an important commission.

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 374

'S[e] recibió copia de órd.n del Virey, revocando su órd.n sobre detenc.n de buq.s españ.s proced.s de Asia; q.e la ha trasmitido al S. p.a su cumplim.o' — the Viceroy's order revoking his earlier order for the detention of Spanish vessels arriving from Asia has been received and passed on for compliance. Manila-trade shipping control relaxed in wartime.

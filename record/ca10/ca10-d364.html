Heading 'Sin fha = Sin lugar.' — 'Que el Gob.r le dijo q.e el rio serviría de lind.o; q.e se los dijo á los M.tros; q.e le dijeron q.e no sabían nada y q.e iban á sembrar las tierras. (Véase docum.to ant.r).' — the San José comisionado reports that the Governor told him the river would serve as the boundary; when he told the Santa Clara ministers, they answered they knew nothing of it and would go on sowing the land. Savage's cross-reference to the preceding document (p. 654).

Heading: 'Remisn de un bando'. 'Envia 2 ejemplªs de bando sobre que se eviten los obstáculos qe impiden á las mugeres y niñas, ocuparse en labores de su sexo.' — two printed copies of the bando removing obstacles to women and girls taking up the occupations of their sex (the 1799 royal decree opening trades to women). Cf. the printed bando itself abstracted at n213 / orig pp. 463-64.

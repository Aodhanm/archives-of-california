'Solicitan se les mande al indio Ambroso q.e ya tiene 3 m.s de prision'

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 345

'Solicitan se les mande al indio Ambroso q.e ya tiene 3 m.s de prision' — the two San Diego ministers jointly request the Indian Ambrosio be sent to them, noting he has already served three months' imprisonment. Longest piece of the Ambrosio dossier (4 tomo pages).

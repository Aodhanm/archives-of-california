'Fragata americana 'Loter'' = 'Abandono de once individuos ingleses'

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 24

'Fragata americana 'Loter'' = 'Abandono de once individuos ingleses': answers Borica's partes of 5/10/12 Nov + 6 Dec '96 and 6 Oct '97 on 'la Fragata mercante americana 'Loter,' su capitan Mr Dovr' [= Otter, Ebenezer Dorr]; auxilios '$187-3rs-10gs' que dho Capn 'satisfizo contra Boston, 41.12 Libras esterlinas'; 'haber abandonado 11 ingleses inclusa una muger' [= Botany Bay convicts]; expediente ordered, S.M. informed; the 11 to Veracruz for transport to Havana and home, their Court's envoy advised; Dorr's letra sent to be collected.

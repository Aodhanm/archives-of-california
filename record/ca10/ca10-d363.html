Provincial State Papers, tomo XVII · BANC MSS C-A 10, document 363

'Que p.r carta del Comis.o de S. José, verá lo q.e este dijo á M.tros de Sta Clara, tocante á linderos y lo q.e respondieron; q.e declare lo q.e convenga.' — encloses the San José comisionado's letter showing what he told the Santa Clara ministers about the boundaries and their reply; asks Borica to rule. Paired with the undated Macario de Castro letter at p. 655 (n265).

Heading 'Bando sobre Rl Orden del disenso pª matrimos'. 'Real Orden sobre que en las causas matrimoniales, cese el fuero militar (V. p 206).' — soldiers lose the military fuero in marriage-dissent suits. Savage's cross-reference '(V. p 206)' points to his own handwritten foliation 206 = leaf n208, the printed bando of the same date.

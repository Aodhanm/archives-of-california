'Padron.' Savage's own bracket: '(Este docº está incompleto por faltarle un pedazo que está roto; en el solo se cuentan 181 almas.)' Gloss: mutilated year-end 1834 census of San Rafael; 181 souls survive on the torn sheet, so the figure is a floor not a total. Same mission whose pueblo boundaries were run 1 Oct 1834 (n269).

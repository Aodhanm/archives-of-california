State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 419

Heading only, no abstract: '1834 = Nbre. 30 = Publo de la Purisima / Domingo Carrillo, comisionado, Marcos Ant.o Vitoria, Mtro. / Inventarios' [= the secularization inventory taken when La Purísima Concepción was converted into a pueblo; Carrillo acting as comisionado, Vitoria as maestro/mayordomo]. 8 orig. tomo pp. of inventory that Savage did NOT transcribe or itemize — he records the heading and the page span only. Savage spells the surname 'Vitoria' (not Victoria).

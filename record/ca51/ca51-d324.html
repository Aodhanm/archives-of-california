'Ingresos y Egresos de la finca, desde 21 Dice 1840, hasta 31 de Enº 1841.' Ingresos $19.00 / Egresos $52.00 / 'Contra la finca $33.00.' [Gloss: six weeks of San Luis Rey accounts totalling nineteen dollars of income.] A hard quantitative index of the mission's post-secularization collapse; contrast San Buenaventura's $1,427 and $2,541 balances in the same months.

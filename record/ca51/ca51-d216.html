Duplicate-copy inventory of ex-mission San Antonio under which Abrego handed over to Villavicencio

State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 216

Duplicate-copy inventory of ex-mission San Antonio under which Abrego handed over to Villavicencio; Savage notes it only in a bracketed Nota appended to the 1836 inventory: 'un Inventario de la misma Ex-Mision, p.r duplicado, fecha Set.e 1.o de 1837, bajo el cual hizo entrega José Abrego á José M.a Villavicencio'. Total $37,639.4.3, less $136.4rs of crédito pasivo. Bundled by Savage under the single cite pp. 408-60.

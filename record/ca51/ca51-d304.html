State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 304

'Ramos de riqueza de la Ex.a Mision.' 'Que desde tiempo inmemorial se ha mantenido aquella finca, mas bien de la industria fabril de zapatería, Carpintería, herrería, teja &c. qe. de los bienes rurales, pr. haber pocos pastos y agua, pr. lo qe. pregunta si sigue la gente en sus labores' [the estate has always lived off its shoemaking, carpentry, smithy and tile trades rather than its rural produce, pasture and water being scarce; he asks whether the people are to continue in these trades]. Rare statement of a mission as a manufacturing rather than pastoral economy. ⚠ Savage's cite 'pp. 275-6' breaks the ascending run — see issues.

n192–n196. 'Acusacion contra Martin Cooper y el Preceptor D. Amado, pr amancebamto y otros delitos' (San Luis Obispo): Cooper knifed 'el Alcalde Serafin' at midnight over the married Margarita and later shot at her husband Narciso; Amado threatened Ana Mª, wife of Bonifacio, with a knife as she washed, her son Julian defending her; aguardiente sold constantly at their house; drunk women answer 'Padre, estaba borracha y no sabia lo qe hacia'; the alcalde shut the school.

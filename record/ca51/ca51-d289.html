Propuesta pa. Mayordomo. No one will take the post but José Mª García, who is Alcalde

State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 289

Propuesta pa. Mayordomo. No one will take the post but José Mª García, who is Alcalde — asks his remaining year be dispensed and the 1er Regidor named in his place. Salary proposed: '$50. una res y una arroba de manteca mensualmente; y ademas 5 almudes de maiz 1½ de frijol y un peso de jabon á la semana' [$50 + a beef + 25 lb lard monthly; maize, beans, soap weekly]. For Mayordomo de campo proposes Leandro Gonzales at $16.4 rs/mo + 4 almudes maize, 1 beans, 6 lb lard, 6 candles, 4 rs soap weekly. Savage cites 'pp. 263-4-268-y 9'. Tomo IX.

Spans n199–n200. 'Ingresos y Egresos qe ha habido en aquella finca desde Enero hasta aquella fecha.' Deficits ('Contra la finca') in 8 of 11 months: Ene $58, Feb $712, Mar $212.1, May $601, Jun $576, Jul $361.4, Oct $77.7, Nov $896.4; surpluses only Abr $24, Ago $387.7, Set $429. Best month Set, ing. $1,044.7. The ex-mission ran at a loss in most months of 1841.

INDIAN VOICE. Verbatim: 'Participando qe los alcaldes y todos los indios se han presentado representando qe desde el mes de Sete de 1839 no se les ha dado nada; pide permiso pa hacerles algunas suministraciones de cuenta de la Mision' [Gloss: the Indian alcaldes and the whole community petition the administrator, stating that nothing has been issued to them for two full years; he asks the governor's leave to supply them at mission expense.] Collective petition transmitted verbatim to the governor.

State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 146

'Que D. Antº del Valle se ha portado con honor en la Admon. de la mision de San Fernando.' [= the Father President certifying that a LAY administrator had conducted himself honorably — a rare friar endorsement of the secularization administration, against the usual grain of Durán's complaints].

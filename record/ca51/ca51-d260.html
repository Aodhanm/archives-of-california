State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 260

«Nombramtº de Preceptor en Mariano Bonilla.» The Diputación has agreed Bonilla be Preceptor of Mission San Fernando at $1,000 salary, and, «careciendo aquel cuerpo del conocimtº de los fondos de la finca», resolves that the encargado report on the matter. [schoolmaster's pay charged to mission funds]

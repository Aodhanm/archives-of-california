State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 412

Abstract. 'Que remite noticia de existencias de los bienes de Campo que Se entregaron al indigena José Manuel, encargado del Pueblo de las Flores' [= schedule of field stock/livestock handed over to the Indian José Manuel, head of the ex-mission pueblo of Las Flores]. Named-neophyte property transfer under secularization; the same Las Flores community bitterly petitions against Pico's cattle six months later in Hartnell's 26 Aug visita report (n289).

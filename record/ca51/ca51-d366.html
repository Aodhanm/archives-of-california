Tomo IX. 'Recibo de documentos' — acknowledges circulars recd 7 March

State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 366

Tomo IX. 'Recibo de documentos' — acknowledges circulars recd 7 March: that all communications to the Gobierno go to the Srio. with the treatment of S.E.; 'nombramto. de Vistador en Guillmo. Hartnell (4 de aquel mes)'; the bando of 27 Feb + nota of 20 March 'sobre division del Departo. en Distritos y Partidos'; nota that 'solicitudes de terrenos y otras se deben remitir á los Prefectos del Distrito del solicitante'; nota naming José Castro and Lic. Cosme Peña Prefectos; and a nota rebuking the Aduana administrators for late accounts. [= the 1839 prefecture reorganization + Hartnell's visitador commission received at the mission level].

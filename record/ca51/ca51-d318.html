State Papers, Missions, tomo VI–XI · BANC MSS C-A 51, document 318

Verbatim: 'Haber despedido á los neófitos Antº y Juvenal, pastores de la borregada, pr qe mataron gran número de ellas y vendieron las pieles á Thomas Park.' [Gloss: two named neophyte shepherds dismissed for slaughtering a large part of the mission flock and selling the skins to the foreigner Thomas Park.] Named Indians acting as principals in the hide trade.

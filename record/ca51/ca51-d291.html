Approving his proposals of employees. Confirms García as mayordomo at $40 + a beef + an arroba of lard monthly and weekly maize/beans/soap (a cut from the $50 Carrillo asked); confirms Leandro Gonzales as mayordomo de campo at the rate proposed. Orders the mayordomo be warned 'qe. dé total cumplimto á la prevencion 7ª del Reglamto. Provisional de Secularizacion de Misiones de 9 de Agosto' [full compliance with article 7 of the Provisional Secularization Regulation of 9 Aug 1834]. Tomo IX.

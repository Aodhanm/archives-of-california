Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 370

José M.ª Estudillo → Solá: history of religious ministry at the Monterey presidio since 1796 — which Franciscans came each Lent to confess the cavalry & infantry & missionize in the chapel (the governor's family attending): Frs. Antonio Jayme, [Lasuén], Francisco Pujol, Baltasar Carnicer, José Viñals, Florencio Ibáñez, Ismael Martín, Juan Amorós; this year Fr. [Vicente F.] Sarría (Father President) less obliging; asks Solá to intervene so the troops can fulfil their Easter duty

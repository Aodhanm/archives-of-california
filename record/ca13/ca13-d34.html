Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 34

Bouchard-raid prisoner deposition of Martín Romero (Paraguay, 18; tailor pressed into service aboard 'La Gentila'). Names 2nd ship 'La Líberty/Libertad' under the American 'Medio Comodoro' (Peter Corney); crew of Moors, English, Portuguese, Americans, Andalusians, Argentines; *Chacabuco* split off for Lima. = expanded Document 2 below

Gov. Solá → the Fathers from San Luis to San Diego (circular): the rebels [Bouchard] hold the Monterey establishment/beach without daring to approach Mission San Carlos; yesterday set fire to the Castillo & Presidio [of Monterey]; keep lookouts, relay their course. ⭐ Followed by the missions' acknowledgment-relay chain with timestamps — Purísima (Fr. Mariano Payeras), Sta Inés (Fr. Francisco Javier Vara), San Buenaventura (José Ruiz), San Fernando (Fr. Marcos A. de Vitoria), San Gabriel (Fr. Joaquín Nuez), San Juan Capistrano (Fr. Gerónimo Boscana), San Luis Rey (Fr. Jaime Escudé) — the coastal warning network in action, 28–30 Nov 1818

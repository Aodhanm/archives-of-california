Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 406

Solá → the RAC Directors (= expanded Document 9): acknowledges their 22 Sep 1819 letter (forwarded via the brig 'Buldakov[?]' by the Governor of Sitka); assures them he has shown all the Russian Emperor's subjects — war-ship officers & private traders alike — his particular goodwill (as Russian officers incl. the current Gov. of Sitka can attest); their nation's people have been & will be especially attended

The Directors of the Russian-American Company (Cramer & H.G. Severin) → Gov. Solá (= expanded Document 8): thank him for receiving RAC officers at the California ports; praise the mutual grain-for-Russian-goods trade; invoke the close ties between the two governments; announce a new RAC frigate ('Borodino[?]' under Lt. Zacarías Ponafidin[?]) bound for the Russian colonies that may touch Alta California — ask aid & good reception

Estado de fuerza of the Mazatlán-squadron detachment to Alta Calif. aboard the 'Cosak' under Capt. Pablo de la Portilla: 1 capt., 2 lts., 2 alféreces, 2 sgts., 2 drummers, 4 corporals, 32 soldiers = 'Total 100' [⚠ listed ranks sum to 45, not 100 — Savage roster discrepancy]. Armament: 23 muskets, 93 bayonets, 93 cartridge-boxes, 93 [tinetas?] (⚠ only 23 muskets — under-armed). Signed Pedro Tomás del Corral

Inventory of the archive of the Monterey Comandancia (by José Estrada)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 357

Inventory of the archive of the Monterey Comandancia (by José Estrada): legajos 'Superior Gobierno' 1774–1800 & 1801–1809; *filiaciones*/revistas/service records; 16 old *filiaciones*; 4 vols. printed Leyes de Indias; 2 vols. Ordenanza de Marina; 2 vols. *actas acordadas*; 8 parchment vols.; 6 governor's circulars of the year. A finding-aid to the lost Monterey archive (cf. the San Diego inventory, Doc 46)

Brígida Solá → Don Pablo Vicente Solá (his niece, Basque Country): family news

Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 258

Brígida Solá → Don Pablo Vicente Solá (his niece, Basque Country): family news — her aunt the nun sent word; they went to Madrid; her sister Joaquina married the *guardia* Joaquín (brother of 'Pepe'); a 'Canido[?]' who lived w/ Solá in Madrid gave news; hears he likes the guitar; begs him to send 'alguna cosita' — she's the only unmarried one, poor; her mother has died

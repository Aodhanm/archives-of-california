Pablo de la Portilla → Solá: complains at length of his imprisonment/confinement

Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 397

Pablo de la Portilla → Solá: complains at length of his imprisonment/confinement — punished, he says, for reporting the foreign frigate that sounded San Diego harbor & entered the bay, faulting Francisco M.ª Ruiz for not preventing it; wants a passport to vindicate himself before Alejo García Conde; is carried confined to Sta Bárbara on the *Reina*; mentions an American schooner buying otters at Sta Bárbara

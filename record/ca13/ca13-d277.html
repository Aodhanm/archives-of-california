Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 277

Antonio Cordero → Gov. Solá: the brig 'Cosak' at Guaymas — tried to water, threatened with sinking for lacking a port-captain; its Capt. Clark[?] jailed; the brig then set sail, raised the Anglo-American flag, fired 3 cannon-shots & fled (with abuse/improperios); orders it seized wherever it arrives, anyone landing arrested, goods confiscated

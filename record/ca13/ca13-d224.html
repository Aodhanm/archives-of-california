Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 224

Gov. Pablo de Solá → José Carvencia: ordered the commandants to aid Maya & the French corvette; the brig 'Antonio' at San Diego finishing its cargo; ⭐ 'la Corbeta francesa, después de 9 días de permanencia en San Francisco, salió para el Noroeste' (the Roquefeuil/*Bordelais* called at SF, 9 days, then NW); the frigate *Cazadora* at Monterey; muses the missions (the province's main sellers) should appoint one buying/selling agent; reflects on the extreme scarcity supplying his troops in his 2 yrs 2 mos here

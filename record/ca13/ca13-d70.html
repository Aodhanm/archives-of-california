Conde del Venadito → Gov. Alta Calif.: on the funds situation — strict orders to the intendant of Guadalajara; the Sonora treasuries can make no *ministración*; no carbines available in Nueva Galicia or Mexico; supposes Solá has by now received the artillery & munitions held at Mazatlán, with artillerymen & workers ready to embark

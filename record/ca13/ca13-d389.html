Declaration of the Captain of the Cocomaricopas (= expanded Document 12)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 389

Declaration of the Captain of the Cocomaricopas (= expanded Document 12): his ranchería east of the Colorado (~130 men; the Chiduma & Cocomaricopa nation; he is the head captain); 6 days to the first presidio, Tucson; *gente de razón* come there to trade; he went for cotton & beads; can carry letters to Tucson in 15–20 days. Taken by Santiago Argüello

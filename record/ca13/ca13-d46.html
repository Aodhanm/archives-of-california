Provincial State Papers, tomo XX · BANC MSS C-A 13, document 46

Inventory of the San Diego Presidio Archive (handover Cmdt. Francisco M.ª Ruiz → José M.ª Estudillo, per Solá's order 29 Sep 1820). Legajo-by-legajo: governors' corresp. (Rivera, Neve, Arrillaga, Borica, J. Argüello, Solá, 1774–1820), *filiaciones*, criminal-process drafts, soldiers' wills, *padrones*, service records, monthly musters from 1781, Borica's reserved files on war-with-the-English prep + San Blas ship-signal plans, the Domínguez–Nieto land dispute, war-matériel inventory. A finding aid to a lost archive

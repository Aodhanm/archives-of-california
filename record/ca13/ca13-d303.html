José de la Guerra → Solá: Colorado-Indian attack at Mission San Buenaventura — 20 Colorado Indians came, repelled by the guard (Cabo Fuentes); during Mass the rebels seized muskets, killing a corporal & a servant; 12 soldiers + a sergeant w/ a cannon sent in pursuit.

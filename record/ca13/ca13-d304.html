Gabriel Moraga → Gov. Solá: expedition against the Amajava (Mojave) — set out with 50 men + 10 vecinos eastward; reached Mongarayait[?]/Agallaga (last pasture, 27 horses lost), carried water in vessels, went inland; a gentile Indian killed one of his boys (to rob his horse & blanket).

José de la Guerra → Gov. Solá: the enemy [Bouchard] sailed from the Ensenada del Refugio toward [Sta Bárbara] after burning & robbing the Ortega/Refugio rancho; ship-sighting reports; can't get Monterey's help; 'no tiene artillería útil ni gente; hará la defensa que pueda'; recalled the Monterey relief; if the rebels don't quit the coast he'll attack them where they land

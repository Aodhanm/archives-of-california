José de la Guerra y Noriega → Gov. Solá: measures to prevent a surprise by the B. Aires expedition — the 2 cannon he has are useless, the militia artillerymen inept ('ni siquiera saben el ejercicio del arma'); asks for matériel & munitions; proposes making 500–600 bows and requesting the missions' best archers (*flecheros*), making machetes, and arming the vaqueros and Indians against the pirates

Asamblea Departamental de Californias, session minutes (Savage copy), Los Angeles, 1845-06-27

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 189

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n156-n190, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. Junio 27 - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, June 27, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] ⭐ Two of the Asamblea's largest acts of 1845 begin here. Second reading approving the closure of an alley decreed by the Ayuntamiento of Los Angeles; first reading of the special commission dictamen on the tract called el Tejon granted by the Government; a petition of several individuals of San Diego for the creation of a Tribunal mercantil; a representation of Juan Avila for himself and his sister Asencion for approval of the grant of the tract called Niguil; a petition of Bacilia Bernal for 1000 varas at the embarcadero of Sta. Clara. Then FIRST READING of the commission charged with the arrangement of tribunals, comprising 12 articles, one part of the proyecto, treating of the Tribunal Superior and the Juzgados de 1ª Instancia. Immediately after, on n178, the Asamblea proceeds by secret ballot to name the persons to be proposed to the Supreme Government of the Nation for the election of Departmental Governor, resulting in the order: 1º C. Pio Pico, 2º C. Juan Bandini, 3º Mariano G. Vallejo, 4º Jose de la Guerra y Noriega, 5º Antº Mª Osio; ordered communicated to the Government for publication. the quinterna proposing Pico first for the governorship, ordered communicated to the Government for publication (the leaf supports the proposal, not a confirmation).

Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Setiembre 3. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, September 3, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading and approval of two Baldios dictamenes on concessions to Jose M.a Ramirez and Bacilia Bernal; petition of Vicente de la Ossa for approval of a tract. The President's proposition to create a Tesoreria de Rentas Departamentales is referred to a special commission of Figueroa and Carrillo. Expedientes for Petaluma, Collayomi, Suisun and Huichica in the Sonoma jurisdiction pass to the Comision de Baldios. Runs n222-n223.

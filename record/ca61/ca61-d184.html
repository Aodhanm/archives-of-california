Asamblea Departamental de Californias, session minutes (Savage copy), Los Angeles, 1845-06-16

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 184

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n156-n190, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845 - Junio 16. - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, June 16, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Petition of Juan Vioget for approval of a land grant made by the Government, to the Comision de terrenos. An expediente promoted by Valle and Aguirre to obtain the tract called el Tejon, adjudicated to them by the Government, referred to the commission composed of Noriega and Figueroa, Valle being unable to act as an interested party. A communication from the Comisionado Hijar is read, urging the Asamblea to make proposals for constitutional reform ('proposta de Gtos. Constitucionales'), indicating two legislative initiatives and the form in which they might be directed. Valle reports the result of the commission that waited on Hijar, who expressed his gratitude and offered his cooperation in whatever might be useful.

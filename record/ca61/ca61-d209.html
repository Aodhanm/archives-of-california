Asamblea Departamental / special commission on the Reglamento de milicias; Comision de Valdios, Los Angeles, 1845-09-28

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 209

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n216-n245, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Set. 28. - Ang.s / (Sesion &.)' [= 1845, September 28 (day digit uncertain, see issues), Los Angeles. Session, etc.] The longest unit in the band, seven leaves. Second reading and general approval of the special commission's dictamen forming the REGLAMENTO DE MILICIAS. Art. 1: from the day this decree is published a Register shall be opened at the Sub-Prefectura, or at the Juzgado de 1.a Instancia where there is no Sub-Prefecto, or at the 1er Juzgado de Paz where there is neither, kept open from 8 to 12 daily so that citizens may voluntarily subscribe among the defenders of independence and of the laws, continuing until each town's assigned number is filled; Art. 2: to enlist in these corps one must be a citizen in the exercise of his rights; further articles to n235. Then the Tesoreria Departamental decree (Art. 1, a Tesorero Departamental at 1200 pesos salary plus 100 for office expenses; Art. 2, he shall give bond to the Governor's satisfaction; Art. 3, from the 1st to the 3rd of each month he shall make the Cortes de Caja of the 1st and 2nd operation prescribed in the Reglamento de Comisarias). Then Baldios business: second reading on Juan M.a Osuna's expediente, approved; first reading on the parajes of Simi, Huichica, Collayomi, Guenoc and Petaluma and on citizen Ant.o Machado; and expedientes reported for El Cajon de San Diego, Los Putos in the Sonoma jurisdiction, Potreros de Pauma in that of San Diego, San Carlos Jonata, Canada de los Osos and Pecho e Islay in the Santa Barbara jurisdiction. Runs n231-n237.

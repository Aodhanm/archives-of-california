Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 176

the Superior Tribunal (court) organization reglamento — 3 Salas (instances), Ministros & Fiscal at 2,000 p./yr, a shared Secretary ⚠ SPAN NARROWED 2026-08-27 (band audit): this record read n156-n190 and concealed NINETEEN separately headed sessions of the Asamblea Departamental, of which its summary described only one. n188 is the true close ('Tomada en consideracion la proposicion de Valle se dio por concluida la Sesion.' + cite pp. 205-7); n189 opens the 9 July session, which runs past the old band end into n191. All nineteen sessions are now catalogued.

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 169

'Feb. 12. (Sesion de este dia.) (Perspectiva de la Guerra civil.)' Spans n134-n135. Abel Stearns excuses his failure to join the Comision. The Juez 1.o of Los Angeles forwards the sworn declaration of vecino Tomás Sanchez y Ávila, who accompanied the comisionado Visente Sanchez to Sta. Barbara: 'Que hallandose en la habitacion del Sor. General D. Manuel Micheltorena, oyó que este Senor dijo á presencia de bastantes personas, que la Asamblea era ilegal y que no la reconocia por nada.' The President held this, with the General's silence, to prove he meant to despise the Asamblea; a new communication was ordered demanding an answer 'en el preciso termino de una hora despues de recivida' - obey the corporation's acts or disown them, in which case providences would be taken for the security of the pueblos. Approved, published and circulated.

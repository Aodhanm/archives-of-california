Asamblea Departamental; Dip. Botello; the President, Los Angeles, 1845-09-22

Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. Set. 22 - Ang.s / (Sesion &.)' [= 1845, September 22, Los Angeles. Session, etc.] THIS is the session the catalog record alludes to with 'installing the Superior Tribunal (Botello)', and it is September, not October. The Tribunal Superior de Justicia del Departamento had still not been installed despite the Assembly's decree. The President states the Governor had not neglected naming Ministers, but the 1er Ministro and the Fiscal had not accepted, and he was waiting to see whether the influence of certain persons could get them to accept. Botello repeats that meanwhile the Suplentes should be called so the Tribunal Superior could be installed with them; his proposition is taken into consideration. Runs n229-n230.

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 294

Acta. The Gob. lays before the Asamblea 'los documentos relativos á la invasion del Norte del Dp.to por una Escuadra de los E. U. de America que ocupó el Puerto de Monterrey', in translation: an official note of 7 July from 'el Comodoro D. Juan D. Sloat' inviting the Governor to come see him; copy of the 'intimacion' to the Comand.te gral. to surrender all in his charge; a proclama to the inhabitants; and the general order given before landing. All Diputados showed 'acendrado patriotismo'; resolved to call the inhabitants to service and form a reglam.to for Cuerpos ausiliares. Spans n373-n375.

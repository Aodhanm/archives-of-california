CAPITAL DISPUTE, ANGELENO VOCAL. “Dice que el mal estado de su salud unido al contrario temperamento de Monterey le impiden concurrir á las sesiones de la E. Jta Dep.l” [= ill health together with the uncongenial climate of Monterey prevent Manuel Requena from attending the Junta's sessions]. A southern vocal declining to sit at the northern capital, three weeks before the Junta's installation of 16 Feb. 1840.

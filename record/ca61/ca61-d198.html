Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Agosto 25. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, August 25, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] The President states that the special Missions commission was dissolved by the Acta of 21 May and a new one is required; Figueroa and Carrillo are named to it. Figueroa invokes Art. 2 of the Assembly's Reglamento on the closing of sessions.

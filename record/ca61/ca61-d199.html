Asamblea Departamental / Comision de Baldios reporting, Los Angeles, 1845-08-27

Heading verbatim: '+ 1845 - Agosto 27. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, August 27, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] First reading of Comision de Baldios dictamenes on the land expedientes of 'El Nicasio' (Pablo de la Guerra and Juan Cooper) and 'Nasagui' (Raimundo Carrillo). Figueroa repeats his proposal that the Assembly close its sessions on the last day of August under Art. 2 of its Reglamento; Botello and Noriega are named to report on it, and Carrillo and Noriega to the Comision de Propios y Arbitrios.

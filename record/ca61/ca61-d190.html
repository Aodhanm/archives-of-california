Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Junio 30. - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, June 30, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading of the special commission dictamen on the tract called el Tejon adjudicated to Valle and Aguirre, approved. Second reading of the special commission dictamen for the arrangement of tribunals; C. Botello, a member of that commission, asks that the session be adjourned and that he be allowed to withdraw art. 7 of the proyecto in order to present it reformed. The petition is granted and the session is raised.

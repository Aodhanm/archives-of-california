'1836 Set. 5 Monterey / J. B. Alvarado, J. M. Maldonado'. Today the E. Diputacion approved the following dictamen: 'No es de las atribuciones de la Dip.n crear la seccion de glosa de las ctas de Mis.s y Propios' [it is not within the Deputation's powers to create the audit section for mission and municipal-fund accounts]. Closes the glosa question opened by Chico at p. 125-8.

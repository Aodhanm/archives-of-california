Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 218

Acta, heading '1846. - Mayo 8 - Ang.s (Sesion 4.)' with '(Dup. á p. 328.)' at the right. Abrego's comunicacion enclosing a facultativos' certificate of his illness — 'Enterado'. Then, in a single line: 'Se dá cta. con 45 espedientes relativos á la Concesion de terrenos por el Gob. Dptal. - Se pasaron á la respectiva comision.' [= the 45 land-grant expedientes of the coverage ledger]. They enter as an undifferentiated bundle: no grantee, paraje or individual date is given here.

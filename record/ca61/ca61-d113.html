Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 113

INDIAN CAMPAIGN. Second of the two undated, unsigned documents: “Que el Gob.dor está anuente haga la espedicion contra los bárbaros sin contraer nuevas deudas.” [= the Governor is willing to make the expedition against the “bárbaros” (gentile Indians) provided no new debts are contracted].

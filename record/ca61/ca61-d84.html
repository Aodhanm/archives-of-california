Junta Departamental to the Governor fixing the secretary's salary

Legislative Records, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 61, document 84

Junta Departamental to the Governor fixing the secretary's salary: “Que la dotacion del Srio de la Jta es mil pesos anuales y que recabe la corresp.te Suprema aprobacion.” [= 1,000 pesos a year for the Junta's secretary, subject to supreme approval].

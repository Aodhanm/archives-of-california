Officer leave: María Bárbara Martínez's son needs to go to México for his patrimony

Provincial State Papers, tomo X–XI · BANC MSS C-A 6, document 158

Officer leave: María Bárbara Martínez's son needs to go to México for his patrimony — but his presence is 'utilísima'; Grijalba can sub 1 yr w/ Sgt. Manuel Vargas (pp.534–535); + 'Informes' (26 Apr): forwards Soler's report; the diezmos to the Rosario cajas; ⭐ 18 mare-herds, 1,582 head extracted, 200 broken *potros* distributed to the 5 presidios (cont.)

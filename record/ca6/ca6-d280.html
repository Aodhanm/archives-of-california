Heading: “sobre remision de desertores.” Abstract: “Avisa qe. ha dado órden al Comand.te de Marina de S.n Blas remita los cinco desertores (á Arrillaga) de buques de Vancouver, los qe. trasportó D.n Salvador Fidalgo en la Fragata Princesa, los qe. se entregarán á aql. en obsequio de la buena armonía.” = five deserters from Vancouver's ships, carried south by Fidalgo in the Princesa, to be sent back from San Blas and handed over to Vancouver in the interest of good relations. Parenthesis is Savage's.

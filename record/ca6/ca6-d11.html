Indigenous homicide / mission-justice case: diligencias vs the indio Manuel (neophyte of Mission San Buenaventura) for killing the indio Francisco with a stone — they had made a bet over a measure of abalones while working, quarreled, fought 'á pedradas'; Francisco struck in the leg, sick 23 days, then died; 6 witnesses; the accused's answers conform; Manuel sent to the calabozo pending superior order

Provincial State Papers, tomo X–XI · BANC MSS C-A 6, document 323

Sobre solicitud de grado militar. Margin mark. «Dice ha recibido triplicada la solicitud q.e acompañó con carta 1.o de Julio, en q.e solicita del Rey el grado de Coronel de Caballería» = the viceroy acknowledges receipt, in triplicate, of Borica's petition of 1 July asking the King for the rank of Colonel of Cavalry. Addressed to Borica by name, months before he reached Monterey as governor.

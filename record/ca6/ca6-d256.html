Traslada notas relativas á la entrega de las Misiones y Presidio de su mando. Arrillaga's instructions, conformable to the Viceroy's orders, for handing Loreto to ‘el Ten.te D.n Fran.co de Roa’: 1.o once the officer is sufficiently instructed Ortega ‘puede retirarse á cualquier parte de la Península q.e le acomode, en calidad de Cap. retirado’; 2.o the Viceroy approved Arrillaga leaving Loreto in Ortega's charge so as to move to Alta Cal.a, Ortega to remain until his successor Roa arrives from the ‘Comp.a veterana de Acapulco’. Sent because Roa ‘pretende regresar’, for Aro to decide on allowing his embarkation.

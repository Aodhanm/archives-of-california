Margin mark. Heading: Toma de posesion del empleo de Virey &. Abstract: Dice q.e en aquella fecha ha tomado posesion de los empleos de Virey, Gob. y Cap. Gral. de estos Dominios (N.E.), y lo avisa p.a su intelegencia. [= Branciforte announces he has assumed the viceroyalty, governorship and captaincy-general of New Spain.] Savage records the marginal note: Circulada: 11 Nov.e 94. NOTE: the leaf dates the letter Julio 12 and says he took possession en aquella fecha; the existing bounded description's 10 Jul 1794 is not what this leaf says.

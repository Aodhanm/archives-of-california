Heading: 1793- Set.e 23- Loreto / Ortega y testigos de asistencia Jacinto Amador y Fran.co Javier Morillo, sobre desfalco. Diligencias on the embezzlement of '$2.511. 5 r.s' (FIVE reales, written twice on this leaf) of Real Hacienda funds by Sarg.to Luis López, 44 años, natural de San José del Cabo, who managed the southern peninsula accounts for many years. Auto of 13 Set.e per Arrillaga's order of 23 de Julio; declaración con cargos 16 de Set.e sworn 'pr. Dios y pr. el Rey, levantando la mano y haciendo la señal de la Cruz'. Asked why he came up short he answered, underlined by Savage, 'q.e se descuidó en no llevar apuntes'; accused of handing cajones de cigarros to various persons and confessing he took aguardiente and other effects in exchange. Margin mark:. Spans n310-n312 (interrogation continues n311, decreto and cite on n312).

Sobre solicitación de reclutas y otros asuntos. Under the order of 24 de Ag.to he sought 6 recruits and on 7 de Oct.e enlisted five: ‘José Jacobo Morillo, José Vicente Feliz, José M.a Morillo, Juan Felipe Ibañez y Man.l Salgado’, and on the 18th ‘Mariano Lopez’; kept in continual ‘escuela de ejercicio de fuego, manejo de armas, amanzadura de mulas’, twelve half-broken mules brought from S.n José now tame. Goleta and Javeque back from Pichilingue, out and home in 15 days despite the Nortes; Loretana expected shortly; ordered the utensils for the fundación sent up first.

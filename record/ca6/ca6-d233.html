Provincial State Papers, tomo X–XI · BANC MSS C-A 6, document 233

Heading: 1793- Dic.e 3- Monterrey / Argüello, habilitado de dicho Presidio. Abstract: 'Certificado de q.e resultaron cargados $12. 6 r.s de mas en factura de México, 24 Feb.o segun cuenta de Ten.te F. Ortega, 1488 -' [year written 1488, plainly a slip for 1788]. This certifies exactly the net overcharge Ortega computed in the preceding letter (12 p.s 6 r.s), which is why the two pieces sit together under Argüello's Dic.e 31 1793 certification block. Margin mark:.

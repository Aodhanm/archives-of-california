A MOUNTED-COURIER RELAY ORDER + its step-by-step RECORD

Provincial State Papers, tomo X–XI · BANC MSS C-A 6, document 207

A MOUNTED-COURIER RELAY ORDER + its step-by-step RECORD — the *correo extraordinario* passing each post with arrival/departure timestamps & signatures: Soledad (Gabriel Moraga), San Antonio, [San Miguel] (Juan de Dios Ballesteros), La Purísima (Javier Alvarado), Sta Bárbara (Goycoechea), San Buenaventura (Ignacio Olivera), San Gabriel (José Mª Verdugo), San Juan Capistrano, San Diego (Grajera), Santo Tomás, San Vicente (José Manuel Ruiz), Santo Domingo, San Rosario, San Fernando, → Loreto (Ortega, 27 Dec 1793) — the full Alta-to-Baja coastal communication network documented (cf. the C-A 13 Bouchard alarm-chain)

Provincial State Papers, tomo X–XI · BANC MSS C-A 6, document 326

Aprobación de órdenes á Arrillaga. Margin mark. «Dice aprueba órdenes q.e dio al Gob. Int.o Arrillaga p.a q.e reconociera las obras de S.n Fran.co y pasase á S.n Diego» = the viceroy approves the orders given to Interim Governor Arrillaga to inspect the works at San Francisco and then proceed to San Diego. Bears on the 1793-94 presidio fortification programme that followed the Vancouver visits.

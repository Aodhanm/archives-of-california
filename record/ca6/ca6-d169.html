Orders to give 'cuantos auxilios necesiten' to the French Royal Navy ships searching for the lost *Lapérouse* expedition: S.M., on the petition of D. Augusto de Urtubise[?], directs the governors of America's colonies to assist the French Navy vessels commanded by *M. de [d']Entrecasteaux* (+ a merchant of the Puerto de San [...], 'Carmón' & 'Dussant'[?]) 'que van en busca de la *Peyrouse* [Lapérouse], cuyo paradero se ignora.'

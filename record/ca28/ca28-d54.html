Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 54

Announces the convenio ending the Echeandía–Zamorano conflict, partitioning Alta California to avoid 'efusión de sangre': (1) until the Supreme Government's appointed chief arrives, jurisdiction divided — from San Gabriel southward under Echeandía, from San Fernando northward under Zamorano; (2) mutual military aid if public peace is disturbed; (3) the ordinary mail leaves Monterey the 7th of each month for San Diego and San Diego the 22nd; accessory conditions pending. [cited: Sav pp.48-49 = HoC 'iii. 47-8']

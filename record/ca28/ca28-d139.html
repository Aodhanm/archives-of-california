Reports ~40 foreigners (Anglo-Americans, English & French) calling themselves hunters at Suisun

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 139

Reports ~40 foreigners (Anglo-Americans, English & French) calling themselves hunters at Suisun; asks orders to remove them as harmful — they appropriate others' property, 'sugieren malas ideas á los indios contra ellos (los blancos) y los impulsan á robar caballos y ganado, dándoles baratijas y trapos viejos en cambio de animales.'

Record of his verbal exchange with Zamorano on the command

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 105

Record of his verbal exchange with Zamorano on the command: recapitulates the 1832 Echeandía–Zamorano convenio (Echeandía San Gabriel→San Diego; Zamorano San Fernando→north) pending the new Gefe; declines to disturb the arrangement, deferring to the Supreme Government [Sav p.82 cited; p.83 gap]

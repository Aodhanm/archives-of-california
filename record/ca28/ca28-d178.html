Public anti-cholera bando to all Californians: general cleanliness; Dr. Manuel Alba's printed curative

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 178

Public anti-cholera bando to all Californians: general cleanliness; Dr. Manuel Alba's printed curative; avoid meat broths/fatty meats/coarse vegetables; build Campos-Santos (cemeteries) where none exist, graves 2½ varas deep; no burial before 24 hrs (against burying the apparently-dead); no funeral bell-tolling; 8 PM curfew (violators → 4 days public labor); liquor sales only 11 AM–3 PM (6-peso fine); the sick to the capital's sole physician/hospital; padres & military commanders to enforce where no ayuntamiento; informers get ⅓ of fines; fines fund epidemic costs.

Exhortativa to the Diputación on the electoral sessions for Diputados

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 88

Exhortativa to the Diputación on the electoral sessions for Diputados: the ayuntamientos of Monterey, LA & San José acted per the 1830 law; secondary sessions to be held in the Casas Consistoriales of Los Ángeles under Echeandía's presidency (arts. 57–58); the northern arms-chief to enforce compliance in recibos & missions; Los Ángeles preferred over Monterey — 'no [ha] recibido órdenes supremas antiguas ni modernas que declaren por capital á Monterey' (the norte–sur capital rivalry) ⚠ UNCITED (Sav pp.71-72)

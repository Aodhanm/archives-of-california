Victoria's deportation aboard the American frigate *Pocahontas* back to Mexico

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 33

Victoria's deportation aboard the American frigate *Pocahontas* back to Mexico; the Diputación Territorial opened its sessions 10 Jan 1832 at Los Ángeles, tasked with (1) the *expediente* for the case against Victoria and (2) substituting the Gefatura Política per the 6-May-1822 decree; congratulates the Diputación on its reinstallation.

On suspected conspirators at San Diego & Santa Bárbara

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 207

On suspected conspirators at San Diego & Santa Bárbara: establish a main barracks at Mission San Gabriel, gather forces, seize the supposed authors of the conspiracy, investigate & bring to sentencing; and if what is imputed to the Padre Presidente / Comisario Prefecto Durán proves true, seize him too, 'con toda discreción y prudencia' [cited: Sav p.149]

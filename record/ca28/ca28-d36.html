Transmit the Diputación's public *Manifiesto*

Departmental State Papers, tomo III · BANC MSS C-A 28, document 36

Transmit the Diputación's public *Manifiesto* — an exposición 'á los Ciudadanos Californios' announcing its 10-Jan reinstallation and setting out 'las causas que produjeron y justificaron el pronunciamiento contra Victoria.' The public charter of the 1831 revolt (drafted by Osio, the future historian). Extends Entry 2 [cited: Sav p.27, HoC v3 'iii. 27-38']

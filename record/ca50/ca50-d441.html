State Papers, Missions, tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 50, document 441

1833 cumulative *Cuadro Estadístico* of the 13 Fernandino (southern) missions (⚠ NOT all 21 — heading reads 'Población total de las 13 Misiones,' after the 1833 Zacatecan division) — living neophyte population 10,482, against 52,882 baptized, 14,533 married, and 38,417 died since founding. The demographic sink at the eve of secularization. *(✎ corrected 2026-07-25 at the leaf, scale 1 — the earlier '82,882/58,417' were scale-2 first-digit misreads, 8-for-5; 82,882 would also exceed the 1817 all-19-mission baptismal total, impossible for a 13-mission subset.)* ganado/cosecha data in a separate estadística

'San Fernando.' [San Fernando, i.e. San Fernando Velicata; Savage gives no surname]. The church 33 varas long and 5 1/2 wide; it is of adobes and jacal. Sacred vessels and ornaments scanty. The fathers' house is spacious, of adobes and jacal.

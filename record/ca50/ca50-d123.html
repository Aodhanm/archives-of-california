State Papers, Missions, tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 50, document 123

*Resumen general de la población de ambas Californias a fin de Dic. de [17]94* — combined summary by civil status (eclesiásticos regulares 58; casados; viudos; solteros) AND by casta (españoles, mestizos [the majority: 5,218 H + 5,099 M], color quebrado [mulatos] 228/205, ⭐ esclavos 196 H + 146 M = 342 slaves). Also gives yr-94 vs yr-93 change

'Sto Domingo.' [Santo Domingo]. The church is 18 varas by 8 wide, of adobe with roof of jacal. Some sacred vessels and jewels and ornaments. The habitations of the fathers were of adobes. Savage's past tense ('eran de adobes') is his own and is preserved here without correction.

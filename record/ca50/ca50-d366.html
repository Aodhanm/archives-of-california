*Relación sobre el terremoto* — 21 Dec 1812 ~10 AM, two quakes 15 min apart: the 1st cracked a church corner, the 2nd toppled it; a quarter of the new houses fell to the foundations, all mission roof-tiles down, many main walls opened — but serviceable 'si no hay mayor novedad'

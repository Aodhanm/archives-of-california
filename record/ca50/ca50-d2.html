Padrón* of the Santa Bárbara presidio population

State Papers, Missions, tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 50, document 2

*Padrón* of the Santa Bárbara presidio population — nominal roll of 67 heads of household (officers, sargentos, cabos, soldados, *vecinos dependientes*, San Blas *paquebot* servants) w/ casta, age, wife, children. Founding SB families: Argüello, Carrillo, Cota, Amador, P. Amador, Lugo, Olivera, Ruiz, Valenzuela, Quijada, Rochín, Cota, Pico (#53 José, mestizo 21), Espinosa, Samaniego; incl. *indios* (Martín Reyes 60; Hilario 'Californio' 22), mulatos, coyotes, lobos

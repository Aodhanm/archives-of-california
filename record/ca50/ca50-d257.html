State Papers, Missions, tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 50, document 257

*Estado general de ambas Californias 1800* — 19,816 total souls; harvest 578,416 fan.; ⭐ 1799–1800 reductions: 220 gentiles in Baja + 2,319 in Alta (a conversion surge from the new second-wave missions); Real-Hacienda cattle at SF/Monterey/SD = 5,235 head ('mas del que se necesita… sirve de estorbo'); much 'ganado alzado' (feral); Baja economy — dates/raisins/figs/aguardiente in the south + silver mines & gold/pearl placers 'como 160 leguas de costa'

La Purísima SECULARIZATION INVENTORY — *Inventario general de los bienes de la Misión* per art. 15 of the Reglamento Provisional de Secularización de 9 Ago 1834. Avaluadores Guillermo G. (William) Dana & Santiago Laqui[?]; Comisionado Domingo Carrillo; Mayordomo José Antonio Domínguez. Full avalúo: casa principal (21 cuartos) $4,300; 14 lesser bldgs $1,295; furniture $2,001.50; almacén $6,255.75; grain $4,821.50; church ornaments $4,944.12; library $655.75; 5 bells $1,000; orchards $758.50; church $490; old mission site $373.50; named ranchos w/ values — Los Alamos $1,185, San Antonio $1,418, Sta Lucía $1,050, San Pablo $1,060, Todos Santos $7,156, Guadalupe $4,965; cattle at La Larga $16,304. Existencia total $62,088.50 − pasivo $1,218.50 = líquido $60,840. See Entry 19

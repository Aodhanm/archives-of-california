'Sn Francisco Javier.' [San Francisco Javier]. Church forty one varas long and 7 1/2 wide, of lime and stone with transept and half orange dome; tower of 3 bodies; sacristy 4 1/2 varas wide by eight long; counter sacristy the same; baptistery 3 1/2 wide by 4 1/2 long; choir the width of the church. Abundant images with jewels, ornaments and sacred vessels of some value; paintings and utensils and much clothing of the priests and of the church. The father's dwelling consists of 2 new rooms of lime and stone, vaulted and tiled. Savage records the library holding verbatim. A farm house with utensils and implements of labour, forge, bakery, carpentry shop etc., and a statement of the livestock property.

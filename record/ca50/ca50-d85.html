'Mision del Rosario.' [Mission of El Rosario]

State Papers, Missions, tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 50, document 85

'Mision del Rosario.' [Mission of El Rosario]. The church is of adobes with roof of jacal, 46 varas long and 9 wide, the sacristy included. A single altar, various images and paintings, some jewels and ornaments and few sacred vessels. The fathers' house is of adobes and has several ample rooms. First of the four Dominican frontier missions abstracted on this leaf.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: '203p 3r 4½g to be credited to the Real Hacienda via Tesorería — minor fiscal'. Read leaf n480 (stamp 476, hand 142) of 168036075_79_4_2, first document. Heading verbatim: '1784. - Junio 30 - S.ta Barb.a / Goicoechea á Fages, sobre abono á la Real Hacienda.' Author/recipient/place/date all match; foot-cite 'p. -326.-' and the hand page '142' both match orig_page 'p.IV 142 (p.326)'; stamp 476 matches savage_page. summary_fidelity WRONG on the sum: the leaf reads 'Acusa recibo de la carta de 3 de Junio, en la que se le dice debe abonarse á esa Habilitacion la cantidad de 203 pesos 6 r.s 11/12 g.s perten.tes á la Real Hac.a y los que deberán rebajarse en Thesoreria de esa comp.a' — i.e. 203p 6r 11/12g, not the catalogued '203p 3r 4½g'. Confirmed at scale=1 with a 3x crop: the reales digit is a looped 6 and the fraction is an unambiguous superscript 11 over 12. The record's direction is also loose (the sum is to be credited TO that habilitación, pertaining to the Real Hacienda, and deducted in that company's Tesorería). Omissions: n480 carries two documents (this and d351); both catalogued.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

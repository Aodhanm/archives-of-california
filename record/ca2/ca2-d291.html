Measures after the fire at 'San Luis' (5 Nov, likely Mission San Luis Obispo)

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 291

Measures after the fire at 'San Luis' (5 Nov, likely Mission San Luis Obispo): cut the Santa Bárbara presidio maize ration by ⅙, buy 100 fanegas from San José; public works completed; sowing done at Los Ángeles; and ⭐⭐ approves the elections of *justicias y demás Ministros de la República* at San Francisco, Santa Clara & San Juan Capistrano → mission-disaster + province-wide elected municipal/mission governance, 1783 → alcalde-rule/californio

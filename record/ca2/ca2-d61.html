Unloading instructions for the frigates *Princesa* & *Favorita*

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 61

Unloading instructions for the frigates *Princesa* & *Favorita*: easiest discharge, semillas measured at the Presidio; ⭐ given 'la afluencia de gentiles que irá á ver la descarga,' post a beach guard AND prevent the sailors from 'alguna falta á los gentiles, que sería de grandes consecuencias, por estar ya acostumbrados al trato fino y atento que se les da' [protecting the Chumash from sailor misconduct — the doc-45 doctrine in supply logistics]; papeleta accounting for the recua conductor; certified Ortega SB 10 Oct '82

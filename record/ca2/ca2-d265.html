On Alférez José Velásquez's reconnaissance of the San Diego→Río Colorado road

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 265

On Alférez José Velásquez's reconnaissance of the San Diego→Río Colorado road: a league-by-league travel diary (Salsipuedes, San Sebastián, Cieneguilla — the Anza-trail desert route; 'varias rancherías de gentiles muy mansos y afables, agua muy escasa, pasto en abundancia') AND ⭐⭐ a signed hand-drawn MAP ('Plan') on n411 (Savage p.408) showing Mission San Diego, Río San Diego, San Luis, the sierra crest, the Camino de Sonora, named rancherías & arroyos, and marked *aguajes* (water sources), signed Velásquez → MAP COLLECTION item (early 1783 MS route-map of the SD backcountry) + exploration + post-Yuma Colorado frontier ⚠ diary leaves n408–409 faded at scale-4

COLORADO-FRONTIER RECONNAISSANCE ORDER — two Indians came for aid (one from the Colorado river-mouth, reporting a good ford + naming places *Vísani* & *Quísani/Cábeza*); Fages sends Alférez José Velásquez + 9 soldiers to reconnoiter the Colorado crossing (Oct 1783, post-Yuma), taking the Indians (one as interpreter), treating/gifting them well, keeping a diary for Croix → exploration + Colorado frontier + Indian informants (cf. d265)

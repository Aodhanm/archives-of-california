Indians' shouts the night he left San Juan (soldier Crispín Pérez in irons there)

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 217

Indians' shouts the night he left San Juan (soldier Crispín Pérez in irons there); plan to put the mission *'en estado de defensa'* (close house-gaps/alleys); AND an Indian stock-killing incident — Indians interrogated over killed animals made *'alborotos,'* the cabo seized their leader 'Francisco' as he fled (*remontaba*), next day punished 2 guilty ones with the azote (freed one, kept the reoffender imprisoned for *hurto*) → third 1783 stock-raiding incident (SD/San Juan) → indian-raiding

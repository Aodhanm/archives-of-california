Postal-charges bando — per Art. 3 of the Comandante General's Superior Instrucción of 18 Dec 1783

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 178

Postal-charges bando — per Art. 3 of the Comandante General's Superior Instrucción of 18 Dec 1783: official correspondence handled by Gobernadores/Alcaldes/jueces to be charged (*portes*) to the interested parties; no letter forwarded by *correo* unless franked; Governors to publish by bando, military jefes for the presidios/tropa. ⚠ certified read at Monterey by Diego González dated '13 junio 1784' — earlier than the 20-June dateline (date-miscopy, verify)

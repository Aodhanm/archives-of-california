CG order (18 Dec 1783) on the penas de Cámara

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 378

CG order (18 Dec 1783) on the penas de Cámara — local judges should commute minor offenses (gambling, drunkenness, fights, *bando*-violations) to pecuniary fines rather than imprisonment & bloated processes (which vex/impoverish offenders more than a fine); the fuller form of d349 → alcalde-rule + legal/social history

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 48

THE COMPLETE MONTEREY HABILITACIÓN INVENTORY, Feb 1782 — the year-end balance: efectos vendibles $233-6-6½ (géneros) + $613-5-6 (efectos) + $1,573-3-5¾ (víveres) + $66-2 efectivo + $118 caballerías = $2,615; $143 semillas deducted (S. Luis); invendibles $1,759-5¾; arms: 49½ pairs of pistols, 5 escopetas, 20 espadas, 17 mohamas de lanza; carpentry & smithy tools; munitions: 7@ 41 lb powder, 2@ 37 lb lead, 70,704 GUNFLINTS, 8 bronze 4-lb campaign cannons + 346 balls, 143 balas de á 6; the coercion kit itemized — 2 cepos con llaves, 8 pares de grillos, 17 grilletes con 4 trozos de cadena; 1 bandera con escudo de armas reales; ⭐ SAL among the three signatories — the quantitative snapshot of a presidio's entire material world

Defensa de reos — frontier courts-martial procedure (Real orden via the Comdte Gral)

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 33

Defensa de reos — frontier courts-martial procedure (Real orden via the Comdte Gral): given the great difficulties in reos' juicios/sumarias, the process run per Ordenanza by the Ayudante/Oficial exercising those functions and judged in a *consejo de oficiales* formed from those present at the Parage; lacking sufficient number, determined by the Tribunal Militar de la Provincia

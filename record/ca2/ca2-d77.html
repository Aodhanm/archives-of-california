THE FIRST SAN JOSÉ MUNICIPAL-ELECTION DOCUMENT: Moraga 'ha hablado al P. Francisco & S. Clara 'para que se efectúen tales elecciones'; + the 20-mules order fulfilled. ⚠ Savage heads it 1782 — context (Fages governing) = Jan 1783.

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 36

The gambling prohibition enforced, with named punishments: the King has prohibited *juegos de envite*; the penas read 'en rueda' to the company; Sgt. Mariano Verdugo: 15 days calabozo + 15 of plantón in the guardia; soldiers Reyes & Cazuela: 15 days plantón; a mare & mule returned to Reyes; the $4 he won from Banegas deducted from his pay [winnings clawed back]

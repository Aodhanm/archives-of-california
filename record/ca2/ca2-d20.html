Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 20

THE SAL HABILITADO APPOINTMENT ORDERED: at San Francisco, minutely examine the distribution under Alférez Ramón Laso's habilitación & the cuenta general start-to-finish against the delivery to the new habilitado José Moraga; then have the company name an apoderado and proceed to the nomination of its habilitado — 'cuyo encargo recaerá en el Alférez Hermenegildo Sal,' the power extended in his favor per the formulario. The founding order of Sal's fiscal career (habilitado 1782–87) — the 'Fiscal Comandante' arc's opening document

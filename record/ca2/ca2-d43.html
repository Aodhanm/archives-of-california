Castigo á rancherías / road / duties: Soler had thought to punish the gentile rancherías near S. Juan Bautista[?] 'en el primer lance, por repugnarles hacerlo á sangre fría' [reluctance to strike in cold blood]; the road-opening under the Alférez; credit only signed reports; jurisdictional platica — the Governor attends only to arms & needn't sign the service books (practice of the Compañías captains/comandantes, as Pascual de Cisneros held); may omit monthly revista extracts but not force-states

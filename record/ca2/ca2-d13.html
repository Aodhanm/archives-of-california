Criminal-procedure code per the Superior Orden of 5 Nov 1781: the *sumaria* formed with sworn witnesses

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 13

Criminal-procedure code per the Superior Orden of 5 Nov 1781: the *sumaria* formed with sworn witnesses; after it, the reo imprisoned and names his own defender (informed, accepts & swears the charge); proofs secured, the *proceso* delivered to the defender; substantiation term = THREE DAYS save urgent cases; then remitted with pruebas y alegatos to the Superior Gobierno

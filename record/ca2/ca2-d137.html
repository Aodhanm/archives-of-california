Distribution of mules & horses in both Californias — price charged to the troops (a 1775–78 dossier)

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 137

Distribution of mules & horses in both Californias — price charged to the troops (a 1775–78 dossier): the Cal. Gobⁿᵒ's 25-Mar-1775 purchase of 200 mules + 200 horses (paid from Álamos), the Viceroy's ruling (w/ Ministers Manuel de Soto & José Rodríguez, 1776) charging each soldier $20/mule & $12/horse per Habilitado J. de Fabres's arancel via the Comisaría de Loreto; the Asesor opinion (Salido/Navarro, 26 Sep 1781) w/ ⭐ the 100%/150% recargo regime quantified (effects surcharged 100% New Cal. / 150% Antigua Cal.), counts (108 mulas/44 caballos/14 potros + losses 38 mulas/27 caballos) — the fiscal-surcharge spine (cf. d70)

Instruction for collecting the 1780 WAR *DONATIVO* (per Real cédula 17 Aug 1780)

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 153

Instruction for collecting the 1780 WAR *DONATIVO* (per Real cédula 17 Aug 1780): a one-time voluntary donation 'para los gastos de la presente guerra' from all classes (españoles, castas, servants, militias, govt offices, alcabala/tobacco admin), collected via bandos + curas + alcaldes mayores, explicitly without coercion, violence or embargo; governors may add local provisions

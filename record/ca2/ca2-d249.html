On the SÍNODOS (crown stipends) to the missionaries

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 249

On the SÍNODOS (crown stipends) to the missionaries — per the Real Cédula of 5 Aug 1773 & Recopilación laws 14/25 tít. 15: each missionary has free faculty to collect/dispose of his *sínodo* w/o the Province's intervention; D. Ramón de Goya (Mexico City merchant) = the Factor who habilitates the *memorias* & collects the sínodos at the 'Casa del fondo piadoso' (the Pious Fund), via the Comandante de Loreto's certification → mission finance / Pious Fund / sínodo system ⚠ faded n395–396

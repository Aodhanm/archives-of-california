THE 1782 YUMA-CAMPAIGN JUNTA RESOLUTIONS — the deliberative core

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 94

THE 1782 YUMA-CAMPAIGN JUNTA RESOLUTIONS — the deliberative core: Croix presiding w/ Intendente Pedro Corbalán + Asesor Pedro Galindo Navarro (sec. Antonio Bonilla), on Neve's requested *auxilios* to continue operations vs the rebel Colorado Yumas; reviews Neve's letters + Capt. José Romeu's campaign *Diario* + Fages's successful S. Gabriel→Colorado march. Numbered provisioning articles (9–12+): flour, 12 axes, 12 hoes, 6 bars, 20 *cuchillos de monte*, spare weapon-sets, cartridges & flints, 2 shipboard cannons from Presidio Sta Gertrudis del Altar w/ carriages/powder/balls, horses & mules to remount the Cal. troops, + the exact timing to occupy the Colorado riverbank. Certified copy by Bonilla, Arizpe 18 May 1782

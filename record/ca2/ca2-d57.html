Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 57

Escolta-inspection report (S. Juan Capistrano + S. Gabriel), the coercive underside: at S. Juan the Indians 'muy atemorizados por el Sermón que les echó [Fages] á su paso para Monterey'; the S. Gabriel Ministers' quejas; 'el indio Domingo' held in a grillete at the Presidio, laboring, 'por tener costumbre de huirse'; ⭐ the cabo's complaint that escolta soldiers went to the port 'no más pª vestirse á las mugeres' [soldier sexual misconduct] — offenders to the *cepo*; soldier Padilla disciplined; Indians who killed a becerro: 5 seized, 25 azotes each + kept 'mancornados' in shackles until freed 'por ruegos de los RR.PP.' [friar intercession]; the fugitive re-seized the same day, in grillos at the Presidio

A Yuma-expedition debt settled 14 months after Rivera's death, at the site where he died

Provincial State Papers, tomo III–IV · BANC MSS C-A 2, document 29

A Yuma-expedition debt settled 14 months after Rivera's death, at the site where he died: credit $35 to soldier Ildefonso Domínguez for a mule & a macho he delivered to Capt. Fernando de Rivera [y Moncada]; the sum charged to Lt. José Fran.co de Ortega; deduct the soldier's débito in the company account

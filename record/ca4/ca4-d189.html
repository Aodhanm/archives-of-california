(p.382–383, cont.) I obsequiaré [reward] this little-captain in name of the King; having explained to P. Medico[?] the insulto for such circumstances, so as not to lose the *pergueria[?] de la amistad*. (Enero 14) ⭐ *Habla de la borrachera en Sta Barbara* [drunkenness at Sta Barbara]: despite illness went to Pres. Sta Barbara; the 'borrachera' [drunkenness/wine-abuse]; Lasso has neither head nor feet, & Sal not encountered though a fool[?]; recommends Gov. with much politics that convene vigilar sobre el abrico de la bebida en el presidio de Sta Barbara (vino y mescal) porque ni para mí se dejaba vino

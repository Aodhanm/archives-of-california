Verbatim heading: '1788 Dic.bre 19 San Diego / Zuniga a Fages Enganche y licenciamiento de unos soldados' [Savage: 'Zúñiga á Fages Enganche y licenciamiento de unos soldados']. English gloss: Zuniga to Fages, enlistment and discharge of some soldiers. Substance, quoted by Savage: 'Los dos reclutas que vinieron en el barco tienen la entrada en la Compañia el 26 del mes pasado, quedando licenciados Man.l Bernal y Roque de Cota.' [The two recruits who came on the ship have their entry into the Company on the 26th of last month, Manuel Bernal and Roque de Cota being discharged.]

Verbatim heading: '1788 Dic.bre 19 San Diego / Zuniga a Fages sobre publicacion de ciertos Bandos' [Savage: 'Zúñiga á Fages sobre publicacion de ciertos Bandos']. English gloss: Zuniga to Fages, on the publication of certain bandos. Substance, running from the foot of n251 to the top of n252: 'Los dos ejemplares de Bandos del S.or Virey pasaron al Cap.n del Presidio de Loreto despues de publicados en este Presidio.' [The two copies of the Viceroy bandos passed to the Captain of the Presidio of Loreto after being published at this presidio.] The cite p. 199 sits at the top of n252.

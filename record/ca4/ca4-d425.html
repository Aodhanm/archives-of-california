Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 425

(a) conclusion of the neófito-Domingo *pesquisa* (the gratified party undertakes to apprehend the Christian Domingo — the flagged battle/pursuit thread); (b) R.O. continuing new Viceroy Manuel Antonio Flores's authority over the Comandancia Gral (after Gálvez); (c) ⭐ a spousal-cruelty case — the Padre Presidente sentenced Francisco Paula García for proven *sevicia* to his wife; a Higuera cut his wife's hair out of jealousy.

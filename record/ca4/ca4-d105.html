'Crueldad contra Indios.' At the Ranchería de la Cepeda[?], a soldier party (the presidiario González's) — over soldier Diez[?]/Gutiérrez's pursuit of an India — wounded her in two or three places and injured an Indian man by throwing him off a cliff (*desbarrancándolo*); Goycoechea sent to bring in those gentiles.

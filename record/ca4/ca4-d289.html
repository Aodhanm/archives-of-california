Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 289

Soler on the items of his extract correcting the accounts of 'el alf.z Hermeneg.do Sal como Habilitado de ese Presidio de Monterey' for 2nd semester 1784, both semesters 1785 and 2nd semester 1786: the sums are legitimate charges embedded in the existencia and should be allowed against Sal's descubierto, but not without confrontation with the vouchers. Closing: 'no encontrando la partida de cargo de la estraccion de 2300 y tantos pesos que percibió el Habilitado y dice no pertenecia á ninguna de las existencias'. Spans n236-n237.

*Mudanza de oficiales en los presidios — discriminación personales* [reassignment of officers]. Long personnel proposal to comandante general: for Presidio Sta Barbara proposed 1º Ortega named, delivering habilitación management to Alf. Ramón Lasso, moving to that of San Fco which Alf. José Argüello held; b/c 1º [José] Ortega sufficient for 2º but for Sta Barbara 2º proposed command of 1º. Discusses officers' merits

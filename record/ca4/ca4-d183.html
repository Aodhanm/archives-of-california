Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 183

cont. account-method: on arrival at Monterey, in Lasso's revista de cuentas found shortfall of 694 pesos; informed superiority not encountered [documented], with time facile to cover next year; now says uncovered 807 pesos; Lasso himself in ración de un soldado soltero, only 1 uniform & 2 shirts, sums paid to soldiers & chocolate/sugar/tobacco/candles/cigars uncovered — attributes to clemency not dishonesty but poca inteligencia. Proposes new bookkeeping operation ('Prima partida de cargo: las existencias que entregan los guarda-almacenes á los habilitados...') per Reglamento; effective caudal received to date confronted with tarifa; then Data/discount method

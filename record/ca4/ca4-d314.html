Heading: 'Argüello á Fages Aplicacion de cierta Pena'. Body: 'Queda enterado de que no debe aplicar al trabajo por via de pena á ningun cristiano ni Gentil, sin su superior ord.n' Gloss: Argüello acknowledges the governor's instruction that he may not sentence any Christian [neophyte] or gentile Indian to labor as a punishment without Fages's superior order. Direct evidence on the limits of presidial-commander punitive-labor authority over Indians.

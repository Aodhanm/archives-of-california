Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 301

Heading: 'Soler á Fages sobre que se revoque una ord.'. Understands that once the Sta Barbara company's Revista is cleared he is to move to that of the Presidio de Loreto. Fages, 'reconociendo el estado de su vista tan lastimada', had allowed him to work at his ease; he could not bring last year's accounts current because no habilitado has documents formalized before all February or mid-March; the work 'cargándole repetidamente la fluxion á los ojos', and in the coming season 'no puede menos de quedarse ciego en los caminos para el Loreto'.

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 327

spans n267-n268. Savage's triple heading: 'Envío de familias / Descripcion de un sitio / Trato con Indias'. (1) Sends the recua to load at the pueblo; the three Romero families and Alvarado go with it, and cabo Macario, his wife pregnant, joins with all his family. (2) 'El sarg.to Alvarado me dió parte de algunos disgustos que habian ocasionado al P. Fray Luis los soldados por la concurrencia con algunas Indias'. (3) Eyewitness site report: permanent water, irrigable land, an estero de lizas a musket-shot from the mission, scarce timber, possibly saline soil; one of the chief sites for the Dominicans.

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 196

(p.421–424, cont.) re the plaza/pleito & R.R. [padres]; Lasso did not form the account but had it formed to deliver the habilitación... account of '86; Goycoechea cannot manage por si; Ortega speaks. (Junio 18) ⭐ *Mal humor de los frailes con la construcción de una casa de pólvora* [friars' ill-humor at building a powderhouse]: Manuel Arellano to be transferred; casa de pólvora sited & commandant [set] on construction; R.R. [padres] said the 6 Indios [labor] went to powderhouse & fell ill, blaming humidity, contagion. ⭐ 'Alude á las noticias de los obsequios hechos á PÉROUSE en México' [refers to news of the gifts made to LAPÉROUSE in Mexico]

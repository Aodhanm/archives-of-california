Sobre Indios huidos* — his inquiry into the San Buenaventura fugitive

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 115

*Sobre Indios huidos* — his inquiry into the San Buenaventura fugitive: informants (both gentiles & Christians) deny the mission Christians incited/fed the fugitives — they were far off eating pine-nuts; 'aun los PP. de San Buenaventura, que dieron la noticia, dicen que todo es falso.' Exonerates the Christians. Orig. pp.214–215.

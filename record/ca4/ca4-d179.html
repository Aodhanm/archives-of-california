Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 179

(p.336–337) *carta particular sobre víveres asuntos*: assures goodwill & friendship for Gov. his compadre; tongue [rumor] will be [heard] the day Gov. commands (added: del Sr Insp[?]); says whatever Gov. could say; 'porque ya nos hemos de haber, la agua de su vista y se irá unos 5 ó 6 días el pueblo' [obscure]; mineral water; won't fail while accounts & adjustments settled. (Sept 23, Monterey) ⭐ *Explicaciones largas sobre la conducta del alcalde HERMENEGILDO SAL* [long explanations on conduct of alcalde Sal] — acknowledges oficio of Sept 22

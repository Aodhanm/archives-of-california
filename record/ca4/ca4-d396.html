Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 396

Delay of a vessel. Verbatim: 'Participa que el barco de D. Estevan Martinez no acaba de llegar á esta Rada á causa de fuertes neblinas y contrarios vientos'. Gloss: reports that Don Estevan Martínez's ship has still not made this roadstead because of heavy fogs and contrary winds.

(p.300–301) *carta particular a su compadre* [Fages]

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 168

(p.300–301) *carta particular a su compadre* [Fages]: strict recruit-selection now established, Blanco to be careful of relapses; no news. (p.302) grace/premio for soldiers to continue serving — will notify presidios Monterey/San Francisco/Sta Barbara of royal order (20 Mar); quinto-completed soldiers using absolute license, if within 11 mo return, granted with antiquity

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 202

(p.444–445, cont.) Tte Ortega would work, por bien ó de otro modo, & when not, Fages would have shown his first intenciones with Lasso; the complaint [queja] is the providencia de Lasso to impede bastimentos; says Sánchez won't return to service nor Cabo en Sta Clara, but when Peralta's motives are well-founded another may be placed there with some [reason] as at Sta Cruz; asks return the account for 30 fanegas trigo. (Junio 10) *Fages→Soler — Apreciaciones sobre carácter de oficio con Sal*: does not admit what Soler writes in prior carta re the papel, alludes it has been very útil to Fages

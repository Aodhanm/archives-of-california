Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 320

Acknowledges the royal decree of 21 July last granting the faculty of signing with a half-signature to Antonio Valdés. Verbatim: 'Queda enterado del R.l decreto de 21 de Julio ult. que da la facultad de firmar con media firma al Exmo Ant.o Valdez' [= Antonio Valdés y Fernández Bazán, Minister of the Indies/Marine]. Routine acknowledgement of a peninsular administrative rule reaching the frontier.

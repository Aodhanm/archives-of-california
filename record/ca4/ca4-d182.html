Explicación larga sobre los habilitados Lasso y Sal

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 182

*Explicación larga sobre los habilitados Lasso y Sal — propone un método nuevo para arreglar las cuentas* [proposes new method to arrange accounts]. Repeatedly informed Fages of Lasso's bad accounts in ramos: ganados, fletes, admin. de tabaco de correos; Fages proposed replacing him. Soler agreed to that presidency; [José] Argüello named to sup. presidency of Sta Barbara so as not to leave uncovered that of Sta Barbara, then San Fco; Alf. Sal to arrange accounts of San Fco but erred with account of the late Sgt. José Moraga, which Soler clarified

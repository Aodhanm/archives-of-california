Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 195

(p.416–417, cont.) cabo Ignacio de Soto, detached at pueblo San José, reprehended a settler occupied guarding the caballada for filthy words[?]; settler drew 'espada y cuchillo... agarrando la espada y cuello,' so settler put in stocks (cepo) with 2 feet in the guardia. (p.418–420) ⭐ *Reprensión del Tte González*: reprehended Tte González for poca inteligencia in prior case, ordering settler only 15 days cárcel, & reprehends violence of cabo Soto in threatening with the espada one not his subordinate. (Marzo 11) *Carta con varias noticias sin interés*: speaks of a plaga de Vallejo[?]

Transfers in and out of the company. Verbatim

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 400

Transfers in and out of the company. Verbatim: 'Dice dará salida de su Comp.a al sold.o Juan de Dios Ballesteros y entrada al de esa de Monterey José Tadeo Sanchez'. Gloss: he will release soldier Juan de Dios Ballesteros from his company and admit José Tadeo Sánchez of the Monterey company.

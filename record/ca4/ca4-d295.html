Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 295

CONTINUATION LEAF (do not catalog separately). This year's salary covers 'mil cuatrocientos p.s al Pres.o de Sta Barbara, 500 al de S.n Diego, 194 al de S.n Fran.co y 256 á Monterey'; refuses to sign libranzas on pay not yet earned; will scale his wife's alimentos 'segun mi posibilidad y accidentes'; answers the orders circulated to the presidios about vice or excess.

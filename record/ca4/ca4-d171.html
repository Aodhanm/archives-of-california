*Extracto de oficios* passed by Ayud. Insp. to Gov. & Insp. this province, numbered 25–31: (25) re partidas entries/exits, asks command definition; (26) re composing tropa mechanics/escopetas at presidios; (27–29) acknowledge receipt royal orders; (30) encloses revista extracts, monthly parts & service records of Pres. San Fco; (31) informs re alcances [debts owed] of late Nic. Bull[?]

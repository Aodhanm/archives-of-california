Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Summary read Alférez Argüello's departure from the Santa Bárbara company as "going as *sereno* to the San Francisco presidio company.". Full-resolution crop (2026-07-17, blind sample audit #10/200): "quien va de Ten.te á la compañía del Pres.º de S.n Fran.co" — he went as TENIENTE (a promotion; José Darío Argüello's attested 1787 transfer), not as sereno.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

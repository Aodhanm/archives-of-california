Second Fages letter of the same date, headed 'Poderes especiales'

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 269

Second Fages letter of the same date, headed 'Poderes especiales'. Communicates a real órden of 2 March transmitted 17 Sept by the Comandante General from the marqués de Sonora: 'Habiendo aprobado el Rey la Instruccion que de su real órden formó el virey conde de Galvez para la mejor defensa de esas Provincias Internas, ha resuelto S.M. que el nuevo virey de ese Reino D. Manuel Ant.o Flores, continúe con la misma autoridad superior dada á dho Gefe' — and that the articles of that Instruction referring to his predecessor are to be understood as applying to the new viceroy. [Gálvez Instruction of 1786 carried over to Viceroy Flores.] spans n225-n226. Marginal mark:

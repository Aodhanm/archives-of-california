Savage heading: 'Recibo y falta de cierta carne'

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 328

Savage heading: 'Recibo y falta de cierta carne'. Last year, by the first courier after the frigates sailed, Zúñiga advised D. Hermenegildo Sal of the receipt of 34 arrobas of meat and the shortfall of 18; Sal answered that Fages wanted D. José Tobar's discharges made by Zúñiga over the merma. Verbatim: 'Y pues la Comp.a no debe sufrir esta falta, solo espero q.e V.S. declare quien la sufre'.

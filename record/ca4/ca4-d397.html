Transfers in and out of the company. Verbatim

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 397

Transfers in and out of the company. Verbatim: 'Quedo enterado de no dar salida de su Comp.a al sold.o Tomas Espinosa ni entrada á Leocadio Sibrian'. Gloss: he is informed that he is not to release soldier Tomás Espinosa from his company nor admit Leocadio Sibrián into it.

Provincial State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 4, document 186

cont. defense: re the papel — on 5 Julio received the order, Dn Herm[enegildo] Sal para en el sig. [entrega?] 2 pesos á la expresada misión de San Antonio; on same day 9 [I] rejoined [as soldado] at San Fco; witness to last-night's conversation via cirujano Dn Isaac[?] Carbajal, remitting me 3 [menos] de papel b/c Dn Hermenegildo could not; narrates a papel written by Soler informing a friend of Sal's revista, an *intriga amorosa* supposed with the WIFE OF SAL ('una intriga amorosa que se le suponía con la mujer de Sal'); Sal had threatened Soler with quarter-arrest; concludes asking his liberty & that Sal's designs be witnessed

Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 35

«Dice embia á los Sold.s Carrabantes, Dominguez, Olivas y Piña, dos carpinteros, y tres Sold.s de la Purissima para la redificacion de ese Presidio, como se los previene en orden de 14 de Agosto.» Goycoechea sends soldiers Carrabantes [Carabantes], Domínguez, Olivas and Piña, two carpenters, and three soldiers from La Purísima for the rebuilding of Fages's presidio [Monterey], per the order of 14 Aug. Five named soldiers of the Santa Barbara company detached as a construction labour draft.

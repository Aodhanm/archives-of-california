Heading 'sobre Publicacion de un Bando'. Will have the bando published in his jurisdiction; it inserts 'la Real orn. de 14 de Abril del año pp.o sobre prohibir que comercien los contadores y empleados en rentas reales' (royal order barring accountants and royal-revenue employees from trading). Text runs from foot of n151 to head of n152.

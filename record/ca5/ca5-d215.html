Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 215

Heading: 'sobre envio de efectos.' Per Fages's superior order of 8 Nov last he remits goods to the habilitación with an attached nota; short of blankets, 'remito cinco carneras buenas que aqui valen 15 pastoras'; 'Metates, y Zapatos no van por qe. de los Primeros aun debo seis qe. me han prestado en esta Mision, y los Segundos solo recibi en la memoria sesenta pares.' Also mantas anchas, '12 1/2 varas de Paño,' ounces of silk and other effects franked that year, besides earlier varas de tripe; cites his own letter of '30 de Octre. de 1789' on why he cut his memoria. Asks the paño and manta be returned next year, plus 'media arova de pita floja,' two dozen cordovanes and three or four lbs. of hilo/mantequilla. Spans n222-n224.

Shortfall in the annual memoria shipped from Mexico

Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 221

Shortfall in the annual memoria shipped from Mexico: 'resultaron faltando veinte fresadas medio Cameras, y Dos Resmas de Papel', with the diligencia of proof enclosed. Asks Fages to pass it to the Factor of the Peninsula, since Argüello's own repeated complaints of 'todas las faltas, y aumentos q.e han resultado en las Memorias anter.s' have never been answered.

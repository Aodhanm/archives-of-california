'Revilla Gigedo á Pedro Fages: Establecer la practica de qe. la California antigua se socorra pr. la via de Sonora.' Order opening the scheme to redirect Baja California's supply away from San Blas and overland through Sonora — the question Fages answers at length on 5 June (n325). ⚠ Savage's heading reads '1790 — Febro 30 — Mejico' (verified at scale=1); 30 February is impossible. Most likely 'Febro. 3º' = 3 Feb 1790, matching the 'manifieste 3 de Febro. del corte. año' Fages says he is answering; recorded here as Savage wrote it, month only.

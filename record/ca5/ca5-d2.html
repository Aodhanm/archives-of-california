Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 2

«...tirando al blanco dos dias de la semana 8 ó 10 tiros... particularm.te cuando llegue algun Barco, noticiandosele quien sea el Comandante y demas Oficiales» — standing orders left on quitting Monterey for Sta Bárbara: keep up the cattle rodeos and report on vacuno/caballar; ration the troop with novillo beef, reporting numbers/values, awaiting his order for the matanza; make 2 ó 3,000 adobes for the presidio chapel; drill half an hour AM and PM; hold revistas de ropa y armas; speed the San Francisco correo; on ANY ship's arrival report her commander and officers, forward her mail without delay, aid her with meat and give milk gratis; corral the caballada, no soldier, boy or woman to take beasts without consent.

Two San Diego mission neophytes held in the presidio calabozo since January

Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 205

Two San Diego mission neophytes held in the presidio calabozo since January: 'dos Indios Neofitos de la Misn. de San Diego, porqe. habiendose ausentado de una Mision, fueron a una Rancheria de Gentiles, pretestando que los enviaban los Ps.' — they 'amararon y asotaron algunos Indios, les robaron sus semillas y coral [corral?], y se sacaron una Gentila,' then killed cattle and took two horses from the mission caballada; gentiles of a rancheria near the presidio's Rancho reported them to Pedro Lisaldy [Lisalde], who arrested them. Named: 'El primero que se llama Gabriel, es un Indio perberisimo, qe. se ha castigado muchas ocasiones, por matador de Ganado, el segundo qe. es Constantino, anda a Monte.' Zúñiga leaves their punishment to Fages.

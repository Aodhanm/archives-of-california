'Fages al Virey: sobre qe. [Antigua] Cal. se socorra pr. la via de Sonora

Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 277

'Fages al Virey: sobre qe. [Antigua] Cal. se socorra pr. la via de Sonora. — Este documento es borrador del anterior, de que se sacó copia.' Fages's working draft of the 5 June informe, bound into the tomo separately from the fair copy; useful as a textual witness but substantively duplicative. The fair copy carries a Nota that it went to S.E. under oficio No. 163 of the same date, with a certified copy to the Comandante General under No. 648.

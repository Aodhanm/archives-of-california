On the estate of the deceased soldier Arrieta

Provincial State Papers, tomo IX · BANC MSS C-A 5, document 240

On the estate of the deceased soldier Arrieta: by Arrillaga's order 'he destribuido alg.os bienes à la tropa, como es, silla, freno, espuelas, juste de Carga, y la Escopeta, q.e se ha agregado al respuesto q.e assiste en estas Fronteras'; the rest is of little value, but he will take the cartuchera himself and the casaca must find a buyer, the valuation to follow. Asks licence to go to San Diego when the ship comes, to outfit himself and settle matters with Tte. Zúñiga.

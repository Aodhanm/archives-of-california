Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House), tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 41, document 134

'Acepta el nombram.to recaido provisionalm.te en el p.a vista de la Ad.a maritima de Mont. con 50$ mensuales.' [= Alvarado accepts the provisional appointment as vista (customs appraiser/inspector) of the Monterey maritime custom house at $50 a month]. Early Alvarado in the custom house, eleven years before he returns as its administrator (see n104-n105).

Naturalization/registration of foreigners (per the 1 May 1828 law)

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House), tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 41, document 44

Naturalization/registration of foreigners (per the 1 May 1828 law): French/Canadian trappers François Turcote, Jean Vaillant & Anastase Cariel, residents of New Mexico who came overland from the Taos valley 'á cazar castores,' having left Ewing Young's company to return to N. Mexico; notes further informations of others arrived by sea.

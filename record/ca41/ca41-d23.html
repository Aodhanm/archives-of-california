Ship movements: Russian brig *Baikal* from Sitka (Capt. Egor Chernov), sailed to San Diego

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House), tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 41, document 23

Ship movements: Russian brig *Baikal* from Sitka (Capt. Egor Chernov), sailed to San Diego; the *Rover* (Cooper) re-entered under Am. flag w/ 'cargamento de Canton y de Europa' consigned to L. Argüello; a Russian brig from Sitka sailed 'p.a Bodega' 18 Dec (RAC supply run).

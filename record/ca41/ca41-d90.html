✓ opening VERIFIED vs scan n81 (2026-07-06): '1839 Nov 16 Mont… sobre Contrabando entre Ross y San Franco — Medidas de un empleado en frontera'; 'de Ross introducen á S. Franco cantidades considerables de mercancías sin pagar derechos; antes había un Receptor en…' *(the Aduana-fronteriza + Leese recommendation + Alvarado's marginal note are on the n82 continuation, not re-read this pass.)* 'Contrabando entre Ross y San Francisco.' Considerable merchandise introduced from Fort Ross to SF duty-free, 'por tierra y mar'; the SF receiver who once watched this is long gone; ships entering SF unload without duties. Per the latest reglamento a 'Aduana fronteriza' (frontier customs house) should be set up; meanwhile place an administrator there. Recommends Jacob (Jacobo) Leese as 'persona única para el puesto.' ⭐ Marginal note in Gov. Alvarado's hand: 'Contestado, y que se debe prevenir la Recept.a en la frontera al S.mo Gob.o.'

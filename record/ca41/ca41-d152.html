SF Bay port regulation (articles): ships must present the consul's patente

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House), tomo I–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 41, document 152

SF Bay port regulation (articles): ships must present the consul's patente; must anchor at Yerba Buena, not Sausalito; whalers get limited licenses with a guard aboard; penalties for non-compliance.

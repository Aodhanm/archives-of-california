Causa criminal: presidiarios Francisco Marmolejo & Octaviano Espinosa accused of stealing an otter pelt…

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXXVIII–LXXXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 20, document 232

Causa criminal: presidiarios Francisco Marmolejo & Octaviano Espinosa accused of stealing an otter pelt ('piel de nutria') from the house of Thomas O. Larkin, 5 Apr 1835. Sargto Andrés Cervantes escribano. Fiscal finds the theft proven but their sentences already served since 12 Jul; asks 2 more years. Statements also incriminate one Carmen García. (No Asesor dictamen / Comdte-Genl decree present here.) Prior record: the two + Cesáreo Sánchez robbed a citizen in Guanajuato 1829, sentenced 6 yrs service in Alta Cal. (pp.300–378).

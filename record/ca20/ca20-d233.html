Tail of Marmolejo case, then ⭐⭐⭐ San Luis Rey neophyte conspiracy begins: causa criminal vs. Indians of ex-mission San Luis Rey accused of an uprising ('tumultuarios'). Report of 16 Apr from Capitán Pablo de la Portilla (of the S.L. Rey force) to Comdte Genl José Figueroa; on the night of 13th the neophytes Julián [Murillo], José Miguel & Nepomuceno, gathered at the Temescal, plotted; would summon the gentiles of the sierra.

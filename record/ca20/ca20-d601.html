'Florencio Serrano — Cta por herramientas.' 'Comprádole picos, palas, hachas &a … $100.' Savage's aside

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXXVIII–LXXXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 20, document 601

'Florencio Serrano — Cta por herramientas.' 'Comprádole picos, palas, hachas &a … $100.' Savage's aside: '(se cree que p.a reparacion de la fortaleza de Mont.)' Gloss: purchase of picks, shovels and axes, Savage inferring it was for repair of the Monterey fort — note this is Savage's conjecture, not the document's statement.

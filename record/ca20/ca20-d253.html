(1) Alcalde of L.A. — 'Sobre causas criminales'

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXXVIII–LXXXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 20, document 253

(1) Alcalde of L.A. — 'Sobre causas criminales': many criminal cases pending for lack of a fiscal (no one will accept the post); Gefe Político replies 3 Aug to elevate the matter to the Sup. Gobierno & meanwhile alternate the fiscal role among capaces as customary; referred to the Diputación (pp.288–9); (2) Maldonado, Secretario de la Diputación (p.289).

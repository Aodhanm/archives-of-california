The Vázquez del Mercado / Ramírez sumaria. Gutiérrez commissions Ayudante de la Plaza José María Cossio to form a *sumaria* on the accusation by Fray Jesús María Vázquez del Mercado, minister of Msn San Antonio de Padua, against administrator José Mª Ramírez, over abuse of the indigenous Toshaya (Guido); orders Cossio treat the Padre with the consideration due his rank/character.

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXXVIII–LXXXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 20, document 258

(1) tail: President granted Anastasio Carrillo's own-petition retirement to Monterey w/ sub-teniente pay (pp.446–7); (2) ⭐ 'Ejecuciones de justicia por la tropa': transcribes a Secretaría de Guerra circular of 1 Dec 1835 — the troop is NOT to carry out by arms the death penalties of the ordinary jurisdiction; governors to provide 'máscaras y verdugo' (masks & executioner) for executions; if unobtainable, organize accordingly.

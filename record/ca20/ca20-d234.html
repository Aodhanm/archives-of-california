(cont.) San Luis Rey / Temecula conspiracy. Julián urged all to oppose giving the 'Rancho de Temécula á S.E. [José Antonio] Estudillo,' since the ex-mission had no dominion in those lands; the Genl had taken much ex-mission cattle unpaid → lands 'ya estaba perdida'; plot to distribute lands & kill all; correos Julián & Andrés sent to summon the gentiles; ref. the earlier conspiracy under Gov. Victoria. Witnesses: 2nd Marcial, 3rd Alejo, 4th José Marcial, 5th Andrés; declaraciones preparatorias of Juan Nepomuceno, José Miguel & Julián Murillo (named as ringleader). Fiscal interrogated 17 Apr. Gutiérrez decree, Monterey 5 Nov 1835: 'Habiendo noticia que los acusados se hallan en libertad por disposición del Sor Comandte Genl mi antecesor, archívese esta sumaria = Gutiérrez' (pp.380–414).

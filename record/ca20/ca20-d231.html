(1) Account of supplies to national troops 1 Jul 1834–30 Jun 1835

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXXVIII–LXXXVIII · BANC MSS C-A 20, document 231

(1) Account of supplies to national troops 1 Jul 1834–30 Jun 1835: yunta de bueyes, 100 vacas, 100 vaquillas, 50 novillos, tons of tasajo, 25 yeguas w/ cría, 6 potros, 2 caballos mansos, 4 bestias mulares, víveres = 1573 pesos (p.321); (2) Alviso, supplies (víveres, jabón, bestias) to individuals of the Colonia in transit through the pueblo = 222 pesos (p.222).

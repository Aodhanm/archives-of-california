Approves Borica's handling of English-ship arrivals (Grajera's carta 159)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 381

Approves Borica's handling of English-ship arrivals (Grajera's carta 159): one at San Diego (29 Aug, 'Mídera'); the English merchant frigate *Resolution*, Capt. Juan Locke[?], at the Ensenada de Todos Santos (25 Nov 94) — given 'los precisos refrescos en uso de la hospitalidad,' its packets for its Court forwarded, but barred from reconnoitering; praises the 25 Jul defense order [E12/E16 — another named ship under the doctrine]

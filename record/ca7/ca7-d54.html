Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 54

Friars sought 2–3 more escort soldiers to push a missionary 'conquista de gentiles' across the bay north of the Port (toward Marin); Pérez Fernández declined (3 reasons: unknown/hostile country; a friar + 2–3 soldiers too weak; he lacks authority), asks instructions. + tule-balsa shipwreck at the Farallones — 2 of 4 SF mission Indians drowned (4th of month) [= prior mil-hist #45]

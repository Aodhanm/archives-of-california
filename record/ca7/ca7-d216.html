Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 216

Instruction for the inspection of the mission escorts (Carmelo→Soledad→S. Antonio→S. Luis), to be run by Tte. Parrilla w/ Alf. Hermenegildo Sal & relief men (see E11): inspect by night the guard, sentinel, arms/munition, dress & mounts; art. 7 — treat the Indians 'sostenido, sin [ofenderlos?] en lo más mínimo, bien sean cristianos ó gentiles'; keep a circumstantial *diario*; if the Teniente is incapacitated, Sal takes over (art. 9); the Comandante to act 'con messura' so neither Padres, troops, nor Indians are aggrieved (art. 10)

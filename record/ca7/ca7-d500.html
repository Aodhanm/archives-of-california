The English merchant whaler 'Resolución' (Capt. Locke) anchored at Ensenada de Todos Santos

Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 500

The English merchant whaler 'Resolución' (Capt. Locke) anchored at Ensenada de Todos Santos; chartered by HBM to carry supplies to 'aquellos Establecimientos,' returned whaling (~300 barrels); Grajera gave hospitality & provisions; captain forwarded 12 pieces of British correspondence through Spanish hands → see E25

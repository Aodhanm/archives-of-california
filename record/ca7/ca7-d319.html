Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 319

Forwards a Contaduría Mayor de Cuentas dossier on the back-pay (*alcances*) of the soldiers who served in the founding expedition to San Diego & Monterey — rules for deceased/deserted/executed men's *alcances* (to overseas heirs via the Juzgado de Bienes de Difuntos de Guadalajara, to kin via the military court, or 'para el alma'); invokes R.O. 1 Jul 1787, the 2½% deduction, & Tte. Francisco Ortega's gratification; Tribunal resolution (4 Feb 95) + Branciforte decree (5 Feb) + Fiscal opinion (14 Feb): repay the retained sums

Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 242

✎✓ RE-READ AT FULL RESOLUTION 2026-09-24 and COLLATED against Vancouver's English original (Lamb, *A Voyage of Discovery*, IV). Savage's Spanish, verbatim from the leaf: *"A mi arribo á este Puerto hallé los dos hombres q.e desertaron el 6 de En.o de 93 del Berg.n de la Real Armada Ynglesa nombrado el Chatham, con otros tres q.e en Dic.bre de 92 desertaron de la urca Dédalo en este Puerto, y á mas **un Reo ingles** q.e desertó de la misma Urca en el puerto de S.n Diego el 7 de Dic.bre de 93 hallándose á mis órdenes; y en virtud de mis anteriores solicitudes **he sido informado p.r el S.r Argüello** habérsele dado órden de que se me entregasen… hallo que solo los dos hombres que desertaron del Chatham el 6 de Enero de 93 en este Puerto, nombrados **Diego Acheson y Diego Landon**, y **el reo** que desertó en S.n Diego el 7 de Dic.bre de 93, llamado **Tomas Smith**, son súbditos de S.M. Británica: no reclamo los tres…"* ⭐⭐⭐ THE COLLATION RESULT: **Savage is faithful on every checkable point** — names, all three dates, the ship, and critically the category. Vancouver wrote *"a British **convict**"*; Savage renders it *"un **Reo** ingles"* and again *"el **reo**… llamado Tomas Smith"*. **The word was in Savage all along.** ⚠ It was THIS CATALOG ENTRY that dropped it, along with the Argüello attribution, and that misspelt Landon as "Lanson". Corrected. The three not claimed are the Dec 1792 *Daedalus* deserters; Vancouver will lay an itemised account before the Lords Commissioners of the Admiralty in London. (Spanish translation tied to Brig. Álava.) ⭐ Thomas Smith is identified in the *Discovery*/*Chatham* musters (Lamb, App. 8) as a **convict who escaped from New South Wales in the *Daedalus***.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read The record described Thomas Smith simply as having deserted the urca Dédalo at San Diego, named the other man “Diego Lanson (Landon)”, and did not mention Argüello.. Full-resolution re-read of n159. Savage writes “y á mas un REO ingles q.e desertó de la misma Urca en el puerto de S.n Diego el 7 de Dic.bre de 93”, and again “el REO que desertó en S.n Diego… llamado Tomas Smith”. He also writes “he sido informado p.r el S.r Argüello”, and spells the name “Diego LANDON”.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

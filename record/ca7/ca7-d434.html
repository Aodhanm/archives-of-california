Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 434

Nootka foreign-ships R.O. (via the Duque de la Alcudia, 20 Jan): per Saavedra's reports of foreign ships at Nootka (Jan 1793–Jul 1794), the King is writing to the Ambassador in London; meanwhile act 'con la mayor prudencia… en el empeño que ya tenemos abandonado la mayor parte de nuestros derechos' — a candid admission of the Spanish retreat from Nootka (evacuated 1795)

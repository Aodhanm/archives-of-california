Roundup of the *cimarrón* (runaway) Indians of Mission San Borja (Baja) by Sgt

Provincial State Papers, tomo XII–XIII · BANC MSS C-A 7, document 460

Roundup of the *cimarrón* (runaway) Indians of Mission San Borja (Baja) by Sgt. Javier Aguilar — with a named *Relación* categorizing ~15 ('Cimarrones': Estanislao 60, 'cimarrón perpetuo,' caught 3×; Vicente; Basilio; Pablo of S. Fernando 70; José Antonio 80 — 'Por Ladrones': cattle-thieves; 'Remitidos á la Frontera': Sixto, the ringleader, wife-stealer & flight-inciter; 'Cogidos en la Contra Costa') + a total of ~60 from San Borja, 41 from San Fernando & more

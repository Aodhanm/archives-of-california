The 'Cuevas attack' (deposition). Sworn deposition on an expedition (*salida*) by Padre Fr. Pedro Cuevas, during which a clash broke out between Christian and gentile Indians of the ranchería of the Asisones, and the padre received arrow wounds. Savage: 'no arroja mas luz sobre el asunto.'

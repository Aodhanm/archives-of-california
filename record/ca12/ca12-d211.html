C70" cross-reference is not checkable from this leaf. date (Set.e 26 = 26 Sept 1807), orig_page (foot "pp 519-21") and savage_page (printed stamp 196, pencil 196) all confirmed. place PARTIAL: the place field is blank, though "México" is carried inside date_text and the leaf heads the entry "Méx.".

SF garrison increase per Viceroy's order (8 Apr 1805)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 20

SF garrison increase per Viceroy's order (8 Apr 1805): add 1 sargento 2º, 4 cabos, 29 soldiers (+ '34 plazas sencillas'); proposes Argüello for sgt-2º & 5 soldiers for cabos; thinks it hard to fill.

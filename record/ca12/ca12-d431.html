THE *MERCURIO*/EAYRS AFFAIR — crew depositions

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 431

THE *MERCURIO*/EAYRS AFFAIR — crew depositions. By Arrillaga's order, José Argüello takes declarations from the Anglo-Americans of the frigate *Mercurio* (seized by Nicolás Noé's *Flora*). Secretary cabo Juan de Ortega; interpreter Juan Díaz. ① Capt. George W. Eayrs (Boston): ~8 years trading these seas with the Russians & NW-Coast gentiles; ~8 American ships on the NW Coast buying otter from the Indians; on this voyage carried provisions — on commission from the Governor of the Russian NW establishments — to the Russian post above Bodega [Fort Ross], delivered May 1813 to commander 'Cuescoff' [Kuskov]; bound for China with furs; Kuskov gave him 2 sealed packets + a box of 6 gold/silver watches to forward. ② John Dillaway, pilot (Boston).

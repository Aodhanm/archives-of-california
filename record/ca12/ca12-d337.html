SF buildings deplorable: storms wrecked the *tercio* warehouse + 4 family quarters + old barracks

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 337

SF buildings deplorable: storms wrecked the *tercio* warehouse + 4 family quarters + old barracks; Castillo de San Joaquín's merlons & esplanade destroyed, artillery barracks in the worst state; no masons/carpenters/smiths or materials; the clothing warehouse broken into & robbed (rotten timbers, thatch roof).

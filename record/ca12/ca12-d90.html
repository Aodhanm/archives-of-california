The Russian frigate *Juno* (Rezanov) at San Francisco

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 90

The Russian frigate *Juno* (Rezanov) at San Francisco. Acknowledges the Gov.'s 19 Apr report of the arrival at SF of the Russian frigate *Juno*, Comd. Dn Resanoff [Rezanov], seeking provisions & repair; asks what was learned of the ship's purpose 'y de los pasaportes con que navega.' [The 1806 Rezanov supply mission from Sitka — Rezanov / Concepción Argüello.]

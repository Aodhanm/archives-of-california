Supplied the Princesa with 300 arrobas of flour

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 118

Supplied the Princesa with 300 arrobas of flour; + a foreign frigate at San Borja (Baja) (unknown name/nationality) with ~80 two-man canoes (~160 hunters) fishing — a large otter-hunting fleet; the Princesa went to find it but it vanished by end of July (per Tte Ruiz).

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 238

The Branciforte Machuca affair: Florentino Machuca hid in inválido Julián Ríos's house (behind his daughter Hermenegilda's bed), struck Ríos & his wife, fled on a horse; resisted arrest (wounding a pursuer) before capture; the daughter fled, found 5 leagues off, detained at Branciforte.

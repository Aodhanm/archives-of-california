The Gabrielino uprising (28 Oct 1810–4 Jan 1811)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 409

The Gabrielino uprising (28 Oct 1810–4 Jan 1811): LA militia/invalids/vecinos served when the Christian Indians of San Gabriel + gentiles tried to attack the mission & pueblo; cabo José Bartolomé Tapia donated $150; full militia roster (Claudio López, the Ávilas, Verdugo, Higuera, Nieto…). Under Sgt. Guillermo Cota until Alférez Moraga stood them down.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read date_text: '1812-10-30 Sta Bárbara'. Leaves n333-n334 (IA 168036075_79_14), printed stamps 326 and 327. DATE IS WRONG: the heading reads '+ 1812. Set. 30 Sta Barb.a' - 30 September 1812, not the catalogued 1812-10-30. Checked at 2x crop; the word is 'Set.' with the long-s/t ligature, and the document itself concerns service that ended in January 1811, so no October date is in play. AUTHOR: leaf 'Cap.n Josef Arguello, Comand.te del Presidio'; catalogue 'Jose Arguello' identifies the right man (Josef = Jose is a legitimate expansion) but drops rank and office. RECIPIENT: the piece is a certification with no addressee named, so blank is correct. ORIG: leaf closes 'pp 1063 - 7' = catalogued '1063-7'. SUMMARY verified line by line against the leaf: 'Certifica que los milicianos de artill.a y los invalidos y vecinos de los Ang.s cooperaron en todo lo que se ofrecio de servicios cuando la revoluc.n de los indios cristianos y gentiles que intentaron atacar la mision de S.n Gabl. y el citado pueblo'; 'el cabo de aquellos milicianos Jose Bartolome Tapia, a mas de sus servic.s personales, ofrecio $150. - como donativo'; roster of cabos and soldados (Claudio Lopez, Bartolo Tapia, the Avilas, Verdugo, Higuera, Nieto...); 'Sirvieron desde 28 Oct. 1810 hasta 4 En.o 1811'; 'por orden del Sarg.to comisionado Guille Cota, hasta que los mando retirar el alferez Gab.l Moraga.' OMISSIONS: n334 also bears Savage's finding-aid note 'pp. 1070-1087 copias de documentos que ya se han estractado en pag.s anteriores de este tomo' - a note, not a document, so not counted as an omission; flagged for the record.

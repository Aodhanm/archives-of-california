A military expedition under Alférez Gabriel Moraga — 25 men (+ chaplain Fr. Marcelino Cipres & interpreters); SF to ready 1 sgt, 1 cabo, 8 soldiers; Moraga holds verbal orders to explore the far side of the river he couldn't cross last time; 5 men drawn from San Luis/San Miguel. [The 1806 Moraga interior/Central Valley expedition.]

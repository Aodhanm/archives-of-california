Interrogation of the brig's crew: ① Benjamin Ingraham, pilot, American (New York)

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIX · BANC MSS C-A 12, document 480

Interrogation of the brig's crew: ① Benjamin Ingraham, pilot, American (New York): arrived San Luis for water/weather, then Sta Bárbara; a year in these seas aboard the brig — which was sold to the RAC ['Ricco'], sailed under American then Russian flag; Capt. Walter Whitmarck[?] (American); the brig belongs to Alexander Baranov, who resides 12 miles [from New Archangel]. [Continues n395.]

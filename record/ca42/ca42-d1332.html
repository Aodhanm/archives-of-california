'Que Demetrio Almendares pide certificado de haber concluido su condena.' [= that Demetrio Almendares asks for a certificate that he has completed his sentence]. Written by the exiled French Picpus priest resident at San Gabriel; a mission-based cleric acting as intermediary in a convict-discharge matter before the Los Angeles alcalde.

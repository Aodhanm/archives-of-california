Verbatim: 'Que el 11 del corriente tomó posesion de la Prefectura' [= that on the 11th of the current month he took possession of the Prefecture]. Dates Cosme Peña's assumption of the Los Angeles prefecture to 11 April 1839 - a fixed point for the prefecture system this tomo is named for.

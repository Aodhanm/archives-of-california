Last document of Tomo V. Verbatim: 'Manda un pliego de Gil Harra[a]' [= sends a dispatch from Gil...]

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1454

Last document of Tomo V. Verbatim: 'Manda un pliego de Gil Harra[a]' [= sends a dispatch from Gil...]. The surname is written with an ambiguous capital and most likely reads Ybarra [= Gil Ybarra, Los Angeles juez de paz], but the letterforms do not settle it, so it is recorded as written and flagged. Sender T. Tapia = Tiburcio Tapia, Angeles.

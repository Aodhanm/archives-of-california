Pacheco reports that during his term of office 10 persons died. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1276

Pacheco reports that during his term of office 10 persons died. Verbatim: 'Que en el tiempo de su Cargo, fallecieron 10 personas.' [= that in the time of his charge, 10 persons died]. San Jose municipal vital return; Salvio Pacheco is a named San Jose officeholder — candidate for the SJ crossref ledger.

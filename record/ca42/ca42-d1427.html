Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1427

Format A entry. Verbatim: 'Que el capitan de la fragata Heros francesa, Augusto Duhaut-Cilly, demanda por $ 50 á J. B. Alvarado' [= that the captain of the French frigate Heros, Auguste Duhaut-Cilly, is suing J. B. Alvarado for 50 pesos]. José Antonio Carrillo reporting a foreign merchant's civil demand to the governor from the Angeles juzgado.

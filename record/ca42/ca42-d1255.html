Escobar asks the governor to send him the sentence passed on the prisoner Pablo Cruz. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1255

Escobar asks the governor to send him the sentence passed on the prisoner Pablo Cruz. Verbatim: 'Que le mande la condena de Pablo Cruz' [= that he send him Pablo Cruz's sentence]. Escobar acts throughout these leaves as the Monterey alcalde/juzgado correspondent, but the heading does not style him alcalde here.

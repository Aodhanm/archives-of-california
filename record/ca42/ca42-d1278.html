MARQUEE. Carrillo reports that the comandante of San Diego has asked him for a relief of 25 armed men to contain the gentiles and neophytes of that jurisdiction who have risen in revolt, and that he will send them. Verbatim: 'Que el Com.te de S. Diego le pide auxilio de 25 hombres armados para contener á los gentiles y neofitos de esa jurisdiccion que se han sublevado; y se los mandará.' Southern-frontier Indian uprising, June 1833.

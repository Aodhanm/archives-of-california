That what the prefecture tells him about the transfer of alférez Macedonio González is irregular, and…

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1547

That what the prefecture tells him about the transfer of alférez Macedonio González is irregular, and that the prefecture is incompetent [to order it]. Verbatim: 'Que es irregular lo que le dice de la traslacion del alférez Macedº Gonzalez. Que la prefectura es incompetente.' Gloss: José de la Guerra y Noriega denies prefectural jurisdiction over a military officer.

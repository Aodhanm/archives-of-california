That the delivery of cattle from Lugo to Sepúlveda was carried out

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1544

That the delivery of cattle from Lugo to Sepúlveda was carried out; that he will press Lugo to present himself to the government. Verbatim: 'Que se verificó la entrega de ganado de Lugo á Sepúlveda. Que urgirá á Lugo pa que se presente al Gobierno.' Gloss: enforcement of a private cattle judgment by the Angeles prefect.

'Que procedan á la detención de los vagos' [= that they proceed to the arrest of vagrants]

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1325

'Que procedan á la detención de los vagos' [= that they proceed to the arrest of vagrants]. A territory-wide circular to alcaldes and comandantes issued the same day as the tribunal-de-vagos order; establishes the May 1833 vagrancy sweep as a coordinated civil-military campaign.

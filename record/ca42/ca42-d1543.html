On the 18th the German merchant N. Fink was found murdered, his door opened after five days shut

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1543

On the 18th the German merchant N. Fink was found murdered, his door opened after five days shut; the judges asked his aid and he ordered four celadores of substance named to patrol with helpers; at 9.30 pm ten bell-strokes signal silence and thereafter anyone in the streets is challenged, must justify his errand, suspects arrested. Verbatim: 'Que el dia 18 encontraron asesinado al comerciante aleman N. Fink... se darán 10 campanadas que indicarán silencio.'

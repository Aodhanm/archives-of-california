Heading reads 'Oposicion á los Picos'. The mayordomo of San Luis Rey, José A

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1549

Heading reads 'Oposicion á los Picos'. The mayordomo of San Luis Rey, José A. Estudillo, appeared with eleven neophytes saying that if the Picos occupied the rancho of Temécula all the Indians would leave the mission; he calmed them and told them to await the superior decision. Verbatim: 'que si los Picos ocupaban el rancho de Femecula todos los indios se irian de la Mision.' [Femecula = Temécula]. No day given in the heading.

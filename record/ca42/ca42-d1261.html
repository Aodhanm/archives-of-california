Governor directs the alcalde to furnish the session hall of the departmental junta. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1261

Governor directs the alcalde to furnish the session hall of the departmental junta. Verbatim: 'Que surta de muebles la sala de sesiones de la junta depart.l' [= that he supply furniture for the departmental junta's session room]. Savage marks the piece a draft: '(Es borrador)'.

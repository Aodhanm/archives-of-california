Pardons the vecino Ynocente García for the escape he committed while a prisoner. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1520

Pardons the vecino Ynocente García for the escape he committed while a prisoner. Verbatim: 'Yndulta al vecino Ynocente García pr la fuga que cometió estando preso.' Gloss: the pardon that answers (but is dated six days before) García's petition at p.s 375-6 — Savage orders by original page, not by date.

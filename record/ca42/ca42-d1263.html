Governor orders the ayuntamiento to take measures to have a jail built. Verbatim

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1263

Governor orders the ayuntamiento to take measures to have a jail built. Verbatim: 'Que tome providencias el ayuntamiento para que se construya una Carcel.'

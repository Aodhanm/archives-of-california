Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1268

De la Guerra reports that, in conformity with the governor's oficio, he has ordered the judicial acts performed by the alcalde suspended. Verbatim: 'Que conforme á su oficio mando se suspendan los autos judiciales hechos por el Alcalde' [= that in accordance with his oficio he orders the alcalde's judicial acts suspended]. The compliance side of the p. 523-524 ruling; note the reply is dated BEFORE the order as bound.

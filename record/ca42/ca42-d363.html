'Sobre movimientos de Frémont': the Gavilán (Hawk's) Peak standoff

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 363

'Sobre movimientos de Frémont': the Gavilán (Hawk's) Peak standoff — Frémont, breaking January's good-faith promise, introduced armed force near Monterey, fortified an eminence of the Sierra del Gavilán at 37° latitude & hoisted the US flag with 'insultos y desafíos'; Castro mustered the militia at San Juan [de Castro] as Cuartel Gral; Frémont fled north by night; cites his correspondence with US Consul Thomas O.

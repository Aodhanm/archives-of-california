Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1430

Verbatim: 'Que no condescienda á las peticiones que los indios de S. Luis Rey han manifestado contra el administrador de dicho.' [= that he is not to yield to the petitions the San Luis Rey Indians have made against the administrator of that mission]. '(Sin firma)' is Savage noting the original is unsigned, not a correspondent name. Direct order constraining an alcalde's handling of Indian complaints against mission administration.

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1519

Protests the cruel punishment (a beating) inflicted on him by order of Comandante González, asks pardon for his escape and restoration of his citizenship rights. Verbatim: 'Representa contra el castigo cruel que le aplicaron (palos) pr orden del Comte Gonzalez, y pide indulto pr la fuga que verificó y que se le restituyan los derechos de ciudadanía.' Gloss: corporal punishment, flight from custody, and civil rights of a vecino.

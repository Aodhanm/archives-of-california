Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1361

'Que espera sus ordenes para que salga una expedicion en busca de Caballada robada.' [= that he awaits his orders for an expedition to go out in search of a stolen horse-herd]. The San José alcalde will not launch a punitive expedition without gubernatorial authorisation.

'Que Juan Bandini quiere reunir sus rancherías y sujetar á la gente a vivir junta.' [= that Juan Bandini wants to gather his rancherías together and compel the people to live in one place]. Six pages in the original. The San Diego alcalde reporting a private ranchero's move to concentrate and control Indian labourers - direct Indian-labour and settlement-coercion evidence.

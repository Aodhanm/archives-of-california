Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1584

“Relacion de ingresos y Egresos.” = statement of the San Diego juzgado's receipts and outlays, transcribed by Savage as a two-column table. INGRESOS: “De Enero á Dbre.” $72 · “Correos.” $4.2 · “Diferencia.” $1.'6. EGRESOS: “Utiles de escritorio y alquiler” $34.6 · “Auxilio á Talamantes y Lugo.” $43.4.6. Both columns ruled off at $77.2.6. Osuna is the San Diego juez re-elected per n560 p.553-4. Note the unusual half-page cite 28½.

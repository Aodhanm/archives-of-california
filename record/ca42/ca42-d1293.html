Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1293

Governor tells Castro not to worry about the alcalde's complaints, and that if he wishes he may sue the alcalde before the alcalde of Monterey. Verbatim: 'Que no tenga Cuidado de las quejas del alcalde y que si quiere, demande á éste ante el alcalde de Monterrey.' Notable for routing a suit AGAINST one alcalde to another town's alcalde — jurisdiction of first instance in practice.

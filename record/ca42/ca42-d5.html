Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read place: ''; date_text: '1828-08-19'; summary: 'Re a $50 sum the vecino Jn Bautista Urbana[?] gave to Auguste Duhaut-Cilly, captain of the French frigate *Le Héros*'. Leaf read: 168036068_81_4 n2 (C-A 42). Heading verbatim (magnified to be certain of the digit): '+ 1828 = Agosto 18 = Angeles. / Jº Aº Carrillo al Gefe Supº polº'. DATE WRONG: the leaf reads 'Agosto 18', the record says 1828-08-19 (the following entry on the same leaf, doc 6, is also headed 'Agosto 18' and is dated correctly, so this is not a deliberate disambiguation). PLACE WRONG: the field is empty although the heading supplies 'Angeles'. AUTHOR PARTIAL: the leaf gives 'Jº Aº Carrillo', the record only 'Carrillo'. SUMMARY_FIDELITY WRONG: the body reads 'Manda de su bolsa $50 que el vecino Jn Bautª Alvarado debe á Augusto Duhant Silly, Capn de la fragata francesa, "Héros".' — the surname is plainly Alvarado, not the record's 'Urbana[?]', and the sense is reversed: Carrillo remits from his own pocket a sum the vecino OWES Duhaut-Cilly, whereas the record says the vecino 'gave' it to him. Orig_page confirmed by the footing 'p. 10-11'; savage_page confirmed by the printed corner '3' (manuscript corner '2'). Omissions OK: three entries on the leaf, three records (docs 4, 5, 6).

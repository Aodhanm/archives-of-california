Audit of the San Gabriel mission dispute (Fr. Tomás Estenaga vs. mayordomo Juan Pérez)

Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1053

Audit of the San Gabriel mission dispute (Fr. Tomás Estenaga vs. mayordomo Juan Pérez): bad management & no accounting; storehouse keys 'manejaban personas de diferentes sexos'; the cattle went 480 (at Bandini's handover) → 804 but only 397 remain, unaccounted; the wheat sowing abandoned; Pérez overdrew his $480/yr salary by $676; recommends removing him

'Que le dará cuenta de los diezmos cuando haya encontrado quien le pueda ayudar como secretario.' [= that he will render the tithe accounts once he has found someone able to help him as secretary]. Third Chabaya letter of 9 Julio 1834; documents that the San José alcalde was operating without a secretary. Sender abbreviated 'P. Chabaya' on this leaf.

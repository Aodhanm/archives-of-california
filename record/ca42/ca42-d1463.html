Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read date_text: '1836-05-23→09-15'. Leaf read: 168036068_81_4 n507 (stamp 507, ink 10). Three blocks: (1) '+ 1836 = Angeles. / M.l Requena al Gefe del Dep.o — / Agosto 2. Sobre Correspondencia. (2 doc.) p.s 60-3. / Sbre 14. Sobre elecciones. (2 doc.) p.s 64-6.'; (2) '+ 1836 = Mzo 23 = Angeles. / Tiburcio Tapia al Gefe del Depart.o / Pide un expediente. / p.s 67-69.'; (3) '+ 1836 = Sbre 15 = Angeles. / Man.l Requena al Gefe del Desp.l / Pide armas de fuego para prevenir el robo de caballada que se sabe meditan algunos malhechores. (El Gob.r le manda entregar diez fusiles). / p.s 70-3.' date WRONG: the earliest date in the span is 'Mzo 23' = 23 MARCH 1836 (verified at 3x magnification: '1836 = Mzo 23 = Angeles'), not the catalogued 1836-05-23; the same catalogue reads 'Mzo' correctly as March elsewhere (row 806, '1831. Mzo. 24' = 1831-03-24). End date 09-15 ('Sbre 15') is right. place PARTIAL: the place field is blank although all three headings read 'Angeles' — the datum survives only parenthetically inside the author field, 'Tiburcio Tapia (Angeles)'. recipient PARTIAL: leaf has 'al Gefe del Dep.o' / 'al Gefe del Depart.o'; catalogue truncates to 'Gefe'. summary_fidelity PARTIAL: the abstract covers blocks 1 and 3 accurately (incl. the horse-raid and the ten muskets) but says nothing of Tapia's 'Pide un expediente' at p.s 67-69, which lies inside the claimed span 60–73 and whose author the row itself names. orig 60–73 and ink 10 are correct. Omissions OK: all three blocks fall inside this row's span (neighbours 1462 on n506, 1464 on n508).

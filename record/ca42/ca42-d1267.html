Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1267

MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project. Governor holds the alcalde's nomination unconstitutional for failure of the legal formalities, and every judicial act of his void. Verbatim: 'Que la nominacion del alcalde no es Constitucional por haberse faltado á las formulas legales - Que todo acto judicial de dicho, será nulo.' Savage's cite carries his own note '(Por mala encuadernación)' warning the original pages are bound out of order.

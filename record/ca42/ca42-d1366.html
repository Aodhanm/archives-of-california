Departmental State Papers, Benicia, tomo Prefecturas y Juzgados I–VI · BANC MSS C-A 42, document 1366

'Que le han dicho que redujo a prision al sirviente de Estevan Munras sin causa suficiente para eso.' [= that he has been told he jailed Estevan Munras's servant without sufficient cause for it]. The government calling a local judge to account for an arbitrary imprisonment; four original pages. Two distinct surnames on this leaf, 'Muños' in the heading and 'Munras' in the body, written differently by Savage.

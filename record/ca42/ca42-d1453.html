Verbatim: 'Manda una sumaria que por su orden practicó en la mision de S. Gabriel el sargento Jº Antº Pico, contra un neofito' [= sends a sumaria carried out by his order at Mission San Gabriel by sergeant José Antonio Pico against a neophyte]. Military officer conducting a criminal investigation against a mission Indian, forwarded through the San Diego prefect/juez.

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n287-n301, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1831 - Enº 11 - Sn Diego. / Rodigo del Pliego á Juan Bandini.' [= [1831 as written] January 11, San Diego. Rodigo [= Rodrigo] del Pliego to Juan Bandini.] Savage's whole abstract, verbatim: 'Le dice qᵉ ha entregado á Tomas Shaw, sobrecargo de la Fragata 'Pocahontas' $100. pr su pasaje (de del Pliego) - p. 521.' Del Pliego tells Bandini that he has handed Tomas Shaw, supercargo of the frigate Pocahontas, $100 for his passage — the parenthesis is Savage's own clarification that the passage was del Pliego's own, not Victoria's. A short receipt-note, but it establishes that the Pocahontas carried at least one paying passenger besides the deposed governor's party, and it fixes Shaw as the man taking the money.

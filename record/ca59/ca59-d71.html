⚠ NOT A RECOVERY — RETRACTED CLAIM, 2026-08-27. This was published as a recovery; independent verification showed the document IS already catalogued (it duplicates the Plan de San Diego record in this volume). The row is retained as a CORRECTION to that record, because the verification did establish two real facts: the Plan begins on this leaf (stamped 338 / ink 336), not where the older record places it, and the older record's incipit has transcription errors against the leaf. Original recovery text follows for the record: this document lay inside the span of a grouped entry covering n322-n341, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'Dios y Libertad. Mont. Agto 24 de 1831 - Manuel Victoria - Al Alcalde del Pueblo de Na Sa de los Angeles.' [= God and Liberty. Monterey, 24 August 1831. Manuel Victoria. To the Alcalde of the Pueblo of Nuestra Senora de los Angeles.] CAUTION: only the closing lines of this document are on n340; its body lies on leaves I did not open (n337-n339 or earlier). The visible close runs 'incurrir en una deformidad qe reprueban las leyes y nada debe hacerse si no es por el termino regular y conforme a las facultades demarcadas a la autoridad que ejerzo, con respecto a las actuales circunstancias en qe me hallo.' A Victoria instruction to the Los Angeles alcalde insisting that nothing be done except by regular process within his own delimited faculties. No record covers it.

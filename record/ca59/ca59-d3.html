Election of California's Diputado to the Mexican Constituent Congress: the electors

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 3

Election of California's Diputado to the Mexican Constituent Congress: the electors — Francisco de Castro (Branciforte/S. José + missions SF/S. José/Sta Clara/Sta Cruz), José Arus[?] (Monterey + missions S. Juan B./S. Carlos/Soledad/S. Antonio/S. Miguel/SLO), Francisco Ortega (Sta Bárbara + its missions + S. Buenaventura/S. Fernando/Sta Inés/Purísima), José Palomares (pueblo de Los Angeles), Ignacio López (S. Diego + missions S. Luis Rey/S. Juan Cap./S. Gabriel) — name Coronel Solá Diputado to the Cortes constituyentes, Luis Antonio Argüello suplente; note the difficulty of the S. Blas/Acapulco passage to reach Mexico

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 24

the Reglamento de Ganados (cattle/branding law): who may own a *hierro* & brand (only rancho-holders; others use *marca y señal*); no saddling another's horse ($10 fine); mustang (*mesteña*) roundups with all *colindantes*; unclaimed *orejano* cattle to the Territory's *propios y arbitrios* — the ranchero economy's legal framework

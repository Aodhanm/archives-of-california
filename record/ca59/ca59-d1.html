'Jura la Independencia de México': the acta by which Gov. Solá + the presidio captains (de la Guerra, Luis Antonio Argüello), militia captains (Navarrete, Portilla), Lts. Estudillo & Estrada, artillery cmdr Manuel Gómez, & friars Payeras & Sarría, convened in the sala de gobierno on news of the Regencia del Imperio, recognize California 'por solo dependiente del Gobierno del Imperio Mexicano, é independiente de la dominación española'; the oath sworn 11 Apr with a solemn Church function; signed by all

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 68

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n322-n341, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'No 4 - Comanda Militar de Sn Diego -' [= No. 4. Military Command of San Diego.] Reply on Carrillo's application for leave to embark at San Diego for the capital of the Republic. The Comandante General's inserted text rules that on the petition 'qe le dirigio el paisano Jose Anto Carrillo debio V. decretar no estar en sus facultades entender en un [asunto]...', that is, the San Diego commander had no authority to entertain it. The body continues beyond the foot of n322.

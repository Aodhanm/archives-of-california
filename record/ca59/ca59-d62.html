Juan Bandini to the Diputación Territorial de la Alta California, San Diego, 1832-01-20

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 62

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n287-n301, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1832 - Enº 20 - Sn Diego. / Juan Bandini á la Diputacion Territorial.' [= 1832, January 20, San Diego. Juan Bandini to the Territorial Diputación.] Savage's whole abstract, verbatim: 'Pide que la diputacion tome las medidas necesarias pª el reintegro del préstamo de $1,600 que levantó el Gefe Politº Intº Dn José Mª de Echeandia pª el embarque de Victoria entre los particulares, y parte de los fondos Municipales de los Angeles.' Bandini asks the Diputación to take the measures needed to repay the $1,600 loan that the interim Jefe Político José María de Echeandía raised for Victoria's embarkation, the money having been raised among private individuals and in part from the municipal funds of Los Angeles. This is the FISCAL half of the Victoria deportation that the existing catalogue misses: the $1,600 of the Pocahontas contract was not treasury money but a forced private and municipal loan, and this letter is the request to make the territory good for it. It stands third in a five-document Bandini sequence on these leaves whose original-page cites ascend 506-13 → 514-15 → 516-7 → 518-9 → 521.

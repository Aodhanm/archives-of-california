Juan Bandini's development manifiesto: measures 'por el fomento del Territorio'

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 19

Juan Bandini's development manifiesto: measures 'por el fomento del Territorio' — open the ports to merchant ships at the agreed points, and cut the 'gravosos' import/export/tonnage duties considerably. *Bandini's economic liberalism on the floor* (cf. his 1830 secularization proposal)

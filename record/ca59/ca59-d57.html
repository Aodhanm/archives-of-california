a scathing anti-mission secularization polemic

Legislative Records, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 59, document 57

a scathing anti-mission secularization polemic: after 'mas de 60 años' under the mission system the neophytes show no 'adelantos,' still prefer their 'antiguas rancherías,' the friars turned the ignorant against 'nuestras instituciones' & ruined 'sus verdaderos patriotas' — *the ideological case for secularization*, in the legislature's record (cf. C-A 19 José Sánchez, C-A 20 property-rights)

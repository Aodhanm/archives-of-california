Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XX–XXXIX · BANC MSS C-A 16, document 534

⚠ a rambling/satirical letter ⭐ on Baja pearls & silver (offered $3,000 for 33 oz of pearls + ~1,000 marks of silver, awaiting a buyer who might pay up to $20,000) interwoven with mission-command gossip (a disputed *comandancia*, a drunken Sunday quarrel ending with a friar drawing a measuring-stick on a corporal, accusations of a friar sleeping with Indian women) — hard to read

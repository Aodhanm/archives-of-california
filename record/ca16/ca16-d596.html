criminal *causa* vs. the paisano Joaquín Román

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XX–XXXIX · BANC MSS C-A 16, document 596

criminal *causa* vs. the paisano Joaquín Román — blasphemy (cursed the consecrated Host, 'que se lo llevaran los demonios en cuerpo y alma'), disrespect toward the presidial officers, threats (witness Manuel Gutiérrez), and abuse/cursing of his wife (who seeks divorce); escribano Juan de Ortega; fiscal refers sentencing up, handing the file to comandante Luis Argüello

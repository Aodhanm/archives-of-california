Arrillaga's last will & testament — dictated as he lay dying at Mission Soledad

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XX–XXXIX · BANC MSS C-A 16, document 610

Arrillaga's last will & testament — dictated as he lay dying at Mission Soledad; burial in a Franciscan habit; 100 masses each at San Antonio & San Miguel; bequests to servants (Manuel Boronda, indigenous servant Joaquino Antonio, Mariano Montañez) & the Indian washerwomen; debts owed him (Estudillo $1,500, R. Estrada $85, 8 soldiers); universal heir his unmarried sister María Josefa in Spain (via cousin Martín de Embil, Veracruz); executor alférez José Mariano Estrada — *expanded below*

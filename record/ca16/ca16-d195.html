So author ('Gov. Arrillaga et al.' — honest compression of three named signatories), place, date 26 Jun 1799, orig 630–58, stamps 118–119 and the two-leaf span are all correct. summary_fidelity WRONG on three transcribed figures plus one dropped line: (1) "Mis.s de la Ant.a Cal. … 469" — catalogue records $467 (the terminal 9 is the same looped, descending 9 as in '109' and '519' two lines away); (2) "Comp.a Volunt.s … 257" — catalogue records $357 (first digit is the flat-based 2, matching the 2 of '242'; at 10x it is not a 3); (3) "Destac.o de Artill.ª … 59" — catalogue records $51 (second digit is unmistakably the looped 9, not a 1; the 1s on this leaf, e.g. '175', '1853', are plain slanted strokes); and (4) the line "Vecinos de Mis.n Todos Santos . 53" is in the leaf's donor list but absent from the abstract. The other nine amounts (109, 24, 257, 545, 1853, 242, 175, 375, 519) and the "signed on various dates" gloss are correct. Omissions OK: n117's first entry is ca16-d194 (Sta Bárbara company 1799, pp 570-3); n118 carries only this document's tail.

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XX–XXXIX · BANC MSS C-A 16, document 34

*Contratas de artesanos* — contracts (by order of Viceroy Branciforte, 19 June) with master artisans for New California: Joaquín Botello (with family, 5-yr term, $1,000/yr + ⅛ of his works' value, King's account) — ⚠ *this abstract reads 'mason,' but C-A 7/Docs 362 & 373 and Botello's own Doc 132 below show Botello is the Sastre (tailor); the actual mason/cantero was Manuel Ruíz (C-A 7 Docs 373/541, $365). The 'mason Botello' here is a conflation — corrected 2026-06-30*; Manuel Martínez [C-A 7 reads *Listonero* Manuel Muñoz] (5 yr, $500/yr + ⅛); broadcloth weaver (*tejedor de la ancha*) Mariano Tobar Méndez [C-A 7 reads *Tejedor* Mariano José Mendoza] (2 family, 5 yr, $600/yr + ⅛), transport at the King's account. ⚠ Trade/name discrepancies with C-A 7's transcription of the same 19-Jun-1795 contracts — reconcile against the originals if cited. Cf. C-A 54 ii.3–13 artisan-instruction program

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XX–XXXIX · BANC MSS C-A 16, document 569

written instructions to his son tte Luis Argüello on taking command of the SF presidio (18 articles; Savage extracts 1–7, 15–18) — discipline; monthly musters of arms/mounts/brands; rotate escort-corporals every 6 mos.; re-roof the presidio using mission Indian day-laborers; ⭐ Art. 3 calls Mission San José's Christian Indians 'alborotadores y sediciosos,' ⭐ Art. 6 the anglo-American ship-exclusion protocol (demand royal passports, else deny the port + courier the gov. & mission *cordillera*); Lenten church-duty; fire-watch — *expanded below*

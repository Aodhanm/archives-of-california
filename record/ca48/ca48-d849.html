Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read 28 licencias "para individuos de la Compañía de S. Fernando.". Full-resolution crops (blind sample audit #146/200, 2026-07-19): the body reads "pª indivs de Compª de SU MANDO" — of his command — and the addressee line reads "Comᵗᵉ de Compª de Cabª permanente de Mon[terrey]."
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

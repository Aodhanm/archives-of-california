Desertores, robo de bestias y daños por las aguas*

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 142

*Desertores, robo de bestias y daños por las aguas*: 5 frontier soldiers deserted with 22 beasts from the Sto Domingo mission/escolta; the Jan–Feb 1779 rains knocked down the SF presidio's new adobe walls (forcing a rebuild in stone) & collapsed most of the warehouse palisade & casamata; Mission Santa Clara & the San José pueblo flooded (the dam carried off, wheat drowned); at SD & Monterey no house free of leaks

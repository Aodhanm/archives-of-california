'Antecedentes de una mortandad' — the causes of the 1781 Yuma Revolt

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 2024

'Antecedentes de una mortandad' — the causes of the 1781 Yuma Revolt: at the expedition's passage through Jalchedun lands the captain took gentile/Christian women; Rivera failed to gift the Yuma chief Palma; the expedition's mules destroyed the mesquite the Yumas live on part of the year → 'apostasía y rebelión'; a Yuma (Limón[?]) killed Capt. Bastón[?]; Palma's brother; the Jalchedunes — see Entry 25

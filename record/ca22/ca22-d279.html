(trunc.) On establishing postal *estafetillas*

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 279

(trunc.) On establishing postal *estafetillas* — correspondence already carried by Indians, presidio officers overburdened; will route mail via Los Álamos/Guaymas (10×/yr vs San Blas 1×); about to move to found the Sta Bárbara Channel presidio + 3 missions *(Savage: 'ya extractada')*

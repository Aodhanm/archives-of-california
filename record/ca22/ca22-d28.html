Bundle of Rl Órdenes communicated: exclude corporals/sergeants from the deserter-*abono*

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 28

Bundle of Rl Órdenes communicated: exclude corporals/sergeants from the deserter-*abono*; European *soldados cumplidos* must return to Spain unless they re-enlist; alcabala exemption (5 yrs, then 5 at half); the *Dieta* on *vínculos*; thanks for *estampilla* signature to the Minister; bando on the *manda* of Gregorio López

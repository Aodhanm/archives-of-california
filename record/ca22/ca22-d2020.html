Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 2020

Junta de Guerra y Rl Hacienda orders for the Yuma campaign (Fages to march from Yuma territory with the rescued families, convoking friendly nations to attack the Yumas); plan for the Yumas/allies to scout a site to re-establish a presidio on the Colorado ensuring free communication; 'Suspensión de guerra' (war suspended to mid-Sept; Colorado unfordable till April); Romeu & Fages retire to their destinations; the Yuma 'Capitán grande' — see Entry 25

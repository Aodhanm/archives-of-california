[numbered borradores] ⭐⭐ N.º 223 (22 Mar 1781) 'Marcha de familias — Expedición al Canal': soldier & settler families to move via Loreto to the port of San Luis; Capt. Rivera y Moncada crosses via the Colorado; the troop/settler *memorias* to garrison the Channel establishments; San Gabriel sends 46 carneros + 114 sheep/goats w/ breeding stock for the settlers; + N.º 220 (cost of arms conducted by Rivera, Álamos surplus); a *cobro* to Trillo y Bermúdez — complements Entry 24

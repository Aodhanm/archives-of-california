⚠ SAVAGE CALENDAR (bounded — dozens of docs; Savage's own tallies '3/5/8/11 doc.' → to be itemised). Résumé of Arrillaga's letters as interim gov. from Loreto after Romeu's death, Mayo 6–Oct 1792, orig pp. 618–643: acknowledges the 10-Apr notice of Gov. Romeu's death; cédulas de inválidos for Ttes. Diego González & José Ortega; monthly correo to leave S. Francisco the 1st; copies of superior orders; assist arriving H.M. ships; ⭐ vigilance over disorders & prohibited gambling in San José (p.621–4); borderol to Diego González; ⭐ the Indian Agustín, after his sentence, to private service until the new gov. disposes of him (p.624–7); the huerta Fages left to be gifted to the [gov.'s] widow; documents for the gov.'s widow to obtain her 2 *pagas de tocas*; ⭐ sangrador José Castillo added to the revista (Oct 16, p.628); ⭐ free the Indian Estanislao (Set 13, p.630–2); note on the 7 artisans carried by the frigate *Concepción*; ⭐ cabo Ignacio Vallejo recalled from San José (vecindario displeased with him, p.632–3); franquear buques/auxilios to the Comte de Sta Bárbara; on a *reo*; R. orden on the 5-year alcabala exemption. *(Leaf-verified n370–373, 2026-08-15: catalog previously mis-read recipient/date/place — was 'Arrillaga (Monterey)→Pérez Fernández, 1794'; the 'Vallejo tending Fages's huerta' line conflated two separate items.)*

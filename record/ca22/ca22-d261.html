Cosechas y ganado*: Los Ángeles harvest 1,800 fanegas, all houses + guard + Casa de Ayuntamiento…

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 261

*Cosechas y ganado*: Los Ángeles harvest 1,800 fanegas, all houses + guard + Casa de Ayuntamiento finished, church building (all adobe); F. Sinova[?] & Manuel Butrón resettled (Butrón → San José); ⭐⭐ first (interim) rancho grants — Juan José Domínguez (ex-SD soldier; 4 mare-herds + ~200 cattle, below the San Gabriel river → Rancho San Pedro), Manuel Nieto, & the children of the widow Ignacia Carrillo (Arroyo Hondo), with conditions not to harm gentiles or mission/pueblo herds; mission+pueblo grain ~20,000 fanegas (no more San Blas seed) — see Entry 20

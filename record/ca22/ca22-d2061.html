1794 San Fran.co / Arrillaga á Sal [Arrillaga to Hermenegildo Sal.] Jun. 17 (3 doc.), pp. 603-4. Heading and cite on n366; the three items themselves are listed at the head of n367: Recibió doc.tos / Que se le mandan de Mexico 20 escopetas y ocho espadas / Que entregue la Habilitacion de San Fran.co á Perez Fern.z. The handover of the San Francisco habilitacion from Sal to Perez Fernandez and the shipment of 20 escopetas and 8 espadas from Mexico. Emitted because no export row cites pp. 603-4; the already published row scanned n367-n369 (orig II:604-611) picks the same arms up from p. 604 onward with the priced relacion, so check for overlap before loading.

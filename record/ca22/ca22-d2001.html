'Documento trunco sobre trato á Indios de Mis.s'

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 2001

'Documento trunco sobre trato á Indios de Mis.s' — the writer has long held 'el expediente de la denuncia hecha por Fr. Ant.º de la Concepción sobre varios desórdenes… y mal trato que se da á los Neófitos por los R.s P.s'; the declarations contradict, the Fiscal wants the informes rectified by others with knowledge; he notes the enormous distance from the scene, the adulteration of noticias, and advises no sweeping conclusion — 'se verá no ser tan cruel ni tirano el trato que se da á los Neófitos.' Continues into the structural analysis: comandante-vs-missionary conflict of interests over neophyte goods and labor, the soldiers' 'comercio con las Indias,' and the P.-vs-Com. disputes over castigos [dup-theme → Vol I Docs 118/144]

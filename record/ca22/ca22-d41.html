Circular + Bando on the acopio de Pieles de Nutria (sea-otter pelts)

Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 41

Circular + Bando on the acopio de Pieles de Nutria (sea-otter pelts): per the plan of Commissioner Vicente Vasadre [y Vega] approved by the Real Audiencia, only the Missions (through the padres) and the Indians may collect/trade pelts; all other inhabitants of the Peninsula absolutely prohibited; pelts found in anyone's hands confiscated (decomiso); commanders to publish & enforce — see Entry 7

Character of the Colorado River Indians & lands, per Fr. Francisco Garcés (who accompanied Lt. Juan Bautista de Anza to the Río Colorado): docility & desire of the riverine nations for padres/Spaniards; lands good for all crops & esp. cattle; flood-prone but with *mesas* fit to settle; area demarcated for the padres — will further 'esta conquista'

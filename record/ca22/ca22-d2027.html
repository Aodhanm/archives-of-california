Provincial Records, tomo I–II · BANC MSS C-A 22, document 2027

Yuma-massacre narrative (augments Entry 25): scouts to the Colorado's north bank; gentiles told Velázquez that Capt. Carrizosa[?] was killed; Salvador Palma & 3 others gathered their people; at 2 a.m. fell on the Padre[s] & Fernando Rivera; soldiers scattered & seized; the missions attacked the same hour; 2 armed horsemen crossed, burned houses, killed Indians; interpreter Javier; alférez Limón; Palma fled to the sierra ~2 months

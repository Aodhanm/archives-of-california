(Alberni → Borica, 30 Apr 1798) ships wintering at SF

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 144

(Alberni → Borica, 30 Apr 1798) ships wintering at SF: if anyone aboard is infected with smallpox, no landing permitted; (1797) the Branciforte settlers may draw water from an arroyo ~200 *varas* off — the ordered *saca de agua* unnecessary.

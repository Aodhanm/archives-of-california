list of the annual San Blas supply ships to California. — The yearly San Blas → California supply vessels & pilots, 1783–96 — frigates *Favorita*, *Princesa*, *Concepción*, *Aránzazu*; packets *San Carlos*, *Aranzazú*; pilots incl. Esteban Martínez (the 1788 Nootka commander), José Cañizares, Francisco de Eliza (1792), Juan Matute (1790/95, the Bodega-expedition commander), Bertodano. → maritime history / the supply line.

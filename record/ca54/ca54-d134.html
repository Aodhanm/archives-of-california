Arrillaga → commandants of San Diego & San Francisco — bar foreign ships from the ports

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 134

Arrillaga → commandants of San Diego & San Francisco — bar foreign ships from the ports. — Transmits the Viceroy's order (14 Apr) + the Royal Order of 10 Aug 1802: foreign vessels in the Mar del Sur, 'so color de la pesca de ballena' (under pretext of whaling), are touching at the California coast and smuggling — they are to be denied entry to the ports per the standing Reales Prevenciones. ⭐ Early-1800s foreign-whaler/contraband problem on the CA coast.,.

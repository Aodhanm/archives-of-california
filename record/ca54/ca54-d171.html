*Hoja de Servicios* of Alférez Hermenegildo Sal. — ⭐ Sal's service record: served in the *Cuera* company 6 yrs 2 mo 17 days as Comisario/guarda-almacén; named Sargento 19 May 1782 (served 2 mo 10 days), Alférez 29 May 1782 (~6 yrs 7 mo by Dec 1788); continues at Monterey. ⭐ Native of Valdemoro [Spain], age 42 (→ b. ~1746); 'honrado y robusto.' → (primary career document — pins Sal's 1782 promotions + birthplace/age).

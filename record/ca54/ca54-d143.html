Real Orden on San Blas ship freight & commerce regulation

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 143

Real Orden on San Blas ship freight & commerce regulation. — On collecting freight (*fletes*) per the *arancel* for goods the commandants, chaplains, officers & missions imported via the brig *Activo* & frigate *Concepción* on the return from California; bars Marina employees/commandants from commerce; registers of cargo + passenger lists at each port. Refs José de Gálvez's 1769 visitation + the Reales Ordenanzas de la Armada. → (officers' private trade on supply ships).

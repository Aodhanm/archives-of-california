service record (*hoja de servicios*) of Sgt./Alférez Mariano Carrillo. — Began service 24 Jul 1756

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 76

service record (*hoja de servicios*) of Sgt./Alférez Mariano Carrillo. — Began service 24 Jul 1756; ~25 yrs by end of 1781. ⭐ Present at the 1769–70 founding expedition to the ports of Monterey & San Diego — held a post with 7 men and attended the scurvy-stricken crew of the packet *San Carlos*; assaulted by gentiles, defended with 5 *soldados de cuera* + 3 volunteers; commanded squads at Monterey & San Diego; in the 1775 San Diego mission revolt ('la sublevación de los indios cristianos de la cristiandad de San Diego, y gentiles de la Sierra, el año de 75') made repeated *entradas*, 'castigó y apresó toda la cabilla.' → (1775 SD revolt), founding-era prosopography.

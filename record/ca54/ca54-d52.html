State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 52

(1) Accounts: the Habil.do Gen.l will owe the presidio only $1,490 after deductions (from $9,349 less $1,572 tobacco, $53 mail, $4,246 *libranzas*); asks the gov. to adopt measures for the relief of soldiers & families. (2) ⭐ The cost of the Castillo/fuerte de San Joaquín [the new SF Fort Point battery at Cantil Blanco] amounts to $1,503.4½, advanced by the habilitación., (construction cost of the battery built 1794).

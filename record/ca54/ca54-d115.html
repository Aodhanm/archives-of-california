State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 115

routine admin bundles. — Notable items amid filler: ⭐ Viceroy's order that the habilitado Cancela hand over sums in favor of Hermenegildo Sal (i. 80–84); ⭐ provisions for a *lotería* (lottery) at Monterey (i. 90–1); arrival of the schooners *Saturnina* & *Mexicana* (i. 92–3) and the brig *Activo* & schooner *Valdesina* (i. 94+) — note *Saturnina*/*Activa* are the vessels named in the Bodega boundary letter (i.119). → (ship identification),.

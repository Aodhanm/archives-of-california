missionary retirement (Fr. Manuel Hortiguela). — Fr. Manuel Hortiguela — 14 yrs in the missions of Santiago & San José del Cabo (Baja, Cape region) + *cura interino* of the mining reales Santa Ana & San Antonio; retirement to be granted, but the R.P. Provincial to persuade him to stay until replaced. Baja admin.

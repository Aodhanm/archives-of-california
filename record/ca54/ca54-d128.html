State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 128

maritime + moral-regulation orders. — (1) Don't detain the San Blas ships & the Manila galleon (*nao de Filipinas*) at Monterey longer than necessary. (2) Heads of families to 'scrupulously correct' their children's 'excesos y desórdenes,' held responsible for resulting disturbances.

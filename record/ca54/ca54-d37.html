muster + equipment, SF presidial company. *(out-of-sequence 1784; founding-era)* — Founder Moraga commanding, 8 yrs after SF founding; 33 men. ⭐ '2 cañones montados en sus cureñas' — the cannon-decline baseline (SF: 2 cannon 1784 & 1787 → 1 burst 1792). ⭐ Decay note: 'en el temporal del agua cayó una esquina de la casamata' (powder-magazine corner collapsed in the rains). → the 1793 Bodega failure, the presidio-decay dossier.

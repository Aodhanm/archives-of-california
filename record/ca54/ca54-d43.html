officer/NCO promotion proposals. — For the new San Diego captaincy: 1) Raymundo Carrillo (33 yrs, cmd. Monterey & Sta Barbara), 2) Manuel Ruiz (Loreto), 3) José de la Guerra y Noriega (Sta Barbara).

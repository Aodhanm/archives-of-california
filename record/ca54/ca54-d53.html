State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 53

arrival of a King's ship. — On 29 Oct the San Blas frigate 'Nuestra Señora del Rosario' (a) 'La Princesa,' from Manila returning to San Blas, anchored; her commander Francisco Maurelle reported sighting the nao de Filipinas (Manila galleon) on the 21st, likely to anchor at Monterey. ⚠️ Savage names the commandant 'Joseph Moraga' — but founder José Joaquín Moraga died 1785; flag as a likely Savage slip (the SF commandant in late 1794 was Argüello/Sal).

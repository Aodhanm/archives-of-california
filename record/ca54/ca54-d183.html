Doc III-13 — Hermenegildo Sal → acting Gov. Arrillaga — 'Sobre la expedición de Vancouver.'

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 183

Doc III-13 — Hermenegildo Sal → acting Gov. Arrillaga — 'Sobre la expedición de Vancouver.' — ⭐⭐⭐ The fullest Sal narrative of Vancouver's SF visit (Nov 1792). The frigate Discovery (Capt. George Vancouver) anchored at Yerba Buena on the 13th in 4½ brazas; Sal sent Alférez Alejo Miranda as pilot 'con orden de no recibir ninguna gratificación'; on the 19th Vancouver rode to Santa Clara with an escort, 7 officers, Sgt. Pedro Amador & 8 soldiers on horses Sal furnished; the brig 'Chatham' under 'Puget' ('supongo es Broughton') arrived the 18th. ⭐ Provisioning list (some on the San Blas commander's account, much 'dado gratis por él' [Sal]): 11 + 2 cows, 2 calves, mutton, 10 arrobas lard, 30 pumpkins, 40 sacks of vegetables, 70 hens + 25 capons. ⭐ Separate note: the brig 'Chatham' (21st) — 'Don Juan de la Cuadra [Bodega y Quadra], no habiéndose remitido lo que le prometió' (the promised guns), Sal asks for a fortification.,. *(Plus a Sal note on Mission Santa Cruz being moved 300 paces uphill after flooding — Savage dates it '1802' but content [='el año anterior'] + Sal's authorship ⚠️ → almost certainly 1792.)*

Solá *borradores*, pt. 4 (artillery & campaigns)

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 154

Solá *borradores*, pt. 4 (artillery & campaigns). — ⭐ 74 militia artillerymen province-wide to be paid as veterans 'por todo el tiempo que estén sobre las armas' (→ ); ⭐ Lt. Manuel Gómez commissioned to emplace up to 3 cannons at Mission San Juan Bautista 'para impedir el desembarco de algunos invasores' (post-Bouchard coastal defense, 1819 → register, ); ⭐ repair of the batteries + Maestranza (ordnance shop); 40 reinforcements arrived from San Blas; Indian campaigns — Estudillo's 30-man *piquete* after horse-raiders, and the successful campaign vs. the Ranchería de los Muquelemnes (Mokelumne) reported to the Viceroy (; = mil-history register #33); Padre Narciso Durán named. Routine: militia pay, licenses, Sgt. José Sánchez → comisionado of Pueblo San José, Martínez transfer request.

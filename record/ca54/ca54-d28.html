State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 28

founding new missions in Baja CA. — Dominican mission Santo Tomás de Aquino (at San Solano) founded; 3 more proposed in the sierras between Rosario & Santo Domingo; $1,000/foundation; over Gov. Pedro Fages's objection; framed as facilitating Alta–Baja CA communication; proposed presidio at Laguna de Santa Olaya. (Addressed to Gov. Romeu — d. Apr 1792, cf. the Sal/Argüello command shuffle.)

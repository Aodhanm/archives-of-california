State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 33

diary, expedition against 'indios agresores.' — Out of Mission Santa Cruz, 9 men + 1 cpl., chasing fugitive Christian Indians + horse thieves; raid on Rancho San Benito; aggressors had fled to the Sierra; horse-thief Indian caught by mission Indians; a gentile captured; some horses recovered; returned the 13th. → (fugitive-neophyte/horse-theft punitive expedition).

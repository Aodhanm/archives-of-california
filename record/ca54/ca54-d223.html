Borica → Viceroy — Arrillaga's skirmish with the Colorado Indians

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 223

Borica → Viceroy — Arrillaga's skirmish with the Colorado Indians. — Forwards Arrillaga's report of his Colorado-River reconnaissance & skirmish: a numerous *indiada* attacked his party; 'prudencia y humanidad… hasta el caso forzoso de repeler la fuerza con la fuerza' — soldiers' gear hit by arrows, 1 mule killed, 8 horses wounded, a musket & lance lost; 2 enemy dead on the road + 3 in the lagoon. On opening CA–Sonora communication, 'ninguno como Arrillaga puede consultar lo que convenga.' → the Indian-raiding dossier, borderlands.

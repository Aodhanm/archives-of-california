Monterey 1809 deployment table + musters. — ⭐ Estudillo's 'estado de fuerza efectiva,' Monterey, 1 Nov 1809 — shows the garrison dispersed: only a few at the presidio, 4 on the battery (guardia de la batería), the rest scattered as mission escorts (San Carlos, San Antonio, Soledad, San Miguel, San Luis), the Rancho del Rey (7), the horse/cattle herds, SF, etc.; ~85 total incl. 5 artillerymen., (garrison dispersal). + cadet service records (Estrada, Santiago Argüello); SD 1784 muster (52).

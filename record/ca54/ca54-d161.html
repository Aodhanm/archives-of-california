Doc II-64 — Hermenegildo Sal → acting Gov. Arrillaga — English ship (Capt. Brown) at SF, and the English at Bodega. — ⭐⭐⭐ Bodega-article document. Sal reports a large English vessel off the SF mouth, 13 Mar 1793; 6 men landed at Cantil Blanco — English, captain named 'Brown,' coming from San Luis, bound for Nootka, begging wood/water/meat without entering port. The ship lingered 10 days 'sin perder de vista á Monterey y San Francisco,' fired a shot under English colors, was seen among the Farallones. ⭐⭐ Sal's assessment: this ship + a *balandra* that put in at Monterey went to Bodega, and 'por los naturales he sabido que aquí [Bodega] estuvieron más de seis meses' — indigenous intelligence that the English had frequented Bodega for 6+ months. Sal keeps livestock withdrawn, a Sunday lookout, 'sin perder instante de hacer correrías.' → (English at Bodega, 1793; extends the i.119 boundary-letter intelligence),, indigenous-intelligence (the 'guarda isleño' reporting ship movements). *(Capt. Brown likely William Brown of the Butterworth NW-coast traders — verify.)*

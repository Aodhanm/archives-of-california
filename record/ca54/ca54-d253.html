the Bouchard deserters' *causa* + a Russian brig

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 253

the Bouchard deserters' *causa* + a Russian brig. — ⭐⭐ Viceroy Conde del Venadito orders a *causa* formed against the 4 men 'que se pasaron… de los buques insurgentes de Buenos Aires' (Bouchard's ships) — named Nicolás Echeverría, Mateo José Pascual, Pedro Caldívar, Juan de la Rosa — Estudillo to prosecute (→ ); ⭐ a 'Bergantín Ruso' set sail from the Sta Bárbara roadstead, 21 Sep 1820 (→ ); a customs note.

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 97

mission-escort inspection + card-game ban. — Orders Parrilla to inspect the escorts of his district — Mission Soledad → San Antonio → San Luis [Obispo] — reviewing the troops' mounts, arms, and clothing, and to promulgate the prohibition of card games (*juegos de naipes*) (bando of 23 Sep), with a 15–20-day deadline. ← End of Tomo I (n0–n131).

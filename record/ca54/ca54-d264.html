Romeu: the Malaspina & Longinos Martínez expeditions + governance

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 264

Romeu: the Malaspina & Longinos Martínez expeditions + governance. — Romeu's chest-pain illness ('no se ha rendido'; he died Apr 1792) + his confirmation as governor (R.O. 18 Feb 1790); ⭐ arrival of the naturalist José Longinos Martínez & botanist Jaime Senseve (royal natural-history commission, on the *Princesa*, 1791); ⭐⭐ the corvettes *Descubierta* & *Atrevida* of Alejandro Malaspina's round-the-world expedition at Monterey (Sept 1791, w/ José Bustamante); death of Alférez José M.ª Estrada (Loreto). → scientific voyages, governance.

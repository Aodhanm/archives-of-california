State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 56

roster of all officers & sergeants in the Californias. — Columns: rank · commission date · age · yrs service. Capt: Loreto — T.Col. Arrillaga (12 Jun 1783, 45, 18). Tenientes: Loreto — José Pérez Fernández (17 Aug 1792, 44, 24); SD — Antonio Grajera (14 Jul 1792, 47, 22); Sta Bárb — Goycoechea (1783, 48, 13); Monterey — Hermenegildo Sal (27 Oct 1795, 49, 13); SF — José Argüello (9 Feb 1787, 42, 23). Alféreces: Loreto — Ildefonso Bernal (10 Jul 1792); SD — Pablo Grijalva (20 Jul 1787, 51, 33); Sta Bárb — Pablo Cota (13 Sep 1788); Monterey — Raymundo Carrillo (27 Oct 1795); SF — Manuel Rodríguez (20 Oct 1792). Sargentos: Loreto — José Manuel Ruiz (8 Feb 1790), Fco Javier Aguilar (22 May 1795); SD — Ignacio Alvarado (26 Feb 1788); Sta Bárb — José de Ortega (26 Dec 1788), Ignacio Olivera (5 Nov 1795); Monterey — Macario de Castro (5 May 1781, 42, 17); SF — Pedro Amador (24 Jul 1787, 52, 30). ⭐ Resolves the Sal-promotion flag: Sal's teniente commission is dated 27 Oct 1795 (consistent with Doc 7 showing him teniente by Dec 1796). → the Sal papers.

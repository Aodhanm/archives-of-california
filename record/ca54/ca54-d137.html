Santa Barbara & San Francisco munitions/arms inventories. — Santa Barbara (Goycoechea, Dec 1794)

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 137

Santa Barbara & San Francisco munitions/arms inventories. — Santa Barbara (Goycoechea, Dec 1794): 128 cannonballs, 100 grapeshot cartridges, '118 cartuchos con bala de á 6,' powder, lead plates — *the source of the '118 cartridges' SB figure cited elsewhere.* San Francisco (Argüello, Dec 1797): 1,441 cannonballs (8–24 lb), 245 arrobas powder, 9,814 musket cartridges. ⭐ SF presidial company Dec 1797 = 36 men (1 sgt, 4 cpls, 31 soldiers), each with escopeta, sword, lance, cartridge-box, *cuera* & *adarga*. → (arms/munitions/garrison strength).

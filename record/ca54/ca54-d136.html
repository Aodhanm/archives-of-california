Real Tribunal de Cuentas / Fiscal de Real Hacienda

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 136

Real Tribunal de Cuentas / Fiscal de Real Hacienda — expediente on the SF presidio's 1801 construction accounts. — The SF habilitado's accounts for construction in 1801 — the guard house, Cuartel (barracks), Almacenes (warehouses), quarters for the Volunteers detachment, and 4 houses for cavalry-troop families — total $2,143; the Tribunal faulted it for lacking a prior estimate/budget/permit. (+ Arrillaga's 1803 orders to continue necessary presidio works.) → (SF building program, 1801).

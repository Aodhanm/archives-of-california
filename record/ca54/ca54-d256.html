ambush near San Vicente / Santa Catalina. — ⭐⭐ An escort transporting 5 captured Indians was ambushed: 2 soldiers killed (Juan Camacho + 1), beaten on the head with stones, muskets found loaded but broken; the prisoners escaped; bodies buried at San Vicente.; logged to the military register as engagement #88 (distinct from the 1804 Santa Catalina victory, #37). ⚠️ Pin the year.

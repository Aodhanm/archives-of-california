State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 114

(Manuel de Vargas → Argüello, Sep 23) many vecinos attest Ávila's bad conduct — 'consideran como un bien á la humanidad el hacerle salir de ese lugar'; (Arrillaga → Argüello, Dec 14) orders Ávila embarked on a San Blas ship — i.e. deported from California. Also two short notes (widow Josefa Gandiaga's accounts; deserter José Pedraza remitted to the naval commander per Nava's order).

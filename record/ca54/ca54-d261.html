LA communion list + an Indian punished. — Los Ángeles pueblo

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 261

LA communion list + an Indian punished. — Los Ángeles pueblo: 116 people (61 men, 55 women) fulfilled the Easter precept (1796 — demographic datapoint); ⭐ Argüello: an 18-yr-old Christian Indian given 25 lashes for throwing a stone (without hitting) at soldier Venancio Galindo, sent to make him pray. → the pueblo papers, the Indian-raiding dossier.

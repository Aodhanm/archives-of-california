Doc III-18 — 1793 armament report + musters. — ⭐⭐⭐ Goycoechea → Arrillaga, 9 Jul 1793 — province armament (the 1793 Nootka-crisis defense assessment): Monterey = 2 six-pounder cannon + 1 small field gun (3 guns), 16-man garrison, 90 pairs of pistols needing repair, 28@ powder, 200 flints — 'should be reinforced with troops + 2 small cannon'; Santa Barbara = ~50 escopetas (23 good), short 27 swords, lances complete.,. Also: Santa Barbara 1793 muster = 61; the Mazatlán Squadron at San Luis Rey, 1835 = 33 men (Portilla/Ibarra; Mexican-era); 1780 Croix regs (officers marrying for widow-pensions; war-tax bando vs. the English).

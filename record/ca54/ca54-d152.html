Solá *borradores*, pt. 2. — Cadet appointments (Joaquín Estudillo → Monterey company

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 152

Solá *borradores*, pt. 2. — Cadet appointments (Joaquín Estudillo → Monterey company; José Guadalupe Moraga → San Diego company); cattle payments to Mission San Juan Bautista; discharges; missionary aid; promotion of Ignacio Martínez (Sta Bárbara). ⭐ Militia-artillery allocation (24 Jul 1817): 23 artilleros milicianos for San Francisco, 20 Monterey, 12 Santa Barbara, 16 San Diego (→ ). ⭐⭐ French merchant corvette *Bordelais* under Camille de Roquefeuil (Sept–Nov 1818) — same ship as the prior year, bound for China, extending the NW-coast fur trade (*peletería*); allowed only strict necessities, no crew–shore contact, must pay duties (→ ). ⭐ Foreign 'rebel' ships off Baja (San Quintín / San Vicente, Feb 1819) — 3 vessels watching the coast, judged 'los rebeldes… ya tienen otro buque de armamento… acaso á alguna parte van á dar el golpe'; hot-shot (*hornillos de bala roja*) ordered (, military).

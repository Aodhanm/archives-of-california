State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 167

Hermenegildo Sal → Cabo Luis Peralta — Instructions for the Mission Santa Cruz escort (25 articles). — A detailed *instrucción* for the escort commander: rations for soldiers & the 6 mission servants; build a communal *rancho*; tobacco/soap/chocolate allowances; daily Rosary; monthly courier to Santa Clara; weekly arms/munitions/clothing review. ⭐ A Neve-style conciliatory Indian-policy code — disarm Indians before they enter the mission; 'buen trato' with the gentiles; if forced to use arms, suspend hostilities the instant they make peace and conciliate 'con la mas posible suavidad'; troops not to enter rancherías or harm Indians; watch for pasture-burning; escort the padres; no gambling. ⭐ Parallels the Carrillo Santa Inés *instrucción* (C-A 11)., indigenous relations.

Gov. Fages (transmitting the Mexico Audiencia / Rengel) — governance succession. — On the deaths of Viceroy Matías de Gálvez and Cmdt.-Gen. Felipe de Neve (both 1784): the Mexico *Audiencia* assumes the Superior Gobierno; José Antonio Rengel acting Cmdt.-Gen.; Fages continues in interim political-military command of the Provincias Internas / Californias pending the King's decision. ⭐ Dates Neve's death (the same Neve whose *Instrucción* is Tomo I Doc 70). → governance, prosopography.

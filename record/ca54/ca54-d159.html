State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 159

Hermenegildo Sal → Gov. — soldier Flores's illicit cattle-trade with the gentiles. — Soldier Bernardino [/Hermenegildo ⚠️] Flores spent '13, 20 días y hasta un mes' slaughtering cattle on the Monterey plain 'para comerciar ilícitamente con los Gentiles' — recommends his banishment to San Blas in irons; accomplice Rafael Gómez (less culpable, 'inducido de Flores') released with admonishment. → (late Sal letter), (illicit frontier trade).

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 80

Easter-communion *cédulas*. — Troops to present *cédulas del cumplimiento de Iglesia* by Pentecost; each commandant to report compliance. ⭐ Followed by the four commandants' receipts, which incidentally fix spring-1793/94 commands: Zúñiga — San Diego (Apr 1), Goycoechea — Sta Bárbara (Apr 8), José Argüello — Monterey (Apr 17), Hermenegildo Sal — San Francisco (Apr 22)..

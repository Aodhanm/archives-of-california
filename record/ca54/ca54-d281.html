VERBATIM HEADING: '1787 = Setiembre 11 = Arispe. / Jacobo Ugarte y Loyola á P. Fages' GLOSS: Commandant-General Ugarte y Loyola to Governor Pedro Fages. SUBSTANCE: 'Manda copia de una disposicion de Fernando José Mangino [interlined: de 4 de Julio] sobre que se establezca una administracion de tabacos en Monterey, en los mismos terminos y circunstancias que la de Loreto.' Cite reads 'f. 43-48 (2 doc.s)'. DISTINCT from the 1787 Ugarte y Loyola item already summarised inside the published n285–n288 row: that one is dated '1787 = Noviembre 7 = Arispe' and cites f. 26-31 (3 docs) on leaf n286, which I opened to confirm the two are different documents.

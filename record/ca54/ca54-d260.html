“Fondo de gratificación” of the Loreto presidial company

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 260

“Fondo de gratificación” of the Loreto presidial company: “Entradas $4002.00 / Salidas 883.00 / Resulta á favor del Fondo $3118.00.” [Year-end statement of the company's gratification fund. Leaf n482 also carries the tail of the officer-promotion ternas document anchored at n477 — the Pablo Cota entry and the recommendation of Sal “p.r su antigüedad, y disposicion p.a visitar las ranch.s de gentiles y mantenerlos pacificos”.]

Borica → Viceroy — settler economy + 5 new missions

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 226

Borica → Viceroy — settler economy + 5 new missions. — ~400 *gente de razón* at San José & Los Ángeles feed themselves + supply the 4 presidios but lack clothing/tools (proposes an annual *memoria* for the settlers); ⭐ on founding 5 new missions in the gaps (SD–SJ Capistrano, San Gabriel–San Buenaventura, etc.) — the 1797 mission-expansion program., mission history.

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 271

province-wide officer/habilitado roster + administrative structure. — ⭐ The officers holding royal-commission posts in California (1802) with commission dates & annual salaries: Loreto — Gov. *vacante* ($4,000), T.Col. Arrillaga (12 Jun 1783, $1,500), Pérez Fernández ($550), Manuel Ruiz ($400); San Diego — Manuel Rodríguez (11 Feb 1802, $550), José Luján ($400); Sta Bárbara — Goycoechea, etc. + a summary of the administrative system (commandants also hold political jurisdiction; habilitados run Real Hacienda accounts unpaid; 'no hay en la Provincia empleos de tiempo determinado, sino el de Gobernador'). → prosopography, (officer salaries). ← END of Tomo IV — and of the volume (n515).

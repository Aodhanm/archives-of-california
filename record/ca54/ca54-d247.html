State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 247

Acknowledges receipt of an account of Ramón Lazo and files it; then “Propone que se reconozca la posibilidad de hacer un camino (nuevo) de San Francisco á Santa Cruz.” [Sal proposes that the feasibility of opening a NEW ROAD from San Francisco to Santa Cruz be surveyed — an early internal-communications proposal; feeds the Sal / Habilitado's Empire corpus.]

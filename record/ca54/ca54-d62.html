labor for the San Diego fort. — Preceded by the timber bill-of-materials for the SD fort (80 posts of 5 *varas*, 80 beams of 7 *varas*, 600 roofing *morillos*). Argüello requests 12 yoke of oxen, fully harnessed, + 12 Indians who know how to drive them from Mission Carmel to haul the timber to the *embarcadero*. ⭐ Mission-Indian labor + oxen mobilized for the San Diego coastal-fort works. → (Indian construction labor).

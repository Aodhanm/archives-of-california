Nicolás Soler → Hermenegildo Sal — Royal Order banning officers' *repartimientos*

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 129

Nicolás Soler → Hermenegildo Sal — Royal Order banning officers' *repartimientos*. — Transmits a Royal Order (via the Cmdt.-Gen., from José de Gálvez) prohibiting captains from making 'repartimientos y habilitaciones' to the soldiers of their own companies (the company-supply profiteering abuse, per the army *ordenanzas*, tomo 1, trat. 11, tít. 10) — to be enforced in presidios, *compañías volantes*, and all troops of New Spain. ⭐ Addressed to Sal at Monterey, 1788 (then storekeeper/alférez).,.

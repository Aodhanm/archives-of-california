State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 249

Krusenstern aid order + SB fiscal. — ⭐⭐ Viceroy Iturrigaray (9 Dec 1803) transmits a R.O. to aid, in case of forced arrival, 'los buques de la expedición Americana Rusa bajo órdenes del capitán Krusenstern' — the first Russian circumnavigation (Russian-American Company, 1803–06) (→ Russian California, borderlands); + the Sta Bárbara company account (de la Guerra & Martínez, 1818, *alcance* $25,463); a Fages→Sal note (1787).

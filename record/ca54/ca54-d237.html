Hermenegildo Sal's 1800 habilitado/Monterey docs

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 237

Hermenegildo Sal's 1800 habilitado/Monterey docs. — ⭐ Sal (Tente de Caballería & Monterey cmdt., months before his death) certifies the artisans contracted to teach the mission neophytes (with their assignments); copies re the master saddler José de los Reyes + wife arriving from San Blas (Borica's note + the contract); the Monterey habilitado annual account (Data $104,460 / *alcance* $4,706). → the Sal papers, artisan program.

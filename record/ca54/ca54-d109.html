José Antonio Rengel (acting Cmdt.-Gen.) → Gov. Pedro Fages — 'Prosperidad de Establecimientos.'

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo I · BANC MSS C-A 54, document 109

José Antonio Rengel (acting Cmdt.-Gen.) → Gov. Pedro Fages — 'Prosperidad de Establecimientos.' — Commends the flourishing pueblos of San José and Los Ángeles (settler-built houses, abundant harvests in pueblos & missions, livestock increase); approves the order that habilitados not request seed from San Blas (local harvest suffices); commends the Los Ángeles pueblo head Hermenegildo Vicente Feliz; on land grants (*registro de sitios*) for grazing with the Asesor's prior opinion. → the pueblo papers (early LA/San José prosperity, 1785).

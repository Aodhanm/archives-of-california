Verbatim heading: '1834. Junio 30. Sn. Franco / Damaso A. Rodriguez - Compa. presidial'. Gloss: half-year return and pay account of the San Francisco presidial company. Substance: officers, sergeants and buglers the same as in 1833; 3 cabos, 22 soldados (some months 1 to 3 soldados more). Haberes y gratificn. of the company from January to 30 June, 4703 pesos.

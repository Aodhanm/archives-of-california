Verbatim heading: '1834 Junio 30. Mont. / José Mª Herrera, Comisº - Piquete del batallon permte de Hidalgo'. Gloss: comisario Herrera on the pay accrued by the Hidalgo battalion piquet. Substance: 'Haberes devengados en los 6 meses de Enero á la fha ---- $26 444.' I read the sum as $26,444, but the digits after '26' are cramped and I am not fully certain of them.

✓ VERIFIED vs scans n139+n141 (2026-07-07): header '1831 Mzo 11 S. Carlos, Gómez, Asesor del Territᵒ — Proceso contra el mayordomo García por haber azotado cruelmente á neófitos y gentiles de S. Diego'; the fiscal's 10-yr-presidio demand + Juan Bandini as *defensor* discrediting the gentile witnesses ('son de gentiles impuestos á echar mentiras') both confirmed. Proceso vs. the mayordomo García of the Estancia de Santa Isabel (SD) for cruelly flogging SD neophytes & gentiles caught for cattle-theft — 7 Indians incl. an old captain of the Ranchería de los Cogriñes[?] flogged to death ('se acabaron 3 rollos de varas,' García tore out the captain's hair). Fiscal del Valle sought 10 yrs presidio, condemning García (an ex-cabo who knew the rules) for punishing 'arbitrariamente.' Defender Juan Bandini (Diputación vocal/interim SD asesor) defended routine flogging — 'tan común en toda la Cal.,' 'el único medio q[u]e hay para contenerlos en los robos' — and argued the captain died of a hunger strike, not the beating. Asesor Gómez: 5 yrs presidio; Gov. Victoria confirmed 20 Apr 1831. → verbatim in Expanded Entry C. Orig. pp.332–510.

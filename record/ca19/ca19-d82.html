Causa criminal: Alcalde Soberanes instructed a sumaria for cattle-theft

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 82

Causa criminal: Alcalde Soberanes instructed a sumaria for cattle-theft — 4 reses killed (tracks found by Luis [G.?], the carcasses buried in the woods near his house) — examining Andrés Escamilla and the militiaman Antonio Aguilar. Closing note: 'Devolvieron parte del robo por conducto del Alcalde, y se castigaron gubernativamente.' Orig. pp.281–291.

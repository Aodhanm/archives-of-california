Verbatim text begins on n208: 'Orden pa qe Peguero recoja el plomo - qe halla' and completes on the first two lines of n209 (printed corner 204): 'en esa Misn (Soledad) sin qe se le ponga pretesto alguno.' Gloss: order that Peguero collect the lead to be found at that mission (Soledad), no pretext to be raised against him.

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 6

San Diego presidial company review, Aug 1829. Capt. José Mª Estudillo; Tte. Argüello; Alférez 1º Ignacio Argüello (absent in México as Habilitado General); 1º agregado Domingo Carrillo; 6 cabos, 51 soldados (short 13) = 58 + officers; active pay $1,425, invalids $227 (incl. Capt. Fco. Mª Ruiz, Tte Juan Mainer[?], 3 sargtos, 9 soldados). SD garrison on the 15th: artillery 19, cavalry 68, Mazatlán active militia 9. Orig. pp.156–164.

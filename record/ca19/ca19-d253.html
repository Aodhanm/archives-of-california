Verbatim heading: '1834 Ab.l 1. Mont. / Jose Rafl. Gonzalez. Admor. de Aduana - Dhos. de importn. cobrados en puerto de San Franco.' Gloss: import duties collected at the port of San Francisco. Substance: the sum was made up by Pedro del Castillo, Receptor de Sn. Fco., alcabalas terrestres 147 pesos, plus 15 pesos from the same on salt landed by the national schooner Refugio; total 162 pesos. Arithmetic checks (147 + 15 = 162). Cite at foot: p 26.

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 128

Deportation roster of the Solís-revolt prisoners shipped on the American barque *Voluntaria/Volunter* to San Blas 'á la disposición del Supremo Gob.no' — 15 individuals: José Mª Herrera (sub-comisario provisional, lodged 'en un camarote de proa de la cámara y recluido á él'), Joaquín Solís (paisano — the revolt's leader), soldiers of the Monterey cav. Raimundo de la Torre, Pablo Vejar, Gonzalo & Victoriano Altamirano, Leonardo Arceo; perm. artillery Máximo & Antonio Guevara, Gracia Larios, cabos Mariano Pequero & Andrés de León (shackled '2 barras con un anillo cada uno'); paisanos Melecio Soto, Serapio Escamilla; and presidiario Inés/José Polanco (10-yr sentence from 1822 ('desde 12 de Nbre de 822')). Received by John Coffin Jones, master of the *Volunteer*. The core Solís-deportation record (cf. Docs 91, 131). Orig. pp.679–680.

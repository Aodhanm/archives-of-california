Verbatim heading: '1834 Ab.l 30. Mont. / Jose Rafael Gonzalez. Admor. de Aduana - Renta recibida de San Diego.' Gloss: revenue received from San Diego. Substance: alcabalas paid at San Diego by the national sloop Mariquita, 1006 pesos 4 reales, plus maritime duties paid at San Diego by the same sloop, 78 pesos 7 reales; total 1085 pesos 3 reales. The peso-real arithmetic checks exactly (1006p4r + 78p7r = 1085p3r), which confirms the reading of the first figure. Cite at foot: p 30. NOTE: same date as an already-published n392 item (Renta de aguardte. y vino, orig pp. 24-9) but a different document and a different page cite. [corner: Savage tomo LXXVI p. 4]

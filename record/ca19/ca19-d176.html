Causa: a representation by Capt. Miguel González against José Joaquín Maitorena

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 176

Causa: a representation by Capt. Miguel González against José Joaquín Maitorena — never prosecuted (no officers available under Victoria, 1831); the Méx. Superioridad referred it to Gov. Figueroa (Apr 1833); Zamorano certifies (16 Jan 1834) the motive ceased on Maitorena's death, and Figueroa ordered it suspended & archived. Orig. pp.128–129.

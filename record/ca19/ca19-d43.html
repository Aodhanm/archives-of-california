Causa 'por adhesión á España' vs. Don Manuel Herrera (b. Tepic, age 55)

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 43

Causa 'por adhesión á España' vs. Don Manuel Herrera (b. Tepic, age 55): accused of telling the ayuntamiento '¿por qué no se pronunciaba por España?' and of not even raising the national flag on the 16th [Sept]. Fiscal: impose the merited penalty. An expulsion-era political-loyalty case. Orig. pp.455–476.

Verbatim heading: '1833 Dic. 31. Santa Barba. / Jose Ma. Herrera, Sub. Comiso. Compa. presidial'. Gloss: year-end accounting of the Santa Barbara presidial company. Substance: 1 Capn. Jose de la Guerra; Alfz. 1o Domingo Carrillo; id. 2o Anasto. Carrillo; 3 cabos, 29 soldados. Force on 30 Nov was the same officers with 2 cabos and 34 soldados, the same as in September and October; in the earlier months of the year, besides the said officers, 2 cabos and 33 soldados. Total haberes y gratificn. of the active company per the accounts 11,615 pesos. Monthly pay of each individual: Capitan 125; 1o Alfz. 50; 2o id. 41.5.3; Cabo 25; Soldado 20. Gratificacion mensual de la Compa. 41.5.04. Cite at foot: pp 364-83. [corner: Savage tomo LXXVI p. 28]

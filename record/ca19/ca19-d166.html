Register of ship arrivals & departures at Monterey, 28 Dec 1830 – 31 Aug 1831: *Entradas* incl. barca Am. *Volunteer* (Capt. Carter), berg. Am. *Convoy* (from the Sandwich Isl.), frag. Am. *Pocahontas*, frag. *Louisa* (from Sitka — RAC), balandra *Guillermo Chiquito*, berg. ingl. *Ayacucho* (from Callao), berg. Mex. *Catalina* (Capt. Holmes, from San Blas); + the matching *Salidas*. Foreign maritime traffic at Monterey, incl. the Russian Sitka connection. Orig. pp.668–669.

Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 146

Report corroborating the imprisoned subalterns' statements (cabo Mariano Vaquero, artilleryman Antonio Guerra, paisanos Sergio Escamilla & Melitón Soto): Escamilla went as commissioner to SB and made the garrison revolt; names M.l Herrera as author of the Plan (per Solís's own declaration); Herrera induced soldier Pablo Vejar to rebel. Orig. (within the process).

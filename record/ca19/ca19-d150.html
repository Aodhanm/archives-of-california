Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo LXIX–LXXVII · BANC MSS C-A 19, document 150

Causa criminal vs. cavalry soldier Juan Ant.o Arceo (Savage writes Arces thereafter) for treacherously wounding the Santa Bárbara neófito Francisco Javier (12 Aug 1831). Asesor Lic. Rafael Gómez (7 Sep 1832): the wound was not grave (the victim recovered quickly, 'fué por causa de sus embriagueces') and had no aggravating circumstance — Arces had already purged the offense with ~6 months' prison; released to continue service. Orig. pp.21–106.

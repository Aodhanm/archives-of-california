Dictamen on the gov.'s request to move to Sta Bárbara

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 23

Dictamen on the gov.'s request to move to Sta Bárbara: the junta is not obliged to leave / abandon the capital; excites the pueblos to the defense of patria and liberty; asks the government for the clearest explanation whether the Sonoma 'aventureros' can with certainty be considered the work of the American government; establish a weekly correo through all the pueblos

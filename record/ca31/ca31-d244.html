On the troop pronunciamiento rejecting Carrillo that forced dissolution of the force

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 244

On the troop pronunciamiento rejecting Carrillo that forced dissolution of the force: Castro to form militias as needed; it was never the Comandancia's business to organize auxiliary corps; cannot strip auxiliary officers per the July supreme order; the Supreme Govt charges putting the towns in a state of defense; strict enforcement of the military-fuero laws

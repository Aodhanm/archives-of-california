[= published record #5] Reglamento for the 'Defensores de la Independencia y de las Leyes' militia — prompted by the occupation *'y de la conocida repugnancia de los ciudadanos pª prestarse voluntariamente al servicio'*: all male citizens 15–60 declared soldiers; compulsory enlistment (fuero eclesiástico / permanent military excepted); all-cavalry companies (86 dragoons, smaller of 50); officers elected & literate, captains named by the gov.; the gov. as supreme chief places the militia at the military commander's disposition; same pay as the army; gov. may modify arts 3 & 5; observed *'hasta sea tiempo de paz.'* Signed Olvera, de la Guerra

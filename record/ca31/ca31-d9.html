Reglamento for the defense of the city: the vecino force divided into 3 companies

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 9

Reglamento for the defense of the city: the vecino force divided into 3 companies — artillery under Miguel Pryor, riflemen under B.D. Wilson, cavalry under Jorge Palomares; a comandante principal chosen by the whole force; subordinate to the comandante general, in accord with the subprefect. Signed M. Requena, E. de Celis, Fran.co Figueroa, B.D. Wilson, Julian Workman; approved by A. Stearns

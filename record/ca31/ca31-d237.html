Confirms the treasury order suspending Micheltorena's debts

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 237

Confirms the treasury order suspending Micheltorena's debts; relays Sutter's warning of foreigners entering via the Sacramento and Pico's order to the Prefect to impede 'la introducción de 1000 familias que se esperan del Oregon'; Pico tied up installing the electoral college to elect Senators; the troops have *'desconocido la autoridad de Dn Antonio Carrillo'*; inadvisable that José A. Carrillo take the Comandancia (troops' ill-will); don't let it be said *'que no se saben gobernar, estando los destinos en manos de hijos de California'*

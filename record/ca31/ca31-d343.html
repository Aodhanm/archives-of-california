'Sobre introducción de extrangeros': analyzing Pico's opening discourse + Bandini's proposal

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 343

'Sobre introducción de extrangeros': analyzing Pico's opening discourse + Bandini's proposal — the fears over foreigner introduction, the ineffective supreme order, and that the influx aims to bring enough foreigners *'para obligar la separación de los habitantes Mejicanos'* (a Texas-style annexation); the national integrity threatened; refs *'cuestiones de Oregon y Californias'* in the govt press *(incomplete)*

On the apprehension of José Antonio Carrillo and accomplices

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 246

On the apprehension of José Antonio Carrillo and accomplices: shock at the party sent to seize the Government's correspondence (*'el acontecimiento de la madrugada del 16'*) — *'un crimen… trascendental, atentatoria á la seguridad individual'*; recalls the officer-assessment list Castro wrote in his own hand; sustained self-rule rhetoric (*'que somos capaces para guiar y desempeñar los destinos que ocupamos… gobernados por nuestros paisanos los Californios'*); asks Castro to put Carrillo & accomplices at the Monterey Prefect's disposition; notes Carrillo spread the false rumor that Pico had rejected Castro as Comandante General (*'falso falsísimo'*); asks Castro to name the LA military commander

Forwards a letter from Mr. [Elijah] White, U.S

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 238

Forwards a letter from Mr. [Elijah] White, U.S. sub-agent of Indian affairs in Oregon, addressed to Pico, + a copy of White's dispatch to Washington; concerns an Oregon Indian-chief matter involving a U.S. national (now naturalized Mexican) who had succeeded at Sutter's establishment in 1844; urges prompt action; references the English frigate *América* about to sail from SF; offers cooperation *(copy/translation made at LA, 24 Sept)*

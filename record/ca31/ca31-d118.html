Los hombres todos murieron, cuatro mujeres tomamos y una niña, una mujer escapó con algunos muchachos'*; the horse and horsehide proved them the raiders. ⚠ my direct read = 5 women (4 taken + 1 escaped) + a girl; the published record #6 says '8 women' — flagged as a probable transcription error in the targeted pass, to reconcile at volume-end

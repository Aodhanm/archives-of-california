Orders for LA's defense / Castro's movements: Pico will *'dar un golpe mortal á la hidra peligrosa'*

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 41

Orders for LA's defense / Castro's movements: Pico will *'dar un golpe mortal á la hidra peligrosa'*; Stearns to *'escitar, promover y apurar hasta la hez á todo el vecindario'* to march to Sta Bárbara under Juan Gallardo and defend the city against 'los anarquistas y los sospechosos'; intelligence from the British warship anchored at Sta Bárbara that Castro reached Monterey the 14th with 70 men then vanished.

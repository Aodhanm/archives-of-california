On the treasury difficulty, *'el acusado de ser Mejicano'*

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 159

On the treasury difficulty, *'el acusado de ser Mejicano'*: with the treasury now at LA, suggests the administrator be ordered not to hand him anything further; verbatim — *'Si entrego la tesorería se me acusa de estar en favor de V.… si no la entrego, que soy mejicano'*

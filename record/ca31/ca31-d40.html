Transmittal/protest quoting Castro's whole 8 Jun communication and answering it

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 40

Transmittal/protest quoting Castro's whole 8 Jun communication and answering it: brands Castro's faction *'anarquistas con maquinaciones audaces'* who would precipitate *'un porvenir lleno de tristeza y lamentación'*; the Assembly's honor *'altamente ultrajado'*; pledges to *'poner un dique á semejantes atentados'*

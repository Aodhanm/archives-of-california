The treasury-transfer controversy from the incumbent's side

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 156

The treasury-transfer controversy from the incumbent's side: in this department *'los asuntos… los hacen siempre personales'*; when Castro learned the Treasury would move, it became public and the population blamed Abrego; charges Pico with favoring his own pueblo and, with revenues scarce, installing a 2,000-peso treasurer + secretary; suggests a Monterey resident (Manuel Castro, Rico, Serrano, or Rafael Sánchez); advises Pico come settle it

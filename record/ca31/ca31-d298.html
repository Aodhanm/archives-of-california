Commission (José María Covarrubias + San Diego juez Ramón Argüello) to the Baja frontier

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 298

Commission (José María Covarrubias + San Diego juez Ramón Argüello) to the Baja frontier: to demonstrate that, no law having separated Baja from Alta California, the frontier's political authorities must remain subject to the Alta California government; use moderation; those who do not recognize the Departmental Government are *'sustraídos del orden constitucional'*; proceed by popular election to name judges

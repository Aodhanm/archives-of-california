Election of new Assembly vocals (Art. 133 Bases Orgánicas)

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 371

Election of new Assembly vocals (Art. 133 Bases Orgánicas): Bandini and Osio tied at 4 votes, decided by lot (a child drew 'Suerte') — Bandini 5th vocal, Abrego 6th, Santiago Argüello 7th; suplentes Joaquín Carrillo, Stearns, Argüello, Olvera, Palomares, Antonio Pico, Joaquín Ortega. ⚠ closely parallels T8-61 (also '1846-10-06') — possibly the same election entered twice; reconcile at volume-end

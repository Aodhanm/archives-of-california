Reconciliation letter on the eve of the crisis

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 3

Reconciliation letter on the eve of the crisis: hopes Castro will join him to combat 'la desgraciada situación del país'; answers Castro's 27 Apr letter; reproaches Castro for provoking the Junta de Guerra; recalls sending Fran.co de la Guerra to invite a meeting Castro 'trató con desprecio'; appeals that both work so 'los pueblos respiren con libertad'; Dn Pablo carries the Assembly's decree. Reiterates his love of country

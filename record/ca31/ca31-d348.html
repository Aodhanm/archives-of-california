Additional articles to the 8 Oct 1845 hide-trade decree

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 348

Additional articles to the 8 Oct 1845 hide-trade decree: hides brought to the beach for export from points without a *'fierro nacional'* need the pass (Art. 6); 5-peso fines; brand-inspection (*visación de cueros*) and confiscation (surplus to the municipalities)

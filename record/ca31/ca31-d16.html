Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 16

Military movement & 'recelo de estranjeros': gathered the useful vecindario at Monterrey, marched to Sta Cruz for horses, joined the comandante general at Sta Clara, drew in the San José vecindario and formed the 2nd section now marching on the Sonoma frontier 'invadida por el enemigo'; great fear 'que los estranjeros quieran apoderarse del departamento como lo hicieron con Tejas'; urges aiding the comandante general against enemies exterior and interior

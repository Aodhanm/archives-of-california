Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 179

Resigns the prefecture: cannot hold authority against *'personas del país que desconozen la de él y del gobr'*; a better state is impossible *'mientras dure la discordia entre Pico y Castro'*; the foreigners threaten; José Castro 'no tiene mas que 20 hombres armados'; José Castro wanted the district put at his disposal to expel the foreigners but Manuel *'desconfiaba de su buen tino'*; begs Pico come to Monterey

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 245

Second letter: writing *'en obvio de dar lugar á todo lo que parezca cuestión entre ámbos,'* he appeals that both share *'la gloria'* of ordering the department by uniting — else *'¿con qué derecho serán acreedores á la gratitud de los Californios?'*; warns that if hijos del país sustain only *'partidos,'* neglect the laws, and fail to protect *'las propiedades amagadas por los indios,'* none will want to be governed by their countrymen; having overthrown *'un Gefe que abusaba del poder'* (Micheltorena), the authorities must act in accord

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 341

To remedy the country's ills, the surplus of 2/3 of departmental revenues (after civil salaries) is to be invested *'precisamente en el alistamiento de hombres que hagan la campaña formal contra los indios bárbaros y seguridad del Departamento'* (Arts 2–7: suspend 1/3 of the civil list if needed; separate accounting; voluntary contributions counted as merit; a donations book; lists published every 15 days; the decree ceases when the cause ends).

On posting a force at the Cajon Pass: given the frequent horse-robberies in the LA demarcation…

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 345

On posting a force at the Cajon Pass: given the frequent horse-robberies in the LA demarcation, establish a year-long contracted detachment *'sin mas objeto que contener á los indios ladrones… y castigarlos'* (Art. 2: the govt may contract a loan + solicit a ranchos' donation; Art. 3: govt names/removes the commander; Art. 4: govt provides arms; Art. 5: govt sets salaries and regulates service)

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 115

'Contribuciones de guerra': given the near-total lack of resources and that *'algunos individuos ingratos á su patria seducidos por los invasores… por temor ó por… conservar… los bienes de fortuna,'* proposes rancho owners (exempt from arms) give horses and cattle; a levy assessed by 5 city notables; Mexican property-owners without rural capital give cash; naturalized foreigners invited to contribute voluntarily

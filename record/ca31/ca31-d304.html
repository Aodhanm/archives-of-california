Distribution of the *Tasso* and *Primavera* duties for the Monterey customs

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 304

Distribution of the *Tasso* and *Primavera* duties for the Monterey customs: income 16,529 (Tasso aforada by J.B. Alvarado at San Diego); expenses to Mellus (8,115), J.A. Carrillo (500, Tribunal minister), Figueroa (390) & Pryor (500, lead for Castro's division), Olvera (300, Assembly sec.), Mellus (370, govt-house furniture), the Governor (2,046), Covarrubias, John Paty of the barca *Don Quijote* (2,000, for the passage of Gen. Micheltorena and his troops), Andrés Pico, Bandini, Alvarado, José Castro, Ignacio del Valle, tobacco for Micheltorena at San Pedro, govt-house repairs (250); balances to 0

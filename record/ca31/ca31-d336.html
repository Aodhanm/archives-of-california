On the sale of Missions: the commission examined Bandini's proposal

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 336

On the sale of Missions: the commission examined Bandini's proposal; agrees the missions must be saved from total ruin but opposes selling them without the government first trying other means; *'es triste ver inocentes pagar deudas ajenas'* (protecting the Indians); the sale would bring no advantage to the government

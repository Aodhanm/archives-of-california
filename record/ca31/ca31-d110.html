Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 110

Conciliatory note (in English): encloses his summons to Castro to surrender, his proclamation to the inhabitants, and the pre-landing general order; assures *'not the least impropriety has been committed'*; comes *'in arms with a powerful force… as the best friend of California'*; invites Pico to meet him at Monterey with a pledge of respectful treatment and free departure; has employed all means *'to stop the sacrifice of human life by the party in the North'*

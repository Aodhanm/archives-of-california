Running whaler-anchorage dispute: the *Espadón* captain doubts the unsealed oficio; requests a tariff

Departmental State Papers, tomo VII–VIII · BANC MSS C-A 31, document 314

Running whaler-anchorage dispute: the *Espadón* captain doubts the unsealed oficio; requests a tariff; whaler goods not in the arancel, captains demand the law; the port captain undercharged (13 vs 30 pesos); the U.S. frigate *Martha* refused the 30-peso anchorage and set sail 8 Nov

'Misiones de los RR.PP. Fernandinos' — mission-by-mission census of Alta California 1815–16

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 185

'Misiones de los RR.PP. Fernandinos' — mission-by-mission census of Alta California 1815–16: for each of the 21 missions (+ presidios & pueblos San José / Los Ángeles), the # of ministers, neophyte baptisms (mayores/menores), and total souls. Total Alta Cal. 1816 ≈ 22,432 souls; net increase 508. A key mission-demography table. Orig. p.546

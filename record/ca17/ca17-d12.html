Full company account (cargo/data). *Cargo:* carryover deficit from Dec 1808 $10,655

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 12

Full company account (cargo/data). *Cargo:* carryover deficit from Dec 1808 $10,655; *memoria* from México $22,222 + San Blas $1,442; company débitos $5,010; derechos (inválidos/montepío/retenciones) $443; rentas $1,654; habilitados' debts to the missions (SF $1,173, Sta Clara $1,258, Sta Cruz $530, San José $287, San Luis Obispo $309) + Monterey presidio $497; total cargo $46,218. *Data:* haberes $18,809; ⭐ 'suministro de víveres á los Americanos y Rusos' $289 (detained American & Russian otter-hunters — pre-Farallones; cf. the 1812–13 Russian-prisoner accounts); etc.; balance $104 in the habilitado's favor.

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 279

Supply crisis: arrival at Monterey of the warship *San Carlos* + merchant schooner *Nueva Reyna de los Ángeles* w/ 100 men but 'sin más auxilios… que el de dos pagas' — keeps Solá sleepless; no funds/artillery received; 400 useless saber-blades returned; wants de la Guerra to carry an 'enérgica representación' to the Viceroy on the province's critical state.

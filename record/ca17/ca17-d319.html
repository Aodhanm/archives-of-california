'Ataque sobre buques; estado indefenso de las provincias'

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 319

'Ataque sobre buques; estado indefenso de las provincias': will keep precaution with every approaching ship lest it carry the expedition prepared by Lord Cochrane & Sir Robert Wilson against the King's American dominions; has only 25–30 men; stresses the presidios' defenseless state on 'esta abierta y dilatada costa'; from 1 May the militia-artillerymen to rotate to the presidio monthly.

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 257

'Castigo por adulterio': re the scandal of the widow Anastasia Zúñiga (Los Ángeles) with the San-Gabriel-mission carpenter José Antonio Ramírez — Ramírez to acknowledge his obligations to mother & daughter & marry if possible (else his pay garnished for the child); jailed a month. The widow to be publicly shamed: head shaved, one eyebrow scraped, displayed bareface at Mass at San Gabriel, then 6 months' servitude & a 'religious life.' Brutal gendered moral discipline.

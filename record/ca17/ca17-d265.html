'Preparativos contra Bouchard': approves taking Fr

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 265

'Preparativos contra Bouchard': approves taking Fr. Luis Martínez to San Juan Capistrano where the rebels had anchored; with the SD troop + de la Guerra's men + *vecinos* + Indian archers, hopes to make the pirates 'respetar… las tropas del Gobierno'; set up horse-relays. Orig. p.385

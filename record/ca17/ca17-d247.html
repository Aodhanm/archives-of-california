Causa criminal — 7 Mission San Diego neophytes (Alejo, Rafael, Antonio, Andrés, Gaspar, Joaquín + 1)…

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 247

Causa criminal — 7 Mission San Diego neophytes (Alejo, Rafael, Antonio, Andrés, Gaspar, Joaquín + 1) tried for robbing the presidio warehouse (11 Mar). Sentences: Alejo 80 lashes (20/day × 4) + 5 yrs presidio w/ grillete at Monterey/SF; the others 80 lashes + 4 yrs; the 2 buyers of stolen goods 2 mo. at Punta de Guijarros; the receivers Dionisio & Andrés 50 lashes + 2 mo.

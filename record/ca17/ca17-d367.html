Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 367

Diligencias on the bodies of soldado Pablo Sarmiento & the neófita Apolonia (mission) found dead — possible homicide. Escribano soldado J. Antonio Pico, cadete José Moraga. Neophytes Apolonio & Urbano arrested on suspicion; fiscal finds no legal proof of homicide → recommends freeing them after 13 months' prison. Orig. pp.277–351.

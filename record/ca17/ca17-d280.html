'Comisión… Expedición contra indios': commissions de la Guerra to go to México to represent the…

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 280

'Comisión… Expedición contra indios': commissions de la Guerra to go to México to represent the province's crisis (a general power from all companies to audit the Habilitado Gral.; $100/mo.); orders Teniente Gabriel Moraga to lead the expedition against the [Colorado/Mojave] Indians, taking 100+ armed mission Indians from San Buenaventura, San Fernando & San Gabriel; 20–25 men from San Diego to combine w/ Sta Bárbara's.

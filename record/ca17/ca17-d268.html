An English warship (corvette) at Monterey since 3 Nov seeking refreshments

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 268

An English warship (corvette) at Monterey since 3 Nov seeking refreshments — 'Viene al Río Columbia con la comisión de verificar su entrega á los Americanos, á cuyo fin conduce á los Comisionados por los Estados Unidos', sailing on the 10th–11th. The restitution of Astoria/Fort George to the U.S. under the Treaty of Ghent (1818) — likely HMS *Blossom*. Orig. p.388

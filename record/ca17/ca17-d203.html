30-year diezmo (tithe) series for the Sta Bárbara jurisdiction, 1786–1815

Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 203

30-year diezmo (tithe) series for the Sta Bárbara jurisdiction, 1786–1815 — yearly figures from the habilitación's general accounts (e.g., 1786 $87; 1787 $469.4 incl. $288 from *pobladores* for *tierras de merced*; 1795 $520.4; 1802–4 $1,571; 1808–14 $1,577; 1815 $288) = $9,478.5 (+$328 for 1794 = $9,826.5). A long-run economic data series. Orig. pp.356–7

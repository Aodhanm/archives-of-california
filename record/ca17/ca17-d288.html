Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military), tomo XL–LII · BANC MSS C-A 17, document 288

'Campaña contra indios': approves the province making its expedition 'en los términos de su demarcación'; San Diego to send a force visiting the points Moraga designates; once the 45 Mazatlán men reach Sta Bárbara, Moraga sets out (command of SB arms left to a Tte. Argüello). ⭐⭐ The Monterey party under Tte. José Mª Estudillo (guide Sgt. José Dolores Pico as *práctico*) to set out 10–15 Oct, enter via San Miguel, scout the laguna de Ugarra[?], Telame, and the San Gabriel river, then return. A multi-pronged interior (Tulares) sweep.

interrogatorio deposition: de la Guerra slaughtered at that spot 3–4 yrs, and earlier 'todavía mas adentro de la poblacion… en el paraje llamado huerta de la Viña, debajo de los olivos'; no complaint in his 2 years as Alcalde; in wet years women wash in the arroyo below; police should have warned; corral/tasajera ready & cattle arrived; damages real but he left Sta Bárbara that day

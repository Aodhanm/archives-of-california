Departmental State Papers, tomo IV · BANC MSS C-A 29, document 329

rebuttal: transcribed Rojo's order only for his ulterior claims, never expecting the Sub-Prefect to back it; recalls Rojo, 'hollando la orn. publicada pr. mandato del Exmo. Sr. Gobr.,' gave a licencia pª. vender licores that bred 'un cúmulo de desórdenes' on San Juan's day, for which Rojo merited a fine yet was shielded; the Sub-Prefect dodges the essential question (whether the order was timely); the alleged harms 'los tengo á los menos por muy exagerados, sino son enteramte. ilusorios'; renews his protest

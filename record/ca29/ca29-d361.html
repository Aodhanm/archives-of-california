(⚠ last document of the volume) Full-text (Savage 'O'-marked). The curtidor (tanner) who was at Santa Clara left his contract at the Mission and J.J. Vallejo hired him at the San José establishment at 20 pesos mensuales, subject to the Gobierno's approval; 'Este hombre nada le grava al Est[ablecimien]to… y queda benficiado el Estab[lecimien]to y todo el público, p[o]rq[u]e siquiera hayan donde comprar' (self-financing labor, public benefit)

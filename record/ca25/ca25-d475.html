Ditto entry (month and day carried down from Julio 30)

Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 475

Ditto entry (month and day carried down from Julio 30): 'Individuos recien llegados fueron socorridos en acto de desembarque y colocados entre familias de celo cristiano; se mando resto a Presidios de Sn Franco, Sta Barbara y Sn Diego pa colocacion.' Newly landed colonists relieved on disembarking and placed with families of christian zeal, the remainder sent to San Francisco, Santa Barbara and San Diego presidios for placement.

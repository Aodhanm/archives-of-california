Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 888

Two items. (a) Continuation of the n328 register: 'Junio 21.... p 221. (2 doc.) - Expediente instruido sobre destinar buque p.a hacer retirar a los extrangeros de las costas, q.e se ocupen en pesca de Nutria.' [= proceedings on assigning a vessel to drive off the foreigners on the coasts engaged in otter hunting] | '„ 23... pp 221-22 (1 doc.) - Que fondeo fragata 'Princesa' con cuatro religiosos y memorias; dicha fragata armada en guerra crusara la costa con objeto de impedir la pesca.' [the Princesa, armed for war, will cruise the coast to stop the hunting] | '„ 30... pp 222. (1 doc.) - Arribo de fragata.' (b) NEW document, verbatim heading: '+ 1806. = Junio 30 = Monterrey. / Gob.r al Virrey. Despachos de oficiales.' Savage abstract: he has received commissions for the newly created officers of both Californias and others promoted in March last; their placement cannot be effected because some are habilitados who must first render account of the funds they handle; and he has ordered Alferez Jose Lugan (or Lujan) transferred to the Presidio of Loreto. The tail of (b) is on n330, pp. 222-23.

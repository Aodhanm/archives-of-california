Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 461

MARQUEE — the 1798 register. VICEREGAL TRANSITION: Branciforte hands the command to Azanza (May — Azanza now Viceroy). OPEN-DOOR/FOREIGN-SHIP: ⭐⭐ the *Otter*/Dorr men* recur (measures on the individuals Dorr left, Feb; suspicion re what was supplied to 'Capt. Dorr', Nov — the 3rd/4th mentions, cf n576); order that no Asia-bound ship remain at these establishments (Dic, Manila-galleon restriction); 3 ships off Acapulco (Nov); the ship *María Magallanes* at Sta Bárbara (Mar). SF/FORTIFICATION: the Rancho del Rey at the SF Presidio (Feb, cf ca24); engineer Córdova's general plan of the presidios (Mar) then his retirement (Dec); build 2 launches at SF (Nov); plan of both Californias + Gulf of Cortés (Nov). INDIAN/MISSION: ⭐⭐ the Sta Clara-mission-vs-San-José-pueblo boundary (Aug, = ca24 Doc 333 + californio land-tenure); ⭐ 'la humanidad con que se ha de tratar á los indios' (Nov, cf ca24 Trato de Indios); the Silverio/Rosa neophyte-punishment consultation (Aug, cf ca24 uxoricide); retreat from founding Mission Sta Catarina (Mar). OTHER: the slave Máximo sentenced (Dic); smallpox not introduced (Nov); the French-émigré officers' list (May); church-sanctuary for military reos (Set).

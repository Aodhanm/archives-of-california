MARQUEE Russian item. Heading '+ 1809 = Marzo 31 = Monterrey. / Gobr al Virrey Sobre desertores &a'. 'Que indios pescadores tomaron a 2 americanos o Rusos qe decian ser desertores de Fragata Rusa llamada Neva; qe comandante de Sn José le noticia qe posteriormente se ha visto dentro del Pto de S Franco, diferentes Cayucos pescadores sin acercarse, recorriendo la costa, qe buque no se ha dejado ver; qe adjunta relacion de lo sucedido entre tropa y dichos cayucos; qe 2 heridos qe se tomaron ha mandado se traten con consideraciones' [= Indian fishermen seized 2 Americans or Russians claiming to be deserters from the Russian frigate Neva; the San José commander reports baidarkas seen inside San Francisco bay working the coast without approaching, no ship sighted; encloses account of the clash between troops and the canoes; the 2 wounded taken are to be treated considerately].

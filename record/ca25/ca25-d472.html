Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 472

Heading: '+ 1800 = Julio 2 = Monterrey. (Sin firma ni direccion.) Sobre siembras insendiadas por indios Ausaymes.' Ausayames rancheria 9 leguas E of Mision de San Juan Bautista has thrice threatened to burn it; aid earlier ordered from Pueblo de San Jose and 'Rancho de Rl Has.da'; they killed '5 gentiles de los Montssones (sic.)'; Sargento Macario Castro sent with a partida, jailed 2 indios two months.

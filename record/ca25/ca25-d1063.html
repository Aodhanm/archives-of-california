Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 1063

Tomo X. Three new blocks, each with its own heading, plus the tail of the block anchored on n524. Verbatim heads and entries: '+ 1803 - Junio 14 = Loreto / Arrillaga á Jose Onsueta. = Que remplazan al artillero que manda para S.n Fran.co p. 518.' | '+ 1803 = Julio 15 = Loreto / Arrillaga á Simon Suarez = Cuentas p. 518-519.' | '+ 1803 = Loreto / Arrillaga á Jose Font' with 'Julio 15 - Cuenta p. 519 (2 doc.)' and 'Agosto 11 - p. 519-520 (3 doc.) Que P. Fermin Fran.co Lasuen falleció el 26 de mes p.p.o'. The leaf also carries, above these, the closing entries of the 1803 Arrillaga to Jose Font block headed on n524: 'Marzo 8 - Cuentas p. 515.-516 (4 doc.)' | 'Abril 19 - Recibió su presupuesto p. 516-517 (2 doc.)' | '- id - 26 - - id - Revista. p. 517.' | '- id - 28 - - id - Solicitud del Cavo. p. 518.' | 'Junio 14 - - id - listas de revista p. 518.' English gloss: they are replacing the artilleryman he is sending to San Francisco; accounts; and, on 11 August 1803, that Fr Fermin Francisco Lasuen died on the 26th of the previous month. Note that the Lasuen death is reported here in the Tomo X series to Jose Font, distinct from the Tomo IX report at n277 (Tomo IX pp. 78-82).

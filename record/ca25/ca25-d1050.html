Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 1050

Own heading, verbatim: + 1804. = Mayo 8. - Loreto. / Gob.r a Virrey. Sobre muertes por gentiles. Body verbatim: Le comunica el mal estado de lo Presidios y escases de tropa; P.P. Ministro de Mision de St.a Clara embiaron 20 cristianos en Solicitud de indios Cimarrones, y q.e mataron los gentiles a uno, salvandose los demas con la fuga; que Comandante de fronteras le dice q.e entre St.a Catalina y S.n Vicente habian matado los gentiles a 2 soldados dirijiendose a Rio Colorado. [English: he reports the bad state of the presidios and the shortage of troops; the fathers of the mission of Santa Clara sent 20 Christian Indians in search of runaway Indians, and the gentiles killed one of them, the rest escaping by flight; the comandante of the frontier tells him that between Santa Catalina and San Vicente the gentiles had killed 2 soldiers who were on their way to the Colorado River. Note: Santa Catalina and San Vicente are the Baja California frontier missions.]

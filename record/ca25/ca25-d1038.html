Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 1038

Verbatim: Acusa recibo de Bulas de la Jurisdiccion para bienio de 1800 y 1801. [English: acknowledges receipt of the bulls for the jurisdiction for the biennium 1800 and 1801.] Under the same Marzo 31. (10 doc.) line. I am inferring, not stating, that these are Bulas de la Santa Cruzada; Savage does not name them further.

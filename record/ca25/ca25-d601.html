Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 601

'27... (1 doc. - Que respecto á noticia dada pr Consul Español en Nueva Yorch qe en dicho Pto se estaba preparando un buque americano llamado Eagle, dara cumplimiento con lo qe se le ordena.' Acknowledges intelligence from the Spanish consul at New York that an American vessel named Eagle was fitting out there, and will comply with his orders. Savage spells it Nueva Yorch.

Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 725

'Enterado de haberse ordenado á Comte Gral. de N.a Galicia remi[ta] lo qe falte á fortalezas y 1 oficial facultativo.' Acknowledges notice that the Comandante General of Nueva Galicia has been ordered to forward what the forts still lack, plus one qualified (facultativo) officer. Second of the 'Nobre 14 (3 doc.)' run.

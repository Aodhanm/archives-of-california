Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 493

'+ 1800 Abril 4 = (no dice lugar.) (Sin firma) Sobre reglamento 15 Dbre 1798, de montepio a favor de Viudas, hijos y madres de Cirujanos del ejercito y Catedraticos de Colegio de Cirugia.' Arts. 1-4 given: 8 maravedis per escudo discounted through army treasuries from the cirujano mayor, army and hospital surgeons, castillos, ciudadelas and presidios; families of catedraticos and anatomical directors admitted to the fund; provisional 1st and 2nd ayudantes contribute while drawing pay; those not paid by treasury must pay in monthly or be charged triple.

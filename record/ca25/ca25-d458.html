MARQUEE — the 1795 viceregal register. OPEN-DOOR/FOREIGN-SHIP dense: 281/290 the Vancouver deserters (collection of their costs); 287/288/300/371 how to manage & the *advertencias* made to Capt. Vancouver; 372 prohibits henceforth receiving from foreigners anything of what is supplied them (re Vancouver's goods); 289 Rl órden: otter-pelt extraction duty-free from Acapulco ('pieles de nutria que se embarquen en la Nao'); 306 foreign vessels' concurrence at Nootka; 307 Rl órden extending this kingdom's commerce with the South-Sea ports; 285 S. Blas–Peninsula commerce; 315 the American ships; 365 the English frigate *Resolución* at Ensenada de Todos Santos; 366 the English frigate *Fénix* at Sta Bárbara (⭐⭐⭐ = the Doc 142 closed-door-protocol ship); 370 precautions with foreign ships; 386 supply English ships only against receipts; 387 entry-signal for S. Blas ships. INDIAN: 299 the Sta Clara Fr. Tomás de la Peña 'calumnia' (cf. Peña investigation); 334/351 gentile killings of S. Francisco neophytes (cf. ca24 Dolores arc). 344 García's overland route S. Gabriel→Moqui (cf. ca24/ca23 Moqui inquiry). 325 Borica's *Despacho de Coronel*; 326 Conde de Colomera Cmdt-Gen of Artillery; 330/342 Fages's testamentaría papers; 357 schooling confined to presidios/escoltas; 358 SF Castillo cost reimbursement; 374 the Bando of peace with France + thanksgiving Mass.

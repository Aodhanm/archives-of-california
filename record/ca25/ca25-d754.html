'Dice haber sabido por carta particular qe Goleta del americano Santiago Smith de Wilcolks nombrada…

Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 754

'Dice haber sabido por carta particular qe Goleta del americano Santiago Smith de Wilcolks nombrada Travilleros fue sorprendida por Ramires en rada de Presº de Loreto, y manifiesta los servicios prestados á la Provª por dicho Smith quien facilitó 717 piezas Lanas á ambas provincias, y qe Ramires siendo un particular no estaba en derecho pa tal procedimiento sin conocimiento del Gobr de la Provª' [= says he learned by a private letter that the schooner of the American Santiago Smith Wilcocks, named Travilleros, was seized by Ramírez in the roadstead of the presidio of Loreto; sets out the services rendered to the province by Smith, who supplied 717 pieces of woolens to both Californias, and that Ramírez, being a private person, had no right to such a proceeding without the knowledge of the governor of the province]. 'del americano' is a caret insertion above the line; 'rada de' likewise inserted.

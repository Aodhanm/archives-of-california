  • Previously read Gap-read record: orig. pp. 122–24 (the otter/Catalina narrative, 1804).. Leaf-verified 2026-07-12: every leaf of C-A 25 carries TWO numbering systems — the original tomo pages (which this record's 122–24 gives) and a handwritten per-tomo LEAF number top-right. Bancroft's footnotes cite the LEAF numbers: his 'Prov. Rec., MS., ix. 122-3' (Hist. Cal. II:90 n.21) is NOT this document but Arrillaga's 28 Jun 1810 Suisun-campaign report at tomo leaves 122–123 = scans n374–375 (now ca25-d37).
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

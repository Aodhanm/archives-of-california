Second bullet of the Enero 20 run, its text completing at the head of n407 (Savage leaf 155.-)

Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 734

Second bullet of the Enero 20 run, its text completing at the head of n407 (Savage leaf 155.-): 'Haberse concluido fuerte de Sn Joaquin, y recomienda á Sub.te de Artilleria Man.l Gomez y á Soldado José Franco por trabajo qe han tenido.' The fort of San Joaquin [San Francisco] is finished; Sola commends Subteniente de Artilleria Manuel Gomez and soldier Jose Franco for their labour. The third document of the run is undetailed.

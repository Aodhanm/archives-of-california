Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 727

'Da parte haberse embarcado pa Sn Blas y trasladarse á Guadalajara el Cadete Gervacio Argüello a ejercer empleo de Habilitado Grãl.' Cadet Gervasio Argüello - the same officer sent overland weeks earlier with secret orders to reconnoitre the Russian post - sails for San Blas and on to Guadalajara to take up the office of Habilitado General.

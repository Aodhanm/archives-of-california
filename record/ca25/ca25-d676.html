Mission-state land conflict. Heading '+ 1810 = Febrero 20 = Monterey. / Gobr a Virrey.' 'Que a vecino Mariano Castro se le retire la gracia concedida pª establecer en el sitio de la Brea, en cuya posecion se halla Mision de Sn Juan Bautista con varias obras qe habia ejecutado y cria de ganado' [= that the grant allowing the settler Mariano Castro to establish himself at the sitio de la Brea be withdrawn, the place being held by Mission San Juan Bautista, which has works built there and cattle grazing].

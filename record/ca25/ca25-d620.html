'Agosto 1o. Sn Antonio... (2 doc.) - Adjunta docs de José de la Guerra Comte de Preso de Sn Diego y Alf. de Arta José Roca.' Encloses papers of José de la Guerra, commandant of the San Diego presidio, and artillery alférez José Roca. Place written into the entry line, so the governor was at Mission San Antonio.

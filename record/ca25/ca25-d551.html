Jul 1 (p.245), 'Sobre dos soldados muertos por indios'

Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 551

Jul 1 (p.245), 'Sobre dos soldados muertos por indios': the cabo of Santa Catalina sent 2 soldiers escorting 5 captive Indians; at a place 'Agua Blanca' the soldiers dismounted to drink, the Indians untied their bindings, stoned the first from his horse killing him, the second fired his escopeta, missed, clubbed with the barrel wounding two, 'pero q.e siendo 5 sus adversarios y fatigado, murió como su compañero á pedradas y golpes.' Jul 21 (p.146, Loreto borrador): causa criminal.

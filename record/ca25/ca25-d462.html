MARQUEE — the 1799 register (closes the 1794–99 viceregal-índice sequence). OPEN-DOOR/FOREIGN-SHIP: ⭐⭐ the American frigate *Eliza* — approves the orders given her + the 24-peso libranza for auxiliary supplies (Ago/Set) = the ca24 n450/451 *Eliza* (denied license, feared smuggler, Boston-bound); ⭐ oath of fidelity required of foreigners residing in these dominions (Jul, + bar dealings with them); 4 Englishmen apprehended at Cabo S. Lúcas sent to S. Blas (Set); measures on ships approaching from the South + attract the Manila galleon (Set); free commerce with the ports of Guayaquil & Panamá (Ago). MISSION/INDIAN: ⭐ the SJC 'india que parió una perra' case (Ago = ca24 Doc 335/288); teaching the neophytes trades via the artisans (Set). SF/FORT: the Fort S. Joaquín + Yerba-Buena-battery reconstruction expediente (Jul) → the Tribunal de Cuentas rules the recomposition unnecessary (Ago). OTHER: census of the King's vassals/inhabitants end-1798 (Ago).

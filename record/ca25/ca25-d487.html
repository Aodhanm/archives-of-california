Provincial Records, tomo VIII–X · BANC MSS C-A 25, document 487

Ditto entry (Octubre 1): 'Que remite libranzas pa qe ponga V.B., la 1a de $550 pr Captn de Compa de Voluntarios Pedro Alberni y 2a pr Sargento de Cuerpo de Artilla Jose Roca de $250, pa su cobro.' Forwards two drafts for countersignature and collection: 550 pesos for Pedro Alberni, captain of the Catalonian volunteers, 250 pesos for artillery sergeant Jose Roca.

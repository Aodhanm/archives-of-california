Opinion on public affairs, men & measures: something must be done quickly to avert the horrors that yet await this unhappy land; all is turmoil & confusion; only by the establishment of a good govt & a proper code of laws will we give the country peace & quiet; there is good reason to suspect intolerable & revolutionary movements on foot which must be nipped in the bud; Gov. must not expect to obtain correct information [from natives]...

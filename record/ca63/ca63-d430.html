(Doctº en inglés) Returns the letter J.J. Warner sent Kearny (19 Apr 1847); on the Indian-agency question has written a letter he will receive soon; Warner's considerations are incontestably exact, but he is not a person in whom the people here & at San Diego have confidence, though intelligent & endowed with a good wife & children; Warner has his house near the 'warm springs' on the Colorado-river road

Doc N.o 1 of 3 in the packet headed '1847. -- Oct. 19th. Ang.s / Ant.o José Cot. — (3 Docs.)'

Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents, tomo Unbound · BANC MSS C-A 63, document 408

Doc N.o 1 of 3 in the packet headed '1847. -- Oct. 19th. Ang.s / Ant.o José Cot. — (3 Docs.)'; spans n280-n281. Savage: 'N.o 1. - A full conveyance and sale to D. José Ant.o Pico and José Ant. Cot of the Mission of S. Luis Rey and the Rancho de Pala in consideration of Two Thousand dollars in silver & $437.50 in grain furnished Goverment in its emergencies with the sole condition of satisfying the debts the Mission may owe in any kind of produce of the Country at such time as said debts may be presented dated May 18th /846 - Ang.s & signed for Pio Pico. - Deed can be proved to have been made out in Aug.t 1846.' [= the Pico sale of Mission San Luis Rey and Rancho de Pala for $2,437.50; Savage records the allegation that the deed, though dated 18 May, was actually drawn up in August 1846 — i.e. backdated.]

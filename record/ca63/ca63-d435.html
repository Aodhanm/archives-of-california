(Doctº en inglés) THE ENCLOSED KELSEY CLEAR-LAKE REPORT (✅ re-verified): sends 2 'bucaroes'; much to do with the cattle, could only find 100 head & 23 oxen; on penning them found several with arrow-wounds; an Indian tells him the Indians are killing the cattle; go see the Indian agent about it; also found some cattle killed a mile from the house — ⚑ the Andrés Kelsey ranch at Clear Lake (the same Kelsey killed by Pomo in 1849 → the Bloody Island massacre precursor)

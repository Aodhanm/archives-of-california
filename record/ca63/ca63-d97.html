Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents, tomo Unbound · BANC MSS C-A 63, document 97

Reports release of a convict (Foxen[?] the convict) as there being no civil authority there & no place to keep the prisoner; key of the juzgado & the staff of office placed with Lieut. Narciso Fabregat, an old Mexn officer & landlord of the building, a respectable gentleman; Custom House papers of the port all sealed up & locked up in the juzgado; Fabregat will allow nothing disturbed; military contribution fund turned over to his successor Capt. Smith

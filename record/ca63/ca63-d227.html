Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents, tomo Unbound · BANC MSS C-A 63, document 227

About retaining Walter Colton as Alcalde of Monterey: aware of importance of Colton's services; will not withdraw him at present; requests Gov. inform him when a suitable person may be obtained to take his place, as his services in the squadron are also needed (Colton was a Chaplain)

(Doctº en inglés) Reports the Indians have been committing many depredations in his District

Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents, tomo Unbound · BANC MSS C-A 63, document 523

(Doctº en inglés) Reports the Indians have been committing many depredations in his District; horses & cattle stolen & killed to a large amount, & the Indians have become so bold as to kill cattle (tame ones) in open day; therefore the Alcalde has sent to apprehend some & has hanged one of the culprits (marked 'Fin' — apparent section end)

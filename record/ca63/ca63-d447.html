Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents, tomo Unbound · BANC MSS C-A 63, document 447

(Docts en inglés) THE SF ALCALDE-RECORDS SEIZURE AFFAIR: Alcalde Leavenworth notifies Halleck (31 May) of the seizure of the papers & records of his office; Notary G. Haven (1 Jun) testifies to the seizure of the alcalde's-office papers by the Sheriff (J.C. Pulis) upon a Justice-of-Peace order; Justice Myron Norton's order (31 May) to Sheriff Pulis to take from the alcalde's office the public records & documents of the District & deliver them to John A. Patterson (Register), & summon Thaddeus M. Leavenworth to appear 1st Monday of June; certified true copy signed George Hyde (Dist. Attorney), John A. Patterson (Clerk of the Justices' Court), H. Gillingham (Municipal Clerk to Alcalde) — the leg/administrative clash between the ayuntamiento's alcalde system & the incoming American Justice/Sheriff apparatus at SF

Requests appointment of Danl. Hill as 1er Regidor (Hill refused, pleading want of capacity, having already filled positions of great responsibility); also appointment of Antº Mª de la Guerra as secretary at $30/mo. Notes Gov. ordered $500 towards a jail in Sta Bárbara but no funds to begin work; asks orders

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read savage_page: '251–252'. Leaves read: 168036073_80_12 n253 (stamped 253, ink 127) and n254 (stamped 254, ink 128). Entry verbatim: "1845 = Sbre 26 = Monterey. / José Abrego á Pico / Sobre pago de libranzas. / Q.e Mellus se resiste á recibir de Larkin los $500 de su libranza, p.r querer plata; q.e por honra del Gob.o, preguntó á Hijar de q.e modo se deben hacer esos pagos; pues q.e hay otras q.e tendran q.e cubrirse. Q.e le resuelva pronto las consultas q.e le remitió. pp 432-3." SAVAGE_PAGE WRONG: the record cites 251–252, but the two leaves bear 253/254 (stamped) and 127/128 (ink) — 251 appears nowhere; verified at magnification on n253 and on n280 that no third number exists in the margins. SCAN_PIN PARTIAL: the document is complete on n253 (heading, body and page-cite, with blank ruling below); n254 opens with a new dated entry (Fran.co Rico á Pico, Sbre 30, p.434 = d424), so the n253–n254 span is overstated by one leaf. SUMMARY PARTIAL: substance is a faithful rendering, but "[William] Mellus" inserts a given name the leaf does not supply (it reads only "Mellus"). Omissions: n253's other content (tail of d421, "tiene pedida una licencia p 429"; Diego Olivera á Pico pp.430-1 = d422) and all three entries on n254 (d424–d426) are catalogued.

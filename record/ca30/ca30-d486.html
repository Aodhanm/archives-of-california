Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read savage_page: '284–285'. Leaves read: 168036073_80_12 n286 (stamped 286, ink 160) and n287 (stamped 287, ink 161); the document spans both, ending "cunstancias. pp 559-60" at the head of n287 — orig_page VI·559–560 confirmed. Verbatim: "1845 – Abril 7 – Angeles. / Sanchez – Coronel – Bacilio Valdes / Ricardo Uribe de 33 años, sentenciado á destierro perpetuo á Sta Bárb.a; p.a q.e pase á Sonoma á cumplir su condena, p.r exigirlo las circunstancias." Author expansions (V. Sanchez → Vicente, Yg Coronel → Ignacio) are supported by the two entries above on the same leaf; 'Bacilio'→'Basilio' is orthographic. Recipient NA: this is a sentencia with three signatories and no addressee on the leaf. SAVAGE_PAGE WRONG: the record cites 284–285; the leaves bear 286/287 (stamped) and 160/161 (ink). Omissions: the leaf's other entries (Montalván sentence pp.555-6 = d484; Uribe consignment pp.557-8 = d485; on n287 p.561 = d487 and pp.562-5 = d488) are all catalogued.

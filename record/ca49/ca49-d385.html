the Hacienda in very bad state — blames Echeandía's administration

Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 385

the Hacienda in very bad state — blames Echeandía's administration: the useful cattle-ranchos finished off, foreign ships paying in goods via Echeandía-placed employees (Bandini, José Antonio Estudillo), unsecured customs houses acting 'absolutos' (Bandini stood guarantor for Fr. Peyri); the Solís revolt 'had support in the soldiers' poverty'; will send a visitador to audit & fix the Hacienda

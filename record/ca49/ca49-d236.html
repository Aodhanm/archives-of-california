neophyte Jácome Jáuregui of Mission San Antonio fled with his family

Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 236

neophyte Jácome Jáuregui of Mission San Antonio fled with his family — wants to live outside the mission (got no land to sow), cannot bear 'la aflicción… y los muchos azotes que les dan'; many kin/friends likewise cannot even come to complain 'á causa de los muchos azotes' (floggings); orders the Alcalde to investigate

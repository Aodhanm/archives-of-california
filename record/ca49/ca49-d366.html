Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 366

'Incursiones' — reports (via the padre of Mission San Francisco Solano, N of SF) that the Commandant of the Russian establishment at Bodega, with 40 armed men & various Indians, approached the mission & did sowing labors, 'como en los años anteriores' (Russian agricultural expansion from Bodega toward the Sonoma frontier); + the SLO padre reports many North Americans near Buena Vista [Lake] who killed an Indian & plan to found an establishment; Victoria will form an expedition vs. the Americans & deal diplomatically with the Russian governor

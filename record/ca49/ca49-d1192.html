Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 1192

thanks Weber for his efforts on behalf of peace, whose conduct brought on him the imprisonment Sutter made him suffer, and asks him to forget all resentment against Sutter and his partisans, as the Govt recommends — the post-Cahuenga reconciliation of the foreign-settler factions (Weber/San José vs. Sutter's Micheltorena riflemen)

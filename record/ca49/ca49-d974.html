major dispatch (topic-header: Tulares; fleet; Dept. commerce; American-adventurer & wild-Indian threats; the Alvarado–Vallejo rift; 'Asuntos de Ross'; ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 'Compra de propiedades Rusas por Sutter') — the Fort Ross sale to Sutter (Tsar's evacuation order; Rotchev's offer to Mexico declined; Ross evacuated 19 Dec 1841, 'ya no tremola el pabellón Ruso'); Sutter's Nueva Helvecia 'entre los indios bárbaros' (defended it with 8 men, settled 300+ gentiles + a school, recovered rancheros' stolen horse-herds via repeated campaigns); the Bartleson–Bidwell party (~30 armed Missourians, Vallejo returned their arms & passported them over Alvarado's objection); Vallejo's 'acrimonia' toward Sutter (dup. pp.35–38) — see Expanded Entry 8

'Reclamos de Extranjeros' — the US frigate 'St. Louis' arrived 22 days after the deportation ship sailed, demanding an accounting; the commander tried to name a US Consular Agent for California (leaving an officer), which Casarín refused to recognize without central authorization but gave a passport; defends the prisoners' humane treatment; suspects indemnity-claim motives *(re-transcribe faint scan before quoting)*

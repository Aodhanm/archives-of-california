Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 457

divides the Department of California into 3 districts & partidas — 1st: Sonoma frontier→SLO (subdiv.: SLO–Llagas cab. San Juan Bautista; Llagas–Sonoma cab. Dolores/SF); 2nd: San Buenaventura→Santo Domingo (subdiv.: Buenaventura–San Fernando cab. Santa Bárbara; Cahuenga–Santo Domingo cab. Los Ángeles); 3rd: [Baja] Rosario→San José del Cabo (cab. La Paz, division suspended pending report); prefects $1,000/yr

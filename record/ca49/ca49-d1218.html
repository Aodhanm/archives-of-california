Assembly decree of 16 May on timber-cutting (*corte de madera*)

Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 1218

Assembly decree of 16 May on timber-cutting (*corte de madera*); admonishes the Sta Bárbara alcalde who 'abandona muy frecuentemente' his post to go to his rancho without leave; ⭐⭐ order to pay the captain of the frigate *Don Quijote* the balance contracted for transporting Gen. Micheltorena & his troops to San Blas

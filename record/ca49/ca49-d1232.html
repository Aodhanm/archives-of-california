Departmental Records, tomo IX–XIV · BANC MSS C-A 49, document 1232

the Govt has learned of Fr. Tomás Estenaga's resistance to the handover of Mission San Gabriel — the commissioners are to get him to yield 'sin recurrir á medios violentos,' and the Prefect is told to forbid the other [friars] from following Estenaga's conduct; + Alcalde Araiza reprimanded for coming to LA 'sin licencia ninguna'; Assembly decrees enclosed

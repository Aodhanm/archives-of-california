SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Agosto 6. = Mont. / (no dice quien) á J. M. Ybarra.' [= 1834, August 6, Monterey. (Does not say who) to J. M. Ybarra.] Savage's abstract: 'Circular á alcaldes de los Angeles, San Buenaventura, Mont. y Comte. Militar de San Diego. Sobre formacion de Ayuntamientos.' The circular carrying the Diputacion's August 1834 ayuntamiento-creation decree out to the four southern and central jurisdictions. Addressee is the military commandant at San Diego; I am inferring, not asserting, that this is Juan Maria Ibarra.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Mayo 2. = Mont. / Figueroa (no dice á quien' [= 1834, May 2, Monterey. Figueroa (addressee not stated).] 'Que comision se ocupe de preferencia y con actividad de formar Reglamento interior.' Figueroa directs that the committee take up, in preference and with despatch, the drafting of the Deputation's internal rules. The order that produces the Reglamento interior de la Diputacion of 16 June 1834 recovered below. Savage leaves the opening parenthesis unclosed on the leaf; transcribed as written.

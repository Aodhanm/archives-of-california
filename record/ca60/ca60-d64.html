SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 28 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 28, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract. Prior acta approved. The dictamen of the Comision de Misiones 'Sobre nombramiento de preceptor en el Pueblo de S.n Antonio' was put to discussion and approved. Appointment of a schoolmaster at the pueblo of San Antonio, referred through the missions commission, which places the ex-mission schools inside the secularization machinery.

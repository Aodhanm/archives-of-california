SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Octubre 8. = Mont. / M. G. Vallejo. á José de la Guerra' [= 1834, October 8, Monterey. M. G. Vallejo to José de la Guerra.] Abstract: 'Le notifica haber sido lecto 7º vocal de la Diputacion Territorial pª los años de 1835, - 36, 37 y 38.' Vallejo notifies De la Guerra of his election as seventh vocal of the Diputacion for the four-year term 1835-1838. Names both principals and fixes the composition of the incoming assembly.

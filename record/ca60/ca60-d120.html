SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 22. = Mont. / Juan B. Alvarado' [= 1834, May 22, Monterey. Juan B. Alvarado.] Abstract: 'Expediente sobre Construccion de Calzada en Mont.' An eight-page file on the building of a calzada (causeway/paved road) at Monterey. Public-works business of the 1834 Diputacion; Alvarado was its treasurer.

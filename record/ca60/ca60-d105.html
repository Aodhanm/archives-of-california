SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 10 = Mont. / Carlos A. Carrillo' [= 1834, May 10, Monterey. Carlos Antonio Carrillo.] 'Dice Que fué admitida renuncia q.e solicitó Srio de Diputacion.' That the resignation requested by the secretary of the Deputation was accepted. Five original pages, closing the Alvarado resignation of 30 April. Carrillo signs, presumably as the presiding or reporting vocal.

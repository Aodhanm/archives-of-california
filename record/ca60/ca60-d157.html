SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Agosto 28. = Mont. / Salvio Pacheco á Gefe Politº' [= 1835, August 28, Monterey. Salvio Pacheco to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Haberse aprovado sueldo de Srio.' Pacheco, acting for the Diputacion, reports approval of the secretary's salary.

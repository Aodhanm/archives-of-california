SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Mont. / Srio de Diputacion á Figueroa. / Mayo 23' [= 1834, Monterey. Secretary of the Diputacion to Figueroa. May 23. The heading line carries no month; Savage put 'Mayo 23' in the body beside the page cite.] Savage folds TWO documents into one entry, itemised on separate lines: '- Aprovacion de dictamen' and '- Id de Arts contenidos en dictamen de obras Publicas.' The Diputacion's approval of a committee report and then of the articles contained in the public-works dictamen. Ties to the Monterey calzada file above.

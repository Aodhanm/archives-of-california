SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 10 = Mont. / José Antº Carrillo á Figueroa' [= 1834, July 10, Monterey. José Antonio Carrillo to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Haber nombrado á Manuel Arzaga, Srio del Ayuntamiento de los Angeles.' Manuel Arzaga named secretary of the Los Angeles ayuntamiento in Moraga's place. Confirms José Antonio Carrillo is the J. A. Carrillo of p. 517.

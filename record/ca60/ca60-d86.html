RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n220-n245, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1833. = Dbre 1º = Mont. / José Tiburcio Castro á Gefe Politico.' [= 1833, December 1, Monterey. José Tiburcio Castro to the Jefe Político.] 'Adjunta acta electoral' — encloses the electoral return. Savage then stacks three sub-entries under the same date: '' ' Acta electoral. p p 318-20'; '' ' Manuel Crespo á José Tiburcio Castro — Le notifica qe fue electo pª formar la junta electoral. p p 326-28'; '' ' Tiburcio Castro á Nicolas Alviso — Le notifica haber sido electo pª junta electoral pª renovacion de oficios del Ayuntamiento de Monterey. p p 329-31.' Together these document the 1833 electoral junta convened to renew the Monterey ayuntamiento's offices.

Diputación Provincial, 1834-06

June 1834: Tomás Yorba's LA fine (for refusing election-commission duty in 1832); the cattle / *mostrencos* (stray-stock) regulation; the question of foreigners' municipal-service obligations at Santa Bárbara; the contaduría de propios y arbitrios. ⭐ Item-level read 2026-08-05: ⭐⭐ the general cattle-slaughter (matanza) suspension (n112); ⭐⭐ the Petaluma grant to Mariano G. Vallejo (n119/n123); ⭐⭐ the Castro / Haro / Quijas / Híjar secularization debate (Castro's mission inventory, Haro's land-measurement, Fr. Quijas's mission-boundary petition, the Híjar commission — the same 1834 propositions cross-referenced in ca39-departmental-state-papers-benicia-vol-ii-survey); García Diego's cattle-slaughter oficio (n131–38); J.B. Alvarado relieved as Secretary; Luis Arenas 'San Felipe' grant

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Octubre 13. = Mont. / J. B. Alvarado á Gefe Politico' [= 1835, October 13, Monterey. J. B. Alvarado to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Pretender José Arceo cantidad de dinero por alcances de asignacion qe Gobno Supmo le ofreció como Colono.' José Arceo claims arrears of the stipend the supreme government promised him as a colonist. A named Híjar-Padrés colonist pursuing his promised pay through the Diputacion after the colony's collapse.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: 'o 1834 = Julio 29 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial, Sobre Reglamento interior' [= 1834, July 29, Monterey. Territorial Diputación. On the internal regulation (rules of procedure).] Thirteen leaves, copied article by article. Opens 'Leida y aprovada la acta anterior. Siguio la discución del reglamento interior pa. el Gobo. de la Diputacion' — the discussion CONTINUED, so articles 1-7 were settled in an earlier session not copied in this run. The text here runs from Título 3º and takes the reglamento through Título 7º. TÍTULO 3º DEL SRIO: art. 8, 'La Diputación tendrá un Srio. nombrado por la misma conforme y en los términos que dispone el artº 4º del decreto de 23 de Junio de 1813'; art. 9 his obligations. TÍTULO 4º DE LAS SESIONES, arts. 10-16: sessions public; begin at 10 a.m. and last three hours; held Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays; provision for secret sessions, with ecclesiastical and religious matters reserved to them. TÍTULO 5º arts. 17-19 and TÍTULO 6º DE LAS COMISIONES, arts. 20-23. Art. 23 fixes the STANDING COMMITTEES: propios y arbitrios; Colonización; terrenos valdíos; Misiones; Gobierno y Policía; Reglamento y Ordenanzas municipales; obras públicas; Industria; Instrucción Pública y Estadística. Arts. 24-34 follow, of which two matter: art. 30 bars the Gefe Político as President, and the first vocal when acting as such, from belonging to any commission; art. 33 empowers commissions to demand from any archive and office of the Territory all instructions and documents they need, excepting those requiring secrecy. TÍTULO 7º DE LAS DISCUSIONES, arts. 35-50 and on. Closes with the absence of Sr. Ortega 'por enfermedad', signed 'José Figueroa = José Ma. Maldonado, Srio.' This session and the next are the constitutional self-organisation of the Californio Diputación, and they answer Figueroa's own complaint on n160 that the body had no reglamento interior.

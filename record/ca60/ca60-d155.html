SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Septiembre 2 = Mont. / Roberto Pardo á Figueroa' [= 1834, September 2, Monterey. Roberto Pardo to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Pide licencia pª correr caballada mesteña en el Tular de Sta Ynes.' Petition to run wild horses in the Tular of Santa Ynez. Granted a year later by the dictamen of 5 Sept 1835 (pp. 591-92) further down the band.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ (Se encuentran 19 recibos sin ningun interes, de pp 532. - 50.)' [= (There are 19 receipts of no interest at all, from pp. 532 to 550.)] Savage's own note in place of nineteen documents he chose not to copy: the remaining vouchers of the Sala de Sesiones account, originals pp. 532-550. Catalogued so that the nineteen suppressed receipts are on the record as a known, deliberate lacuna in the Savage copy rather than an absence in the original.

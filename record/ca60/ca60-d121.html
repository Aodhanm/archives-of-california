SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 10º. = Mont. / Figueroa á Presidente de la Diputacion' [= 1834, May 10, Monterey. Figueroa to the President of the Diputacion.] Abstract: 'Formacion de Compañias de milicia activa.' Figueroa puts the raising of active-militia companies before the Diputacion. Pairs with the Carrillo item of 1 Aug 1834 (pp. 485-86) and the San Diego sorteo file of 1835 later in the band.

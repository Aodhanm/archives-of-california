SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Octubre 30 = Mont. / Figueroa á Prestte de Diptn' [= 1834, October 30, Monterey. Figueroa to the President of the Diputacion.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que adjunta lista nominal de individuos qe J. M. Hijar nombró pª preceptores de 1ªs letras.' Figueroa lays before the assembly the nominal roll of primary schoolmasters appointed by José María Híjar. The covering letter to the roster on the next two leaves.

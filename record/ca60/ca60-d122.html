SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Enero 17 = Mont. / Figueroa al mismo, á Presidente y vocales' [= 1834, January 17, Monterey. Figueroa to the same, to the President and vocales.] Abstract: 'Dice Que gavinetes de Europa tratan de Colocar á Infante Franco de Paula en clase de Emperador de México.' Figueroa reports to the assembled Diputacion the rumour that European cabinets intend to place the Infante Francisco de Paula on a Mexican throne. A monarchist-conspiracy warning reaching the far northwestern frontier.

Diputacion Territorial (session acta); Secretary Jose Ma. Maldonado, then Juan B. Alvarado, Monterey, 1835-09-01

Legislative Records, tomo II · BANC MSS C-A 60, document 72

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n191-n219, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Sbre 1o = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835 - September 1 - Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Heading on n218, text completed on n219. Prior acta approved. Maldonado 'presento indice de documentos pertenecientes al archivo' (presented an index of the documents belonging to the archive) and then withdrew, Juan B. Alvarado taking his place. Account was then taken of various dictamenes on lands. Directly relevant to this project: an 1835 index of the Diputacion's own archive, made by its outgoing secretary, is noticed here and has no catalog record. NOTE: this is the session that establishes the September 1835 sequence begins at n218, INSIDE d9, not at n220 where d10 (summarised as 'the Sept 1835 sessions') starts.

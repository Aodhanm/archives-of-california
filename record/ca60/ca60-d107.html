SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Abril 28. = Mont. / Agustin B. Zamorano' [= 1834, April 28, Monterey. Agustín Vicente Zamorano (initialled 'B.' by Savage).] 'Dice 'Relacion de los Sitios Concedios que aun se encuentran pendientes en la Diputacion'' — a schedule of the land-grant sites already conceded but still pending before the Deputation, drawn up by Zamorano, the territorial secretary and printer. A two-page NAMED LIST of pending Alta California land grants as of 28 April 1834, i.e. a snapshot of the grant docket on the eve of secularization. Savage copies the title only and not one site name, so the list survives only as a title here; this is precisely the kind of item a band record makes invisible. Savage writes the middle initial 'B.' where the printer's name is normally Agustín Vicente Zamorano, and 'Concedios' for 'Concedidos'; both kept as written.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Noviembre 9. = Mont. / (Sin firma) á J. M. Herrera, Sub-comisario' [= 1834, November 9, Monterey. (Unsigned) to J. M. Herrera, Sub-comisario.] Abstract: several schoolmasters have represented their need of pecuniary aid; Herrera is to give them what is strictly necessary for leaving the capital; on the road they have vagajes and food at no cost; on arrival at their destinations they are to receive whatever further aid they need. The dispersal of the colony's teachers to their posts, with the fiscal mechanics spelled out.

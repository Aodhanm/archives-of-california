July 1834: the secularization 'masa central de bienes' — the pooling of the missions' property (the reglamento's core mechanism; a ministry order on the missions' pending *créditos*); the contaduría approved. ⭐ Item-level read 2026-08-05: a July land-grant cluster — terreno 'Alcalones' to Candelario Valencia (n142), grants to Juan Alvarez & Joaquín Bernal ('Sañanta,' n148), Luis Arenas 'San Felipe,' Hartnell referred (Colonización + Terrenos Baldíos committees); the San José judgeship made provisional pending the elevation of Los Angeles to Territorial Capital (n144); ⭐ mission cattle-slaughter (matanza) exceptions granted to service mission debts (a mission Padre Ministro, then Mission San Luis Rey, n149–150)

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Nbre 5. = Mont. / Hijar á Figueroa' [= 1834, November 5, Monterey. Híjar to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que queda impuesto de lo acordado pr la Diptn pª la entenanza de primeras letras, manifestandole que es de opinion se le concedan á la Colonia los preceptores qe se le habian señalado.' Híjar accepts the Diputacion's resolution but presses that the schoolmasters originally assigned to the colony be left to it. The point of friction between the colony's establishment and the territorial government.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Junio 17 = Sn José de Guadpe. / Pedro Chavoya á Gefe Politico' [= 1834, June 17, San José de Guadalupe. Pedro Chavoya to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Le comunica haber nombrado Srio de juzgado de su cargo, á José Fernandez.' The San José alcalde reports his appointment of José Fernandez as secretary of his court. A named San José municipal officer, useful for the SJ pueblo corpus.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 5. = Mont. / David Spence a Gefe Poltº' [= 1835, September 5, Monterey. David Spence to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Dice Que desea saber si llegara ó no jurisdiccion hasta rancho de San Andres.' Spence asks whether the jurisdiction extends as far as Rancho San Andrés. A named ranch tested against a municipal boundary, and the concrete case behind the jurisdiction-limits dictamen.

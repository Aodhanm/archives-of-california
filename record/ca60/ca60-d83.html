SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Octubre 14. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835, October 14, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] 'Se dio Cuenta con varios dictamenes Sobre Terrenos.' Account taken of various dictámenes on lands. Savage omits the 'Aprovada acta anterior' formula here, the only Octubre acta in the run that lacks it. Five original pages abstracted to one line.

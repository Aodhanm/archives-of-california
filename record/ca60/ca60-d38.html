SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 5. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 5, Monterey. Territorial Deputation. (Marginal mark: '+'. Savage abstract, opening 'Dice.') ⚠ The day is written '5.' in a hand identical to the 'Julio 5.' heading on n147, but this acta's own page cite, pp. 127-29, falls between the 6 July session (pp 126-27) and the 8 July session (pp. 129-33). I record the digit as written and do not commit to a corrected day.] Savage's abstract in full: 'Dice. Aprovada acta anterior. Se dio lectura á expedientes referentes á terrenos, quedando pendiente su discusion pª proxima Sesion.' A procedural sitting: land expedientes read, discussion carried over. Savage names none of them, so the specific grants cannot be identified from this leaf; the pending Bernal 'Sayanta' petition of the 5 July session is the obvious candidate but that is inference, not on the leaf.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Oct.e 23 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 23, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract. Prior acta approved. First reading given to a dictamen presented by the Comision de Gob.o 'relativo a nombramiento y honorario de Srio acordado por Ayunt.o de S. Barb.a' (on the appointment and fee of a Secretary agreed by the Ayuntamiento of Santa Barbara). Municipal-office business for the newly erected Santa Barbara ayuntamiento.

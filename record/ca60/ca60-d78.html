SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Sbre 15. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835, September 15, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] 'Dice: Aprovada la acta anterior. Se dio cuenta con varios dictámenes sobre Conseciones de terrenos.' Prior acta approved; account taken of several committee reports (dictámenes) on concessions of land. Four original pages reduced to one sentence. Note Savage's spelling 'Conseciones', kept as written.

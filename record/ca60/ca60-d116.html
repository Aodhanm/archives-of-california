Ybarra (addressee not stated).] 'Dice Que el veindario de dicho Ptº desea con ancia la declaracion de Ayuntamiento con motivo de pasar el nº de sus habitantes, de 1.000.' The vecindario of Santa Barbara eagerly desires to be declared an ayuntamiento, its population having passed 1,000. A dated population claim for Santa Barbara in July 1834 and the demographic argument on which the town's municipal status was granted.

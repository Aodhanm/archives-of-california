SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 6 = Mont. / Diputacion Teritorial' [= 1834, July 6, Monterey. Territorial Deputation. (Marginal mark: '+'. Savage abstract, opening 'Dice:'. The day digit is a cursive 6, distinguished at magnification from the figure-eight he uses for 8 two entries later and from the flat-topped 3 on n145. On this heading he writes 'Teritorial' with a single r; on the neighbouring headings the r is doubled.)] Savage's abstract in full: 'Dice: Aprovada acta anterior, se puso á discucion dictamen presentado por la Comision de Gobº y Policia referente á proposicion que hace Juzgado de 1ª instancia del Pueblo de Sn José pª qe se apruebe el nombramiento qe tiene hecho aquel Ayuntamiento de Srio. Aprovado.' One item only: final approval of the San José de Guadalupe Ayuntamiento's appointment of a secretary for its Juzgado de 1ª instancia. This closes a thread running through four consecutive sessions in this band (first reading 28 June on n141, second reading 1 July on n143, discussion and two propositions 3 July on n146, approval here).

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Octubre 28 = Mont. / José Castro á Ayuntamiento' [= 1835, October 28, Monterey. José Castro to the Ayuntamiento.] Abstract: 'Dice Que Alvarado propuso se colocara el retrato de Figueroa en el Salon de Sesiones de Diptn y qe se levante un monumento á su memoria.' Alvarado moves that Figueroa's portrait be hung in the Diputacion's session hall and a monument raised to his memory. The origin of the Figueroa commemoration whose committee report follows on n270-n271.

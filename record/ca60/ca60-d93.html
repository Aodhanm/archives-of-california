SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Junio 16 = Mont. / Carlos A. Carrillo' [= 1834, June 16, Monterey. Carlos Antonio Carrillo.] 'Reglamento interior de [la] Diputacion' — the heading and the words 'Reglamento interior de' close n229, the word 'Diputacion' and the page cite open n230, so the title itself is split across the leaf break. Sixteen original pages: the standing orders of the Territorial Deputation, drafted by Carlos Antonio Carrillo on Figueroa's 2 May instruction. Not the same document as the 16 June 1834 Figueroa order already catalogued at p.457 (doc 29), which shares the date but concerns the San Diego ayuntamiento expediente. A sixteen-page constitutional text of the Californio legislature with no record of its own.

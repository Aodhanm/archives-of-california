SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 3 = Mont. / José Antº Carrillo (no dice á quien)' [= 1834, May 3, Monterey. José Antonio Carrillo (addressee not stated).] 'Que Sesiones de Diputacion se reunan á las 10 de la mañana.' That the Deputation's sittings convene at ten in the morning. A one-page procedural motion, but it dates the sitting hour of the 1834 Diputación and it is by José Antonio Carrillo, not his brother Carlos Antonio, who dominates the rest of this bundle. Same day as Carlos Antonio Carrillo's two ayuntamiento propositions at p.452.

SESSION ACTA Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 3. = Mont. / (Sin firma) á Alcalde de S. José.' [= 1834, July 3, Monterey. (Unsigned) to the Alcalde of San José.] Abstract: 'Le comunica haberse dado cuenta con su oficio y no haber habido lugar á su peticion.' Formal notice to the San José alcalde that his office was reported to the assembly and his petition denied. Pairs with the Carrillo entry above.

García to Figueroa.] 'Sobre si extrangeros radicados, con familia y bienes raices están ó no obligados á prestar sus servicios' — on whether resident foreigners with families and real property are or are not obliged to render public/militia service. A direct document on the legal status of naturalised foreigners in Alta California.

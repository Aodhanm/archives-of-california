Verbatim heading: '1828 Set. 11. Rosario / Riesgo - á Herrera - Rentas de tabaco, pólvora y papel sellado' English gloss: demand for the accounts of the tobacco, gunpowder and stamped-paper revenues. Substance: requiring of him by supreme order the immediate remission of the cuentas de consumos, valores y gastos of the rentas de tabaco, pólvora y papel sellado. Herrera acknowledges this order in the already-catalogued item at n118 (1829 Mzo 30, San Diego, p.18).

On abuses in foreign commerce tolerated by Calif.'s political authority; instructions to correct

Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury), tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 40, document 104

On abuses in foreign commerce tolerated by Calif.'s political authority; instructions to correct: enforce new Ley de Aranceles (art.3 cap.1), stop illegal silver/gold bullion (pasta) extraction, charge ensaye/quinto/minería duties, forbid sales aboard ship, weekly reports of national & foreign ship entries/exits; cites frigates *Correo* (Courier) of Boston & *Franklin* and brig *Damián*[?] (pp.793–800; Corresp.925–32).

Verbatim heading: '1828 Mayo 3. Rosario / Riesgo - á Herrera - Sobre asegurar los derechos de toneladas' English gloss: securing the tonnage duty on cargoes entering Californian ports. Substance: informs him, by Supreme Government order of 12 de Abril último, that when the manifests of cargoes reaching ports of the Californias are presented 'dén los consignatarios de ellos la fianza de las toneladas que mida el buque'; and that likewise in the ajuste drawn up for the cargo it be made to appear what has been collected for the tonnage duty.

Pay claims of Alférez Luis Mª Ramírez, Subtⁿ Juan Rocha & civilian Vicente Gómez

Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury), tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 40, document 10

Pay claims of Alférez Luis Mª Ramírez, Subtⁿ Juan Rocha & civilian Vicente Gómez; pay left with Agustín V. Zamorano (acting habilitado at San Blas/Tepic); daily ration for Gómez only. ⚠ Savage note: this is the 'famous Vicente Gómez 'el Capador'' who later killed Alférez Ramírez on the Baja frontier

Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury), tomo I–V · BANC MSS C-A 40, document 329

Corte de Caja (monthly cash account) for March, by ramos. Data incl.: Cía de Caballería $566, Piquete de Mazatlán, Hospital, sangrador Manuel Crespo $13, cadete Francisco Araujo $20, Ig. Zamorano $30 (gastos de Tesorería), remitted to Presidio SF $967, Artillería $271 + $212, Infantería $111, remitted to habilitación of San Diego $1,423 & of SF $410, 150 costales de lona $150; Suma Data $4,505.1.4. Cargo: prior balance $4,589.3.6 + $1,500 from Aduana admor Rafael González; Suma $6,089.3.6; Existencia $1,584.2.2 (in víveres y efectos). p 746

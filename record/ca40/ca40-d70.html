Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'SB tithe (*diezmo*) account: $2,641 collected, 5% comisario commission'. Leaf n66 (stamp 67, ink 66) of 168036070_81_2, second entry: 'Importe total de diezmos cobrados .... $2640.- / Comision del Comisario 3% ..... 69.- / $2571.-'. The arithmetic closes only as 2640 - 69 = 2571, which is the leaf's own total; at scale 1 the final digit of the importe is a closed 0, unlike the stroke-form 1s of '2571' and '1272' on the same leaf, and the commission glyph reads 3, not 5. The collected total is $2,640 with a commission entered as 3%, not $2,641 at 5%.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

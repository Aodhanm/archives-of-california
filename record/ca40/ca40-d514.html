Circular on circumstances of assuming military command: summoned by ailing Gen. José Figueroa (oficio 22 Set), who died en route; convened a junta militar of garrison officers & federation employees; noted his inaptitude & being Spanish-born (son of an American); accepted per ordenanza succession; from today assumes military command of the territory

Baja-supply-crisis dossier: govt drew $12,000 on the Sinaloa treasuries ($8k Rosario/$4k Arizpe) but couldn't fulfill; granular disbursements via habilitado of Loreto José Mª Mata ($1500), seed-contractor ($3000), Manuel Gutiérrez ($3003); 800 fanegas maize + 100 beans + 14 tercios chile shipped to Guaymas; remarks Herrera's cargo left the Californias 'better attended than in many years'; loops in Comandante Genⁿ Figueroa

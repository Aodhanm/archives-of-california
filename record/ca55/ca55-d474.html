State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 474

Verbatim heading: 'Julio 29.' (date-only continuation of the Pallas entries above). Substance verbatim: 'Que se sirva dar su permiso p.a q.e se egecute el traslado de la Mision del parage llamado Casilepe al segundo llamado Ajanteguedo.' Gloss: asks the governor to grant permission for the transfer of the mission from the site called Casilepe to the second called Ajanteguedo. Both place names are underlined by Savage.

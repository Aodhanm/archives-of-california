Heading verbatim: 'Junio 30. (3. Dctos) pp 70 - 72.-73.' (1) artesanos' salary accounts for '96 remain in the R.l Tribunal y Audiencia de Cuentas for glosa; (2) will habilitar the companies' memorias in proportion to funds, excepting Monterey; drawing if needed up to 4000 p.s for Voluntarios/Artilleros; Artillero effects-notes go to Sarg.to José Roca at S. Fran.co; (3) has passed the R.l hacienda accounts of the 4 presidios of Nueva California to the Tribunal for glosa. ⚠ dcto 3's substance also absorbed into d390's summary — the entries are distinct.

Verbatim heading: 'Sbre. 26.' (date-only continuation under the 1800 Obre. 29 Marquina to Arrillaga heading at n410). Substance verbatim: 'La causa del Indio Cristiano Blas Calderon la he pasado a la R.l Audiencia de Guadalajara a quien toca determinarla por no ser dtho. Reo Indio Gentil ni Neofito.' Gloss: a jurisdictional ruling. The case of the Christian Indian Blas Calderon is remitted to the Real Audiencia of Guadalajara, to whom it belongs to decide, because the accused is neither a gentile Indian nor a neophyte. Month abbreviation as written is Sbre; see issues.

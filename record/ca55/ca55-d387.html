State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 387

Informe on Borica's petition (via Fiscal Hernandez de Alba) that the King ship an annual memoria de efectos to the vecindarios of Los Angeles and S. Jose de Gracia at 15 pct surcharge. Retrospect on the Californias; critique of the mission economy; full commerce program. Q: 'mientras las Misiones... formando solamente Pueblos de Yndios... no se conoce industria ni comercio' [missions as mere Indian pueblos, no industry or trade, block all progress].

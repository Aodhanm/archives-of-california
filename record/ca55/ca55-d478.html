Verbatim heading: '1792 Nbre. 6. Mont. / Jose Arguello al Gob.or D. J. Joaq.n de Arrillaga' Substance verbatim: 'Voy a hacer q.e el cabo Macario de Castro se entregue de la escolta y comision del pueblo de San Jose, y el cabo Jose Vallejo se traslade a la mision de la Soledad.' Gloss: Cpl. Macario de Castro is to take over the escolta and commission of the pueblo of San Jose, and Cpl. Jose Vallejo to transfer to Mission Soledad. Note: a second, separately abstracted 6 Nov 1792 Arguello letter, distinct from the two-document entry at n402 by its own page cite.

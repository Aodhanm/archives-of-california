English-translation letter proposing Russo-Spanish commerce & joint fur-hunting. Hagemeister left ill

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 333

English-translation letter proposing Russo-Spanish commerce & joint fur-hunting. Hagemeister left ill; the RAC brig *Ilmena* is bound for 'Port Ross' with St-Petersburg papers & goods; sends agent Khlebnikov ('Clebnicoff') to arrange 'a commercial intercourse'; offers goods for grain; recalls Hagemeister's petition to the Russian ambassador at Madrid for leave to hunt furs on the California coast 'in company with His Catholic Majesty's subjects, on equal shares'; gift of a Russian glass

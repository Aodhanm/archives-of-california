State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 239

Early San Francisco mineral discovery: sends samples of three kinds of mineral/ore discovered in that jurisdiction (~1 legua off), with named claims 'Santa Sra. del Carmen,' 'San José,' 'La Descubridora'; rough assay of their silver/gold content (mostly fire-ores, 'muy ordinarios'); found via soldier Juan Bernal

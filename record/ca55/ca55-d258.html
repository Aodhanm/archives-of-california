Savage's heading reads ‘1733 ⟦sic, for 1793⟧. Marzo 27. – S. Diego. / J. Joaq.n de Arrillaga – á José Argüello – Mont.’ His abstract, in full: «Previniéndole, no acceda á la solicitud de los dos desertores Ingleses de que habló en Carta del 2 del corriente, los cuales habían solicitado abrasar ⟦abrazar⟧ la Religión Católica, dando esta razon como motivo principal de su desercion: en el supuesto, de que debiendo S.E. determinar de ellos, era necesario aguardar hasta entonces ó no ser q.e sucediese antes peligro de muerte.» So: Arrillaga instructs Argüello not to grant the request of two English deserters who had asked to embrace the Catholic religion, giving that as the chief motive of their desertion. Because the Viceroy must rule on their disposal, Argüello is to wait until then, unless danger of death should intervene first. The record carries no reference to Bodega and none to any ship; neither word appears on the leaf. A separate letter of the same date, ca54-d74, concerns five English deserters and the terms of their delivery to Vancouver.

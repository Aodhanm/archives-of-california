Verbatim heading: '31 Marzo.' (date-only continuation of the 1798 Garcia/San Blas entry above). Substance: through the habilitado gral of those presidios, the Procurador de la Religion de S. Fernando, his sindico in Tepic, and from these almacenes, there is remitted by the Fragata Concepcion the value in viveres of 5060 p.s 17 mar.s, itemised verbatim: 'Al presidio de S. Fran.co 803-1-10 / Al de Mont 2275-4-23 / Al de Sta Barbara 502-2. 30 / Voluntarios d Cataluna 838-4. 31 / Para Artilleros 339. 3. 2' and, concluding at the top of n379, 'Para Volunt.s en Mont 300-7. 33.'

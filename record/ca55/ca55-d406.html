'Que el habilitado D. Manuel de Corcaba [?], pidio el pago de 64 pieles de Nutria pertenecientes a la Comp.a del Presidio de Sta. Barbara, q.e formada liquidacion resultaron abonables a dha. Comp.a 59 pieles, las 5 restantes se habian ya satisfecho al Factor Jose Arvide [?]' = sea-otter pelt account of the Santa Barbara company settled at 59 of 64 skins, the other five already paid to the Factor. Surname reading uncertain (Corcaba / Carcaba); 'Arvide' also uncertain. Savage prints no closing period.

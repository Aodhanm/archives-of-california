Heading verbatim: '1800 Nbre. 5 Megico. / Jimenez.' Opening verbatim

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 454

Heading verbatim: '1800 Nbre. 5 Megico. / Jimenez.' Opening verbatim: 'Resumen del Situado q.e corresponde a los cuatro Presidios de la Alta California en los anos que se espresan segun los pormenores q.e acompanan los Habilitados particulares, y conforme al Reglamento de 1.o de Junio de 1779, y posteriores resoluciones.' Then a full table, figures as written (pesos.reales.granos): San Diego on account of the haber of 1801, 14.739.7.0, with 100 p. added for a difference noticed in the sum of the Regulacion (00.100.0.0), bracketed to 14.839.7.0; Sta. Barbara on the same account 15.750.6.0; San Fran.co 9.885.0.0; Monterey 21.089.1.9 5/8 less the 3.548.7.3 5/8 added by the alcance said to have come out in the Real Hacienda account of the year 99 for not yet having been liquidated, bracketed to 17.540.2.5 1/2; to the artisans their salary in the present year of 1800, 1.658.0.10; to the nine pobladores of the Villa de Branciforte with ration only at 5 p. each, 540.0.0; to the Compania de Voluntarios de Cataluna their haber, 11.930.6.8; to the piquete de Artilleria the same, 3366.6.4; total 'Asciende el todo a 75511 p.s 5 rs. 9 1/2 gr.' = 75.511.5.9 1/2, which sum is to be delivered in the present year to the Habilitado gral. Closing verbatim: 'Mesa de California de la Cont.a mayor del Real Tribunal y Aud.a de Cuentas de Megico a 4 de Obre. de 1800 = Joaquin Jose Gomez y Vazquez.' Runs across three leaves: heading and opening on 58. (stamp 363), the whole table on 59. (stamp 364), closing lines and page cite on 60. (stamp 365).

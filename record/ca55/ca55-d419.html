Sequel on the same fugitive crisis and on Indians killed. VERBATIM: 'Que llego un indio llamado Celestino el cual vio llegar a la casa de los P.P. 4 nuevos Cristianos Natanes de los q.e habian ido a buscar a los 7 cristianos q.e mataron'. Gloss: an Indian named Celestino reported four newly Christian Natanes arriving at the padres' house, of the party sent to find the seven Christians who were killed; today the padres sent to Udon with two of the four Natanes to la otra banda.

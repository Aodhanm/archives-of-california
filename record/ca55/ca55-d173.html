The wreck of the supply ship *San Carlos* at San Francisco

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 173

The wreck of the supply ship *San Carlos* at San Francisco: rebuts a complaint that the mission fathers failed to aid it the night it was lost — P. Martín was alone (the other ill at Santa Clara); he sent every available Indian, who worked 3 days & nights up to the waist in water, then 50 more for the salvage maneuvers, and provided the survivors with food

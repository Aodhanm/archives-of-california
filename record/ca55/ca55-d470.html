Verbatim heading: '1795. En.o 14. Mision de S. Fernando. / Fr. Jorge Coello, al Gob.or Diego Borica.' Substance verbatim: 'Que se observara esactam.te el tratar con los oficiales y tripulacion de las embarcaciones Ynglesas de D. Jorge Vancover.' Gloss: the minister undertakes to observe exactly the rules on dealing with the officers and crews of the English vessels of George Vancouver. San Fernando here is Velicata in Baja California; San Fernando Rey in Alta California was not founded until 1797.

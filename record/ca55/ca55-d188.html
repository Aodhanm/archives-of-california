On the Río Colorado (Yuma) catastrophe & orders to pursue its authors

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 188

On the Río Colorado (Yuma) catastrophe & orders to pursue its authors — quotes his 26 Aug prevención (awaiting confirmation of 'la catástrofe' before grave measures; leave presidio garrisons, assemble the rest of the troop against any rebel-Indian incursion & to support the punitive operations); convened a Junta de Guerra y Real Hacienda; passed orders to Gob. Militar Jacobo Ugarte y Loyola & Tte. Col. Pedro Fages; the expedition commander to circle the Río Colorado, reach the ruined establishments, then hand the command to Neve [cf. ]

On his resignation as governor: a year begging the Viceroy to accept it, in vain

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 21

On his resignation as governor: a year begging the Viceroy to accept it, in vain; could not list his grievances 'en un tomo entero'; asks influence so his *renuncia* be approved and he be given a *Intendencia de Provincia* or named Ministro of a Caja Real; published the bando on the oath to the Constitution; wishes the Obispo de Sonora would visit (confirmations suspended >24 yrs)

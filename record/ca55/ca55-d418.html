State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 418

Report on a mission-sponsored search for runaway Christian Indians across the Bay. VERBATIM: 'Que hoy ha llegado el Indio Julian q.e venia de la otra vanda, donde ocho dias antes lo mandaron los P.P. con dos muchachos a q.e buscaron Cristianos simarrones.' Gloss: the Indian Julian returned today from the far shore (the East Bay), where the padres had sent him eight days earlier with two boys to look for Christian fugitives.

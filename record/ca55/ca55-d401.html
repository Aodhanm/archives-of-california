State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 401

'Copia de las Condiciones propuestas a S. E. por el Yngeniero 2.o D. Miguel Constanzo p.a las contratas de los artesanos q.e han de remitirse a la Peninsula de Californias con el objeto de q.e ensenen sus oficios a los indios de ella.' Five numbered terms: 1a teaching to last four or five years, enough time for the Indians to learn a mechanical trade, return transport to S. Blas at Real Hacienda cost; 2a monthly salary per each man's exercise and ability plus rations for their families, administered by the missions to which they are assigned; 3a telares utiles e instrumentos of their professions at Real Erario cost, shipped on the same vessel, most manufacturable at S. Blas by that department's carpenters and smiths, the rest sent from Guadalajara or Megico; 4a if after their contract they wish to stay 'en calidad de vecinos' they get lands, some cattle and other aid 'p.a formarse un establecim.to ventajoso'; 5a sending the families successively is most conducive, since San Blas ships can carry no more than one or two families at a time.

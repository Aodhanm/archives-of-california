State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 204

Letter on the founding of Mission Santa Cruz — received Gov. Romeu's copy of the Viceroy's order on the two new missions; awaits Sal's arrival to carry it out; coordinating the road being worked, supply transport & custody, the friars destined for the founding, and a *casita* the Indians are to build at the site; warm thanks for Sal's help

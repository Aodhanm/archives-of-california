Climate/health *relación* for 1797: many frosts, great winds Jan–May, thick summer fogs

State Papers, Sacramento, tomo VI–IX · BANC MSS C-A 55, document 164

Climate/health *relación* for 1797: many frosts, great winds Jan–May, thick summer fogs; deaths from *tabardillo* (typhus) & pleurisy, many in the presidio from consumption & ulcers; abundant births & good wheat, but rats, mice & moles destroyed the seed, even on irrigated land

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Copia de una Bula (16 dic 803) prorrogando 6 años el mando del Tesoro Genl. de los Reales Ejércitos y Armada'. Leaf read: 168036074_80_8_2 n198 (printed stamp '198', hand '197'). Heading '+ 1805 - Loreto. / Gob á Com.te de Mont.' confirms author, recipient, place. SUMMARY = WRONG. The leaf reads verbatim (magnified): 'Mayo 7- Envia Copia de Brebe, 16 Dic.e 803, q.e proroga p.r otros 6 años el mando del Vicario Gral. de los R.s Ejércitos y Armada. p. 661.' The catalogue renders this as 'Copia de una Bula (16 dic 803) prorrogando 6 años el mando del Tesoro Genl. de los Reales Ejércitos y Armada'. Two errors: (1) 'Vicario Gral.' (the Vicario General Castrense, the military vicariate) has become 'Tesoro Genl.' — a different office entirely, and the one substantive fact the abstract exists to carry; (2) 'Brebe' (a papal breve) has become 'Bula'. Date 'Mayo 7' and 'p. 661' are correct. OMISSIONS = OK: the leaf's four entries (Mayo 7 p.661; '30' 2 docs p.662; Junio 10 2 docs p.662; 'A los Comisionados de los Pueblos' Junio 12 pp.663-5) map to docs 725-728.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

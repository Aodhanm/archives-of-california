  • Previously read savage_page: 'B-62'. Leaf read: 168036074_80_8_2 n244. SAVAGE_PAGE = WRONG. The leaf's top right carries printed stamp '244' and, beneath it, Savage's own hand-written '19.' (magnified). The catalogue records 'B-62', which is simply orig_page 62 with a 'B-' prefix — the original tomo page, not the copyist's page. I verified the B-series pagination directly: n226 is hand-numbered '1.' under the heading '+ 1800 = Feb.o 8 = Loreto. — / Gobernador Interino Arrillaga á José Font.', n240 is '15', n242 '17', n243 '18', n244 '19' — i.e. hand page = n − 225 exactly. The defect is systematic, not local: every ca26 record from doc 834 (n226, 'B-1') onward carries savage_page = 'B-' + orig_page, so ~450 records in this volume have a savage_page that is a duplicate of the original-tomo page rather than a read value. The correct value here is 19 (stamp 244).

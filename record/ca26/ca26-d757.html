Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'El Alférez de la Guerra se queja de que le hacen pagar derechos por 2 barriles de mescal que introdujo'. Leaf read: 168036074_80_8_2 n206 (stamp '206', hand '205'). Heading '+ 1806 - Mont. / Gob á Sarg.to Manuel Gomez.' and sub-heading 'A Condestable Sánchez.' confirm author, recipient, place; 'Mayo 8' and 'p. 681 -' confirm date and orig_page. SUMMARY = WRONG. Magnified, the leaf reads verbatim: 'Mayo 8 — Q.e Alferez de la Guerra se queja de q.e se ha opuesto á pagar derechos de 2 barriles mescal q.e introdujo — p. 681 —'. The catalogue renders it 'El Alférez de la Guerra se queja de que le hacen pagar derechos por 2 barriles de mescal que introdujo'. This inverts the transaction: the leaf records a complaint that the addressee HAS REFUSED to pay the duties ('se ha opuesto á pagar'), the catalogue a complaint that duties are BEING EXACTED ('le hacen pagar'). The complainant, the goods and the quantity are right; the fiscal fact is reversed. OMISSIONS = OK: six entries on the leaf (Marzo 26, Abril 14, '30', Mayo 8 to Roca, Mayo 8 to Sánchez, '9') are covered by docs 753-758.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: "Mission of Sta Cruz no novedad; asked missionaries about feeding Branciforte pobladores — 'tan maíz no pueden porque no tienen mas de la pura ración para la escolta'"; orig_page: 'p.571–575'; scan: 'n382'. n382 (stamp 384 / ink 189) carries the tail of a long artillery and coastal-defence letter: no ball of the second gun's calibre and no confidence in grapeshot for it, the more serviceable guns being repaired and re-carriaged 'con el temor de que no queden mui buenos; porque no hay errero', more than twenty escopetas needing light repairs and others lacking stocks with which vecinos who have none could be armed, all sheet lead cast into balls and buckshot, and cartridges being made 'donde me alcanse el papel'; and an explicit distrust of the Indian auxiliaries, 'mejor se deve reselar de ellos, y que con mucha facilidad sigan el partido contrario'. The row describes Mission Santa Cruz reporting no novedad and the feeding of the Branciforte pobladores. The savage_page 384 is correct; the summary is not of this document.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

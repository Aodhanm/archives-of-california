The heading below is what the manuscript leaf actually carries. ⭐ VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Mayo 23. S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Fragata 'Princesa'». SUBSTANCE: reports that the frigate «N.a S.ra del Rosario, alias la Princesa» has appeared off the mouth of the Río de la Purísima, her commander D.n García Serpe y Manrique, pilot Salvador Menéndez, chaplain P. Ximénez; she carried many people and a good number of sick, and lay beyond la Gaviota because the weather and fog would not let her make the anchorage.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Extracto de revista Aug'; orig_page: 'p.139–140'; scan: 'n245'. n245 (stamp 249 / ink 52) carries the frigate 'N.a S.ra del Rosario, alias la Princesa' sighted off the mouth of the Rio de la Purisima with many sick aboard (pp.151-152) and, next day, her anchoring with the Santa Barbara and San Diego memorias, 160 plazas from captain to pages, and 34 scorbutic and contagious sick landed into a barraca built so as not to harm the people of Santa Barbara (pp.153-154). The claimed pp.139-140 are on n241 and are the mission libranzas. No August extracto de revista.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

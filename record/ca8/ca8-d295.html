Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Monthly Jul; road construction; Mission SBV escolta affairs'; orig_page: 'p.127–131'; scan: 'n240–241'. n240-n241 (stamps 244-245) carry three arms appraisals for invalid and retiring soldiers, itemised to the peso (escopeta 5 ps, cuera 15 ps, cartuchera 1 p 4 r.s, adarga 2 ps, macho manco 18 ps, 3 caballos mancos 27 ps, at pp.132-133; cuenta de ajuste pp.131-134; a second appraisal pp.130-136), then four libranzas for what the missions of S.n Juan Capistrano, S.n Gabriel, S.n Buenaventura and S.ta Barbara were owed at the end of December (p.139) and a libranza of 2,761 p.s 5 r.s to Ten.te Ant.o Grajera (p.140). No July monthly report, road construction or San Buenaventura escolta affairs.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Abril 2. S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica». SUBSTANCE: answering the very reserved oficio of 18 February, the coin of which he gave account is what circulates here, and as Borica tells him «no quieren decir falsos las dos ojas que señalan»; he remains careful to collect those that are false and will give account of them.

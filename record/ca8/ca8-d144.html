Correction to this entry.
  • ⚠ This row is NOT an erroneous entry — its document is real and its scan span is right. The «Extracto de la Gazeta de Londres del 1.º de Agosto de 1796» does run n84–n86, closing on n86 (stamp 90) at «p. 271» after the seven numbered Spanish grievances against Britain. Only the CITATION is wrong: the row cites p.272–276, which is where the three padrones on n87 sit. The extract's own pages are ~270–271.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Monthly Mar; correo; road repairs; livestock from missions'; orig_page: 'p.99–103'; scan: 'n228–229'. n228-n229 (stamps 234-235) carry the quadruplicated hojas de servicios of the officers and first sergeant with the conduct report on his subalterns (p.99), the reply to the very reserved letter of 18 February about coin 'que no quieren decir falsos las dos ojas que señalan' (p.100), the answer to a reparo on the debts of the alferez, Sgt Jose Ortega, cabo J.n Romero and other soldiers (pp.102-103), and the order that the escopetas ripolenas already distributed to the vecinos be supplemented with flints charged to the gratification fund and 2,000 reserve cartridges delivered to the comisionado (p.104). No correo, road repairs or mission livestock.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

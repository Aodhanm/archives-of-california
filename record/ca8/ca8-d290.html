Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Extracto de revista Apr'; orig_page: 'p.111–112'; scan: 'n233'. n233 (stamp 239 / ink 42) carries the close of the recount of Bulas de la Santa Cruzada for the biennium 92-93 before Goycoechea and Fr. Jose de Miguel, witnesses Pablo de Cota and Jose Tadeo Sanchez, itemised 3 bulas de vivos at two pesos, 49 at 8 reales, 287 at 2 reales, 5 de Caticinos, 5 de Composicion at 12 reales and 74 de difuntos (pp.117-118), followed by the 26 June Monterey 'Documento de la quema de Bulas'. No extracto de revista at the claimed pp.111-112.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Multiple admin items; construction reports. The claimed original pages fall inside Arrillaga's reconnaissance diary. Savage's own bracketing citations fix this stretch: the San Ildefonso alliance extract closes on n90 (stamp 94) at «pp. 276-278», and Arrillaga's «Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado» (headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente») closes on n96 (stamp 100) at «pp. 279-345». Original pages 279–345 are therefore consumed by that one diary, and no Sal monthly report can sit inside them. On the leaves this row is bound to: n103 (stamp 107) carries the body of «Franqueas á artesanos» (p. 375), «Formacion de diarios por los Cavos» (pp. 376-377), and «1796 = Marzo 25 _ S.ta Cruz / (Firmado): José Ant.nio Sanchez / Regreso de soldados» (p. 378).
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

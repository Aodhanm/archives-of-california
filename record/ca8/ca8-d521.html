VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Junio 30. S.n Fr.co / Arguello á Borica. Fundación de la misión de S.n José

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIV–XV · BANC MSS C-A 8, document 521

VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Junio 30. S.n Fr.co / Arguello á Borica. Fundación de la misión de S.n José. etc.» SUBSTANCE: encloses the certificate of Sargento Pedro Amador attesting that possession was taken of the site of la Alameda where Mission San José was founded; Amador stayed twenty days and on leaving put cabo Alejo Miranda in charge with a «papel de puntos»; delivered to cabo Alejo, besides the utensils ordered, two yoke of oxen (because the mission fathers needed their own) for the troop to cart materials for the guard-house and barracks; asks to whom the tools used in those works were charged.

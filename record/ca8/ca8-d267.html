The heading below is what the manuscript leaf actually carries. ⭐⭐⭐ VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Julio 20. S.n Fr.co / Arguello á Borica. El virey Branciforte no permite se hagan construcciones militares nuevas. Dificultad en componer las existentes y para construccion de la 2.a Batería de Yerba Buena. Poca esperanza de auxilio de las misiones. Atrasos posibles.» SUBSTANCE: it is very painful that the Viceroy Branciforte will not allow a presidio, quarters for his company and for the volunteer detachment to be built at this port; he must proceed with the greatest economy and merely repair the existing fabric; the strong continual winds destroy the thatched roofs, which must be renewed annually, and the almacenes, guard-house and cavalry barracks must be renewed entirely; and if the second battery of Yerba Buena is to be built, earth, water and timber must be carried on men's shoulders. On mission labour: «Quedan las misiones de S.n Fr.co y S.ta Clara no son como se les pide, sino como y cuando quieren darlo, como ha sucedido en la construccion de Bateria y le consta al Ingeniero D.n Alberto de Cordoba».

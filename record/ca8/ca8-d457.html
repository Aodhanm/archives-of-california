Provincial State Papers, tomo XIV–XV · BANC MSS C-A 8, document 457

VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Junio 23. Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Borica. Lanas. recuas - mais.' GLOSS: wool, pack trains, maize. SUBSTANCE: says that yesterday he sent alferez Pablo Cotta to fetch the 200 arrobas de lana contracted at the mision de Sn Juan, which has orders to deliver them to the Los Angeles vecino Fr.co Reyes, who is to carry them as far as the capital at 6 reales the arroba. He complains of little recua and little maize.

The heading below is what the manuscript leaf actually carries. ⭐ VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Abril 11. S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Nombram.to de vicario foraneo». SUBSTANCE: understands by the oficio of 22 March that the Bishop of Sonora, Fr. Francisco Rouset de Jesús, has named Fr. Fermín Francisco de Lasuén, present President of these missions, his vicario foráneo; he has had him recognised as such so that he may use his faculties in this jurisdiction, and will give him the necessary assistance.

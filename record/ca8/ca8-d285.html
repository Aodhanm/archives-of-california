Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Monthly Feb; supply shipments; livestock movements'; orig_page: 'p.92–96'; scan: 'n225–226'. n225-n226 (stamps 231-232) carry the padron de almas transmittal (p.91), the cuenta de diezmos of 96 with its libranza for the Guadalajara cajas reales (pp.92-93), a pliego itemising the armament, saddlery, clothing and mounts of the eight bachelor soldiers passing to the presidio (p.94), and the statement of each of those eight Indians' credit and debit including supplies since 1 January (p.95). No February monthly report of supply shipments and livestock movements.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Extracto de revista; detailed estado de fuerza for SB company'; orig_page: 'p.87–91'; scan: 'n223–224'. the declared span pp.87-91 carries, across n223-n224 (stamps 229-230), the obligation extracted from the retired Ten.te Fr.co Ortega and his three sons (p.86), eight equipped soldiers sent to the mission with orders to halt some days at S.n Luis (p.87), the reply to the Real Tribunal de Cuentas' reparos (p.88), 'Diarios de comand.tes' remitting five diaries, four for December by the escolta commanders plus Sgt Jose de Ortega's own, the November one missing because the S.n Buenaventura commander was ill (p.89), and the duplicated gratification-fund and retention accounts (p.90), closing with the padron de almas for the presidio, its three missions and the pueblo de los Angeles (p.91). A padron of souls is not an estado de fuerza. ⚠ CA8-REPAIR-PLAN-2026-09-03 listed this row as CORRECT; the leaf disproves it.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

PLACE = PARTIAL (place column empty; the three places are folded into the author string). RECIPIENT = NA: these are submitted diaries with no addressee in any heading. SUMMARY = WRONG on two points, both inside what reads as a quotation. The leaf reads: 'Nada digno de mención excepto que Inocencio Mesa salió con su mujer el 11 de Julio y los gentiles lo mataron porque su padre antes havia matado algunos gentiles de este Valle.' First, 'lo mataron' is singular: only Mesa is said to be killed, not 'Mesa + wife murdered'. The catalogue's own d379 confirms it, recording that 'Mesa's widow identified the killer'. Second, the catalogue quotes 'un padre antes había matado algunos gentiles de este Valle'; the leaf plainly reads 'su padre', that is Mesa's own father, so the motive is a family vendetta, not a killing by a mission priest. That single letter reverses the meaning of the sentence. Omissions OK: n390 holds only these three diaries and all three are described.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

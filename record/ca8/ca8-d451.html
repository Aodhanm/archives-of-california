VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Abril 1.o Sta Barbara / Goycoechea a Borica.' (Savage writes Goycoechea a Borica with an acute accent on the a). GLOSS: Goycoechea to Governor Borica, Santa Barbara, 1 April 1797. SUBSTANCE: acknowledges receipt of an oficio conveying a Royal order that intimates obedience to all officers and dependientes de marina for the orders of Viceroy Branciforte, and that commands them to represent their needs and the considerations of what is necessary to the Royal service through his conduct.

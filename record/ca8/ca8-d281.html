  • Previously read summary: 'Extracto de revista Sep; estado de fuerza'; orig_page: 'p.79–81'; scan: 'n220'. n220 (stamp 226 / ink 29) carries the tail of an inventory (including the order to Ten.te Hermenegildo Sal to draw up a diligencia with two witnesses accrediting the 14 tercios of steel lost or burnt in the fire of 1789) at p.73, Arguello on settling the invalids' account, and Arguello acknowledging the model for separating the Mexico memorias from those of S.n Blas (pp.76-77).

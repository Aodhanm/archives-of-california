Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Extracto de revista Apr; state of force'; orig_page: 'p.29–31'; scan: 'n203'. n203 (stamp 209 / ink 12) carries a letter on the presidio's fabric: most roofs need re-tiling, the almacenes, guardia and cuartel de Cavalleria must be renewed entirely, the second Yerba Buena battery would need earth, water and timber carried on men's shoulders, and the missions of S.n Francisco and S.ta Clara supply Indian labour 'no son como se les pide, sino como y cuando quieren darlo', as the engineer Alberto de Cordova can attest.

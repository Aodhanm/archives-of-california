Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Monthly Jul; Mission San José founding details; Indian labor arrangements; Amador arrives. n211–212 (stamps 217–218) carry the 1797 Raymundo-expedition dossier — a padre asking for «una espedicioncilla … con 4 sacos de trigo» (p.59) and «1797 Junio 27 / Fray José Maria Fernandez al gob.r Borica. The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Monthly report Oct; construction, Indian labor. n86 (stamp 90) carries the CLOSE of the London Gazette extract — «7.º La detención de varias embarcaciones españolas y las vejaciones que diariamente cometen los comandantes de las embarcaciones británicas» — ending at p.271; n87 (stamp 91) carries three padrones at pp.272–275. There is no October 1795 monthly report on either leaf.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

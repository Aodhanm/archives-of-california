Provincial State Papers, tomo XIV–XV · BANC MSS C-A 8, document 148

«Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado» — headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente», sin firma, with the pencilled note «The original is signed José Joaq.n Arrillaga». Day-by-day from «Junio 14 de 1796» (embarked for S.n Luis in the schooner 'Saturnina') to «Noviembre 21» (returned to S.n Vicente, where the diary was drawn up); journey through the Baja California frontier and the margins of the Colorado; Chamijag and Madroño indigenous groups; geographic observations. 67 original pages

Correction to this entry.
  • ⚠ Two corrections, both material. (1) PAGES: the diary's true span is «pp. 279-345» — Savage's own closing citation on n96 (stamp 100) — not p.287–299. It is a 67-page document, not a 13-page one, and the understatement desynchronised the rest of the sequence: the catalogue lost 46 original pages here (345−299) and never recovered them, so EVERY following row cites an original page too low for its own savage binding, by +43 to +54, and the shortfall NEVER CLOSES (still +45 at savage 131, the end of the sequence; measured at 24 savage points against leaf reads). The immediately invented span pp.300–346 was filled by the constructed rows d149–d164; the offset itself continues well past them. (2) DATE: the row is dated 1795; the document is headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente», is unsigned with the pencilled note «The original is signed José Joaq.n Arrillaga», and its day-entries run from «Junio 14 de 1796» to «Noviembre 21». Full title: «Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado».
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

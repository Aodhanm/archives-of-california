Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Extracto de revista Jan 1796; estado de fuerza. The claimed original pages fall inside Arrillaga's reconnaissance diary. Savage's own bracketing citations fix this stretch: the San Ildefonso alliance extract closes on n90 (stamp 94) at «pp. 276-278», and Arrillaga's «Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado» (headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente») closes on n96 (stamp 100) at «pp. 279-345». Original pages 279–345 are therefore consumed by that one diary, and no Sal monthly report can sit inside them. On the leaves this row is bound to: n102 (stamp 106) carries «Publicacion de Cedula» (pp. 371-372, Sal reporting he published the royal cédula of 6 June 1795 creating a consulado at Guadalajara), «Sobre artilleros» (pp. 373-374), and the heading «Franqueas á artesanos» carried over. No January extracto de revista.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

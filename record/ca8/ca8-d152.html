Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Extracto de revista Nov; estado de fuerza. The claimed original pages fall inside Arrillaga's reconnaissance diary. Savage's own bracketing citations fix this stretch: the San Ildefonso alliance extract closes on n90 (stamp 94) at «pp. 276-278», and Arrillaga's «Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado» (headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente») closes on n96 (stamp 100) at «pp. 279-345». Original pages 279–345 are therefore consumed by that one diary, and no Sal monthly report can sit inside them. On the leaves this row is bound to: n99 (stamp 103) carries «1796 = Marzo 31 = San Francisco / H. Sal á D. Borica / Entrado á escolta de un soldado» (p. 356), «1796 = Marzo 31 _ S.n Francisco / H. Sal á D. Borica. / Sobre un sentensiado» (pp. 357-358), and the heading of a «Carta de aviso» of Marzo 30 carried over. Sal is the correspondent, but these are March 1796 letters, not a November 1795 extracto de revista.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

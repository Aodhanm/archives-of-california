Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Monthly report; Indian labor; supply situation. The claimed original pages fall inside Arrillaga's reconnaissance diary. Savage's own bracketing citations fix this stretch: the San Ildefonso alliance extract closes on n90 (stamp 94) at «pp. 276-278», and Arrillaga's «Diario de los reconocimientos que verificó el teniente coronel que suscribe en la Frontera de la gentilidad de la antigua California y márgenes del Colorado» (headed «1796 = Diciembre 9 = S.n Vicente») closes on n96 (stamp 100) at «pp. 279-345». Original pages 279–345 are therefore consumed by that one diary, and no Sal monthly report can sit inside them. On the leaves this row is bound to: n105 (stamp 109) carries «1796 = Abril 7 = S.n Francisco. / (Firmado): Raymundo Carrillo / Certificado» (pp. 383-384) and «1796 = Abril 4 = Sta Barbara / F. Goycoechea á D. Borica / Construccion de una Capilla» (p. 385).
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

Cuentas con oficiales de Marina y Fortificaciones.» SUBSTANCE: Teniente de Fragata Ramón Saavedra having delivered the iron and serviceable timber to the habilitado, a relación will follow; Teniente de Navío Jacinto Caamaño supplied only eight palanques for moving the cannon, some waxed cloths and a quantity of old cordage for the fascines, very bad. Whatever is consumed on the fortification works now building must be evidenced by documents approved by the Engineer; the sergeant of artillery José Roca will take charge of the surplus stores belonging to the batteries.

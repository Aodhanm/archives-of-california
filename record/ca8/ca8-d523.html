VERBATIM HEADING: «Remita cuenta de tabacos». SUBSTANCE: the tobacco account with the inventory of stock at the last of December 96 and a copy of the libranza; like documents remitted to the administrador of Monterey; receipt to the conductor of the situado for a single case of cigars, the four missing cases having been delivered at Monterey by Francisco Hijosa, comisario of San Blas.

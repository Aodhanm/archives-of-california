S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Fragata 'Princesa' anclada». SUBSTANCE: yesterday the frigate anchored, carrying the memorias of the presidios of Santa Bárbara and San Diego, her officers as named and a bleeder, and 160 plazas from captain to pages; at the captain's request 34 sick with scurvy and other contagious diseases are to be landed, for whom a barraca is to be built so as not to harm the people of Santa Bárbara.

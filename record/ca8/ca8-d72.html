Instructions to Sgt. Pedro Amador: military expedition to Sta Cruz

Provincial State Papers, tomo XIV–XV · BANC MSS C-A 8, document 72

Instructions to Sgt. Pedro Amador: military expedition to Sta Cruz. Depart with 6 soldiers, arrive 2–3 AM undetected; find Indian informant re threats to kill padres/tropa; keep diario; apprehend cabecillas; 'do least damage to gentiles — if battle, fire first to the air as sign of peace'; report to Governor near Monterey

Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read summary: 'Monthly report; mentions sending Córdova to explore; asks about escolta size'; orig_page: 'p.253'; scan: 'n78'. n78 (stamp 82 / ink 81) carries the tail of a missionary-service certificate (17 years in Baja California, with marginal notes for Fr. Jose Estevez 16, Fr. Jose Lafuente 22, Fr. Jose Garcia 16) at p.253, Arrillaga's certificate of 7 Sept 1796 from Sto Tomas confirming what the padre presidente Cayetano Pallas had stated at p.254, and at its foot the OPENING of Alberto de Cordova's defence informe to the marques de Branciforte. There is no monthly report on the leaf.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

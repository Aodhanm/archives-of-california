Correction to this entry.
  • Previously read Extracto de revista Sep; estado de fuerza. n84 (stamp 88, ink 87) carries «1796 = Abril 30 = S.n Francisco / Pedro Alberni á D. Borica. Sobre fragatas» (pp.269–270) and an undated, unsigned «Extracto de la Gazeta de Londres del 1.º de Agosto de 1796» — not a September 1795 revista. The row above does not describe this leaf.
The originals of these records burned in the 1906 San Francisco fire. What survives is Thomas Savage’s 1870s copy, and this page describes that copy.

*Remisión de documentos / Fundación de una Misión* (2 ofs) — forwards Extracts & company State; encloses Sgt. Ignacio Olivera's document showing that on 8 Sept. last possession was taken of the new mission, named San Fernando Rey de España, where 13 persons of both sexes have so far been baptized; the Guardia-Almacén is finished, 2 houses begun, & the missionaries hold the former house of Reyes & other dwellings

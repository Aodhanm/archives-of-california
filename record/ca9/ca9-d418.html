Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 418

*Abono á herreros / Rl resolución / Indulto á desertores / Media firma* (4 ofs) — to credit blacksmiths Faustino Arriola & José Arroyo their pay from 16 March; will instruct subordinates on the R. resolution re the *expediente* of María Rosique, food-vendor; noted S.M. grants indulto to deserters of the naval guild (gremio de la Marina); & that Don Juan Manuel Álvarez may sign with surname only except on documents bearing the royal signature

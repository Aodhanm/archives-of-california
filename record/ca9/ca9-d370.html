Medidas contra una invasión* — Monterey's defense plan against invasion

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 370

*Medidas contra una invasión* — Monterey's defense plan against invasion: troops supplied with shotguns, swords, lances & munitions; the nearby Mission, Real de Caballada & R.Hac. Rancho ordered that at the first cannon-shot the rally point — at full gallop — is the Presidio; bastimentos to be disabled so they can't shelter the enemy

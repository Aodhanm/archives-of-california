*Remisión de armamento* — the Viceroy ordered the armament shipped on the *Concepción* distributed among the Invalids & pueblo vecinos; sent the Pueblo 9 shotguns, 16 cartridge-boxes, 2 lead plates, a mold, paper for a cartridge reserve & 300 flints, ordered weekly arms review + one target round at presidio & mission escoltas

Reglamento — administración de aguardiente* — holds the R.O

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 188

*Reglamento — administración de aguardiente* — holds the R.O. of 20 Dec lifting the prohibition on cane brandy (*aguardiente de caña*) + its administration *Reglamento*; thinks the 2–3 southern Baja cane-growers (and one wanting a cane-mill/still) fall outside it, Baja being 'realmente un país libre' (no *alcabala*, exempt for 10 years)

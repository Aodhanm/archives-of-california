*Precauciones contra un ataque* — on the gentile rumors of attacking San José, sent Sgt. Amador + 3 soldiers; if a sortie were needed to break up the gentile *sediciones*, to ask cabo Vallejo for 6 of the best-armed men and mount the sortie with 10 men, using his experience's precautions

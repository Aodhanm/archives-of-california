*Pelea* — Fr. Mariano Yoldi reports that at the Mission San Miguel rancho two vaqueros & soldier Ignacio Acevedo were wounded; sent the alférez + cabo + 4 soldiers to seize the aggressors, chief being the indio Luis, one of the two 'most perverse' of that mission, who'd been kept here shackled (*mancornado*) at labor

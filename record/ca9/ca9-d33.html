Prevenciones al Cabo sobre los trabajos y el servicio*

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 33

*Prevenciones al Cabo sobre los trabajos y el servicio* — leaves the corporal the stockade plaza marked out (70 varas long, 60 at the narrowest); troop to share the mission works equally; 3 of the Padres' rooms fenced & today began plastering, to be roofed so the Padres can live there; on timber-hauling & felling the cabo is instructed; soldiers never to go without their arms; pass arms-&-cartridge review every 8 days

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 44

*Sobre unos almacenes y cierto cargo* — will hand over the *Habilitación* once the case of the imprisoned Indians & Christian fugitives is done; the warehouses are fire-prone (*propensas á un incendio*) & need rebuilding; disputes charging only his company for the late Raymundo Carrillo's deficit (artillery & volunteer detachments also draw rations); the 1791 *descubierto* of Ramón Laso (now retired at 200 p/yr) should be reintegrated by Laso

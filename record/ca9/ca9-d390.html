Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 390

*Bando sobre aguardiente / Baja de revista / Economía en las distribuciones* (3 ofs) — received Viceroy Branciforte's bando re cane-*aguardiente* made before the lifting of its ban being sold; will strike carpenter Salvador Béjar from the muster once he enlists as soldier in the S. Diego company; ordered to economize stores in the Almacén & the year's Memorias in case no ships bring the '98 Memorias, given the war with England

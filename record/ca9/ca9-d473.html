*Permuta / Bulas & Cuentas de R. Hac. / Avisos de buques / Nombramiento de Vicario / dependientes de Marina* (5 ofs) — Invalid cédula for Francisco García (to enter at Sta Bárbara); per Branciforte the Monterey Habilitado administers the Ramo de Bulas & R. Hac. accounts for all presidios, Grajera rendering his to treasurer José María Bucheli; prompt warnings of ships to prevent landings; the Bishop of Sonora named Fr. Fermín Francisco de Lasuén his vicario foráneo; naval dependents arriving at these ports to be under the Viceroy's orders

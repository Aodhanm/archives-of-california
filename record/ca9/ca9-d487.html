*Nombramiento de Virey / Concesión eclesiástica / Provisiones a una fragata / Mermas de ciertos efectos* (4 ofs) — noted Azanza is the new Viceroy; Bishop Francisco Rouset's concession to Lasuén & friars administering Sacraments to the military; requested 500 arrobas of flour from Mission San Diego for the frigate Princesa; sends (signed by 1st pilot Salvador Menéndez) the document justifying the shortfalls (mermas) of the San Blas goods received in 1793

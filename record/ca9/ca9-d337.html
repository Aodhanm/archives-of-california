Investigación de lo mismo* — no ship seen elsewhere within 30 leagues

Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 337

*Investigación de lo mismo* — no ship seen elsewhere within 30 leagues; re-examined the two original spotters — an 'Indio Castellano' saw 17 sails, the San Fernando mayordomo 14, seemingly bound for Ensenada de Todos Santos — but the Todos Santos cabo saw nothing *(the alarm largely deflated — likely a false alarm)*

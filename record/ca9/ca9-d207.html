*(five oficios)* — (a) soldier Ignacio M. Frasviña's absolute discharge (on replacement); (b) the mail was at sea for San Antonio de la Huerta within 24 h; (c) received the Sargento appointment for cabo José Joaquín Amador; (d) ⭐ the R.O. to detain by reprisal all English ships in our ports, later adjusted to the formal war declaration; (e) will list the attached Invalids' pay in the muster extracts

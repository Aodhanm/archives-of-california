*Enojo de unos PP / Enfermedad de un soldado* — the Padres asked for soldiers to fetch cattle; when he said he couldn't decide, they got angry; what the Gov. ordered is all done, but the Padres want soldiers 'trabajen mas y como si fueran gananes' (farm-hands); Salvador Higuera asks relief from the horse-herd (hip ailment) and his discharge

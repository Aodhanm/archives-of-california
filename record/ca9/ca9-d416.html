Provincial State Papers, tomo XVI · BANC MSS C-A 9, document 416

*Muerte á un Indio cristiano* — the Padre sent Christian Indians to fetch a Gentile woman to marry a Christian who had been her husband in heathendom; returning with her, they passed a ranchería where an old Gentile with two sons killed Toribio (one of the party) on suspicion he had bewitched/poisoned (enyerbado) his Christian daughter who died at this mission; Briones set out today with 3 soldiers & Christian Indians to seize the malefactors

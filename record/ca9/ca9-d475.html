*Declaración de Guerra / Precauciones contra Hostilidades* (2 ofs) — noted the Declaration of War against the English; should they attack he will keep the battery in best state, reinforce the garrison, maintain continuous coastal patrols (descubiertas), & train the volunteer & leather-jacket artillerymen in the cannon & their arms, per B.'s order of the 13th

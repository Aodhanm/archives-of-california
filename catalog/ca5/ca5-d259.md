# Printed cédula ordering observance in the Indies of the papal brief on meat. Verbatim: 'se permite comer carnes los Sába

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n251** · Sav p. 250/248 · tomo p. p.463-66 · 1790-12-20 · Savage abstract · 

Printed cédula ordering observance in the Indies of the papal brief on meat. Verbatim: 'se permite comer carnes los Sábados que no sean de quaresma, ó precepto Eclesiastico de ayuno.' Savage records the full California bando chain: 'Mandado publicar por Romeu en Monterey 2 de Enero de 1792'; by Hermenegildo Sal at San Francisco 26 Feb 1792; by José Argüello at Monterey 29 July 1792; by Felipe de Goycoechea at Santa Bárbara 8 Aug 1792; by Juan Pablo Grijalva at San Diego 14 Aug 1792; by Arrillaga at Loreto 14 Oct 1792.

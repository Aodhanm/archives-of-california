# Adultery/scandal case at the pueblo: 'El Poblador Franco. Abila, a quien no han bastado amonestaciones, ni amenasas para

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n218–n219** · Sav p. 217/215 · tomo p. pp. 373-74 · 1790-04-19 · Savage abstract · 

Adultery/scandal case at the pueblo: 'El Poblador Franco. Abila, a quien no han bastado amonestaciones, ni amenasas para evitarle la mala amistad con la mujer de Alegre'; while at the pueblo guardia Argüello sent 'al sargento Amador, y al regidor Claudio' to see whether Abila was in the field with Alegre and his wife — found the three in conversation at his guardia, so he jailed them. Earlier 'se pelearon las mujeres de estos porcelos, qe. tubo la de Abila, a quien mordió un dedo la de Alegre,' leaving Abila's wife gravely ill. Abila remitted in irons to the presidio with the Indian prisoners; Alegre's wife 'queda desposita en casa de Ramon Bojorques' pending transfer to the pueblo guardia. Spans n218-n219.

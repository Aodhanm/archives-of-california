# Arrillaga names and swears the two attesting witnesses required in the absence of a notary: 'nombro por testigos de assi

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n22** · Sav p. 22/21 · tomo p. — · 1789-08-06 · Savage abstract · 

Arrillaga names and swears the two attesting witnesses required in the absence of a notary: 'nombro por testigos de assistencia à Juan Nepamuceno de Orbiña y José Migl. de Irive à quienes se hara compareser ante mi para que Juramentados en firma, ofrescan guardar y cumplir fidelidad, y secreto en el asunto de que trata'. Both appear the same day, swear 'en forma de derecho', accept the appointment and sign with him. Useful roster evidence for the Loreto garrison's literate personnel. Spans n22–n23.

# Acuse recibo of the borderol of his alcances, 514 pesos 1 real for 1788, received 'por el conducto del Ten.te D.n Jose d

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n132–n134** · Sav p. 131/129 (doc runs to 133/131) · tomo p. p.231-32-33 · 1789-06-17 · verbatim transcription · 

Acuse recibo of the borderol of his alcances, 514 pesos 1 real for 1788, received 'por el conducto del Ten.te D.n Jose de Zuñiga'. Disputes the ajuste: it runs only 1 July 1787 to Dec 1788, omits the first semester of 1787 (debit 243 p 1 r 8 gr); he left that presidio 22 March 1787 with 418 p drawn against pay, so 'me parese no devia de resulta mas devito, que 143 p.s'; queries a charge of '4 p.s 5 r.l 6 gr.s de los tres vagajes de carga, que vajé hasta la Mision de San Antonio d.o la de el Carmello'; his postage payments are unallowed. Spans n132-n134.

# Forwards the muster extracts and effective-strength returns for Nov. and the current Dec., with certification of Cabo Ma

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n225** · Sav p. 224/222 · tomo p. p.385 · 1790-12-05 · Savage abstract · 

Forwards the muster extracts and effective-strength returns for Nov. and the current Dec., with certification of Cabo Marcos Chaboya's discharge and his replacement by Soldado Manl. Boronda. Four private files stay vacant: 'quedando cuatro plasas sencillas vacantes por no haber encontrado en ese distrito Individuos que las ocupen' (no men in the district to fill them). 'No ocurre novedad en dho. Presidio, ni en las Escoltas.'

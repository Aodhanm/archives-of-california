# Arrillaga orders notification of the defensor Estrada to produce the witnesses offered in his petition of 3 August, and 

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n23** · Sav p. 23/22 · tomo p. — · 1789-08-06 · Savage abstract · 

Arrillaga orders notification of the defensor Estrada to produce the witnesses offered in his petition of 3 August, and citation of the apoderado del Real Fisco Rosales 'à efecto de que los vea jurar, y ejecutado, se examinen dhos testigos al tenor del interrogatorio que incluye el Superior Despacho del Exmo. y Señor Virrey de N.E.' Both are notified the same day and 'dijeron estan prontos a cumplir con lo mandado', signing with Arrillaga and the testigos de asistencia Urbiña and Irive. Spans n23–n24.

# On the claim of soldier Ignacio Higuera against his ajuste: 'diciendome este de que no estar satisfecho, de su ajuste po

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n105** · Sav p. 104/102 · tomo p. p.167-168 · 1789-12-29 · Savage abstract · 

On the claim of soldier Ignacio Higuera against his ajuste: 'diciendome este de que no estar satisfecho, de su ajuste por su libreta que le entregué'; G. asked him for a written representación and still waits; 'Se que dho Higuera ha perdido la Libretta'. Higuera served long at Loreto unpaid; a mule he left 'en las fronteras de Californias' first judged useless is now allowed at 15 p.; alcance 264 p., debt charge c.160, leaving c.100. Ends with a reconvención owed by Soldado Tomás Espinosa.

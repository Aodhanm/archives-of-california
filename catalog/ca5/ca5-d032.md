# «Participa que Fr. Oramas se fué á la sierra á confesar á un individuo, sin contenerle las obligaciones con aquella esco

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n87** · Sav p. 85/83 · tomo p. p.115-116 · 1789-08-27 · Savage abstract · 

«Participa que Fr. Oramas se fué á la sierra á confesar á un individuo, sin contenerle las obligaciones con aquella escolta ni sus suplicas, y consulta que hara si los viages se repiten.» Goycoechea complains that Fr. Oramas went up into the sierra to confess an individual, undeterred by the obligations imposed on the escort or by Goycoechea's entreaties, and asks what he should do if such journeys are repeated — friar-vs-comandante friction over escort duty and unauthorised travel into gentile country.

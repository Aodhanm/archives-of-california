# Acknowledges the superior order of 7 February directing a sortie into the sierra after the 'Capitanejos' now held prison

**C-A 5 (Provincial State Papers), scan n240** · Sav p. 239/237 · tomo p. p.425-26 · 1790-01-12 · Savage abstract · 

Acknowledges the superior order of 7 February directing a sortie into the sierra after the 'Capitanejos' now held prisoner at Mission Santo Domingo. Verbatim: 'Dice que recibió la superior órden de 7 de Febrero, en que se sirve decir la salida à la sierra, en busca de los Capitanejos, presos en la Mision de Santo Domingo.' Gloss: the San Vicente punitive expedition against the Baja capitanejos; the named ringleaders were already jailed at Santo Domingo.

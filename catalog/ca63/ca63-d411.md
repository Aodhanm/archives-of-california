# Doc N.o 2 of 3 in the Cot packet; spans n281-n282. Savage: 'Doct. N.o 2: - A Memorial to Com. Stockton setting forth the

**C-A 63 (Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents), scan n281** · Sav p. 279 [stamp] / 278 [hand] · tomo p. — · 1846-08-22 · Savage abstract · 

Doc N.o 2 of 3 in the Cot packet; spans n281-n282. Savage: 'Doct. N.o 2: - A Memorial to Com. Stockton setting forth the fact of the above purchase and that he had been forcibly dispossessed by Lieut. Col. Fremont on his way from S. Diego to los Ang.s in Aug.t 1846 and claiming to be again put in possession thereof pledging himself to abide by the decision of the proper tribunals, as to the validity of title &.' Then Savage's own underlined editorial head 'Remarks.': 'Object evidently the onus probandi upon Gov.t wich would have been the result had his request been granted - Date Aug.t 22.nd /846 - signed for Ant.o Cot.'

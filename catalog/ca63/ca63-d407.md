# Savage's head: '1849. - July 23.d - Sta. Clara. W. Campbell & Jas. Alex. Forbes Esq. — to Gov.r Riley.' Abstract, in Sav

**C-A 63 (Miscellaneous / Unbound Documents), scan n279** · Sav p. 277 [stamp] / 276 [hand] · tomo p. — · 1849-07-23 · Savage abstract · 

Savage's head: '1849. - July 23.d - Sta. Clara. W. Campbell & Jas. Alex. Forbes Esq. — to Gov.r Riley.' Abstract, in Savage's English: 'Representation, that say: it has become notoriously public in the Mission of Sta. Clara and its vicinity: that the Missionary Priest of the same, with or without the cooperation of his spiritual Superior the Rev. Father Ansar [= Fr. José Antonio Anzar, San Juan Bautista?] has alienated or conveyed, all the landed property of the Missions of San Joseph, Sta. Clara, Sta. Cruz and San John.' [Note the sweep: ALL the landed property of four missions.]

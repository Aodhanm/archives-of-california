# Anglo-American timber-ship order. Transcribes a Viceregal decree re an Anglo-American frigate (capt. 'Robert[?]') arrive

**C-A 17 (Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n337** · Sav p. 338 · 1820-01-20 · Savage abstract · 

Anglo-American timber-ship order. Transcribes a Viceregal decree re an Anglo-American frigate (capt. 'Robert[?]') arrived at San Blas from Guayaquil intending to load California timber for the San Blas apostadero; warns the captain that otherwise his ship & cargo would be confiscated if he passed to these [Calif.] ports. Orig. p.370.

**C-A 53 (State Papers, Missions and Colonization), scan n158** · Sav p. leaf ink corner 248 · tomo p. p.leaf printed stamp 160; document's own cite pp. 356-7 (given at the close on n159) · not stated on the leaf; Savage explicitly heads it 'Sin fecha ni direccion / Sin firma' · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n148-n162, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o Sin fecha ni direccion / Sin firma / Adiciones que presenta la Comisión de arbitrios.' [= Undated and unsigned additions submitted by the arbitrios commission, including the ordinance's proposed title] Art. 1 gives the proposed title: 'Plan de propios y arbitrios para fondos municipales de los Ayuntamientos del Territorio de la Alta Califª'. Art. 2: the Ayuntamientos are to apply through the ordinary channels for ejidos and propios lands to be assigned to each pueblo. Art. 3: otter and beaver hunting contractors pay 4 reales per skin of a vara or more. Art. 4 (on n159): the tonnage dues on foreign vessels ceded to the States by art. 4 of the maritime customs tariff of 16 Nov 1827 to be requested from the Supreme Government for the Territory as propios, the proceeds to enter the Deputation's treasury. Art. 5: a voluntary contribution from all national and foreign vessels anchoring in Monterey bay for the sole object of building a wharf, collected and held by the Captain of the Port and ceasing when the work is finished. Art. 6: the plan to be reported to the Supreme Government with a request for approval by the General Congress. Art. 7: given the urgency of funds, and under attribution 4 of art. 335 of the constitution then in force and with the Jefe Político's prior assent, the arbitrios agreed in articles 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11 and 12 are to be used.

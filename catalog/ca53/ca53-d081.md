# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n14

**C-A 53 (State Papers, Missions and Colonization), scan n154** · Sav p. 156 [stamp] / 244 [ink] · tomo p. pp. 352-3 · 1834-07-24 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n148-n162, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1834 Julio 24 Monterey / José A. Carrillo, Frano de Haro José A. Estudillo' [= Commission's dictamen on the Jefe Político's landing-duty proposition, with further revenue prevenciones] Reports that in the session of the 15th the Jefe Político's proposition was passed to the commission; the commission finds it just and proper to the Territory and reports in four articles, adding other prevenciones it thinks useful to the funds. Art. 1 sets medio real per bulto for national vessels and one real for foreign vessels at Monterey, San Francisco, Santa Barbara, San Pedro and San Diego. Art. 2 leaves the branding-iron register provisionally with the Ayuntamientos, the alcaldes to charge two reales per registration to the municipal fund. Art. 3 charges every individual placed in the carcel by competent authority 12 reales in silver on release. Art. 4 fixes the liquor duty at 3 pesos per barrel of aguardiente, 12 reales per barrel of wine and 2 pesos per barrel of angélica.

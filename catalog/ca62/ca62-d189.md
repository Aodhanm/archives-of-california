# Orders him to march at once on the Pueblo de Todos Santos and bring in the alcalde D. Franco Cota 'amarrado … Codo con C

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n204** · Sav p. 182 [stamp] / 21 [hand] · tomo p. p. 214 · 1848-01-19 · Savage abstract · 

Orders him to march at once on the Pueblo de Todos Santos and bring in the alcalde D. Franco Cota 'amarrado … Codo con Codo, pié á tierra para mayor seguridad de su persona' [bound elbow to elbow and on foot, for greater security of his person], the administration of justice to pass to whomever the law prescribes.

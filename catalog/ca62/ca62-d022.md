# NEVE'S 1782 FOUNDING INSTRUCTION for the Santa Bárbara presidio + the 3 Channel missions (SB / San Buenaventura / La Pur

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n21–n41** · Sav p. 22–42 · tomo p. p.551–574 · 1782-03-06 (San Gabriel; SB-copy hdr «18 May 1782, Ortega, Sta Bárbara») · verbatim transcription · 

NEVE'S 1782 FOUNDING INSTRUCTION for the Santa Bárbara presidio + the 3 Channel missions (SB / San Buenaventura / La Purísima), signed San Gabriel 6 Mar 178[2], Felipe de Neve: Chumash-relations doctrine (win the *capitanejos* w/ gifts/interpreters, «nuestro corto número… nunca tan respetable como la muchedumbre», the admirable *canoas*/tomols, gradual reducción); build palisade→walls/bastions→storehouse→chapel; ⭐ graduated Indian-punishment schedule (15 azotes wounding / 25 killing a mula-caballo, «que sirva de ejemplo», proceed by simple información); arms-control (don't sell blade-pieces to the gentiles); SD↔Monterey mail-relay; supply logistics (San Blas seeds, «cosechas del pueblo de la Reina de los Ángeles»)

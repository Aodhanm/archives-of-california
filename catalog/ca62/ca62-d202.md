# States that he cannot obey the march order so soon 'por falta de recursos pecuniarios, y solo podrá mantenerlos en la po

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n209** · Sav p. 187 [stamp] / 27 [hand] · tomo p. p 263 · 1847-10-04 · Savage abstract · 

States that he cannot obey the march order so soon 'por falta de recursos pecuniarios, y solo podrá mantenerlos en la poblacion' [for want of money; he can only maintain them in the town]. Earliest document in this stretch (Oct 1847).

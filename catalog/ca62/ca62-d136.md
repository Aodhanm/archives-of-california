# Circular requisition of horses: the Mexican force of 120 men must march on the Puerto de la Paz, so within four days 'te

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n169** · Sav p. 148 [stamp] / 22 [hand] · tomo p. p p. 154 — 56. · 1847 Novre 19 · Savage abstract · 

Circular requisition of horses: the Mexican force of 120 men must march on the Puerto de la Paz, so within four days 'tendrán lista toda la Caballada de su jurisdicion, pa servicio de la Nación, en inteligencia que Cualesquiera falta que halla seran responsables' [every jurisdiction to hold its whole horse-herd ready for the Nation's service, defaulters answerable]. Savage adds 'Se procedió á Cumplir inmediatamte'.

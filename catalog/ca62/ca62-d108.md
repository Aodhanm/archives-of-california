# Campaign orders. Says his Comandancia General will punish the enemy as a good Mexican should: 'que a las 6 de la manana 

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n151** · Sav p. 130 [stamp] / 4 [hand] · tomo p. p 50 · 1848-01-21 · Savage abstract · 

Campaign orders. Says his Comandancia General will punish the enemy as a good Mexican should: 'que a las 6 de la manana marcharan 40 infantes, para el Pueblo de Todos Santos a las ordenes del Alferez ayudante y Comandante de la Compa de Loreto Manuel Calderon' [40 infantry march at 6 a.m. for Todos Santos under Alferez Manuel Calderon, to keep public order and guard the powder being manufactured]. Has recalled Alf. Aguilar to try to organise a force of at least 40 men and send it to S. Hilario, al sondeadero, to escort incoming auxilios.

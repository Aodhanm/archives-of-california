# Answering a communication of the 21st. Vicente Mejía 'ha seducido á la tropa de Mulege, que desertará con todo y armamen

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n199** · Sav p. 177 [stamp] / 16 [hand] · tomo p. p 180 – 2 · s.f. [1847, late Dec] · Savage abstract · 

Answering a communication of the 21st. Vicente Mejía 'ha seducido á la tropa de Mulege, que desertará con todo y armamento, y demas municiones' [seduced the Mulegé troop into deserting with arms and munitions], he being pledged to the Territory's defence with the Comandancia general de Sonora, all 'por conseguir ser Gefe Politico y Comandte pral'; foreseeing many actions of war he resigned to retire home. Mulegé cavalry has arrived and moves to the Pueblo de Santiago for orders; powder to be forwarded, arms collected in the ranchos; 100 infantry are marching from Sonora.

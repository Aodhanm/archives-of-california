# C. Antº Martínez came in to present himself to that court and, interrogated on the enemy's movement, said they occupied 

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n208** · Sav p. 186 [stamp] / 26 [hand] · tomo p. p p. 259 – 60 · 1847-11-27 · Savage abstract · 

C. Antº Martínez came in to present himself to that court and, interrogated on the enemy's movement, said they occupied the Casa Cural and that 'les habian agregado los siguientes traidores Californios: José Ygº Duarte, Rosario Duarte, Santa Maria Duarte, Ecsaltacion Canseco, Cruz Duarte, Pedro Ojeda y Jacinto Perez' [the following Californio traitors had joined them].

# Undated entry marked with Savage's '+' under the same correspondent heading. 'Remite el Correo que se ha tomado al enemi

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n209** · Sav p. 187 [stamp] / 27 [hand] · tomo p. p 266 – · s.f. [1847, after Nov 19] · Savage abstract · 

Undated entry marked with Savage's '+' under the same correspondent heading. 'Remite el Correo que se ha tomado al enemigo con las Comunicaciones traducidas, por las que verá sus temores'; as for the captured courier, 'esta bueno en castigo, filiarlo para que sirva en la tropa veterana' [it would be good punishment to enlist him in the veteran troop].

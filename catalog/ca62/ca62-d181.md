# He may dispose of the cavalry at Santiago (from San José) and that of Comondú 'para hostilizar al enemigo que está en S 

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n200** · Sav p. 178 [stamp] / 17 [hand] · tomo p. p. p 183 – 190 · 1847-12-27 · Savage abstract · 

He may dispose of the cavalry at Santiago (from San José) and that of Comondú 'para hostilizar al enemigo que está en S José'; no powder sent as none is yet made. 100 infantry and 2 guns are coming from the Comandante gral de Sonora. Reports that 'en Mazattan han sido derrotados los Americanos' and that a ship loaded with wounded lies in the Puerto de la Paz. Yesterday he had the paisano José Uribe shot as a salteador and deserter who had volunteered then deserted, 'para escarmiento de los desertores que se llevan las armas'.

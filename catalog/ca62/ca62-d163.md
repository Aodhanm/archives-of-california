# 1o of '(2 Docs.)'. Acknowledges Pineda's note transmitting that of the Gefe Superior Politico, 'noticiandole la prision 

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n189** · Sav p. 167 [stamp] / 6 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 112 - 13. · 1848 Enero 25 · Savage abstract · 

1o of '(2 Docs.)'. Acknowledges Pineda's note transmitting that of the Gefe Superior Politico, 'noticiandole la prision de 5 norte Americanos y un deshaturalizado Mejicano; solemnizandose dhas. noticias con repiques de Campanas y vivas por la tropa á su mando' [= notifying him of the capture of 5 North Americans and one denaturalized Mexican, the news solemnized with peals of bells and cheers from the troops under his command].

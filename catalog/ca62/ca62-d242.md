# Second dated entry under the same heading. «Le remite una Proclama que la Junta Territorial ha ordenado se publique en t

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n227** · Sav p. 205 [stamp] / 45 [hand] · tomo p. pp 416 · 1847-11-21 · Savage abstract · 

Second dated entry under the same heading. «Le remite una Proclama que la Junta Territorial ha ordenado se publique en todos los Pueblos de la Baja Califa que no esten ocupados por los N[or]te Americanos» = forwards a proclamation the Junta ordered published in every Lower California pueblo not occupied by the North Americans - a direct statement that part of the peninsula was under US occupation.

# 'En igual fha se ordenó no se tocará de ningun modo los intereses del Americano Mr Pedro Davis' [= on the same date it w

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n163** · Sav p. 142 [stamp] / 16 [hand] · tomo p. p 109. · 1848 Mayo 7 [inferred from 'en igual fha'] · Savage abstract · 

'En igual fha se ordenó no se tocará de ningun modo los intereses del Americano Mr Pedro Davis' [= on the same date it was ordered that the property of the American Mr Pedro Davis be in no way touched.] Follows the abstract ending on this leaf, which had asked that Davis's family be spared 'por que no deben pagar estos indivíduos los Crímenes de él' [they should not pay for his crimes].

# spans n158-n159. Treason and the Santiago revolt. Acknowledges the letter of the 23rd: 'queda enterado del Crimen de tra

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n158** · Sav p. 137 [stamp] / 11 [hand] · tomo p. p 74 - 5 · 1848-01-26 · Savage abstract · 

spans n158-n159. Treason and the Santiago revolt. Acknowledges the letter of the 23rd: 'queda enterado del Crimen de traidor de Ecsaltacion Canceco [Canseco?] que se hizo prisionero Con las armas en la mano en la fila del enemigo' [taken prisoner arms in hand in the enemy ranks], a crime to be punished at once. Alferez J. M. Aguilar was to occupy Santiago with a piquete; the ringleaders there have always adhered to the enemy and 'el Oro del enemigo anda Con mucha actividad, por lograr el aumento de sus filas' [enemy gold is busy swelling their ranks]. Alf. Calderon cannot be moved from Todos Santos with his 45 infantry. 50 infantry of the Guardia Nacional del Sur under Capn Fernandez march at 6 a.m. for La Paz. Names the traitors Leonardo Araiza, Antonio Ruiz and Maurino Algarin.

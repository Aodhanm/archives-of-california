# Urgent order to march with the force of his command — Savage notes the abstract 'no dice pa qe punto sea la marcha' — 's

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n203** · Sav p. 181 [stamp] / 20 [hand] · tomo p. p 202 · 1847-12-28 · Savage abstract · 

Urgent order to march with the force of his command — Savage notes the abstract 'no dice pa qe punto sea la marcha' — 'sin perdida de 1 minuto marche, dejando 5, ó 6 hombres, para que lleven las mantas' [without losing a minute, leaving 5 or 6 men to carry the blankets].

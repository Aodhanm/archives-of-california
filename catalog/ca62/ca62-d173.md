# The Loreto ayuntamiento being disposed 'á la defensa de la Patria', it is to summon the inhabitants of its jurisdiction 

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n195** · Sav p. 173 [stamp] / 12 [hand] · tomo p. p. 151 - 52. · 1847 Set. 27 · Savage abstract · 

The Loreto ayuntamiento being disposed 'á la defensa de la Patria', it is to summon the inhabitants of its jurisdiction and read them the enclosed proclamation, 'con los nombram.tos del 1o. y 2o. Comand.tes Juan Vargas y Manuel Aguiar, á quienes tomaria desde luego el juram.to de ley p.a entrar á ejercer su encargo' [= together with the commissions of the 1st and 2nd commanders Juan Vargas and Manuel Aguiar, who are to be sworn in at once to take up their charge].

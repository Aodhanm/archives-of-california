# Pineda asks the guerrilla commander to certify his reception: 'Le dice al Comte si al tiempo que se presento en el Puebl

**C-A 62 (Archives of California, Miscellaneous), scan n153** · Sav p. 132 [stamp] / 6 [hand] · tomo p. p 57. · 1847-??-12 [Savage: '(No dice el mes)'] · Savage abstract · 

Pineda asks the guerrilla commander to certify his reception: 'Le dice al Comte si al tiempo que se presento en el Pueblo, se le recibio Como Comandte Principal de la Baja Califa, y Con el entusiasmo que ese pueblo ha manifestado al Supo Gobo' [whether, when he presented himself in the pueblo, he was received as Principal Comandante of Baja California, and with the enthusiasm that town has shown to the supreme government]. Savage explicitly notes the month is not given in the original; day = 12.

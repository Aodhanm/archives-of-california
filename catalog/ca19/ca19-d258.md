# Verbatim heading: '1833 Dic. 30 Sn. Franco / Mariano G. Vallejo, Comisario del puerto - Compa. presidial.' Gloss: year-e

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n419** · Sav p. 414 · tomo p. pp. 416-28 · 1833-12-30 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1833 Dic. 30 Sn. Franco / Mariano G. Vallejo, Comisario del puerto - Compa. presidial.' Gloss: year-end account of the San Francisco presidial company drawn by Vallejo in his capacity as comisario of the port. Substance: 1o Alferez, the said Vallejo, monthly pay 50 pesos; 2o Damaso A. Rodriguez 41.5.3; Sargtos. Pablo Pacheco y Cayetano Juarez, 30 each; 2 clarines 15 each; 4 cabos 25 each; 30 soldados 20 each; gratificn. mensual de la Compa. 41.5.4. Savage adds: 'Hubo meses de 31 solds. y otros de 32', and that the presupuestos are incomplete but the year haberes and gratificacion of the company can be calculated at about 11,000 pesos. The final figure is written with three closed slanted loops matching Savage zero form elsewhere on the leaf (compare 50 and 4703), so I read it as 11,000; the monthly total implied by the pay scale (about 943 pesos) supports a rounded 11,000. Cite at foot: pp 416-28. [corner: Savage tomo LXXVI p. 30]

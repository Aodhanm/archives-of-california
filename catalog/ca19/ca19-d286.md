# Verbatim heading: '830 - Eno 19 = S. Migel / (Solis)'. Verbatim text begins on n208: 'Orden pa qe Peguero recoja el plom

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n208** · Sav p. 203 / 77 · tomo p. — · 1830-01-19 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '830 - Eno 19 = S. Migel / (Solis)'. Verbatim text begins on n208: 'Orden pa qe Peguero recoja el plomo - qe halla' and completes on the first two lines of n209 (printed corner 204): 'en esa Misn (Soledad) sin qe se le ponga pretesto alguno.' Gloss: order that Peguero collect the lead to be found at that mission (Soledad), no pretext to be raised against him. The heading falls inside the gap; only the tail spills into the already-catalogued n209-n236 run, which itemises nothing. [orig cite: autos; no p. cite written on the leaf]

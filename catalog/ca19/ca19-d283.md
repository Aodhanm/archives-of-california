# Verbatim heading: '1829 = Nbre 28 = Misn de Dolores. / Solis al cabo Joaqn Pina'. Verbatim text: 'Orden pa qe tome polvo

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n208** · Sav p. 203 / 77 · tomo p. — · 1829-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1829 = Nbre 28 = Misn de Dolores. / Solis al cabo Joaqn Pina'. Verbatim text: 'Orden pa qe tome polvora y se ejecuten tres descargas de artilla; una cuando entre pr la puerta prial, otra cuando se apruebe el Plan y la otra cuando las Comps se retiren a sus destinos.' Gloss: Solis orders the corporal to draw powder and fire three artillery salutes, one as he enters by the main gate, a second when the Plan is approved, and a third when the companies withdraw to their posts. IMPORTANT CROSS-LINK: this appears to be the underlying order behind the causa already catalogued at n78-n85 in this volume, 'Sublevacion de Solis, the Cabo Joaquin Pina salute causa' (opened March 1830).

# Verbatim heading: '1833. Dic. 31. Mont / Jose Ma. Herrera, Sub-Comiso. - Compa. del escuadron activo de Mazatlan.' Gloss

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n418** · Sav p. 413 · tomo p. pp. 390-404 · 1833-12-31 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1833. Dic. 31. Mont / Jose Ma. Herrera, Sub-Comiso. - Compa. del escuadron activo de Mazatlan.' Gloss: year-end pay account of the Mazatlan active squadron. Substance: Capn. Pablo de la Portilla, monthly pay 100 pesos; Tente. Juan Ma. Ibarra 54.3.9; id. retirado Narciso Fabregat 15.7.11 (antes de retirarse 51.3.3); 3 sargtos., monthly haber each 22.4; 2 cabos 18.4; 31 soldados 17.4; gratificacion mensual de la compa. 8.2. Total de haberes y c.a de la Compa. este ano 10,175 pesos. Fabregat retirement began 29 July, and he was paid the salary of his effective post through the 28th inclusive. Cite at foot: pp 390-404. NOTE: distinct from the published n374-n375 row (2 Dec 1833, orig pp. 169-231) which covers the same unit under a different date and page cite. [corner: Savage tomo LXXVI p. 29]

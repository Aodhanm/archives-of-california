# Verbatim heading: '1834. Abl 1. Sta Barbª / Anastº Carrillo - Compª presidial.' Gloss: same officer, the March and April

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n458** · Sav p. 453 · tomo p. pp. 243 & 246, 305 · 1834-04-01 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1834. Abl 1. Sta Barbª / Anastº Carrillo - Compª presidial.' Gloss: same officer, the March and April 1834 pay of the Santa Bárbara company. Substance: 'Haberes de la Compª activa en Marzo $989.-; id id id en Abril 989.- = $1978.-' with Savage parentheses noting how the inválidos appear in July and August and in June.

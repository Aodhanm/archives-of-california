# Verbatim heading: '1829 - Eno 19 (sic) = / Solis'. Verbatim text: 'Pasaporte pa Peguero y 9 hombres qe lo acompanan a Mo

**C-A 19 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n208** · Sav p. 203 / 77 · tomo p. — · 1829-01-19 [so dated in the original; Savage writes '(sic)' against it, i.e. 1830 is intended] · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1829 - Eno 19 (sic) = / Solis'. Verbatim text: 'Pasaporte pa Peguero y 9 hombres qe lo acompanan a Mont.' Gloss: passport for Peguero and the nine men accompanying him to Monterey. The '(sic)' is Savage own flag on the year: the entry that follows it on the same leaf is dated 830 Eno 19, so the original document carried 1829 where January 1830 was meant.

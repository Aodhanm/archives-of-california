# Castro proclamation body (verbatim): laments the armed disturbance 'en el nombre del vecindario del pueblo de Sta Bárbar

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n397** · Sav p. 398 · tomo p. p.613[?] (T.86) · (1845 Dec 15 Castro proclamation, cont.) · verbatim transcription · 

Castro proclamation body (verbatim): laments the armed disturbance 'en el nombre del vecindario del pueblo de Sta Bárbara'; urges inhabitants to restore 'la paz y el sosiego público,' invokes obedience to the Gobernador, calls for union — political address amid the Pico–Castro divided governance of late 1845

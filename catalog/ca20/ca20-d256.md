# (1) tail of 'Gastos secretos': by Ministerial order 400 pesos delivered to Mariano Chico, Gefe Político de Alta Cal., fo

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n221** · Sav p. 222 · tomo p. pp.348–9 · pp.364–6 · (3rd doc begins) · (cont.) · 21 Mar 1836 Arizpe · 21 Mar 1836 Arizpe · Savage abstract · 

(1) tail of 'Gastos secretos': by Ministerial order 400 pesos delivered to Mariano Chico, Gefe Político de Alta Cal., for secret expenses (pp.348–9); (2) Carrillo to Sub-Comisario: asks promptly what has been collected of the extraordinary war-subsidy (law 21 Nov 1835), reportable every 8 days — Savage's note: 'el subsidio que Cal. jamás envió ni un solo peso á México' (pp.364–6); (3) Carrillo to Sub-Comisario, Secretaría de la Comdcia Genl (continues n222).

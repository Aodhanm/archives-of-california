# Certified copy of the Comand.te Gral's resolutions on monthly commissary musters, arts. 1-3: 'En los dias primeros del m

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n447–n448** · Sav p. 448/45 · tomo p. p.54-5 · 1780-09-21 · Savage abstract · 

Certified copy of the Comand.te Gral's resolutions on monthly commissary musters, arts. 1-3: 'En los dias primeros del mes hasta el 7 y 8, se pasarán estas revistas, uniéndose toda la tropa presente de la Comp.ª'; if the caballada would suffer, only the garrison need form. The pié de lista is to note 'los ausentes, empleados, enfermos y presos'. Art. 2 imposes a uniform formulario (no. 1) on all Presidios de la Frontera; art. 3 requires duplicate monthly extracts. Ends 'Son copia de q.e certifico, &c = Bonilla.' Spans n447-n448.

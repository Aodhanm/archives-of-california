# Artillery company muster/return: '2 sarg.tos 2.os, 1 tambor, 8 cabos, 32 artilleros' = 'Total 43 hombres', distributed '

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n485** · Sav p. 486/8 · tomo p. p.66-67 · 1832-11-03 · Savage abstract · 

Artillery company muster/return: '2 sarg.tos 2.os, 1 tambor, 8 cabos, 32 artilleros' = 'Total 43 hombres', distributed '1 sarg.to, 5 cabos, 12 sold.s en Mont. / 5 id en S.n F.co / 2 cabos y 9 id en Sta Barb.a / 1 sarg.to, 1 tambor, 1 cabo, 6 sold.s en San Diego'. 'Revista pasada ante José Joaq.n Gómez, Sub-Comisario provisional de Mont.' [= presidial artillery distributed across all four presidios]. No individual soldiers named beyond Medrano and Gómez.

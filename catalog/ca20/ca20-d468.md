# 'Exist.ª de municiones hasta la fha: 25 @ 14 lb.s pólvora, 6070 cartuchos, 101 @ 13 lb plomo, 3752 piedras.' San Diego h

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n454** · Sav p. 455/52 · tomo p. p.181-2 · 1796-12-31 · Savage abstract · 

'Exist.ª de municiones hasta la fha: 25 @ 14 lb.s pólvora, 6070 cartuchos, 101 @ 13 lb plomo, 3752 piedras.' San Diego holds no cannon shot in this return, only small-arms stores. Signed by a Pablo Grijalba — same name as the 'Sarg.º Juan Pablo Grijalba' of the 1782 San Francisco muster below, but the leaf does not identify them.

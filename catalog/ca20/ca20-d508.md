# Spans n478-n479. 'Sobre individuos sentenciados á presidio en Cal.a'. Carrillo joined the captain of the Mexican brig 'M

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n478–n479** · Sav p. 479/1 · tomo p. p.12-13 · 1830-04-14 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n478-n479. 'Sobre individuos sentenciados á presidio en Cal.a'. Carrillo joined the captain of the Mexican brig 'María Ester' to put the Gefe Político's 13 March questions to the convicts; the captain said it was pointless 'porque todos darían respuestas á su gusto', already proven since some questioned 'en sus respuestas se diéron por sumam.te inocentes'. 'Que el Cap.n alega no poder llevar mas de 11 presos, seg.n convenio verbal con el Gefe Político, entre ellos á Sacramento.' Convicts to stay at Sta Bárbara pending the government's decision; list to follow after landing.

# (1) 'Retiro del Capitán Ignacio Argüello' — transcribes a Ministro de Guerra y Marina office of 15 Oct: captain of the p

**C-A 20 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n210** · Sav p. 211 · tomo p. pp.7–9 · pp.21–3 · 25 Nov 1835 Horcasitas (Sonora) · 25 Nov 1835 Horcasitas · Savage abstract · 

(1) 'Retiro del Capitán Ignacio Argüello' — transcribes a Ministro de Guerra y Marina office of 15 Oct: captain of the presidial company can't come to Cal. to serve as captain (granted 10 Aug 1834), prefers retirement, to enjoy it in Jalisco; despatch of Capt. commission to be returned (pp.7–9); (2) Nicanor Estrada restored to his employ, dismissed 1833, included in amnesty of 2 May 1834 (pp.21–3).

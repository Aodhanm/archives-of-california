# 'Tercer documento.' Heading written out in full: '1825 Marzo = 12 = Acapulco / José Mª Echeandía á José Mª Herrera'. Sav

**C-A 27 (Departmental State Papers), scan n115** · Sav p. 120 [stamp] / 119 [folio] · tomo p. pp 440 = 5 [bundle cite, written only at the end of the four documents on n116] · 1825 Marzo 12 [1825-03-12] · Savage abstract · 

'Tercer documento.' Heading written out in full: '1825 Marzo = 12 = Acapulco / José Mª Echeandía á José Mª Herrera'. Savage's entire abstract is the single line 'Este documento es la repeticion del primero' [= this document is a repetition of the first]; he copies no text. This heading is the key that identifies the 'J. M. E (sic)' of the whole bundle as Echeandía.

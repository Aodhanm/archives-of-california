# Conclusion only — the heading stood on the leaf (Savage stamp 153) MISSING from the IA scan. On embarking an artillery p

**C-A 27 (Departmental State Papers), scan n145** · Sav p. 154 [stamp] / 163 [pencil] · tomo p. pp.563–6 · 1826 [heading lost on the missing stamp-153 leaf; between d164 (1826-09-15 SD) and d165 (1826-10-24 Sta B.)] · Savage abstract · 

Conclusion only — the heading stood on the leaf (Savage stamp 153) MISSING from the IA scan. On embarking an artillery party for Acapulco: will march 'para esta Ciudad, con todos los Artilleros del Destacamento'; any wishing to stay remain 'incorporados á la partida que queda del referido Gonzalez'; 'he tratado el embarque mío, un Cabo y nueve Artilleros por el corto estipendio de 200$'; if the party must not leave until a national warship comes, asks that it 'se halle reunida en ese punto que es de donde descienden los Buques para de S. Blas ó de Acapulco'. Recovered 2026-09-02.

# Closing portion only; the opening lies on the missing leaf. González protests that he is not being recognised 'como Coma

**C-A 27 (Departmental State Papers), scan n105** · Sav p. 109 [stamp] / 108 [folio] · tomo p. pp. 418 á 420 · n.d. [heading, date and place are on the leaf physically absent from the IA scan (stamp 108 / folio 107); certified copies 1827 Feb 24 and 1827 Aug 2] · Savage abstract · 

Closing portion only; the opening lies on the missing leaf. González protests that he is not being recognised 'como Comandante de Armas ni del Distrito, lo pone en conocimto para que le sea admitido su relevo, y no se quede haciendo un papel ridículo' [= he is recognised neither as Comandante de Armas nor of the District, and asks that his relief be accepted so he is not left playing a ridiculous part]. Signed '= Migl Gonzales = Al Comandte general de ambas Californias = Es Copia = Agustín V. Zamorano'. Savage's own note follows: '(Este documto es copia de José Mª Ybarra en 24 de Febo de 1827 y de Zamorano Agto 2 del mismo año)' [= the text descends through a copy by José María Ybarra, 24 Feb 1827, and one by Zamorano, 2 Aug 1827].

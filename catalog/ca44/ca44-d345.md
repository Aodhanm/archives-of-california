# Dissolution of mutinous army corps: Congress empowers the government to disband all permanent and active corps that whol

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n252** · Sav p. 252 [stamp] / 99 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 540-41 · 1833-11-15 · Savage abstract · 

Dissolution of mutinous army corps: Congress empowers the government to disband all permanent and active corps that wholly or largely rose against the institutions, and orders that in the general escalafon, beside the number of each dissolved corps, this note be entered: 'Dejo de existir p.r haberse sublevado contra la Constitucion federal.' Gloss: ceased to exist for having risen against the federal Constitution.

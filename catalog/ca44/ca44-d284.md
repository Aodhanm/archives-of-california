# Decree declaring repealed the law of 23 May 1829 which had declared the sowing and sale of tobacco free, and ordering th

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n226** · Sav p. 226 [stamp] / 75 [hw] (anchor leaf); cite on 227 [stamp] / 76 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 440-42 · 1832-05-26 · Savage abstract · 

Decree declaring repealed the law of 23 May 1829 which had declared the sowing and sale of tobacco free, and ordering that the monopoly of leaf tobacco remain in force in all the States of the Republic. '...mandando quede subsistente el estanco del tabaco en rama en todos los Estados de la Republica. - mandado publicar en Cal. el 10 de Febrero de 1833 por el Gral. Figueroa' [= restoration of the tobacco estanco]. Anchored at n226; spans n226-n227.

# Spans n186-n187. Decree pardoning from the capital sentence imposed by ordinary court-martial the prisoners Julian Mejia

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n186–n187** · Sav p. 186 [printed stamp] / 35 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 243 · 1829-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n186-n187. Decree pardoning from the capital sentence imposed by ordinary court-martial the prisoners Julian Mejia, Manuel Trujillo, Pedro Sanchez, Antº Lopez and Ignº Delgadillo, and ordering the competent tribunal to apply whatever penalty it deems just. Savage: 'Decreto indultando de la pena capital a q. fueron condenados en consejo ordº de guerra a los reos Julian Mejia, Manuel Trujillo, Pedro Sanchez, Antº Lopez e Ignº Delgadillo; y mandando q. el tribunal competente aplique a los eximidos, la q. estimare de justicia.'

# Decree of the General Congress authorising the government to issue bills to the value of two million pesos at a premium 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n220** · Sav p. 220 [stamp] / 69 [hw] (anchor leaf); cite on 221 [stamp] / 70 [hw] · tomo p. p. 401 · 1831-09-06 · Savage abstract · 

Decree of the General Congress authorising the government to issue bills to the value of two million pesos at a premium of 5 per cent a month, payable in hard money. 'Decreto del Cong.o Gral. autorizando al Gob.o para la emision de letras valor de dos millones de pesos con el premio de un 5 p% al mes pagaderos en dinero efectivo' [= two-million-peso short-term borrowing]. Anchored at n220; spans n220-n221.

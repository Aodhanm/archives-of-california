# Decree ordering the government to put in hand at once the urgent works demanded by the Huehuetoca drainage, spending $20

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n218** · Sav p. 218 [stamp] / 67 [hw] · tomo p. p. 390 · 1831-02-09 · Savage abstract · 

Decree ordering the government to put in hand at once the urgent works demanded by the Huehuetoca drainage, spending $20,000 on it. 'Decreto determinando disponga desde luego el Gob.o se pongan en ejecucion las obras urgentes que demandan el desagüe de Huehuetoca, invirtiendo en ella la cantidad de $20,000' [= emergency funding for the Valley of Mexico drainage canal].

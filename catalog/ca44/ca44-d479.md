# Bustamante, using extraordinary faculties, extinguishes the 5th permanent infantry regiment and the Capital's active inf

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n297** · Sav p. 2... [printed stamp, right edge lost to defective scan] / 14... [handwritten, below stamp, also cut] · tomo p. pp. 663-6[4] (cite partly cut by scan defect) · 1840-07-30 · Savage abstract · 

Bustamante, using extraordinary faculties, extinguishes the 5th permanent infantry regiment and the Capital's active infantry regiment for their mutiny, creating a new Regimiento Ligero in their place. 'estinguiendo el 5.o Regimien[to] de infanteria permanente, por la defec[cion] y atentado q. cometió en la madruga[da] del 15 del presente... sublevan[dose] contra el Spmo. Gob.o' [= the units that joined the 15 July 1840 federalist pronunciamiento in Mexico City]. Bracketed line-ends reconstructed; right edge of leaf lost.

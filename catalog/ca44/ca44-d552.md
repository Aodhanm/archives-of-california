# Gov. Echeandia forwards to the alcalde of San Jose a ruling on naval accounting jurisdiction. Verbatim: 'Que Comisarias 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n324** · Sav p. 324 [stamp] / 22 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 108-109 · 1827-10-24 · Savage abstract · 

Gov. Echeandia forwards to the alcalde of San Jose a ruling on naval accounting jurisdiction. Verbatim: 'Que Comisarias Grales de Departam.tos Maritimos, y en su respectivo caso las Subcomisarias desempenaran las atribuciones q.e las ordenanzas y reglamentos actuales encargan al M.trio. de cuenta y razon de Marina' [= the General Commissaries of the Maritime Departments, and where applicable the Subcommissaries, will exercise the powers that current ordinances assign to the Ministry of account and reckoning of the Navy]. Place given as Mexico though the addressee is the San Jose alcalde.

# Decree directing that the government leave unfilled, the first time and whenever they fell vacant, the posts it judged u

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n223** · Sav p. 223 [stamp] / 72 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 418-20 · 1831-05-21 · Savage abstract · 

Decree directing that the government leave unfilled, the first time and whenever they fell vacant, the posts it judged unnecessary in the Comisarias, reporting immediately to Congress if in session or as soon as it met. 'Decreto previniendo q.e el Gob.o dejase sin proveer... las plazas q.e considerase innecesarias en las Comisarias, dando cuenta inmediatam.te al Congreso' [= retrenchment of commissariat staff].

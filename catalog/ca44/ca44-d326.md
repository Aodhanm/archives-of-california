# Model no. 1, for the certification of the local authority or governor of a State or Territory. 'Modelos N.o 1 y 2 para l

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n226** · Sav p. 226 [stamp] / 75 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 437-38 · 1832 (sin fecha) · Savage abstract · 

Model no. 1, for the certification of the local authority or governor of a State or Territory. 'Modelos N.o 1 y 2 para la certificacion de la autoridad local ó gobernador de un Est.o ó Territorio, y el segundo, p.a Patentes á fin de asegurar las propiedad de invencion' [= blank certificate to be signed by a local authority or territorial governor - the form a Californian governor would have used]. Doc 1 of 2; Savage distinguishes the two by function only.

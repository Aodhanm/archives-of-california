# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) '1819 Octbre. 4. Mont. / Sola. al Co

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n127** · Sav p. 127 [stamp] / 127. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 575. 576. / pp. 577. 578. / p. 579 · 1819 Oct 4; 1819 Oct 21; 1819 Oct 28 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) '1819 Octbre. 4. Mont. / Sola. al Com.do del Pueblo.', pp. 575. 576: the community fence to be made as always, and all who sow within it obliged to its conservation; approves having sent Chaboya and Rochin to Mission San José to learn as Herreros; and he may take ten fanegas of Diezmo wheat. (b) Octbre 21, pp. 577. 578, verbatim: 'Al maestro de Escuela haga V. q.e no se embejezca en construir su casa p.a q.e continue en la enseñanza de esos jóvenes.' (c) Obre 28, p. 579: encloses the instancia of the vecino Tomás Patrón with his proveído, for report by the comisionado and the Alcalde; and six or eight fanegas of wheat to be supplied to the Alcalde.

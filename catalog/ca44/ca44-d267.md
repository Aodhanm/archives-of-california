# Decree of the Chambers establishing Mexican legations to the powers of Europe and America, with a salary of eight to ten

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n221** · Sav p. 221 [stamp] / 70 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 402-3 · 1831-05-25 · Savage abstract · 

Decree of the Chambers establishing Mexican legations to the powers of Europe and America, with a salary of eight to ten thousand pesos, and two to three thousand for each of their secretaries. 'Decreto de las Camaras estableciendo legaciones Mejicanas para las potencias de Europa y America... publicado en Cal. por Figueroa el 17 de Marzo d 1833' [= diplomatic establishment; note of its promulgation in California].

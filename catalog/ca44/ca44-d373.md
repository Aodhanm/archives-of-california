# Spans n265-n266. Decree with extraordinary faculties declaring that Vicente Guerrero merited until his death the title B

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n265** · Sav p. 265 [stamp] / 112 [hw] · tomo p. p. 583 · 1833-11-16 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n265-n266. Decree with extraordinary faculties declaring that Vicente Guerrero merited until his death the title Benemerito de la Patria, and ordering his ashes brought from Oaxaca to the capital. Savage: 'declarando que el C. Vicente Guerrero merecio hasta su muerte el titulo de Benemerito de la Patria, y ordenando q. se conduscan de acuerdo con las autoridades Supremas de Oaxaca sus cenizas a la Capital p.a q. sean depositadas en la Urna de los heroes de la Independencia.'

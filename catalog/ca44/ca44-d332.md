# Decree authorising the President to take the field: 'Decreto autorizando al Presidente de la Republica p.a poder mandar 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n246** · Sav p. 246 [stamp] / 93 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 512-13 · 1833-06-01 · Savage abstract · 

Decree authorising the President to take the field: 'Decreto autorizando al Presidente de la Republica p.a poder mandar personalmente el ejercito, si asi lo consideraba conveniente a la conservacion de la tranquilidad publica.' Gloss: the President may command the army in person if he judges it convenient to the preservation of public tranquillity. The legal predicate for the 5 July decree abstracted on n241.

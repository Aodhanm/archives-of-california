# Decree restoring citizen Mariano Bayas to his post of Lieutenant of the active battalion of Puebla, of which he had been

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n227** · Sav p. 227 [stamp] / 76 [hw] · tomo p. p. 443 · 1832-02-25 · Savage abstract · 

Decree restoring citizen Mariano Bayas to his post of Lieutenant of the active battalion of Puebla, of which he had been deprived for having separated himself from his colours. 'Decreto reponiendo al C. Mariano Bayas en su empleo de Teniente del batallon activo de Puebla de que fué privado por haberse separado de sus banderas' [= reinstatement after desertion].

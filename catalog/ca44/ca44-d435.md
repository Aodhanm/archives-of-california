# Congress cedes to the State of Queretaro the cavalry barracks next to the city Alameda, so it may be assigned to the civ

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n272** · Sav p. 272 [stamp] / 119 [hw] · tomo p. p. 607 · 1834-04-19 · Savage abstract · 

Congress cedes to the State of Queretaro the cavalry barracks next to the city Alameda, so it may be assigned to the civic militia. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o cediendo al Estado de Queretaro, el Cuartel de Caballeria inmediato a la Alameda de la Ciudad, a fin de que lo destine a la milicia civica de la misma.'

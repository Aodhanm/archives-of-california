# Congress authorizes the government to relocate all who pronounced against the institutions and held safe-conducts to liv

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n272** · Sav p. 272 [stamp] / 119 [hw] · tomo p. p. 606 · 1834-04-03 · Savage abstract · 

Congress authorizes the government to relocate all who pronounced against the institutions and held safe-conducts to live at fixed places, sending them where they could do no harm. Savage: 'Decreto del Congreso, autorizando al Gob.o para q. a cuantos se pronunciaron contra las instituciones y tubiesen resguardas para vivir en determinados puntos, los destinase a aquel en q. no pudiesen ser perjudiciales.' Reading 'resguardas' [= resguardos?] uncertain.

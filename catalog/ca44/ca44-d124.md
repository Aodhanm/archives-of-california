# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of the Ag.to 5 order, pp. 608.

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n134** · Sav p. 134 [stamp] / 134. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 608. 609. / pp. 610. 611. · 1818 Aug 5; 1818 Aug 10; 1818 Aug 11 · verbatim transcription · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of the Ag.to 5 order, pp. 608. 609, containing two things at once: the agregados of D. Ygn.o Ortega's Rancho - 'el nombrado Cuervo, Julián Cantua, Andrés García, Jesus Romero y Fran.co Lara' - to be examined before the Regidor and Romualdo Gonzalez on the killing of cattle, with instructions on the school if Buelna or Carlos Castro are busy sowing ('que quede la escuela cerrada por algunos dias'); and, verbatim, 'Aqui se halla una fragata Rusa y solicita trigo el q.e he dispuesto se le franquee, avise al vecindario y diga cuantas fanegas podrán vender'. (b) Ag.to 10, pp. 610. 611: the wheat the vecinos can sell is noted, but the deal is not yet closed, so suspend the shipment. (c) 'Ag.to 11 = Culpables en matar ganado' opens, closing on n135.

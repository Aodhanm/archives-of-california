# Congress authorizes the government to raise up to one and a half million pesos in cash by loans or advances, paying the 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n271** · Sav p. 271 [stamp] / 118 [hw] · tomo p. p. 604 · 1834-03-10 · Savage abstract · 

Congress authorizes the government to raise up to one and a half million pesos in cash by loans or advances, paying the corresponding premium. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o gral. autorizando al Gob.o p.a proporcionarse por prestamos o anticipaciones, hasta millon y medio de pesos en numerario, abonando el premio corresp.te.' The word 'abonando' is a caret insertion above the line.

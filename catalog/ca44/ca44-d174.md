# Decree empowering the government to allow a one-third percent wastage allowance on gunpowder issued by the warehouse-kee

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n184** · Sav p. 184 [printed stamp] / 33 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 226 · 1829-05-06 · Savage abstract · 

Decree empowering the government to allow a one-third percent wastage allowance on gunpowder issued by the warehouse-keeper of that branch, extended to all such keepers in the Republic. Savage: 'Decreto facultando al Gobº pª que pueda abonar el 1/3 p.% de mermas en la polvora q. despache el guarda-almacen del ramo, haciendose extensivo a todos los de la Repª' NOTE: this heading alone in the run lacks Savage's marginal cross.

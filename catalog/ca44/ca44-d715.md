# Close of the 1821 Jan 28 'Sobre arresto y castigo' order, and the substantive part of it: eight days arrest for those wh

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n111** · Sav p. 111 [stamp] / 111. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 511. 512. · 1821 Jan 28 · Savage abstract · 

Close of the 1821 Jan 28 'Sobre arresto y castigo' order, and the substantive part of it: eight days arrest for those who helped kill and eat the Novillo and the Buey; to cut this old vice at the root, one or two celadores are to be appointed with a gratification of one third of what they denounce and prove stolen; Tiburcio Vazquez to pay the value of the ox and the steer to whoever proves ownership; the soldier Gerbasio Soto, an accomplice, is not to go unpunished and orders go to his Comandante; the invalid Briones to gather in his daughters; if Antonino Buelna were ill his brother Joaquín could take his place as schoolmaster with some gratification; and Rafael Romero is approved to settle in the pueblo in his trade of herrero, the fragua to be made available to him, with the boys sent by Sola's disposition to the Missions put under Romero's care.

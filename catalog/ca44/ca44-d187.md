**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n189** · Sav p. 189 [printed stamp] / 38 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 255 · 1829-10-14 · Savage abstract · 

Guerrero, under extraordinary powers, substitutes the building that had been the Convento de Belemitas with that of the ex-Inquisition for the establishment of the great national invalids' home.

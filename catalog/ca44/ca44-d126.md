**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n162** · Sav p. 162 [stamp] / 11. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 87-88. · 1824 Oct 6 · Savage abstract · 

'Juan Guzman S.rio de Estado, remitiendo ejemplares de la Constitucion Federal de la Rep.a' [= forwards printed copies of the Federal Constitution of the Mexican Republic]. This is the transmittal by which the 1824 federal constitution reached the provinces, filed among the San Jose papers.

# Decree dispensing citizen Jose Valentin Baz nine months and citizen Bartolome Saviñon a full year of the required term s

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n188** · Sav p. 188 [printed stamp] / 37 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. pp. 250-51 · 1829-05-06 · Savage abstract · 

Decree dispensing citizen Jose Valentin Baz nine months and citizen Bartolome Saviñon a full year of the required term so that they might take the bachelor's degree in the faculty of Canons. Savage: 'Decreto dispensando al C. Jose Valentin Baz el termino de 9 meses y al C. Bartolome Saviñon, el de un año, para q. pudiesen recibirse el grado de bachiller en la facultad de Canones.'

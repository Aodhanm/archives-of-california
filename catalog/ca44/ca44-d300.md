# 'Decreto determinando q. los retiros consedidos y que se consedan a los individuos del ejercito y marina, se arreglen al

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n216** · Sav p. 216 [stamp] / 65 [hw] · tomo p. p. 383 · 1831-03-30 · Savage abstract · 

'Decreto determinando q. los retiros consedidos y que se consedan a los individuos del ejercito y marina, se arreglen al sueldo q. se disfruta en la infanteria de linea conforme a sus clases.' [= retirements already granted and to be granted to members of the army and navy are to be regulated on the pay enjoyed in the infantry of the line according to their classes]. Bears on the retirement pay of presidial soldiers.

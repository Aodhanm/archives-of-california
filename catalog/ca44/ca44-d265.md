# Decree abolishing the protomedicato and substituting a board named facultad medica del Distrito, of eight physician-surg

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n220** · Sav p. 220 [stamp] / 69 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 399-400 · 1831-11-21 · Savage abstract · 

Decree abolishing the protomedicato and substituting a board named facultad medica del Distrito, of eight physician-surgeons and four pharmacists, superseding the law of 23 Dec. 1830. 'Decreto mandando cesar el protomedicato, sustituyendolo con una junta con el nombre de facultad medica del Dist.o... compuesta de ocho profesores médico cirujanos y cuatro farmaceuticos, subrogando la ley de 23 de Dbre. de 1830' [= reorganisation of medical licensing].

# Decree of President Victoria adopting the presidial-company system for the defence of the Internal States of the East an

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n303** · Sav p. 303 [printed stamp] / -1- [handwritten, ABOVE the stamp; Tomo III foliation restarts here] · tomo p. pp. 2-3 · 1826-03-21 · Savage abstract · 

Decree of President Victoria adopting the presidial-company system for the defence of the Internal States of the East and West and the Territory of New Mexico. 'Adoptando el sistema de Compañias presidiales p.a defenza de Estados internos de Oriente, Occidente y Territorio de Nuevo Méx.' [= the 1826 reorganisation of the northern frontier garrisons; note California is not named]. First dated document of Tomo III.

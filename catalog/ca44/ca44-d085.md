# (a) Close of the Apr 3 order: the chapel, the acequia and Branciforte. (b) Apr 8, pp. 445. 446, the strongest item in th

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n94** · Sav p. 94 [stamp] / 93. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 443. 444. / pp. 445. 446. / pp. 447. 48. · 1799 Apr 3, Apr 8, Apr 12 · Savage abstract · 

(a) Close of the Apr 3 order: the chapel, the acequia and Branciforte. (b) Apr 8, pp. 445. 446, the strongest item in this gap: 'las tierras q.e poseen los gentiles deben quedarles intactas y no deben ser molestados ni en sus siembras ni ganados' [the lands the Gentiles hold must be left to them intact and they are not to be molested in their sowings or their stock]. (c) Apr 12, marked '(2 Dctos)', pp. 447. 48.

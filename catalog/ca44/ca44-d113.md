# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. '1819. Mzo. 25. San Antonio / Sola al Co

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n123** · Sav p. 123 [stamp] / 123. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 558. 559. · 1819 Mar 25 · verbatim transcription · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. '1819. Mzo. 25. San Antonio / Sola al Com.do del pueblo de S. José = Sobre arresto de Militares'. A jurisdiction and fuero document. On the notice due to the Alf.z del R.l Cpo. de Art.a D. Manuel Gomez for the apprehension of the Cabo de Milicias of his Corps Rafael Soto and of M.a Ant.a Mesa: the exceptions and privileges of the Royal Corps of Artillery differ from those the general ordinance of the Army sets, and so, verbatim, 'todo indibiduo q.e dependa de la jurisdicion de dho. R.l cuerpo q.e resida en ese pueblo inclusas las mugeres y familias de todos, cuando se vea V. en necesidad de arrestarlos por delitos q.e cometan, al mismo tiempo q.e me de V. parte lo hará tambien al referido oficial'. Sola adds that he has represented to the Viceroy the frequent scandals these militiamen give. The lower part of the leaf opens the Apr 26 cimarrones order catalogued at n124.

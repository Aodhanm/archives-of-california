# Decree requiring all individuals holding real property in the District and Territories who had never resided, or should 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n192** · Sav p. 192 [printed stamp] / 41 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 273 · 1829-05-23 · Savage abstract · 

Decree requiring all individuals holding real property in the District and Territories who had never resided, or should henceforth cease to reside, ten years in the nation to pay 5 percent on the net rent of those properties, over and above the contributions they already pay. Savage: 'Decreto previniendo q. todos los individuos q. tuviesen bienes raizes en el Distº y Territorios y nunca hubiesen recidido o en delante dejasen de recidir por diez años en la Nacion pagasen un 5 p.% sobre la renta liquida de dhos. bienes...' [= absentee-owner surtax reaching Alta California]

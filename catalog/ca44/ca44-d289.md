# Decree authorising the government to admit recognised credits against the Nation in payment of sums to be paid into the 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n228–n229** · Sav p. 228 [stamp] / 76 [hw] (anchor leaf); cite on 229 [stamp] / 77 [hw] · tomo p. p. 453 · 1832-08-11 · Savage abstract · 

Decree authorising the government to admit recognised credits against the Nation in payment of sums to be paid into the Treasury under the contracts and loans made in virtue of the faculties granted by the decree of 27 May last, preferring those for pensions, salaries of employees and montepios, which would also be admitted as cash. '...prefiriendo los de pensiones, sueldos de emplea[dos], y montepios, que se admitiran tambien como dinero efectivo'. Anchored at n228; spans n228-n229 (cite p. 453 fetched from n229).

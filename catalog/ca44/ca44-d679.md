# Savage abstract: 'Decreto, declarando vigentes leyes prohibitivas de juegos de suerte y azar' = decree declaring in forc

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n359** · Sav p. 359 [stamp] / 70 [hw, BELOW stamp] · tomo p. p. 393 · 1830-02-23 · Savage abstract · 

Savage abstract: 'Decreto, declarando vigentes leyes prohibitivas de juegos de suerte y azar' = decree declaring in force the laws prohibiting games of chance and hazard. Gambling bans of exactly this kind were the standing business of California alcaldes' bandos.

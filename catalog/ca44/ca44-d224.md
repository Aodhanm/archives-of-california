# Decree declaring the enjoyment of montepio (with the interlineated caret 'a las viudas') for officers who died during th

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n186** · Sav p. 186 [printed stamp] / 35 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 242 · 1829-09-12 · Savage abstract · 

Decree declaring the enjoyment of montepio (with the interlineated caret 'a las viudas') for officers who died during their exile in consequence of the pronunciamiento known as that of Montaño. Savage: 'Decreto declarando el goze de Monte-pio [interlin. 'a las viudas'] de los oficiales fallecidos, durante su destierro, en consecuencia del pronunciamto. llamado de Montaño.'

# One-line order for timber: 'Que remita las 12 trozas de Roble' [= that he forward the 12 oak logs]. Savage gives no furt

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n149** · Sav p. 149 [stamp] / 149 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 677. 678. · 1816 Dec 3 · Savage abstract · 

One-line order for timber: 'Que remita las 12 trozas de Roble' [= that he forward the 12 oak logs]. Savage gives no further detail; the logs are presumably for presidio or bridge construction, but he does not say.

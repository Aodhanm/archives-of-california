# Commemorative pyramid decree, ordering a monument where the Spaniards surrendered to the Mexican army, with a marble tab

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n244** · Sav p. 244 [stamp] / 91 [hw] · tomo p. p. 502 · 1833-04-29 · Savage abstract · 

Commemorative pyramid decree, ordering a monument where the Spaniards surrendered to the Mexican army, with a marble tablet inscribed: 'La Nacion Mejicana = Triunfo de sus invasores = Venciendo al ejercito espanol = El once de Set. de mil ocho cientos veinte y nueve.' Gloss: The Mexican Nation triumphed over its invaders, defeating the Spanish army, the eleventh of September 1829 [= the Barradas expedition surrender].

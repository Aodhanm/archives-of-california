# Congress pardons the condemned man Narciso Leon from the death penalty, leaving it to the competent tribunal to impose s

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n182** · Sav p. 182 [printed stamp] / 31 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 217 · 1829-03-01 · Savage abstract · 

Congress pardons the condemned man Narciso Leon from the death penalty, leaving it to the competent tribunal to impose some other extraordinary punishment. Savage: 'Decreto del Congreso indultando al reo Narciso Leon de la pena de muerte, dejando a el juicio del Tribl. competente, imponerle alguna otra extraord.' Heading reads 'Marzo 1º' (verified by enlargement).

# 'Decretos sobre el dro. de consumo impuesto a los generos, frutos, efectos y licores estrangeros.' [= decrees on the con

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n215** · Sav p. 215 [stamp] / 64 [hw] · tomo p. p. 382 · 1831-04-02 · Savage abstract · 

'Decretos sobre el dro. de consumo impuesto a los generos, frutos, efectos y licores estrangeros.' [= decrees on the consumption duty laid on foreign goods, produce, effects and liquors]. Savage writes Decretos in the PLURAL under a single date and a single page cite (p. 382) and does not distinguish the pieces; rowed once because he supplies no count. Directly relevant to the duties levied on foreign trade at California ports.

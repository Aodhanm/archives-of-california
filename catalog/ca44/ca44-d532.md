# Decree to Figueroa ceding ex-Jesuit property to the states. Verbatim: 'Decreto. Sobre q.e se ceden a los Estados los edi

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n331** · Sav p. 331 [stamp] / 29 [hw] · tomo p. p. 145 · 1834-01-13 · Savage abstract · 

Decree to Figueroa ceding ex-Jesuit property to the states. Verbatim: 'Decreto. Sobre q.e se ceden a los Estados los edificios q.e fueron conventos, Colegios y oratorios de los ex-Jesuitas y q.e se hallen situados en sus respectivos territorios' [= the buildings that were convents, colleges and oratories of the ex-Jesuits, located in the respective territories, are ceded to the states].

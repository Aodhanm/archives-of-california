# Decree of Congress granting citizen Juan Andres Velarde, for a term of ten years, the exclusive privilege of using a new

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n225** · Sav p. 225 [stamp] / 74 [hw] · tomo p. p. 436 · 1832-04-14 · Savage abstract · 

Decree of Congress granting citizen Juan Andres Velarde, for a term of ten years, the exclusive privilege of using a new system of amalgamation and cupellation which he invented for working metals. 'Decreto del Congreso concediendo al C. Juan Andres Velarde por el termino de diez años, privilegio esclusivo para usar un nuevo sistema de amalgamacion y Capelacion q.e inventó para veneficiar metales' [= ten-year patent for an ore-refining process].

# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of Jun 27, pp. 520. 521: Roble

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n114** · Sav p. 114 [stamp] / 114. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 520. 521. / pp. 522. 523. / p. 524 / pp. 525. 526. · 1821 Jun 27, Jun 28, Jun 29, Aug 11 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of Jun 27, pp. 520. 521: Robles has been punished and admonished by Sola and his predecessors for bad conduct, so keep watch on him. (b) Jun 28, pp. 522. 523: Pedro Amador to vacate the house in the coming month of July and hand it to whoever sold it to him. (c) Jun 29, p. 524: go to Pedro Amador's house with two witnesses, read him the memorial and deliver it. (d) Ag.to 11, pp. 525. 526: the release of Antonio Chavoya and Domingo Peralta noted; the little fruit some get from their work; watch Robles's conduct.

# Decree granting the pueblo of Tlacotalpan on the Costa de Sotavento permission to open, by itself or through contractors

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n187** · Sav p. 187 [printed stamp] / 36 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 248 · 1829-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

Decree granting the pueblo of Tlacotalpan on the Costa de Sotavento permission to open, by itself or through contractors, a canal at the point called el Conejo to communicate with the Gulf. Savage: 'Decreto concediendo permiso al pueblo de Tlacotalpan de la Costa de Sotavento, pª q. por si, o por empresarios abriese un canal en el punto nombrado el Conejo q. se comunique con el mar del Norte.' [= a pueblo-level public-works grant, though not a Californian one]

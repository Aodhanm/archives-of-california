# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of the preceding letter, pp. 5

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n125** · Sav p. 125 [stamp] / 125. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 566. 567. / pp. 568. 569. · 1819 Jun 25; 1819 Jul 2 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of the preceding letter, pp. 566. 567: the arrival of the Neophyte Gaspar, who will be very useful for his carpenter's trade; Sola cannot accede to the request for Ant.o Larios as herrero; the vecindario should first attend to the cercas, then begin the maestro's house; paper will be sent at the first opportunity; and D. Pedro Amador may sell a barrel of Ag.te, permitted without setting a precedent. (b) '1819. Junio 25. San Juan / Sola al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José = Sobre conducir arinas á Mont.', pp. 568. 569: the bergantines 'San Carlos y Cosak' are about to arrive with troops, munitions and funds, and flour is needed for the bread to be supplied at this Presidio, so warn the vecinos to convey what flour they can, understanding they will be paid on the ships' arrival. (c) 'Julio 2 = Sobre robo de Caballos (2 Dctos)' opens, closing on n126.

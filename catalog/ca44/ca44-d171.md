# Decree ordering collection of the 3 percent derecho de consumo in the district and territories of the federation, except

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n183** · Sav p. 183 [printed stamp] / 32 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 221 · 1829-02-24 · Savage abstract · 

Decree ordering collection of the 3 percent derecho de consumo in the district and territories of the federation, excepting goods exempt from the internal alcabala. Savage: 'Decreto ordenando cobrar el 3 p.% de dro. de consumo en el distrito y territorios de la federacion, exeptuandose los exentos del pago de Alcabala intº' [= consumption duty extended to the Territories, i.e. reaching Alta California]

# spans n149-n150. Sola orders a full census of the pueblo and its ranchos: 'Que mande una relacion gral del vecindario y 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n149–n150** · Sav p. 149 [stamp] / 149 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 679. 680. · 1815 Oct 15 · Savage abstract · 

spans n149-n150. Sola orders a full census of the pueblo and its ranchos: 'Que mande una relacion gral del vecindario y Ranchos, lista de hombres, mug.s, Ninos y ninas especificando sus edades y oficios q. egercen, num.o de casas, tambien si hay Yglesia y lista de los ornam.tos y basos sagrados q. haya.' [= a general return of householders and ranchos, men, women, boys and girls with ages and trades, number of houses, whether there is a church, and an inventory of its ornaments and sacred vessels]. Heading on the leaf reads 1815. Octre. 15. Mont. / Sola al Comisionado del pueblo.

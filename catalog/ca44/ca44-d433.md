# Congress repeals in all its parts the decree of 28 Nov. 1813 [sic in Savage; almost certainly a slip for 1833] which had

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n271** · Sav p. 271 [stamp] / 118 [hw] · tomo p. p. 605 · 1834-04-21 · Savage abstract · 

Congress repeals in all its parts the decree of 28 Nov. 1813 [sic in Savage; almost certainly a slip for 1833] which had fixed the pay and rank due to ministers of state on leaving office. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o derogando en todas sus partes el Decreto de 28 de Nob. de 1813, por el cual se declaraba el haber y consideracion q. debian tener los Srios. del despacho cuando fuesen exonerados por dimision espontanea o por q. el Gob.o eligiese otro en uso de sus facultades.'

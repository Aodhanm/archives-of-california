# (a) pp. 466. 467: 'Que se nombre un Juez de Aguas q.e las reparta por turnos' [that a water judge be named to distribute

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n97** · Sav p. 97 [stamp] / 96. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 466. 467. / p. 468 / pp. 469. 470. / pp. 471. 472. / pp. 473. 474. / pp. 475. 476. · 1799 (Jul 15, Jul 29, Sep 3, plus one undated head) · Savage abstract · 

(a) pp. 466. 467: 'Que se nombre un Juez de Aguas q.e las reparta por turnos' [that a water judge be named to distribute the water by turns] - the pueblo's irrigation office. (b) Jul 15, '(2 Dctos)': p. 468, Manuel González cedes ground for the plaza and is to be compensated with an equal piece of realengo; pp. 469. 470, José Pliego alias Jalapa sent to work wool with Rafael Villavicencio. (c) Jul 29, pp. 471. 472: Bernardo Heredia and Juan Saer. (d) Sbre 3, '(3 Dctos)': pp. 473. 474, recovery of pósito seed loans; pp. 475. 476, no one is to sell mescal nor playing cards, with a watch that English goods are not sold.

# Congressional decree on the 10 per-cent consumption surcharge collected at the Mexico City customhouse under the laws of

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n297** · Sav p. 2... [printed stamp, right edge lost to defective scan] / 14... [handwritten, below stamp, also cut] · tomo p. pp. 665-66 · 1840-06-09 · Savage abstract · 

Congressional decree on the 10 per-cent consumption surcharge collected at the Mexico City customhouse under the laws of 26 Nov. and 27 Dec. 1839, ordering that from 1 June of the current year 1 per cent be deducted and applied to that customhouse's office expenses. 'Decreto del Cong.o relativo al P[roduc]to del 10 p.p. de aumento de consumo recaudado en la Aduana dela Capital en virtud de las leyes de 26 de No[v.] y 27 de Dbre. de 1839' [= earmarking of customs revenue].

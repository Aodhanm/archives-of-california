# Congress authorizes the government to reorganize public instruction in all its branches in the Federal District and Terr

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n268** · Sav p. 268 [stamp] / 115 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 589-90 · 1833-10-19 · Savage abstract · 

Congress authorizes the government to reorganize public instruction in all its branches in the Federal District and Territories, with power to spend what is needed. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o Gral. autorizandolo al Gob.o para arreglar la ensenanza publica y todos sus ramos en el Dist.o fdral. y Territorios, pudiendo invertir al efecto las cantidades necesarias.' Base authority for the Gomez Farias education reform.

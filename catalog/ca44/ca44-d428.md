# Congress fixes the staff establishment of the general Comisaria of the port of Matamoros, assigning $9,800 to the purpos

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n269** · Sav p. 269 [stamp] / 116 [hw] · tomo p. p. 600 · 1834-04-08 · Savage abstract · 

Congress fixes the staff establishment of the general Comisaria of the port of Matamoros, assigning $9,800 to the purpose. Savage: 'Decreto del Congreso determinando la planta de empleados de la Comisaria gral. del Puerto de Matamoros, a cuyo efecto se asignaba $9.800.'

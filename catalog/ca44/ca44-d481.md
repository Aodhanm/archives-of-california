# Decree closing the port of Campeche, Departamento de Yucatán, to foreign commerce and to escala and cabotaje trade, effe

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n298** · Sav p. 298 [printed stamp] / 145 [handwritten, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 667 · 1840-06-25 · Savage abstract · 

Decree closing the port of Campeche, Departamento de Yucatán, to foreign commerce and to escala and cabotaje trade, effective six months after publication in the Republic for foreign trade and on the 15th of the coming month for coasting trade. 'clausurando el Puerto de Campeche en el Departam.to de Yucatan para el comercio estrangero y de escala y cabotaje' [= commercial reprisal against separatist Yucatán].

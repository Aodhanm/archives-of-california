# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) '1819. Fbro. 25 San Antonio / Sola, 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n122** · Sav p. 122 [stamp] / 122. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 555. 556. / p. 557 · 1819 Feb 25; 1819 Feb 1 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) '1819. Fbro. 25 San Antonio / Sola, al Com.do del Pueblo de S. José = Sobre el Maestro de escuela y siembras', pp. 555. 556: tell the maestro Buelna his licence was given for illness, and being well Sola has determined he return to service, though meanwhile the cirujano is to examine him and he continues in his employment; much maize must be sown besides the wheat; a tercio of chile in his hands is for the soldiers' rancho and Tte. Estudillo will send for it; and the petition of the invalid Bartolomé Pacheco is enclosed for report. (b) A rare bottom-up piece: '1819. Fbro. 1º Pueblo de San José / Los vecinos del Pueblo de S. José al Alcalde José Castro', p. 557 - the vecinos ask him to solicit the Governor's permission to kill their orejano and unbranded cattle, and at the margin permission is granted for the present year to kill the ganado mostrenco belonging to the vecinos.

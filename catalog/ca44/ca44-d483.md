# Decree of the National Congress ordering observance of the law of 18 Dec. 1839 which freed the sowing, sale, circulation

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n299** · Sav p. 299 [printed stamp] / 146 [handwritten, below stamp] · tomo p. pp. 669-70 · 1840-06-12 · Savage abstract · 

Decree of the National Congress ordering observance of the law of 18 Dec. 1839 which freed the sowing, sale, circulation and export of tobacco in the Departamento de Chiapas. 'mandando observar la ley de 18 de Dbre. de 1839 que declaró libre la siembra de tabaco, su venta, circulacion y exportacion en el Departamento de Chiapas' [= exemption of Chiapas from the tobacco monopoly].

# Military-service exemption. Verbatim: 'Que individuos que ejerzan profecion Maritima quedan eceptuados del servicio Mili

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n352** · Sav p. 352 [stamp] / 63 [hw] · tomo p. p. 357 · 1829-11-20 · Savage abstract · 

Military-service exemption. Verbatim: 'Que individuos que ejerzan profecion Maritima quedan eceptuados del servicio Militar.' Gloss: men following a maritime profession are exempted from military service. Month written 'Nbre' with tilde; the capital reads M-like but the abbreviation and the 1832 back-reference on n354 ('decreto de 20 de Nbre de 829') fix it as Noviembre.

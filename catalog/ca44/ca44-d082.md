# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Dec 2: 'Que acabado el Puente, ese v

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n91** · Sav p. 91 [stamp] / 90. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 429 / pp. 430. 431. / pp. 432. 433. · 1797 Dec 2; 1797 Dec 5; 1799 Jan 16 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Dec 2: 'Que acabado el Puente, ese vecindario siembre todo el trigo posible en sus tierras ó en las del Coyote, cuidando no cometan escesos con los gentiles', and the hemp must be worked in due season. (b) Dec 5: the maize harvest reached 1902 fanegas and the frijol 160; stimulate the vecinos to apply themselves to the wheat sowing; the retired cabo Miguel Pacheco to keep the post and Guard in his absence. (c) 1799 Jan 16, Monterey: returns approved the appointment of Alcalde and Regidores that the vecindario made for the present year; urge the vecinos to sow all the wheat they can and all the hemp seed there is.

# Decree authorising the executive, while the war stirred up by the dissidents of the South lasts, to grant military ranks

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n219** · Sav p. 219 [stamp] / 68 [hw] · tomo p. p. 396 · 1831-02-09 · Savage abstract · 

Decree authorising the executive, while the war stirred up by the dissidents of the South lasts, to grant military ranks to officers and sergeants solely for actions of war and distinguished services in the field. 'Decreto autorizando al ejecutivo... haga concesiones de grados militares á los oficiales y sargentos unicam.te por acciones de guerra y servicios distinguidos habidos en campaña' [= battlefield promotion power during the southern rebellion].

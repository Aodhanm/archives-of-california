# Decree declaring it within the faculties of the Supreme Government to issue a passport to, and expel from the territory 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n226** · Sav p. 226 [stamp] / 75 [hw] · tomo p. p. 439 · 1832-02-22 · Savage abstract · 

Decree declaring it within the faculties of the Supreme Government to issue a passport to, and expel from the territory of the Republic, any non-naturalised foreigner whose residence it judged prejudicial to public order. 'Decreto declarando estar en las facultades del Sup.o Gob.o espedir pasaporte y hacer salir del territorio de la República á cualquier estrangero, no naturalizado, cuya permanencia califique perjudicial al orden publico' [= summary expulsion power over foreigners].

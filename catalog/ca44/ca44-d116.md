# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of Julio 2, cited by Savage 'p

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n126** · Sav p. 126 [stamp] / 126. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 170. 171. [written thus; apparently for 570.571] / p. 572 / pp. 573. 574. · 1819 Jul 2; 1819 Jul 4 and one undated · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. (a) Close of Julio 2, cited by Savage 'pp. 170. 171.' though the run is in the 570s: the invalid José Aguila and Joaquín Buelna have arrived at the pueblo; the vecindario cannot gather flour for Monterey; and, on the theft of the Yegua Caponera with twelve horses belonging to the Alcalde, 'cada dia me ratifico mas en el concepto del abandono con q.e miran sus bienes los vecinos de ese pueblo', with an order that the vecindario gather the tame caballada into their corrals. (b) p. 572: noted that he has imprisoned Antonio Castro for the knife wound he gave the militiaman of the R.l Cpo. de Art.a Cruz Chaboya; form the sumario, reporting to the Tte. grad.o of that Royal Corps D. Manuel Gomez. (c) Julio 4, pp. 573. 574: form a small sumario on the wound given by the boy Castro to the other named Chaboya.

# Congress adds two more members to the body which, under art. 2 of the decree issued the same day, composed the Direccion

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n275** · Sav p. 275 [stamp] / 122 [hw] · tomo p. p. 616 · 1834-04-21 · Savage abstract · 

Congress adds two more members to the body which, under art. 2 of the decree issued the same day, composed the Direccion general de Instruccion publica. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o gral. aumentando dos individuos mas, a los q. conforme el Art. 2o del Decreto espedido en la misma fha. componian la Direccion general de Instruccion publica.' Distinct from the other 21 Apr 1834 decree at p. 605.

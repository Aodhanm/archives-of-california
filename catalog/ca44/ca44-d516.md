# Decree that national goods, produce and wares exported shall be free of all duty, except gold at 2.5 per cent and silver

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n314** · Sav p. 314 [stamp] / 12. [hw] · tomo p. pp 43-44 · 1826-04-24 · Savage abstract · 

Decree that national goods, produce and wares exported shall be free of all duty, except gold at 2.5 per cent and silver at 3.5. Savage: 'Decreto q.e efectos, frutos y generos nacionales [interlined: que se exportan] estaran libres de todo derecho, ecepto el oro q.e pagará 2 1/2 % y plata 3 1/2.' Note the interlined insertion 'que se exportan' in Savage's copy.

# Decree renewing for ten further years, as regards federal duties, the ten-year privilege granted by the decrees of 8 Oct

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n274** · Sav p. 274 [stamp] / 121 [hw] · tomo p. p. 614 · 1834-02-27 · Savage abstract · 

Decree renewing for ten further years, as regards federal duties, the ten-year privilege granted by the decrees of 8 Oct. 1823 to the articles they name, counted from publication. Savage: 'Decreto previniendo q. el privilegio q. por diez anos consedieron los decretos de 8 de Oct. de 1823, a los art.s q. ellos expresan se conseden nuevam.te respecto de los dros. de la federacion por diez anos mas, contados desde la publicacion de este decreto.'

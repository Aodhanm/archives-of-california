# Decree directing the government to liquidate the sum owed on account of the paper money of Texas and to pay whatever res

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n188** · Sav p. 188 [printed stamp] / 37 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 249 · 1829-05-08 · Savage abstract · 

Decree directing the government to liquidate the sum owed on account of the paper money of Texas and to pay whatever results, as agreed with the interested parties. Savage: 'Decreto disponiendo proceda el Gobº a liquidar la cantidad que se deba en razon del papel moneda de Tejas, y a pagar la q. resulte segun convenga con los interezados.' [= federal settlement of the Texas paper-currency debt]

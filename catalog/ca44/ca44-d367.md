# Duplicate entry, marked (Dup.). Verbatim: «Decreto relativo a la ceremonia q. debia observarse, al entrar el Vice Presid

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n263** · Sav p. 263 [stamp] / 110 [hw] · tomo p. p. 573 · 1833-04-31 [sic, as written by Savage] · Savage abstract · 

Duplicate entry, marked (Dup.). Verbatim: «Decreto relativo a la ceremonia q. debia observarse, al entrar el Vice Presidte a desempenar el poder ejecutivo. (Vease p. 112 [?].)» Gloss: the same ceremonial decree entered at n256-n257 under 31 March 1833. Savage heads it «1833 - Abril 31 - Mejico», an impossible date; almost certainly a slip for Marzo 31. His cross-reference digits read 112, but the matching entry stands at his extract pp. 103-104.

# Accounting form for the maritime customs houses: 'Modelo o formulario para las liquidaciones diarias y mensuales q. debi

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n242** · Sav p. 242 [stamp] / 89 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 497-98 · 1833 [no month or day given by Savage] · Savage abstract · 

Accounting form for the maritime customs houses: 'Modelo o formulario para las liquidaciones diarias y mensuales q. debian formar las Aduanas maritimas (en Mejico) del total de dros. recaudados y aplicacion del 20 p.% y demas deducciones q. se hacian.' Gloss: model for the daily and monthly liquidations the maritime customs houses had to draw up, showing total duties collected, the 20 per cent application and other deductions. Savage supplies only the year.

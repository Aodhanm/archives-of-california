# (a) pp. 477. 478: Juan Saer's mother pays, but he is to wear a grillete until the end of October cleaning the plazas; An

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n98** · Sav p. 98 [stamp] / 97. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 477. 478. / pp. 479. 480. / p. 481 / pp. 482. 483. · 1799 Sep 14 and undated; then 1822 Jan 24 and Jan 13 · Savage abstract · 

(a) pp. 477. 478: Juan Saer's mother pays, but he is to wear a grillete until the end of October cleaning the plazas; Ant.o Acebes stays on his solar. (b) Sbre 14, pp. 479. 480: Larios warned over a false debt and horse theft by the mozo Juan Rosas; watch for prohibited goods and liquor. (c) A new heading, twenty-three years later in date: '1822. En.o 24. Mont. / Sola al Sarg.to Luis Peralta Com.do del Pueblo S. José', p. 481, approving the re-elections for Alcalde and Regidor and the election of a second Regidor. (d) 'Enero 13', pp. 482. 483: José Archuleta and José de Jesus Romero to leave for their destinations.

# Treaty of amity, navigation and commerce concluded at London on 15 June 1827, with an additional article, between the Me

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n192** · Sav p. 192 [printed stamp] / 41 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. pp. 264-72 · 1829-06-16 · Savage abstract · 

Treaty of amity, navigation and commerce concluded at London on 15 June 1827, with an additional article, between the Mexican United States and the King of the Netherlands, through the plenipotentiaries of both governments. Savage: 'Tratado de amistad navegacion y comercio, celebrado en Londres el 15 de Junio de 1827, con un Artº adicional, por los E.U. Mejicanos y su Magestad el Rey de los Paises Bajos por medio de Plenipotenciarios de ambos Gobos.' Savage's heading date 1829 Junio 16 = the Mexican promulgation, not the signing.

# Congress orders that the Electoral Junta of the Capital alone, which named the District's deputies, shall for this once 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n268** · Sav p. 268 [stamp] / 115 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 594-95 · 1833-07-03 · Savage abstract · 

Congress orders that the Electoral Junta of the Capital alone, which named the District's deputies, shall for this once name the entire Ayuntamiento. Savage: 'Decreto del Cong.o mandando que la Junta Electoral de solo la Capital q. nombro los Diputados del Distrito nombrase por esta vez el Ayuntam.to en su totalidad.' Municipal-electoral precedent of interest for pueblo governance comparison.

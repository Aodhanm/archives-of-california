# Decree ordering the formation of a scientific establishment comprising the three following branches: antiquities, produc

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n220** · Sav p. 220 [stamp] / 69 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 397-98 · 1831-11-21 · Savage abstract · 

Decree ordering the formation of a scientific establishment comprising the three following branches: antiquities, products of industry, natural history, and a botanical garden. 'Decreto mandando formar un establecimiento cientifico q.e comprendiese los tres ramos q.e siguen: antigüedades, productos de industria, historia natural, y jardin botánico' [= foundation of a national scientific/museum establishment].

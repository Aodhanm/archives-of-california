# Decree declaring contrary to art. 157 of the Federal Constitution the Estado de Occidente legislature's decree no. 97 of

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n184** · Sav p. 184 [printed stamp] / 33 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 225 · 1829-03-09 · Savage abstract · 

Decree declaring contrary to art. 157 of the Federal Constitution the Estado de Occidente legislature's decree no. 97 of 20 December, which had declared D. Francisco Iriarte ineligible for the offices of governor and vice-governor. Savage: 'Decreto declarando contrario al Artº 157... el Decreto Nº 97 de 20 de Dbre. espedido por la H. Legislatura del Estado de Occidente q. declaro a D. Francisco Iriarte, inhabil pª los empleos de Gobr. y Vice-Gobernador.'

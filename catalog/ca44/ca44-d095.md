# Sola orders a second bridge built at the pueblo now that the Pajaro bridge is done, and the vecinos named on the enclose

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n149** · Sav p. 149 [stamp] / 149 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 675. 676. · 1816 Dec 9 · Savage abstract · 

Sola orders a second bridge built at the pueblo now that the Pajaro bridge is done, and the vecinos named on the enclosed list to turn out for it: 'Habiendose concluido el puente del Pajaro, y teniendo q. construir otro en ese pueblo, hara V. concurran los vecinos q. cita la adjunta lista y quede en los propios terminos q. la al espresado pajaro; esto es, bien hecha.' [= the new bridge is to be built to the same standard as the Pajaro one]. Direct evidence of drafted vecino labour on pueblo public works, with a nominal list attached in the original.

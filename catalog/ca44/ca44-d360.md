# Verbatim: «Decreto del Congo gral. declarando vigente la Ley de 23 de Mayo de 1832.» Gloss: the general Congress declare

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n259** · Sav p. 259 [stamp] / 106 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 562-63 · 1833-06-01 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: «Decreto del Congo gral. declarando vigente la Ley de 23 de Mayo de 1832.» Gloss: the general Congress declares the law of 23 May 1832 to be in force. Savage gives no further description; the substance of the 1832 law is not stated on the leaf.

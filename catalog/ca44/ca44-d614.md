# Revival of an earlier decree. Verbatim: 'Se declar[a] vigente decreto de 20 de Nbre de 829.' Gloss: the decree of 20 Nov

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n354** · Sav p. 354 [stamp] / 65 [hw] · tomo p. p. 368 · 1832-05-23 · Savage abstract · 

Revival of an earlier decree. Verbatim: 'Se declar[a] vigente decreto de 20 de Nbre de 829.' Gloss: the decree of 20 November 1829 is declared in force again — i.e. the maritime-profession exemption from military service abstracted at p. 357 (n352).

# Congressional decree authorizing the executive to call up local militia from states bordering Durango. Verbatim: 'Decret

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n321** · Sav p. 321 [stamp] / 19 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 92-93 · 1826-03-24 · Savage abstract · 

Congressional decree authorizing the executive to call up local militia from states bordering Durango. Verbatim: 'Decreto sobre q.e Gob.o podra disponer hasta de 3000 hombres de la milicia local de Estados limitrofes, al de Durango, p.a la pacificacion de este.' [= up to 3,000 men of the local militia of the states bordering Durango may be deployed for its pacification].

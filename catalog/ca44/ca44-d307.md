# Decree fixing the closure of the extraordinary sessions of the General Congress for the 15th of the current month. 'Decr

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n219** · Sav p. 219 [stamp] / 68 [hw] · tomo p. p. 395 · 1831-12-10 · Savage abstract · 

Decree fixing the closure of the extraordinary sessions of the General Congress for the 15th of the current month. 'Decreto determinando la clausura de las sesiones extraord.s del Cong.o Gral. p.a el dia 15 del corriente' [= adjournment of the extraordinary session]. Pairs with the 30 July 1831 decree that opened the same session.

# (a) 'Marzo 20 = Sobre escuela y Matanza de ganado', pp. 542. 543: Sola sends D. Rafael del Valle to the pueblo to treat 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n119** · Sav p. 119 [stamp] / 119. [hw] · tomo p. pp. 542. 543. / p. 544 · 1820 Mar 20; 1820 Apr 28 · Savage abstract · 

(a) 'Marzo 20 = Sobre escuela y Matanza de ganado', pp. 542. 543: Sola sends D. Rafael del Valle to the pueblo to treat with the Comandante, Alcalde, Regidores and vecinos about his taking over as schoolmaster in place of Antonino Buelna, and, if that is effected, to see that the house where the school is to be placed has room for it; and he accedes to the matanza of ganado mayor alzado on last year's terms, but proposes that each year a hundred or more arrobas of sebo from that matanza be set aside for the adorno of the church and for building an adobe house for the said school and a jail. (b) Abril 28, p. 544: the certification of the church cumplimiento received; del Valle has taken the school; and the list of what each vecino can collect yearly in sebos for church and jail is to be sent in due time.

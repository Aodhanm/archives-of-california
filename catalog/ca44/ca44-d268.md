# Decree providing that secular and regular ecclesiastics, submitting themselves to arts. 1 and 2 of the law of 17 Decembe

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n221** · Sav p. 221 [stamp] / 70 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 404-5 · 1831-05-20 · Savage abstract · 

Decree providing that secular and regular ecclesiastics, submitting themselves to arts. 1 and 2 of the law of 17 December 1824, might apply to the government for the reward of their services rendered in the first eleven years of independence. 'Decreto prebiniendo que los eclesiásticos seculares y regulares... puedan ocurrir al Gob.o por el premio de sus servicios prestados en los once primeros años de independencia' [= clerical service rewards].

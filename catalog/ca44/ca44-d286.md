# Decree ordering that there be a Comandancia General in the State of Querétaro, its demarcation to be that of the state i

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n227** · Sav p. 227 [stamp] / 76 [hw] · tomo p. p. 444 · 1832-04-23 · Savage abstract · 

Decree ordering that there be a Comandancia General in the State of Querétaro, its demarcation to be that of the state itself. 'Decreto ordenando q.e en el Estado de Querétaro hubiese una Comand.a Gral. cuya demarcacion fuese la del propio estado' [= creation of a state-wide military command].

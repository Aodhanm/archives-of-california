# Echeandia again to the same San Jose alcalde, forwarding a congressional decree. Verbatim: 'Decreto de Congreso Gral. so

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n324** · Sav p. 324 [stamp] / 22 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 110-11 · 1827-11-07 · Savage abstract · 

Echeandia again to the same San Jose alcalde, forwarding a congressional decree. Verbatim: 'Decreto de Congreso Gral. sobre batallon activo de Guanajuato.' [= decree of the General Congress on the active battalion of Guanajuato]. Savage writes 'al mismo', tying it to the preceding San Jose addressee.

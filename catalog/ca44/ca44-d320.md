**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n224** · Sav p. 224 [stamp] / 73 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 422-24 · 1831-05-27 · Savage abstract · 

Decrees laying down the form in which the government was to fill the posts of the general directorate and treasury, the comisarias and maritime custom-houses etc., and the persons to be employed to that end.

# 'Decreto de Farias reformando el Art. 1o del Decreto de 4 de Feb. ultimo espedido á consecuencia de la ley de 16 [sic; =

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n281** · Sav p. 281 [stamp] / 128 [hw] · tomo p. p. 634 · 1834 Apr 11 · Savage abstract · 

'Decreto de Farias reformando el Art. 1o del Decreto de 4 de Feb. ultimo espedido á consecuencia de la ley de 16 [sic; = 6?] de Abril de 1830, sobre colonizacion.' = Farías amends Art. 1 of the decree of 4 Feb. 1834 issued under the colonization law of April 1830. Savage plainly writes 16; the law in question is the ley de colonización of 6 April 1830 [?]. Directly relevant to the Mexican colonization regime under which California grants were made.

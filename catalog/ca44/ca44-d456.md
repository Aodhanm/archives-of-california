# Decree that officers claiming service in the 1810 insurgency must submit ORIGINAL papers, never copies, to the junta de 

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n309** · Sav p. 309 [stamp] / 7. [hw] · tomo p. p 21 · 1826-07-19 · Savage abstract · 

Decree that officers claiming service in the 1810 insurgency must submit ORIGINAL papers, never copies, to the junta de calificacion. Savage: 'Decreto sobre que documentos que presenten gefes q.e sirvieron en la revolucion de 810 á junta de calificacion, sean los originales y de ninguna manera copias.'

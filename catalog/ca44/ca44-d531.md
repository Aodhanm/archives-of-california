# Printed amnesty decree for deserters, forwarded to Figueroa; Savage abstracts all three articles. Verbatim: 'Que deserto

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n331** · Sav p. 331 [stamp] / 29 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 143-44 · 1833-07-16 · Savage abstract · 

Printed amnesty decree for deserters, forwarded to Figueroa; Savage abstracts all three articles. Verbatim: 'Que desertores de clase de Sargento abajo q.e se presentaren al servicio a los 8 dias de publicado este decreto en lugar de su residencia a autorida[d] Militar o en su defecto a la politica, quedaran libres de las penas establecidas' [= deserters of sergeant's rank and below who present themselves within 8 days of publication are freed of the ordinance penalties]; art. 2 credits 6 months' service; art. 3 extends the pardon to deserters who carried off clothing or arms.

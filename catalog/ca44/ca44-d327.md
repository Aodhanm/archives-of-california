# Duty exemption decree: 'Decreto de Santa-Anna determinando queden libres de dro. ciertos art.s muebles en el Dist.o y Te

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n244** · Sav p. 244 [stamp] / 91 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 503-4 · 1833-05-25 · Savage abstract · 

Duty exemption decree: 'Decreto de Santa-Anna determinando queden libres de dro. ciertos art.s muebles en el Dist.o y Territorios de la federacion.' Gloss: certain movable goods are freed of duty in the Federal District and the Territories of the federation, a class of jurisdiction that included Alta California. Note: a second, different decree of the same date (tobacco) is abstracted on n246.

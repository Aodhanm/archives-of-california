# Decree ordering that the separate handling of letters (apartado) in the post offices continue on the same terms observed

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n183** · Sav p. 183 [printed stamp] / 32 [hw foliation, below stamp] · tomo p. p. 220 · 1829-02-21 · Savage abstract · 

Decree ordering that the separate handling of letters (apartado) in the post offices continue on the same terms observed hitherto. Savage: 'Decreto ordenando continuen en los mismos terminos q. hasta la fha. se habia observado, el apartado de Cartas en las oficinas de Correos.' [= postal apartado service unchanged]

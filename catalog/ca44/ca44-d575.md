# Printed decree to Figueroa on retirement pay for Spaniards in the military medical corps. Verbatim: 'Que Espanoles emple

**C-A 44 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n330** · Sav p. 330 [stamp] / 28 [hw] · tomo p. p. 142 · 1833-10-26 · Savage abstract · 

Printed decree to Figueroa on retirement pay for Spaniards in the military medical corps. Verbatim: 'Que Espanoles empleados en cuerpo de Sanidad militar, obtendran sus retiros conforme al reglamento de la materia, y con la parte del sueldo q.e actualmente percivan por el empleo q.e tenian antes de la ley de 30 de N.bre de 829.' [= Spaniards in the military health corps will get their retirements per regulation, at the salary share of the post held before the law of 30 Nov. 1829].

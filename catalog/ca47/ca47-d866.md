# En.o 23: 'Enterado de haberse instalado Tribunal de Circuito, con jurisdiccion a Territorio de Cals y ser el el juez' — 

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n196** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.3-4 · 1828-01-23 · Savage abstract · 

En.o 23: 'Enterado de haberse instalado Tribunal de Circuito, con jurisdiccion a Territorio de Cals y ser el el juez' — acknowledges the Circuit Court has been installed with jurisdiction over the Territory of the Californias and that the addressee is its judge.

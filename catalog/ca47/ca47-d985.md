**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n235** · Sav p.  · tomo p. — · 1828-12-06 · Savage abstract · 

LONG VERBATIM ROSTER (begins ~p.224; no inline cite; spans n235-n239 and continues past this range). Heading (a struck word 'Españoles' precedes it): 'Relación de los militares Españoles que conforme á ley 20 de Dice y órdenes posteriores, han prestado juramto con espresión de sus clases, edades, tiempo de permanencia en el país, servicios &.&.' A detailed biographical padrón of Spanish-born men who swore the oath under the 20 Dec anti-Spanish law. Entries within this range: (1) C. José Mª Estudillo, Capt. of the San Diego presidial company, age 56, 41 yrs in the Republic, 33 in the Territory, married to a Mexican with 6 children/12 grandchildren, swore Independence in 1822; (2) C. José Bandini, retired militia captain (despacho 10 May 1822), married twice in Lima, commanded the frigate 'Reina de los Ángeles' — 'este su buque fué el 1º que pr su voluntad arboló la bandera mexicana', age 57, ill with gout, recently settled from San Blas, profession the navy; (3) Padre Fr. Antonio Menéndez, Dominican, 12 yrs a missionary on the Baja Cal frontier, now provisional chaplain of the San Diego presidial company, age 43; (4) C. Manuel Gutiérrez, bachelor ranchero, 'capitalista de consideración en el país', age 82, 40 yrs in the Territory, of Pueblo de los Ángeles; (5) Vicente Cané, of Monterey jurisdiction, bachelor, marine of the ship 'Asia' (now 'Congreso'), age 34; (6) C. Juan Mariner, retired artilleryman (Tente de premio), of Los Ángeles, casa propia, age >60, 33 in Territory; (7) a second Manuel Gutiérrez, bachelor labrador, age 45, 7 yrs in Territory, of Los Ángeles with vineyard of 3000 vines; (8) Franco Cásares, age 36, 11 yrs in Territory, of Monterey; (9) C. José Amesti, age 36, in Territory since 1821, Monterey jurisdiction; (10) Esteban Munras, of Monterey/San José, age 39, 8 yrs in Territory. Roster continues beyond n239.

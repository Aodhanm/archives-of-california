# (INCOMING; 3 docs) 'Remision y recibo de docs — Qe. ha descubierto una conspiracion qe. los del Territorio fraguaban con

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n211** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.55-6 · 1829-06-23 · Savage abstract · 

(INCOMING; 3 docs) 'Remision y recibo de docs — Qe. ha descubierto una conspiracion qe. los del Territorio fraguaban contra los forasteros, ó qe. ellos llaman de la otra banda, pretendiendo deponer á aquellos qe. tienen algunos empleos pr. el Gobno. Gral' — reports discovery of a conspiracy the Territory's natives were plotting against the outsiders (those they call 'de la otra banda'), to depose govt-appointed officeholders; an artilleryman and a repentant cavalry soldier revealed the plan; with the plaza commander & officers he set up a defense, 3 nights under arms, and the conspirators did not dare rise in open rebellion.

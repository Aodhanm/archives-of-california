**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n213** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.67-8 · 1828-01-11 · Savage abstract · 

(2 docs) 'Trascribe oficio de Comte. de Compa. de Sta Barba. en qe. acompaña cuenta de lo suministrado pr. Habo. á individuos de la Maestranza pa. compostura de cureñas y cañones' — transcribes the Sta Bárbara company commander's office enclosing an account of supplies furnished by the habilitado to the Maestranza workmen for repair of gun-carriages and cannon; likewise a second enclosing the habilitado's account of supplies to the San Blas Commissary.

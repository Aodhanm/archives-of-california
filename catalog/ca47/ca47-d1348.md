# (2 docs) 'Qe se observe estrictamte su órden circular 29 Eno en qe prohibe qe se permita á ningun estrangero internarse 

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n342** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.597-8 · 1828-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

(2 docs) 'Qe se observe estrictamte su órden circular 29 Eno en qe prohibe qe se permita á ningun estrangero internarse en el Territorio, bajo ningun pretexto' — that his circular order of 29 January [1828] be strictly observed, prohibiting any foreigner from penetrating the interior of the Territory under any pretext.

# 'Entregar el mando de la Comp.a a Ten.te Ign.o Martinez, p.r su solicitud en q.e manifiesta estar enfermo, no poder cons

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n307** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.484-5 · 1828-10-07 · Savage abstract · 

'Entregar el mando de la Comp.a a Ten.te Ign.o Martinez, p.r su solicitud en q.e manifiesta estar enfermo, no poder conseguir recursos p.a la guarnicion, q.e hta se ha visto precisada a mendigar el pan, y ha quedado reducida a Cap.n, Ten.te y Alferez Graduado.' Gloss: hand company command to Lt. Ignacio Martinez per Arguello's request; Arguello is ill and cannot provision the garrison, which has even been forced to beg for bread and is reduced to a captain, lieutenant and graduated alferez.

# 'Que se sirva abstenerse del conocimto de la causa de Alferez Ramirez pr bigamia, pr qe la Cedula Rl 5 Febo 1770, no der

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n395** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.813-14 · 1828-03-31 · Savage abstract · 

'Que se sirva abstenerse del conocimto de la causa de Alferez Ramirez pr bigamia, pr qe la Cedula Rl 5 Febo 1770, no derogada, se lo prohibe' — to desist from taking cognizance of Alferez Ramirez's bigamy case, because the unrepealed Royal Cedula of 5 Feb 1770 forbids it.

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n210** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.50-3 · 1829-04-22 · Savage abstract · 

(INCOMING; 6 docs) 'Remision de docs. militares. Falta total de semillas pa. alimento de la tropa: los Ps. se niegan á facilitarlas' — remittal of military docs; total lack of grain to feed the troops, the missionary Fathers refuse to supply it; and that the Comte. of San Francisco charged a 2nd habilitación on the detached artillery troop without being a habilitado.

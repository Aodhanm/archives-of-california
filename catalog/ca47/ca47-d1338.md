# 'Haber destinado á otras Compañias á soldados qe elevaron representacion contra él, sin poder probar los cargos qe le hi

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n340** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.589 · 1828-11-24 · Savage abstract · 

'Haber destinado á otras Compañias á soldados qe elevaron representacion contra él, sin poder probar los cargos qe le hicieron' — has sent to other companies the soldiers who raised a representation against him without being able to prove the charges they made.

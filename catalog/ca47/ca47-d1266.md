# 'No serle posible conceder permiso pa qe Bergantin Ingles 'Buitre' arribe á Pto de Sn Luis Obispo, Cojo &c' — that it is

**C-A 47 (Departmental Records), scan n323** · Sav p.  · tomo p. p.531 · 1828-11-07 · Savage abstract · 

'No serle posible conceder permiso pa qe Bergantin Ingles 'Buitre' arribe á Pto de Sn Luis Obispo, Cojo &c' — that it is not possible to grant permission for the English brig Buitre to put in at the port of San Luis Obispo, Cojo, etc.

# Communicates supreme order of 28 Aug: Spaniards arriving at Republic's ports as captains/supercargos/pilots of friendly 

**C-A 40 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury)), scan n106** · Sav p. 106 · tomo p. 105 · 1828 Set 11 · Savage abstract · 

Communicates supreme order of 28 Aug: Spaniards arriving at Republic's ports as captains/supercargos/pilots of friendly or neutral-nation ships may land only for cargo business under authorities' bond, must re-embark, may not stay ashore or intern in the Republic (pp.887–8).

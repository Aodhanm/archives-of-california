# On export of fine metals & coin, & vigilance over the missionaries: since there is no caja de ensaye, provisionally char

**C-A 40 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury)), scan n111–n112** · Sav p. 111–112 · tomo p. p.110–111 · 1828 Oct 5 · Savage abstract · 

On export of fine metals & coin, & vigilance over the missionaries: since there is no caja de ensaye, provisionally charge 10% all-duties on gold/silver bullion export, watching esp. exports/coin made by the Padres Misioneros & others; requires bond & guías reported within 90 days by land post (pp.955–6, 958).

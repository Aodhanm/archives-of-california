# 'Sobre toma de razon de despachos de Ofic.s' [on the registration of officers' commissions]. Returns the despachos of Ca

**C-A 40 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury)), scan n74** · Sav p. 75 / 74 · tomo p. pp 541-2. · 1827-05-06 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre toma de razon de despachos de Ofic.s' [on the registration of officers' commissions]. Returns the despachos of Capn Jose de la Guerra y Noriega; Tentes Jose Ma Estudillo, Ignacio Martinez and Juan Mariner; and Alfereces Jose Joaqn Maitorena and Jose Sanchez, 'para que los acompanen con copias en papel sellado, pa que quede en el archivo' [so they may be accompanied by copies on stamped paper for the archive]. Before receiving their new despachos they must pay the cost of the papel sellado on which they are to be entered.

# 'Sobre nombramto de Comiso subalto en San Franco.' Notifies him that he has appointed Joaqn Estudillo 'Admor de Correos 

**C-A 40 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury)), scan n75** · Sav p. 76 / 75 · tomo p. pp 590-1. · 1827-07-14 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre nombramto de Comiso subalto en San Franco.' Notifies him that he has appointed Joaqn Estudillo 'Admor de Correos y Comiso Subalto en San Fco' [Administrator of Posts and Subaltern Comisario at San Francisco] and asks that he be recognized as such by the civil and military authorities.

# Makes known that foreigners Richard Exter and Julian Wilson, on their petition, have Supmo Gob permission to hunt fine-f

**C-A 40 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia (Commissary & Treasury)), scan n107** · Sav p. 107 · tomo p. 106 · 1828 Agt 7 · Savage abstract · 

Makes known that foreigners Richard Exter and Julian Wilson, on their petition, have Supmo Gob permission to hunt fine-fur animals & export pelts under bond of alcabalas; hunting in the north (New Mexico & Alta California); requires exact count/size/class of pelts (pp.897–911).

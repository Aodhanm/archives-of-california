# Heading verbatim: 'Al Alferez de Loreto Yldefonso Bernal' Entry verbatim: 'Mayo 31 - Sobre pago de sueldos a Ten.te reti

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n193** · Sav p. 192 / 192. · tomo p. p. 433 · 1794-05-31 · verbatim transcription · 

Heading verbatim: 'Al Alferez de Loreto Yldefonso Bernal' Entry verbatim: 'Mayo 31 - Sobre pago de sueldos a Ten.te retirado D.n Diego Gonzales - p. 4333.' GLOSS: same day, to the alferez of Loreto Ildefonso Bernal, on paying the salary of the retired lieutenant Diego Gonzales. The page cite on the leaf reads 4333, four digits, which I take to be a slip of Savage pen for 433 since every neighbouring entry on this leaf cites 432-433; I have not verified that against another copy.

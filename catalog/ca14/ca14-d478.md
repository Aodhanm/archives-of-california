Heading verbatim: Marzo 5 (11 Docs) - pp. 326-9. Gloss: Arrillaga to the Viceroy, 5 March 1794, abstract of eleven documents, running from the foot of corner 133 across corner 134 and onto the upper half of corner 135. Substance: remits the state of armament and artillery in the presidios, noting what needs repair; remits drafts of the tobacco branch for 91 and 92; that under the order of 17 de Set.e directing him to hand the command to Ten.te Coronel Diego de Borica, nombrado Gob. p.r su Magestad, he has directed the Com.te Int.o de Loreto to carry it out in his place; that it has been the practice for governors to put the visto bueno on drafts but, being at a great distance from Loreto, the Com.te Int.o had to endorse the $4.662 draft the Hab.o Gral. drew in favour of the Factoria de Guadalajara; that with the order of 5 Oct.e (93) he received the despacho of Ten.te de Pres.o S.n Diego in favour of Ant.o Grajera; that he received the R.l Cedula of 27 Feb.o granting viceroys and governors the faculty to license militiamen and private persons to pass to Spain; that he remits the packets sent for the Com.te de Espedicion Inglesa de Limites G. Vancouver, quien se encuentra en Sandwich; that he received the order of 29 Oct.e with the ruling of the Junta Sup.r de R.l Hda on the $39.721 that the memorias sent by the Comandancia de S.n Blas to the presidios of the Californias amounted to; that Sta Barb.a has advanced more than $8.000 in necessary supplies and Monterey $7.000 y mas; that he remits the general extracts of 88 and 9 of the Presidio de Loreto; that he received the R.l orden of 20 Julio (93) prohibiting the introduction of alhajas, ropas, o estampas q.e tengan pinturas o inscripciones alusivas a la deprabada libertad de Francia; and the remission of the accounts of iron and steel received and spent from En.o de 81 hasta Dic.e de 91.

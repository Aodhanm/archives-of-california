# Verbatim: '(4 Docs)': fuga de un indio de la Frag.a *Concepción*; receipt at San Blas of THE FIVE ENGLISH DESERTERS conf

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n196** · Sav p. 195 [stamp] / 195. [hand] · tomo p. p.xxi.447–8 · 1794-07-09 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: '(4 Docs)': fuga de un indio de la Frag.a *Concepción*; receipt at San Blas of THE FIVE ENGLISH DESERTERS confirmed; by the Virrey's order the troop of the Compañía Fija that Juan Matute left at S.n Francisco handed over, as of the 1st, to Cap.n Salvador Menéndez of the *Concepción*; sobre trabajos de artesanos. (Eliza now commands San Blas — Quadra dead, cf. 'el finado Cuadra' n212.)

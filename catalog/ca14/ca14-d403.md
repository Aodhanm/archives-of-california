**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n299** · Sav p. 297 [stamp] / 3 [hand] · tomo p. p. 4 · 1775-01-02 · Savage abstract · 

“Otro del mismo al mismo aprobándole ‘la mudada del presidio á las orillas del rio.’” [= another from the same to the same approving the removal of the presidio to the banks of the river]. Which presidio is NOT stated in the índice — no identification asserted. doc 2 of 2 under this date; Savage gives one page cite for both pieces.

# Verbatim: 'Participa q.e el dia 4 arribo a S.n Jose del Cabo la Fragata Britanica -La Resolucion,- mandada p.r Juan Lock

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n2** · Sav p. 3 [stamp] / 2. [hand] · tomo p. p. 43 · 1795-11-07 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Participa q.e el dia 4 arribo a S.n Jose del Cabo la Fragata Britanica -La Resolucion,- mandada p.r Juan Locke (sic), 22 tripulantes; q.e ya habia estado antes en la Bahia de Todos Santos, ocupada en la pesca de ballena, q.e les produjo mas de 500 barriles de aceite. p. 43-'. Gloss: reports the 4th arrival at San Jose del Cabo of the British frigate Resolution under Juan Locke [Savage's own '(sic)'], 22 crew, previously in Bahia de Todos Santos whaling, with a take of over 500 barrels of oil. Foreign-vessel intelligence.

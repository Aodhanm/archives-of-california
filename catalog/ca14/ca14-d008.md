# Verbatim: 'Informa q.e la Fragata Britanica -Resolucion,- segun declaracion del Cap.n p.r medio de interprete, hacia tre

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n3** · Sav p. 4 [stamp] / 3. [hand] · tomo p. pp. 44-5 · 1796-04-08 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Informa q.e la Fragata Britanica -Resolucion,- segun declaracion del Cap.n p.r medio de interprete, hacia tres anos q.e habia salido de Londres con el objeto de la pesca de ballena: q.e arribo a los establecim.tos Portugueses, paso al Asia y alli tuvo noticia q.e en las Costas de Cal abundaba: q.e iba regresarse a Europa p.r el Cabo Buena Esperanza, no habiendo encontrado ballena de la clase q.e deseaba: q.e pregunto p.r el valor de los efectos y se informo de la riqueza del pais &c &c. - pp. 44-5.' Gloss: the interrogation, through an interpreter, of the master of the Resolution: three years out of London whaling, touched at the Portuguese establishments, went on to Asia where he heard whales abounded off the California coasts, was returning to Europe by the Cape of Good Hope not having found the class of whale he wanted, and had asked the price of goods and informed himself of the wealth of the country. Second half of the Resolution file begun at n2.

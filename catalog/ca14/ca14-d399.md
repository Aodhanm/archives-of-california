# “El Virey ordenó á Phelipe de Neve pasase á relevar á Phelipe Barry en el gobᵒ de Calˢ.” [= Neve ordered to go relieve F

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n298** · Sav p. 296 [stamp] / 2 [hand] · tomo p. p. 3 · 1774-10-28 · Savage abstract · 

“El Virey ordenó á Phelipe de Neve pasase á relevar á Phelipe Barry en el gobᵒ de Calˢ.” [= Neve ordered to go relieve Felipe Barri in the governorship of the Californias]. Savage writes the governor's surname ‘Barry’; the Spanish original form is normally Barri — transcribed as written, no correction made.

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n300** · Sav p. 298 [stamp] / 4 [hand] · tomo p. p. 16 · 1778-09-03 · Savage abstract · 

“Un oficio del Comandᵗᵉ Genˡ al Gobᵉʳ Neve aprobando fundᶜⁿ de San José Guadalupe, ‘y llamada á Arizpe al Capⁿ Dⁿ Fernando de Rivera y Moncada.’” [= approving the founding of San José de Guadalupe and summoning Rivera y Moncada to Arizpe]. doc 1 of 3 under this date — Savage marks the other two only as “Otro del mismo al mismo”, with a single page cite for all three.

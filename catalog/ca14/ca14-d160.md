# LAND DISTRIBUTION RULES: Suertes de tierra dimensions; tax of ½ fanega maíz; vecinos as domiciliados; 5 years + 1 day → 

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n177–n178** · Sav p.  · tomo p. xxii.410–11 · 1793-12-29 · Savage abstract · 

LAND DISTRIBUTION RULES: Suertes de tierra dimensions; tax of ½ fanega maíz; vecinos as domiciliados; 5 years + 1 day → eligible for state offices; abandoning suertes = forfeiture of ALL property; retired military exempt from taxes while alive (heirs pay). República offices voluntary for military

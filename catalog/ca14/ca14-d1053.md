# CRITICAL INTELLIGENCE: Tobar on goleta *Sutil* explored coast Bucareli→Monterey. May 28: sighted frigate ~15 leagues fro

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n244–245** · Sav p.  · tomo p. ~xxii.612–13 · 1796-07-07 · Savage abstract · 

CRITICAL INTELLIGENCE: Tobar on goleta *Sutil* explored coast Bucareli→Monterey. May 28: sighted frigate ~15 leagues from Paso de Martín de Aguilar / Columbia River — fog prevented examination. At Nootka June 15: Indians said Broughton there 10 days prior. June 19: American frigate 'El otro Boston' Capt. Bour (sic) presented Spanish consular passports. Intelligence: English brigantine had orders from Court to harass Spaniards; frigates from England bound to found establishment at Nootka; Americans planned settlement at 54°5'N with two ships

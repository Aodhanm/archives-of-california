**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n191** · Sav p. 190 [stamp] / 190. [hand] · tomo p. p. 429 · Nov.e 14, year not stated on the leaf; probably 1793 (see issues), inference not verified · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: Nov.e 14. (2 Docs) - p. 429. Standing under the same recipient heading, Arrillaga a Oficiales Reales del Rosario. Substance verbatim in part: Remite cuenta de diezmos de Jurisdiccion de Mont. de 1792. - Que Ten.te de Comp.a Presidial de Mont, Diego Gonzales, ha logrado su despacho de retiro en calidad de disperso. Gloss: forwards the account of tithes of the Monterey jurisdiction for 1792, and reports that the lieutenant of the Monterey presidial company, Diego Gonzales, has obtained his retirement papers in the status of disperso. The Diego Gonzales retirement is the person-layer item; the export has no ca14 record naming him.

# Verbatim (personal): arrived at the capital 8 months after leaving home; 'ya no quiere ser pretendiente' (Savage: earlie

**C-A 14 (Provincial State Papers), scan n207** · Sav p. 206 [stamp] / 206. [hand] · tomo p. p.xxi.530–1 · 1794-11-13 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim (personal): arrived at the capital 8 months after leaving home; 'ya no quiere ser pretendiente' (Savage: earlier spoke of $10,000 remitted to Cádiz); the Sonora government would never suit him without its annexed office, 'p.r q.e rebajarian el sueldo'; directs Miranda's funds to Yribarren; expects José de las Heras to have remitted the $20,000 on Ant.o Cordero's account, freeing Borica of responsibility. Companion to the Sonora-pretension letters on n216.

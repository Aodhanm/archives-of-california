# Instruction for the inspection of the mission escorts (Carmelo→Soledad→S. Antonio→S. Luis), to be run by Tte. Parrilla w

**C-A 7 (Provincial State Papers), scan n142–n143** · Sav p. 139–140 · tomo p. 370–371 · 1794-12-11 · Savage abstract · 

Instruction for the inspection of the mission escorts (Carmelo→Soledad→S. Antonio→S. Luis), to be run by Tte. Parrilla w/ Alf. Hermenegildo Sal & relief men: inspect by night the guard, sentinel, arms/munition, dress & mounts; art. 7 — treat the Indians 'sostenido, sin [ofenderlos?] en lo más mínimo, bien sean cristianos ó gentiles'; keep a circumstantial *diario*; if the Teniente is incapacitated, Sal takes over (art. 9); the Comandante to act 'con messura' so neither Padres, troops, nor Indians are aggrieved (art. 10)

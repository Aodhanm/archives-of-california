**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n430** · Sav p. 430 [stamp] / 51 [hand] · tomo p. p.482-483 · 1843-03-15 · Savage abstract · 

Same heading block (1843 = Angeles / Micheltorena al Prefecto). Entry: Marzo 15 = Que haga alistar 600 novillos para la goleta California. Gloss: orders 600 steers made ready for the schooner California. Ship name given verbatim in Savage quotation marks. Complements the 200 novillos for Santa Barbara at n429 p. 470-471.

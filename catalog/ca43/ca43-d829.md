# Marked. No subject rubric given. Order of the Gob.o Sup.mo 'que no se permita la esportacion de numerario libre de derec

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n448** · Sav p. 448 [stamp] / 2 [hand] · tomo p. pp 9-10 · 1840-03-02 · Savage abstract · 

Marked. No subject rubric given. Order of the Gob.o Sup.mo 'que no se permita la esportacion de numerario libre de derechos, sea p.a gastos de pasageros o de buques' [no duty-free export of specie, whether on account of passengers' or ships' expenses]. Bears directly on Monterey's trade with foreign vessels.

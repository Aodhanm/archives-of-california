# Heading verbatim: '1833 José M.a Garayalos Secret.o de la Corte sup.a al Juez de 1.a inst.a de Monte.' Entry verbatim: '

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n353** · Sav p. 353 [stamp] / 126 [hand] · tomo p. p.547 · 1833-12-18 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '1833 José M.a Garayalos Secret.o de la Corte sup.a al Juez de 1.a inst.a de Monte.' Entry verbatim: 'D.bre 18. Remite la causa de Ant.o Hernandez p.r homicidio p.a su cumplimiento. pide recibo'. Gloss: the cause of Antonio Hernández for homicide is returned to Monterey for execution of the sentence, with a receipt requested. Surname read as Garayalos on the leaf; the reading is not certain.

# MUTINY of the Los Angeles garrison, night of 15 Sep 1845: after the Independence-day salva the troops disowned Comand.te

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n199** · Sav p. 199 [stamp] / 109 bis [hand] · tomo p. p.284 · 1845-09-30 · Savage abstract · 

MUTINY of the Los Angeles garrison, night of 15 Sep 1845: after the Independence-day salva the troops disowned Comand.te José Antonio Carrillo and Capitán José Carrillo; order restored by naming Teniente de Caballería Antonio Somosa to command the plaza company. Verbatim: 'se le dio parte de un motin militar por la guarnicion de la Capital (Ang.s) desconociendo la tropa a sus Jefes'. Military-register candidate.

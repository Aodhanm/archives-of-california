# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim: 'Ag.to 10 Dice que el Tribunal

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n361** · Sav p. 361 [stamp] / 134 [hand] · tomo p. p. 572 · 1842-08-10 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim: 'Ag.to 10 Dice que el Tribunal esta enterado de haber pasado por el Juzgado de su Cargo al Comand.te militar las primeras diligencias practicadas en averiguacion del asesinato Cometido en la persona de Martin Vela cuyos indicios aparecen Contra el Soldado Jose Mojica'. Gloss: the court forwarded the first proceedings in the murder of Martin Vela to the military commander, the evidence pointing to the soldier Jose Mojica. An earlier stage of the case whose 22 July entry (p.574) is already catalogued at n362.

# Heading: 'Dec.o de 23 Jul. 1833 de Farias sobre empleados españoles'. Arts. 1-9 transcribed verbatim; spans n56-n57. Art

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n56** · Sav p. 57 [stamp] / 9 [hand] · tomo p. p. 104 · 1833 Jul 23 (pub. Monterey 1834 Feb 4) · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 'Dec.o de 23 Jul. 1833 de Farias sobre empleados españoles'. Arts. 1-9 transcribed verbatim; spans n56-n57. Art. 1: 'Los españoles que tengan cargo ó empleo de provision de los Poderes federales, se consideraran en lo sucesivo como retirados ó jubilados' [Spaniards holding federal office to be treated as retired/pensioned]. Arts. 2-3 graduate pensions by years served and deny double campaign credit to Spaniards who fought against independence; art. 6 exempts those who helped deliver the ship 'Asia'; art. 9: 'Por este decreto no se deroga el de 3 Mayo 1833.' Doc 7 of the 9-doc 1834 Feb 4 Figueroa block.

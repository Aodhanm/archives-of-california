# Calendar entry; marginal mark 'v.'. Savage: 'Decreto de Gomez Farias reformando el Art. 10 del decreto de 4 de Feb. ulti

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n128** · Sav p. 128 [stamp] / 42 [hand] · tomo p. p. 109 · 1834-04-11 · Savage abstract · 

Calendar entry; marginal mark 'v.'. Savage: 'Decreto de Gomez Farias reformando el Art. 10 del decreto de 4 de Feb. ultimo, expedido a consecuencia de la ley de 16 de Abril 1830 sobre Colonizacion.' [= Gomez Farias amends art. 10 of the decree of 4 Feb. (1834) issued under the colonization law.] NOTE: Savage's figure plainly reads '16 de Abril 1830'; the Mexican colonization law is dated 6 April 1830, so this is probably a slip in Savage or his original. Recorded verbatim, not corrected.

# Prefect Castro publishes by bando a communication from the interim Governor transcribing Gov. Alvarado's, which in turn 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n165** · Sav p. 165 [stamp] / 78 [hand] · tomo p. p. 193 · 1839-09-28 · Savage abstract · 

Prefect Castro publishes by bando a communication from the interim Governor transcribing Gov. Alvarado's, which in turn inserted the Minister of the Interior's note of August, quoted verbatim: 'Exmo. Sr. Hoy se traslada al Consejo de Gob.o en consulta la nota de V.E. de 9 de Marzo ult.o y se incluye el testimonio que acompañó de las actas de la Junta Dptal. q. contienen los acuerdos sobre elecciones de Diputados al Cong.o gral. y de indivíduos á la Junta Dptal.' [= Mexico City acknowledges the Junta's election acts and refers them to the Council of Government]. Marginal '+'.

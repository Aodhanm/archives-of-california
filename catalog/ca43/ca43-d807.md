# Secularization-era neophyte emancipation (mechanics under Figueroa): the neophytes Félix Mendoza & Luis Romero denied th

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n408** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.247–258 · 1834 · Savage abstract · 

Secularization-era neophyte emancipation (mechanics under Figueroa): the neophytes Félix Mendoza & Luis Romero denied their *carta de emancipación* (for having gambled, p.247–48); Asunción Bocanegra's son released from Mission San Miguel to join him (p.249–50); ⭐ *carta de emancipación* granted to the neophyte Juan José (p.256); + form causa against José M.ª Blanquel & Hipólito Mejía, who escaped from Esteban Munras's rancho taking arms and the best horses (p.257–58).

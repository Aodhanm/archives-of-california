# Peralta: the Contra Costa lands 'no tienen medidas fijas,' keeping vecinos in continual disputes — proposes commissionin

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n332** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 430–436 · 1839 · Savage abstract · 

Peralta: the Contra Costa lands 'no tienen medidas fijas,' keeping vecinos in continual disputes — proposes commissioning someone to distribute lands & set mojoneras (boundary markers) per law, with two witnesses, diligencia in duplicate (p.430–1); Guerrero: on diezmos (tithes) — some refuse to pay (p.435–6). East Bay land-tenure + tithe resistance.

# Peninsula Indian raid: consults whether he may request from Comandante Francisco Sánchez the aid of the Dolores detachme

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n326** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.408–409 · 1839 · Savage abstract · 

Peninsula Indian raid: consults whether he may request from Comandante Francisco Sánchez the aid of the Dolores detachment — the vecindario being small and 'los indios [están] llegando á S. Mateo, habiéndose llevado la caballada del Rancho de las Pulgas' (the Indians reaching San Mateo, having carried off the Rancho de las Pulgas horse-herd). → indian-raiding register.

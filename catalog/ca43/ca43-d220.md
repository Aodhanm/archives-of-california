# Heading '1834 Feb. 4 Monterey / Figueroa — Sobre alcaldes'. 'Publica el sig.te dec.o de 5 Ag.o 1833 de Farias'. Arts. 1-

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n66** · Sav p. 67 [stamp] / 19 [hand] · tomo p. p. 136-7 · 1833 Ag.o 5 (pub. Monterey 1834 Feb 4) · Savage abstract · 

Heading '1834 Feb. 4 Monterey / Figueroa — Sobre alcaldes'. 'Publica el sig.te dec.o de 5 Ag.o 1833 de Farias'. Arts. 1-8, spans n66-n67; arts. 1-3 concern the federal city only. Art. 4: prisoners taken in the other municipalities of the federal District and Territories to be lodged in their jails 'á disposicion de los alcaldes const.les' who, for causes under arts. 9 cap. 2, 11 cap. 3 and 5 cap. 4 of the decree of 9 Oct. 1812, may take cognizance and determine them. Art. 5: alcaldes to decide causes within fifteen natural days on pain of public reprimand in the newspapers. Art. 6: monthly lists of causes to the gobernador or jefe político. Art. 7: alcalde jurisdiction ceases once the juez de 1a instancia takes cognizance. Art. 8: penalties enforced by the governor or jefes políticos.

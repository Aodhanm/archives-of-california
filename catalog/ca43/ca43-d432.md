# Governor publishes by bando his decree convoking extraordinary sessions: 'Publica por Bando su decreto, en q. convoca á 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n163** · Sav p. 163 [stamp] / 76 [hand] · tomo p. p. 191 · 1839-02-20 · Savage abstract · 

Governor publishes by bando his decree convoking extraordinary sessions: 'Publica por Bando su decreto, en q. convoca á Sesiones Extraords. p.a el 25 del corriente á las 9 de la mañana, á la Junta Territorial p.a erigirse en Departamental.' [= summons the Territorial Junta for the 25th at 9 a.m. so that it may constitute itself as the Departmental Junta, under the 1836 centralist laws]. Marginal '+'.

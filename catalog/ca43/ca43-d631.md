# Land-denouncement file rejected. Verbatim (Savage's whole abstract): 'Expediente de denuncio de terrenos no admitida'. G

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n282** · Sav p. 282 [stamp] / 56 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 217-18 · 1841-08-23 · Savage abstract · 

Land-denouncement file rejected. Verbatim (Savage's whole abstract): 'Expediente de denuncio de terrenos no admitida'. Gloss: the government returns/refuses to admit a denuncio (claim/denunciation) file on vacant land forwarded by the interim prefect of the 2nd District; Savage gives no claimant or tract name. Marginal '+'.

# Publishes by bando the decree of the Cong. Gral. of 15 July 1839: 'los Tribunales Superiores de los Departamtos. nombrar

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n154** · Sav p. 154 [stamp] / 68 [hand] · tomo p. p. 186 · 1839-12-22 · Savage abstract · 

Publishes by bando the decree of the Cong. Gral. of 15 July 1839: 'los Tribunales Superiores de los Departamtos. nombraran en sala plena ... un numero de suplentes igual al de sus Ministros y fiscal propietarios' [= departmental superior tribunals to name, in full session, alternates equal in number to their ministers and fiscal, the day after receiving the decree and thereafter each 31 December].

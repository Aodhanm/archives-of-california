# Ruling that the office of Juez de 1ª Instancia is vested in the Alcalde 1º (and, in default, whoever performs those func

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n372** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 634+ · 1844-10 · Savage abstract · 

Ruling that the office of Juez de 1ª Instancia is vested in the Alcalde 1º (and, in default, whoever performs those functions) — alcalde-as-judge; + the Tribunal's procedure for its obligatory weekly Saturday *visita de cárcel* under art. 16 of the 28 Feb 1834 decree (asking each Friday whether the Saturday visit can be made).

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n141** · Sav p. 141 [stamp] / 55 [hand] · tomo p. p. 153 · 1835-09-09 · Savage abstract · 

Marginal mark 'v'. Heading: '1835.- Set. 9.- Mejico.' Abstract: 'Decreto & declarando el Cong. Gral. investido por la Nacion de amplias facultades, aun para variar la forma de Gobierno y constituirla de nuevo.' [Gloss: the general Congress is declared invested by the Nation with ample powers, even to alter the form of government and constitute it anew - the enabling act of the centralist turn.]

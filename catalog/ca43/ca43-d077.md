# Resolution of the Branciforte affair (Doc 76): the ayuntamiento named Rafael Castro and Branciforte remains aggregated t

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n394** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 90–94 · 1836-01-30 / 02-03 · Savage abstract · 

Resolution of the Branciforte affair (Doc 76): the ayuntamiento named Rafael Castro and Branciforte remains aggregated to the Monterey ayuntamiento — noting the election had only '6 ó 7 votantes, la mayoría del pueblo sin votar' (p.90–91); ⭐ the estate of the late Gov. José Figueroa liquidated — Bonifacio Madariaga sold 5 of his silver plates ($100 valued, only $60 paid, p.93–94).

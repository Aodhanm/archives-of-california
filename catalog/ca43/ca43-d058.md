# Dolores/SF vs Pueblo de Alvarado (San José) jurisdiction-boundary dispute: the encargados de justicia José Antonio [Alvi

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n342** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 477–480 · 1841-04-29 · Savage abstract · 

Dolores/SF vs Pueblo de Alvarado (San José) jurisdiction-boundary dispute: the encargados de justicia José Antonio [Alviso] & Juan (John) Coppinger at San Francisquito and the Rancho de las Pulgas sawmill are being pulled before the Alvarado juez; Guerrero consults on re-running the dividing line per the 1834 decree, with a neighboring-judge citation and an *agrimensor* (surveyor), the jurisdiction shrinking daily.

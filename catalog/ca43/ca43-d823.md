# Marked +. Heading: 'Sobre derechos de los tejidos de algodon.' Pavon notifies a Congress decree communicated by the Mini

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n441** · Sav p. 441 [stamp] / 7 [hand] · tomo p. pp 452-3 · 1837-06-03 · Savage abstract · 

Marked +. Heading: 'Sobre derechos de los tejidos de algodon.' Pavon notifies a Congress decree communicated by the Ministerio de Hacienda dated 23 May: 'deben pagar en el puerto los tejidos de algodon estrangeros, a su internacion, 4 centavos de peso por vara cuadrada, ademas de los derechos de arancel' [foreign cottons pay 4 centavos per square vara on internation, over and above tariff]; and extends to the whole Republic the decree of 28 Feb 1828 freeing nationally made cotton, wool and silk cloth, home-grown cotton and its yarns from all duty.

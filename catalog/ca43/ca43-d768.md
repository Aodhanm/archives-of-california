# Judicial independence: on a *cartilla/instrucción* some judges sought via the Governor, the Tribunal rules the juzgados 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n366** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.585–587 · 1844-05-13 · Savage abstract · 

Judicial independence: on a *cartilla/instrucción* some judges sought via the Governor, the Tribunal rules the juzgados govern by the 11 May 1837 decree and finds it strange (*extrañando*) that judges address the Governor when they should address the Tribunal directly (p.585–6); + remits the Tomás Pacheco vs Pedro Chaboya dispute over the terreno de los Cerritos (Micheltorena sending it to proof, p.586–7).

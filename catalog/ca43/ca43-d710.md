# Municipal ordinance of the Pueblo de Dolores (arts. 5–7): no branding in the field or out of season without notice to th

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n328** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.413–415 · 1839-06-25 · Savage abstract · 

Municipal ordinance of the Pueblo de Dolores (arts. 5–7): no branding in the field or out of season without notice to the Juzgado/administrator, no sale of *reses orejanas* without proof of ownership; vecinos must attend rodeos; wood-extraction/desmonte pays 4 reales per carreta leaving the pueblo's demarcation (p.413); + Guerrero's consult on wood-cutting for extraction beyond the jurisdiction. Early SF municipal governance.

# 'Ano de 1843 / Aduana maritima de Monterey. Declaraciones de pasajeros estranjeros.' = Monterey maritime customs house, 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n6** · Sav p. 7 [stamp] / 6 [hand] · tomo p. p. 78 · 1843 · Savage abstract · 

'Ano de 1843 / Aduana maritima de Monterey. Declaraciones de pasajeros estranjeros.' = Monterey maritime customs house, declarations of foreign passengers, 1843. Marked '0' in the margin, not the usual '+'. It sits out of sequence at p.78 inside an otherwise 18th-century alcabala file, so I read it as a wrapper/cover sheet bound in; rowed per the no-bounding ruling. HIGH PRIORITY: customs house + foreigners.

# spans n191-n192. 'Bando prohibiendo pasquines.' Verbatim: 'Ha notado este Gobo. y Comandancia General con bastante desag

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n191–n192** · Sav p. 191 [stamp] / 104 [hand] · tomo p. p. 273 · 1844-02-04 · verbatim transcription · 

spans n191-n192. 'Bando prohibiendo pasquines.' Verbatim: 'Ha notado este Gobo. y Comandancia General con bastante desagrado, que en estos dias han aparecido algunos pasquines injuriosos á clases y personas que reciprocamente deben respetarse'; grants popular action so any citizen may denounce the authors. Penalties: 1o an author who is a civil or military employee suffers suspension and loss of half pay for three months, under art. 142 of the Bases Orgánicas; 2o a non-employee, a $100 fine or fifty days' prison; 3o whoever writes a libel dictated by another incurs the author's penalty; 4o using the Padre Nuestro or any prayer as a lampoon carries the same penalties; 5o on repetition the Gobierno or Comandancia General will act 'gubernativamente' with stronger measures. Marginal mark 'o'.

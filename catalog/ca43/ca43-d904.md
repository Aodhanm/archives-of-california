# Verbatim heading: '1833 = Diciembre 4 = Monterrey / Diego Feliz á J.é Figueroa'. Entry verbatim in full: 'Que siendo ino

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n405** · Sav p. 405 [stamp] / 26 [hand] · tomo p. p.224-225 · 1833-12-04 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1833 = Diciembre 4 = Monterrey / Diego Feliz á J.é Figueroa'. Entry verbatim in full: 'Que siendo inocente del robo por el cual está en carcel, solicita activen su causa y le den mientras con que comer.' Gloss: a prisoner petition. Diego Feliz, protesting his innocence of the theft for which he is jailed, asks that his case be pushed forward and that meanwhile he be given something to eat. Savage adds immediately below, verbatim, 'p 226. (en blanco)'.

# Verbatim: 'Circular impresa de la Acta de Sesion de la Asamblea Dptal. fha. 15 del p[te.?] mes. (Vease p. 101 de estos e

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n190** · Sav p. 190 [stamp] / 103 [hand] · tomo p. p. 272 · 1844-08-28 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Circular impresa de la Acta de Sesion de la Asamblea Dptal. fha. 15 del p[te.?] mes. (Vease p. 101 de estos estractos.)' Gloss: printed circular of the minutes of the Departmental Assembly session of 15 August 1844. Savage's cross-reference to 'p. 101 de estos estractos' resolves to the handwritten folio 101 = leaf n188, where the same circular is entered at pp. 266-69 — a second original of the same document, separately paginated, so a separate row. Marginal mark 'v.'

# Verbatim: 'Bando determinando ciertas providencias de rigor, contra los revoltosos del orden publico, con motivo de un p

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n195** · Sav p. 195 [stamp] / 108 [hand] · tomo p. p. 275 · 1844-11-18 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Bando determinando ciertas providencias de rigor, contra los revoltosos del orden publico, con motivo de un pronunciamiento q. estalló en el Rio (Monterey.) (Vease p. 47 - Vol. X. Proclamations, - Inga.)' Gloss: measures of rigour against disturbers of public order on account of the pronunciamiento that broke out at 'el Rio' near Monterey, i.e. the opening of the November 1844 revolt against Micheltorena; countersigned by Secretary Manuel Jimeno. Savage cross-refers to an English-language copy at p. 47 of Vol. X of the Proclamations series. Marginal mark 'v.'

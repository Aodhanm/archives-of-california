**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n147** · Sav p. 147 [stamp] / 61 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 165-68 · 1836-05-20 · Savage abstract · 

Marginal 'v'; heading carries '(Dup. 2a)' and '(Val. Doc.)'. 'Discurso pronunciado por el Comandante Gral. D. Mariano Chico &. despues de tener lugar el acto del Juramento de las Bases Constitucionales.' [Gloss: Chico's speech following the ceremony of swearing the Bases Constitucionales - note Savage titles him Comandante General here, not Gefe Politico.] Date 20 May sits three days before the ceremony fixed for Sunday the 22nd by his bando of 19 May (next row but one) - flagged, not corrected.

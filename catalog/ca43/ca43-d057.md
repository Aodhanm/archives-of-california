# On raising the *plano* (plat) of the Establecimiento de Dolores to order the solares the Governor conceded to the vecind

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n338** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 460–464 · 1840-02-02 · Savage abstract · 

On raising the *plano* (plat) of the Establecimiento de Dolores to order the solares the Governor conceded to the vecindario, the center to be the Iglesia, requesting leave to recompose ruined ex-mission buildings the vecinos have occupied for years (p.460–1) — early town-of-Dolores urban planning; a suit over an orphans' claim (p.462–4); and a promise to act as soon as the transgression of the foreign-ship cabotage rules is observed (p.464).

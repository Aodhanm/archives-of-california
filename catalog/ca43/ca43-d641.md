# The solemn promulgation of the Bases Orgánicas de 1843 (per the 8 Jun decree): the Bases to be published next Sunday 'co

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n286** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.232–233 · 1843 · Savage abstract · 

The solemn promulgation of the Bases Orgánicas de 1843 (per the 8 Jun decree): the Bases to be published next Sunday 'con toda magnificencia' by a national bando through the customary streets — Prefect, secretary, Judge & Mayor General, troop escort, *repiques* and artillery salvos. Centralist-constitution civic ceremony.

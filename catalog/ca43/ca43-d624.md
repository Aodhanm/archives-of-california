# In compliance with the superior order of yesterday on the appointment of a Juez de Paz to discharge in this cabecera the

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n276** · Sav p. 276 [stamp] / 50 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 202-3 · 1841-07-31 · Savage abstract · 

In compliance with the superior order of yesterday on the appointment of a Juez de Paz to discharge in this cabecera the functions of the court of first instance, the Prefectura holds that C. José Z. Fernandez has every circumstance desirable for the charge, and asks that it be laid before the governor for approval. 'Esta Prefectura Cré que el C José Z. Fernandez, reune cuantas circunstancias se decean para este encargo.'

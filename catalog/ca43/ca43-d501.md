# 'Bando previniendo medidas de defensa contra los aventureros de Meric. (Vease p. 121 de estos estractos.) p. 300.' = a S

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n219** · Sav p. 219 [stamp] / 129 [hand] · tomo p. p. 300 · 1846-06-17 · Savage abstract · 

'Bando previniendo medidas de defensa contra los aventureros de Meric. (Vease p. 121 de estos estractos.) p. 300.' = a SECOND copy, at p. 300 of the original bound volume, of the 17 June 1846 defense bando against Merritt's adventurers. Savage himself cross-references his own transcript at internal page 121 - which is the handwritten folio of leaf n211, where the first copy is entered. Rowed separately per the no-bounding ruling: distinct entry, distinct original page cite. Marginal 'v.'

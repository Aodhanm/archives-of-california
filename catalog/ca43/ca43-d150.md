# After a short account of the expedition made under his orders by 'los dos buques rusos Nadeschda, Capitan Krusenstern, y

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n37** · Sav p. 38 [stamp] / 37 [hand] · tomo p. p.337-339 · 1806 Apr 20 · Savage abstract · 

After a short account of the expedition made under his orders by 'los dos buques rusos Nadeschda, Capitan Krusenstern, y Neva, Capitan Lissianski', Rezanov asks leave to buy, paying in pesos, 200 fanegas de trigo, cebada, chicharos y abas; 100 arrobas de arina, 50 de sal, 300 arrobas de mantequilla (Savage: the doc says trains cens arobes du beurre, supposing it meant trois cents arrobes de beurre) y otro tanto de sebo.

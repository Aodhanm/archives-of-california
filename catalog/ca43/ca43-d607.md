# spans n266-n267. Transmits a Ministerio de lo Interior communication with the President's order that Sundays be kept: ar

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n266–n267** · Sav p. 266 [stamp] / 40 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 168-70 · 1841-05-11 · verbatim transcription · 

spans n266-n267. Transmits a Ministerio de lo Interior communication with the President's order that Sundays be kept: artisans may not open shops, no one may do servile work except farm labourers, travellers not to be impeded; offenders admonished and, if they repeat, governor and prefects to use the discretion allowed by arts. 4 and 64 of the Ley de 20 de Marzo de 1837. 'que se guarden los domingos sin permitir que los artesanos abran sus oficinas, ní que ninguna persona se ocupe en trabajos serviles, ecepto los labradores.'

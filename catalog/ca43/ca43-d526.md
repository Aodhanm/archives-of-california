# Orders that without loss of a moment the elections referred to by the Juzgado de S. Juan B[autist]a [?] be held next Sun

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n236** · Sav p. 236 [stamp] / 10 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 30-1 · 1839-03-18 · Savage abstract · 

Orders that without loss of a moment the elections referred to by the Juzgado de S. Juan B[autist]a [?] be held next Sunday 'sin escusa ni pretesto algo', warning the Alcalde that 'aun cuando no hubieran el dia senaldo los compromisarios que menciona, la eleccion debia quedar hecha, pues segun la Ley basta el concurso de la mitad y uno mas'. Marginal 'o'. Juzgado abbreviation checked at scale=1, still slightly ambiguous.

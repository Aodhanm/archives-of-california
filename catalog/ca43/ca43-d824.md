# Marked. Heading: 'Sobre pagos de Militares, empleados civiles &a.' Spans n442-n443. General order of the Gob. Supremo on

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n442–n443** · Sav p. 442 [stamp] / 8 [hand] · tomo p. pp 478-81 · 1837-11-04 · Savage abstract · 

Marked. Heading: 'Sobre pagos de Militares, empleados civiles &a.' Spans n442-n443. General order of the Gob. Supremo on payment priority given the treasury's penury: 'primero y de toda preferencia se paguen los gastos de militares en activo servicio, hospitales militares, bagages, conducciones, movim.tos de tropas'; customs and collecting offices then pay their own staff, excluding pensions, montepios, jubilaciones and suspended employees' pay; any remainder prorated 'con absoluta igualdad proporcional' among retirees, cesantes, widows and pensioners.

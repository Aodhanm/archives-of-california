**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n432** · Sav p. 432 [stamp] / 53 [hand] · tomo p. p.495 · 1844-07-06 · Savage abstract · 

Heading (marginal +): 1844 = Julio 6 = Monterrey / Vicente Gomez al tesorero del departa.to. Text: Solicita separarse de la tesoreria. (En seguida con firma de Micheltorena, se admitio la renuncia) [Savage spacing leaves this ambiguous between con firma de and confirma de; transcribed with the reading that construes]. Gloss: Vicente Gomez asks to be relieved of the departmental treasury, and the resignation was accepted immediately over Micheltorena signature.

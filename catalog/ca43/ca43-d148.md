# Reports what Capt. Chvostov of the frigate Juno told him: he was going to Japan as ambassador, showed passports of Portu

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n36** · Sav p. 37 [stamp] / 36 [hand] · tomo p. p.334-335 · 1806 Apr 14 · Savage abstract · 

Reports what Capt. Chvostov of the frigate Juno told him: he was going to Japan as ambassador, showed passports of Portugal, England and Holland, and needed provisions for the crew; he was addressed as Excelencia and 'trahe varias cruzes é insignias y una llave de oro colgada amas en los pliegos de la casaca, y una banda encarnada terciada á la derecha' (Savage marks Sic) [= wears crosses and insignia, a gold key hung in the folds of his coat, and a crimson sash across his right].

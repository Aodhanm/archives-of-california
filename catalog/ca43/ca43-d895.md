# Under the block heading, verbatim, '1836 = Branciforte / Alejandro Rodriguez, Miguel Villa Grana'. Second of the two ent

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n393** · Sav p. 393 [stamp] / 14 [hand] · tomo p. p.87-89 · 1836-01-20 · Savage abstract · 

Under the block heading, verbatim, '1836 = Branciforte / Alejandro Rodriguez, Miguel Villa Grana'. Second of the two entries in that block. Verbatim in full: 'Enero 20 ... (Sobre lo mismo que precede).' Gloss: on the same matter as the preceding item, that is the Branciforte alcalde-and-sindico election; Savage gives nothing beyond the parenthetical rubric. Note the dating runs backwards inside the block: this Enero 20 piece follows the Enero 22 acta cited at p. 85-86.

# On the vecino Antonio Moreno's denunciation of the judge of the Pueblo de San José for dereliction, the governor orders 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n263** · Sav p. 263 [stamp] / 37 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 158-9 · 1841-05-05 · Savage abstract · 

On the vecino Antonio Moreno's denunciation of the judge of the Pueblo de San José for dereliction, the governor orders the prefect to report whether it is true and, if so, to fine him 25 pesos and admonish [the judges] to fulfil their duties. 'el vecino Antonio Moreno en Contra del Juez del Pueblo de Sn José de falta de Cumplimtº en sus deberes ... le imponga una multa de 25 pesos.'

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n219** · Sav p. 219 [stamp] / 129 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 298-99 · 1846-07-07 · Savage abstract · 

Marked '(Dup.)' at the head and '+' in the margin. 'John D. Sloat - Comander in Chief of the U.S. Naval Forces in the Pacific Ocean. Proclama a los Californios en Ingles y Espanol, anunciando venir en son de guerra, con motivo de haber sido declarada esta, entre Mejico y los E.U.' = Sloat's proclamation to the Californians, in English and Spanish, announcing that he comes in a state of war, war having been declared between Mexico and the United States. The U.S. seizure of Monterey.

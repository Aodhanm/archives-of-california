# The Gertrudis Garibay saga (cf. Docs 8, 61): the imprisoned Garibay is cited to make her *exposición* in the causa she b

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n388** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.62–69 · 1833 · Savage abstract · 

The Gertrudis Garibay saga (cf. Docs 8, 61): the imprisoned Garibay is cited to make her *exposición* in the causa she brings against Sgt. Antonio Solórzano 'por haber violado la prisión de la referida' (p.62); an informe praising her conduct in the proceso against Isidoro Rosas 'por haber inferido heridas á la mencionada y querido forzar' (wounding + attempted rape, p.63–64); witnesses cited (Tomás del Castillo, Núñez Uballe, Gil Cano, Luis Plasencia, Felipe García, p.65–66); the troop needs an artillero & cook (p.67–69). → women's/carceral history flag.

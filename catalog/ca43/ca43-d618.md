# The governor replies to the prefect's note of today that he may well designate the person to serve the Sub-Prefectura of

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n273** · Sav p. 273 [stamp] / 47 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 191-2 · 1841-07-30 · Savage abstract · 

The governor replies to the prefect's note of today that he may well designate the person to serve the Sub-Prefectura of the partido of the Pueblo de San José and exercise the powers the law grants. Marginal decree, Julio 31 de 1841: 'propongase al Superior Gobnº la persona del Sr Dn Antonio Suñol para el Cargo de Sub-Prefecto del Pueblo de San Jose de Alvarado (sic).'

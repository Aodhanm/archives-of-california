**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n373** · Sav p. 373 [stamp] / 146 [hand] · tomo p. p.[no page cite given by Savage] · 1844-09-13 · Savage abstract · 

Entry verbatim: '13 Ordena se remita la Causa Criminal de Gil Sanchez al Tribunal Sup.or'. Gloss: orders the criminal cause of Gil Sanchez sent up to the Superior Tribunal. The same cause is described at n372 (p.p. 623-4) as being for the murder of Fulgencio Robles. Savage gives this entry no page cite.

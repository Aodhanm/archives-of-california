# Heading (marginal +): 1843 = Noviembre 23 = Monterrey / Feliciano Vivaldo a Alvarado. Text: Que en la tienda de Franc.o 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n430** · Sav p. 430 [stamp] / 51 [hand] · tomo p. p.485-486 · 1843-11-23 · Savage abstract · 

Heading (marginal +): 1843 = Noviembre 23 = Monterrey / Feliciano Vivaldo a Alvarado. Text: Que en la tienda de Franc.o Real, se ha encontrado un soldado tirado y golpeado con un garrote dicho Jorge. Gloss: reports that a soldier named Jorge was found lying in Francisco Real store, beaten with a garrote. [The heading year reads 1843 and the addressee is Alvarado, who was not then governor; recorded as written, without speculation.]

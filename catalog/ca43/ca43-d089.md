# Transmits for publication the decree barring all ships, under penalty of *comiso*, from bringing foreign-manufactured go

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n296** · Sav p.  · tomo p. 266–267 · 1844-09 · Savage abstract · 

Transmits for publication the decree barring all ships, under penalty of *comiso*, from bringing foreign-manufactured goods via Mazatlán, San Blas or other south-coast ports of the Republic (protectionist trade control, p.266); accompanies 8 copies of the acta installing the Asamblea Departamental (p.207).

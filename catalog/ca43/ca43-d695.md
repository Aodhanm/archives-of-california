# Figueroa approves Gregorio Briones as Alcalde de Contra Costa (p.375); Haro reports taking the declaration of Cayetano J

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n318** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.375–379 · 1835 / 1838–39 · Savage abstract · 

Figueroa approves Gregorio Briones as Alcalde de Contra Costa (p.375); Haro reports taking the declaration of Cayetano Juárez (alleges ignorance; a 20-peso fine is just, p.376–7); Haro remits Juan Sánchez, detained for various robberies, to be judged by preference (p.378–9). SF/Dolores district justice.

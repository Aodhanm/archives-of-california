**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n141** · Sav p. 141 [stamp] / 55 [hand] · tomo p. p. 156 · 1835-03-21 · Savage abstract · 

Marginal 'v'. Spans n141-n142 (abstract begins bottom of n141, completes at head of n142). 'Decreto del Cong. & declarando que los Diputados de la Baja y Alta Cal. tubiesen voz y voto en el Congreso en la formacion de leyes y decretos.' [Gloss: the deputies of Baja and Alta California are to have voice AND vote in Congress in the making of laws and decrees - territorial representation upgraded from consultative voice.]

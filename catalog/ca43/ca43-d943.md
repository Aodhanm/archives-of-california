**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n340** · Sav p. 340 [stamp] / 113 [hand] · tomo p. p.p.470-1„ · 1840-04-17 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '1840 San Francisco / Fran.co Guerrero al Juez de Paz de Monterey'. Entry: 'Abril 17. Dice: que en la Barca Guipuzcuana remite á la Prefectura 2 presos José Brenguin [?] y Gorge [?], le encarga le remita los grillos'. Gloss: two prisoners shipped from SF to the Prefecture aboard the bark Guipuzcoana, shackles to be returned — April 1840, the Graham-affair deportation window (the Guipuzcoana carried the Graham exiles that month; cf. d616). Ship-registry crossref: Barca Guipuzcuana.

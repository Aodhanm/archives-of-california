# On the prefect's note of today urging that the Juez de 1ª Instancia reside at this point, the governor replies that he m

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n275** · Sav p. 275 [stamp] / 49 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 200-1 · 1841-07-30 · Savage abstract · 

On the prefect's note of today urging that the Juez de 1ª Instancia reside at this point, the governor replies that he may propose the person having the necessary qualifications to serve that court. Marginal decree, Monterey Julio 31 de 1841: 'Propongase al Superior Gobierno la persona de Dn José Z. Fernandez quien reune á juicio de esta Prefectura los requicitos nesesarios.'

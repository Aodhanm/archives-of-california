**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n1** · Sav p. 2 [stamp] / 1 [hand] · tomo p. p. 1-12 · 1571-11-01 · Savage abstract · 

'Cedula real sobre alcabalas' '(Doc. impreso)' = royal cedula establishing/regulating the alcabala. No addressee given. This is the founding statute the whole of Tomo I turns on; every later item in the tomo cites or applies it.

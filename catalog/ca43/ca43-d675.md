# Circular on the reunion of the political & military command: quoting the Sec. de Guerra y Marina (21 Jan), the President

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n306** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.320–322 · 1836-01-02 · Savage abstract · 

Circular on the reunion of the political & military command: quoting the Sec. de Guerra y Marina (21 Jan), the President has determined the mando militar & político of Alta California remain united — Castro reports he has this day handed the political command of the Territory to Comandante General D. Nicolás Gutiérrez after the customary oath (p.320); + Castro's oath-day discourse as Prefect of the 1st District (S. José, Mar 1839, p.321–2).

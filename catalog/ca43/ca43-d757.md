# Verbatim: 'Ordena se pongan en libertad inmediatamente a 2 Indios de San Antonio acusados de robo de Caballada'. Gloss: 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n359** · Sav p. 359 [stamp] / 132 [hand] · tomo p. p.p 564-5 · 1842-07-16 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Ordena se pongan en libertad inmediatamente a 2 Indios de San Antonio acusados de robo de Caballada'. Gloss: orders the immediate release of two Indians of [Mision] San Antonio held on a charge of horse-herd theft (robo de caballada). Indian-policy item: a superior-court release order overturning custody on a stock-theft accusation. Marginal '+'. Addressee inferred, not stated - see issues.

# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim: 'El Sup.r Tribunal teniendo pr

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n365** · Sav p. 365 [stamp] / 138 [hand] · tomo p. p. 583 · 1843-10-03 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim: 'El Sup.r Tribunal teniendo presente el estravio que sufren alg.s asuntos Judiciales, por ser remitidos por los Juzgados de Paz al Conocim.to de la Prefectura a cuya autoridad no Compete. En acuerdo de hoy dispuso que por circular prevenga a los Jueces del 1.er Distrito remitan directamente al Tribunal'. Gloss: judicial business is going astray because juzgados de paz route it through the Prefectura, which has no competence; by circular the judges of the 1st District are to remit directly to the Tribunal.

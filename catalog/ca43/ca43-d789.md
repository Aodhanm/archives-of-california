# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1844 Nov.re 23 Monter

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n377** · Sav p. 377 [stamp] / 150 [hand] · tomo p. pp 642 · 1844-11-23 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1844 Nov.re 23 Monterey / Juan Malarin al Juez de 1.a Instancia'. Entry verbatim: 'Ordena se ponga en libertad absoluta a Jose M.a Bravo en atencion al tiempo que ha estado detenido'. Gloss: orders the unconditional release of Jose Maria Bravo in view of the time he has already been held. Belongs with the Tribunal run against arbitrary detention (cf. the Pablo Juan ruling, p.638-9).

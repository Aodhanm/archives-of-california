# 'Que todas las aduanas han de Corresponder semanariamente con la de la Capital.' = every customs house must report weekl

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n9** · Sav p. 10 [stamp] / 9 [hand] · tomo p. p. 107 · 1776-05-14 · Savage abstract · 

'Que todas las aduanas han de Corresponder semanariamente con la de la Capital.' = every customs house must report weekly to the Mexico City aduana. Structural rule for the whole customs network the Monterey aduana later belongs to.

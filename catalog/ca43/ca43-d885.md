# Verbatim heading: '1833 Set. 19. Méx. / Pavon - al Ad.mor de la Ad.a de Mont.' (no subject rubric given). Savage's abstr

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n437** · Sav p. 437 [stamp] / 3 [hand] · tomo p. pp.172-5 (Depl State Papers tomo VII) · 1833-09-19 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1833 Set. 19. Méx. / Pavon - al Ad.mor de la Ad.a de Mont.' (no subject rubric given). Savage's abstract verbatim: 'Orden del Gob.o Sup.mo que se envie mensalm.te á la Secret.a de Hac.da una noticia de los buques ajustados y de los que se hayan de ajustar. Se exita el exacto cumplim.to' Gloss: a supreme government order that a monthly return be sent to the Ministry of Finance of the vessels already settled with (ajustados) and of those still to be settled, with an exhortation to exact compliance. A second, distinct 19 Sep 1833 item, separated from the preceding one by its own page cite.

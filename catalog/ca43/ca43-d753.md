# Reply to the judge's nota oficial of 15 June about complaints made by prisoners at the ordinary jail visita of 11 June. 

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n358** · Sav p. 358 [stamp] / 131 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 555-6 · 1842-06-17 · Savage abstract · 

Reply to the judge's nota oficial of 15 June about complaints made by prisoners at the ordinary jail visita of 11 June. Verbatim: 'se encargue unicamente de procurar el medio mas oportuno a fin de que los reos que se hallan comunicados en la Carcel de la Poblacion, se les proporcione cuando menos una hora del Sol en el lugar y con las circunstancias que le dicte la prudencia'. Gloss: having accepted the judge's justos descargos, the Tribunal charges him only with seeing that prisoners held in the town jail get at least one hour of sunlight, in whatever place and manner prudence dictates. Direct sequel to the 11 June visita report abstracted at n354-n357.

# The Prefectura holds it absolutely necessary that the Juzgado de 1ª Instancia reside at this point, Monterey having been

**C-A 43 (Departmental State Papers, Monterey), scan n276** · Sav p. 276 [stamp] / 50 [hand] · tomo p. p. 204 · 1841-07-30 · Savage abstract · 

The Prefectura holds it absolutely necessary that the Juzgado de 1ª Instancia reside at this point, Monterey having been declared provisionally cabecera of the district, and asks the governor to proceed to appoint a judge with the requisite qualities. 'en Virtud de haber sido declarado interinamte cabezera del Distrito.'

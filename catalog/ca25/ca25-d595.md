# Underlined year change: '1807.= Fbro. 18 Sta Barbara. pp 253-56. (6 doc)'. First item: 'Haber fondeado embarcacion desco

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n347–n348** · Sav p. 345/96. · tomo p. p.253-56 · 1807-02-18 · Savage abstract · 

Underlined year change: '1807.= Fbro. 18 Sta Barbara. pp 253-56. (6 doc)'. First item: 'Haber fondeado embarcacion desconocida entre Sta Barbara y Sn Diego.' An unidentified vessel had anchored between Santa Barbara and San Diego. Line spans n347-n348. This is the only place the year 1807 is written in this whole stretch; every later entry through n351 carries it by implication.

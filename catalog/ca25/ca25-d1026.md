**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n301** · Sav p. 299 / 50 · tomo p. p 145 · 1804-07-10 · Savage abstract · 

NEW SECTION HEAD + first register line. Verbatim: '+ 1804. = Loreto / Borradores de Comunicaciones del Gob.r al Virrey.' then 'Julio 10 p 145. (2 doc. / - 30 @ de greta p.a fabrica de losa burda. / - Socorros q.e se deben suministrar á desertores de buques extrangeros.' [= Loreto, drafts of the Governor communications to the Viceroy; 10 July 1804, 2 documents: 30 arrobas of greta (litharge, the lead glaze) for the manufacture of coarse earthenware; and the relief to be furnished to deserters from foreign vessels.] The upper two thirds of the leaf is the tail of the Vancouver/O.Cain otter letter anchored on n298, closing 'pp. 143-45.' Nothing in the export cites Tomo IX p.145 for 1804.

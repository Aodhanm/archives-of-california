# Lower half of the leaf. Verbatim heading: '+ 1806 = Monterrey. / Borradores de Comunicaciones de Gob.r al Virrey.' Singl

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n330** · Sav p. stamp 328 / pencil 79 · tomo p. p. 223 · 1806-07-01 · Savage abstract · 

Lower half of the leaf. Verbatim heading: '+ 1806 = Monterrey. / Borradores de Comunicaciones de Gob.r al Virrey.' Single entry verbatim: 'Julio 1.o ... p. 223. (1 doc. - Propone creacion de oficiales p.a S.n Diego; q.e Raymundo Carrillo es el unico oficial q.e no ha sido removido; pide asenso p.a Sorgt.o de Loreto, Fran.co Javier Aguilar p.r tener 37 anos de servicios, y su retiro p.r tal motivo.' [= proposes the creation of officer posts for San Diego, Raymundo Carrillo being the only officer not removed; asks promotion for the sergeant of Loreto, Francisco Javier Aguilar, on 37 years of service, and his retirement on that account]. The top of this leaf is the tail of the Junio 30 letter (interiors), and the foot carries the heading of the document catalogued as record 563 (already_catalogued).

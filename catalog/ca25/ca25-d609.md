# 'Junio 1o... (1 doc) - Que buque anclado en Sn Quintín, se cree Ockain.' The vessel anchored at San Quintín is believed 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n349** · Sav p. 347/98. · tomo p. p.265-66 · 1807-06-01 · Savage abstract · 

'Junio 1o... (1 doc) - Que buque anclado en Sn Quintín, se cree Ockain.' The vessel anchored at San Quintín is believed to be the Ockain. Savage spells it Ockain in quotes; this is very probably the Boston ship OCain (Winship), the Russian-American Company contract otter-hunting vessel then working the peninsula coast, but the leaf offers no master, flag or cargo.

# Verbatim, with an interlineated name carried above the line on a caret: Que condestable de Artilleria [above the line: J

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n282** · Sav p. 280 / 32.- · tomo p. pp. 108-109 (3 doc.) · 1804-11-29 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim, with an interlineated name carried above the line on a caret: Que condestable de Artilleria [above the line: Joaquin Sanchez] es encargado de cosecha de Cañamo. [English: that the artillery gunner Joaquin Sanchez is placed in charge of the hemp harvest.] The name was added afterwards, in the same hand, above the line.

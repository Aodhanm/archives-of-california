# Leaf opens with its own date line, verbatim: Marzo 31. (10 doc.) The series heading stands on n251, verbatim: + 1800 = M

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n252** · Sav p. 250 / 2 · tomo p. p. 4 · 1800-03-31 · Savage abstract · 

Leaf opens with its own date line, verbatim: Marzo 31. (10 doc.) The series heading stands on n251, verbatim: + 1800 = Monterrey. / Borradores de comunicaciones de Com.te (no dice a quien.) First of the ten drafts of that date, verbatim: Que esta enterado de declaracion de Guerra contra Rusia. [English: acknowledges that he is informed of the declaration of war against Russia.] The word Rusia carries an ink blot over the s but is legible. Spain, allied to France, was at war with Russia in this period; the entry records the news reaching Monterey.

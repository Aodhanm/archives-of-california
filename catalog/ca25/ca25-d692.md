# 'Que se han tomado declaraciones a desertores de Fragatas Ma-carc[?] y Mercurio' [= depositions taken from deserters of 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n372** · Sav p. 369/120 · tomo p. p.357 · 1810-02-21 · Savage abstract · 

'Que se han tomado declaraciones a desertores de Fragatas Ma-carc[?] y Mercurio' [= depositions taken from deserters of the frigates Ma-carc(?) and Mercurio]. (1 doc.) First entry under the header '1810 = Monterey. / Borradores de comunicaciones del Gobr al Virrey.' Mercurio = the American Mercury; the first ship name is broken across the line (Ma- / carc) and I cannot resolve it - a ship-registry lead.

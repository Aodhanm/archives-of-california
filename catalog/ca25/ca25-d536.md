# Same Julio 30 batch (7 doc, pp. 78-82), described document: 'Que por haber muerto en Pres.o de Monterey Fr. Fermin Fran.

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n277** · Sav p. 275/27 · tomo p. p.78-82 · 1803-07-30 · Savage abstract · 

Same Julio 30 batch (7 doc, pp. 78-82), described document: 'Que por haber muerto en Pres.o de Monterey Fr. Fermin Fran.co Lasuen recayo la presidencia en Fr. Esteban Tapis.' = because Fr. Fermin Francisco Lasuen has died at the Monterey presidio, the presidency of the missions has devolved on Fr. Esteban Tapis. The governor's own contemporary notice of the succession to the father-presidency.

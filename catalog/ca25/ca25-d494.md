# '+ 1800. = Abril 4 (no dice lugar.) (Sin firma) Ynstruccion pa regimen en recaudacion de fondos y pago de pensiones de M

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n267–n269** · Sav p. hw 17 / stamp 265 (doc spans hw 17-19 / stamps 265-267) · tomo p. p.60-62 · 1800-04-04 · Savage abstract · 

'+ 1800. = Abril 4 (no dice lugar.) (Sin firma) Ynstruccion pa regimen en recaudacion de fondos y pago de pensiones de Monte-pio establecido a favor de Viudas, Huerfanos y madres de Cirujanos &a.' Arts. 1-4: half-pay retained since 1 Dbre by the tesoreria mayor and army treasuries from surgeons of infantry, cavalry, dragoons, artillery, military hospitals and castillos; pensions to be assigned from 1 July; contributors not subject to muster pay at the nearest army treasury; 'en dominios de Yndias' the same discounts in maravedis de plata per peso fuerte, intendants remitting yearly accounts to the President of the Junta. Spans n267-n269.

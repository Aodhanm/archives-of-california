# Last leaf of Tomo IX. One register entry, verbatim: '' 3. pp. 631.33 (1 doc.) - Queda impuesto de venir un oficial de Ar

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n451** · Sav p. stamped 447 / pencil 198 · tomo p. pp.631-633; and the notes cover pp. 634-666 and pp. 667-763 · 1818 [month is a ditto of N.bre from the previous entry on n450] 3; and the two index notes are undated · Savage abstract · 

Last leaf of Tomo IX. One register entry, verbatim: '' 3. pp. 631.33 (1 doc.) - Queda impuesto de venir un oficial de Artilleria con destino á la Prov.a p.a remplazo de esta arma.' Then TWO finding-aid notes in which Savage records what fills the rest of the original volume without copying it, verbatim: 'Indice de comunicaciones remitidas por el Virrey á Gob.r.s de Cal.s de 1799 á 1814. pp. 634-66.' and 'Yndice de oficios de Gob.r Arrillaga al Virrey - 1800 - 1804. pp 667-763.' English gloss: an artillery officer is coming to the province as a relief for that arm; then, index of communications sent by the Viceroy to the governors of the Californias, 1799 to 1814, occupying original pages 634 to 666; and index of dispatches of Governor Arrillaga to the Viceroy, 1800 to 1804, occupying original pages 667 to 763. The two notes are a statement of nearly 130 original pages of Provincial Records IX that Savage saw and did not transcribe, which is why they matter.

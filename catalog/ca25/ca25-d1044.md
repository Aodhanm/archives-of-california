# Two entries under the ditto date, verbatim: Sobre construccion de barca plana p.a socorro de Guarnicion de San Diego y P

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n282** · Sav p. 280 / 32.- · tomo p. pp. 105-106 (3 doc) · 1804-11-16 · Savage abstract · 

Two entries under the ditto date, verbatim: Sobre construccion de barca plana p.a socorro de Guarnicion de San Diego y Punta de Guijarros. And: Que soldados q.e se mutilen en Servicio se les expida retiro. [English: on the building of a flat boat for the relief of the garrison of San Diego and Punta de Guijarros; and that soldiers who are maimed in service are to be given their discharge.] Punta de Guijarros is the Ballast Point battery site at San Diego.

# 'Da parte, arribada de goleta Rusa Icuna Chirico, conduciendo á tente de dicha nacion Jacob Paduskim quien le entregó un

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n423** · Sav p. 419 | 170 · tomo p. pp 552-55 · 1817 Mayo 12 · Savage abstract · 

'Da parte, arribada de goleta Rusa Icuna Chirico, conduciendo á tente de dicha nacion Jacob Paduskim quien le entregó un pliego.' [= reports the arrival of the Russian schooner (name uncertain), bringing a lieutenant of that nation, Jacob Paduskim, who handed over a dispatch]. First letter of the ship name reads I / S / Y at scale=1; if S, 'Scuna' = escuna/schooner and the vessel would be the Chirikov, and 'Paduskim' would be Yakov Podushkin - PROBABLE identification only, NOT verified.

# Entry text opens at the head of n406: 'Acusa recibo de órden reservada sobre valerse de la fuerza pa retirar á Coscoff, 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n406** · Sav p. 154.- · tomo p. p.514-522 · 1816-11-14 · Savage abstract · 

Entry text opens at the head of n406: 'Acusa recibo de órden reservada sobre valerse de la fuerza pa retirar á Coscoff, y dice aucilios qe necesita pa ejecutarlo.' Sola acknowledges receipt of the RESERVED ORDER to use armed force to remove Kuskov [from Ross], and states the auxiliaries he needs to execute it. Third of the 'Nobre 14 (3 doc.)' run.

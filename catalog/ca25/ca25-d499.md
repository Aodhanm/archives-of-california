**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n270** · Sav p. 268/20 · tomo p. p.65 · 1803-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Le adjunta Sumaria formada p.r Comandante de Presidio de S.n Diego, Sobre lo acontesido en alojamiento de piquete de artilleria q.e guarnece la Punta de Guijarros.' = encloses the summary inquiry drawn up by the San Diego comandante on what happened over the quartering of the artillery picket garrisoning Punta de Guijarros. Same battery involved in the Lelia Byrd affair two entries below.

# Register run continuing the block whose head is at the foot of n302. Verbatim: '' 26 pp 147-48 (3 doc. - Negarsele aucil

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n303** · Sav p. stamped 301 / pencil 52. · tomo p. pp.147-154 · 1804-07-26 to 1804-09-03 · Savage abstract · 

Register run continuing the block whose head is at the foot of n302. Verbatim: '' 26 pp 147-48 (3 doc. - Negarsele aucilios á buque 'Alexander' y haberse hecho á la vela' | 'Agosto 31. p 149-50 (4 doc.) - Cedulas de retiro - Que se observe R.l órden de 1784 sobre arribadas de buques extrangeros' | 'S.tbre 1.o pp. 150-51. (5 doc.) - Remision de facturas. - Que Caballeros de cualquier órden q.e sean no pueden casarse sin licencia del Consejo - Cedula de retiro de cabo Estanislao Salgado. - Capellanes no exijan Cuarta funeral de Militares.' | '' 2. pp. 151-52 (3 doc.) - Sobre prosesos. - Hacer retirar buques extrangeros.' | '' 3 pp. 152-54. - Fondeó Bergantin 'Valdéz' - Solicitud de jeneros de los 4 Pres.os - Recibo de 74 fuciles.' English gloss: Jul 26, aid refused to the ship Alexander and her sailing away. Aug 31, retirement cedulas; the royal order of 1784 on the putting in of foreign vessels is to be observed. Sep 1, invoices forwarded; knights of any order whatever may not marry without licence of the Council; retirement cedula for Corporal Estanislao Salgado; chaplains are not to demand the funeral quarter from soldiers. Sep 2, on lawsuits; foreign ships to be made to withdraw. Sep 3, the brig Valdez anchored; a request for goods for the four presidios; receipt of 74 muskets.

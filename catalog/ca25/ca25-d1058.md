# Register run continuing the block whose head is on n292. Verbatim: 'Mayo 5 pp 129-30 (3 doc.) - Relacion de invalidos. -

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n293** · Sav p. stamped 291 / pencil 43. · tomo p. pp.129-130 · 1804-05-05 to 1804-05-07 · Savage abstract · 

Register run continuing the block whose head is on n292. Verbatim: 'Mayo 5 pp 129-30 (3 doc.) - Relacion de invalidos. - Sobre fundacion de Villa Branciforte.' | '' 7 p 130 (3 doc.) - Misioneros, Mariano Yold, Mariano Apolinar y Tomas Valdellon solicitan permiso, retirarse de la Pen.a' English gloss: May 5, a return of invalids and a document on the founding of Villa Branciforte. May 7, the missionaries Mariano Yold, Mariano Apolinar and Tomas Valdellon ask leave to withdraw from the Peninsula. Not covered by the existing n292 record, whose date range stops at Apr 9.

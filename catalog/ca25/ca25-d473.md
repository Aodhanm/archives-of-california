# NOT a separate Savage entry: the second half of the Julio 2 document, recorded here only as content. 'Ultimamente con fe

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n260** · Sav p. hw 10 / stamp 258 · tomo p. p.40-42 · 1800-06-26 · Savage abstract · 

NOT a separate Savage entry: the second half of the Julio 2 document, recorded here only as content. 'Ultimamente con fecha de 26 de Junio en el mismo Arroyo de San Benito... mataron dos de la otra nacion los ausayames, amenasando quemar la Mision.' Fr. Pedro Martinez reports the deaths, threats against the indio milpero, and the firing of 'el llano del pajanal' which burned a piece of wheat. Sargento Moraga [Gabriel?] sent with a cabo and 9 men to seize the capitanejos; he brought in the capitanejo and 13 indios ausaymes, the capitanejo and 2 indios of the Rancheria del Carcadero[?], and a fugitive christian indian of Mision de Santa Cruz.

# 'Que avise al habilitado a fin[?] que este ult.o pague á J.[ph] Unsueta.' [That he notify the habilitado so that the lat

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n513** · Sav p. 509 / 61 · tomo p. p.456 · 1807 Abril 28 · Savage abstract · 

'Que avise al habilitado a fin[?] que este ult.o pague á J.[ph] Unsueta.' [That he notify the habilitado so that the latter pay J. Unsueta.] The word read here as 'a fin' could also be read 'ofis'; sense favours a fin. Companion piece to the preceding row, same date.

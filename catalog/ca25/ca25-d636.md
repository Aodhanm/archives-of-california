# Second item of the Agosto 1 line and last entry on n351: 'Precio de cáñamo á $31 @.' Price of hemp at 31 pesos the arrob

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n351** · Sav p. 349/100. · tomo p. p.274-75 · 1807-08-01 · Savage abstract · 

Second item of the Agosto 1 line and last entry on n351: 'Precio de cáñamo á $31 @.' Price of hemp at 31 pesos the arroba[?]. The peso sign and the figure 31 are clear at scale=1 but the unit mark reads as the arroba symbol and the price is implausibly high for hemp, so re-check against the original cite pp. 274-75 before using the figure.

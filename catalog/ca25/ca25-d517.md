# Double-ditto line (= Junio 25): 'Remite nota de efectos q.e Solicitan habitantes de [struck: de; interlined: p.as] los 4

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n274** · Sav p. 272/24 · tomo p. p.71 · 1803-06-25 · Savage abstract · 

Double-ditto line (= Junio 25): 'Remite nota de efectos q.e Solicitan habitantes de [struck: de; interlined: p.as] los 4 Presidios de Californias y provicion p.a tropa en proximo ano 1804.' = forwards the list of goods requested by the inhabitants of the four Californias presidios and the provisioning of the troops for the coming year 1804. Two large ink X strokes are drawn across this entry.

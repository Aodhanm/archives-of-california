# First bullet under the double-ditto line '' ' 20 pp 530 32 (3 doc.)' [= 1817 Enero 20]: 'Haber en ensenada de Sn Pedro b

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n406** · Sav p. 154.- · tomo p. p.530-32 · 1817-01-20 · Savage abstract · 

First bullet under the double-ditto line '' ' 20 pp 530 32 (3 doc.)' [= 1817 Enero 20]: 'Haber en ensenada de Sn Pedro buque de 3 palos y ser Americano.' Reports a three-masted AMERICAN vessel lying in the ensenada of San Pedro. Ties to the armed-boat landing at San Pedro described in Sola's parte No 100, cited in the Enero 2 informe at n411.

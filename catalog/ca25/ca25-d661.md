# 'Retiro de Soldº de Sn Diego Felipe Romero' [= retirement of San Diego soldier Felipe Romero, whose petition was registe

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n369** · Sav p. 366/117 · tomo p. p.343-45 · 1809 · Savage abstract · 

'Retiro de Soldº de Sn Diego Felipe Romero' [= retirement of San Diego soldier Felipe Romero, whose petition was registered 26 May, p 322]. Doc 1 of a (6 doc.) line whose dateline reads verbatim '[9/X]bre. 27. Monterrey.' - the month abbreviation is ambiguous at scale=1 between 9bre (Noviembre) and Xbre (Diciembre); its position between p 343 and the 'Diciembre 1º' entry at p 346 favours Noviembre. Day 27 is certain.

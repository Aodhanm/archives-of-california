# 'Que a desertores de fragatas extrangeras, se destinen a reparos de obras de Presºs' [= deserters from foreign frigates 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n369** · Sav p. 366/117 · tomo p. p.339 · 1809-10-29 · Savage abstract · 

'Que a desertores de fragatas extrangeras, se destinen a reparos de obras de Presºs' [= deserters from foreign frigates are to be assigned to repair work on the presidios]. Doc 2 of the (2 doc.) line. Direct sequel to the Neva/American deserter items; foreign deserters converted into presidio labour.

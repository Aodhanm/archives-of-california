# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '+ 1805. = Fbro. 8 = L

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n313** · Sav p. stamp 311 / pencil 62 · tomo p. p. 176 · 1805-02-08 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '+ 1805. = Fbro. 8 = Loreto. / Gob.r a Virrey. Sobre herreros.' [= on blacksmiths]. Savage abstract: the need for armourers in the presidios is set out; the reglamento assigns two blacksmiths to the Presidio of Monterey at 15 pesos monthly, yet there is only one, at San Diego; that a single man be appointed at 30 pesos salary 'q.e sepa de herrero y armero p.a q.e ensene a los indios' [who knows both blacksmithing and gunsmithing, so as to teach the Indians]. The words between '$15 mensales' and 'y sin embargo' are a two-letter abbreviation I cannot read with confidence (possibly c/u, cada uno).

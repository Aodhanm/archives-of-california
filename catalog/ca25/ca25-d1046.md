# Two entries under the ditto date, verbatim: Embarque de Piquete Voluntarios de Cataluña y familias en fragata Princesa y

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n282** · Sav p. 280 / 32.- · tomo p. pp. 106-108 (4 doc) · 1804-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

Two entries under the ditto date, verbatim: Embarque de Piquete Voluntarios de Cataluña y familias en fragata Princesa y Bergantin Activo. And: Relacion de invalidos. [English: embarkation of the detachment of Catalonian Volunteers and their families aboard the frigate Princesa and the brig Activo; and a return of invalids.] Names both vessels for the withdrawal of the Catalonian Volunteers.

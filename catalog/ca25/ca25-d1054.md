# At the foot of the leaf, below the close of the Diesmos letter, verbatim: Julio 4 - Cedulas de retiro. pp 141-42 (3 doc)

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n297** · Sav p. 295 / 46. · tomo p. pp. 141-142 (3 doc) · 1804-07-04 · Savage abstract · 

At the foot of the leaf, below the close of the Diesmos letter, verbatim: Julio 4 - Cedulas de retiro. pp 141-42 (3 doc) - Militares separados del servicio usaran uniforme de retirados. [English: patents of retirement; military men separated from the service are to wear the uniform of the retired.] A separate register entry, not part of the Diesmos document above it.

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n315** · Sav p. stamp 313 / pencil 64 · tomo p. p. 180 · 1805-03-15 · Savage abstract · 

Lower half of the leaf. Verbatim heading: '+ 1805. = Loreto. / Borradores de comunicacios del Gob.r al Virrey.' (Savage writes 'comunicacios', dropping the -ne-; transcribed as written). Single entry verbatim: 'Marzo 15. ... p 180 (1 doc.) - Misiones solicitan polvora p.a fiestas.' [= the missions request gunpowder for fiestas]. The upper half of this leaf is the tail of the 11 March 1805 letter, listed under interiors.

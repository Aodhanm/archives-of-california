# 'Avisa arribo de fragata Americana llamada 'Sultan,' y su salida pa establecimientos Rusos.' — reports the arrival of th

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n393** · Sav p. 141. · tomo p. p.485-89 · 1816-08-03 · Savage abstract · 

'Avisa arribo de fragata Americana llamada 'Sultan,' y su salida pa establecimientos Rusos.' — reports the arrival of the American frigate Sultan and her departure for the Russian establishments. Third of the Agosto 3 group. Direct evidence of the American-Russian coasting traffic touching Alta California in 1816.

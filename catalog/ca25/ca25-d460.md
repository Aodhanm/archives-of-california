# MARQUEE — the 1797 register (Anglo-Spanish war ongoing). FOREIGN-SHIP/WAR: 17 English sails sighted on the coast (Ene); 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n174–183** · Sav p. 173–182 · tomo p. 561–63 (índice; orig-p pin TODO) · 1797 · Savage abstract · 

MARQUEE — the 1797 register (Anglo-Spanish war ongoing). FOREIGN-SHIP/WAR: 17 English sails sighted on the coast (Ene); the offensive/defensive alliance with the French Republic (Oct, San Ildefonso 1796); the frigates *Concepción* & *Princesa* stationed to guard the coast (Ago); an espionage-declaration bando (Ago); ships seen near S. Diego (Dic); loss of the packet *San Carlos* (Jul). FOUNDING/SF: the Villa de Branciforte founding + the 17 settlers moved there (Ene/Nov) & the ⭐ Sta Cruz-mission-vs-Branciforte proximity conflict (the Colegio S.Fernando's objection, Set); engineer Alberto de Córdova's SF-fort/Yerba-Buena-battery works (Jul–Ago) then his retirement to Mexico (Dic); surveys of the 5 new mission sites approved (Oct); José Argüello Lt of the SF company. INDIAN: ⭐⭐ the Sacalanes Indians incident + Sgt Amador's mission expedition (Dic — = [[ca24]] Doc 208, closing the 1795 Dolores massacre arc); the Silverio/Rosa uxoricide (Abr, cf. ca24); offices of *República* (neophyte governance) in the missions (Dic). OTHER: the Moqui route/journey again (Jul); 19 Guanajuato vagrants sent to the presidios (May, penal-settlement); smallpox-prevention measures (Feb); the 200-musket/16,000-flint arms shipment (Feb); census of inhabitants end-1796 (Nov); new Montepío Militar reglamento (Dic).

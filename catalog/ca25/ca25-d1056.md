# NEW SECTION HEAD, verbatim: '1804. = Loreto. / Borradores de Comunicaciones de Gob.r al Virrey.' Register entries verbat

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n284** · Sav p. stamped 282 / pencil 34. · tomo p. pp.113-117 · 1804-01-03 to 1804-02-25 · Savage abstract · 

NEW SECTION HEAD, verbatim: '1804. = Loreto. / Borradores de Comunicaciones de Gob.r al Virrey.' Register entries verbatim: 'Enero 3. p. 113. (1 doc.) - Arribo de fragata Am.a á S.n Diego' | '' 27. p. 113. (2 doc.) - Sobre gastos en obras de oficinas de Pres.o S.n Fran.co - Orden 11 de Julio 1803 sobre q.e á reclutas y tropa sobrante p.a cuerpos del ejército, se les acredite segun sus clases haberes y gratificacion q.e á dotacion de dhos cuerpos' | '' 28. pp 113-115 - Sobre que tropa no use luto, sino de la clase de mariscales de campo arriba y tropa de casa R.l - Remite causa criminal contra soldado de Monterrey Mariano Duarte' | 'Febrero 25. p.115.-117. (2 doc) - Que caso q.e arribe á costas de Jurisd.n [interlineated on a caret: expedicion] Americana - Rusa mandada p.r Capt.n Krusentrer sera socorrida conforme á órden de 27 Julio 1803.' English gloss: Jan 3, an American frigate arrives at San Diego. Jan 27, costs of building the San Francisco presidio offices; the order of 11 July 1803 on crediting recruits and surplus troops. Jan 28, troops are not to wear mourning except for field marshals and upward and the royal household troops; the criminal case against the Monterey soldier Mariano Duarte is forwarded. Feb 25, should the American-Russian expedition commanded by Captain Krusentrer (Krusenstern) reach the coasts of this jurisdiction it is to be succoured under the order of 27 July 1803. The Krusenstern item is the second of the two documents under Febrero 25 and is not in the export; the first is the letter already catalogued at n285.

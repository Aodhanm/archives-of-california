# Heading '+ 1803. = Mayo 1.o = (Loreto) / Gob.r a Virrey. Sobre guardia a bordo de fragatas p.a'. Forwards the S.n Diego 

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n270–n271** · Sav p. 268/20 · tomo p. p.66 · 1803-05-01 · Savage abstract · 

Heading '+ 1803. = Mayo 1.o = (Loreto) / Gob.r a Virrey. Sobre guardia a bordo de fragatas p.a'. Forwards the S.n Diego comandante's parte on 'lo Sucedido con fragata Anglo-Americana Lelia Bird Pormout. Su Cap.n Guillermo Shaler' [= Lelia Byrd, Wm Shaler]: reached that port 17 March, sailed the 22nd, anchored the 24th at Pto. de S.n Quintin; judging the affair 'de consideracion' he ordered diligencias juridicas; guards now placed aboard frigates, not done for earlier arrivals, 'q.e de otro modo Seria imposible impedir contrabandos'. spans n270-n271

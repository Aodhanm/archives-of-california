# '18 ... (1 doc.) - Captura de 2 indios de establecimiento Ruso.' Capture of two Indians belonging to a Russian establish

**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n345** · Sav p. 343/94. · tomo p. p.247 · 1806-11-18 · Savage abstract · 

'18 ... (1 doc.) - Captura de 2 indios de establecimiento Ruso.' Capture of two Indians belonging to a Russian establishment. That is the whole of Savage's text: no names, no place of capture, no ship. Nov 1806 is the Rezanov/Juno year and six years before Ross, so these are most likely RAC Kodiak/Aleut contract hunters, but the leaf does not say so.

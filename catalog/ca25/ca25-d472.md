**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n259–n261** · Sav p. hw 9 / stamp 257 (doc spans hw 9-11 / stamps 257-259) · tomo p. p.40-42 · 1800-07-02 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: '+ 1800 = Julio 2 = Monterrey. (Sin firma ni direccion.) Sobre siembras insendiadas por indios Ausaymes.' Ausayames rancheria 9 leguas E of Mision de San Juan Bautista has thrice threatened to burn it; aid earlier ordered from Pueblo de San Jose and 'Rancho de Rl Has.da'; they killed '5 gentiles de los Montssones (sic.)'; Sargento Macario Castro sent with a partida, jailed 2 indios two months. Spans n259-n261.

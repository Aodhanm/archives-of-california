**C-A 25 (Provincial Records), scan n348** · Sav p. 346/97. · tomo p. p.256-57 · 1807-02-19 · Savage abstract · 

'19. Sobre barco Americano pp 256-57 (2 doc.)' On an American ship. Savage gives the subject line only, no vessel name, master, cargo, or place, and marks two documents with no per-document detail. Sits one day after the unidentified-vessel report, so the two are probably the same episode, but the leaf does not connect them.

# Heading verbatim: 1828. Febrero 1o Sant.a Barb.a / Anastacio Carrillo. Entry verbatim: Certifica: la Revista pasada a la

**C-A 56 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n90** · Sav p. 91 · tomo p. p. 577 · 1828-02-01 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: 1828. Febrero 1o Sant.a Barb.a / Anastacio Carrillo. Entry verbatim: Certifica: la Revista pasada a la Comp.a de Caball.a Presidial, followed by ditto leader marks and the cite p. 577. Gloss: Carrillo certifies the review passed on the presidial cavalry company. The fourth letter of pasada is ambiguous on the leaf and may read parada.

# Leaf heading verbatim: 1823. S. Franco / Ygo Martinez al Gefe. S. Luis A. Arguello. Entry verbatim: Abril 1o. Da parte h

**C-A 56 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n81** · Sav p. 82 · tomo p. pp. 537-8 · 1823-04-01 · Savage abstract · 

Leaf heading verbatim: 1823. S. Franco / Ygo Martinez al Gefe. S. Luis A. Arguello. Entry verbatim: Abril 1o. Da parte haber fondeado a las 11 del dia en este Puerto el Bergn Yngs Juan Begg, en el mismo ofo participa haber Caido a las Cercanias del Rancho de S Pedro el Indio ladron Ponponio. Gloss: the English brig Juan Begg anchored at 11 in the morning; the same oficio reports that the Indian robber Ponponio (Savage spells it with two n, the outlaw usually written Pomponio) had come down upon the neighbourhood of the Rancho de San Pedro. The sense of Caido a is raided rather than captured, since he was still at large.

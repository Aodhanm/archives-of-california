**C-A 56 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n89** · Sav p. 90 · tomo p. p. 575 · 1828-02-01 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: 1828. Santa Barbara / Anastacio Carrillo ( No dice Con q.e Cara.cter), the last word written with a superscript contraction over Car. Entry verbatim: Feb.o 1o Certifica la Revista pasada por el a la Comp.a de Cab.a de Sta Barbara. Gloss: Carrillo certifies the muster review he passed on the Santa Barbara cavalry company; Savage notes in the heading that the document does not state in what capacity the signer signs.

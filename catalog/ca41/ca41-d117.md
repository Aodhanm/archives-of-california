Appointment notice. 'Que Antonio Trejo ha sido nombrado Celador de la aduana de Monterey con el sueldo de $700 anuales.' [= Antonio Trejo named celador (customs guard) at Monterey, $700 a year]. Same salary band as the two celadores listed in the March 1845 payroll.

# Savage records only the heading and the words 'Benito Diaz, Receptor.' with no further abstract — an unelaborated entry 

**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n117** · Sav p. 117 / 10 · tomo p. p 481 · 1835 Ag.to 26 · Savage abstract · 

Savage records only the heading and the words 'Benito Diaz, Receptor.' with no further abstract — an unelaborated entry noting Benito Diaz acting as Receptor at Santa Barbara. (Cf. his formal appointment at n121, orig. p 772, dated 1835 Julio 3, which stands EARLIER in date but LATER in the volume's pagination.)

# Verbatim heading (no date line; Savage enters it as a book, marked with his cross): Libro de fianzas de la Aduana Mariti

**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n139** · Sav p. 139 / 15 · tomo p. p. 428-80 · s.f. (account book for the fiscal year ended 30 June 1834) · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading (no date line; Savage enters it as a book, marked with his cross): Libro de fianzas de la Aduana Maritima de S.n Diego; por dros. pertenecientes á la Federacion, correspond.te al año economico que finalizo en 30 de Junio 1834. English gloss: Bond register of the San Diego Maritime Custom House, for duties belonging to the Federation, for the fiscal year ended 30 June 1834. Substance: an entire bond book (libro de fianzas) for the San Diego aduana, the security instruments taken for federal duties, covering the economic year to 30 June 1834, that is the Angel Ramirez period. It is the San Diego counterpart to the Libro manual de Cargo y Data for the San Francisco aduana entered just above it on n138. Entirely on gap leaf n139; cite p. 428 - 80.

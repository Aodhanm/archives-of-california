**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n101** · Sav p. 101 / 3 · tomo p. p. 126-127 · 1844-05-21 · Savage abstract · 

Appointment notice. 'Que J.e M.a Cruz ha sido nombrado Alcayde de la aduana de Mont.y con $1000 de sueldo annual y con dispensa del requisito de posesion.' [= Jose Maria Cruz named alcaide (warehouse keeper) of the Monterey custom house at $1,000 a year, with the bond/possession requirement waived]. Amount verified at high magnification as 1000; agrees with the payroll on n102.

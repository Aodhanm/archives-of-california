**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n105** · Sav p. 105 / 7 · tomo p. p. 478 · 1845-12-30 · Savage abstract · 

'Que el dia 20 del p.p.o recibio de J. B. Alvarado la adm.n interina de la aduana.' [= on 20 November 1845 he took back the interim administration of the custom house from Alvarado]. Closes the loop opened by the 15 May 1845 letter above; Alvarado's tenure as administrator therefore ran 1 May to 20 Nov 1845.

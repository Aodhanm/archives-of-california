# 'Que en el dia presto juramento del destino de receptor quedando en actividad de su empleo.' [= took the oath of office 

**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n106** · Sav p. 106 / 8 · tomo p. p. 716. · 1846-04-19 · Savage abstract · 

'Que en el dia presto juramento del destino de receptor quedando en actividad de su empleo.' [= took the oath of office as receptor that day and is now active in the post]. Pairs with Fitch's resignation two months earlier: Carrillo is his successor at San Diego. LAST document of the Vol. VI section; the leaf then closes with a rule, '(Fin)', and the copyist's note quoted in issues.

# Verbatim heading: 1845. - Set. 26. Mont. / Admor. Alvarado - al Receptor B. Diaz. English gloss: Administrator J. B. Alv

**C-A 41 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Custom House)), scan n134-n135** · Sav p. 134 / 10 · tomo p. p. 242 · 1845 Set. 26 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: 1845. - Set. 26. Mont. / Admor. Alvarado - al Receptor B. Diaz. English gloss: Administrator J. B. Alvarado to Receptor Benito Diaz (San Francisco), Monterey, 26 September 1845. Substance, Savage abstract verbatim: Trascrive la of. f.ha. 9 del corriente relativa á que no ponga embarazo á buques balleneros q.e fueran hacer la aguada al Sausalito, ni estorbase q.e saliesen sus chalupas á buscar los viveres necesarios á los ranchos de su inmediacion. (The abbreviation read here as f.ha. is written in a compressed flourish; the sense is oficio dated the 9th of the current month.) The Monterey aduana forwards an order restraining the San Francisco receptor: he is not to obstruct whalers going to water at Sausalito, nor stop their boats from going to the neighbouring ranchos to buy provisions. This is the administrative reversal that the earlier hard line against Sausalito (see the published n132 row of 24 Feb 1844) had imposed. The heading stands at the foot of n134 and the body runs onto gap leaf n135, closing there with the cite p. 242.

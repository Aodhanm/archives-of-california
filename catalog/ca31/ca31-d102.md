# Dictamen on *'las amenazas de José Castro'*: his communications attack all guarantees/rights; since the *'comandante gen

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n65–n68** · Sav p. ~62–65 · tomo p. VII:240–244 · 1846-06-22 · verbatim transcription · 

Dictamen on *'las amenazas de José Castro'*: his communications attack all guarantees/rights; since the *'comandante general interino'* with men *'cuyas costumbres inspiran toda clase de desconfianzas'* may approach, arm the district to bar *'estos malhechores'*; accuses Castro of disposing of the department *'á su arbitrio,'* imposing estado de sitio/martial law, *'erigiéndose en un dictador absoluto.'* Signed by ~80 LA vecinos & foreign residents — Requena, Figueroa, Botello, Temple, Workman, Vignes, Cota, Sepúlveda, Dalton, Bell, Carpenter, Alexander, Sexton, Snyder, Wolfskill, Flügge, Barton, Stone, B.D. Wilson, Barnett, F.P.F. Temple, Robidoux, Hugo Reid, Rowland, Palomares, Coronel, V. Sánchez, Chávez

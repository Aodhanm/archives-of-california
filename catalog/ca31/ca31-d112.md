# 'Incita á oposición': encloses Sloat's official summons and his own replies; marching south with 160 men to unite with P

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n77–n79** · Sav p. 73–74 · tomo p. VII:266–269 · 1846-07-11 · Savage abstract · 

'Incita á oposición': encloses Sloat's official summons and his own replies; marching south with 160 men to unite with Pico *'pª salvar el país y el decoro del gobierno Nacional'*; *'aun es tiempo'* — the invaders hold only Monterey and San Francisco; urges gathering men, matériel and horses; reports all American emigrants gathered at Sacramento and Sonoma under Capt. Frémont, marching overland per the squadron's orders, 'the north abandoned'

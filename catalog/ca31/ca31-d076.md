# Treasury quarrel: asks to suspend changes to the departmental treasury administration until they meet, citing 'la invasi

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n41–n42** · Sav p. 41–42 · tomo p. VII:174–176 · 1846-03-01 · Savage abstract · 

Treasury quarrel: asks to suspend changes to the departmental treasury administration until they meet, citing 'la invasión' and the northern frontier's danger; *'la introducción continua de aventureros de los Estados Unidos me obliga á no poderme alejar mucho de estos puntos'*

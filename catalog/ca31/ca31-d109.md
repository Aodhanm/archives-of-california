# [= published record #4] Landing orders to the American squadron (in English), bound into the Californio archive: *'We ar

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n73–n75** · Sav p. 70–71 · tomo p. VII:261–262 · 1846-07-07 · verbatim transcription · 

[= published record #4] Landing orders to the American squadron (in English), bound into the Californio archive: *'We are now about to land on the territory of Mexico, with whom the U.S. are at war… our duty to take California but to preserve it afterwards as a part of the U.S. at all hazards… cultivate the good opinion of the inhabitants, whom we must reconcile'*; boats to stay near shore ready to repel attack; *'No man is to quit the ranks or to enter any house… avoid that eternal disgrace… by indignity offered to a single female'*; *'Plunder of every kind is strictly forbidden'*; *'before God and our country.'* ⚠ orig page is VII:261–262 (the published record #4 gave 281–2 — corrected here)

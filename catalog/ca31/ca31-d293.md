# Records the Comandante's use of armed force to make the outgoing Gastelum respect Mancilla's credential; Gastelum's obst

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n186–n188** · Sav p. 52–54 · tomo p. VIII:151–153 · 1845-01-26 · Savage abstract · 

Records the Comandante's use of armed force to make the outgoing Gastelum respect Mancilla's credential; Gastelum's obstinate refusal (alleging no order from the Political Government, not recognizing Mancilla's credential); the handover done at Ensenada owing to Gastelum's grave illness; the religious oath; Gastelum's note that the Comandante had offered to back his refusal *(copy signed by Mancilla, Santo Tomás)*

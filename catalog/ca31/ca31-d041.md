# Orders for LA's defense / Castro's movements: Pico will *'dar un golpe mortal á la hidra peligrosa'*; Stearns to *'escit

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n27** · Sav p. 27 · tomo p. VII:92–95 · 1846-06-21 · Savage abstract · 

Orders for LA's defense / Castro's movements: Pico will *'dar un golpe mortal á la hidra peligrosa'*; Stearns to *'escitar, promover y apurar hasta la hez á todo el vecindario'* to march to Sta Bárbara under Juan Gallardo and defend the city against 'los anarquistas y los sospechosos'; intelligence from the British warship anchored at Sta Bárbara that Castro reached Monterey the 14th with 70 men then vanished. Copia certificada, Moreno

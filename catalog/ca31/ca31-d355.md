# On the Governor's demand that the Assembly relocate to Santa Bárbara (Pico making it *'responsable ante Dios y la nación

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n247–n248** · Sav p. 112–113 · tomo p. VIII:374–376 · 1846-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

On the Governor's demand that the Assembly relocate to Santa Bárbara (Pico making it *'responsable ante Dios y la nación'*): the commission declines — the capital [LA] is not invaded and is the Assembly's legal seat; the Government is itself heading north; the Assembly has always heeded it. Examined 8 documents on the Sonoma events (*'dos aspectos, uno como causa nacional y otro como atentado criminal de aventureros'*); asks the government *'si hay datos positivos pª considerar el hecho de los aventureros como obra del Gobierno Americano'*; excite the pueblos to defense; establish a weekly correo

# Order to raise 50 men 'pª escarmentar los invasores': transcribes Manuel Castro's parte (marching Monterey→San Juan Baut

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n29–n30** · Sav p. 29–30 · tomo p. VII:104–106 · 1846-06-23 · Savage abstract · 

Order to raise 50 men 'pª escarmentar los invasores': transcribes Manuel Castro's parte (marching Monterey→San Juan Bautista, 19 Jun); Pico orders Stearns + the capital's judges to raise 50 armed men and march north *'á escarmentar aquellos infames aventureros'*; declares traitors *'los ciudadanos mejicanos que no quieren prestarse'*

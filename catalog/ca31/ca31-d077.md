# Post-secularization clergy poverty: claims the former-mission finca as the parish house; asks whether he is not entitled

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n42–n43** · Sav p. 42–43 · tomo p. VII:177–178 · 1846-03-10 · Savage abstract · 

Post-secularization clergy poverty: claims the former-mission finca as the parish house; asks whether he is not entitled to the church's property for the cult and his subsistence now the padres' sínodo is gone; two years served unpaid; warns of *'el abandono de estas almas que volverán pronto á la gentilidad — ya no será por cobardía'*

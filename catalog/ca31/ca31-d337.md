# 'Proyecto de decreto sobre Misiones' — the operative text of Pico's 1846 mission liquidation. Preamble (verbatim): the m

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n224–n229** · Sav p. 89–94 · tomo p. VIII:328–334 · 1846-03-23 · verbatim transcription · 

'Proyecto de decreto sobre Misiones' — the operative text of Pico's 1846 mission liquidation. Preamble (verbatim): the mission system was *'una conquista suave, llena de piedad… el simiento de la prosperidad'* but *'debía concluirse'* to raise upon it *'el edificio de la riqueza pública'*; population growth + the colonization laws increased *'capitales, industria y propietarios'*; the missions' decadence and debts compel action (refs the decrees of 15 & 28 May 1845). Articles: Art. 1 — the govt may act to avoid the total ruin of San Gabriel, San Luis Rey, San Diego & like missions; Art. 2 — bankruptcy laws apply to their debts; Art. 3 — if sale to private parties is determined, by public auction (remate público) with the customary notices; Art. 4 — after debts are paid, any surplus goes to the Indians of the community of the sold estate; Art. 5 — always ensure the subsistence of the padres ministros *(continues n230)*

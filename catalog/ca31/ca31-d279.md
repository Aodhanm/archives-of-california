# On 'falta de juzgados, desórdenes cometidos por oficiales': his resignation declined; the substitute alcalde abolished t

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n174–n179** · Sav p. 40–44 · tomo p. VIII:113–119 · 1845-06-12 · Savage abstract · 

On 'falta de juzgados, desórdenes cometidos por oficiales': his resignation declined; the substitute alcalde abolished the court's rulings, put a foreigner over municipal funds at 20%, clashed with garrison officers, surrendered the vara and quit; Monterey went a month with no judge; Capt. José de la Torre named but 'muchos no lo quieren obedecer'; the officers went out 'en gallo' beating Mexicans and smashing their windows; cattle-thefts rising; the jail turned to a guardhouse, prisoners freed after 2–3 days; officers tore down the posted bando bearing his signature; the breakdown extends to San Juan, San José, Santa Cruz, San Francisco; 'los juzgados quedan cerrados'

# [= published record #6] 'Matanza de Indios': his 22 Aug punitive expedition against *'indios ladrones'* attacked a ranch

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n85** · Sav p. 80 · tomo p. VII:285 · 1846-08-23 · Savage abstract · 

[= published record #6] 'Matanza de Indios': his 22 Aug punitive expedition against *'indios ladrones'* attacked a ranchería of the Amajaques — *'que contenía 5 hombres y 5 mujeres. Los hombres todos murieron, cuatro mujeres tomamos y una niña, una mujer escapó con algunos muchachos'*; the horse and horsehide proved them the raiders. ⚠ my direct read = 5 women (4 taken + 1 escaped) + a girl; the published record #6 says '8 women' — flagged as a probable transcription error in the targeted pass, to reconcile at volume-end

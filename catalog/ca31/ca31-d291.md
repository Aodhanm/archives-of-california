# 'Sobre atropellamiento': Gastelum, ordered to exercise the frontier juez-de-paz office, would not convene the vecindario

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n184–n185** · Sav p. 49–50 · tomo p. VIII:147–149 · 1845-10-14 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre atropellamiento': Gastelum, ordered to exercise the frontier juez-de-paz office, would not convene the vecindario at San Vicente to rotate the office despite his word; Mancilla was surprised in his home by four armed men without a warrant, assaulted, and dragged out before passing vecinos

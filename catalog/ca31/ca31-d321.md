# Itemized claim against the government (via Castro, Alvarado & Carrillo, chiefs of the Division of the North) for goods l

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n216–n217** · Sav p. 81–82 · tomo p. VIII:294–295 · 1845-03-01 · Savage abstract · 

Itemized claim against the government (via Castro, Alvarado & Carrillo, chiefs of the Division of the North) for goods lost in the Feb-1845 Micheltorena campaign at San Buenaventura — a saddle (30), bridle, 2 sarapes, valise, saltcellar, tongs, rifle (50), garniel, horse (15) = 121 pesos; the gov. gave 30 cash + a 91-peso draft on Stearns

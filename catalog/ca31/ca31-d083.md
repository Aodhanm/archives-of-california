# Deliberations on Frémont at Gavilan Peak: Castro reports Frémont *'aparapetado en la sierra del Gabilan'* awaiting reinf

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n50–n51** · Sav p. 50–51 · tomo p. VII:194–197 · 1846-04-11 · verbatim transcription · 

Deliberations on Frémont at Gavilan Peak: Castro reports Frémont *'aparapetado en la sierra del Gabilan'* awaiting reinforcements though he hid his aims from the U.S. consul; commissioner Andrés Castillero sought supreme-govt aid; the junta finds Castro's presence and the north's fortification indispensable, invites Pico to confer (else establish the cuartel general at Sta Clara). Signed Castro, M.G. Vallejo, J.B. Alvarado, Víctor Prudon, José Antonio Carrillo, Manuel Castro; sec. Francisco Arce

# Familiar letter on the 1845 treasury transaction with Castro — reducing the dept's revenues to what the Monterey customs

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n137–n139** · Sav p. 4–6 · tomo p. VIII:10–11 · 1845-07-28 · Savage abstract · 

Familiar letter on the 1845 treasury transaction with Castro — reducing the dept's revenues to what the Monterey customs produce, *'que corta de raíz las diferencias'*; paying preferentially the debts *'contraídos por nosotros y por el General Micheltorena'* will restore commercial confidence; good administration *'es la clave de la prosperidad en todos los países cultos'*

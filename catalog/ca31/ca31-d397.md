# On a conspiracy by foreigners: denunciations of a plot for *'asesinatos, robos y otros desórdenes horribles'*; deserting

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n274–n275** · Sav p. 139–140 · tomo p. VIII:480–483 · 1846 · Savage abstract · 

On a conspiracy by foreigners: denunciations of a plot for *'asesinatos, robos y otros desórdenes horribles'*; deserting sailors hiding in the unpopulated country; the Prefect (Castro) to apprehend every foreigner who entered without legal requisites (excepting those married in the country or with capital & an honest livelihood), secure them from the ranchos to San Francisco, embark them and remit them as prisoners to the Supreme Government at Acapulco (the same in the South)

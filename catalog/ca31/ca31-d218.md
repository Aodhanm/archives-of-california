# The brig *Soledad* brought the Ministro de Relaciones' communications: the Supreme Govt's *'mejores intenciones'* for Ca

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n134–n135** · Sav p. 1–2 · tomo p. VIII:5–7 · 1845-07-11 · Savage abstract · 

The brig *Soledad* brought the Ministro de Relaciones' communications: the Supreme Govt's *'mejores intenciones'* for California; wishes the Assembly propose laws and nominate a President; will send 'una división… con un buen Gefe que no oprima al Ciudadano pacífico' to sustain the authorities and defend against foreign aggression

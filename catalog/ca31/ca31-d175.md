# Urges Pico to come to Monterey and show energy sustaining his authority *'amenazada por unos hombres inquietos'* — if he

**C-A 31 (Departmental State Papers), scan n111–n112** · Sav p. 106–107 · tomo p. VII:382–383 · 1846-03-02 · Savage abstract · 

Urges Pico to come to Monterey and show energy sustaining his authority *'amenazada por unos hombres inquietos'* — if he stays away *'no será gobernador sino de los Ángeles'*; the national treasury the first thing he and Castro must settle; the commission gone to Mexico (hopes not like Castañares's)

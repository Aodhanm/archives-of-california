# CONTENT NOTE ONLY: missions weave most of their own blankets, sayal and jerga, advancing in tanning and in shoes, costal

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n393** · Sav p. 392 / 68. · tomo p. p.[p.p. 239-242. closes here] · 1798 Feb. 1.o [continuation] · Savage abstract · 

CONTENT NOTE ONLY: missions weave most of their own blankets, sayal and jerga, advancing in tanning and in shoes, costales, aparejos, cojinillos and boots; pottery and ribbons at Monterey; soap works advancing; a water mill building at Sta Cruz under engineer Córdoba and one finished at S.n Luis Obispo; «los americanos dejados por Dorr no aceptaron armar el molino de arte mayor - pero hicieron una lancha» [= the crewmen left by Ebenezer Dorr of the Otter, 1796]; at villa Branciforte the nine Guadalajara settlers, 2 invalids and a discharged soldier work with their families: «A todos se les obliga al trabajo diario». Only «450 personas de todas edades y sexos... de gente de razon» in N.a Cal.a; «En el manejo de mis.s no se mescla el gob.no solo hace sus insinuac.s verbales». In 97 «5 mis.s en N.a Cal.a, 4 por S.n Fr.co [?= colegio de S.n Fernando] y 1 por dominicos». [Duplicate of the n392 row — do NOT ingest twice.]

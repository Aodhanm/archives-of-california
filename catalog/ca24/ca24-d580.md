# (3 docs) Remits 25½ arrobas of hemp for San Blas (asks Real Hacienda pay 3½ pesos/arroba); retirement request of surgeon

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n428** · Sav p. 427 · tomo p. 103 · 1798 Oct 23–24 · Savage abstract · 

(3 docs) Remits 25½ arrobas of hemp for San Blas (asks Real Hacienda pay 3½ pesos/arroba); retirement request of surgeon Pablo Soler; license to P. Manuel Fernández ('carácter intrépido y violento… mal manejo en las misiones y con los neófitos'); proposes extracting 1,000–1,500 fan. wheat @ 20 rls to supply ships & benefit the 'infelices' farmers of San José & Branciforte [pp.431–5]

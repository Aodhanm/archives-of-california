# “q.e pueden pedir al Sup.or Gob.no se retiren los vecinos de Buenavista.” [= that they may petition the superior governm

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. stamp 342 / ink 18. · tomo p. p. 57. · 1802 Junio 16 · Savage abstract · 

“q.e pueden pedir al Sup.or Gob.no se retiren los vecinos de Buenavista.” [= that they may petition the superior government for the withdrawal of the Buenavista vecinos]. The sequel to his February refusal: he will not order the eviction himself but points the Carmelo padres to Mexico. Settles the place-name spelling.

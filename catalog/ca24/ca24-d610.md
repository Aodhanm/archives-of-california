**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n384** · Sav p. 383 / 59. · tomo p. p. 211-2. · 1797 Nov. 13 (heading “Id.”) · Savage abstract · 

“Dice q.e no se remitió el quinto de perlas porq.e Arrillaga estaba reconociendo terreno de la frontera y del Colorado y su encargado Fr.co Roa[?] en Loreto era de poco empeño.” = the royal fifth on pearls was not remitted because Arrillaga was reconnoitring the frontier and Colorado country and his deputy Francisco Roa[?] at Loreto was slack.

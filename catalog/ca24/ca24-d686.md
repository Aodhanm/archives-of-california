# BORICA'S REPORT OF THE MEASURES HE TOOK IN 1796. Underlined heading: “Informa de las providencias q.e tomó en 1796.” Fou

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n404** · Sav p. 403 / 79. · tomo p. p. 288-292. (cite closes at foot of the entry on n405) · 1797 Febrero 1 [Id.] · Savage abstract · 

BORICA'S REPORT OF THE MEASURES HE TOOK IN 1796. Underlined heading: “Informa de las providencias q.e tomó en 1796.” Founded primary schools at the San Francisco and Loreto presidios (so all presidios of the peninsula and the pueblo of San José now have them); introduced small stock at San José, Los Angeles, Monterey and San Francisco, driven 130 leagues; doubled agriculture at San José; promoted hemp. Verbatim: “Con motivo de haber dejado en Monterrey el capitan amer.o Dorr varios individuos de su nacion y de la Inglesa, me aproveché de su industria p.a emprender la fabrica de una lancha capaz, un molino de arte mayor y carretas ligeras y de mejor construcc.n q.e los del pais.” Also six Spanish youths learning tailoring; neophytes of Mission San Juan taught to make blankets and cotton cloth; no apprentices found for ribbon-making; sites reconnoitred for settlement by Borica personally and by Alberni with Córdoba; San Diego fort and esplanade inspected by Córdoba, a spring recovered, and strict orders given against gente de razón committing excesses with christian and gentile india women [continues on n405].

# 'Ranchos reales. Aranceles': defends the price-schedules (aranceles) & the Ranchos Reales against mission market power —

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n425–n426** · Sav p. 424–425 · tomo p. 100–101 · 1798 Agosto 4 · Savage abstract · 

'Ranchos reales. Aranceles': defends the price-schedules (aranceles) & the Ranchos Reales against mission market power — since all the missions 'componen un cuerpo que podría dar la ley/precio que gustara á los consumidores,' aranceles forestall monopoly & protect the soldier's family; to be extinguished when the peninsula is more populated; Mission SF had 710 souls end-1797 incl. 50 huidos, 110 died / 15.07% over 4 yrs [pp.411–18]

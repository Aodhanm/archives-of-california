# First document of the new double-underlined section “Arillaga á Presidentes y Misioneros de la N.a Cal.” — “Aprueba se r

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n339** · Sav p. stamp 338 / ink 14. · tomo p. p. 52. · 1803 Sept. 6 · Savage abstract · 

First document of the new double-underlined section “Arillaga á Presidentes y Misioneros de la N.a Cal.” — “Aprueba se retire el P. Isidoro Barcenilla y reconoce su exacto cumplim.to como Ministro.” [= approves the retirement of Padre Isidoro Barcenilla and acknowledges his exact fulfilment as minister]. Alta California Franciscan, governor's testimonial on the record.

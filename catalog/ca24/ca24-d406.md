# “Da licencia á q.e se retire P. Jacinto Lopez, M.tro de la mis.n S.n J.n Bautista. y dará el corresp.te aviso al comand.

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n340** · Sav p. stamp 339 / ink 15. · tomo p. p. 54 · 1801 Agosto 31 · Savage abstract · 

“Da licencia á q.e se retire P. Jacinto Lopez, M.tro de la mis.n S.n J.n Bautista. y dará el corresp.te aviso al comand.te de la fragata Princesa D.n Ramon Saavedra.” [= grants leave for Padre Jacinto López, minister of Mission San Juan Bautista, to retire, and will notify the commander of the frigate Princesa, Don Ramón Saavedra]. Ties a named friar's departure to a named ship and its named commander.

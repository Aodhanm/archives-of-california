Borica's three-point programme for the province, Savage quoting: “Dice q.e para el progreso del pais se necesita: aumentar su pueblo con familias de todos oficios y artes: abrir la communicac.n segura con las Prov.s de Sonora y Nuevo Mex.o y entablar un comercio directo con nuestras Islas Filipinas” [= to advance the country one must increase its population with families of every trade and craft, open secure communication with Sonora and New Mexico, and establish direct trade with the Philippines].

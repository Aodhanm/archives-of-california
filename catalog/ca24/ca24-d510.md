# 'Hablando de las misones de S.n Fr.co y Sta Clara dice qe se espera catequisar 800 gentiles, siendo la causa (de present

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n362** · Sav p. 361 | 37. · tomo p. p.121. · 1795 Feb. 1. · Savage abstract · 

'Hablando de las misones de S.n Fr.co y Sta Clara dice qe se espera catequisar 800 gentiles, siendo la causa (de presentarse a las misiones) la grande escasez de semillas qe padecen' [speaking of the missions of San Francisco and Santa Clara, says 800 gentiles are expected to be catechised, the cause of their presenting themselves at the missions being the great scarcity of seeds they suffer]. Famine-driven conversion; the parenthesis is Savage's interlinear insertion.

# Will watch that counterfeit coin is not introduced, and denies that foreign vessels have left any. Verbatim: “‘En esta p

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n403** · Sav p. 402 / 78. · tomo p. p. 287 · 1797 Enero 20 [Id.] · Savage abstract · 

Will watch that counterfeit coin is not introduced, and denies that foreign vessels have left any. Verbatim: “‘En esta prov.a no se introduce mas moneda q.e la q.e remite el Hab. Gral y Minist. de S.n Blas. Ninguna han dejado los buques estrang.s ... ni el bergantin activo q.e refrescó en Monterey, á su retirada de China.’”

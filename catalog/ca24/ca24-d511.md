# Abstract struck through in Savage's own hand except the words 'ajustes de la tropa'. What is legible under the cancellat

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n362** · Sav p. 361 | 37. · tomo p. p.122 · 1795 Feb. 9. · Savage abstract · 

Abstract struck through in Savage's own hand except the words 'ajustes de la tropa'. What is legible under the cancellation: 'Pide qe los M.ros de R.l Hac.a concluyan los ajustes de la tropa de la Peninsa' [asks that the ministers of the Royal Treasury complete the pay-settlements of the Peninsula troops]. Recorded as written, with the cancellation noted rather than silently dropped.

# routine travel: leaves this mild-temperature site, sails via S. Luis in the goleta; the 24th leaves Loreto in the goleta

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n458** · Sav p. 457 (=134) · tomo p. p. 572; pp. 573 · 1794 Julio 20 / Julio 28 · Savage abstract · 

routine travel: leaves this mild-temperature site, sails via S. Luis in the goleta; the 24th leaves Loreto in the goleta with family; P. Apolinario to accompany; disembark at Sta Ana with family & part of the crew, overland via fuero to S. Fernando; asks 12 mules, 12 escort-soldiers, & '80 ó 100 Indios de la mis.ⁿ p.ᵃ ayudar en el camino' (80–100 mission Indians to help on the road)

# (cont. Vancouver order) ⭐⭐⭐ if any foreigner comes to the immediate missions, hold absolutely no direct or indirect deal

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n467** · Sav p. 466 (=142) · tomo p. p. 589; + · 1794 Nov 12 / Nov 25 · Savage abstract · 

(cont. Vancouver order) ⭐⭐⭐ if any foreigner comes to the immediate missions, hold absolutely no direct or indirect dealing 'con sus oficiales y tripulaciones ni menos proveerlos de víveres' except in case of genuine need to take some port, then furnish only the aid hospitality requires; names capt. George Vancouver & capt. Puget commanding the fragata 'Discovery' y bergantín 'Chatham' — if they arrive at a port under pretext of resupply/watering, be watchful; Nov 25 to Sta Cruz: Vancouver

# to López: hurry Morrison to finish the mill per the fathers' instructions; ⭐ Agto 14 'Estados por mis.' — asks each miss

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n472** · Sav p. 471 (=148) · tomo p. pp. 603–4; 604 · 1795 Julio 27 / Agto 14 · Savage abstract · 

to López: hurry Morrison to finish the mill per the fathers' instructions; ⭐ Agto 14 'Estados por mis.' — asks each mission send one of the enclosed model-forms so exact notices of its state reach him by March yearly; could add lines for harvest — wine, aguardiente, panocha, figs — but leaves to the ministers' prudence

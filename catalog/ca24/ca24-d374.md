# to Salazar 'Cuestion de autoridad' — advises conforming to his prelate's dispositions but always w/ love toward the Indi

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n471** · Sav p. 470 (=147) · tomo p. p. 601; 601; 602–3 · 1795 Julio 23 / Julio 23 / Julio 23 · Savage abstract · 

to Salazar 'Cuestion de autoridad' — advises conforming to his prelate's dispositions but always w/ love toward the Indians & the troop; the govt rarely intervenes in questions btwn prelates & subjects; to Landaeta: glad the Indian question resolved w/o greater harm on the far shore, advises the fathers keep peace w/ the Indians; ⭐ to Pallás: 'Dice q. su mujer dará luz pronto á un 'Californio ó una Californiaʼ' (says his wife will soon give birth to a Californian)

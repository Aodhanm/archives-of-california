# 'Trato de los Indios en las misiones': the rigores against the Indians of Mission San Francisco (Dolores) ceased since O

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n422–n423** · Sav p. 421–422 · tomo p. 97–98 · 1798 Julio 1 · Savage abstract · 

'Trato de los Indios en las misiones': the rigores against the Indians of Mission San Francisco (Dolores) ceased since Oct 1796 — now three hot meals daily, no whipping/mistreatment, more prudent labor; credits repeated confidential letters to P. Pres. Lasuén; names P. José María Fernández (prior SF minister) as the true author of the abuses, who feuded with companion P. Martín Landaeta over abandoning 'the old method,' then retired to his colegio 'con los sentidos algo trastornados' [pp.403–4]

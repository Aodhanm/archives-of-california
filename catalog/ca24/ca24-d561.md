**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n372** · Sav p. 371 / 47. · tomo p. p. 167 · 1795 Junio 10 (heading “Id”) · Savage abstract · 

Whole abstract is a parenthesis: “(Cumanás[?] - puerto menor.)” = a note that Cumaná[?] is a minor port. Savage wrote no further abstract; the place-name reading is uncertain and the subject may be the customs classification of puertos menores.

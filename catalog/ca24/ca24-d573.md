# 'Notes sobre la misión Sta Cruz': per engineer Córdoba, 3,435,600 var² of irrigable land (2,315,700 opened / 1,120,000 u

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n424** · Sav p. 423 · tomo p. 99 · 1798 Agosto 1 · Savage abstract · 

'Notes sobre la misión Sta Cruz': per engineer Córdoba, 3,435,600 var² of irrigable land (2,315,700 opened / 1,120,000 unopened); pastoral lands 8–9 leguas × 1½, 7 permanent-water arroyos; Córdoba says no good site for a settlement between Sta Cruz & SF on the east; end-1797: 495 neophytes, 750 cattle, 500 horses, 29 mules, 1,560 sheep; harvest 200 fan. maize / 400 wheat / 8 beans-lentils; ⭐ ~11¾%/yr mortality 1792–96 [pp.408–10]

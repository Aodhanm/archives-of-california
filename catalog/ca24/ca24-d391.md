# Savage indexes this twice, first as a bare register line and then with the full heading; ONE row, see issues. “Dice q.e 

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n338** · Sav p. stamp 337 / ink 13. · tomo p. p.49. · 1804 En.o 30 · Savage abstract · 

Savage indexes this twice, first as a bare register line and then with the full heading; ONE row, see issues. “Dice q.e no se quiere meter en la cuestion q.e tiene con su padre compañero - hasta q.e decida el P. P.te - en seguida pide esplicaciones al P. P.te sobre el mismo asunto.” [= declines to intervene in his quarrel with his fellow padre until the Padre Presidente decides, and then asks the Presidente for explanations on the same matter]. A named quarrel between the two ministers of Santa Gertrudis.

# “lo ‘ruega y encarga’ sobre unas providen.s” = begs and charges him regarding certain provisions/dispositions. The phras

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n348** · Sav p. 347 / 23. · tomo p. p. 66 · 1805 Julio 9 · Savage abstract · 

“lo ‘ruega y encarga’ sobre unas providen.s” = begs and charges him regarding certain provisions/dispositions. The phrase ruega y encarga is Savage’s quoted extract from the original.

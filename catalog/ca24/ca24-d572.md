# 'Misión de San Luis Rey de Francia. Indios': founded 13 Jun 1798 at native site *'Tacaymé'*; surrounding Indians of 'bel

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n423–n424** · Sav p. 422–423 · tomo p. 98–99 · 1798 Agosto 1 · Savage abstract · 

'Misión de San Luis Rey de Francia. Indios': founded 13 Jun 1798 at native site *'Tacaymé'*; surrounding Indians of 'bella índole'; 77 baptized on founding day; captains of the immediate rancherías with wives + 29 more; with this the 5 approved missions are concluded, no novelty among gentiles except near San José from Christians fled from old Mission SF over prior mistreatment ('hoy se les da bueno y todo está tranquilo') [pp.406–7]

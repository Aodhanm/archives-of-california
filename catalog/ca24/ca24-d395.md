# “Pide q.e le mande la certificac.n dada por el medico sobre enfermedad del P. Mis.o Placido Sanz - p.a darle licencia de

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n338** · Sav p. stamp 337 / ink 13. · tomo p. p. 50. · 1805 Marzo 18 · Savage abstract · 

“Pide q.e le mande la certificac.n dada por el medico sobre enfermedad del P. Mis.o Placido Sanz - p.a darle licencia de embarque.” [= asks for the physician's certificate on the illness of missionary Plácido Sanz so as to grant him leave to embark]. Names the sitting Padre Presidente of the Baja missions, Miguel Gallego, and shows a medical certificate required before a friar could leave.

# begs Coller (no mules at S. Borja), forwards the letter to Arrillaga; to P. Mariano Fernández: the 24 leguas of the trip

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n462** · Sav p. 461 (=137) · tomo p. p. 578; 579–80 · 1794 Agto 4 / Agto 6(?) · Savage abstract · 

begs Coller (no mules at S. Borja), forwards the letter to Arrillaga; to P. Mariano Fernández: the 24 leguas of the trip; due to a very strong sun & the río of Sta Mª Magdalena being high, decided to make the trip overland from S. Marcos, arriving the 20th; 'un temperam.ᵗᵒ q. nos parece la gloria' — commends his health to God

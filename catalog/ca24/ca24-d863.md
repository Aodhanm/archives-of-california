# Agto 28: 'Solicitud de Pablo Soler. El habilitado de Monterey, Hermenegildo Sal, y Adm.ᵈᵒʳ del Ramo de Cruzada' — licens

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n454** · Sav p. 453 (=129) · tomo p. pp. 557–8; + · 1799 Agto 28 / Agto 29 · Savage abstract · 

Agto 28: 'Solicitud de Pablo Soler. El habilitado de Monterey, Hermenegildo Sal, y Adm.ᵈᵒʳ del Ramo de Cruzada' — licenses to PP. José de la Cruz Espí, Miguel Giribet, Agustín Merino & Benito Catalán; + Agto 29: 'Buques ingleses — Pelea con ellos' — Borica does not believe English ships are in the Gulf of Calif.; if they are, they are fishing; the most credible is that enemy ships try to trade w/ merchants of the south; sgto Aguilar withdrew w/ 14 men of the crew of the two that were near cabo San Lucas, w/ death of an Englishman

# “sobre dar libertad á un Indio Neofito preso.” [= on setting at liberty an imprisoned neophyte Indian]. The governor wri

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. stamp 342 / ink 18. · tomo p. p.58. · 1802 Dic. 16 · Savage abstract · 

“sobre dar libertad á un Indio Neofito preso.” [= on setting at liberty an imprisoned neophyte Indian]. The governor writing directly to the ministers of San Juan Capistrano about a single jailed neophyte; the Indian is not named. Direct evidence of gubernatorial review of mission punishment.

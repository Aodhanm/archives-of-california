# 'Miseria de los pobladores de San José y Los Ángeles': lacking any merchant to supply consumer goods, the settler famili

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n428–n429** · Sav p. 427–428 · tomo p. 103–104 · 1798 Oct 25 · Savage abstract · 

'Miseria de los pobladores de San José y Los Ángeles': lacking any merchant to supply consumer goods, the settler families are reduced to going 'casi desnudas' or buying from ship crews at prices ≥50% over the general prices of México; the necessity is extreme & all the pobladores 'disconsoladísimos' with the scarcity of goods [pp.435–6]

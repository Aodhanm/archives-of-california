# “Ha dado licencia al P. Antonio de la Concepcion, de S.n Miguel, se retire por loco.” = licence granted to padre Antonio

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n379** · Sav p. 378 / 54. · tomo p. p.190-1 · 1797 Set.bre 13 · Savage abstract · 

“Ha dado licencia al P. Antonio de la Concepcion, de S.n Miguel, se retire por loco.” = licence granted to padre Antonio de la Concepción of San Miguel to retire as insane. [= Fr. Antonio de la Concepción Horra, removed from newly founded Mission San Miguel; his later denunciation prompted the 1800 inquiry.] Page cite written without the p. prefix.

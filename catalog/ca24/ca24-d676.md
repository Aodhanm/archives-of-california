# Conveyed the thanks of King and viceroy to all in Antigua (Baja) California who contributed to the costs of the last war

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n402** · Sav p. 401 / 77. · tomo p. p. 279. · 1797 Enero 13 [Id.] · Savage abstract · 

Conveyed the thanks of King and viceroy to all in Antigua (Baja) California who contributed to the costs of the last war. Verbatim: “Dió las gracias del Rey y virrey, á todos individuos de la Ant.a Cal q.e contribuyeron p.a los gastos de la ult.a guerra.”

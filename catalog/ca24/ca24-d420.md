# “Conviene en q.e se retire el P. José Estevan a su colegio de S.n Fernando.” [= agrees that Padre José Estevan withdraw 

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. stamp 342 / ink 18. · tomo p. p. 58. · 1802 Ag.o 21 · Savage abstract · 

“Conviene en q.e se retire el P. José Estevan a su colegio de S.n Fernando.” [= agrees that Padre José Estevan withdraw to his College of San Fernando]. The College of San Fernando in Mexico marks him as an Alta California Franciscan.

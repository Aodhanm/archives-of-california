# “Sobre forma de certific.s de fe de vida y asistencia de los P.P. en sus ministerios.” [= on the form of the certificate

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. stamp 342 / ink 18. · tomo p. p.58 · 1802 Nov. 6 · Savage abstract · 

“Sobre forma de certific.s de fe de vida y asistencia de los P.P. en sus ministerios.” [= on the form of the certificates of life and of the padres' attendance at their ministries]. The paperwork by which friars' presence, and thus their stipends, was proved.

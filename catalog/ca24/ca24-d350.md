# (cont. monstruosidad) buried next morning by order of the 2nd corporal; + 'Sobre la quiebra del Ayud.ᵗᵉ Insp.ᵗᵒʳ D. Nico

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n448** · Sav p. 447 (=123) · tomo p. pp. 515–7; + · 1799 Marzo 27 / 1799 Abril 1 · Savage abstract · 

(cont. monstruosidad) buried next morning by order of the 2nd corporal; + 'Sobre la quiebra del Ayud.ᵗᵉ Insp.ᵗᵒʳ D. Nicolás Soler' (on the bankruptcy of Adjutant-Inspector Nicolás Soler); + Abril 1: 'Solicita licencia. Sus servicios' — Borica's own service record: 36 yrs served, 25 in continuous campaigns against barbarous nations, inspector of presidios, 10,478 leagues on horseback, asks to be placed at Villarcayo(?) or Alberni; wishes to go to Spain

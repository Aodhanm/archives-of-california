# Continuation of the friar-difficulties letter re P. Concepción / P. José of S. Miguel; licenses of PP. Diego García & Jo

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n440** · Sav p. 439 (=115) · tomo p. (cont.) · 1798 Dic · Savage abstract · 

Continuation of the friar-difficulties letter re P. Concepción / P. José of S. Miguel; licenses of PP. Diego García & José Mª Fernández retiring to the colegio; P. Fernández's mission-management; then a NEW Indian-treatment item begins: 'El trato ó tratam.ᵗᵒ q. se dá á los Indios es muy duro, en la mision de S. Fern.ᵈᵒ á ese cruel…' (the treatment given to the Indians is very harsh, in mission San Fernando cruel)

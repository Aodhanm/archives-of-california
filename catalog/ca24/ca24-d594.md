# “[interlined: Dice q.e] Remite la causa del indio Juyurt[?], de S.n Juan Capistrano q.n mató á su muger Tomasa Cojoni.” 

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n382** · Sav p. 381 / 57. · tomo p. p.200 · 1797 Oct. 17 (year inherited) · Savage abstract · 

“[interlined: Dice q.e] Remite la causa del indio Juyurt[?], de S.n Juan Capistrano q.n mató á su muger Tomasa Cojoni.” = forwards the criminal case of the Indian Juyurt[?] of San Juan Capistrano who killed his wife Tomasa Cojoni. Both Indian names are Savage's spellings; the man's name is an uncertain reading.

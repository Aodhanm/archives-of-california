# 'Intervencion — amenazas de …' — re Indians fled to the 'otra banda' (far shore) despite instructions to the contrary; c

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n470** · Sav p. 469 (=146) · tomo p. pp. 599–600 · 1795 Julio 5 · Savage abstract · 

'Intervencion — amenazas de …' — re Indians fled to the 'otra banda' (far shore) despite instructions to the contrary; concern the fugitives may draw others; Borica to preserve tranquility, if the Indians pass to the far shore providing others go too; his authority to abstain absolutely from despatching any Indian without pretext to the far shore

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n335** · Sav p. stamp 334 / ink 10. · tomo p. — · 1802 Junio 30 [Savage: ditto marks] · Savage abstract · 

“Se queja q.e no se dijo misa en Loreto (pres.) desde Pasqua de Espir.o S.to hasta f.ha y pide se ponga remedio.” [= complains that no mass has been said at the Loreto presidio from Pentecost until now, and asks that it be remedied]. Governor complaining of clerical neglect of the garrison.

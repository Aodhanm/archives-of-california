# CONTINUATION ONLY, listed for completeness and deliberately not counted as a document. Closes an abstract on Real Hacien

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n330** · Sav p. stamp 329 / ink 5. · tomo p. p. 22-23. · [continuation - date on n329] · Savage abstract · 

CONTINUATION ONLY, listed for completeness and deliberately not counted as a document. Closes an abstract on Real Hacienda accounting for the four Alta California presidios: computations of what the companies are owed, arrangement of the memorias requested through the viceroy, the habilitado general and the comisario of San Blas, and the comisario's complaint that he must buy for the new establishments at a season when good quality and easy prices are impossible. Ends flush right at p. 22-23.

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n336** · Sav p. stamp 335 / ink 11. · tomo p. p. 45. · 1802 Set.bre [day not given; yr inherited] · Savage abstract · 

“Acusa noticia de q.e en Abril, 28, - tomó posesion del empleo de Presid.te de las mis.s de Ant.a Cal.a” [= acknowledges the news that on 28 April he took possession of the office of President of the missions of Antigua California]. Dates a change in the Dominican presidency; the incumbent is not named here (Miguel Gallego appears in that office at n338).

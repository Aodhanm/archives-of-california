# Founding of Sta Catalina Martir on Nov.re 12 by P. José Loriente, comisionado of P. Pres.te Cayetano Pallás, with sarg.t

**C-A 24 (Provincial Records), scan n387** · Sav p. 386 / 62. (spans n387-n388) · tomo p. p. 223 · 1797 Dic. 6 [year inherited] · Savage abstract · 

Founding of Sta Catalina Martir on Nov.re 12 by P. José Loriente, comisionado of P. Pres.te Cayetano Pallás, with sarg.to Man.l Ruiz. Buildings described (church/granary 12 varas, oven, corral, guard-house, a small bulwark for three canoncitos). On Indian policy: «y qe hará castigar á los qe cometan alguna falta con ellos, adelante de los mismos Naturales agraviados» — offenders against Indians to be punished in front of the aggrieved natives. Continues on n388.

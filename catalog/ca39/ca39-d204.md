# Verbatim heading: '+ Enero 11 (2 docs) pp.107-9'. Substance: that he accompanies a recibo for 250 pesos received from th

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n255** · Sav p. 254 / 30 · tomo p. pp. 107-9 · 1843-01-11 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ Enero 11 (2 docs) pp.107-9'. Substance: that he accompanies a recibo for 250 pesos received from that Aduana to aid the espedicion of his mando; and that he has communicated to the Comandante Militar de Monterey that the Cap.n José Mejía and the Ten.te Mariano Villa are on licencia outside the Cuartel, and that the same or prorateo be conceded to them.

# Verbatim heading: '+ Diciembre 10 (2 docs.) pp.50-54 y 59', under the running heading carried from the preceding leaf, '

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n244** · Sav p. 243 / 19 · tomo p. pp. 50-54 y 59 · 1839-12-10 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ Diciembre 10 (2 docs.) pp.50-54 y 59', under the running heading carried from the preceding leaf, '1839 - Santa Bárbara / Juan B. Alvarado al Adm.or de Aduana de Montérey.' Gloss: two documents of 10 Dec 1839. Substance: approves the nombramiento of Fran.co Guerrero as Encargado interino de la Aduana fronteriza de S.n Fran.co; and communicates the contestacion given by the Comand.te Militar de S.ta Bárbara on the reclamacion made by the Adm.or of that Aduana over the derecho de tonelage charged at Santa Bárbara to the Goleta Ayacucho. The leaf head also carries Savage cross-reference '(vide p.20.*)'. [corner: copybook pen]

# Verbatim heading: '+ Marzo 8 pp.291-92', under the heading '1844. Los Angeles / Pio Pico al Adm.or de Aduana, Monterey.'

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n291** · Sav p. 290 / 66 · tomo p. pp. 291-92 · 1844-03-08 [so headed by Savage; I infer 1845, see issues] · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ Marzo 8 pp.291-92', under the heading '1844. Los Angeles / Pio Pico al Adm.or de Aduana, Monterey.' begun on the preceding leaf. Substance: that he pay to Don Juan Paty the sum of 1,000 pesos for the trasporte of the Gral D.n Manuel Micheltorrena and the other oficiales y tropa accompanying him, and that he make the payment before the departure from that Puerto of the buque Dn Quijote. See issues on the 1844 vs 1845 heading year. [corner: copybook pen]

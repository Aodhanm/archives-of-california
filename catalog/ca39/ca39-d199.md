# Verbatim heading: '+ Mayo 19. p.84', under the heading '1840 - Monterey / Alvarado al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' begun on 

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n249** · Sav p. 248 / 24 · tomo p. p. 84 · 1840-05-19 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ Mayo 19. p.84', under the heading '1840 - Monterey / Alvarado al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' begun on the preceding leaf. Substance: that in virtue of the proroga asked by Tomás Shear, Sobrecargo de la Fragata Manson, to sell his efectos al menudeo, it is conceded to him until the end of next August, the gobierno reserving the right to rescind this gracia if within the time prefixed 'se recibiesen otros efectos'.

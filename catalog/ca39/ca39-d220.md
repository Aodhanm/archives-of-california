# Verbatim heading: '1830. En.o 15. Campo Nacional / Jose Solis al Comand.te gral. de este Territorio.' Gloss: Solis, from

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n125** · Sav p. 125 [stamp] / 4 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 31-32 · 1830-01-15 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1830. En.o 15. Campo Nacional / Jose Solis al Comand.te gral. de este Territorio.' Gloss: Solis, from the National Camp, to the Comandante General of this Territory. Substance, following Savage closely: 'Que la tropa no entrega las armas mientras no sean pagados sus haveres, q.e si quiere tomarles algunas declaraciones puede citarme un lugar proporcionado p.a q.e contestemos y llevare cuatro soldados y un cabo p.a q.e ellos den sus quejas y VS. se satisfaga y p.a q.e vea si esto se ha causado por falta de VS. o de sus subalternos, q.e si quiere romper las ostilidades ellos estan prontos a sostenerse.' That is: the troops will not surrender their arms until their back pay is settled; if the Comandante General wishes to take depositions he may name a suitable place, and Solis will bring four soldiers and a corporal to state their grievances so that it may be seen whether the fault lies with the Comandante General or his subordinates; and if he wishes to open hostilities they are ready to stand their ground. A letter from the revolt camp itself, written while the rising was still armed.

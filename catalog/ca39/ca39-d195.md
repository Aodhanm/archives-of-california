**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n245** · Sav p. 244 / 20 · tomo p. p. 66 · 1839-07-26 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ 1839 - Julio 26 - Mont. / Man.l Jimeno al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' Substance: that he transcribed his oficio to the Comand.te Militar de S.ta Bárbara so that he abstain from charging derechos de tonelaje on the buques that touch at that rada. Pairs with the Ayacucho tonnage complaint abstracted on the preceding leaf. [corner: copybook pen]

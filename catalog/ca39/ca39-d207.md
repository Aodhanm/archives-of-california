# Verbatim heading: '1845 - Angeles / Gob.or Pio Pico al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' then '+ Marzo 12 (2 docs.) pp.294-97'. S

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n292** · Sav p. 291 / 67 · tomo p. pp. 294-97 · 1845-03-12 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1845 - Angeles / Gob.or Pio Pico al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' then '+ Marzo 12 (2 docs.) pp.294-97'. Substance: that the sueldos of the empleados of that Aduana are not included in the órden suspending the pago de cuentas; and that he thanks him for the aclaracion he makes, 'sobre que las Bases Orgánicas no conceden mas q. la vigilancia á los Gobernadores en la Hacienda Gral', and that he counts on his cooperacion y ausilio to introduce the mejoras ó reformas necessary for the Gob.o del Departam.to, which he intends to propose to the Supremo Gobierno.

# Verbatim heading: '1843 - Angeles / Micheltorrena al Adm.or de Aduana Mont.' with 'Manuel Castañares' written beneath, t

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n255** · Sav p. 254 / 30 · tomo p. p. 106 · 1843-01-10 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1843 - Angeles / Micheltorrena al Adm.or de Aduana Mont.' with 'Manuel Castañares' written beneath, then the entry '+ Enero 10 p.106'. Substance: that when he gave his instructions to the Comand.te Militar to put up to one hundred auxiliares under arms in that place, resting on his assertion that they would occasion only the expense of food, he gave the order to the Adm.or of that Aduana solely because of the notorious scarcities; and that he provide only víveres to the auxiliares of that point at the orders of the Comandante Militar, 'durante la permanencia de la Escuadrilla Americana ó hasta nueva órden'.

# The Daniel Ferguson murder causa: the Scot Daniel Ferguson found dead 4 July with 7 stab wounds between the two lagunas 

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n408–n412** · Sav p. 407–411 · tomo p. pp.229–353 · 1841-12-23 · Savage abstract · 

The Daniel Ferguson murder causa: the Scot Daniel Ferguson found dead 4 July with 7 stab wounds between the two lagunas on the Salinas road; examined by Juez de Paz Simeón Castro with Santiago Stokes as surgeon; José Antonio Arana jailed for 'homicidio y adulterio'; the widow M.a del Cármen Ruiz adulterous and 'quizás de acuerdo p.a el asesinato'; defensor Miera y Noreña ('maestro de escuela en la ex-mision de San José') excused, then Mariano Bonilla; 23 Dec sentence = expulsion from the Department or presidio; 24 Dec José R. Estrada, prefecto del 1er Distrito, reports to the Secretario. 1877 informant postscript traces Arana to S.n F.co, Sta Cruz, Mont. (1855-56). [Row rebuilt 2026-09-02 from the stretch re-read: the old row's span n401–n409 falsely covered four other causas (now d225–d228), and its San José/Castro/Anza fields belonged to the incest causa d227.]

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n247** · Sav p. 246 / 22 · tomo p. pp. 75-76 · 1839-03-30 [year from the running heading] · Savage abstract · 

OUTSIDE THE ASSIGNED GAP LIST, see issues. Verbatim heading: '+ Marzo 30 pp.75-76'. Substance: that he order the empleados of his dependencia, the Oficial primero Don Pablo de la Guerra, and the Comandante de Celadores Don Eugenio Montenegro, to present themselves tomorrow at the casa de Gob.o to take the juramento de estilo, and that the first give fianza for 1,500 pesos.

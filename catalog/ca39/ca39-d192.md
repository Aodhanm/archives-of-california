# Verbatim heading: '+ 1839 - Noviembre 20 - Mont. / Manuel Jimeno al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' Gloss: Jimeno to the Admini

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n244** · Sav p. 243 / 19 · tomo p. pp. 55-57 · 1839-11-20 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ 1839 - Noviembre 20 - Mont. / Manuel Jimeno al Adm.or de Aduana, Mont.' Gloss: Jimeno to the Administrator of Customs, Monterey. Substance: that it is necessary that a person of the Aduana Marítima de San Fran.co take charge provisionally, but he does not approve that a comerciante be named for it as had been recommended, 'por no ser conveniente á los intereses del erario'.

# Verbatim heading: '+ 1844 - Marzo 18 - Monterey / Adm.or de Aduana al Gob.or Pio Pico.' Substance: that having no fondos

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n291** · Sav p. 290 / 66 · tomo p. p. 293 · 1844-03-18 [so headed by Savage; I infer 1845, see issues] · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ 1844 - Marzo 18 - Monterey / Adm.or de Aduana al Gob.or Pio Pico.' Substance: that having no fondos in the Aduana, and not being able to obtain that the 1,000 pesos required be lent to him to be repaid, it is impossible for him to comply with the órden received. Direct reply to the Juan Paty transport order above. [corner: copybook pen]

# Verbatim heading: '+ 1841 Oct. 11 Los Ang.s / Santiago Argüello, Prefecto del 2.o Distrito - causa por ataque á un juez.

**C-A 39 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n407–n408** · Sav p. 406–407 (stamps) · tomo p. pp.187–228 · 1841-10-11 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ 1841 Oct. 11 Los Ang.s / Santiago Argüello, Prefecto del 2.o Distrito - causa por ataque á un juez.' Juez habilitado Juan B.ta Leandry investigates Juez 1.o de Paz Ign.o Palomares's complaint that Enrique Sepúlveda, drunk, disrespected and tried to attack him in his own house — the judge injuring Sepúlveda's eye with his cane's regatón in self-defence; Leandry found no grounds, eye loss self-caused, $10 fine; elevated to the Gob. 10 Nov (Narciso Botello, secretario). Recovered 2026-09-02.

# The 1824 Chumash Revolt at La Purísima: investigation of what the rebel Indians robbed, took & burned from the mission e

**C-A 18 (Dep. State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n149–n150** · Sav p.  · tomo p. LVI.529–67 · 1825-07-15 · Savage abstract · 

The 1824 Chumash Revolt at La Purísima: investigation of what the rebel Indians robbed, took & burned from the mission escort (cabo + soldiers + their families) when they rose on 24 Feb 1824; the escort lost everything; the rebels held soldiers & families captive in the 'nueva ranchería de sublevados'; the alcaldes' claim that all was returned is false. Goal: make the Mission compensate the troops

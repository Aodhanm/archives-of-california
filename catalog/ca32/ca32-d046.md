# Two Victoria borradores: (a) argues against posting revolution-partisan officers to California, blaming Ayudante Inspect

**C-A 32 (Departmental State Papers (Miscellaneous)), scan n86–n90** · Sav p. 87–91 · tomo p. XIV:112–114 · 1831-10-26/27 · Savage abstract · 

Two Victoria borradores: (a) argues against posting revolution-partisan officers to California, blaming Ayudante Inspector José María Padrés & the weakness of predecessor Echeandía, and narrates a Los Ángeles judicial-corruption case (fiscal Capt. Leonardo Barroso); (b) 'Sobre socorros á las tropas' — the English brig 'Ayacucho' from Lima (Esteban Anderson, ~$11,000 duties), clothing the troops off customs revenue amid 'nakedness and misery,' permitting prohibited-cloth imports, and declining any personal reimbursement so as not to aggravate the scarce funds

# Report of a failed campaign against unconverted Indians. Heading: '1804 - Set.e 27 - Sta Clara - Cabo Luis Peralta á un 

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n335** · Sav p. 334 [stamp] / 331 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 824-25 · 1804-09-27 · Savage abstract · 

Report of a failed campaign against unconverted Indians. Heading: '1804 - Set.e 27 - Sta Clara - Cabo Luis Peralta á un Capitan (no dice cual)'. Abstract: 'Avisa q.e la espedicion q.e hizo contra indios gentiles no tuvo resultados satisfactorios p.r falta de buenos guias' [= his expedition against gentile Indians produced no satisfactory result for want of good guides]. Addressee unnamed in the original; the reply/orders on this same campaign appear later in the volume (Argüello to Sarg.to Luis Peralta, 27 Set 1804, pp. 851-2, leaf n356) — note Savage calls him Cabo here and Sargento there.

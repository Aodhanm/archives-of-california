# ★ HEMP CULTIVATION SKEPTICISM — didn't request tools, superioridad sent them voluntarily; 'I have my suspicions hemp won

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n318–319** · Sav p. 317–18 · tomo p. p.314–15 · 1803 Aug 16 · Savage abstract · 

★ HEMP CULTIVATION SKEPTICISM — didn't request tools, superioridad sent them voluntarily; 'I have my suspicions hemp won't prosper much, because they lack conviction of its utility, the natural laziness (*desidia*) of the residents, and especially because they lack the multitude of gentiles they formerly had in their service.' Orders Comisionado to verify shipment; presses for results as Superioridad watches this branch with interest. pp. 783–4, 789–90

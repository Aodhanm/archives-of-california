# Rubric: 'De la Guerra a Arrillaga. Fabricacion de loza' [= pottery manufacture]. 'Que con greta qe. habia en Mision de S

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n263** · Sav p. 262 [stamp] / 259 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 637-8 · 1803-08-03 · Savage abstract · 

Rubric: 'De la Guerra a Arrillaga. Fabricacion de loza' [= pottery manufacture]. 'Que con greta qe. habia en Mision de Sn. Carlos mando dar principio a la construccion de loza, pero despues de tantas fatigas, ha visto qe. el Alfarero no se costearia ni dandole gratis los ingredientes, qe. la loza es mala y su costo triple qe. la de Sn. Blas, y se gravara la tropa, qe. es la qe. mas la consume, si se le obliga a tomarla' [= using clay (greta) available at Mission San Carlos he had pottery-making begun, but after much effort finds the potter could not pay his way even with free ingredients, the ware is bad and costs three times the San Blas product, and the troops, its chief consumers, would be burdened if forced to buy it]. Savage's heading abbreviates the place as 'Mont'. Given name of De la Guerra not stated in the abstract.

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n336** · Sav p. 335 [stamp] / 332 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 828-9 · 1804-10-30 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: '1804 - Oct.e 30 - S.n Fran.co - Argüello á Arrillaga.' Abstract: 'Avisa q.e falleció el Cabo Nicolas Berreyesa el 20 de aquel mes, de dolor de Hígado' [= corporal Nicolás Berreyesa died on the 20th of that month of a liver complaint]. Note: two distinct documents on this volume are dated 1804 Oct 30 from Argüello to Arrillaga (this one, pp. 828-9, and the Vicario Castrense letter at p. 832, leaf n337) — they are separate pieces, not a duplicate entry.

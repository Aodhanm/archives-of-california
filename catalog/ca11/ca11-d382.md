# Ecclesiastical jurisdiction over the province. Heading: '1804 - Oct.e 30 - S.n Fran.co - Argüello á Arrillaga. Vicario C

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n337** · Sav p. 336 [stamp] / 333 [hand] · tomo p. p. 832 · 1804-10-30 · Savage abstract · 

Ecclesiastical jurisdiction over the province. Heading: '1804 - Oct.e 30 - S.n Fran.co - Argüello á Arrillaga. Vicario Castrense de Cals.' Abstract: 'Que queda enterado de haber nombrado el Obispo de Sonora Vicario Forense y Castrense p.a la Provincia al P. Fr. Estevan Tapis' [= acknowledges that the Bishop of Sonora has named Fr. Estevan Tapis vicar forane and military vicar for the province]. Savage writes 'Vicario Forense'; the standard term is vicario foráneo.

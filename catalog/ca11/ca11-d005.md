# Heading verbatim: '+ 1800. Dic.e 12 - Monterrey / Pedro de Alberni á Arrillaga. Fallecim.to del Habilitado de Monterrey,

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n10** · Sav p. 11 | 10. · tomo p. pp. 22-3- · 1800-12-12 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '+ 1800. Dic.e 12 - Monterrey / Pedro de Alberni á Arrillaga. Fallecim.to del Habilitado de Monterrey, Hermenegildo Sal.' Body verbatim: 'Dá parte q.e á las 10 1/2 de la noche, del 8 Dic.e falleció el Habilitado Sal y Ten.te de aquel Presidio, recibiendo todos los sacram.tos y dejando en su testam.to p.r 1.er Albacea á Argüello y 2.o á Sarg.to de Artilleria José Roca.' [= reports that at 10:30 at night on 8 December the habilitado Sal, lieutenant of that presidio, died, having received all the sacraments and leaving in his will Argüello as first executor and the sergeant of artillery José Roca as second]. Gives the death of Hermenegildo Sal precisely: 8 Dec. 1800, 10:30 p.m., Monterey, reported four days later. Entry begins and ends on this leaf.

# ★ FOUNDING OF MISSION SANTA INÉS — formal act of taking possession of paraje called Lajalup (in native language) for fou

**C-A 11 (Provincial State Papers), scan n363–364** · Sav p. 362–63 · tomo p. p.359–60 · 1804 Sep 17 · Savage abstract · 

★ FOUNDING OF MISSION SANTA INÉS — formal act of taking possession of paraje called Lajalup (in native language) for founding Mission Santa Inés; cross raised and adored; chapel/ramada formed; first Mass celebrated and sung by Father President Fr. Estevan Tapis and Fr. Marcelino Ciprés; founders Fr. José Antonio Calzada and Fr. Romualdo Gutiérrez; solemn Te Deum sung. Copy certified by Arrillaga, Loreto, Nov 22, 1804. p. 876

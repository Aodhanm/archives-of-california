# Heading verbatim: '1800. Dbre. 16. Megico. / Virrey Marquina a D. Jose Joaquin de Arrillaga.' Gloss: payment for rations

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n353** · Sav p. 49. (Bancroft stamp 354) · tomo p. pp. 82. · 1800-12-16 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '1800. Dbre. 16. Megico. / Virrey Marquina a D. Jose Joaquin de Arrillaga.' Gloss: payment for rations issued to four English prisoners at Loreto. Abstract: the habilitacion of Loreto having spent 343 p. 2 rs. 6 g. on the racion de Armada supplied to 4 prisioneros Ingleses, he has resolved that the stated sum be delivered by these cajas matrices to Tte. Coronel D. Manuel Carcaba, habilitado gral. of those presidios.

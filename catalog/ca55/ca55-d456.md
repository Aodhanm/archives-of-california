# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '1794 Julio 19. Mision

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n384** · Sav p. 80. (Bancroft stamp 385) · tomo p. pp 306. 8. [expansion: pp. 306-308] · 1794-07-19 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '1794 Julio 19. Mision de S. Vicente. / Fr. Cayetano Pallas al Gob.or de Calif.a' Substance verbatim: 'Que me dice el R.do P Fr. Raf.l Caballero Ministro en la Mision de S. Pedro Martir q.e debido a las eladas y a las muchas tormentas, sin repugnancia del Alferez D. Pablo Grijalba tenia resuelto mudarse en la semana q.e hoy finaliza.' Gloss: the minister of San Pedro Martir has resolved, on account of frosts and storms and with Alferez Pablo Grijalba raising no objection, to move within the week.

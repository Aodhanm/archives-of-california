# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1800. Dbre 18 Megico.

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n353** · Sav p. 49. (Bancroft stamp 354) · tomo p. pp 83. · 1800-12-18 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1800. Dbre 18 Megico. / Marquina al Gob.or Int.o de Californias.' Gloss: standing reporting order on enemy shipping. Abstract: informed by the report of the Comandante de las armas of the nuevos establecimientos that a frigate of 10 guns was sighted; the warnings given on that account seem good to him; and that to however many national vessels reach the ports of that peninsula, whether or not warships, everything occurring with enemy ships cruising in those seas must be communicated.

# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: 'Sbre. 27.' (date-only

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n411** · Sav p. 107 (Bancroft stamp 412) · tomo p. pp 618 - 20. [expansion: pp. 618-620] · 1800, Sbre. 27 as written (month reading flagged in issues) · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: 'Sbre. 27.' (date-only continuation under the same n410 heading). Substance verbatim: 'Que hallandose determinado por esta superioridad q.e a los artesanos contratados p.a ensenar sus oficios se les acuda con la 8.a parte de utilidades de lo q.e trabajen deducidos gastos, he resuelto q.e se observe la espresada determinacion con los artesanos Mariano Tapia y Manuel Munoz.' Gloss: the artisans contracted to teach their trades are to receive an eighth part of the profits of what they make, expenses deducted; that ruling is extended to Mariano Tapia and Manuel Munoz.

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '1802. En.o 19. Domingo de Predicadores, q.e a pesar de haber cumplido los Religiosos sus decenio, continuen desempenando su Ministerio mientras llegan otros p.a relevarlos.' Gloss: Viceroy Marquina orders the Dominican ministers of the Baja California missions to stay at their posts past their ten-year terms until replacements arrive.

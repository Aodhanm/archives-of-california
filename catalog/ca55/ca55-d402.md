# Heading verbatim: 'Julio 12.' (short-form heading; year, writer and place carry over from the '1802. Julio 13 Megico. / 

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n356** · Sav p. 52. (Bancroft stamp 357) · tomo p. pp. 90. · 1802-07-12 (year and writer inferred from the preceding heading) · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: 'Julio 12.' (short-form heading; year, writer and place carry over from the '1802. Julio 13 Megico. / Virrey Marquina al Gob.or Int.o de Californias' heading on the preceding leaf, so 1802 and Marquina are inferred). Abstract: that the petition of the artillero Juan Mariner is not granted, and the same must be understood of others of the Piquete de Artilleria who claim the same, unless they hold their licences.

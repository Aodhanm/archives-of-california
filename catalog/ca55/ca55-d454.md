**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n383** · Sav p. 79. (Bancroft stamp 384) · tomo p. pp 303. [expansion: p. 303] · 1795-01-14 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: '1795. En.o 14. Mision de S. Fernando. / Fr. Jorge Coello, al Gob.or Diego Borica.' Substance verbatim: 'Que se observara esactam.te el tratar con los oficiales y tripulacion de las embarcaciones Ynglesas de D. Jorge Vancover.' Gloss: the minister undertakes to observe exactly the rules on dealing with the officers and crews of the English vessels of George Vancouver. San Fernando here is Velicata in Baja California; San Fernando Rey in Alta California was not founded until 1797.

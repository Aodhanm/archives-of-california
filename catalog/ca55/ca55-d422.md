# Spans n387-n388. THE SAN CARLOS MASS ORDER. Croix, acknowledging Fages' letter no. 36 of 26 May, VERBATIM: 'sobre q.e lo

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n387** · Sav p. 83.-84. [Bancroft stamps 388-389] · tomo p. pp. 369. - 70. [expansion: 369-370; third digit carries a 9's descender, NOT 361] · 1783-06-27 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n387-n388. THE SAN CARLOS MASS ORDER. Croix, acknowledging Fages' letter no. 36 of 26 May, VERBATIM: 'sobre q.e los Ministros de la Mision de San Carlos han dejado de ir a celebrar el Santo Sacrificio de la misa al Presidio de Mont. desde el dia 10 de Marzo pp.do'. He orders Fages to address 'oficios de ruego y encargo' to the R.o P.e Presid.te Fr. Junipero Serra, reminding him of the reply to Croix's order of 28 May (answering Fages' letter of 28 Feb., no. 9, reporting the same lapse at the San Francisco presidio), and to report the outcome. Heading marked in the left margin.

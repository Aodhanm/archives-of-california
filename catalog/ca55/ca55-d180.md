# Foreign-ship exclusion order: forwards the Viceroy's note (17 Aug) on the entries the American frigate *Alexander* made 

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n112** · Sav p. 113 · tomo p. p.i.597–598 · 1803-11-08 · Savage abstract · 

Foreign-ship exclusion order: forwards the Viceroy's note (17 Aug) on the entries the American frigate *Alexander* made into Peninsula ports; reiterates the Reales Órdenes prohibiting foreign vessels; orders the commanders to 'de ningún modo franqueen auxilios á buques extranjeros, á menos que no se vean obligados á discreción'

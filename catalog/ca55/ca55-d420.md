# Spans n386-n387. Arrival at Monterey of the Malaspina-era exploring schooners. VERBATIM: 'Que el 22 del pp.do Sbre. dier

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n386–n387** · Sav p. 82.-83. [Bancroft stamps 387-388] · tomo p. pp. 351. 52. [expansion: 351-352] · 1792-10-01 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n386-n387. Arrival at Monterey of the Malaspina-era exploring schooners. VERBATIM: 'Que el 22 del pp.do Sbre. dieron fondo en este puerto las Goletas de S.M. la Sutil y Megicana, proced.tes del descubrim.to del estrecho de Juan de Fuca.' Gloss: on 22 September last His Majesty's schooners Sutil and Mexicana anchored in this port, coming from the exploration of the Strait of Juan de Fuca. Heading marked '+'.

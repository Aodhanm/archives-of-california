# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1802. Julio 12. Megic

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n356** · Sav p. 52. (Bancroft stamp 357) · tomo p. pp. 94 · 1802-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1802. Julio 12. Megico. / Marquina al Gob.or Int.o de Californias.' Abstract in full: 'Enterado de los estragos causados por los uracanes en los dias 2 y 17. de Febrero en el presidio de San Fran.co' (informed of the ravages caused by the hurricanes on 2 and 17 February at the presidio of San Francisco). Note this is a second Marquina letter of 12 July 1802, distinct from the pp. 90 one earlier on the same leaf.

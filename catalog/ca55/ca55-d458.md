# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: 'Julio 29.' (date-only

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n384** · Sav p. 80. (Bancroft stamp 385) · tomo p. pp 322. 25. [expansion: pp. 322-325] · 1794-07-29 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Verbatim heading: 'Julio 29.' (date-only continuation of the Pallas entries above). Substance verbatim: 'Que se sirva dar su permiso p.a q.e se egecute el traslado de la Mision del parage llamado Casilepe al segundo llamado Ajanteguedo.' Gloss: asks the governor to grant permission for the transfer of the mission from the site called Casilepe to the second called Ajanteguedo. Both place names are underlined by Savage.

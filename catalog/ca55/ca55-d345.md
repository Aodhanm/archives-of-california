# 'Sobre establecimiento de la Bodega' — Matute reports it has not been possible to establish at the port; Sal to receive the war-supplies, 10 soldiers & 5 artisans from the *Aránzazu*

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n297–n298** · Sav p. 298–299 · tomo p. p.viii.~667–670 · 1793-07-25 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre establecimiento de la Bodega' — forwards Tte. de Fragata Juan Matute (Comte. del Pto. de la Bodega)'s original oficio reporting that it has NOT been possible to make the establishment at that port («no le ha sido posible hacer establecimiento en aquel Pto.»); Matute asks Sal to receive with account all the goods & war-supplies (pertrechos de guerra), plus 10 military men («diez individuos militares») & 5 artisans brought on the frigate *Aránzazu*. Sal awaits Arrillaga's orders; a house stands ready for the artisans (Salvador Méndez to spare them shipboard rations), the presidio cuartel suffices for the soldiers, and if both arrive early he will employ the troops in the guardia de prevención and the mason, carpenter & smiths at their trades. The failed 1793 Spanish Bodega-occupation attempt

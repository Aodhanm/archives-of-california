# Heading verbatim: '1797. Mayo 24. Megico. / M. Corcaba al Gob.or Diego de Borica.' Gloss: San Blas charges for presidial

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n352** · Sav p. 48. (Bancroft stamp 353) · tomo p. pp 79. · 1797-05-24 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '1797. Mayo 24. Megico. / M. Corcaba al Gob.or Diego de Borica.' Gloss: San Blas charges for presidial supplies, and the resulting balances of the four habilitados. Abstract: with the charges the Ministerio de San Blas sent for the viveres y efectos supplied from its warehouses, the refilling of the aguardiente barrels at Tepic, freight etc., and with some credits to the Monterey habilitado, Monterey is left owing 7625 p. 6 rs. 6 3/4 g. and San Francisco 2764 p. 2 rs. 7 1/4 g., while Santa Barbara is in surplus by 2801 p. 1 r. 6 1/2 g. and San Diego by 2917 p. 2 rs. 7 1/2 g.

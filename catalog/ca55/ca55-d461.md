# Report on a mission-sponsored search for runaway Christian Indians across the Bay. VERBATIM: 'Que hoy ha llegado el Indi

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n386** · Sav p. 82. [Bancroft stamp 387] · tomo p. pp. 340. 41. [expansion: 340-341] · 1795-06-20 · Savage abstract · 

Report on a mission-sponsored search for runaway Christian Indians across the Bay. VERBATIM: 'Que hoy ha llegado el Indio Julian q.e venia de la otra vanda, donde ocho dias antes lo mandaron los P.P. con dos muchachos a q.e buscaron Cristianos simarrones.' Gloss: the Indian Julian returned today from the far shore (the East Bay), where the padres had sent him eight days earlier with two boys to look for Christian fugitives. Heading marked 'x' in the left margin.

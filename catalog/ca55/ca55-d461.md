# Verbatim heading: '1798. Mzo. 27. San Blas. / Jose Garcia al Gob.or de Calif.a' Substance: a nota settling with the pres

**C-A 55 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n378** · Sav p. 74. (Bancroft stamp 379) · tomo p. pp 244. 45. [expansion: pp. 244-245] · 1798-03-27 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1798. Mzo. 27. San Blas. / Jose Garcia al Gob.or de Calif.a' Substance: a nota settling with the presidial habilitaciones the sums for auxilios furnished to the ships, plus reintegro for mermas and return of embases. Figures verbatim: 'Al Presidio de S. Fran.co 2269. 5. 11 / Al de Mont 50. 6. 7 / Al de Sta. Barbara 25. 5. 17' with the ruled total '2246. 1. 1'. The total as written does not equal the sum of the three lines; a deduction ('deducirase q.e se debuelven') is announced but not itemised on the leaf.

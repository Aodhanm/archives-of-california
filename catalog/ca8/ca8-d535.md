# VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Abril 10. S.ta Barbara / Goycoechea á Borica. Se reparta armam.to a vecinos». SUBSTANCE: the es

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n229** · Sav p. 235 / 38 · tomo p. p.104 · 1797-04-10 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Abril 10. S.ta Barbara / Goycoechea á Borica. Se reparta armam.to a vecinos». SUBSTANCE: the escopetas ripoleñas have been distributed to the vecinos since last year; he now dispatches the alférez to put in practice the distribution of flints, charged to the fondo de gratificación, to every vecino who has a weapon, and to deliver to the comisionado the two thousand cartridges held in reserve.

# VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Junio 23. Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Borica. Paga de 2256 p.s por obra material en el pres.o de 

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n253** · Sav p. 256 / 59 · tomo p. p. p. 179 - 180. · 1797-06-23 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Junio 23. Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Borica. Paga de 2256 p.s por obra material en el pres.o de Sta Barb.a' GLOSS: payment of 2,256 pesos for the material works of the Santa Barbara presidio. SUBSTANCE: by Borica oficio of 26 April Goycoechea is instructed of the resolution taken by Viceroy Branciforte, having conformed, that the ministros de Real Hacienda satisfy Dn Manuel de Carcaba 'la cantidad de 2256 p.s 1 tomin y 5/8 g.s que importaron los gastos erogados en la obra material de este pres.o de Sta Barb.a'; Borica likewise directs Goycoechea to credit the same sum to the fondo de gratificacion charged with it, which he will do in the first account he forms to that fund.

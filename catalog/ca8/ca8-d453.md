# VERBATIM HEADING: '1793. Dic.bre 31. Pres.o de Sta Barbara. Recuento de bulas.' GLOSS: Formal count of the Bulls of the 

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n232-n233** · Sav p. 238-239 / 41-42 · tomo p. p. p. 117 - 118. · 1793-12-31 · verbatim transcription · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1793. Dic.bre 31. Pres.o de Sta Barbara. Recuento de bulas.' GLOSS: Formal count of the Bulls of the Holy Crusade remaining at the Santa Barbara presidio, 31 Dec 1793. SUBSTANCE: begins on gap leaf n232 and closes on n233. Lt. Felipe de Goycoechea, before the notary and R.P. Fr. Jose de Miguel as chaplain of the troop, brings the subscribing witnesses to attend the recuento of the Bulas de la Sta Cruzada for the biennium 92 y 93. Itemised verbatim: '3 bulas de vivos de a dos pesos / 49 ... 8 reales / 287 ... 2 reales / 5 ... Laticinios ... 2 reales / 5 ... Composicion a 12 reales / 74 ... de difuntos a 2'. These sum to exactly 423. Signed Phelipe de Goycoechea, Fr. Jose de Miguel, Pablo de Cota, Jose Tadeo Sanchez, with the certification 'Es copia de su original de que certifico. Felipe de Goycoechea.' This is the itemised basis of the 423 figure in the 3 Apr 1797 letter and of the 423 sumarios burned at Monterey (Documento de la quema de Bulas, 26 Jun 1797, which begins at the foot of n233).

# VERBATIM: «Extracto de la alianza entre la España y la Republica francesa firmada en S.n Ildefonso á 2 de Fructidor (23 

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n88–n90** · Sav p. 92–94 · tomo p. p.276–278 · 1796-08-18? · verbatim transcription · 

VERBATIM: «Extracto de la alianza entre la España y la Republica francesa firmada en S.n Ildefonso á 2 de Fructidor (23 de Agosto) y ratificada en 26 del mismo por el consejo de los Ancianos en Paris» — sin fecha ni lugar, sin firma. Numbered articles; art. 4 the requisitioned party to furnish 15 ships of the line, 3 of 3 decks or 80 guns and 12 of 70–74, plus 6 frigates, provisioned 6 months and rigged for a year; art. 5, 18,000 infantry and 6,000 horse for Europe or for the defence of the contracting parties' colonies in the Gulf of America; art. 18, England being the only power against whom Spain has direct grievances, the alliance is to operate against her alone during the present war, Spain remaining neutral toward the other powers armed against France

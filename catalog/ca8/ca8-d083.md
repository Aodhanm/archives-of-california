# 5 NEW MISSIONS PLAN: SD→SJC, SG→SBV, SL→SA, SC→Sta Clara, Sta Clara→SF. Sites: Paló (14 lg from SD), Las Pozas, San Fran

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n46–50** · Sav p. 51–54 · tomo p. 168–179 · 1796-02-26 · Savage abstract · 

5 NEW MISSIONS PLAN: SD→SJC, SG→SBV, SL→SA, SC→Sta Clara, Sta Clara→SF. Sites: Paló (14 lg from SD), Las Pozas, San Francisco Solano (7–8 lg from Sta Clara). Save 15,000 p escoltas. Catalan Volunteers + 18 artillerymen cover ports; rotate escoltas for new missions. Indians of sierra 'de carácter broncos y feros.' 1,000 p/mission to Colegio de S. Fernando

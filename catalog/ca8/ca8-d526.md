# VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. En.o 12 S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Diarios de comand.tes». SUBSTANCE: passes five diari

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n224** · Sav p. 230 / 33 · tomo p. p.89 · 1797-01-12 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. En.o 12 S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Diarios de comand.tes». SUBSTANCE: passes five diaries, four for the occurrences of December drawn up by the commanders of the escoltas subject to this presidio and one by Sargento José de Ortega on his tour of those escoltas in the same month; the other, for November, is the one missing from the commander of San Buenaventura, who omitted its remission in December through illness.

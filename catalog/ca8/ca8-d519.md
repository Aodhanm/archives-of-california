**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n257** · Sav p. 260 / 63 · tomo p. pp.198–199 · 1797-06-19 · Savage abstract · 

Leaf-verified 2026-09-22 (ca8 repair plan Phase 3), previously UNCATALOGUED: the leaf was claimed by a constructed row, so coverage never saw this document. VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Junio 19. S.ta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Borica. Avalúo de mermas en viveres y efectos traidos por la 'Princesa'». SUBSTANCE: of the 350 pesos 6 reales of victuals and effects remitted to this habilitación, the shortfalls and wastage amounted to 19 pesos 1 real, as the enclosed copy of the receipt shows in detail; alférez Pablo Cotta intervened and the pilot Menéndez, acting as purser of the Princesa, signed with him.

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n199** · Sav p. 205 / 8 · tomo p. pp.24–25 · 1797-05-23 · Savage abstract · 

Rewritten 2026-09-22 from a direct reading of the leaf (previously read only in summary). The entry formerly described a document the leaf does not hold; its erratum records the original text. ⭐⭐ VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Mayo 23. S.n Fr.co / Arguello á Borica. Instrucciones para comisionado - arreglo de la escolta para la nueva fundacion de la mis.n S.n Jose.» SUBSTANCE: acknowledges the instructions for the comisionado of the new foundation of Mission San José; he will inform him and the soldier-secretary of the commission of their contents, and take copies of the bandos and orders posted in the guard-houses of the older missions, and of the arancel de precios, so that the comisionado may observe them to the letter; he is preparing the escolta against the day the Padre Presidente calls for it.

# Leaf-verified 2026-09-22 (ca8 repair plan Phase 3), previously UNCATALOGUED: the leaf was claimed by a constructed row o

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n109** · Sav p. 113 / 112 · tomo p. pp.395–396 · 1796-07-02 · Savage abstract · 

Leaf-verified 2026-09-22 (ca8 repair plan Phase 3), previously UNCATALOGUED: the leaf was claimed by a constructed row of the d149–d164 series, so coverage never saw this document. ⭐⭐ VERBATIM HEADING: «Sobre buques estrangeros». SUBSTANCE: transmits the Viceroy's order of 29 December last on English ships, which are to «subsistir solo el tiempo preciso que exije la hospitalidad con prohibicion a todo particular, la venta, cambio o permuta de efectos estrangeros» (sic, as Savage writes it).

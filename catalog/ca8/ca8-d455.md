# VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Junio 19. Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Borica. Modo de acomodar un soldado enfermo.' GLOSS: how to

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n253** · Sav p. 256 / 59 · tomo p. p. p. 181 - 182. · 1797-06-19 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1797. Junio 19. Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Borica. Modo de acomodar un soldado enfermo.' GLOSS: how to place a sick soldier. SUBSTANCE: Goycoechea says he sent the said Alvarez to the pueblo either to settle there like the other labradores, or to hire himself out in service to some private person until he recovers from his ailments, all consequent to the superior orders of Borica.

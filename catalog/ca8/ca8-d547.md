# VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Mayo 24. S.n Fr.co / José Arguello á Borica. Abilitado en Monterrey José Perez?». SUBSTANCE: tw

**C-A 8 (Provincial State Papers), scan n196–n197** · Sav p. 202 / 5 · tomo p. pp.16–17 · 1797-05-24 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: «1797. Mayo 24. S.n Fr.co / José Arguello á Borica. Abilitado en Monterrey José Perez?». SUBSTANCE: two hundred pesos were supplied by the habilitación at Monterey to alférez José Pérez on account of his pay; since he has none due but this year's, Argüello has warned that habilitado not to pay libranzas that are not against credit, and will watch its fulfilment.

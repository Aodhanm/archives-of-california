# '1799 = Nbre 8 = Loreto / Arrillaga á Borica' (no subject line). Received a copy of the Royal Order on the sentence give

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n311** · Sav p. 292 / 290 · tomo p. p 758 · 1799-11-08 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 8 = Loreto / Arrillaga á Borica' (no subject line). Received a copy of the Royal Order on the sentence given against the Governor and defendants which comprehended 'la entrega de la plaza de Figueroa' [the surrender of the fortress of Figueroa]. A Peninsular court-martial result circulated to the Californias.

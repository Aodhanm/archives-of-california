# '1798 = Abril 12 = S. Franco / Arguello á Pedro Amador / Prevenciones sobre gentiles' [Instructions concerning the genti

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n140** · Sav p. 124 / 123 · tomo p. pp 218-220 · 1798-04-12 · Savage abstract · 

'1798 = Abril 12 = S. Franco / Arguello á Pedro Amador / Prevenciones sobre gentiles' [Instructions concerning the gentiles]. Orders Amador to report on the Ministro's expedition to the gentilidad; all orders sent him are to be obeyed; and the gentiles are NOT to be forced to bring them out of their rancherias. At the foot Amador answers from S. José, April 24: the Ministro went al Colorado; on seeing him they fled because they said they wanted to baptize them; the cabo has been warned of his duty and of the punishment if he fails in it.

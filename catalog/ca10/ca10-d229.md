# 'Sobre pretension del Cirujano Soler' — his predecessor recommended to S.M. surgeon Pablo Soler's promotion 'á la clase 

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n187** · Sav p. 188/186 · tomo p. p.418 · 1798-12-22 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre pretension del Cirujano Soler' — his predecessor recommended to S.M. surgeon Pablo Soler's promotion 'á la clase de primeros de su Rl Armada'; the resolution has not arrived and Soler now asks retirement 'con el goce de honores y uso de uniforme de Ayudante Consultor de aqe Rl Cuerpo'; Azanza rules he should await the answer to his first petition.

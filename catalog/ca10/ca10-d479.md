# 'Sin fha = S. Diego / Domingo Cruz á Borica' (no subject line) [Undated, San Diego]. Asks that his son José, serving in 

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n308-n309** · Sav p. 289-290 / 287-288 · tomo p. p 747 · sin fecha · Savage abstract · 

'Sin fha = S. Diego / Domingo Cruz á Borica' (no subject line) [Undated, San Diego]. Asks that his son José, serving in the Monterey company, pass to that of San Diego where he has another son named Domingo. In the margin Borica says he is to be informed whether José wishes to move to that presidio; Sal replies that they wish to pass to Santa Bárbara so as to be at their father's side.

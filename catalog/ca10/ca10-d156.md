# '1797 = Fbro 15 = México / Alva al Virey / Sobre permiso á los Mtros Caballero' [On leave for the Caballero ministers]. 

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n143** · Sav p. 127 / 126 · tomo p. p 228 · 1797-02-15 · Savage abstract · 

'1797 = Fbro 15 = México / Alva al Virey / Sobre permiso á los Mtros Caballero' [On leave for the Caballero ministers]. That before granting the licence the Governor of California be consulted on the nature of the fathers' illnesses, and in view of these the permission sought be given. Savage adds: 'Es copia por Bonilla en Fbro 18.'

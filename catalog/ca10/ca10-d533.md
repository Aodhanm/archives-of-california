# 'qe recibió libranza de $24'25 dada por Rowan pa refrescos pa su buqe al cf de James & Tom Perkins de Boston; qe ha disp

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n339** · Sav p. 339/337 · tomo p. p.903-4 · 1799-09-19 · Savage abstract · 

'qe recibió libranza de $24'25 dada por Rowan pa refrescos pa su buqe al cf de James & Tom Perkins de Boston; qe ha dispuesto lo necesº pa su cobro.' [= the viceroy has the bill of exchange for 24 pesos 2 reales 5 gr. drawn by Rowan (= James Rowan, supercargo of the Eliza) to pay for provisions supplied to his ship, charged to the account of James & Thomas Perkins of Boston, and has arranged its collection] — direct documentary link between the 1799 American visitor and the Perkins house of Boston.

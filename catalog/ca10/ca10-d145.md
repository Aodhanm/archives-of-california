**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n138** · Sav p. 122 / 121 · tomo p. pp 211-212 · 1782-09-09 · Savage abstract · 

'1782 = Stre 9 = S. Franco / Gabriel Antº Moraga al Comᵗᵉ Inspᵣ / Solicitud de cadete' [1782, Sept. 9, San Francisco. Gabriel Antonio Moraga to the Commandant Inspector. Application for cadetship]. Asks for the cordones de cadete in the San Francisco presidial company. Marginal: Soler to the Inspector, from S. Gabriel, Ntre 1[?] de 82, that if he has the age his request be granted; Neve, May 31 of 83, from el Rio, says he takes a soldier's place.

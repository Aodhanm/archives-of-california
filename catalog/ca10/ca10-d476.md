# '1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Recibo de cédulas de inválido' [Receipt of invalids' certificates]. Received

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n307** · Sav p. 288 / 286 · tomo p. pp 742-43 · 1799-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Recibo de cédulas de inválido' [Receipt of invalids' certificates]. Received 4 cédulas de inválido for sergeant J. Macario Castro and soldiers Migᶫ Espinosa, J. Luz García and Luis Romero. The said sergeant has been destined as Comisionado of S. José; once Vallejos makes the handover he will destine him as Comisionado to the Villa de Branciforte.

# '1798 = Enero 12 = Loreto / Arrillaga / Cuenta de productos de Rᶫ Hacda.' [Account of the yield of the Royal Treasury]. 

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n138-n139** · Sav p. 122-123 / 121-122 · tomo p. pp 213-214 · 1798-01-12 · Savage abstract · 

'1798 = Enero 12 = Loreto / Arrillaga / Cuenta de productos de Rᶫ Hacda.' [Account of the yield of the Royal Treasury]. Yields of several branches 1792-96: diezmos $1147.2.2; ganado alzado 3201; sal 989.6; correos 665..5; fletes de mar 47.6; reconocimᵗᵒ 107; tabacos 17412.6; quintos 2.3.11. Notes state the Habilitado takes 10% of diezmos, 8% of tobacco, 10% of posts, nothing on the other branches, and that bulls (bulas) may yield more than $50.

# '1799 = Dbre 2 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Macario Castro received the comisión of S. José from Ignº V

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n309** · Sav p. 290 / 288 · tomo p. pp 750-51 · 1799-12-02 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Dbre 2 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Macario Castro received the comisión of S. José from Ignº Vallejos; Castro will receive his new destination within 3 days, Gabriel Moraga passing, after the handover, to do duty at the Presidio until further order.

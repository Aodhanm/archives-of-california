# '1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Sobre reconocimᵗᵒ como Admor Pral.' [On his recognition as Principal Adminis

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n306** · Sav p. 287 / 285 · tomo p. pp 737-38 · 1799-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Sobre reconocimᵗᵒ como Admor Pral.' [On his recognition as Principal Administrator]. Is informed of the oficio addressed to Alberni that Sal be recognized as Administrador Principal de ramos de Rᶫ Hacienda, and that if in collecting and safekeeping, in contraband cases, in seizures and in arresting offenders he should require assistance, it be granted him by the commandants and their subordinates.

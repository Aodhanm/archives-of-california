# 'Direccion de pliegos': sent by extraordinario the pliego enclosed for the Come Gral; his answer just received and dispa

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n21** · Sav p. 22/21 · tomo p. p.41-43 · 1799-07-20 · Savage abstract · 

'Direccion de pliegos': sent by extraordinario the pliego enclosed for the Come Gral; his answer just received and dispatched 'al puerto de Sa Lugarda, donde subsistirá el Barco qe lo condujo para trasladárselo'; the Come Gral orders 'los ofs Rs de la Rl Caja del Rosario, satisfagan los costos del extraordinario'; a packet of pliegos y cartas sent to the Admor de Chihuahua 'pa que pronto los mande al Virey.'

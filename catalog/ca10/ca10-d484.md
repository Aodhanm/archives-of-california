# '1799 = Dbre 31 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Sobre Comisᵈᵒ de S. José y Branciforte' [On the commissioners of San José an

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n310-n311** · Sav p. 291-292 / 289-290 · tomo p. pp 755-57 · 1799-12-31 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Dbre 31 = Monterey / Sal á Borica / Sobre Comisᵈᵒ de S. José y Branciforte' [On the commissioners of San José and Branciforte]. On the oficio ordering him to watch the commissioners of S. José and Branciforte, he says the one at S. José he believes will manage with the required skill, and of the one at Branciforte, who is Ignº Vallejos, he does not doubt his good performance. As to the toma de agua at Branciforte, perhaps it cannot be done because the people are few and some of them useless.

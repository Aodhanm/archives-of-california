# '1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Received a Royal Order that natives of the Island of Minor

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n307** · Sav p. 288 / 286 · tomo p. p 741 · 1799-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Received a Royal Order that natives of the Island of Minorca found in His Majesty's dominions be sought out so as to give them the destination the order specifies. A non-California Real Orden entered into the California record.

# '1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Encloses a report on the memorial of the soldier Domingo C

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n308** · Sav p. 289 / 287 · tomo p. pp 744-45 · 1799-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Encloses a report on the memorial of the soldier Domingo Cruz, who asks a transfer for his son to Santa Bárbara, and a letter of the soldier Luis Romero asking leave to go to Loreto to see his parents.

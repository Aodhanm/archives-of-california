# '1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Is informed of the Royal Disposition that the ports of 'Gu

**C-A 10 (Provincial State Papers), scan n306-n307** · Sav p. 287-288 / 285-286 · tomo p. pp 739-40 · 1799-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

'1799 = Nbre 30 = Monterey / Sal á Borica' (no subject line). Is informed of the Royal Disposition that the ports of 'Guancacho y Pacamayo' enjoy the same franchises as the lesser ports of North America, at the request of the Viceroy of Peru. A non-California imperial commercial decree circulated into the Californias.

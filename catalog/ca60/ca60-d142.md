# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n258** · Sav p. 258 / 256 · tomo p. p 527. · 1834-07-22 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 22 = Mont. / Alvarado á Figueroa.' [= 1834, July 22, Monterey. Alvarado to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Acompaña cuenta de gastos erogados en muebles y adorno de Salon de Sesiones.' Alvarado transmits the account of what was spent on furniture and decoration for the session hall.

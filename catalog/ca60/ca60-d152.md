# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n263** · Sav p. 263 / 261 · tomo p. pp 574 - 76. · 1834-11-05 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Nbre 5. = Mont. / Hijar á Figueroa' [= 1834, November 5, Monterey. Híjar to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que queda impuesto de lo acordado pr la Diptn pª la entenanza de primeras letras, manifestandole que es de opinion se le concedan á la Colonia los preceptores qe se le habian señalado.' Híjar accepts the Diputacion's resolution but presses that the schoolmasters originally assigned to the colony be left to it. The point of friction between the colony's establishment and the territorial government.

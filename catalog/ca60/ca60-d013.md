# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n63–n69** · Sav p. stamps 64–70 / ink 62–68 · tomo p. pp 53 = 57 · 1834-05-10 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n53-n107. Heading verbatim: 'o 1834. = Mayo 10 = Mont. / Diputacion. Sesion del dia de la ftha' [= 1834, May 10, Monterey. Diputación. Session of this day's date.] ⭐ The land-grant session. Oficios: Gefatura Política of 9 del corriente with a Monterey ayuntamiento dictamen fixing the portero's duties; an oficio of the Alcalde de la Villa de Branciforte reporting arbitrios agreed there to pay the preceptor de primeras letras and the Srio, namely 'por un palo tumbado para el comercio pagará el tumbador un peso por vara de grueso' and 'por cada piel de nutria un peso'; a solicitud of the Ministro de la Misión de Sn Franco for the arreglo de linderos of that community's lands, to terrenos baldíos. Obras públicas reported AGAINST the Calzada: the work would give the town lustre and commerce a boost but total want of funds makes its paralización indispensable; the presupuesto is out of proportion to municipal income, the ayuntamiento having itself confessed in its acta of 11 March that Monterey's funds do not cover even the Secretaría and the escuela; and the 'fábrica de Casas Consistoriales y Cárceles públicas' for the security of delinquents should be preferred. Gobierno y policía on the Secretary's resignation: grant it; his claims of $1179-5 rs are legal and should be paid promptly; he ceases as Contador de propios, a commission given him 16 July 1830. THE 24-25 GRANTS were listed by name and place: 'Dn Juan Malarin el parage nombrado Guadalupe = C. Graciano Manjarrés. Saucito. C. José Mª Armenta = Punta de pinos á los hijos del finado Ygnacio Ortega. San Ysidoro. C. José Mª Arce Los Pilarcitos. A los C.C.s Máximo Martinez y Domingo Peralta. Cañada del corte de madera. A los C.C.s Bartolo Pacheco y Mariano Castro. = Sn Ramon Cº Rafael Castro. Aptos, Madama Mª Castro de Shoquel = C. José Antº Bolcoff Sn Agustin = C. Santiago E Arguello = Mili... = Cº Franco Perez Pacheco = Ausaymas = C. Joaquin Castro = San Andres = C. Carlos Antº Carrillo = Sespe = Cristina Delgado = Rincon de las Salinas = José Antonio Romero Plan del Fucho = C. José Gonzales San Antº (a) el pescadero = C. Juan Bandini = Tecate = Juan B. R. Cooper Rio del Sacramento = C. Felipe Hernandez Laguna de las Calabazas = Catarina Manzancli = Laguna Seca = Franca Mesa = Corral de Tierra = Cº Carlos Castro = Sn Franca de las Llagas.' The commissions attached to each a uniform formula: 'Se aprueba la concesion hecha al C. N. del parage nombrado tal. con dicho en tal fecha de entera conformidad con lo prevenido en la ley de 18 de Agosto de 824 y el artº 5º del Reglamento de 21 de Nbre de 1828.'

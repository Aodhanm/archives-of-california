# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n223** · Sav p. corner stamp 224 / ink 222 · tomo p. p p 298 - 302. · 1835-10-14 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Octubre 14. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835, October 14, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] 'Se dio Cuenta con varios dictamenes Sobre Terrenos.' Account taken of various dictámenes on lands. Savage omits the 'Aprovada acta anterior' formula here, the only Octubre acta in the run that lacks it. Five original pages abstracted to one line.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n233** · Sav p. corner stamp 234 / ink 232 · tomo p. p. 425. · 1834-04-30 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Abril 30. = Mont. / J. B. Alvarado á Gefe Politico' [= 1834, April 30, Monterey. Juan Bautista Alvarado to the Jefe Político.] 'Hace renucia de Srio. de la Diputacion, por estar enfermo.' Alvarado resigns the secretaryship of the Deputation on grounds of illness. Dated and documented, and the resignation is answered on 10 May 1834 (p.430-34 below) as having been accepted. Alvarado nevertheless reappears as secretary in the 1 September 1835 acta at n218-n219, where he takes Maldonado's place. Savage's 'renucia' for 'renuncia' kept as written.

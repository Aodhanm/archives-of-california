# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n221** · Sav p. corner stamp 222 / ink 220 · tomo p. p. p. 278 - 81. · 1835-09-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Sbre 15. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835, September 15, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] 'Dice: Aprovada la acta anterior. Se dio cuenta con varios dictámenes sobre Conseciones de terrenos.' Prior acta approved; account taken of several committee reports (dictámenes) on concessions of land. Four original pages reduced to one sentence. Note Savage's spelling 'Conseciones', kept as written.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n249** · Sav p. 250 / 248 · tomo p. p 497 · 1834-07-03 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 3. = Mont. / (Sin firma) á Alcalde de S. José.' [= 1834, July 3, Monterey. (Unsigned) to the Alcalde of San José.] Abstract: 'Le comunica haberse dado cuenta con su oficio y no haber habido lugar á su peticion.' Formal notice to the San José alcalde that his office was reported to the assembly and his petition denied. Pairs with the Carrillo entry above.

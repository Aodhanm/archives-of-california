# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n249** · Sav p. 250 / 248 · tomo p. pp 495 - 96. · 1834-06-28 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Junio 28 = Mont. / Carlos Antonio Carrillo (no dice á quien' [= 1834, June 28, Monterey. Carlos Antonio Carrillo (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Dice: Que no ha lugar á la lisitud que hace el juzgado de 1ª Instancia de Sn José de Guadalupe.' The Diputacion rejects a petition from the court of first instance at San José. The counterpart refusal is sent to the alcalde five days later (p. 497).

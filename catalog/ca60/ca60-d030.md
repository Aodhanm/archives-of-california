Alvarado del empleo de Srio de la Diputacion Territorial que ha servido y solo continuará sus funciones hasta el momento qº se presente su sucesor.' APPROVED UNANIMOUSLY, the appointment of a successor being deferred to the next session. Art. 2 declared legal the sums the Secretary states are owed him, 'acendientes á un mil ciento setenta y nueve pesos cinco reales', and recommended their prompt satisfaction. Carrillo (D J A.) objected that the account the Secretary presented does not appear sufficiently justified to legalise the alcances it indicates, being drawn up by the interested party himself; Carrillo (D C.) defended the commission. The PRESIDENT said the account is a relación in which Alvarado himself set out his assignments with certifications and the sums the síndicos of some points have paid him on account, but that this is a matter the Corporación should not occupy itself with, being proper to the officer of the contaduría the Diputación ought by law to have; he asked the matter return to the commission until the Tesorería is systematized, then be examined and returned to the body for approval if found convenient, and remarked that some day these sums might perhaps be covered, such at present being the scarcity of funds 'que no han dado pª cubrir los gastos erogados en la compostura del salon de Sesiones'. ART. 2 TOTALLY REJECTED on a vote, the affirmative being Sres. Carrillo (D C.) and Estudillo. Art. 3, that Alvarado cease in the comisión de contador de propios y arbitrios entrusted to him by this Corporación on 16 July 830, was objected to by Carrillo (D J A.) as outside the commission's brief since the Secretary's petition does not refer to it; Castro answered that a later law establishing a Contaduría General de propios in the capital of Mexico derogates the one that gave rise to the appointment, so that the Secretary must in fact be excluded from it; ART. 3 APPROVED. The President then moved an additional article, admitted and APPROVED at once: that the Gefe Político, in communicating to the Secretary the resolution on his separation, give him in the name of the Corporación 'las gracias por la honradez, desinteres y exactitud con que ha desempeñado sus funciones'. [Beyond the band, on n136-n138, the same acta takes up the reglamento interior project: art. 1 (deputies reaching the capital on 1 June under the Gefe Político's convocatoria to swear before him, whereupon the Diputación is declared installed) drew the President's argument that the commission had had art. 334 of the Spanish Constitution in view, mistakenly, since that article was given for the creation of these bodies and not for the epochs of their meeting, the Diputación in his view needing to exist all year with its sessions permanently open; Carrillo (D J A.) replied from the March sanction date of the Constitution that the measure could only be prospective; the President asked the article return to the commission for a measure closer to the Territory's present circumstances 'por el diferente sistema que la nacion ha adoptado muy distante del qº se adoptó en la constitucion de España'; the commission withdrew art. 1, then art. 2, then the whole project, offering to present the reglamento at the immediate session; acceded to, and the session adjourned.]"

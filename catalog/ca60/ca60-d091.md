# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n229** · Sav p. corner stamp 230 / ink 228 · tomo p. p p 369. · 1834-05-02 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Mayo 2. = Mont. / Figueroa (no dice á quien' [= 1834, May 2, Monterey. Figueroa (addressee not stated).] 'Que comision se ocupe de preferencia y con actividad de formar Reglamento interior.' Figueroa directs that the committee take up, in preference and with despatch, the drafting of the Deputation's internal rules. The order that produces the Reglamento interior de la Diputacion of 16 June 1834 recovered below. Savage leaves the opening parenthesis unclosed on the leaf; transcribed as written.

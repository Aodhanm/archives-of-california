# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n270** · Sav p. 270 / 268 · tomo p. p.NO PAGE CITE GIVEN. Savage omitted it. It must fall between p. 609 and p. 611, but I have not seen a number on the leaf and do not assert one. · 1835-10-18 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Octubre 18. = Mont. / (Sin firma) á Ayuntamiento de Capital' [= 1835, October 18, Monterey. (Unsigned) to the Ayuntamiento of the Capital.] Abstract: 'Dice que Diptn en sesion de 3 del p.pº aprovo el pago de $20 mentuales qe dicho Ayuntamiento señaló á José Arana por custodia de presos.' The Diputacion, in its session of the 3rd of last month, approved 20 pesos a month to José Arana for the custody of prisoners. A named Monterey gaoler and his wage. Savage writes 'mentuales'.

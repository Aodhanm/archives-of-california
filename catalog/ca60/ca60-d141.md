# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n258** · Sav p. 258 / 256 · tomo p. p. 525. · 1834-08-09 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Agosto 9 = Mont. / Figueroa á J. B. Alvarado.' [= 1834, August 9, Monterey. Figueroa to J. B. Alvarado.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que se aprueva el gasto erogado en Compostura de Salon de Sesiones de Diputacion.' Figueroa approves the expense of refitting the Diputacion's session hall. This is the item the single band record d34 was built around; it is one of 53 documents in the band, not its subject.

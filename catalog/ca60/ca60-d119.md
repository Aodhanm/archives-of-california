# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n246** · Sav p. 247 [printed stamp] / 245 [ink] · tomo p. pp. - 465 - 69. · 1834-08-06 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Agosto 6. = Mont. / (no dice quien) á J. M. Ybarra.' [= 1834, August 6, Monterey. (Does not say who) to J. M. Ybarra.] Savage's abstract: 'Circular á alcaldes de los Angeles, San Buenaventura, Mont. y Comte. Militar de San Diego. Sobre formacion de Ayuntamientos.' The circular carrying the Diputacion's August 1834 ayuntamiento-creation decree out to the four southern and central jurisdictions. Addressee is the military commandant at San Diego; I am inferring, not asserting, that this is Juan Maria Ibarra.

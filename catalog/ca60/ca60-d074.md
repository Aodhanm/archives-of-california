# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n219–n220** · Sav p. 220–221 (printed corner stamps) · tomo p. pp. 268-70 · 1835-09-05 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n191-n219, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835 = Sbre 5 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835 - September 5 - Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] THE STRADDLING DOCUMENT. Heading and opening on n219, the last leaf of d9; the rest of the entry sits on n220, the first leaf of d10. Prior acta approved; account taken of three oficios from the Alcalde of the Pueblo de los Angeles concerning timber cutting by vecinos of that pueblo, referred to the Comision de Misiones; and an oficio from the Alcalde of the Capital asking clarification of the limits of jurisdiction, referred to the Comision de Gobernacion. Because it spans the d9/d10 seam, no single record describes it.

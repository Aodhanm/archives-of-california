# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n259** · Sav p. 259 / 257 · tomo p. pp. 552 - 53. · 1834-10-08 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Octubre 8. = Mont. / M. G. Vallejo. á José de la Guerra' [= 1834, October 8, Monterey. M. G. Vallejo to José de la Guerra.] Abstract: 'Le notifica haber sido lecto 7º vocal de la Diputacion Territorial pª los años de 1835, - 36, 37 y 38.' Vallejo notifies De la Guerra of his election as seventh vocal of the Diputacion for the four-year term 1835-1838. Names both principals and fixes the composition of the incoming assembly.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n268** · Sav p. 268 / 266 · tomo p. pp 602 - 603. · 1835-09-23 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Sbre. 23. = Mont. / Salvio Pacheco á Gefe Politico.' [= 1835, September 23, Monterey. Salvio Pacheco to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Haber pasado á Comision de Gobno, unida á la de propios y arbitrios, dictamen sobre limites de jurisdiccion de Municipalidades.' The report on the LIMITS OF MUNICIPAL JURISDICTION is referred to the Government committee sitting jointly with that of propios y arbitrios. The words 'limites de' are inserted interlineally by Savage. Directly relevant to the pueblo-boundary question.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n270** · Sav p. 270 / 268 · tomo p. p. 611 · 1835-10-28 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Octubre 28 = Mont. / José Castro á Ayuntamiento' [= 1835, October 28, Monterey. José Castro to the Ayuntamiento.] Abstract: 'Dice Que Alvarado propuso se colocara el retrato de Figueroa en el Salon de Sesiones de Diptn y qe se levante un monumento á su memoria.' Alvarado moves that Figueroa's portrait be hung in the Diputacion's session hall and a monument raised to his memory. The origin of the Figueroa commemoration whose committee report follows on n270-n271.

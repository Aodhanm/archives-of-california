SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n135-n150. Heading verbatim: 'o 1834 = Julio 10. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial. = Ympuesto sobre matanzas &ª' [= 1834, July 10, Monterey. Territorial Deputation. Tax on slaughterings, etc. (Marginal mark: 'o'. Full transcription, opening 'Leida y aprovada la acta anterior'. The day reads '10', a 1 followed by a 0, checked at magnification.)] THE SAN LUIS REY MATANZA SCANDAL, in full transcription. The President reports that having received an oficio from 'el Padre ministro de la Mision de Sn Luis Rey' about the order sent him respecting matanzas, he had asked that it be laid before the Deputation together with the minuta of his reply, in which he had allowed the padre's pretension (finding it just) on account of the pending créditos, on condition that the number be communicated so the corporation could be informed, and that he would report the result. Carrillo (D. J. A.) says that by the oficio just read the padre has NOT suspended the matanza and it is to be believed he will not, and asks that another providencia be issued to make him do so, since the date of receipt of the President's reply suggests the minister took it as the permission being granted. Carrillo (D. C.) then names the contractors: 'son bien conocidos los dos individuos con quien esta haciendo la matanza el Padre de San Luis, quales son los C. C. Silvestre de la Portilla y Juan Osuna: que estos van á medias con la Mision: que es muy escandalosa la matanza que se verifica y mucho el exeso que se comete, y mas cuando dicha Mision no tiene ganado mesteño, cosa contraria á lo que se vierte en el oficio'. The President answers that no one can speak with certainty, 'que en efecto ha habido muchos abusos de grande magnitud, pero que no han tenido cosa que se los impida y han obrado discrecionalmente: que nada se consigue con medidas paliativas y que solo prohibiendolo totalmente ó nombrando un individuo que se encargase de la especulacion podrian cuartarse males tan grandes', and asks that a proposition be framed. Carrillo (D. J. A.) asks the oficio and the reply go to a commission for a dictamen. The President replies that as to his determination to concede or not he was empowered by the corporation itself, and believed he could accede in view of the mission's undeniable créditos. The debate continues past n151.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n198** · Sav p. stamp 199 / ink 197 · tomo p. pp 233 - 34. · 1834-10-25 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n191-n219. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 25 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 25, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract. Prior acta approved, then (interlined above the line by Savage) 'Se dio lectura a dictamen de comis.n Sobre Concesion de terrenos a varios'. A reading of a commission report on grants of land to several persons. The individual grantees are not named in the abstract.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n231** · Sav p. corner stamp 232 / ink 230 · tomo p. p. 395. · 1834-08-09 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Agosto 9. = Mont. / J. B. Alvarado (no dice á quien)' [= 1834, August 9, Monterey. Juan Bautista Alvarado (addressee not stated).] 'Sobre adorno de Salon de Diputacion.' On the decoration of the Deputation's hall. Completes the Sala de Sesiones sequence: Figueroa assigns a room in his own house (29 April 1834, doc 23), the furniture expense is approved (15 May 1834, doc 24), Alvarado writes on its decoration (this piece), and on 12 October 1835 the session votes to hang Figueroa's portrait there. The $440 total appears in the band at n246-n270.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n264** · Sav p. 264 / 262 · tomo p. p 585. · 1834-09-02 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Septiembre 2 = Mont. / Roberto Pardo á Figueroa' [= 1834, September 2, Monterey. Roberto Pardo to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Pide licencia pª correr caballada mesteña en el Tular de Sta Ynes.' Petition to run wild horses in the Tular of Santa Ynez. Granted a year later by the dictamen of 5 Sept 1835 (pp. 591-92) further down the band.

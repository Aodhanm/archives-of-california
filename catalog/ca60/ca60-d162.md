# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n267** · Sav p. 267 / 265 · tomo p. p 600. · 1835-09-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 15. = Mont. / Salvio Pacheco (no dice á quien)' [= 1835, September 15, Monterey. Salvio Pacheco (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Habersele concedido terreno á José Lorenzo Quijas.' A grant of land to José Lorenzo Quijas, made in the same 15 September 1835 session. The name is that of the Franciscan José Lorenzo de la Concepcion Quijas, though the leaf gives no title and I do not assert the identification.

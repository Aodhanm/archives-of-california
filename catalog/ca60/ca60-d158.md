# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n265** · Sav p. 265 / 263 · tomo p. pp 591 - 92. · 1835-09-05 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 5. = Mont. / Manl. Jimeno á Gefe Politico' [= 1835, September 5, Monterey. Manuel Jimeno to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Que se concede á Roverto Pardo Corrida de Caballada mesteña que pidió.' Pardo's 1834 petition for the Santa Ynez tular is granted. Note Savage's spelling 'Roverto'.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n268** · Sav p. 268 / 266 · tomo p. pp 604 - 605. · 1835-09-05 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 5. = Mont. / David Spence a Gefe Poltº' [= 1835, September 5, Monterey. David Spence to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Dice Que desea saber si llegara ó no jurisdiccion hasta rancho de San Andres.' Spence asks whether the jurisdiction extends as far as Rancho San Andrés. A named ranch tested against a municipal boundary, and the concrete case behind the jurisdiction-limits dictamen.

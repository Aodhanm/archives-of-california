# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n257** · Sav p. 257 / 255 · tomo p. pp. - 520 - 21. · 1834-06-18 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Junio 18 = Angeles / José Perez, Srio. del Ayuntamiento á Figueroa.' [= 1834, June 18, Los Angeles. José Perez, Secretary of the Ayuntamiento, to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Le comunica haber manifestado Vicente Moraga, no poder desempeñar el empleo de Srio.' Reports that Vicente Moraga has declared himself unable to serve as secretary of the Los Angeles ayuntamiento. Sets up the Arzaga appointment three weeks later.

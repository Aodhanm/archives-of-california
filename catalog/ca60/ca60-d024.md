# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n105–n107** · Sav p. stamps 106–108 / ink 104–106 (the acta continues onto n108 = stamp 109 / ink 107 and past it) · tomo p. p.no page cite falls within the band; the acta is still running at n107 and closes only beyond the band boundary · 1834-06-12 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n53-n107. Heading verbatim: 'o 1834 = Junio 12 = Mont. / Diputacion Provincial = Sobre desvanecer embustes de enemigos del Gobº, evitar matanza de ganado &a' [= 1834, June 12, Monterey. Provincial Deputation. On dispelling the falsehoods of the enemies of the Government, preventing cattle slaughter, etc.] ⚠ THIS ACTA IS CUT IN HALF BY THE BAND BOUNDARY: it opens on n105 inside band d5 and runs on past n107 into n108 and beyond, i.e. into the span of catalog record 6 (n108–n134). Approval of the previous acta was SUSPENDED because Figueroa observed that it omitted the discussion arising from an incidente raised by the comisión de Gobº y policía in the expositive part of its dictamen, 'en que hace referencia á los presidiarios que manda el Supmo Gobºo á este pais y á la traslacion de Presidios', to which he had replied in order to satisfy the public. Correspondence: an official communication from the Secretaries of the H. Legislatura del Estado de Puebla enclosing a manifiesto issued by that chamber 'con el objeto de desvanecer las impreciones que puedan hacer las falzas maquinaciones y embustes de que se valen los enemigos del Sistema de Gobºo para alucinar á los sencillos e ignorantes'. ⭐ 'Oficio del C. Pio Pico vocal de esta corporacion fhã en la Mision de Sn Gabriel en 2 del Corriente' reporting that he is ill at that place and cannot continue his journey to join the corporation; answered that the corporation feels his misfortune and awaits him in the Capital as soon as he recovers (this documents the 'impedimento físico' invoked on 27 May). Oficio of the Comandante Militar de Sta Barbara enclosing the Alcalde's report that no funds remain in the fondo municipal to remit to the Diputación's treasury, some débitos being owed by individuals. Oficio of the Alcalde del Pueblo de Sn José de Guadalupe reporting absolutely no existing funds to remit. Oficio of the Comandante Militar de San Diego reporting he has remitted the account of sums collected there for derechos municipales and issued a libramiento in favour of the Diputación's treasury. Representación of 'los C.C. Felix Gallardo y Manuel Arzaga, vecinos del Estado de Sonora' asking licence to set up a 'fábrica de Aguardiente mescal en las inmediaciones de Sn Felipe' and reduction of the $16 municipal duty assigned to the grape brandies of the Territory; to industria y agricultura. Representación of 'el Cº Salvio Pacheco vecino del Pueblo de Sn José de Guadalupe' asking leave to brand his stock on the ejidos of that pueblo's community, his herds being abundant, and to kill some bulls in the open range, the Gefe Político asking a measure reconciling community and private interests (this last item lies on n108, outside the band).

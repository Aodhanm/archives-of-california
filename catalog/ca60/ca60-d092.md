# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n229** · Sav p. corner stamp 230 / ink 228 · tomo p. p p 370. · 1834-05-03 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 3 = Mont. / José Antº Carrillo (no dice á quien)' [= 1834, May 3, Monterey. José Antonio Carrillo (addressee not stated).] 'Que Sesiones de Diputacion se reunan á las 10 de la mañana.' That the Deputation's sittings convene at ten in the morning. A one-page procedural motion, but it dates the sitting hour of the 1834 Diputación and it is by José Antonio Carrillo, not his brother Carlos Antonio, who dominates the rest of this bundle. Same day as Carlos Antonio Carrillo's two ayuntamiento propositions at p.452.

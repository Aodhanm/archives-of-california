# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n260** · Sav p. 260 / 258 · tomo p. pp 555. - 56. · 1834-10-30 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Octubre 30 = Mont. / Figueroa á Prestte de Diptn' [= 1834, October 30, Monterey. Figueroa to the President of the Diputacion.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que adjunta lista nominal de individuos qe J. M. Hijar nombró pª preceptores de 1ªs letras.' Figueroa lays before the assembly the nominal roll of primary schoolmasters appointed by José María Híjar. The covering letter to the roster on the next two leaves.

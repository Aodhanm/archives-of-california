# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n162–n163** · Sav p. n162 stamp 163 / ink 161 to n163 stamp 164 / ink 162 · tomo p. pp 160 - 64 · 1834-07-22 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 22 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial = Consecion de terrenos y licencia.' [= 1834, July 22, Monterey. Territorial Diputación. Concession of lands and leave of absence.] Heading and opening on n162, close on n163. Dictamen of the Comisión de Colonización y terrenos valdíos on the concession made by the Sr. Gefe Político of the tract 'comprendido entre el Arroyo de las nueces y los golgones' in favour of JUANA SANCHEZ DE PACHECO. Approved, resolving that the expediente return to the Gefe Político 'pa. su curso'. Then: 'Que habiendo pedido licencia los vocales de la Diputacion Antº Estudillo y Franco. de Haro por 3 meses, se hizo extensiva esta proposicion para todos y se aprovó' [vocales José Antonio Estudillo and Francisco de Haro having requested three months' leave, the proposition was extended to all members and approved]. 'Se levantó la Sesion pública pa. entrar en Secreta.' NOTE on the place name: Savage's hand reads 'golgones' (magnified and checked); I infer, but have not confirmed from any source on this leaf, that this is his rendering of Bolbones, i.e. the Rancho Arroyo de las Nueces y Bolbones.

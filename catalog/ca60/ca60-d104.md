# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n233** · Sav p. corner stamp 234 / ink 232 · tomo p. p. 429. · 1832-12-31 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1832. = Dibre. 31. = Mont. / José Aguilar' [= 1832, December 31, Monterey. José Aguilar.] 'Certifica habersele pagado á J. B. Alvarado á cuenta de sueldos como Srio de Diputacion $326 = 75.c' Aguilar certifies that Alvarado has been paid $326.75 on account of his salary as secretary of the Deputation. A hard salary figure for a named Californio officeholder at a fixed date, and the earliest-dated document in the whole band, three years before the sessions the band record claims to describe. Savage writes the month 'Dibre'.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n265** · Sav p. 265 / 263 · tomo p. p. 590. · 1835-08-28 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Agosto 28. = Mont. / Salvio Pacheco á Gefe Politº' [= 1835, August 28, Monterey. Salvio Pacheco to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Haberse aprovado sueldo de Srio.' Pacheco, acting for the Diputacion, reports approval of the secretary's salary. Closes the Maldonado salary sequence.

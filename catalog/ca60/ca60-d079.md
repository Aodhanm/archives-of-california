# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n221** · Sav p. corner stamp 222 / ink 220 · tomo p. p.p. 282 - 85. · 1835-09-21 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Sbre 21 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial.' [= 1835, September 21, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] 'Dice: Aprovada la acta anterior. Se dio cuenta con varios dictámenes sobre terrenos.' Prior acta approved; account taken of several dictámenes on lands. Four original pages, again abstracted to one line. The gap between this acta (ending p.285) and the next (beginning p.287) leaves original p.286 unaccounted for in the copy.

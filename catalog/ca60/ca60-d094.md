# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n230** · Sav p. printed corner stamp 231 · tomo p. pp. 387-89 · 1834-04-29 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n220-n245, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Abril 29 = Mont. / Figueroa, á Presidente y vocales de la Diputacion' [= 1834, April 29, Monterey. Figueroa to the President and members of the Diputación.] 'Haber señalado sala de su casa habitacion pª que la Diputacion celebre sus sesiones' — Figueroa reports having designated a room in his own residence as the chamber in which the Diputación is to hold its sessions. This is the origin of the Sala de Sesiones whose furnishing expenses appear in the next entry and again in record d17.

# Recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a grouped entry 

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n230** · Sav p. printed corner stamp 231 · tomo p. pp. 387-89 · 1834-04-29 · Savage abstract · 

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n220-n245, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Abril 29 = Mont. / Figueroa, á Presidente y vocales de la Diputacion' [= 1834, April 29, Monterey. Figueroa to the President and members of the Diputación.] 'Haber señalado sala de su casa habitacion pª que la Diputacion celebre sus sesiones' — Figueroa reports having designated a room in his own residence as the chamber in which the Diputación is to hold its sessions. This is the origin of the Sala de Sesiones whose furnishing expenses appear in the next entry and again in record d17.

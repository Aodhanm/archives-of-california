# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n249** · Sav p. 250 / 248 · tomo p. p - 494. · 1834-06-17 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Junio 17 = Sn José de Guadpe. / Pedro Chavoya á Gefe Politico' [= 1834, June 17, San José de Guadalupe. Pedro Chavoya to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Le comunica haber nombrado Srio de juzgado de su cargo, á José Fernandez.' The San José alcalde reports his appointment of José Fernandez as secretary of his court. A named San José municipal officer, useful for the SJ pueblo corpus.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n162** · Sav p. n162 stamp 163 / ink 161 · tomo p. pp 143 = 49 · 1834-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 12 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 12, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage abstract, four lines: 'Dice: Aprovada acta anterior. Se propuso se nombrara un preceptor pa. Villa de Branciforte. Aprovado' [prior acta approved; it was proposed that a preceptor (schoolmaster) be named for the Villa de Branciforte; approved]. Public-schooling measure for Branciforte, the follow-through on the second reading taken in the 10 July session.

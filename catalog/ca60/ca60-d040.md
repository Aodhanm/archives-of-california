(6) THE BANDOS DICTAMEN (municipal ordinance power): art. 2 leaves in force only those articles that can emanate from the Gefe Político's gubernatorial authority, and bandos proposed by Constitutional Alcaldes are submitted to his inspection; a third proposition asks the Cámaras de la Unión to define (calificar) the faculties of this Territory's ayuntamientos and alcaldes. Carlos A. Carrillo and José Antonio Carrillo both speak. (7) THE ARBITRIOS SCHEDULE, item by item: a solar tax of 2 reales per square vara (Carrillo pressing for 1½), ejidos let in censo enfitéutico, a duty on maderas (timber), and a slaughter duty of 1 real per head of cattle, ½ real per sheep, 2 reales per pig. (8) THE OWNERSHIP QUESTION, the heart of the session: José Antonio Carrillo holds the Misiones must pay 'por considerarlas dueñas del ganado que matan' and 'calificó dueñas á las misiones ... por ser estas los legítimos dueños de los bienes que tienen en posecion'; Figueroa answers that if only slaughter for the abasto público is meant, neither the Misiones nor the proprietors would pay. (9) Figueroa states that the Diputación does not have a reglamento interior for its sessions, and asks the commission of the ramo to present one 'cuanto antes para no tropesar en escollos'. Session suspended at 2 p.m. NOTE: the heading sits on n150, the context leaf immediately before the band; eleven of its twelve leaves lie inside d8."

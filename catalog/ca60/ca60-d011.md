# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n199** · Sav p. p 237. · tomo p. p.200 (printed corner stamp) · 1834-10-30 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of a band record spanning n191-n219, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 30 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial Sobre que quede vigente cobro de derecho de maderas.' [= 1834 - October 30 - Monterey. Territorial Deputation. On the timber duty remaining in force.] Prior acta approved. First reading given to two dictamenes of the Comision de Propios y Arbitrios: (1) on the Ayuntamiento de la Capital's communication to the Gefe Politico asking that collection of the derecho de maderas (timber duty) remain in force; (2) on the same body's consulta whether a salary may be assigned to the member of the corporation who serves as its Secretary. Two propositions were then put to discussion, of which the first was approved: 'Que las reducciones y pueblos de indigenas estan sugetos a autoridad politica de los pueblos cabeceras de partido que hasta la fecha se han reconocido con este caracter.' That is a substantive ruling subordinating indigenous reducciones and pueblos to the political authority of the recognised cabecera pueblos of each partido. Nothing in d9's summary ('Monterey sindico/secretary; jurisdiction disputes; municipal business') surfaces it.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n162** · Sav p. n162 stamp 163 / ink 161 · tomo p. pp 150 - 59 · 1834-07-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 15 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 15, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage abstract: 'Dice: Aprovada acta anterior, se dió lectura á renuncia presentada por Vicente Moraga Sindico de Ayuntamiento de Los Angeles. Fue aprovada.' [prior acta approved; the resignation tendered by Vicente Moraga, síndico of the Ayuntamiento of Los Angeles, was read and approved]. Ten original pages compressed by Savage into two lines, so the underlying acta is substantially longer than the abstract.

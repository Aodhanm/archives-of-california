# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n264** · Sav p. 264 / 262 · tomo p. pp 588. = 89. · 1835-08-05 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Agosto 5. = Mont. / J. M. Maldonado.' [= 1835, August 5, Monterey. J. M. Maldonado.] Abstract: 'Haber aprovado el ayuntamiento el sueldo de 20 $ mensuales á Srio.' The ayuntamiento approves 20 pesos a month for its secretary. Set against the 50 pesos voted the Diputacion's secretary in 1834 (p. 517), this gives the municipal-to-territorial salary ratio.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n257** · Sav p. 257 / 255 · tomo p. p 519. · 1834-08-09 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Agosto 9 = Mont. / (Sin firma) á J. B. Alvarado, Tesorero de Diputacion' [= 1834, August 9, Monterey. (Unsigned) to J. B. Alvarado, Treasurer of the Diputacion.] Abstract: 'Adjunta oficio.' A covering note only, but it fixes Alvarado's office: Tesorero de la Diputacion in August 1834.

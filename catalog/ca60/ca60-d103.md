# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n233** · Sav p. corner stamp 234 / ink 232 · tomo p. p. p. 426. - 28. · 1834-04-30 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Abril 30 = Mont. / Juan B Alvarado' [= 1834, April 30, Monterey. Juan Bautista Alvarado.] 'Relacion de gastos de la Diputacion.' Alvarado's statement of the Deputation's expenses, rendered the same day he resigns the secretaryship. Three original pages of itemised legislative expenditure, none of which Savage copies. Pairs with the Aguilar certificate below and with the Sala de Sesiones expense items.

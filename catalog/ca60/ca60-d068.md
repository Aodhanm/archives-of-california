# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n206** · Sav p. stamps 207, 214 / ink 211, 212 · tomo p. pp 247. = 49. · 1834-11-03 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n191-n219. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = N.bre 3 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial. Sobre estraccion de maderas &.a' [= 1834, November 3, Monterey. Territorial Deputation. On the extraction of timber, etc.] The SECOND session of 3 November 1834, the extraordinary sitting called for six that afternoon at the close of the ordinary session. A distinct acta with its own heading, page cite and closing vote. Savage abstract. Prior acta approved; the propositions of the Comision de Arbitrios put to discussion. 1a: 'Los Ayuntamientos en sus demarcaciones municipales de conformidad con art.o 8.o cap.t.o 1.o de decreto de 23 Junio de 1813 dictaran las providencias conducentes p.a la concervacion y repoblacion de los montes, estableciendo segun sus atribuciones penas correccionales a los infractores.' Approved. 2a prohibits the export of timber outside the Territory; carriage from port to port requires a written permit from the Alcalde; contrabandistas are fined the value of the wood as appraised by two peritos, the proceeds to the municipal fund. 3a: national and foreign vessels, 'previa calificacion del Capitan de P.to y permiso de Alcalde', may take the timber they need 'p.a sus usos ordinarios y reparacion de averias'. Approved. Carrillo then 'propuso q.e se declararan cerradas las sesiones extraordinarias y q.e vocales se retirasen a sus hogares. Aprovado.' This closes the 1834 extraordinary sessions; the register does not resume until 25 August 1835. A forestry and timber-export code for California, and the direct antecedent of the Los Angeles timber-cutting oficios of 5 September 1835.

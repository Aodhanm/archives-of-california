# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n256** · Sav p. 256 / 254 · tomo p. p 514 · 1834-07-02 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 2. = Mont. / Srio de Diputacion, á Ayuntamiento de Mont.' [= 1834, July 2, Monterey. Secretary of the Diputacion to the Ayuntamiento of Monterey.] Abstract: 'Haber nombrado Srio á José Mª Maldonado.' Notifies the Monterey ayuntamiento of the appointment of José María Maldonado as secretary. First of a three-document sequence on the secretaryship and its salary.

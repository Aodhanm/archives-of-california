# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n248** · Sav p. 249 / 247 · tomo p. pp 488 - 89 (2 doc.) · 1834-05-23 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Mont. / Srio de Diputacion á Figueroa. / Mayo 23' [= 1834, Monterey. Secretary of the Diputacion to Figueroa. May 23. The heading line carries no month; Savage put 'Mayo 23' in the body beside the page cite.] Savage folds TWO documents into one entry, itemised on separate lines: '- Aprovacion de dictamen' and '- Id de Arts contenidos en dictamen de obras Publicas.' The Diputacion's approval of a committee report and then of the articles contained in the public-works dictamen. Ties to the Monterey calzada file above.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n247** · Sav p. 248 / 246 · tomo p. pp. 480 - 482. · 1834-05-10 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 10º. = Mont. / Figueroa á Presidente de la Diputacion' [= 1834, May 10, Monterey. Figueroa to the President of the Diputacion.] Abstract: 'Formacion de Compañias de milicia activa.' Figueroa puts the raising of active-militia companies before the Diputacion. Pairs with the Carrillo item of 1 Aug 1834 (pp. 485-86) and the San Diego sorteo file of 1835 later in the band.

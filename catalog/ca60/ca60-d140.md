# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n257** · Sav p. 257 / 255 · tomo p. pp 522 = 24. · 1834-07-10 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 10 = Mont. / José Antº Carrillo á Figueroa' [= 1834, July 10, Monterey. José Antonio Carrillo to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Haber nombrado á Manuel Arzaga, Srio del Ayuntamiento de los Angeles.' Manuel Arzaga named secretary of the Los Angeles ayuntamiento in Moraga's place. Confirms José Antonio Carrillo is the J. A. Carrillo of p. 517.

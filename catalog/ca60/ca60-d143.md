# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n258** · Sav p. 258 / 256 · tomo p. p 528. - 29 · 1834-07-01 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 1º = Monterey / Alvarado (no dice á Quien)' [= 1834, July 1, Monterey. Alvarado (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Cuenta documentada, dónde consta haberse gastado $ 440 = 7.rs en adorno de Salon de Sesiones.' The itemised, voucher-backed account: 440 pesos 7 reales spent fitting out the Diputacion's chamber. The receipts follow on pp. 531-50.

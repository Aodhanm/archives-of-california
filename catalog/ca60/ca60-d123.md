# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n248** · Sav p. 249 / 247 · tomo p. pp. 485 = 86. · 1834-08-01 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Agosto 1º = Mont. / C. A. Carrillo (no dice á Quien.' [= 1834, August 1, Monterey. C. A. Carrillo (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Sobre creacion de Compañias militares.' Carrillo, president of the 1834 Diputacion, on the creation of military companies. Initial reads C (compare the clearly different J of J. A. Carrillo on n256/n257).

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n259** · Sav p. 259 / 257 · tomo p. pp 532. - 50. · undated; Savage's own editorial note, standing between the 18 Jul 1834 receipt and the 8 Oct 1834 letter · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ (Se encuentran 19 recibos sin ningun interes, de pp 532. - 50.)' [= (There are 19 receipts of no interest at all, from pp. 532 to 550.)] Savage's own note in place of nineteen documents he chose not to copy: the remaining vouchers of the Sala de Sesiones account, originals pp. 532-550. Catalogued so that the nineteen suppressed receipts are on the record as a known, deliberate lacuna in the Savage copy rather than an absence in the original.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n166** · Sav p. n166 stamp 167 / ink 165 · tomo p. pp. 191 = 96 · 1834-07-22 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 22. = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 22, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage abstract: 'Dice: Aprovada la acta anterior. Se dio cuenta con una representacion del C. Antº Chavoya relativa á qe. se le permita hacer una Corrida de Caballada mesteña para sus urgentes necesidades. Se pasó á Comistn. de Gobno.' [the petition of citizen Antonio Chavoya to be permitted a roundup of wild horses for his urgent needs; passed to the Comisión de Gobierno]. A LATER PENCIL HAND, WRITING IN ENGLISH, has added beneath the page cite: 'see also 161-2' — a cross-reference to Savage's pp. 161-2, i.e. the first-run 22 July acta at n162-n163. That annotation is external evidence, on the leaf, that the two July runs were recognised as parallel.

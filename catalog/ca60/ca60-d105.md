# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n234** · Sav p. corner stamp 235 / ink 233 · tomo p. p. p. 430 - 34 · 1834-05-10 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 10 = Mont. / Carlos A. Carrillo' [= 1834, May 10, Monterey. Carlos Antonio Carrillo.] 'Dice Que fué admitida renuncia q.e solicitó Srio de Diputacion.' That the resignation requested by the secretary of the Deputation was accepted. Five original pages, closing the Alvarado resignation of 30 April. Carrillo signs, presumably as the presiding or reporting vocal.

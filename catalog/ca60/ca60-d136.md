# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n256** · Sav p. 256 / 254 · tomo p. pp. - 515. - 16. · 1834-07-01 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 1º = Monterey / (No dice Quien) á Maldonado' [= 1834, July 1, Monterey. (Does not say who) to Maldonado. A stray letter-form follows '1º' in the date line.] Abstract: 'Le notifica que la Diputn lo nombro Srio.' The letter of appointment itself, notifying Maldonado that the Diputacion has named him secretary.

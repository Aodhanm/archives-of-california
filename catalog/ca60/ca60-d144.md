# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n259** · Sav p. 259 / 257 · tomo p. p - 531. · 1834-07-18 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 18. = Mont. / José Almonte á Alvarado' [= 1834, July 18, Monterey. José Almonte to Alvarado.] Abstract: 'Recibo por valor de $ 20 importe de 500 pies de madera.' Receipt for 20 pesos for 500 feet of lumber, one of the vouchers behind the 440-peso account. A concrete price for sawn timber at Monterey in 1834.

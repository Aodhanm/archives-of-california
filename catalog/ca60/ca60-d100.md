# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n232** · Sav p. corner stamp 233 / ink 231 · tomo p. p 418. · 1834-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 - Mayo 9. = Mont. / José Figueroa á Presid.te y vocales de Diputacion' [= 1834, May 9, Monterey. José Figueroa to the President and members of the Deputation.] 'Acompaña dictamen relativo á obligaciones del portero.' Figueroa forwards the committee report on the duties of the doorkeeper. Trivial in itself but it dates the Deputation's staffing of its own chamber and it is the cover for the Maldonado dictamen at pp. 419-22 below.

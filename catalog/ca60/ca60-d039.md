# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n149** · Sav p. n149 = stamp 150 / ink 148 · tomo p. pp. 129-33 · 1834-07-08 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n135-n150. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 8 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 8, Monterey. Territorial Deputation. (Marginal mark: '+'. Savage abstract, opening 'Dice:'. The day digit is a figure-eight, checked at magnification against the 6 on n148 and the 5s on n147 and earlier on this same leaf.)] Savage's abstract in full: 'Dice: aprovada la acta anterior, Se dio cuenta con oficio del P. M. de la Mision, relativo á matanzas de ganado, pidiendo hacer moderada matanza con algunos de razon pª cubrir algunas deudas que tiene contraidas la referida mision. Concedido.' A mission Padre Ministro asks leave to make a moderate cattle slaughter in partnership with 'algunos de razon' (i.e. contracting with gente de razón) to cover debts the mission has contracted; GRANTED. This is the decision the 10 July session (next row) then reopens as a scandal. Savage's abstract does not name the mission; the 10 July acta identifies the correspondent as the Padre ministro of Mission San Luis Rey, and the two are very likely the same matter, but the identification is inference and is not on this leaf. Substantively this is the Deputation licensing mission-herd liquidation on the eve of secularization.

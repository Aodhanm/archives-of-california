# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n263** · Sav p. 263 / 261 · tomo p. pp 577 - 80. · 1834-11-09 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Noviembre 9. = Mont. / (Sin firma) á J. M. Herrera, Sub-comisario' [= 1834, November 9, Monterey. (Unsigned) to J. M. Herrera, Sub-comisario.] Abstract: several schoolmasters have represented their need of pecuniary aid; Herrera is to give them what is strictly necessary for leaving the capital; on the road they have vagajes and food at no cost; on arrival at their destinations they are to receive whatever further aid they need. The dispersal of the colony's teachers to their posts, with the fiscal mechanics spelled out.

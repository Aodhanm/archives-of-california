# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n229** · Sav p. corner stamp 230 / ink 228 · tomo p. p p 366 - 67. · 1833-12-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1833 = Dbre 15. = Mont. / Nicolas Gutierres' [= 1833, December 15, Monterey. Nicolás Gutiérrez.] 'Acta electoral.' The electoral return. This closes the December 1833 Monterey electoral-junta bundle whose earlier pieces the catalog already holds (the Tiburcio Castro notices and the Jimeno/Aguilar credential scrutinies, docs 19-22); the final return itself had no record. Nicolás Gutiérrez is the same officer notified of his election on 1 December at pp. 336-38.

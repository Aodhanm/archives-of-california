# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n234** · Sav p. corner stamp 235 / ink 233 · tomo p. p p 438 = 39. · 1834-04-28 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Abril 28. = Mont. / Agustin B. Zamorano' [= 1834, April 28, Monterey. Agustín Vicente Zamorano (initialled 'B.' by Savage).] 'Dice 'Relacion de los Sitios Concedios que aun se encuentran pendientes en la Diputacion'' — a schedule of the land-grant sites already conceded but still pending before the Deputation, drawn up by Zamorano, the territorial secretary and printer. A two-page NAMED LIST of pending Alta California land grants as of 28 April 1834, i.e. a snapshot of the grant docket on the eve of secularization. Savage copies the title only and not one site name, so the list survives only as a title here; this is precisely the kind of item a band record makes invisible. Savage writes the middle initial 'B.' where the printer's name is normally Agustín Vicente Zamorano, and 'Concedios' for 'Concedidos'; both kept as written.

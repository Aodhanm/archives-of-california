# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n267** · Sav p. 267 / 265 · tomo p. p - 599. · 1835-09-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 15. = Mont. / José Castro' [= 1835, September 15, Monterey. José Castro.] Abstract: 'Que en sesion del dia de la fecha, aprovo la Diputacion la propn del dictamen antecedente.' The Diputacion adopts the San Diego sorteo dictamen in session of that day. This is the only trace in the band of the session of 15 September 1835; the acta itself is not copied.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n262** · Sav p. 262 / 260 · tomo p. pp 563-71. · 1834-11-07 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Nbre. 7. = Mont. / (Sin firma ni direccion.)' [= 1834, November 7, Monterey. (Unsigned and without address.)] Abstract: 'Haber nombrado preceptor de la escuela primaria y normal de Mont. á Dn José Mª Hijar.' Híjar himself named schoolmaster of the primary and normal school of Monterey. Nine pages in the original, the longest single item in this stretch of the band, and directly bearing on Híjar's position in California after the colony's collapse.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n250** · Sav p. 251 / 249 · tomo p. p 499. · 1834-06-15 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '1834 = Junio 15. = Mont. / Pedro Chavoya á Figueroa' [= 1834, June 15, Monterey. Pedro Chavoya to Figueroa. NOTE: this entry carries NO + or o margin mark, unlike its neighbours.] Abstract: 'Dice: Que ha prohibido las corridas de ganado y caballada mostrenca, en cumplimiento de órden qe recibio.' The San José alcalde reports that he has banned the rodeos of unbranded (mostrenco) cattle and horses in obedience to an order received. Opens the mostrenco dispute that dominates the next five leaves.

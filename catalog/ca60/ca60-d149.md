# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n262** · Sav p. 262 / 260 · tomo p. pp. 558 - 61. · 1834-10-31 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 31. - Mont. / José Figueroa (no dice á quien.)' [= 1834, October 31, Monterey. José Figueroa (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Haber aprovado la Diputacion 7 Arts de dictamen sobre instruccion pública.' The Diputacion approves a seven-article report on public instruction, the day after the Híjar roster was tabled. The legislative foundation of the 1834 California school plan.

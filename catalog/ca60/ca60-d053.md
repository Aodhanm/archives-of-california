# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n168–n169** · Sav p. n168 stamp 169 / ink 167 to n169 stamp 170 / ink 168 · tomo p. pp. 201.203 · 1834-07-26 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 26 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 26, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Short entry, heading on n168 and close on n169: 'Dice. Aprovada acta anterior, s[e] ... puso á discucion un dictamen sobre terreno.' [prior acta approved; a dictamen on land was put to discussion]. Savage gives no outcome and names no grantee.

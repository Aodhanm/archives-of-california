# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n231** · Sav p. corner stamp 232 / ink 230 · tomo p. p p 391 - 93. · 1834-05-30 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Mayo 30. = Mont. / Srio. de la Diputacion á Gefe Politico' [= 1834, May 30, Monterey. Secretary of the Deputation to the Jefe Político.] 'Que en acuerdo de 15 del mismo aprovo la Corp.n dictamen q.e te acompaña.' That by resolution of the 15th of the same month the Corporation approved the accompanying dictamen. Three original pages. The 15 May 1834 resolution referred to is the one for which Carlos A. Carrillo's note at p.390 (doc 24) is the other surviving trace.

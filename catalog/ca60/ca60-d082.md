# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n223** · Sav p. corner stamp 224 / ink 222 · tomo p. p p 292. = 97. · 1835-10-12 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Octubre 12 = Mont / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1835, October 12, Monterey. Territorial Deputation (session record).] Prior acta approved; account taken of various dictámenes on lands. Then the resolution: 'Que se coloque retrato de finado Gral Figueroa en el Salon de Sesiones de la Diputacion, en prueva de aprecio por su distingudo mérito' (that the portrait of the late General Figueroa be placed in the Deputation's session hall, in proof of esteem for his distinguished merit). This is the Diputación's FIRST commemorative act for Figueroa, thirteen days after his death, and it stands a month and more before the 'monumento á la memoria del finado Gral José Figueroa' of 21 November 1835 that the catalog already holds at n271. It also ties the Sala de Sesiones documents on n230-n231 (Figueroa's own room, its furnishing, its 'adorno') into a single thread.

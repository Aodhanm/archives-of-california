# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n235** · Sav p. 236 [stamp] · tomo p. p.— (no cite on the part of the leaf read) · 1833-09-22 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n220-n245, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'O 1833. = Sbre. 22. = Sn. Diego. / Instancia de vecinos de Sn Diego sobre creacion de autoridades Municipales.' [= 1833, September 22, San Diego. Petition of the vecinos of San Diego for the creation of municipal authorities.] The petition itself, copied out rather than merely abstracted. The text begins 'Los C.C. José Antº Estudillo, Juan B. Osuna, Franco Mª Alvarado, Ma…' — the named signatories are the San Diego citizens who asked for municipal self-government. This is the foundational petition behind San Diego's ayuntamiento of 1834 and is worth a full transcription pass.

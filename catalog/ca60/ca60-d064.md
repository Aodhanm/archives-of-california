# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n198** · Sav p. stamp 199 / ink 197 · tomo p. pp 235 - 37. · 1834-10-28 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n191-n219. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 28 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 28, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract. Prior acta approved. The dictamen of the Comision de Misiones 'Sobre nombramiento de preceptor en el Pueblo de S.n Antonio' was put to discussion and approved. Appointment of a schoolmaster at the pueblo of San Antonio, referred through the missions commission, which places the ex-mission schools inside the secularization machinery.

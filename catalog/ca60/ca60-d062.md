# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n197-n198** · Sav p. stamps 198-199 / ink 196-197 · tomo p. pp 232 - 33. · 1834-10-23 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n191-n219. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Oct.e 23 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 23, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract. Prior acta approved. First reading given to a dictamen presented by the Comision de Gob.o 'relativo a nombramiento y honorario de Srio acordado por Ayunt.o de S. Barb.a' (on the appointment and fee of a Secretary agreed by the Ayuntamiento of Santa Barbara). Municipal-office business for the newly erected Santa Barbara ayuntamiento.

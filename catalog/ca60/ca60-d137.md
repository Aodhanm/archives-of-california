# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n256** · Sav p. 256 / 254 · tomo p. pp 517 - 18. · 1834-07-01 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 1º = Mont. / J. A. Carrillo á Figueroa' [= 1834, July 1, Monterey. J. A. Carrillo to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Habersele señalado á Srio de la Diputacion $ 50 mensales.' The secretary of the Diputacion is assigned 50 pesos a month. A hard salary figure for the territorial assembly's staff. Initial is J (compare the C of C. A. Carrillo on n248/n250); José Antonio Carrillo is signing on n257 in the same hand.

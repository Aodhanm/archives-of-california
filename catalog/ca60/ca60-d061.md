# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n197** · Sav p. stamp 198 / ink 196 · tomo p. pp 231-232. · 1834-10-18 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n191-n219. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Octubre 18 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, October 18, Monterey. Territorial Deputation.] Savage abstract ('Dice.'). Prior acta approved. Vocal Pio Pico proposed 'que por conducto del Gefe Politico, se invitase al Sr Dn Jose M.a Hijar a que si no tuviere inconveniente manifestase a la Corporacion, instrucciones que tenga de la Superioridad.' Approved. This is the Diputacion's own move to get sight of Hijar's instructions, the day after Figueroa laid the Hijar papers before it.

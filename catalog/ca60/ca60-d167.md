# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n269** · Sav p. 269 / 267 · tomo p. pp 608 - 609. · 1835-10-13 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Octubre 13. = Mont. / J. B. Alvarado á Gefe Politico' [= 1835, October 13, Monterey. J. B. Alvarado to the Gefe Politico.] Abstract: 'Pretender José Arceo cantidad de dinero por alcances de asignacion qe Gobno Supmo le ofreció como Colono.' José Arceo claims arrears of the stipend the supreme government promised him as a colonist. A named Híjar-Padrés colonist pursuing his promised pay through the Diputacion after the colony's collapse.

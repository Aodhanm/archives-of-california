# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n164** · Sav p. n164 stamp 165 / ink 163 · tomo p. pp. 177 - 183 · 1834-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Julio 12 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 12, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage abstract: 'Dice: Aprovada acta Antr. Se dió cuenta con dictamen de Comisr. de Mises., relativo á oficio de P. de Misn. de S. Luis Rey pidiendo licencia para hacer matanza de ganado. Se le dispensaron los tramites, y puesto á discucion se aprovó.' [dictamen of the Comisión de Misiones on the oficio of the padre of Mission San Luis Rey requesting licence to slaughter cattle; trámites dispensed, put to discussion and approved]. The outcome of the San Luis Rey matanza fight opened in the long 10 July acta: the licence was granted. In the first run this same subject is dated 10 July, here 12 July.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n255** · Sav p. 255 / 253 · tomo p. pp. - 508 - 10. · 1834-07-21 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 21. = Mont. / Antº Chavoya á Figueroa' [= 1834, July 21, Monterey. Antonio Chavoya to Figueroa.] Abstract: 'Pidiendo licencia pª hacer corrida de Caballada mesteña.' An individual licence application to round up wild horses, filed immediately after the collective petition. Shows the ban being worked around case by case.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n231** · Sav p. corner stamp 232 / ink 230 · tomo p. p p 398 - 99. · 1833-06-19 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1833. = Junio 19. = Mont. / J. Figueroa á Diputacion.' [= 1833, June 19, Monterey. José Figueroa to the Deputation.] 'Acompaña art.s de Reglamento Municipal.' Figueroa transmits articles of the Municipal Regulation to the Deputation. The cover letter for the Reglamento Municipal copied out at pp. 400-417 immediately below. CAVEAT: the second digit of the day is read as 9 (a closed upper loop over a heavy vertical, matching Savage's 9 in 'pp 292 = 97' on n223 rather than his small hooked 7 in 'pp 316-17' on n227), but 17 cannot be excluded at this scan resolution. The year 1833 is unambiguous.

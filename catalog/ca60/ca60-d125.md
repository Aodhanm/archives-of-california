# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n248** · Sav p. 249 / 247 · tomo p. pp 489 - 92. · 1834-05-27 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Mayo 27. = Mont. / Srio. de Diputacion á Legislaturas de varios Estados.' [= 1834, May 27, Monterey. Secretary of the Diputacion to the Legislatures of various States.] Abstract: 'Acusándo recibo de varias comunicaciones.' The California Diputacion acknowledging receipt of communications from the legislatures of several states of the Mexican federation. Evidence of the territorial assembly's correspondence with sister legislatures.

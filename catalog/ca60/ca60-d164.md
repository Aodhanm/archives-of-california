# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n268** · Sav p. 268 / 266 · tomo p. p 603. · 1835-09-23 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1835. = Septiembre 23. = Mont. / Juan B Alvarado. (no dice á quien)' [= 1835, September 23, Monterey. Juan B. Alvarado (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Que en sesion de la fha se aprovo el antecedente dictamen.' The Diputacion approves the municipal-jurisdiction report in session of 23 September 1835. Again the only trace of that session; no acta is copied.

# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n247** · Sav p. 248 / 246 · tomo p. pp 483 - 84. · 1834-01-17 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Enero 17 = Mont. / Figueroa al mismo, á Presidente y vocales' [= 1834, January 17, Monterey. Figueroa to the same, to the President and vocales.] Abstract: 'Dice Que gavinetes de Europa tratan de Colocar á Infante Franco de Paula en clase de Emperador de México.' Figueroa reports to the assembled Diputacion the rumour that European cabinets intend to place the Infante Francisco de Paula on a Mexican throne. A monarchist-conspiracy warning reaching the far northwestern frontier.

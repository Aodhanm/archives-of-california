# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n250–n251** · Sav p. 251–252 / 249–250 · tomo p. p 502. · 1834-06-21 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Junio 21. = Mont. / Srio de Diputacion á Alcaldes de Sn José, Monterey y Comte. de Sn Franco.' [= 1834, June 21, Monterey. Secretary of the Diputacion to the alcaldes of San José and Monterey and the Comandante of San Francisco.] Abstract: 'Adjunta Arts aprovados por la Diputacion, con motivo de háberse opuesto varios vecinos á prohivicion hecha por Gefe Polto, sobre Caballada y ganado mostrencos.' Circulates the approved articles, and states outright that VARIOS VECINOS HAD OPPOSED the Gefe Politico's ban. Documents popular resistance producing a legislative response.

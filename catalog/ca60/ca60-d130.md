# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n250** · Sav p. 251 / 249 · tomo p. pp 500 - 501. · 1834-06-19 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n246-n270. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Junio 19 = Mont. / C. A. Carrillo. (no dice á quien)' [= 1834, June 19, Monterey. C. A. Carrillo (does not say to whom).] Abstract: 'Dice: Haber aprovado la Diputacion, dictamen sobre no permitirse corridas de ganado, y Caballada mostrencal.' The Diputacion formally approves the committee report banning mostrenco round-ups. The legislative act behind the ban the vecinos then petition against.

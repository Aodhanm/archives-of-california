# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n189** · Sav p. n189 stamp 190 / ink 188 · tomo p. pp 220 = 21 · 1834-08-01 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Agosto 1º = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, August 1, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage gives only a subject line, no body: 'Sobre concesion de varios terrenos' [on the concession of various lands]. Two original pages. No grantee or tract is named on the leaf, so the individual grants of this session are recoverable only from the destroyed original.

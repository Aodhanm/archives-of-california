# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n189** · Sav p. n189 stamp 190 / ink 188 · tomo p. pp 217 = 20 · 1834-07-31 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n151-n189. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834 = Julio 31 = Mont. / Diputacion Territorial' [= 1834, July 31, Monterey. Territorial Diputación.] Savage abstract: 'Dice: Aprovada la acta anterior. Se puso á discucion dictamen de obras públicas sobre construccion de Casas consistoriales de dicha Capital. Aprovado.' [the dictamen of the public-works commission on the construction of the Casas Consistoriales of the said Capital (Monterey) was put to discussion and approved]. The approval of the Monterey town-hall building project floated the day before.

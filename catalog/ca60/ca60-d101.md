# SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band

**C-A 60 (Legislative Records), scan n232** · Sav p. corner stamp 233 / ink 231 · tomo p. p p 419. = 22. · 1834-03-22 · Savage abstract · 

SESSION ACTA catalogued by the ca60 DECOMPOSITION 2026-08-27: it had no record of its own, lying inside an umbrella band that spanned n220-n245. Heading verbatim: '+ 1834. = Marzo 22 = Mont. / J. M. Maldonado' [= 1834, March 22, Monterey. José María Maldonado.] 'Dictamen sobre obligaciones de portero.' The committee report on the doorkeeper's duties, four original pages, forwarded by Figueroa on 9 May. The month reads Marzo, not Mayo, at magnification.

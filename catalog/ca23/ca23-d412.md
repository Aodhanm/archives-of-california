# 'Noticia haber arribado el 22 Lancha 'S.ta Lucia' con su esposa y niño.' — the lancha Santa Lucía arrived on the 22d wit

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n178** · Sav p. 175 · tomo p. p.371-2 · 1783-05-26 · Savage abstract · 

'Noticia haber arribado el 22 Lancha 'S.ta Lucia' con su esposa y niño.' — the lancha Santa Lucía arrived on the 22d with his wife and child (I infer = Doña Eulalia de Callis and son, joining Fages at Loreto; Savage does not name her).

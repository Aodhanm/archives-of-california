# 'Envío de un buque' — requests a ship to bring grain from the Sonora coast, not having received remesas from México nor 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n119** · Sav p. 118/119 · tomo p. p.233-234 · 1783-07-03 · Savage abstract · 

'Envío de un buque' — requests a ship to bring grain from the Sonora coast, not having received remesas from México nor from that department (San Blas); only the Sta Lucía available; warns Cap. Joaquín Cañete of that Presº that when Province ships need repair or crew, apply directly to him (Trillo).

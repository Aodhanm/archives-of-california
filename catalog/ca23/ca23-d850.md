# (sin fecha) 'Reparto de terrenos = Exhortación al trabajo' [companion writes 'Repartode'] — received list of individuals

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n323** · Sav p. 319-320 · tomo p. p.54-57 · 1796-01 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha) 'Reparto de terrenos = Exhortación al trabajo' [companion writes 'Repartode'] — received list of individuals at LA who have/lack land; distribute to all who lack & are heads of family, give royal possession & documents free; (cont. n324) this suffices without forcing them to sow; permit no idleness, all must work; after distributing tell each how much land remains for new settlers & if marked, per Reglamento, the parts belonging to Propios & what their produce is destined for. Spans n323-n324.

# 'Remision de cuenta de pólvora &c' — warns among matters that it suits he finish before year-end the títulos de posesion

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n114** · Sav p. 112 · tomo p. p.221-222 · 1783-06-21 · Savage abstract · 

'Remision de cuenta de pólvora &c' — warns among matters that it suits he finish before year-end the títulos de posesion to the residents [San José land titles], & that every 6 months he must remit a powder-&-munitions account per plaza, valuing at 2 rls each per Reglamento de Presidios.

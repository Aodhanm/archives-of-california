# 'Llegada de su famᵃ' (spans n109–n110) — gives part of the arrival at Loreto of his wife & child; early July will move t

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n109** · Sav p. 108 · tomo p. p.213-214 · 1783-06-05 · Savage abstract · 

'Llegada de su famᵃ' (spans n109–n110) — gives part of the arrival at Loreto of his wife & child; early July will move to Monterey embarking there in the balsa of Sn Luis, saving of this way some 200 leagues of very heavy road.

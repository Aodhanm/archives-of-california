# Donativo — follow Viceroy's & his example, cede a year's salary to the King, esp. the Sgt. (Gayecochea) to encourage Ort

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n309** · Sav p. 306 · tomo p. p.37 · 1795-10-19 · Savage abstract · 

Donativo — follow Viceroy's & his example, cede a year's salary to the King, esp. the Sgt. (Gayecochea) to encourage Ortega & accommodated soldiers to make donativos; if they offer beasts/cattle admit them & sell for the troop.

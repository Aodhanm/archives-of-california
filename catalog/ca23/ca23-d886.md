# 'Reparto de tierras' — bear in mind the lands needed for the new foundations; these must be preferred as the legitimate 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n339** · Sav p. 334-335 · tomo p. p.70-72 · 1796-04-18 · Savage abstract · 

'Reparto de tierras' — bear in mind the lands needed for the new foundations; these must be preferred as the legitimate owners' domain of everything & the territory; (cont. n340) if he can't get [settlers] to consent to abandon the pueblo, bring in some of their sons to keep them company — this is the 5th; how permit taking livestock to provision presidio, missions etc.? — the company that is [there] with much care. Spans n339-n340.

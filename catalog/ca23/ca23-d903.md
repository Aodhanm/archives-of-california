# 'Concesion p.[ara] criar ganado' — grant to Pico[?] the livestock at the place he requests (of those not needed for foun

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n347** · Sav p. 342 · tomo p. p.80-81 · 1796-06-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Concesion p.[ara] criar ganado' — grant to Pico[?] the livestock at the place he requests (of those not needed for foundation), without prejudice to the gov't's provisions, on condition he raise 'doble número' of ganado lanar (double the sheep) to cattle; & admit Pico in his company, who also raises livestock.

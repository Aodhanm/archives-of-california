# penal bando (cont.): (5) if malefactors reach 6 at once, despatch to presidio; (6) father-ministers using these Indians 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n426** · Sav p. 418 · tomo p. p.284–285 · 1795-11-01 · Savage abstract · 

penal bando (cont.): (5) if malefactors reach 6 at once, despatch to presidio; (6) father-ministers using these Indians in escorts supply the ration; (8) same rules for gentiles but maintained by the gratification fund; (9) obedience will contain the disorder; (10) post the disposition in all guard corps, presidios, escorts & pueblos.

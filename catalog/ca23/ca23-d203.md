**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n341** · Sav p. 336 · tomo p. p.73–74 · 1796-05-07 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.; also sin fecha) only cavalry horses, milk cows & indispensable oxen allowed residents; May 7: 60 fanegas maize en route; ⭐'Llegada de la fragata 'Concepción'': *'...la fragata 'Concepción', Cap. Salazar, procedente de Manila, con el objeto de reponer viveres...'* frigate *Concepción* (Capt. Salazar, from Manila) arrived to replenish; aided with vegetables & about to sail; in future inquire about the navigation & the countries whence ships come⭐.

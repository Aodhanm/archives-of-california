# (sin fecha) 'Precauciones contra la fragata de guerra 'Providencia'' — VERBATIM: 'al Cap. de la marina británica Don Gui

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n348** · Sav p. 343 · tomo p. p.81-82 · 1796 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha) 'Precauciones contra la fragata de guerra 'Providencia'' — VERBATIM: 'al Cap. de la marina británica Don Guillermo Bright[?] Comand.te de la fragata 'Providencia' armada en guerra... no se le consienta, mucho menos que desembarque gente.' GLOSS: Borica offered the British navy captain, commander of the war-armed frigate Providencia now at Monterey, whatever aid he needs to continue his navigation; consequently if he tries to put in on those (southern) coasts on pretext of provisions etc., do NOT permit it, much less landing people; do this courteously ('acomedidamente'); ensure exact compliance, notifying the escorts so no one is ignorant of it.

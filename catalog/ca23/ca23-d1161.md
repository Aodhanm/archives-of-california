# 'Promocion de matrimonios' (doc spans n413-n414) — promote marriages by every kind of honest means so the new establishm

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n413–n414** · Sav p. 405 · tomo p. p.253-257 · 1795-04-13 · Savage abstract · 

'Promocion de matrimonios' (doc spans n413-n414) — promote marriages by every kind of honest means so the new establishments increase; aid the single soldier who marries (fund + a year's supplement on his account); aid fathers of contingents, satisfiable in a year from crops & livestock; certified copy posted in the guard corps of all missions incl. San José & Ntra Sra de Guadalupe [Branciforte]; monthly diary notice of marriages with names & sums supplied.

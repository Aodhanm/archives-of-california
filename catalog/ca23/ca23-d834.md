# 'Sobre el amancebamiento y adulterio' — when concubinage is proven, admonish the accomplices; if repeated, punish the ma

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n317** · Sav p. 313 · tomo p. p.46-47 · 1795-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre el amancebamiento y adulterio' — when concubinage is proven, admonish the accomplices; if repeated, punish the man by separating him from where his accomplice is; if married, notify his wife; if incorrigible, deposit the mancebas in honest houses at a decent trade with good example, keeping proportion to circumstances.

# (cont.; also Apr 13) drought causes seed shortage — sow all irrigable land; go in person or send alférez to move settler

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n334** · Sav p. 329 · tomo p. p.65–66 · 1796-04-07 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.; also Apr 13) drought causes seed shortage — sow all irrigable land; go in person or send alférez to move settlers; ready horses & mules for seed; ⭐Apr 13 'Aprendizage de alfarería, armería y sastrería': *'Ha llegado á la Península ya un Maestro Alfarero, y espero... otro de Sastre y un armero... se necesitan jóvenes de 13 á 16 años que se dediquen á ellos...'* a master potter has arrived, awaits tailor & armorer; need youths 13–16 to apprentice with parents'/guardians' consent.⭐

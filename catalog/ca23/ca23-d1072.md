# Warn the LA vecindario that all robbery or trafficking of cattle, though for pretext of trinkets ('juguetes'), is punish

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n389** · Sav p. 384 · tomo p. p.188-194 · 1798-10 · Savage abstract · 

Warn the LA vecindario that all robbery or trafficking of cattle, though for pretext of trinkets ('juguetes'), is punished with 6 months' irons; the merchant Gallego to cease making raffles ('rifas') & selling drink.

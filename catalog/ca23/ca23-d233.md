# (cont.; May 19, 22, 23, 30) draw on landless San José & LA residents for the new villa; buy Mission wool; ⭐May 19: Missi

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n371** · Sav p. 366 · tomo p. p.130–136 · 1797-05-02 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.; May 19, 22, 23, 30) draw on landless San José & LA residents for the new villa; buy Mission wool; ⭐May 19: Mission San Fernando to be founded midway between San Buenaventura & San Gabriel; wool-cloth factory; ⭐May 23: 'Llegada de la fragata 'Princesa' á la Purísima con mucha gente á bordo'; ⭐May 30: Princesa at Sta Bárbara, many crew with scurvy & contagious diseases; carries the memorias.

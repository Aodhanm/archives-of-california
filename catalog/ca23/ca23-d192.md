# (sin fecha ×3) send libranzas favoring La Purísima & Sta Bárbara; ⭐danger the comdte's honor ran at the court-martial ov

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n330** · Sav p. 325 · tomo p. p.61–62 · 1796 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha ×3) send libranzas favoring La Purísima & Sta Bárbara; ⭐danger the comdte's honor ran at the court-martial over imprisoned Indians Bonifacio & Ma.

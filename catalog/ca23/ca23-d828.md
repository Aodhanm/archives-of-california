# 'Sobre una escaramuza con los gentiles' — VERBATIM: 'yendo los indios gentiles á vengar[se] á la Mision de [San?] Fern.[

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n313** · Sav p. 310-311 · tomo p. p.43-45 · 1795-11-02 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre una escaramuza con los gentiles' — VERBATIM: 'yendo los indios gentiles á vengar[se] á la Mision de [San?] Fern.[ando?] algunas muertes, fueron sorprendidos... la prision de dos capitanes[,] la fusion[?] de 6 ó 7 de ellos' GLOSS: avenging gentiles surprised — 2 captains captured, 6-7 of them shot/killed[?], some fugitive Christian Indians & a vaquero killed; to avoid grave consequences of this 'unruly act of the Christian Indians': bring [Domingo] to presidio to work engrillado; most vigorous diligence to seize the fugitives, esp. Salina; form clear circumstantial inquiry; (cont. n314) at Viceroy's orders form sumaria vs Indian residents & gentiles; sumaria vs Soldier Grijalva[?] Guzmán[?] for letting 2 prisoners escape — irons, aired mornings & evenings; call the offended rancheria captains & show them the justice of the Spaniards. Spans n313-n314.

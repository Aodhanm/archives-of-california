# thanks congratulation on grade of Coronel de Caballería; ⭐'Sobre el amancebamiento y adulterio' — when concubinage prove

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n317** · Sav p. 313 · tomo p. p.46–47 · 1795-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

thanks congratulation on grade of Coronel de Caballería; ⭐'Sobre el amancebamiento y adulterio' — when concubinage proven, admonish; if repeated, separate the man; if married, notify his wife; if incorrigible, deposit the mancebas in honest houses at a decent trade.

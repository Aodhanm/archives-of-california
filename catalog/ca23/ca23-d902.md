# (sin fecha) 'Sobre culpabilidad de Ávila' — return the diligencias done at Los Angeles connecting to those done there ov

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n347** · Sav p. 342 · tomo p. p.80-81 · 1796 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha) 'Sobre culpabilidad de Ávila' — return the diligencias done at Los Angeles connecting to those done there over the finding of a burial & the collar of a shirt at the paraje where Manuel Francisco Ávila disappeared; act with great prudence & sagacity; vehement indications that Antonio Ignacio Ávila[?] is the author — remit the accused to San Diego to stay in the presidio of Punta de Guijarros.

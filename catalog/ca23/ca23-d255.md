# (Jul 19; Aug 23, 26; Sep 9; Oct 18) ⭐Jul 19: though he doubts the 19 enemy vessels at sea will put in, build a fort at S

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n393** · Sav p. 388 · tomo p. p.207–213 · 1799-07-04 · Savage abstract · 

(Jul 19; Aug 23, 26; Sep 9; Oct 18) ⭐Jul 19: though he doubts the 19 enemy vessels at sea will put in, build a fort at San Fernando to safely deposit the archive, armament & church paraments; Aug 23: Habilitado gral appointment (Capt. Enciano Antonio Columna[?]); ⭐Aug 26: 'Aviso de buques enemigos'⭐; Sep 9: expel Soldier Juan Mora; ⭐Oct 18: solicit fugitive Indians of Mission San Fernando humanely — the end is to reduce & subject, not destroy the gentiles.

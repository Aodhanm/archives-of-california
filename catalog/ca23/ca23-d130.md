# 'Sobre guardias del Inspector Sobr' — asks Romeu use influence with his wife to make the journey to the Province (transp

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n72** · Sav p. 73 · tomo p. p.130-131 · 1782-12-21 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre guardias del Inspector Sobr' — asks Romeu use influence with his wife to make the journey to the Province (transport charged to Neve); disputes over 'El Reglamento' made by his predecessor (approved by King) since it supposes in some cases conflict betw Govt & Inspection; (cont. n73, orig p.130–31) embarrassed in his projects by importunate demands, avoiding discord w/ Ayudᵗᵉ Dn Nicolás Soler; asks for a written declaration of the faculties belonging to both employments.

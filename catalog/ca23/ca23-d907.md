# 'Sobre compra de lana' — while livestock increases at that foundation & of San Juan, need to buy wool for the loom estab

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n350** · Sav p. 345 · tomo p. p.83-84 · 1796-07-04 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre compra de lana' — while livestock increases at that foundation & of San Juan, need to buy wool for the loom established at that presidio (Monterey); buy 200 arrobas even at 4 r. more per arroba than the Arancel sets; remit soon.

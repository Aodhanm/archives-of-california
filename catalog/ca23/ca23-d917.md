# 'Sobre carneros y lana' — content with the purchase of sheep & wool, remit at first opportunity; wants the wool for a we

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n354** · Sav p. 349 · tomo p. p.88-89 · 1796-08-06 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre carneros y lana' — content with the purchase of sheep & wool, remit at first opportunity; wants the wool for a weaver (tejedor) to propagate that trade in the Peninsula & supply the public & royal service at accommodating prices, so nothing burdens the treasury.

# Though the Superior Gov't can't decide on supplements to soldiers, Borica will make the greatest efforts with the Vicero

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n387** · Sav p. 382 · tomo p. p.183-184 · 1798-08-16 · Savage abstract · 

Though the Superior Gov't can't decide on supplements to soldiers, Borica will make the greatest efforts with the Viceroy for the interested habilitados & the troops who must suffer the charge, given the good faith of the supplements & the troop's innocence.

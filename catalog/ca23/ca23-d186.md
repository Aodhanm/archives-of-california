# (also sin fecha) ⭐Huaynambé[?] & Bernarda accomplices in murder of gardener Márcos — remit to Real Audiencia of Mexico; 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n323** · Sav p. 319 · tomo p. p.54–55 · 1796-01-08 · Savage abstract · 

(also sin fecha) ⭐Huaynambé[?] & Bernarda accomplices in murder of gardener Márcos — remit to Real Audiencia of Mexico; 'Que asegure y cuide á los asesinos de Márcos' — the 3 Indians & the India who cut Márcos's throat: jail in irons, inspect prison daily, abundant ration & cover, until the audiencia sentences; 'Reparto de terrenos = Exhortación al trabajo' — distribute LA land to all landless heads of family, royal possession & documents free.

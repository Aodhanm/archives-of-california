# 'Sobre levantar la cuarentena' (reprimand, doc spans n383-n385): received the document signed by all presidio officers, 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n383** · Sav p. 378 · tomo p. p.172-176 · 1798-05-28 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre levantar la cuarentena' (reprimand, doc spans n383-n385): received the document signed by all presidio officers, Alférez Rodríguez, the Concepción's officers, first pilot & the 8 clerics; reasons for lifting the Viceroy-ordered quarantine (circular 28 Feb) NOT sufficient; the south wind no equal to smallpox's ravages; should not have permitted land-ship communication nor mission Indians assisting the fathers; if smallpox recurs no signatures will free him from just charges; the King informed will resolve; clerics may go to the missions, memorias to warehouses; observe the Viceroy's orders to the letter.

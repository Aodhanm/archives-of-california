# 'Sobre no permitir cabalgar á indios' — replies to Lasuén's oficio of 22nd: the word 'excso [exceso]' in the prior is no

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n64** · Sav p. 65 · tomo p. p.110-113 · 1787-08-23 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre no permitir cabalgar á indios' — replies to Lasuén's oficio of 22nd: the word 'excso [exceso]' in the prior is not absolute but controls only re Mission de Sn Luis, where such circumstance is noted; could even wholly forbid Indians riding as practiced in Sonora missions; (cont. n65, orig p.111–13) asks the declared word not be made mysterious.

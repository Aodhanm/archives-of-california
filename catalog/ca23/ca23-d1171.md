# 'No admision de mercancías extrangeras' (doc continues n418): 'debe entenderse de efectos y manufactura precisamente ame

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n417** · Sav p. 409 · tomo p. p.263-268 · 1795-06-08 · Savage abstract · 

'No admision de mercancías extrangeras' (doc continues n418): 'debe entenderse de efectos y manufactura precisamente americana y española; que las [mercancías] extrangeras han de caer en la pena de comiso'. GLOSS: San Blas-Peninsula commerce limited to American & Spanish goods; foreign goods confiscated with legal rigor; only Spain's registers or the habilitado gral's memorias may carry foreign effects; California's commerce licit; publish by bando & comply.

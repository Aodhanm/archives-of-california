# 'Llegada de la fragata 'Concepción'' (doc spans n381-n383): 'Queda entendido de la llegada de la fragata 'Concepción' ap

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n381–n383** · Sav p. 376 · tomo p. p.168-171 · 1798-05-12 · Savage abstract · 

'Llegada de la fragata 'Concepción'' (doc spans n381-n383): 'Queda entendido de la llegada de la fragata 'Concepción' apestada de viruela.' GLOSS: smallpox-ship quarantine — troop detail on the beach to bar all land-ship contact; papers/goods fumigated with sulphur on the beach; barracks built downwind of presidio/mission/ranchería under Alférez Manuel Rodríguez; officers, clerics & passengers subsisted through the 40-day quarantine; baggage aired & fumigated; then free to destinations; bear in mind the advantages of inoculation.

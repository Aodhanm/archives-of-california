# 'Sobre el establec.[imiento] de la escuela' — school establishment (heading n309, content n310): all soldiers & resident

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n309** · Sav p. 306-307 · tomo p. p.37-39 · 1795-10-19 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre el establec.[imiento] de la escuela' — school establishment (heading n309, content n310): all soldiers & residents contribute to the schoolmaster's subsistence, 125-130 p. annual (married soldiers 1 p., unmarried 1/2 p., rest from residents); read at fiesta so pueblo & company see the Gov't's intent; teach Christian doctrine 'lo primero' & reading & writing; boys attend mornings, help parents afternoons; primers & paper at pupils'/parents' cost then via habilitado; then re pension. Spans n309-n310.

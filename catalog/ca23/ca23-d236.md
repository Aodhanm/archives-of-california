# (Oct 14–20, Nov 8, 11, Dic 7) abusos de la memoria; ⭐Oct 15: take a roster of the young bachelors; war circumstances & t

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n374** · Sav p. 369 · tomo p. p.147–150 · 1797-10-11 · Savage abstract · 

(Oct 14–20, Nov 8, 11, Dic 7) abusos de la memoria; ⭐Oct 15: take a roster of the young bachelors; war circumstances & the two coastal frigates retiring to San Blas oblige recourse to the youth (militia levy)⭐; ⭐Dic 7 (Gayecochea) re Ortega debt: 'Ortega es un ladron, Arriola lo mismo: no los pierda V. de vista para contenerlos con rigor. Sea V. sostenido en todo lo fuerte.' watch Ortega & sons pay & not undersell — 'Ortega is a thief'⭐.

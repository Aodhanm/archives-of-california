# 'Precauciones para evitar el hambre por la falta de aguas' (measures to avert famine from water shortage); (cont. n334) 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n333** · Sav p. 328-329 · tomo p. p.64-66 · 1796-04-07 · Savage abstract · 

'Precauciones para evitar el hambre por la falta de aguas' (measures to avert famine from water shortage); (cont. n334) drought of past years & this present one cause great seed shortage — sow all irrigable land & huertas; if needed go in person or send the alférez to move settlers at various points/rancherias; ready horses & mules to carry seed where needed. Spans n333-n334.

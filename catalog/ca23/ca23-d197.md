# (cont.) apprentices Christianly educated, maintained & clothed; don't fix on exact age for potter & armorer, any for tai

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n335** · Sav p. 330 · tomo p. p.66–67 · 1796-04-15 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.) apprentices Christianly educated, maintained & clothed; don't fix on exact age for potter & armorer, any for tailor; tailor & armorer to stay at that presidio & San Francisco, potter until finishing the mission Indians' instruction then northern missions; Apr 15: transfer indebted soldiers to other companies.

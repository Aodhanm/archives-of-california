# 'Sobre las obligaciones de los Misioneros' — 9-point questionnaire for the Viceroy on the fathers' chaplain service: Mas

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n403** · Sav p. 398 · tomo p. p.234-235 · 1794-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre las obligaciones de los Misioneros' — 9-point questionnaire for the Viceroy on the fathers' chaplain service: Mass at the presidio every feast day; feast days lost when creeks swell; doctrine on Sundays; baptize/marry/bury; come when called for confession; confess year-round; Easter compliance at mission or presidio; mission-presidio distance; grave defects in spiritual assistance noted.

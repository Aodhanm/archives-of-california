# smallpox quarantine (cont.): officers, clerics & lay-passengers aided during the 40-day quarantine, housed to avoid cont

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n382** · Sav p. 377 · tomo p. p.169–171 · 1798-05-12 · Savage abstract · 

smallpox quarantine (cont.): officers, clerics & lay-passengers aided during the 40-day quarantine, housed to avoid contagion, with a guard; only individuals handle disembarked baggage/memorias; provide carretas & oxen; Alférez Rodríguez[?] to ventilate & fumigate all effects with sulphur; after 40 days & no novelty they may proceed; the precautions will free the Province of the scourge, bearing in mind the advantages of vaccination ('inoculación')⭐.

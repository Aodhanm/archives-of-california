# (also sin fecha) ⭐'Concesion p. criar ganado' — grant Pico[?] the livestock at the place he requests on condition he rai

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n347** · Sav p. 342 · tomo p. p.80–81 · 1796-06-09 · Savage abstract · 

(also sin fecha) ⭐'Concesion p. criar ganado' — grant Pico[?] the livestock at the place he requests on condition he raise double the sheep to cattle, & admit Pico in his company; ⭐'Sobre culpabilidad de Ávila' — return the LA diligencias re a burial & shirt-collar found where Manuel Francisco Ávila disappeared; act with prudence, vehement indications that Antonio Ignacio Ávila[?] is the author — remit accused to San Diego (Punta de Guijarros).

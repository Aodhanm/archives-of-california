# (Mar 25; Apr 12, 18; May 17, 21, 31; Jun 5) courteously require fathers remove the corral prejudicing the pueblo; donati

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n391** · Sav p. 386 · tomo p. p.199–204 · 1799-03-06 · Savage abstract · 

(Mar 25; Apr 12, 18; May 17, 21, 31; Jun 5) courteously require fathers remove the corral prejudicing the pueblo; donativos for the King (350 p.); fathers' letter-porterage franchise stems from a 1781–82 order; ⭐May 21 'Montepío de Ortega'; ⭐Jun 5: the Indian Silverio, accomplice of Rosa in Rebeca's murder, sentenced to 8 years' hard labor in irons; Rosa to be deported⭐.

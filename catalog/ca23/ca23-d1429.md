**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n489** · Sav p. 204 · tomo p. p.487 · 1797-01-07 · Savage abstract · 

(Satisfaccion al padre Magín; doc spans n489–n490) 'Que luego que Vds esta se trasladarán á la mision de Sta Clara y presentandose á su ministro el R.P. Fray Magín Catalá le darán satisfaccion...' — Moraga & Vallejo to go to Sta Clara and give Fr. Magín Catalá satisfaction for the discourteous manner used toward him in the measuring/cultivation of lands adjoining the mission, promising future respect and reporting compliance.

# 'No querer pagar franqueo de cartas P. Serra' — sends note from P. Serra manifesting reasons not to pay postage; (cont. 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n80** · Sav p. 81 · tomo p. p.148 · 1783-01-11 · Savage abstract · 

'No querer pagar franqueo de cartas P. Serra' — sends note from P. Serra manifesting reasons not to pay postage; (cont. n81, orig p.148) the postage of correspondence in that Escolta, saying the letters he sends be kept if not franked, against superior orders of which he has copies, & that ecclesiastics are not exempt from postage.

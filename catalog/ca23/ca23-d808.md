'Distribución de efectos de la Memoria' — from gratification-fund attentions formed list of goods from the memoria to sell freely at port, prices expressed; sales before alférez & sargento, product of 579 p. added to gratification fund; rest supplied to company at cost + merma; list posted in guard corps.

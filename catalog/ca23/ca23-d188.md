# (also 1795 Feb 1) return libranza, diezmo product of 95 = 520 p. 4 r.; Cosío ramo = 86 p.; ⭐'Sobre los bienes de Álvarez

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n325** · Sav p. 321 · tomo p. p.57 · 1796-02-01 · Savage abstract · 

(also 1795 Feb 1) return libranza, diezmo product of 95 = 520 p. 4 r.; Cosío ramo = 86 p.; ⭐'Sobre los bienes de Álvarez' — keep goods of the deceased Juan Álvarez for his legitimate heir of the 1st marriage; ⭐⭐'Sobre la expedicion á Nuevo México' (to Comdte San Buenaventura): pleased with the news on carrying out the New Mexico expedition; before formalizing wishes to communicate with that Province's governor [cont. n327]. (n326 = retake/duplicate of Savage 321.)

# 'Sobre secuestro de los bienes de Ortega' — keep in practice the orders Lt.-Col. Arrillaga communicated on the embargo o

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n349** · Sav p. 344 · tomo p. p.82-83 · 1796-06-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre secuestro de los bienes de Ortega' — keep in practice the orders Lt.-Col. Arrillaga communicated on the embargo of goods & sale of the frigates & wine etc. to satisfy the debt of 2894 p. 5 r. in which Capt. José Ma. de Ortega left the company of Loreto in his time as habilitado; informing of the goods conceded him & the sums his sons will earn to satisfy for their father; take care those goods not stray, to the prejudice of the creditors.

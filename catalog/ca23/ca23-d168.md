# 'Aprehensión de Ávila' — offer rewards to Indians to seize fugitive Ávila, said to be among fugitive gentiles ('tulares 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n305** · Sav p. 302 · tomo p. p.33 · 1795-10-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Aprehensión de Ávila' — offer rewards to Indians to seize fugitive Ávila, said to be among fugitive gentiles ('tulares huyendo') with Christians from San Buenaventura; ⭐re frigate 'Aránzazu': habilitado Careaga to send the flags the Viceroy ordered for the Peninsula, Aránzazu to carry them; on the 22nd the Aránzazu anchored & tested cannon fire.

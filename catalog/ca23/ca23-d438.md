# Jan 22: Agustin Narvaez married Bernardo Rosales(?); the settlers may marry when they wish p.816; ⭐Jan 31 (VERBATIM): 'V

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n576** · Sav p. 288 · tomo p. pp.816–818 · 1799-01-22 · Savage abstract · 

Jan 22: Agustin Narvaez married Bernardo Rosales(?); the settlers may marry when they wish p.816; ⭐Jan 31 (VERBATIM): 'Virey manda pagar $187 3 Rls suplidos al Comdte de la fragata amª [americana] 'Nutka' Boston en 1796' p.817 — Savage's reading 'Nutka' is almost certainly a miscopy of the American ship *Otter* (out of Boston, Capt. Ebenezer Dorr, Oct 1796); FLAG uncertain; Feb 4: received diary (Branciforte) p.817; Feb 4: his honor & advancement require he stay there p.817-18 (Moraga).

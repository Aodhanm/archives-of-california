# 'Derechos de Alcabala' (spans n108–n109; repeated on overlapping scan n110): warns to collect alcabala dues with entire 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n108–n109** · Sav p. 107 · tomo p. p.212-213 · 1783-05-30 · Savage abstract · 

'Derechos de Alcabala' (spans n108–n109; repeated on overlapping scan n110): warns to collect alcabala dues with entire subjection to orders, since only Misión de Sn Ignacio enjoys the privilege of not paying them for 4 years, granted by superior order 24 Enero 1802 [sic — likely 1782].

# (cont.; also Jun 10 ×2) maize & bean should be abundant; comisionado to examine on transit how Indians work & live; no m

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n294** · Sav p. 292 · tomo p. p.19–21 · 1795 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.; also Jun 10 ×2) maize & bean should be abundant; comisionado to examine on transit how Indians work & live; no maize at San Gabriel & San Buenaventura, some at San Juan — bad crop year; release Soldier Macedonio Romana after 2 months' arrest; charge troop for cattle consumed & strayed reses.

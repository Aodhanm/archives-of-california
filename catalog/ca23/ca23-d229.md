# (Dic 6, 11, 29; 1797 Jan 2) Expediente de situados; Ortega received voluntarily on his father's account; ⭐Dic 11: Vicero

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n367** · Sav p. 362 · tomo p. p.107–110; 111 · 1796-11-30 · Savage abstract · 

(Dic 6, 11, 29; 1797 Jan 2) Expediente de situados; Ortega received voluntarily on his father's account; ⭐Dic 11: Viceroy approved the means to convert Indians — give the Missionaries all aid; Dic 29 (2 docs): settlers to raise less cattle, ranch-dwellers to live in pueblo; Ortega & sons obliged to pay the debt, San Francisco credited 60 p.; Jan 2 (Borrador): recibo de estractos gratis.

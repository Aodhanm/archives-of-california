# 'Sobre tranquilidad pública' — Barreras[?] let 2 gentile Indians escape who went about inviting the Christians to fight 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n314** · Sav p. 311 · tomo p. p.43-45 · 1795-11-02 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre tranquilidad pública' — Barreras[?] let 2 gentile Indians escape who went about inviting the Christians to fight at Arnael[?]; put Barreras in irons 2 months, aired twice daily; avoid all effusion of blood among the gentiles & esp. don't involve the Christians.

# (sin fecha) 'Castigo y Corrección de indios' — approves the measures to punish the Indians 'Serranos' of the ranchería d

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n336–n337** · Sav p. 331-332 · tomo p. p.68-69 · 1796-04 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha) 'Castigo y Corrección de indios' — approves the measures to punish the Indians 'Serranos' of the ranchería de Napalayegua[?]; (cont. n337) glad that on that occasion the Indians of that ranchería could receive baptism, as well as those of Matilha & Somes; but still don't reduce them — many inconveniences in not taking them to live at the missions. Spans n336-n337.

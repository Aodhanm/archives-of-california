# Márcos-murder justice — Huaynambé[?] & Bernarda accomplices in the murder of the gardener Márcos: remit them to the Real

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n323** · Sav p. 319 · tomo p. p.54-55 · 1796-01-08 · Savage abstract · 

Márcos-murder justice — Huaynambé[?] & Bernarda accomplices in the murder of the gardener Márcos: remit them to the Real Audiencia of Mexico; 'Que asegure y cuide á los asesinos de Márcos' — the 3 Indians & the India who cut Márcos's throat found deserving of ordinary penalty: don't set them to public works but jail them in irons/chains, inspect the prison daily, give abundant ration & cover for the cold, until the audiencia sentences them.

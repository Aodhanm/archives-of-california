# (Oct 20, 22; Nov 30) ⭐'Reconocimiento de Calahuasa' (recon cont.); warn LA vecindario that cattle robbery/trafficking (e

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n389** · Sav p. 384 · tomo p. p.188–194 · 1798-10-19 · Savage abstract · 

(Oct 20, 22; Nov 30) ⭐'Reconocimiento de Calahuasa' (recon cont.); warn LA vecindario that cattle robbery/trafficking (even for 'juguetes') = 6 months' irons; merchant Gallego cease raffles & selling drink; send hemp & cotton seed; good they build the LA jail; ⭐Nov 30: urge LA to open the acequia; even the Alcalde plays much — enforce it.

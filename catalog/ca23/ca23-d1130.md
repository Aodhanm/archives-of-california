# VANCOUVER: 'Al Comand.te de la expedicion inglesa Jorge Vancouber y Peter Pungei[?] que se hallan en aquel puerto (Monte

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n402** · Sav p. 397 · tomo p. p.232-233 · 1794-11-12 · Savage abstract · 

VANCOUVER: 'Al Comand.te de la expedicion inglesa Jorge Vancouber y Peter Pungei[?] que se hallan en aquel puerto (Monterey) con la fragata 'Descubierta' y bergantín 'Chatham'...' GLOSS: as ordered, foreign ships sighted must be required to leave port waters, aided only in urgent necessity; Vancouver & [Puget], at Monterey with the Descubierta[sic] & Chatham, offered whatever aid; if they come to the southern ports on pretext of provisions or watering, refuse — courteously tell them to leave.

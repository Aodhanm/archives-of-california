# (sin fecha) 'Sobre colonos y empeños' — convenient the son of Santiago Pico goes to the pueblo to keep & maintain his fa

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n344** · Sav p. 339-340 · tomo p. p.76-79 · 1796-05 · Savage abstract · 

(sin fecha) 'Sobre colonos y empeños' — convenient the son of Santiago Pico goes to the pueblo to keep & maintain his father's house, lands & goods; (cont. n345) convenient settlers increase their vineyards with plots of vines & cattle — that's the true basis of an establishment, strayed cattle not so useful; a good officer must be upright in justice, condescendence & piety to one side — not indulge a soldier viciously with 100 p. he can't repay. Spans n344-n345.

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n403** · Sav p. 398 · tomo p. p.234–235 · 1794-11-28 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre las obligaciones de los Misioneros' — questionnaire on how the fathers assist troop & residents as chaplains: (1) Mass at the presidio every feast day? (2) how many feast days without Mass when rivers swell? (3) Sunday doctrine? (4) baptize/marry/bury? (5) come for confession when called? (6) confess year-round? (7) do they oblige Easter compliance at mission or presidio? (8) mission-to-presidio distance? (9) grave defects in spiritual assistance.

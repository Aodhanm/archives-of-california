# 'Aprendizage de alfarería, armería y sastrería' — VERBATIM: 'Ha llegado á la Península ya un Maestro Alfarero, y espero 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n334–n335** · Sav p. 329-330 · tomo p. p.65-67 · 1796-04-13 · Savage abstract · 

'Aprendizage de alfarería, armería y sastrería' — VERBATIM: 'Ha llegado á la Península ya un Maestro Alfarero, y espero en el buque de las memorias otro de Sastre y un armero de habilidad.' + 'se necesitan jóvenes de 13 á 16 años' GLOSS: master potter arrived; tailor & skilled armorer awaited on the supply ship; to spread these trades among the gente de razón, need youths 13-16 (sons of invalid troops & settlers, with parents'/guardians' consent) to apprentice; (cont. n335) Christianly educated, maintained & clothed while apprenticing; age flexible (2 yrs more or less), build matters for potter & armorer, any will do for tailor; tailor & armorer to stay at that presidio & San Francisco, the potter to the northern missions after instructing that mission's Indians. Spans n334-n335.

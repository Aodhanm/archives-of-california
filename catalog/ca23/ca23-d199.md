# (cont. ×3) ...ranchería de Napalayegua[?], approves; glad the Indians there & of Matilha & Somes could receive baptism, 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n337** · Sav p. 332 · tomo p. p.68–69 · 1796 · Savage abstract · 

(cont. ×3) ...ranchería de Napalayegua[?], approves; glad the Indians there & of Matilha & Somes could receive baptism, but don't reduce them yet; will send 150 fanegas maize; remit 50 of paper & 2 boxes of Puebla soap; will process the invalidez-creditors relation.

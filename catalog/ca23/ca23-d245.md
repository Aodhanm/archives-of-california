# (cont.; May 18, 28) if he prevents this plague he does the King & Province a service; May 18: remit all soap on the frig

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n383** · Sav p. 378 · tomo p. p.169–172 · 1798-05-12 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.; May 18, 28) if he prevents this plague he does the King & Province a service; May 18: remit all soap on the frigate *Concepción*; May 28 (2 docs): buy 187 novillos & vacas at 5 p.; ⭐May 28 'Sobre levantar la cuarentena' — received his office of 25 June[sic] signed by all officers, Rodríguez, the Concepción crew, pilot & 8 clerics [scolding re lifting quarantine, cont.].

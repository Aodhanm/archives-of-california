# 'Sobre modo de tratar á gentiles' — VERBATIM: 'Le recomienda qe cuide no experimenten el mas leve daño los gentiles inme

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n91** · Sav p. 92 · tomo p. p.177-178 · 1783-03-05 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre modo de tratar á gentiles' — VERBATIM: 'Le recomienda qe cuide no experimenten el mas leve daño los gentiles inmediatos al Presº de su mando, qe conservarlos...' [Recommends he take care the gentile Indians near his presidio suffer not the least harm; to conserve them] (cont. n92, orig p.177–8) in peace, & re the non-celebration of Masses await the superior's order, observe the greatest indifference w/ the friars w/o admitting conversation on the matter. Begins n91 (leaf orig p.172–177), continues n92.

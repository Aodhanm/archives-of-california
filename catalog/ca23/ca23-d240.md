# 'Pedido de un buque á Sn Blas' (spans n120–n121) — Comandᵗᵉ Genl provided 18 Octe '82 for ship-repair; occur to the Coma

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n120–n121** · Sav p. 118/119 · tomo p. p.234-235 · 1783-07-03 · Savage abstract · 

'Pedido de un buque á Sn Blas' (spans n120–n121) — Comandᵗᵉ Genl provided 18 Octe '82 for ship-repair; occur to the Comandᵗᵉ de Sn Blas; hence asked a ship, the Balandra 'El Pilar' being useless, to bring grain from Sonora coast to the Province. (Survey row #133 reads the sloop's name as 'El Filer' — companion reads 'El Pilar'.)

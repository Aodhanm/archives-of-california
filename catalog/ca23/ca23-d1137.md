# Every month a sergeant or corporal review the escorts, correcting light faults & reporting grave ones, passing revista; 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n405** · Sav p. 400 · tomo p. p.237-240 · 1794-12-11 · Savage abstract · 

Every month a sergeant or corporal review the escorts, correcting light faults & reporting grave ones, passing revista; each escort to have 2 escopetas, 40 flints (piedras de chispa) & 60 cartridges per man of infantry.

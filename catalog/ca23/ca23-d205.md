# (cont.) Nieto keeps his cultivated lands, rest reverts to San Gabriel Mission; pass certified copy to the Comdte of Sta 

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. 338 · tomo p. p.75 · 1796-05-17 · Savage abstract · 

(cont.) Nieto keeps his cultivated lands, rest reverts to San Gabriel Mission; pass certified copy to the Comdte of Sta Bárbara for the fathers & Nieto; ⭐May 17 'Compra de ganado lanar' — buy 400–500 sheep with rams though a bit dear, pasture until San Diego reports its purchase, then send the flock to Monterey.

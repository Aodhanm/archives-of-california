# 'Compra de ganado lanar' — buy 400-500 sheep (borregas) with their rams, though paid a bit dear, keeping & pasturing unt

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n343** · Sav p. 338 · tomo p. p.75 · 1796-05-17 · Savage abstract · 

'Compra de ganado lanar' — buy 400-500 sheep (borregas) with their rams, though paid a bit dear, keeping & pasturing until San Diego comdte reports having made another purchase, then remit & the flock sent to that presidio (Monterey); undertake diligently, verify the purchase, pay promptly.

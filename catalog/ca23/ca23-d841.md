# 'Sobre expedicion á N.[uev]o México' — Moqui/New Mexico expedition inquiry. VERBATIM: 'quienes conocen los terrenos inte

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n320** · Sav p. 316 · tomo p. p.50-51 · 1795-12-14 · Savage abstract · 

'Sobre expedicion á N.[uev]o México' — Moqui/New Mexico expedition inquiry. VERBATIM: 'quienes conocen los terrenos intermedios desde la mision de S.[a]n Gabriel hasta los pueblos del Moqui del Nuevo México, viage que hizo el padre Garcés' GLOSS: inquire among troop, settlers & Indians who know the terrain from Mission San Gabriel to the Moqui pueblos of New Mexico — the route Fr. Garcés made — question minutely on terrain, climate, great rivers, tribes & pueblos, their character, distances; inform of means for a formal expedition, or send Indians who for the interest of a gift would carry a letter to that Province's Governor & bring back the reply.

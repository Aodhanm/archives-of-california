# (May 2, 8, 12) delivery of cattle to Verdugo[?]; reduction of gentiles to San Luis; ⭐death of Martín Reyes of Los Angele

**C-A 23 (Provincial Records), scan n381** · Sav p. 376 · tomo p. p.168–169 · 1798-05-01 · Savage abstract · 

(May 2, 8, 12) delivery of cattle to Verdugo[?]; reduction of gentiles to San Luis; ⭐death of Martín Reyes of Los Angeles; ⭐⭐May 12 'Llegada de la fragata 'Concepción'' — smallpox-ship quarantine: *'Queda entendido de la llegada de la fragata 'Concepción' apestada de viruela...'* frigate *Concepción* plague-stricken with smallpox; (1) prevent all land–ship contact, troop on the beach; (2) fumigate papers/goods with sulphur; (3) build barracks near water, winds away from presidio/mission, to lodge Alférez Don Manuel Rodríguez [cont. n382]⭐⭐.

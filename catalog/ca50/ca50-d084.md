# 'San Fernando.' [San Fernando, i.e. San Fernando Velicata; Savage gives no surname]. The church 33 varas long and 5 1/2 

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n109** · Sav p. 110 / 108 · tomo p. p.643-46 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'San Fernando.' [San Fernando, i.e. San Fernando Velicata; Savage gives no surname]. The church 33 varas long and 5 1/2 wide; it is of adobes and jacal. Sacred vessels and ornaments scanty. The fathers' house is spacious, of adobes and jacal. Original page span read as 643-46: the closing two digits are blotted and written over, and 46 is the reading consistent with the next item opening at 647.

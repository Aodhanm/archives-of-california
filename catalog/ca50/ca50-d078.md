# 'Comondu.' [Comondu]. The church is of three naves, 30 varas long and 13 wide, entirely flagged with dressed stone, vaul

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n107** · Sav p. 108 / 106 · tomo p. p.612-17 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Comondu.' [Comondu]. The church is of three naves, 30 varas long and 13 wide, entirely flagged with dressed stone, vaulted roof, walls of stone, and choir, sacristy and baptistery of the same. Has 3 altars, 25 paintings and 6 images. Rich sacred vessels, jewels of silver, ornaments of tissue and other cloths. The fathers' house is very spacious, of lime and stone, with furniture, utensils, offices, storerooms etc., and a library of 126 volumes.

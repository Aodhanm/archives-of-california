# 'Sto Tomas de Aquino.' [Santo Tomas de Aquino]. Church of adobe with roof of petates and jacal, 12 varas long and 5 wide

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n110** · Sav p. 111 / 109 · tomo p. p.662-67 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Sto Tomas de Aquino.' [Santo Tomas de Aquino]. Church of adobe with roof of petates and jacal, 12 varas long and 5 wide. The fathers' house also of adobes. Ornaments and jewels scanty. The smallest church fabric recorded in the entire 1793 series (12 x 5 varas), the northern terminus of the Dominican chain as inventoried here.

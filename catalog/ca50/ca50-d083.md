# 'Sn Francisco Borja.' [San Francisco de Borja]. Church and sacristy of adobes and tule; the first is 32 varas long and 7

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n109** · Sav p. 110 / 108 · tomo p. p.639-42 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Sn Francisco Borja.' [San Francisco de Borja]. Church and sacristy of adobes and tule; the first is 32 varas long and 7 wide. Three altars, 20 pictures. The baptistery of equal material. The fathers' house is of lime and stone. There are various other offices and habitations.

# 'San Ignacio' [San Ignacio]. The church is of one nave with its transept, 44 varas long and 7 1/2 wide; paved floor, vau

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n108-n109** · Sav p. 109 / 107 to 110 / 108 · tomo p. p.630-35 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'San Ignacio' [San Ignacio]. The church is of one nave with its transept, 44 varas long and 7 1/2 wide; paved floor, vaulted roof, walls of lime and stone, likewise the 2 sacristies, baptistery and chapel, as also the choir. Four altars, 47 canvases with gilt frames, fourteen sculpted images, 17 canvases in the sacristy. Sacred vessels, jewels and vestments rich and abundant, and likewise ornaments and clothing. Fathers' houses: 2 halls and 2 bedchambers of lime and stone with vaults, 7 offices and other rooms for the lodging of travellers, widows, orphans etc. Livestock and harvests; 800 fig trees, 50 date palms, 150 pomegranates. The richest of the northern inventories in this run.

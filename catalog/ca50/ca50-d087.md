# 'Sn Vicente Ferrer.' [San Vicente Ferrer]. The church is of adobes and roof of tule; it is 22 varas long and 6 3/4 wide.

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n110** · Sav p. 111 / 109 · tomo p. p.658-61 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Sn Vicente Ferrer.' [San Vicente Ferrer]. The church is of adobes and roof of tule; it is 22 varas long and 6 3/4 wide. Few sacred vessels and jewels. This is the mission whose alferez Ildefonso Bernal reports to Borica at n177 (23 Sept 1795) and whose 1794 report appears at n176.

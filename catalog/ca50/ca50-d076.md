# 'Mision de Todos Santos.' [Mission of Todos Santos]. Church 24 varas long by nine wide, with an octagonal chapel of Na S

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n106** · Sav p. 107 / 105 · tomo p. p.587-97 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Mision de Todos Santos.' [Mission of Todos Santos]. Church 24 varas long by nine wide, with an octagonal chapel of Na Sra del Rosario finished in a vault, all of adobes and timber; the sacristy at one side. Has jewels, ornaments and vestments fine, of gold and silver and fine stones; diverse images with rich clothing. The fathers' houses are of adobes with mortar roofs and stone corridors, consisting of diverse rooms. There is a forge; one cane mill, formerly two, of wood; the mill house flat-roofed and of stone; five kettles and 2 stills, and sugar, panocha and aguardiente are made. Savage notes the abstract is followed in the original by an account of the mission's rancho and livestock and of its tillage, stores and effects.

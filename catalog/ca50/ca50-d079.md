# 'La Purisima.' [La Purisima]. The church is 25 varas long and 6 1/2 wide, flagged, with jacal roof and walls of adobes, 

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n107-n108** · Sav p. 108 / 106 to 109 / 107 · tomo p. p.618-23 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'La Purisima.' [La Purisima]. The church is 25 varas long and 6 1/2 wide, flagged, with jacal roof and walls of adobes, the sacristy and baptistery the same. Various jewels, scanty ornaments and some clothing. The fathers' house is of adobe, spacious; library of 200 volumes; utensils for carpentry and a forge; and an account of the livestock and agricultural property. Note this is the BAJA California Purisima, distinct from the Alta California mission of the same name whose 1792 census stands at n103.

# 'Na Sra de Guadalupe' [Our Lady of Guadalupe]. The church is 32 varas long and seven wide, its walls of adobes and roof 

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n108** · Sav p. 109 / 107 · tomo p. p.624-29 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Na Sra de Guadalupe' [Our Lady of Guadalupe]. The church is 32 varas long and seven wide, its walls of adobes and roof of azotea (flat terrace). The sacristy is of the same material. Ornaments, jewels, ornaments etc. (Savage's repetition); the fathers' house is a hall with its bedchamber of adobes and azotea roof, and five offices.

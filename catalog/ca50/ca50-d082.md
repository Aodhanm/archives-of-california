# 'Sta Gertrudis.' [Santa Gertrudis]. The church and sacristy are of adobes and tule; few ornaments and jewels; 'escases d

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n109** · Sav p. 110 / 108 · tomo p. p.636-38 · 1793 · Savage abstract · 

'Sta Gertrudis.' [Santa Gertrudis]. The church and sacristy are of adobes and tule; few ornaments and jewels; 'escases de todo' [scarcity of everything]. The shortest inventory in the whole 1793 series (3 pages) and the starkest statement of the north-frontier missions' poverty against the wealth of Loreto, Comondu and San Ignacio.

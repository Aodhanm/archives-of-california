# Heading: 1834. Dic. 31. Pueblo Sn Antonio / Manuel Crespo. : Padron general de la poblacion [= general census of the pop

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n424** · Sav p. 416 / 53 · tomo p. pp. 710=21. · 1834 Dic. 31 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 1834. Dic. 31. Pueblo Sn Antonio / Manuel Crespo. : Padron general de la poblacion [= general census of the population]. Poblacion total .... 557. almas. Savage records only the total. Distinct from the Nov. 12 1834 padron of the same pueblo abstracted on n427 (569 almas), so the two are separate documents with different totals six weeks apart.

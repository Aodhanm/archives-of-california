# Spans n426-n427 (n427 carries the pencil running head Dec 1834). Heading: 1834. Alta Cal. (Dic 31) / Informes Estadistic

**C-A 50 (State Papers, Missions), scan n426** · Sav p. 418 / 55 · tomo p. pp. 768.=72 y 789=804. · 1834 Dic 31 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n426-n427 (n427 carries the pencil running head Dec 1834). Heading: 1834. Alta Cal. (Dic 31) / Informes Estadisticos de los Ministros de las Misiones de Alta California sobre su poblacion, bautismos, matrimonios, difuntos, y Ramos de agricultura en 1834 [= statistical reports of the mission ministers on population, baptisms, marriages, deaths and agriculture]. Savage then lists: Eran Ministros de las Misiones en 1834 los siguientes: Fr. Ramon Avella [= Ramon Abella] and Felipe Arroyo [= Arroyo de la Cuesta] braced together En Sn Luis Obispo; Pedro Cabot, ditto, Sn Gabriel; Fr. Antonio Jimeno, Sta. Barbara; Fernando Martin, San Diego; Buenaventura Fortuni and Vicente Pascual Oliva braced together Sn Luis Rey; Jose Ma de Zalvidea, Sn Juan Capistrano; Francisco Gonzalez de Ibarra, Sn Fernando; Blas Ordaz, Sn Buenaventura; Juan Moreno, La Purisima Concepcion; Jose Joaquin Jimeno, Sta. Ines; Tomas Eleuterio Estenaga, Sn Gabriel. A near-complete 1834 roster of Alta California mission ministers with their stations; note San Gabriel is given twice (Cabot and Estenaga) and the Cabot assignment should be verified against other sources.

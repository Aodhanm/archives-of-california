# Vicente Sanchez acknowledges receipt of his expediente. Verbatim: 'Recibió su expediente.' [= he received his file]. San

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n453** · Sav p. 37 · tomo p. p. 537. · 1833-06-24 · Savage abstract · 

Vicente Sanchez acknowledges receipt of his expediente. Verbatim: 'Recibió su expediente.' [= he received his file]. Sanchez was a leading Los Angeles officeholder of the period; the abstract does not say which expediente.

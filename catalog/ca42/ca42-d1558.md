**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n551–n552** · Sav p. ink 54 · stamp 551 · tomo p. p.s 474-76. [Tomo VI] · 1829-10-31 · Savage abstract · 

Report of entries and clearances of vessels at Los Angeles, spans n551-n552. September: entry 11th, a brigantine-schooner (name queried) from the Sandwich Islands, 182 tons, cap. Gmo Aralon [?], China goods; departure 17th. Verbatim: 'Bergantin Goleta (?) procedente de las islas de Sandwich; 182 ton; su cap. Gmo Aralon; con efectos de China.' Continues on n552 with the Broclina, Tomasa Noule, Wilmenton, Tridente and Rosalía.

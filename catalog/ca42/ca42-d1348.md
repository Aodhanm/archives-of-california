# 'Que con ningun pretesto se detenga la correspondencia.' [= on no pretext is the correspondence to be detained]. Savage'

'Que con ningun pretesto se detenga la correspondencia.' [= on no pretext is the correspondence to be detained]. Savage's '(Sin lugar)' stands in place of the place of writing and '(Sin firma)' in place of the sender; neither is a datum.

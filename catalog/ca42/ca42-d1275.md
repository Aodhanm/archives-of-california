# Garcia petitions to be excused from military service on medical grounds. Verbatim: 'Solicita le dispensen del servicio m

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n454** · Sav p. 38 · tomo p. p. 540-541 · 1833-06-04 · Savage abstract · 

Garcia petitions to be excused from military service on medical grounds. Verbatim: 'Solicita le dispensen del servicio militar por tener dilatada la pupila del ojo derecho y una fistula en el ano.' [= asks exemption from military service, having a dilated right pupil and an anal fistula]. Unusually specific medical detail for this series.

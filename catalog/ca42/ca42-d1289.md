# MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project. Governor rules the alcalde had no right to remove the regidor Juan Jose Castro fro

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n456** · Sav p. 40 · tomo p. p.571-572 · 1833-06-01 · Savage abstract · 

MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project. Governor rules the alcalde had no right to remove the regidor Juan Jose Castro from office and orders him restored. Verbatim: 'Que no teniendo derecho para haber quitado de su empleo al regidor J.n José Castro, le reponga en su destino.' First sub-entry of a grouped heading '1833 = Monterrey / El Gov.r al alcalde de Branciforte'. Opens the Branciforte constitutional quarrel that runs to n458.

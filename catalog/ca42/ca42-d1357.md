# 'No hay a conceder la instancia de Vicente Moraga por haber sido hecho pasado el tiempo fijado por la ley.' [sic; = the 

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n472** · Sav p. stamp 472 / ink 56 · tomo p. p. 718 · 1833-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

'No hay a conceder la instancia de Vicente Moraga por haber sido hecho pasado el tiempo fijado por la ley.' [sic; = the petition of Vicente Moraga is not to be granted, having been made after the time fixed by law]. Denies the síndico excusal at p. 714-715. Heading year reads 1833 though it answers a petition of 25 Dic 1833 and sits beside 1834 rulings; recorded as written, see issues.

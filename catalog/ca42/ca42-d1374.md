**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n477** · Sav p. stamp 477 / ink 61 · tomo p. p. 755-756 y 759-760 · 1834-07-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Que el segundo regidor contravino á la ley dando permiso en una casa de jugar.' [= that the second regidor broke the law by giving permission in a gambling house]. The San José alcalde denouncing a sitting regidor directly to Figueroa. Non-contiguous cite, the document being split around p. 757-758. Sender written 'Chabaya' [= Pedro Chaboya, San José alcalde 1834, inferred].

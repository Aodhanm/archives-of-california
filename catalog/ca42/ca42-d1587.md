# Heading “1840 = Abre 26 = Angeles”; “Remisión de expedts” = remittance of expedientes. Month written “Abre” = Abril, con

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n559** · Sav p. stamp 559 · ink 62. · tomo p. p.s 532-3. · 1840-04-26 · Savage abstract · 

Heading “1840 = Abre 26 = Angeles”; “Remisión de expedts” = remittance of expedientes. Month written “Abre” = Abril, confirmed by direct glyph comparison with the round-O “Obre 31” on n551 (see issues). First appearance in this stretch of [Santiago] Argüello writing from Angeles, who also heads the 1841 entry on n551.

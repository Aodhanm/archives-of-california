# Commends the judges who took cognizance of the Fink killing and urges vigilance to root out the vagrants. Verbatim: 'Rec

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n547** · Sav p. ink 50 · stamp 547 · tomo p. p. 424-5. [Tomo VI] · 1841-01-23 · Savage abstract · 

Commends the judges who took cognizance of the Fink killing and urges vigilance to root out the vagrants. Verbatim: 'Recomienda á los jueces que conocieron en la muerte de Fink y que se desvelan pr estirpar los vagos.' Gloss: part of the January 1841 Los Angeles murder-and-vagrancy cluster.

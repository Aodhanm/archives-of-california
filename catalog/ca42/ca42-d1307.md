# 'Recibió sus cuentas.' [= he has received his accounts]. 'Guadalupe' as a jurisdiction recurs three times on n460-n461 a

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n460** · Sav p. 44 · tomo p. p. 610-612. · 1833-10-12 · Savage abstract · 

'Recibió sus cuentas.' [= he has received his accounts]. 'Guadalupe' as a jurisdiction recurs three times on n460-n461 and is most likely the pueblo de San José de Guadalupe (its alcalde in these entries is Salvio Pacheco), but Savage never writes it out; see issues.

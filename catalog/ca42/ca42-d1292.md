**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n457** · Sav p. 41 · tomo p. p. 576-577 · 1833-06-02 · Savage abstract · 

The removed regidor Juan Jose Castro complains that the alcalde has accused him unjustly. Verbatim: 'Que el alcalde se queja de él injustamente.' [= that the alcalde complains of him unjustly]. Castro's own answer in the Branciforte quarrel.

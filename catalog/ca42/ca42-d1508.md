# Because the harvest failed the farmers wish to pay 2 pesos in place of each fanega of seed owed as tithe. Verbatim: 'A c

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n542** · Sav p. ink 45 · stamp 542 · tomo p. p. 355-7.-8. [Tomo VI] · 1827-09-03 · Savage abstract · 

Because the harvest failed the farmers wish to pay 2 pesos in place of each fanega of seed owed as tithe. Verbatim: 'A causa de la pérdida de la cosecha los agricultores quieren dar $2 en lugar de cada fanega de semilla en pago del diezmo.' Marked '(2 doc.)'; this is doc 1 of 2 under the cite.

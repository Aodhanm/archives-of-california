# Governor orders the ayuntamiento to take measures to have a jail built. Verbatim: 'Que tome providencias el ayuntamiento

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n451** · Sav p. 35 · tomo p. p. 517 · 1833-05-31 · Savage abstract · 

Governor orders the ayuntamiento to take measures to have a jail built. Verbatim: 'Que tome providencias el ayuntamiento para que se construya una Carcel.' [= that the town council take steps for a jail to be built]. Second sub-entry of the grouped heading opened on n450; only the dittoed date line 'Mayo 31 -' and the flush-right cite sit on this leaf.

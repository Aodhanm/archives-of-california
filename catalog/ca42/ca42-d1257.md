**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n449** · Sav p. 33 · tomo p. p. 507-508 · 1833-05-02 · Savage abstract · 

Truncated, unsigned and unaddressed document on the fees of the ayuntamiento's secretary. Verbatim: 'Sobre honorarios del secretario del ayuntamiento' [= on the fees of the town-council secretary]. Both parenthetical lines are Savage's own editorial notes, not data on the document's origin.

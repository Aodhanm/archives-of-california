# Second sub-line under the grouped heading begun on n463. 'Que como único juez, debe conocer de las Causas de Contrabando

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n464** · Sav p. 48 · tomo p. p. 634 · 1833-05-18 · Savage abstract · 

Second sub-line under the grouped heading begun on n463. 'Que como único juez, debe conocer de las Causas de Contrabando.' [= that as sole judge he must take cognizance of contraband causes]. An explicit jurisdictional ruling vesting smuggling prosecutions in the single local judge; marquee material for the Alcalde Rule project.

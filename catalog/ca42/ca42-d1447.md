# Format A entry. Verbatim: 'Que necesita papel sellado.' [= that he needs stamped paper]. Escobar appears elsewhere in th

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n493** · Sav p. stamp 493 / ink 77 · tomo p. p. 960-961 · 1833-02-14 · Savage abstract · 

Format A entry. Verbatim: 'Que necesita papel sellado.' [= that he needs stamped paper]. Escobar appears elsewhere in this tomo as the Monterey alcalde; stamped paper was required for valid judicial acts, so the shortage is a working constraint on the juzgado.

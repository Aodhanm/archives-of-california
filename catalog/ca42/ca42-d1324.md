# 'Que constituya el tribunal de vagos.' [= that he constitute the vagrancy tribunal]. Direct order to an alcalde to stand

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n464** · Sav p. 48 · tomo p. p. 647-648 · 1833-05-18 · Savage abstract · 

'Que constituya el tribunal de vagos.' [= that he constitute the vagrancy tribunal]. Direct order to an alcalde to stand up a tribunal de vagos; pairs with the same-day general order at p.649 (n465). Core Alcalde Rule material on the alcalde as instrument of labour discipline.

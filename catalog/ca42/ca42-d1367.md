# 'Que los que suscriben nombraron sindico del ayuntamiento á Teodosio Garcia y juez de campo á Mª Bosanda' [= that the un

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n475** · Sav p. stamp 475 / ink 59 · tomo p. p. 739-740 (2 docˢ) · 1828-01-01 · Savage abstract · 

'Que los que suscriben nombraron sindico del ayuntamiento á Teodosio Garcia y juez de campo á Mª Bosanda' [= that the undersigned named Teodosio Garcia síndico of the ayuntamiento and Mª Bosanda juez de campo]. New Year's Day municipal appointments at Monterey with five signers named. Document 1 of 2 on this cite. Readings 'Desoria' [?], 'Cazery' [?], 'Bosanda' [?] and 'sindico' [?] are uncertain; 'Bosanda' may be Boronda but I have not confirmed it.

# Has re-elected Don Raymundo Carrillo subprefect of Santa Bárbara. Verbatim: 'Enero 9. Ha reelegido á Dn Raymundo Carrill

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n547** · Sav p. ink 50 · stamp 547 · tomo p. p. 426-7. [Tomo VI] · 1841-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

Has re-elected Don Raymundo Carrillo subprefect of Santa Bárbara. Verbatim: 'Enero 9. Ha reelegido á Dn Raymundo Carrillo Subprefecto de Sta Bárbara.' Gloss: named subprefect, Santa Bárbara — pairs with Carrillo's resignation six days later at p.s 428-9.

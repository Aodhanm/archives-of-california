# 'Que le mande papel sellado' [asks the subaltern comisario of the port to send him stamped paper]. Escobar acting as alc

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n479** · Sav p. 63 · tomo p. p.Tomo V p. 774 · 1833-01-04 · Savage abstract · 

'Que le mande papel sellado' [asks the subaltern comisario of the port to send him stamped paper]. Escobar acting as alcalde/juez; stamped paper was the required medium for judicial acts, so this is juzgado supply business.

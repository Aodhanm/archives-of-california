# The Branciforte alcalde reports that when he had to arrest the resident Miguel Villagrano the regidor refused to obey hi

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n456** · Sav p. 40 · tomo p. p. 574-575 · 1833-06-01 · Savage abstract · 

The Branciforte alcalde reports that when he had to arrest the resident Miguel Villagrano the regidor refused to obey him in the matter. Verbatim: 'Que habiendo tenido de arrestar al vecino Miguel Villagrano, el regidor reusó á obedecerle en esa circunstancia' [= having had to arrest the neighbour Miguel Villagrano, the councilman refused to obey him]. The alcalde's side of the quarrel; surname reads Villagrano [Villagrana?].

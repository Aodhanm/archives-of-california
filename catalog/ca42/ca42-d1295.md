# MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project. Governor orders the alcalde never again to suspend any functionary from his post w

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n458** · Sav p. 42 · tomo p. p. 581. · 1833-06-11 · Savage abstract · 

MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project. Governor orders the alcalde never again to suspend any functionary from his post without justified cause. Verbatim: 'Que no vuelva á suspender de su empleo á ningun funcionario sin Causa justificada' [= that he not again suspend any official from office without justified cause]. Closing ruling of the Branciforte quarrel begun at p. 571-572.

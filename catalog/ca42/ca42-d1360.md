# 'Que en la acta de eleccion de ayuntamiento, no se ha cumplido con la ley' [= that in the act of the ayuntamiento electi

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n473** · Sav p. stamp 473 / ink 57 · tomo p. p. 724-725 · 1834-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Que en la acta de eleccion de ayuntamiento, no se ha cumplido con la ley' [= that in the act of the ayuntamiento election the law has not been complied with]. The government's finding against the Los Angeles municipal election whose acta Carrillo forwarded on 26-27 Dic 1833 (p. 720-723). Sender unsigned per Savage.

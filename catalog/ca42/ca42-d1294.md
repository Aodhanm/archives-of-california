# The Branciforte alcalde reports that the regidor has been restored to his office and encloses a report on the case. Verb

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n457** · Sav p. 41 · tomo p. p. 579-580 · 1833-06-06 · Savage abstract · 

The Branciforte alcalde reports that the regidor has been restored to his office and encloses a report on the case. Verbatim: 'Que el regidor está repuesto en su empleo y manda un informe sobre el Caso.' [= that the councilman is reinstated in his post, and he sends a report on the case]. Names the Branciforte alcalde as Jose Antonio Robles.

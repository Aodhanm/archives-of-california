# Says the murderers of Fink are A. Valencia, S. Linares and José Duarte; proposes that the vagrants be sent to the fronti

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n548** · Sav p. ink 51 (left of stamp) · stamp 548 · tomo p. p. 430-32. [Tomo VI] · 1841-01-22 · Savage abstract · 

Says the murderers of Fink are A. Valencia, S. Linares and José Duarte; proposes that the vagrants be sent to the frontier. Verbatim: 'Dice qe los asesinos de Fink son A Valencia, S Linares, y Jose Duarte. Propone que se remitan los vagos á la frontera.' Gloss: three named suspects plus a deportation proposal for vagos.

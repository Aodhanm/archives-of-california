# Governor orders the alcalde to keep an account of the fees the court generates. Verbatim: 'Que lleve Cuenta de los derec

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n449** · Sav p. 33 · tomo p. p. 509 · 1833-05-02 · Savage abstract · 

Governor orders the alcalde to keep an account of the fees the court generates. Verbatim: 'Que lleve Cuenta de los derechos que produsca el juzgado' [= that he keep account of the fees the juzgado produces]. Opens the Monterey juzgado fee-accounting series continued at p. 549-556 (n455).

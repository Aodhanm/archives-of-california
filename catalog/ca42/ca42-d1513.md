# Whether he may impose fines on the disobedient. Verbatim: 'Que si impone multa á los desobedts.' Gloss: the alcalde asks

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n543** · Sav p. ink 46 · stamp 543 · tomo p. p.s 364-5. [Tomo VI] · 1827-02-07 · Savage abstract · 

Whether he may impose fines on the disobedient. Verbatim: 'Que si impone multa á los desobedts.' Gloss: the alcalde asks whether his office carries the power to fine those who defy his orders — a direct query about the scope of alcalde authority.

# Doc 2 of 2 under the same cite, itemised per the 08-12 no-bounding rule. Savage abstracts the pair together as one summa

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n492** · Sav p. stamp 492 / ink 76 · tomo p. p. 930-934 (2 doc.) · 1833-02-10 · Savage abstract · 

Doc 2 of 2 under the same cite, itemised per the 08-12 no-bounding rule. Savage abstracts the pair together as one summary; the second physical document is almost certainly the enclosed request from the comandante of San Luis Rey, but its own text is not separately abstracted. Content as abstracted: request for 15-20 men against Indian 'convulsiones' over the repartimiento de tierras.

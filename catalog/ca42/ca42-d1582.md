# “Que los bienes de José Perez se extraigan de Sn Pascual.” = that José Pérez's goods be removed from San Pascual. Attach

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n558** · Sav p. stamp 558 · ink 61. · tomo p. p.s 525-6. · 1840-02-24 · Savage abstract · 

“Que los bienes de José Perez se extraigan de Sn Pascual.” = that José Pérez's goods be removed from San Pascual. Attachment/removal of property at the San Pascual [Indian pueblo, San Diego jurisdiction] — flagged as possible Indian-pueblo property business, not confirmed.

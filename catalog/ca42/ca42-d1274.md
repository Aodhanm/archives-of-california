# Governor sends a decree to the alcalde of Guadalupe. Verbatim: 'Manda un decreto.' [= sends a decree]. Guadalupe is not 

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n454** · Sav p. 38 · tomo p. p. 539 · 1833-06-16 · Savage abstract · 

Governor sends a decree to the alcalde of Guadalupe. Verbatim: 'Manda un decreto.' [= sends a decree]. Guadalupe is not identified in the abstract; given the San Diego dateline it may be the Baja California frontier settlement, but I am NOT asserting that.

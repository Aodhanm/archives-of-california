# Nil return on vagrancy: the Branciforte alcalde reports there are no vagrants. Verbatim (the entire abstract): 'Que no h

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n458** · Sav p. 42 · tomo p. p. 582-583 · 1833-06-22 · Savage abstract · 

Nil return on vagrancy: the Branciforte alcalde reports there are no vagrants. Verbatim (the entire abstract): 'Que no hay vagos.' [= that there are no vagrants]. Direct evidence of the vagrancy-reporting duty laid on alcaldes and of a negative return being filed.

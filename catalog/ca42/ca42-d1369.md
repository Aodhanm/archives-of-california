# 'Que le remite á dos conspiradores contra el gorierno.' [sic 'gorierno' for gobierno; = that he remits to him two conspi

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n475** · Sav p. stamp 475 / ink 59 · tomo p. p. 742-743. · 1835-05-05 · Savage abstract · 

'Que le remite á dos conspiradores contra el gorierno.' [sic 'gorierno' for gobierno; = that he remits to him two conspirators against the government]. The Governor forwarding two political prisoners to the supreme court; original p. 741 is skipped between this cite and the preceding one.

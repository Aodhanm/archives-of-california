# Savage abstracts the whole heading as “Asuntos sin importancia” (= matters of no importance); no contents given. Cite ca

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n552** · Sav p. stamp 552 · ink 55. · tomo p. p.s 477-80. · 1828-02-02 · Savage abstract · 

Savage abstracts the whole heading as “Asuntos sin importancia” (= matters of no importance); no contents given. Cite carries the marker “(2 doc.)” so two physical documents sit under it — this is doc 1 of 2. Moreno is the same aduana guard named in Buelna's contraband denunciation at n553 (p.481-3), which makes the pair less trivial than Savage's dismissal implies.

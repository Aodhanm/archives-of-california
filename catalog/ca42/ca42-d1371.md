# 'Que remitió al asesor del territorio, una causa contra 2 reos.' [= that he forwarded to the territorial asesor a case a

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n476** · Sav p. stamp 476 / ink 60 · tomo p. p. 746-748 · 1835-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Que remitió al asesor del territorio, una causa contra 2 reos.' [= that he forwarded to the territorial asesor a case against two prisoners]. The Angeles judge routing a criminal cause up to the legal assessor. I am inferring the sender is Francisco Javier Alvarado, alcalde of Los Angeles in 1835; the leaf gives only 'Franᶜᵒ J. Alvarado'.

# “Lugo consulta si podrá actuar en causas de parientes suyos.” = [Felipe] Lugo asks whether he may act as judge in causes

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n560** · Sav p. stamp 560 · ink 63. · tomo p. p.s 570-72. · 1840-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

“Lugo consulta si podrá actuar en causas de parientes suyos.” = [Felipe] Lugo asks whether he may act as judge in causes involving his own relatives. MARQUEE for the Alcalde Rule project: an explicit 1840 recusal-for-kinship consulta from a Los Angeles juez de paz — the same question that voided regidor Castro's election at San Francisco in 1835 (C-A 42 n515, p.143-145).

# Governor refuses the alferez's petition of 20 May. Verbatim: 'Que no hay lugar á Concederle su solicitud de 20 de Mayo.'

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n452** · Sav p. 36 · tomo p. p. 530 · 1833-06-03 · Savage abstract · 

Governor refuses the alferez's petition of 20 May. Verbatim: 'Que no hay lugar á Concederle su solicitud de 20 de Mayo.' [= that there are no grounds to grant his petition of 20 May]. The petition's subject is not stated in the abstract.

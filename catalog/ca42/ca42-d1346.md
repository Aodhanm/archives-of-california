# 'Que estando enfermo el General, despacha al medico Stokes para que asista á la junta de medicos que debe haber.' [= the

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n469** · Sav p. stamp 469 / ink 53 · tomo p. p. 698-699 · 1835-09-26 · Savage abstract · 

'Que estando enfermo el General, despacha al medico Stokes para que asista á la junta de medicos que debe haber.' [= the General being ill, the physician Stokes is dispatched to attend the junta of physicians to be held]. Sender unsigned per Savage: '(Sin firma)'. I am inferring 'el General' = Figueroa, who died 29 Sept 1835, three days after this date; the leaf does not name him.

# Which asesor is to be consulted while Don Cosme Peña has not yet arrived. Verbatim: 'Que á que asesor consultá mientras 

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n546** · Sav p. ink 49 · stamp 546 · tomo p. p.s 406-9. [Tomo VI] · 1834-05-30 · Savage abstract · 

Which asesor is to be consulted while Don Cosme Peña has not yet arrived. Verbatim: 'Que á que asesor consultá mientras llega Don Cosme Peña.' Gloss: the territorial government without a legal assessor pending Cosme Peña's arrival — the professional-legal-advice gap behind alcalde adjudication.

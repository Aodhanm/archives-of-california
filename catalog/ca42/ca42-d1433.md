# Verbatim: 'Que le hará reconocer por Gefe Politico del territorio.' [= that he will have him recognised as Political Chi

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n491** · Sav p. stamp 491 / ink 75 · tomo p. p. 909 · 1836-09-26 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Que le hará reconocer por Gefe Politico del territorio.' [= that he will have him recognised as Political Chief of the territory]. Late September 1836, i.e. the eve of the Alvarado revolt; the San José alcalde pledging to have the addressee acknowledged as jefe político.

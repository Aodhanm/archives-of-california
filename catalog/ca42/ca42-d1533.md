# Received the plan and estimate for the work on the Monterey town hall, will examine them and make such observations as o

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n546** · Sav p. ink 49 · stamp 546 · tomo p. p.s 411-13. [Tomo VI] · 1834-01-31 · Savage abstract · 

Received the plan and estimate for the work on the Monterey town hall, will examine them and make such observations as occur to him. Verbatim: 'Recibió el plano y presupsº de la obra de las casas consistoriales de Mont. y los examinará y hará las observaciones que le ocurran.' Gloss: Zamorano on the Monterey casas consistoriales project — municipal public works.

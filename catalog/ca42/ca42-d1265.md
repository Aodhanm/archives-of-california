# The ayuntamiento congratulates the governor on the country's peace being restored. Verbatim: 'Que se alegra el ayuntamie

**C-A 42 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n451** · Sav p. 35 · tomo p. p. 519-520 · 1833-04-17 · Savage abstract · 

The ayuntamiento congratulates the governor on the country's peace being restored. Verbatim: 'Que se alegra el ayuntamiento que se haya celebrado la paz del pais.' [= that the council rejoices that the peace of the country has been concluded]. Second sub-entry of the same grouped heading; note the date runs BACKWARD from the sub-entry above it (Mayo 31 then Abril 17).

# Heading verbatim: '1824- En.º 29- Mont. / Diputacion Provincial. Dros. á buques balleneros.' [= Duties on whaling vessel

**C-A 59 (Legislative Records), scan n29** · Sav p. 30 [stamp] / 28 [ink] · tomo p. p. 40 · 1824-01-29 · Savage abstract · 

Heading verbatim: '1824- En.º 29- Mont. / Diputacion Provincial. Dros. á buques balleneros.' [= Duties on whaling vessels]. Resolves that every foreign vessel entering under the title of whaler and anchoring at any point of the Province shall contribute 'cinco reales pr. tonelada'; whalers and all other foreign vessels are not exempted from paying for firewood taken for provision, 'á razon de seis reales pr. cada lanchada'; the impost on timber taken for the vessels themselves is left to the Administrador de Rentas públicas, proportioned to the size and thickness of the pieces. Savage's cite: p. 40.

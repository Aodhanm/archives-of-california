# ⚠ NOT A RECOVERY — RETRACTED CLAIM, 2026-08-27. This was published as a recovery; independent verification showed the do

**C-A 59 (Legislative Records), scan n340** · Sav p. 336 · tomo p. p.stamped 338 · the leaf reads '7 de Enero de 1831'. Since it transmits a plan pronounced 29 November 1831, the year as written is internally impossible; 1832 is the obvious reading, but I am reporting the leaf verbatim and flagging 1832 as INFERENCE, not fact. · Savage abstract · 

⚠ NOT A RECOVERY — RETRACTED CLAIM, 2026-08-27. This was published as a recovery; independent verification showed the document IS already catalogued (it duplicates the Plan de San Diego record in this volume). The row is retained as a CORRECTION to that record, because the verification did establish two real facts: the Plan begins on this leaf (stamped 338 / ink 336), not where the older record places it, and the older record's incipit has transcription errors against the leaf. Original recovery text follows for the record: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n322-n341, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'No 15 - Juzgado de 1a Insta del Pueblo de Na Sa de los Angeles = Acompaño a V.S. el plan pronunciado la noche del 29 de Novre pr el vecindario del Presidio de Sn Diego, despues de haber aceptado pr 2a vez este vecindario al objeto a qe fue promovido. - Dios y Libertad - Pueblo de los Angeles 7 de Enero de 1831 - Manuel Dominguez - Sr Comte Gral, y Gefe Polito de la Alta Cal.' [= No. 15. Court of First Instance of the Pueblo of Nuestra Senora de los Angeles: I forward to Your Lordship the plan pronounced on the night of 29 November by the vecindario of the Presidio of San Diego, after this vecindario had for a second time accepted the object for which it was promoted. God and Liberty. Pueblo de los Angeles, 7 January 1831. Manuel Dominguez. To the Comandante General and Jefe Politico of Alta California.] The covering oficio under which the Plan of San Diego reached the territorial government. Short but load-bearing: it is the transmittal that establishes the Plan's route into the official record, and it states that the Los Angeles vecindario had accepted the pronunciamiento's object 'pr 2a vez', a second adherence. Manuel Dominguez, who signs here as the Angeles judge of first instance, is also among the signers of the San Diego plan as re-adopted (per the catalog's own d55 summary). Entirely unrecorded.

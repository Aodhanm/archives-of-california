**C-A 59 (Legislative Records), scan n331** · Sav p. 328 · tomo p. p.stamped 330 · not stated on the portion visible on n331; the immediately preceding text cites Victoria's order 'con fecha 13 de Julio ulto', so mid-1831 (inference) · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d54, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'No 8 - El Co Manuel Victoria, Comte Gral. y Gefe Polito de la Alta Cal. = Concedo libre y seguro pasaporte a Dn Abel Stearns pa qe pase a Mex. a la conclusion de' [= No. 8. The Citizen Manuel Victoria, Comandante General and Jefe Politico of Alta California: I grant free and safe passport to Don Abel Stearns to pass to Mexico at the conclusion of...] Victoria's passport expelling the Massachusetts-born merchant Abel Stearns from Alta California. The document immediately above it on the same leaf, closing '= Victoria =', is Victoria's ruling on Stearns's petition: by the order of 13 July last Stearns is not strictly obliged to go to Mexico and 'ira a donde le acomode', but if he means to petition he must do so to the Supremo Gobierno, to whom Victoria has already sent 'los motivos qe han esijido mi providencia pa apartarle de este Territo con los demas antecedentes qe pr decirlo asi testifican la falsedad de lo qe ahora alega, pretendiendo eludir su salida'; Victoria refuses him departure by way of San Francisco, and states that since all the papers have gone to the Ministerio 'no queda otro documento qe entregarle mas qe el pasaporte pa salir en la 1a ocasion de buque qe se presente.' Two hidden documents on one leaf, both bearing on Stearns's 1831 expulsion.

**C-A 59 (Legislative Records), scan n340** · Sav p. 336 · tomo p. p.stamped 338 · 1831-08-24 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d54, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'Dios y Libertad. Mont. Agto 24 de 1831 - Manuel Victoria - Al Alcalde del Pueblo de Na Sa de los Angeles.' [= God and Liberty. Monterey, 24 August 1831. Manuel Victoria. To the Alcalde of the Pueblo of Nuestra Senora de los Angeles.] CAUTION: only the closing lines of this document are on n340; its body lies on leaves I did not open (n337-n339 or earlier). The visible close runs 'incurrir en una deformidad qe reprueban las leyes y nada debe hacerse si no es por el termino regular y conforme a las facultades demarcadas a la autoridad que ejerzo, con respecto a las actuales circunstancias en qe me hallo.' A Victoria instruction to the Los Angeles alcalde insisting that nothing be done except by regular process within his own delimited faculties. No record covers it.

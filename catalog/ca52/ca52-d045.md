# Los Angeles 1786 possession dossier (roster ~COMPLETE, item-read) — the formalization of the LA founders' titles: per Fa

**C-A 52 (State Papers, Missions and Colonization), scan n280–n303** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.285–307 · 1786-09-04 · Savage abstract · 

Los Angeles 1786 possession dossier (roster ~COMPLETE, item-read) — the formalization of the LA founders' titles: per Fages's mandate & Neve's Reglamento, the 'nueve Pobladores' each granted a solar (20×40 varas) + 4 suertes (200 v²) + a registered brand. ⭐ COMPLETE 9-poblador roster: Antonio Clemente (José proxy), Antonio Navarro (absent, Clemente proxy), José Vanegas, Pablo Rodríguez, José Moreno, Basilio Rosas, Alejandro Rosas, José Sinova, Manuel Camero (n288); witnesses Vicente Feliz & Roque de Cota; most illiterate (signed by cross). ⭐⭐ Ends with a 1786 PLANO/MAP of the pueblo (n302 — plaza+church-cross, grid of solares, suertes, Río de Porciúncula, Acequia Madre). + land-measure policy (peonía/caballería/sitio; 4-legua grants, n320) ⚠ SPAN NARROWED 2026-08-27 (Phase-4 audit): this band read n280–305 and swallowed TWO unrecorded documents beginning on n304. The dossier CLOSES on n303 (stamp 305 / ink 308): 'En el Rl Pres.o de Sta Barba a 18 dias del mes de Set.re de 1786. Yo D.n Jose Arguello... firme de que day fe = Jose Arguello', Savage's cite 'pp. 710-727 y 29-30'.

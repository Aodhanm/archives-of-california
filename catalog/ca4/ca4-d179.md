# (top, cont.) closes: authority & I will remit to Excmo., supplicating this oficio join with my information & be of his a

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n122** · Sav p. 121/122 · tomo p. pp.338–343 · 1787 Jul 14 · Savage abstract · 

(top, cont.) closes: authority & I will remit to Excmo., supplicating this oficio join with my information & be of his agrado (pp.338–343). Then new: ⭐ *carta enredada* [tangled letter] explaining difficulties Soler finds in his account-arrangements as Ayud. Insp. Feels Gov. has cut the Gordian knot; defends taking [decisions] precipitately, honor at stake; defends Lasso deemed unfit as habilitado; Goycoechea cannot be habilitado por poca inteligencia & broken health ('no puede dar ese encargo como abilitado, por su poca inteligencia y su quebrantada salud'); wants very intelligent man; will pass this oficio to more intelligent [officers]

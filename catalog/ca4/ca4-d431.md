# Verbatim heading: '1788 Abril 17 San Diego / Fages a Josef de Ortega Decreto del Soberano' [Savage: 'Fages á Josef de Or

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n217** · Sav p. 217 / 32 · tomo p. p. 102 · 1788-04-17 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 Abril 17 San Diego / Fages a Josef de Ortega Decreto del Soberano' [Savage: 'Fages á Josef de Ortega Decreto del Soberano']. English gloss: Fages to Josef de Ortega, the Sovereign decree. Substance: communicates the Real Decreto of 21 July last, by which His Majesty grants to the Exmo Sor D. Antº Valdez, Secretario de Estado y del Despacho de Yndias, the faculty of signing with a half-signature ('la facultad de firmar con media firma'). NOTE this is Fages transmitting the decree outward and is distinct from the three acknowledgements already in the export (n14 p.38-40 Ugarte to Fages 21 Nov 1787; n263 p.229 Zuniga 19 Apr 1788; n292 p.322 Goycoechea 12 May 1788).

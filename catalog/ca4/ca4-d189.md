# (p.393–394) *Cargos contra el habilitado Sal*: habilitado Sal must give accounts of '82 & '83, only encounters that Sal 

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n132** · Sav p. 133/130 · tomo p. pp.393–396 · 1787 Agosto 8 & Agosto 5 · Savage abstract · 

(p.393–394) *Cargos contra el habilitado Sal*: habilitado Sal must give accounts of '82 & '83, only encounters that Sal owes his Compañía 2,922 pesos; though the form not correct for the operation, requires what he asks; supposing quiebra can grow, will lower with alcances demonstrated in '86 & semestre '87. (p.395–396) *Cargos contra Sal*: asks liberty for Hermenegildo Sal, against whom proceso being formed for instancia of a soldier imprisoned at Pres. San Fco, per sup. orden del Gob., not proven cause

# Savage heading: 'Remision de Inventarios'. Encloses the inventory of this habilitación's effects signed by the company's

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n269** · Sav p. 269 [stamp] / 84 [hand] · tomo p. p. 248 · 1788-02-25 · Savage abstract · 

Savage heading: 'Remision de Inventarios'. Encloses the inventory of this habilitación's effects signed by the company's apoderados, and likewise the inventory of the goods of the prisoner Fran.co Avila in the pueblo of San José. Verbatim: 'el Inventario de los bienes del reo Fran.co Avila en el pueblo de San José, los q.e conserva su Apoderado Ramon Bozorques' [Savage spells it Bozorques; = Bojorques/Bojórquez].

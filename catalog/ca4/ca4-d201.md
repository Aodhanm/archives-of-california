# (p.446–447, cont.) solicited his enmienda; placement of Argüello inexcusable, as is permanence of Lasso at San Diego; ne

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n144** · Sav p. 145/142 · tomo p. pp.446–449 · 1787 Junio 10 & Julio 6 · Savage abstract · 

(p.446–447, cont.) solicited his enmienda; placement of Argüello inexcusable, as is permanence of Lasso at San Diego; necessity that Lasso manage a habilitación is clear; even better if he trained at Sta Barbara for contabilidad, aiding Ortega so he might work; acquired trigo del Carmelo & a tercio arina[?] de San Luis; 'manda memorias su mujer, la comadre de Soler.' (Julio 6) *Fages→Soler — Apreciaciones sobre oficiales*: reproaches Soler for having had so much consideration for Lasso; regrets not having placed Argüello in his puesto as planned; asks report re Diego [González] re the asunto de Sta Clara y los Castros

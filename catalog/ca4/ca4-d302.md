**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n248** · Sav p. 248 [stamp] / 63 [hand] · tomo p. p. 187 repetida · 1788-03-01 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 'Soler á Fages Enfermedad de un oficial'. Whole abstract: 'El Teniente de esta Comp.a se halla bastantem.te agravado de su accidente; sin embargo, que no parará el espediente de lo que se le confiera, y tiene á su cargo' [the lieutenant of this company is much worsened by his ailment; still, the business entrusted to him will not stop]. Officer unnamed by Savage; the Sta Barbara teniente in 1788 was Felipe de Goycoechea [?] - not stated in the document.

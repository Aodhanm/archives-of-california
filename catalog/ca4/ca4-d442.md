# Verbatim heading: '1788 Julio 29 - Chihuahua / Ugarte á Fages / Gracia de inválido á militares inutilizados en el servic

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n201-n202** · Sav p. n201 stamp 201 / hand 16; n202 stamp 202 / hand 17 · tomo p. pp 40-42 · 1788-07-29 · verbatim transcription · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 Julio 29 - Chihuahua / Ugarte á Fages / Gracia de inválido á militares inutilizados en el servicio'. English gloss: Ugarte to Fages, the grant of invalid (disability-pension) status to soldiers disabled in the service. Substance, verbatim: 'Comunica una Rl orden de 16 de Marzo trasmitida por el Ministro, Bailío Frey Antº Val=dez al Virey de México y por este á dho Ugarte en 3 de Julio.' Operative clause, verbatim: 'Que todo individuo que se inutilice en faenas del servicio, aunque no sea en función de guerra, debe gozar la gracia de inválido.' The point of the order is that disability incurred in work details, not only in combat, qualifies a man for the inválido grant. Document opens at the foot of n201 under a + mark and closes at the head of n202, where Savage writes the page cite. The upper half of n201 is the tail of the mando-militar royal order anchored at n206... corrected: anchored at n197 (export doc for n197 to n200), which ends on n201 with the cite pp 34-39.

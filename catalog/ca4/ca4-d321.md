# spans n264-n265. Estrada, alférez habilitado of Loreto, answers the charges: he has reformulated the 1786 Real Hacienda 

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n264** · Sav p. 264 [stamp] / 79 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 236-238 · 1788-04-01 · Savage abstract · 

spans n264-n265. Estrada, alférez habilitado of Loreto, answers the charges: he has reformulated the 1786 Real Hacienda account with the voucher for the 900 pesos of 'toros orejanos ó cimarrones'; after eight years as habilitado he will take no further charge and asks that another person be named to receive royal funds. On the herds: 'Los toros orejanos se nombran tales en Californias por ser fieras de los montes'. Cites his predecessor comisario D. Fran.co Alvarez Osorio; argues oaths are needless where the vouchers are legitimate.

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n307** · Sav p. 307 / 122 · tomo p. pp. 369-370 · 1788-10-01 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 Oct.re 1.o Sta Barbara / Pablo Cota a Fages Entrega de una Escolta' [Savage: '1788 Oct.re 1.º Sta Bárbara / Pablo Cota á Fages Entrega de una Escolta']. English gloss: Pablo Cota to Fages, handover of an escolta. Substance: he reports having handed over to the cabo Jose M. Ortega the escolta of La Purisima Concepcion with everything pertaining to it by duplicate inventory, having stopped eight days to instruct him in his new obligations ('habiendose detenido ocho dias en instruirle en sus nuevas obligaciones'). Ties to the Purisima founding and construction records already in the export at n272 and n278.

# *Lujo en los soldados* (luxury among soldiers): added to oficio to presidios the following prevenciones — prohibited sol

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n113** · Sav p. 112 dup /113 · tomo p. pp.310–312 · 1787 Mar 5 · Savage abstract · 

*Lujo en los soldados* (luxury among soldiers): added to oficio to presidios the following prevenciones — prohibited soldier's use of 'galas impropias y costosas, reprehendiendo el particular lujo de uso de sedas de la China y pieles de Nutria' [otter-fur], which ruins the soldier's economy, so he can maintain his family & buy arms/powder as needed. (p.312) received & announced royal order re premio for years of service & deserter-apprehension, changing to money premio

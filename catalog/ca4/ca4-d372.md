# Heading: “sobre una testamentaria.” Holds the inventories of the goods of “el difunto D. Ant.o de Ocio” and the debts; w

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n290** · Sav p. 290 [stamp] / 105 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 315-317 · 1788-08-16 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: “sobre una testamentaria.” Holds the inventories of the goods of “el difunto D. Ant.o de Ocio” and the debts; will notify “D. Ant.o de Mena suegro del referido Ocio” so the children of the first marriage may appear at the concurso de acreedores; the mine administrator to allow the widow the two thirds previously set for her and the children's maintenance; the estate bears clerk, paper and postage costs, “pero que el sarg.to Lopez es acreedor á que se le gratifique, pues ha formado los inventarios.”

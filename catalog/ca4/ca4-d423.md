# Heading: 'Goycoechea á Fages sobre demanda de unos recibos'. Verbatim: 'Queda entendido de exigir constancia de los coma

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n314** · Sav p. 314 [stamp] / 129 [hand] · tomo p. p. 391 · 1788-04-03 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 'Goycoechea á Fages sobre demanda de unos recibos'. Verbatim: 'Queda entendido de exigir constancia de los comandantes de las embarcaciones de lo que le hayan de entregar que conduzcan del Presº de Monterey'. Gloss: understands that he is to require written receipts from the commanders of the vessels for whatever they are to hand over to him that they carry from the presidio of Monterey. Note the date jumps back to April 1788 after the December entries; see issues.

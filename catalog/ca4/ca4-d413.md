# spans n309-n310. Heading: 'Goycoechea á Fages sobre castigo á un soldado'. Verbatim: 'Dice qe está enterado del castigo 

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n309–n310** · Sav p. 309 [stamp] / 124 [hand] · tomo p. p. 379 · 1788-12-24 · Savage abstract · 

spans n309-n310. Heading: 'Goycoechea á Fages sobre castigo á un soldado'. Verbatim: 'Dice qe está enterado del castigo qe le previene imponga al soldº Feyjoo, pero qe habiendo resultado ser una pura sospecha, suspende el castigo hta qe se resuelva como sea de su agrado.' Gloss: acknowledges the order to punish the soldier Feyjoo, but the charge having turned out to be mere suspicion he suspends the punishment until Fages rules as he pleases. Company commander declining to execute a governor's punishment order on evidentiary grounds.

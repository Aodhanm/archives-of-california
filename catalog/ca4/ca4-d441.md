# Verbatim heading: '1788 En.o 31 Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Fages Envio de Documentos' [Savage: '1788 En.º 31 Sta Barb.a /

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n307** · Sav p. 307 / 122 · tomo p. pp. 372-373 · 1788-01-31 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 En.o 31 Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Fages Envio de Documentos' [Savage: '1788 En.º 31 Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea á Fages Envio de Documentos']. English gloss: Goycoechea to Fages, dispatch of documents. Substance: encloses the Estractos de revista and Estados de fuerza corresponding to December last, and the hojas de servicio of the officers of this Company to the end of last year; likewise the Relacion de temperamentos of this jurisdiction, and the Estado showing the existence of livestock of all species and the number of harvests raised by the pobladores y vecinos of the pueblo de la Reina de los Angeles. NOTE: same date (31 Jan 1788) as the already-catalogued n308 record ('Pesquisa del neofito Domingo', pp. 374-376) but a different document with its own heading and its own page cite.

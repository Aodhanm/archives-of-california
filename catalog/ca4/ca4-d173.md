# continuation: re Lasso's mishandling/insufficiency of accounts of Pres. del canal [Sta Barbara], impossibility given Zúñ

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n116** · Sav p. 115 dup /116 · tomo p. p.(cont. pp.316–318) · 1787 Jun 4 · Savage abstract · 

continuation: re Lasso's mishandling/insufficiency of accounts of Pres. del canal [Sta Barbara], impossibility given Zúñiga's command; separates that command from Ortega whose accounts unclear; Soler swears as eyewitness Lasso did not understand accounts he rendered. Proposes for the habilitación of Sta Barbara Alf. HERMENEGILDO SAL ('Propone para la habilitación de Sta Barbara al Alf.z Hermenegildo Sal, pudiendo ocupar su lugar Dn Ramón Laso'), b/c with Goycoechea's instruction Fages could command 2º of Monterey; considers both suitable. Sal responsible for having delivered habilitación of Pres. de San Fco to Ramón Lasso

# spans n266-n267. Savage heading: 'Ajuste de un licenciado'. Encloses the settlement drawn up for the discharged soldier 

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n266** · Sav p. 266 [stamp] / 81 [hand] · tomo p. p. 241 · 1788-03-26 · Savage abstract · 

spans n266-n267. Savage heading: 'Ajuste de un licenciado'. Encloses the settlement drawn up for the discharged soldier José Alejandro Solis, who asked to go to Loreto, which Zúñiga granted pending Fages's approval. Verbatim: 'Acompaño copia del ajuste que se le ha formado al licenciado José Alejandro Solis' and 'Juan Alvarez está licenciado en el mismo dia de Solis'. Two named discharged soldiers.

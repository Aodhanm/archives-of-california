# Short dated ruling with no place and no correspondents in the heading, following directly on the China-goods letter. Ver

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n297** · Sav p. 297 [stamp] / 112 [hand] · tomo p. p. 341 · 1788-09-30 · Savage abstract · 

Short dated ruling with no place and no correspondents in the heading, following directly on the China-goods letter. Verbatim in full: 'No satisface lo espuesto y no se debe cargar á la tropa'. Gloss: the explanation given does not satisfy, and the cost is not to be charged to the troop. Savage marks it with 'o'. Almost certainly Fages' answer, but Savage does not say so.

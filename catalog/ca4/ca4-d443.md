# Verbatim heading: '1788 Abril 9 - Santa Bárbara / El Ayudante Inspor Nicolas Soler al sargento Pablo Cota sobre ocupacio

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n203** · Sav p. n203 stamp 203 / hand 18 · tomo p. p 45 · 1788-04-09 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 Abril 9 - Santa Bárbara / El Ayudante Inspor Nicolas Soler al sargento Pablo Cota sobre ocupacion de tropa' (Savage abbreviates Inspector as Inspor with a raised or). English gloss: the Ayudante Inspector Nicolás Soler to Sergeant Pablo Cota, on the employment of troops. Whole entry, verbatim: 'Páseme Vm una noticia puntual de las faenas en que se emplea la tropa de su cargo, y de las raciones con que la asiste.' A demand for a precise return of the work details the Santa Bárbara troop is being put to and the rations issued for them. Marked with a small o rather than a + in Savage margin. First of three documents on this leaf.

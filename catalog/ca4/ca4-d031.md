RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n16-n29, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1787. Oct. 23. Arispe. / Com.dte gral Loyola al Gob.or Fages (Monterrey). (Indios)' [= 1787, October 23, Arispe. Commandant General Loyola to Governor Fages (Monterey). (Indians)] Body as written on the leaf: 'Por los oficios de vm. no 435 y 440 de 26 de Junio y 10 de Agosto ultimos quedo enterado de la general tranquilidad que se observa entre los Indios Cristianos y Gentiles que habitan en esa Peninsula.' Loyola states he is informed, by Fages's oficios nos. 435 and 440 of 26 June and 10 August, of the general tranquility observed among the Christian and Gentile Indians inhabiting that peninsula. The subject tag '(Indios)' is underlined in the heading. This belongs to a recurring 1787 series of such acknowledgments in this volume, but it is a distinct instance: the catalogued neighbours cite different oficio numbers and different dates (d34 = 13 Sep 1787, oficios 421/423, orig p.70; d41 = 20 Jun 1787, oficio 411, orig p.88). I did not find the word 'Peninsula' glossed on the leaf itself, so I do not assert which peninsula is meant.

# spans n269-n270. Savage heading: 'sobre un pleito'. Acknowledging Hidalgo's oficio on the suit between Josefa M.a Cota a

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n269–n270** · Sav p. 269 [stamp] / 84 [hand] · tomo p. p. 250 · 1788-02-20 · Savage abstract · 

spans n269-n270. Savage heading: 'sobre un pleito'. Acknowledging Hidalgo's oficio on the suit between Josefa M.a Cota and the soldier J. Ygn.o Higuera and the sentence Hidalgo passed. Verbatim: 'digo q.e arrestaré al sold.o y q.e no se proceda á la censura interin doy parte al Gob.dor'. Arrillaga arrests the soldier but suspends the ecclesiastical censure until he reports to the governor.

# Verbatim heading: '1788 Oct.re 3 Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Fages Falta de un documento' [Savage: 'Goycoechea á Fages Fal

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n306** · Sav p. 306 / 121 · tomo p. p. 367 · 1788-10-03 · verbatim transcription · 

Verbatim heading: '1788 Oct.re 3 Sta Barb.a / Goycoechea a Fages Falta de un documento' [Savage: 'Goycoechea á Fages Falta de un documento']. English gloss: Goycoechea to Fages, a missing document. Substance, quoted in full: 'Acompaña la copia que faltaba de las facturas venidas de México y S. Blas' [Encloses the copy that was missing of the invoices come from Mexico and San Blas]. Directly continues the invoice business already recorded on n304 (Goycoechea, 1 Oct 1788, p. 360, in the export).

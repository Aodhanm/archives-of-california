# Spans n298-n299. Completion of the presidio quadrangle. Verbatim: 'Participa haberse cerrado el cuadro de este Presidio 

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n298–n299** · Sav p. 298 [stamp] / 113 [hand] · tomo p. p. 346 · 1788-10-21 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n298-n299. Completion of the presidio quadrangle. Verbatim: 'Participa haberse cerrado el cuadro de este Presidio y concluido sus techos de teja, y haber puesto interinam.te puntales á las casas'. Gloss: reports that the quadrangle of this presidio has been closed and its tile roofs finished, and that props have been set temporarily to the houses. Dated construction datum for the Santa Bárbara presidio.

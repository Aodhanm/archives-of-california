# 'Cristianos huidos en la Ranchería de Calandarruca [Calandarruca/Calanderruca]. Pasos prudentes para la captura del capi

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n141** · Sav p. 142/139 · tomo p. pp.434–436 · 1787 Abril 10 · Savage abstract · 

'Cristianos huidos en la Ranchería de Calandarruca [Calandarruca/Calanderruca]. Pasos prudentes para la captura del capitancillo de allí.' [Christian [Indian] fugitives in the ranchería of Calandarruca; prudent measures to capture the little-captain there]. On consequence of damages [daños] in the ganado made by the gentiles del llano & another band of the río, after execution of rodeos, soldiers who did it apprehended a punta [herd], took a prisoner in the guardia. In view of instructions to have much prudence with the Indios, Soler disposed the Pres. sent 8 men to the ranchería of Calandarruca, guided by the Indios prisoners, 'á fin de que asegurando el 2º [Christian] al capitancillo, condescendiese á ese fin'; would obsequiar & intimidate the pena to which exposed if he opposed retiring the daños del ganado & consenting other cristianos fugitivos return to their ranchería. La partida regresó el día siguiente augmentada de 7 Indios de la misión del Carmelo & 5 cristianos y algunos gentiles; last mando el largo para conseguir confianza in the capitancillo; 5 cristianos presented voluntarily á su misión, further prevented by lluvias

# Savage heading: 'Publicacion de cierto bando'. Reports the bando has been published at this presidio. Verbatim: 'Se ha p

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n263** · Sav p. 263 [stamp] / 78 [hand] · tomo p. p. 230 · 1788-04-19 · Savage abstract · 

Savage heading: 'Publicacion de cierto bando'. Reports the bando has been published at this presidio. Verbatim: 'Se ha publicado en este Pres.o el bando q.e trata sobre la estraccion de reos refugiados á sagrado' [= the removal of criminals who have taken sanctuary in a church]. Touches the church-asylum / immunidad eclesiástica question at a frontier presidio.

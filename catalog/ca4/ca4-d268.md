# On a pay deduction against Alférez Hermenegildo Sal. Verbatim in full: 'En los sucesivos ajustes que le haga Vm. al alfé

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n226** · Sav p. 226 [stamp] / 41 [hand] · tomo p. p. 121 · 1788-07-13 · Savage abstract · 

On a pay deduction against Alférez Hermenegildo Sal. Verbatim in full: 'En los sucesivos ajustes que le haga Vm. al alférez D. Hermeneg.do Sal le ha de descontar los dos tércios de paga que tengo mandado deje en abono de su descubierto.' [In future settlements with Alférez Sal, deduct the two-thirds of pay Fages has ordered withheld against his shortfall.] Directly relevant to Sal's habilitado accounts. Marginal mark: +

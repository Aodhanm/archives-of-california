# Heading: 'Soler á Fages sobre revision de unas cuentas'. Full first-person copy. Acknowledges Fages' order of 14 June no

**C-A 4 (Provincial State Papers), scan n239** · Sav p. 239 [stamp] / 54 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 160-164 · 1788-07-05 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 'Soler á Fages sobre revision de unas cuentas'. Full first-person copy. Acknowledges Fages' order of 14 June not to delay the 'Rev.ta del Pres.o de Sta Barbara'; asks for the comprobantes because his approval fell 'sin el conocimiento todo'. On the habilitado's summary: 'resultándole unos 400 p.s de alcances', larger in the accounts because a charge for covering a mission's private debt was omitted where it 'debió ser Data'. Maxim: 'De cuentas malas por unos mismos comprobantes, resúmen malo'.

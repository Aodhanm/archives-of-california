**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n120** · Sav p. leaf ink corner 43 · tomo p. p.leaf printed stamp 121; document's own cite p. 429-30 · 1839-02-28 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1839. Feb. 28. Mej. - / Pavon al admor de la adª de Monterey. / Contrabando.' [= Warning on the brig Pearl, out of Liverpool, from the Mexican consul there] Transcribes a communication from the Mexican consul at Liverpool on the sailing of the brig Pearl from that port for Iztapa and San Blas. The consul says the cargo, quoted by Savage, 'no es inferior en su valor á los anteriores qe se han introducido clandestinamte en aquel puerto y el de Mazatlan'. Pavón tells him to keep watch on that vessel. A named-ship contraband warning reaching Monterey; a candidate for the ship registry.

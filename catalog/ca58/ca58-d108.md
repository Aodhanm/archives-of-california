[= Decree on payment of the extraordinary levy (six copies filed)] Decree on payment of the arbitrio extraordinario, filed in six copies. It sits directly upstream of the already-catalogued d79 (Echeverria, 19 Nov 1838, on the recaudacion of the extraordinary four-million arbitrio, n101), so the two belong to one fiscal sequence of which only the later half is catalogued.

# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n11

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n129** · Sav p. leaf ink corner 52 · tomo p. p.leaf printed stamp 130; document's own cite p.p. 491-2 · 1839-05-11 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1839. Mayo 11. Mejº / Pavon al admor de la Adª marª de Mont. / Vigilancia sobre las aduanas.' [= The interim President requiring superior chiefs to watch over customhouse employees] Transcribed in full. By an order of the 8th the Ministro de Hacienda copied to him what the Ministro de Guerra y Marina had said on the 6th: even though by the natural order the gefes superiores de Hacienda must watch over the maritime customhouses and all employees of that branch, the interim President wants a particular prevention made that the superior chiefs watch over those employees, requesting whatever information they need, and correcting any fault they notice in the distribution of funds and in their respective management.

# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n151** · Sav p. 152 · tomo p. p. 666 · 1841-02-20 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n132-n151, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '1841. Feb. 20. Mej. / Echeverria - . Decreto prohibiendo la introduccion de hilazas a la Republica.' [= Echeverria. Decree prohibiting the importation of thread into the Republic.] Protectionist decree barring hilazas (spun thread) from entry into the Republic. Followed on the same leaf by 'Un duplicado ... p. 667-8'. This is one of the two items the band record actually names in its summary, which shows the band summary was built from the last leaf and not from the span.

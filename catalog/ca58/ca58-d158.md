# Recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit — no record existed: this document lay inside a grouped entry spanning n11

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n127** · Sav p. 128 [stamp] / 50 [ink] · tomo p. p. 480-1 · 1839-01-17 · Savage abstract · 

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n117-n131, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '1839. En. 17. Mej. / Mariano Calderon - Circular á los adminisres de rentas - Guias y Tornaguias.' [= Calderón's circular calling in the tornaguías of expired guías] Asks that within one month the respective tornaguías of the guías the administrators have issued, whose second term has already expired, be in his office, or failing that the due proofs that the payment of the corresponding duties has been charged to them in that office, in conformity with article 26 of the decree that established the Inspección de Rentas.

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n28-n39, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ Agosto 27. p p = 422 = 3.'

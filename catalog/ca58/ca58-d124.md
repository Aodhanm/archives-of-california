# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n116** · Sav p. leaf ink corner 39. · tomo p. p.leaf printed stamp 117; document's own cite p. 394, with sub-entries to p. 402 · 1838-03-22 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n101-n116, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1838. Mzo 22. Mej. / Pavon al gefe sup.r de Hac.a de Cal.s, Mont. / Mzo 22. Remite papel sellado. p. 394.' [= 1838 - March 22 - Mexico. Pavon to the superior treasury chief of the Californias, Monterey. March 22. He forwards stamped paper.] Heads a clustered entry. Savage then lists under it, each with its own date and cite: 'Feb. 20... p. 395-6 / Asunto identico'; 'Marzo 24. p. 397. Avisando que se deben habilitar los libros p.a las cuentas de las oficinas recaudadoras del Dept.o respectivas al decimo quinto ano economico que comenzara en Julio 1o del presente ano.' (notice that the account books of the Department's collecting offices must be made ready for the fifteenth economic year beginning 1 July of the present year); and 'Abril 7. p. 398-402. Disposic.s super.s sobre la introduccion de generos de algodon, lana y sedas y rebozos.' (superior orders on the importation of cotton, wool and silk goods and rebozos). The 24 March item is the anos economicos material that d66's summary claims, but it sits on n116, outside d66's cited page range.

# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n149** · Sav p. 150 · tomo p. pp. 652-3 · 1841-12-27 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n132-n151, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1841. Dic. 27. Mej. / Pavon al admor de la aduana mar.a de Mont.' [= Pavon to the administrator of the maritime customhouse of Monterey.] Encloses two copies of the decree issued by the Ministerio de Justicia y Instruccion publica dated 13 October last, ordering that all tribunals and juzgados of every class and category are obliged to state the Ley, Canon or Doctrina on which they ground their sentences. Substantively this is a judicial-reasoning mandate reaching California through the customs channel, relevant to the Alcalde Rule work: it is a direct central-government requirement that judgments be reasoned from cited law.

# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n150** · Sav p. 151 · tomo p. pp. 656-9 · 1841-12-16 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n132-n151, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1841. Dic. 16. Mej. / Trigueros, M.tro de Hac.a / Decreto, extinguiendo los empleos de Gefes superiores de Hac.a' [= Trigueros, Minister of the Treasury. Decree abolishing the posts of superior chiefs of the Treasury.] Decree suppressing the office of gefe superior de Hacienda. This matters for California administrative history because the immediately preceding item on n149 is addressed to precisely that officer in the Department of California, so the two entries together show the post being used and then abolished within the same year.

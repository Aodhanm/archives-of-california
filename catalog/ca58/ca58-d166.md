# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n11

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n131** · Sav p. leaf ink corner 54 · tomo p. p.leaf printed stamp 132; page cite not visible on this leaf, the text continues past n131 · 1839-08-24 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1839. Ag.º 24. Mejico. / J. Y. Pavon, director gl de Rentas al admor de la adª marª de Mont. Renta de Tabacos.' [= Pavón as director general of Rentas requiring a second monthly return on foreign tobacco consumption duties] Transcribed in full. On what the Director of the Tabacos enterprise stated in an oficio of the 10th of that month, the dirección general has resolved that the maritime and frontier customhouses of the Republic be advised that, besides the monthly return already ordered of the import duties caused by foreign tobaccos under the supreme order of 1 February last, contained in that same dirección's circular numbered (3/0) of the same date, they also form and remit in like terms another statement showing the amount of the consumption duties caused by those foreign tobaccos at the time of their introduction, in conformity with the law of 2 April 1831. This document runs past the band's last leaf into the span of the next record, d89.

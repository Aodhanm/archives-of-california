# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n140** · Sav p. 141 · tomo p. pp. 555-6 · 1840-02-26 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n132-n151, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1840. Feb. 26. Mej. / Echeverria, M.tro de Hac.a / Decreto que principia: En la cuenta de valores de la Hac.a publica, los estados particulares y el general, distinguiran los sueldos de los otros gastos de administracion.....' [= Echeverria, Minister of the Treasury. Decree beginning: In the account of revenues of the public Treasury, the particular statements and the general one shall distinguish salaries from the other costs of administration.] Accounting decree separating salaries from other administrative expenditure in Treasury revenue statements. Recorded chiefly as proof that the band's stated cite is wrong: this leaf sits eight leaves inside the span n132-n151, yet its pages are 555-6, well below the band's stated floor of 605.

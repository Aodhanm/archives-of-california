# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n28

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n38** · Sav p. printed 39 / handwritten 39. · tomo p. pp. 431-4 · 1826-05-23 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n28-n39, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1826. = Mayo 23 = México. / Ramos Arizpe, Ministro de Justicia. = Decreto. (Por duplicados.' [= 1826, May 23, Mexico. Ramos Arizpe, Minister of Justice. Decree. (In duplicate.) The closing parenthesis is not written on the leaf.] Abstract as written: 'Que la Suprema Corte ejerza las atribuciones de la ley de 9 de Octubre de 812 concernientes á las Audiencias de Ultramar.' That the Supreme Court exercise the powers, assigned by the law of 9 October 1812 to the overseas Audiencias, that is, the transfer of colonial-era appellate jurisdiction to the republic's Supreme Court. Distinct from the 14 February 1826 Arizpe decree on n26.

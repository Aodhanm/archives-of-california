# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record 

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n69** · Sav p. — · tomo p. p.270-271 · 1829-03-16 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n53-n71, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1829. Marzo 16. Mexº. / Aviso al publico sobre tabacos. / (2 docs) p. 270.-1.' [= 1829, March 16, Mexico. Notice to the public concerning tobacco. (2 documents.)] Two documents on the tobacco monopoly/notice, and the first 1829-dated entry in the whole run. Its position is the evidentiary hinge for the boundary finding: it proves 1829 begins on n69, not n72.

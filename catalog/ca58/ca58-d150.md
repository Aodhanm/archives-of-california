RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1839. Mej. / Pavon al admor de la adª de Mont. / Dic. 30. y Dic. 2. - (2 decs) p. 450-3. / Remision de documtos (ya estraidos) / Nov. 6. p. 454-5. / Sin interes / Oct. 26. p. 456. / Resolucn del presidte, ser nulo el libramto de 40 mil pesos dado al francez J. Augº Rollan, los cuales pretende Rollan le habrian sido tomados por la fuerza por el gefe sublevado José Urrea, en Sta Anna de Tamaulipas.' [= A composite Pavón entry of four sub-dated items, the last voiding the 40,000 peso draft to the Frenchman Rollan] Four sub-entries under one heading. The 26 October item is substantive: the President's resolution that the draft of 40,000 pesos given to the Frenchman J. Augusto Rollan is null, which sums Rollan claims had been taken from him by force by the rebel chief José Urrea at Santa Anna de Tamaulipas. This is the Tamaulipas rebellion material the record's star gestures at but does not cover.

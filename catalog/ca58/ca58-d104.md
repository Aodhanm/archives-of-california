**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n94** · Sav p. 95 [stamp] / 17 [ink] · tomo p. p. 206 · 1836-07-19 · Savage abstract · 

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n89-n100, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1836. Julio 19. Mej. - / J. de la Fuente al Jefe politº. de A. Cal. / Sobre modo de abonarse las donativas para los gastos de guerra contra los sublevados de Tejas. - p. 206.' [= On the manner in which the donations for the war expenses against the Texas rebels are to be credited] Addressed specifically to the Jefe Político of Alta California, on how the war donativos for the Texas campaign are to be credited.

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n26** · Sav p. printed 27 / handwritten 27. · tomo p. pp. 316-18 · 1826-02-14 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n16-n27, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1826 = Febrero 14 = México. / Ramos Arizpe, Ministro de Justicia al Jefe Politico de Ambas Californias.' [= 1826, February 14, Mexico. Ramos Arizpe, Minister of Justice, to the Jefe Politico of Both Californias.] Abstract as written: 'Decreto, dando el tratamiento de Excelencia al Presidente de la Suprema Corte, y el de Señoría á sus ministros y Fiscales, etc.' Decree granting the style of Excelencia to the President of the Supreme Court and that of Señoria to its ministers and fiscales. Addressed to California's own jefe politico, so it is a California-received document.

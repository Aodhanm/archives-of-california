RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n117-n131, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: 'o 1838. Mayo 31. Mej. / Pedro Echeverria, presidte del Banco Nacional al gefe superr de Hac.ª del Depto de Cals. / Contabilidad' [= Echeverría, president of the Banco Nacional, seeking a return of unpaid active credits] Transcribed in full.

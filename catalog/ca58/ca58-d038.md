# STRUCTURAL TITLE LEAF, no document. Reads: 'Superior Government State Papers / Benicia / Vol. 8. XXI / Decrees & Despatc

**C-A 58 (Superior Government State Papers, Benicia), scan n78** · Sav p. 1. (ink) / 79 (stamp) · tomo p. p.[none - title leaf] · [section covers] 1831-1841 · Savage abstract · 

STRUCTURAL TITLE LEAF, no document. Reads: 'Superior Government State Papers / Benicia / Vol. 8. XXI / Decrees & Despatches / 1831 - 1841. / Sturtz 10'. Both the arabic 8 and the roman XXI are struck through. 'Sturtz 10' [reading uncertain, possibly Shurtz] is written and underlined at the foot. The ink foliation RESETS here from 19 to 1; the Bancroft stamp continues unbroken (78 to 79). On this leaf alone the ink number sits ABOVE the stamp rather than below it.

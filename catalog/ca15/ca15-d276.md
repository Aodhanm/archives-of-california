# 24 sailors of the San Blas Departamento de Marina served as laborers (sirvientes) at Missions San Buenaventura & Santa B

**C-A 15 (Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n203** · Sav p. 198 · tomo p. 392 · 1786-12-31 · Savage abstract · 

24 sailors of the San Blas Departamento de Marina served as laborers (sirvientes) at Missions San Buenaventura & Santa Bárbara and the SB presidio this year; the habilitado supplied them effects worth $1,403.5.2; 6 returned to San Blas in October. Orig. p.392.

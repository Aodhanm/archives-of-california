# Serra certifies the burial in the presidio chapel of Alférez Dn Mariano Carrillo, Habilitado of the company, native of L

**C-A 15 (Provincial State Papers, Benicia (Military)), scan n133** · Sav p. 131 · tomo p. 710 · 1782-01-28 · Savage abstract · 

Serra certifies the burial in the presidio chapel of Alférez Dn Mariano Carrillo, Habilitado of the company, native of Loreto, who died on the afternoon of the 27th [Jan 1782]. (Confirms Carrillo's death date; cf. Doc 157.) Orig. p.710.

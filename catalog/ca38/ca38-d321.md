# Circular of the appointment of Don Pedro Fernandez del Castillo as Ministro Tesorero General, Don Antonio Mª de Esnaurri

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n114** · Sav p. 114 [stamp] / 113 [hand] · tomo p. p 723. · 1846-01-15 · Savage abstract · 

Circular of the appointment of Don Pedro Fernandez del Castillo as Ministro Tesorero General, Don Antonio Mª de Esnaurrizar having been separated from that post. Verbatim: 'Circular del nombramiento de Ministro Tesorero General á Dn Pedro Fernandez del Castillo por separacion de dicho empleo Dn Antonio Mª de Esnaurrizar.' IDENTIFIES the A. M. Esnaurrizar who signs the 1845 circulars on n107-n110.

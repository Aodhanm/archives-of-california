# Order to the Department's revenue collectors to press collection of debts owed the treasury and pay over the treasury's 

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n107** · Sav p. 107 [stamp] / 106 [hand] · tomo p. p. 681. · 1845-10-01 · Savage abstract · 

Order to the Department's revenue collectors to press collection of debts owed the treasury and pay over the treasury's share. Verbatim: 'Orden Circular á los recaudadores del Departamento cobren las deudas del Erario y las enteren en la Tesorería la parte que le toque.' Gloss: circular to departmental recaudadores to collect Erario debts and remit the portion due to the Tesorería.

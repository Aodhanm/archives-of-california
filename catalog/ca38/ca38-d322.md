# Cross-reference note in place of an abstract: this circular is already abstracted at p. 719-20, foja 112 of this book. V

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n114** · Sav p. 114 [stamp] / 113 [hand] · tomo p. p. 724. · 1846-01-15 · Savage abstract · 

Cross-reference note in place of an abstract: this circular is already abstracted at p. 719-20, foja 112 of this book. Verbatim: 'Esta Circular esta estractada en la p. 719-20 foja 112 de este libro.' ARCHIVAL APPARATUS: proves the smaller ink hand number in the top corner (112 on leaf n113) is the volume's own foja series used for internal cross-references, distinct from the original document pagination.

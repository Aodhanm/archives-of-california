**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n105** · Sav p. 105 [stamp] / 104 [hand] · tomo p. p.p. 668.-9, · 1845-09-10 · Savage abstract · 

'Circular en que se ordena el numero de acemilas que hande tener los Cuerpos del Ejercito' with a table: 'Infanteria .... 598 Mulas / Caballeria .... 517 Yd. / Total .... 1,515, mulas.' [= fixing the pack-mule establishment of the army corps]. NB the arithmetic does not close: 598 + 517 = 1,115, but the total is written 1,515 [?].

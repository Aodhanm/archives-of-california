# Circular in which the President orders the Treasurer to press collection of all outstanding sums AND to ascertain the ex

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n109** · Sav p. 109 [stamp] / 108 [hand] · tomo p. p p 695 - 6. · 1845-11-08 · Savage abstract · 

Circular in which the President orders the Treasurer to press collection of all outstanding sums AND to ascertain the existence of estates, lands, capital and any other property that may belong to the treasury. Verbatim: 'averigue la existencia de fincas, terrenos, Capitales, y Cualquiera otra propiedad que pueda Corresponder al Erario.' Gloss: an order to inventory national-treasury real property in Alta California on the eve of the war.

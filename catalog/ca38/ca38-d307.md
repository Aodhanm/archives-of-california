# Circular resolving the query on deducting a quarter part from employees' salaries, in view of what the heads of offices 

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n110** · Sav p. 110 [stamp] / 109 [hand] · tomo p. p. p. 700 701. · 1845-12-05 · Savage abstract · 

Circular resolving the query on deducting a quarter part from employees' salaries, in view of what the heads of offices have represented. Verbatim: 'resolviendo la consulta relativa á la deduccion de una 4ª parte á los sueldos de los empleados, con presencia de cuanto han espuesto los gefes de oficinas.' Gloss: 25 percent salary withholding, contested by office heads.

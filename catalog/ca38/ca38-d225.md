# 'Que se previno al Admor de la Aduana de Veracruz y Tamaulipas que las noticias de Cargamentos de Esportacion, la remita

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n78** · Sav p. 78 [stamp] / 77 [hand] · tomo p. pp. 494. 5. · 1844-05-18 · Savage abstract · 

'Que se previno al Admor de la Aduana de Veracruz y Tamaulipas que las noticias de Cargamentos de Esportacion, la remitan á los Consules respectivos pr los Vapores' [= the customs administrators of Veracruz and Tamaulipas were directed to forward export-cargo returns to the respective consuls by the steamers]. Consular reporting and steam-packet routing.

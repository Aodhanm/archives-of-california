# President orders that all stamped paper, of whatever class, be received from the government engraver at the Sello office

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n107** · Sav p. 107 [stamp] / 106 [hand] · tomo p. p.p. 682.-3. · 1845-10-15 · Savage abstract · 

President orders that all stamped paper, of whatever class, be received from the government engraver at the Sello office with the national arms engraved and the seal blank. Verbatim: 'El Presidente dispone que todo el papel sellado sea de la Clase que fuere, se recibirá del Mismo del grabador del Gobno, en la oficina del Sello, grabadas las armas nacionales con el Sello en Blanco.'

# Circular inserting the Treasurer of Puebla's communication that the Administrator of Tlalpan [?] disposed of 220 pesos b

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n110** · Sav p. 110 [stamp] / 109 [hand] · tomo p. p. 702. · 1845-12-13 · Savage abstract · 

Circular inserting the Treasurer of Puebla's communication that the Administrator of Tlalpan [?] disposed of 220 pesos belonging to the Créditos branch, and ordering such abuses to cease. Verbatim: 'el Admor de Tlalpan dispuso de 220$ pertenecientes al ramo de Créditos y se ordena cesen esos abusos.' Place-name reading uncertain.

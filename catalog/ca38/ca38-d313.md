# Circular by which the President restores the general offices of the Hacienda to the full exercise of their functions, an

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n112** · Sav p. 112 [stamp] / 111 [hand] · tomo p. p 710. · 1846-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

Circular by which the President restores the general offices of the Hacienda to the full exercise of their functions, and provides that no post be filled without a prior spontaneous nomination from the office concerned. Verbatim: 'restablece á las Oficinas generales de Hacienda en el pleno ejercicio de sus funciones, y que no se provea empleo ningº sin previa propuesta espontanea de dcha oficina.' Follows the Paredes coup.

# Asks for reports on a petition of Ygº Serra [?], who seeks payment from the Supreme Government of certain sums he specif

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n108** · Sav p. 108 [stamp] / 107 [hand] · tomo p. pp 691-2. · 1845-10-28 · Savage abstract · 

Asks for reports on a petition of Ygº Serra [?], who seeks payment from the Supreme Government of certain sums he specifies. Verbatim: 'Pide informes sobre una peticion de Ygº Serra, solicitando del Gobno Supo que se le paguen ciertas cantidades qe espresa.' Named claimant against the treasury, routed through the California commissary. Surname reading uncertain (Serra / Berra).

# Communication directing that the number of meritorios be increased so that henceforth this grade be the starting point f

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n113** · Sav p. 113 [stamp] / 112 [hand] · tomo p. pp 719. - 20. · 1846-01-13 · Savage abstract · 

Communication directing that the number of meritorios be increased so that henceforth this grade be the starting point for placements or promotions as they occur. Verbatim: 'previene se aumente el numero de meritorios para que en lo succesivo este sea el principio de las colocaciones ó accensos que ocurrieren.' Gloss: unpaid apprentice clerks made the entry rung of the civil service ladder.

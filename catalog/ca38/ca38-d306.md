# Circular notifying that, recovered from his illnesses, he resumes charge of the Dirección General de Alcabalas y Contrib

**C-A 38 (Departmental State Papers, Benicia), scan n109** · Sav p. 109 [stamp] / 108 [hand] · tomo p. p 698. · 1845-12-04 · Savage abstract · 

Circular notifying that, recovered from his illnesses, he resumes charge of the Dirección General de Alcabalas y Contribuciones Directas. Verbatim: 'restablecido de sus enfermedades, vuelve á encargarse de la Direccion gral de Alcabalas y Contribs directas.' Note recipient is the Departmental Treasurer at La Paz (Baja California).

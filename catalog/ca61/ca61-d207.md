RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of a band record spanning n216-n245, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Set. 19. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, September 19, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Communication from the Sub-Prefectura of the Partido de Santa Barbara enclosing the actas of the primary and secondary elections for naming deputies to Congress and to the Assembly. Botello, president of the commission drafting the Reglamento de las fuerzas, asks for the supreme disposition authorizing the Governor to name the chiefs and officers of those forces. The Governor presents a proclamation urging the inhabitants of the Department to present themselves for enlistment. Runs n228-n229.

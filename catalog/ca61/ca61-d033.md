# '1836 Jun. 16 Monterey / Alvarado P.e Maldonado Srio'. Today the Junta Dep.l approved that in place of the words 'todos 

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n25** · Sav p. 21 / 20 · tomo p. p. 143-6 · 1836-06-16 · Savage abstract · 

'1836 Jun. 16 Monterey / Alvarado P.e Maldonado Srio'. Today the Junta Dep.l approved that in place of the words 'todos los martes, jueves y sábados' in this corporation's reglamento there be substituted 'dos dias que señalará el pres.te al terminar cada sesion' [two days the president will designate at the close of each session].

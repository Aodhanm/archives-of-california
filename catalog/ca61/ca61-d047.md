**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n29** · Sav p. 25 / 24 · tomo p. p. 176-9 · 1836-07-19 · Savage abstract · 

'1836 Jul. 19 Monterey / Chico á la J.ta Dep.l'. It is general and uniform practice in bodies of this class that the presidency fall on the most senior individual among those present at the session whenever by any accident the Jefe Superior Politico does not attend the Junta Departamental.

# 'Documentos repetidos y tachados' - Savage's own note that original Tomo III pp. 421-53 hold only repeated and struck-ou

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n74** · Sav p. 70/69 · tomo p. p. 421-53 · — · Savage abstract · 

'Documentos repetidos y tachados' - Savage's own note that original Tomo III pp. 421-53 hold only repeated and struck-out documents and were therefore not copied. Stands between the Adiciones al Reglamento (ends at the top of n74) and the 1 Apr. 1840 secret session.

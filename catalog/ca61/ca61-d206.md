# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d1

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n240** · Sav p. 238. · tomo p. pp. 305-6 · 1845-10-01 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d187, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Oct. 1.o - Ang.s / (Sesion &.)' [= 1845, October 1, Los Angeles. Session, etc. [second entry under the same date]] This is the ONLY document in the band that the catalog record actually describes, and it is a two-line abstract on a single leaf: 'Se refiere a la eleccion de Senadores de la Republica en num.o de catorce individuos.' The election of fourteen Senators of the Republic. Preceded on the same leaf by Botello's motion to prolong the sessions in order to install the Tribunal Superior, with Pico and Bandini named.

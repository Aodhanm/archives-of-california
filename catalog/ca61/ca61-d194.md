RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d187, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Agosto 29. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, August 29, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading of the Baldios dictamenes: 'Nicasio', stated at sixteen sitios de ganado mayor, adjudicated to citizens Pablo de la Guerra and Juan Cooper, and 'Nasagui' adjudicated to citizen Raimundo Carrillo, both approved in general. The special commission's dictamen on Figueroa's Clausura de Sesiones proposition is approved by majority. Leave is granted to deputies resident outside the city to withdraw for the rest of the year once the election of Senators due on 1 October is made and the Tribunal de Justicia del Departamento is installed.

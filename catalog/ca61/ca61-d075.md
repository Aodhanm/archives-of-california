# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n61

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n71** · Sav p. leaf corner 67/66 · tomo p. p. 412 5/8 · 1840-12-20 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 3 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside a band record spanning n61-n72, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1840 Dic. 20 Sta Barb.a / Nicolas Den, médico' [= 1840, 20 December, Santa Bárbara. Nicolás Den, physician.] One-line entry in the middle of n71: 'Certifica la enfermedad de Anast.o Carrillo', that is, Den certifies Anastasio Carrillo's illness. It is the supporting medical certificate filed behind Carrillo's excuses in the heading immediately above. Den appears elsewhere in the catalog (C-A 30, 34, 35, 42) but has no C-A 61 record at all, so this is a new attestation of him acting as physician in a Junta matter.

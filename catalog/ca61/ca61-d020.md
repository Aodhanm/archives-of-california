# '1836 Mayo 28 Monterey / Raf.l Gomez á Chico'. Refuses the charge of diputado: if the Junta is the Departamental one the

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n19-n20** · Sav p. 15 / 14 ; 16 / 15 · tomo p. p. 108-11 · 1836-05-28 · Savage abstract · 

'1836 Mayo 28 Monterey / Raf.l Gomez á Chico'. Refuses the charge of diputado: if the Junta is the Departamental one the vocales ought to be five, not seven, and named in a different form; and if it is the Diputacion Territorial, he lacks the vecindad required. Ends at the top of n20.

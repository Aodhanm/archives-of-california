# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which 

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n176** · Sav p. copy-volume stamped 172 (second corner 74) · tomo p. p.Savage's own in-text cite at the close: "p.p. 185-86" · 1845-06-25 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845 - Junio 25. - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, June 25, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] A one-leaf session, heading at the top of n176. Second reading of the commission dictamen on the Ortega y Stokes expediente, approved. First reading of the gobernacion dictamen on Miguel Prayer's petition about closing an alley. The President states that, a majority of the members of the Asamblea being now present, the next session should take up naming the quinterna, so as to take advantage of the imminent sailing of a vessel to remit it to the Supreme Government for the purpose of the Department having a Governor. Figueroa states that doubts had arisen in the commission charged with the arrangement of tribunals, and that for that reason it had not presented the proyecto in this session as promised in the last.

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n220** · Sav p. 118. · tomo p. pp. 261-266 · 1845-08-29 · Savage abstract · 

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n216-n245, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Agosto 29. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, August 29, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading of the Baldios dictamenes: 'Nicasio', stated at sixteen sitios de ganado mayor, adjudicated to citizens Pablo de la Guerra and Juan Cooper, and 'Nasagui' adjudicated to citizen Raimundo Carrillo, both approved in general. The special commission's dictamen on Figueroa's Clausura de Sesiones proposition is approved by majority.

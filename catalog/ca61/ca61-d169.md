# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which 

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n159-n160** · Sav p. copy-volume stamped 155-156 (second corner 57-58) · tomo p. p.Savage's own in-text cite at the close: "p.p. 152-55" · 1845-05-21 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. Mayo 21. - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, May 21, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Heading at the top of n159. Second reading and approval of the Warner expediente; second reading of the gobernacion dictamen on the Secretary of the Asamblea's petition, approved. Communication from Diputado Noriega answering the convocatoria, promising to attend despite convalescing. The Comision de terrenos reports on the partition of the parage called Valle de San Jose held by Antº Suñol y socios, four articles approved. The President reports that Juan Bandini, given the commission to confer with Padre N. Duran on the ruinous condition of the Missions in order to prevent the extinction of those establishments, has returned; a vote of thanks to Bandini and to Padre Duran is agreed for their good disposition in cooperating with the Government in the progress of the Department.

# '1845. Enero 28 - Ang.s / Sesion Extraord. de este dia.' Opened at four in the afternoon. The President proposed 'que la

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n131** · Sav p. 127/29. · tomo p. pp. 104-5. · 1845-01-28 · Savage abstract · 

'1845. Enero 28 - Ang.s / Sesion Extraord. de este dia.' Opened at four in the afternoon. The President proposed 'que la Asamblea solicitase de Juan B. Alvarado y José Castro informe circunstanciado del motivo que ha habido para la alteracion publica q.e se nota; y la llegada de ellos á la Cabeza de fuerzas á esta Capital' - approved. Carrillo moved to notify the pueblos by decree that the Asamblea had opened its sessions in this Capital in consequence of the public disturbance - approved.

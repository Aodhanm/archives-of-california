# RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which 

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n157-n158** · Sav p. copy-volume stamped 153-154 (second corner 55-56) · tomo p. p.Savage's own in-text cite at the close: "p.p. 149-52" · 1845-05-19 · Savage abstract · 

RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT 2026-08-27 — this document had NO record: it lay inside the span of band record d166, which satisfied every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Mayo 19 - Angˢ / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, May 19, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading of the timber dictamen, approved. First reading of the Comision de terrenos dictamen approving the grant made to Juan J. Warner of the parage of San Jose by the Colonel of the Department. Petitions of Antº Suñol y socios (division of the tract called Valle de San Jose) and of C. Antº Machado (complaining the Ayuntamiento had stripped him of land in the suburbs of the city of Los Angeles). Then two items of real weight, read verbatim at full resolution on n158: 'Presidᵗᵉ informa a la Asamblea de la pacificacion, de la frontera de Baja California. - De enterado con satisfaccion' and 'El mismo Presidᵗᵉ hizo presente una nota, recivida del Capitan de Puerto de San Diego diciendo haber recivido informes extraoficiales, de que Mexico y las E.U. se habian declarado la guerra, con motivo de la anexion de Texas. - A la Comision de gobernacion.' This is a US-war alarm reaching the Asamblea in MAY 1845, roughly eleven weeks earlier than the August 4 alarm that record d167 is built around, and it arrives by a different channel (the Captain of the Port of San Diego, not the Monterey consul's letters from Mazatlan).

**C-A 61 (Legislative Records), scan n224** · Sav p. 122. · tomo p. pp. 272-74 · 1845-09-10 · Savage abstract · 

Previously uncatalogued — recovered by the 2026-08-27 coverage audit: the document lay inside the declared span of a grouped entry covering n216-n245, which had passed every completeness check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Set. 10. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, September 10, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading and approval of the Comision de Misiones dictamen. Art. 1: before any mission is leased (arriendo) its debts must be paid, and where the mission is exhausted and cannot pay, the Governor is to consult the Rev. P.

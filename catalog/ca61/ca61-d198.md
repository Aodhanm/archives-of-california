RECOVERED BY THE BAND AUDIT WAVE 2 (2026-08-27) — no record existed: this document lay inside the span of band record d187, which passed every coverage check while concealing it. Heading verbatim: '+ 1845. - Set. 10. - Ang.s / (Sesion de este dia.)' [= 1845, September 10, Los Angeles. Session of this day.] Second reading and approval of the Comision de Misiones dictamen. Art. 1: before any mission is leased (arriendo) its debts must be paid, and where the mission is exhausted and cannot pay, the Governor is to consult the Rev. P. Prefecto about covering them from the pious funds. Art. 2: the Governor is to place the missions in good hands, prevent waste and desfalcos, and enforce the responsibility of the guilty.

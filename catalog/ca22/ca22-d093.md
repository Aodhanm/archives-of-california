# Acks the appointment of Brigadier Caballero [Teodoro] de Croix as Comandante General of the Provincias Internas (this pr

**C-A 22 (Provincial Records), scan n67–n68** · Sav p. 67–68 · tomo p. 101 · 1777-06-03 · Savage abstract · 

Acks the appointment of Brigadier Caballero [Teodoro] de Croix as Comandante General of the Provincias Internas (this province + Sonora, Sinaloa, Nueva Vizcaya, Nuevo México, Coahuila, Texas); henceforth to deal with him directly on govt matters, though provisions/effects still to be requested from the Viceroy's government

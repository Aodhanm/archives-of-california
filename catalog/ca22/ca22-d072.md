Cattle-slaughter for troops/sailors (per Sup. Decreto 14 Jul 1774): no vaqueros/running-horses; no meat supplied for >1 yr; ordered a matanza of 50 bulls at the Real de Sta Ana (Nov) — tasajo cost 10 reales/arroba vs 8 from the missions; recommends placing a southern mission's *Temporalidades* under the governor for meat supply *(vide p.137)*

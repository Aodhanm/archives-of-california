# Cattle-increase to soldiers ('que el aumento de ganado á los soldados sea proporcionado á que no abandonen sus obligacio

**C-A 22 (Provincial Records), scan n357** · Sav p. 357 · tomo p. II:560–563 · 1794-09-12 · Savage abstract · 

Cattle-increase to soldiers ('que el aumento de ganado á los soldados sea proporcionado á que no abandonen sus obligaciones'); ⭐⭐ approves the proceeding against a Russian ship-captain ('el capitán Ruso') for carrying off 2 Christian Indian women — an early Russian-contact incident, leaf-verified n357 (Set. 12, 1794); ⭐ orders soldiers not punished with cueras while on sentinel; Mission Sta Cruz relocated to a paraje less exposed to flooding; repair church/warehouses/troop-quarters; ⭐ Sgt agregado José Pérez Fernández named Alférez of S. Francisco (Oct. 16, p.563)

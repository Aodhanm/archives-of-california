# 1794 Mis.n S. Fran.co / Arrillaga á Sal [Arrillaga to Hermenegildo Sal.] Jun. 10, pp. 602-3, verbatim: Que puede tomar c

**C-A 22 (Provincial Records), scan n366** · Sav p. 365 (stamped) / 148 (inked) · tomo p. pp. 602-3 · 1794-06-10 · Savage abstract · 

1794 Mis.n S. Fran.co / Arrillaga á Sal [Arrillaga to Hermenegildo Sal.] Jun. 10, pp. 602-3, verbatim: Que puede tomar clavazon de la pertenec.te al establec.to de Bodega (que ya no se verifica por ahora) p.a el baluarte. The nails set aside for the projected Bodega establishment, now stated not to be going ahead for the present, are released to the San Francisco baluarte. Direct documentary evidence of the abandonment of the Bodega post being converted into materiel for the San Francisco defences.

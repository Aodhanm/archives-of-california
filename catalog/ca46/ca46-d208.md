# Headed 'Miñon Comte. Grãl. de Califs.' With his oficio of 24 Sept. received copy of the Mayor General's despatch announc

**C-A 46 (Departmental Records), scan n163** · Sav p. 162 / 161. · tomo p. p.112 · 1824 Dice. 17 · Savage abstract · 

Headed 'Miñon Comte. Grãl. de Califs.' With his oficio of 24 Sept. received copy of the Mayor General's despatch announcing 'haber sido nombrado pa. Comte. Gl. de ambas Califas. el Sr. Brigr. gdo. Dn. Juan José Miñon', 'lo qe. he comunicado ya á los Comtes. de los 4 Presos.' (Miñón never came; Echeandía was eventually sent instead.)

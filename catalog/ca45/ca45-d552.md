# Verbatim heading: '1836. Nov. 29. Mont. — El Congreso Constituy.te lo nombra Coronel a M. G. Vallejo.' The Congreso Cons

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n199** · Sav p. 198 [stamp] / 19 [ink] · tomo p. p.108 · 1836-11-29 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1836. Nov. 29. Mont. — El Congreso Constituy.te lo nombra Coronel a M. G. Vallejo.' The Congreso Constituyente names Mariano Guadalupe Vallejo Coronel — the revolt-era commission (29 Nov 1836, the day he was made comandante general); pairs with d86's 1838 comandante-general commission and d143's 1842 northern-line command.

# Heading: 'El visitador de mis.s preso.' Orders the juez to take information on the report that the visitador de misiones

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n249** · Sav p. 248 [stamp] / 69 [hw] · tomo p. p. 307 · 1840-05-16 · Savage abstract · 

Heading: 'El visitador de mis.s preso.' Orders the juez to take information on the report that the visitador de misiones William Hartnell has been arrested at San Francisco by the military command: 'Le manda tomar informes sobre la noticia de haber sido apresado el visitador de mis.s, Guillermo Hartnell, en S. Fr.co por orden de la comandancia militar' [= inquiry into the arrest of mission-visitor Hartnell at San Francisco]. Falls in the weeks of the Graham-affair foreigner crackdown.

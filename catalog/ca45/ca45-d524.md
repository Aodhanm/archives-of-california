# Verbatim heading: '+ 1823 - Abril 22 - San Francisco. / Arguello al Ayuntamto de San Jose.' Gloss: 22 April 1823, San Fr

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n7** · Sav p. 7 [stamp] / 6 [ink] · tomo p. pp. 25-26 · 1823-04-22 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ 1823 - Abril 22 - San Francisco. / Arguello al Ayuntamto de San Jose.' Gloss: 22 April 1823, San Francisco, Arguello to the Ayuntamiento of San Jose. SUBSTANCE, verbatim in full as Savage gives it: 'Requiriendo q. se verifique el sorteo ordenado pa los q. deban servir de soldados pa las Companias de Sn. Franco y Monterey' [= requiring that the sorteo ordered be carried out for those who must serve as soldiers for the Companies of San Francisco and Monterey]. Page cite on the same line: pp.25-26. This is the chase after the draft ordered on 8 and 28 March 1823 (entries at pp. 7-12 and pp. 15-16).

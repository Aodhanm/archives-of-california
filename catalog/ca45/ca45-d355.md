# Compelling an unwilling judge to take office. Verbatim: 'Comunica que con fha. 3 de Mzo se le ordenó, q. se hiciere carg

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n379** · Sav p. 376 [stamp] / 11 [hw] · tomo p. p. 50 · 1843-04-29 · Savage abstract · 

Compelling an unwilling judge to take office. Verbatim: 'Comunica que con fha. 3 de Mzo se le ordenó, q. se hiciere cargo del empleo de Juez suplente... que de no verificarlo se le multará en $20 y dará cta al Gobº como en desobediencia.' Gloss: recalls the 3 March order that Guillen assume the post of juez suplente; since he appears to have treated superior orders with contempt he is to receive the office at once, on pain of a $20 fine and report to the Governor for disobedience. Doc 1 of 2 under Savage's '(2 Docs.)' heading; the second is separately dated Junio 16.

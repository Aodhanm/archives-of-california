# A suit over a horse has been laid before him in which Gabriel Alvarado is implicated, so he thinks a confrontation of th

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n318** · Sav p. 316 [stamp] / 14 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 62-3 · 1838-03-15 · Savage abstract · 

A suit over a horse has been laid before him in which Gabriel Alvarado is implicated, so he thinks a confrontation of the parties entirely necessary: “Que se le ha presentado una demanda sobre un caballo y en la cual se halla complicado Gabriel Alvarado, por lo que cree de todo punto un careo.”

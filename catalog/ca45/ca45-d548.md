# Under the standing heading carried from n115, '1833. En.o 29. Mont. / José Figueroa al Alcalde de S. José'. Entry verbat

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n117** · Sav p. 117 [stamp] / 115 [ink] · tomo p. pp.488, 89. · 1833-02-13 · Savage abstract · 

Under the standing heading carried from n115, '1833. En.o 29. Mont. / José Figueroa al Alcalde de S. José'. Entry verbatim: 'F.bro. 13. pp 488. 89. Que ningun individuo bajo ningun pretesto corte arboles de los q.e forman la calzada q.e se dirige á Sta. Clara.' (Savage writes 'pretesto' and 'dirige'.) [= that no individual, under any pretext, cut trees from among those that form the causeway running to Santa Clara.] A tree-protection order for the planted avenue between the pueblo of San José and Mission Santa Clara.

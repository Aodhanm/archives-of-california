# Fuero militar refused against the juez de paz. Verbatim: '...á cuyo Juzgº rehusaba presentarse (Vallejo), alegando á su 

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n380** · Sav p. 377 [stamp] / 12 [hw] · tomo p. p. 67 · 1843-12-22 · Savage abstract · 

Fuero militar refused against the juez de paz. Verbatim: '...á cuyo Juzgº rehusaba presentarse (Vallejo), alegando á su favor el goze del fuero militar... bajo la pena de $50 de multa si no lo verifica.' Gloss: the juez de paz of San José had laid before the Sub-Prefect all documents in the suit between Jesus Vallejo and the apoderado (interlined: D. Guillermo Gulnac) of D. Miguel de Pedrorena, before whose court Vallejo refused to appear, claiming military fuero; he is ordered to appear at nine next morning with all papers, under a $50 fine, with notice to the Governor.

# Because the rancho de las Pulgas falls within his jurisdiction, the plaintiff George must sue Guillermo Smith before HIM

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n317** · Sav p. 315 [stamp] / 13 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 54-5 · 1835-08-05 · Savage abstract · 

Because the rancho de las Pulgas falls within his jurisdiction, the plaintiff George must sue Guillermo Smith before HIM and no one else: “Que por estar el rancho de las Pulgas comprendido en su Jurisdiccion, el Demandante George debe demandar á Guillermo Smith ante él y no ante otra persona.”

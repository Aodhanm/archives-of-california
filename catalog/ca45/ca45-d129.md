# Bando on commerce. Art 1: 'Queda sin ningun efecto el bando de Julio 30 1844 qe prohibe la introduccn a este depto de ef

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n273–n274** · Sav p. 272 [stamp] / 93 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 398-9 · 1845-03-22 · Savage abstract · 

Bando on commerce. Art 1: 'Queda sin ningun efecto el bando de Julio 30 1844 qe prohibe la introduccn a este depto de efectos estrangeros nacionalizados procedentes de los Puertos de la costa del Sur de la Repa' [= the 30 July 1844 ban on nationalised foreign goods coming from the southern Pacific ports of the Republic is revoked]. Art 2 charges the administrador of the Monterey maritime custom-house and the receptores at the other ports with its observance and with the escala and cabotaje laws. spans n273-n274.

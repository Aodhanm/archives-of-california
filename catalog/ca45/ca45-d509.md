# Abril 12 (a second entry of that date, entered out of page order). Verbatim: 'Que siempre q.e salgan vecinos en persecuc

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n120** · Sav p. 120 / 118. · tomo p. pp. 511. 12. · 1833-04-12 · Savage abstract · 

Abril 12 (a second entry of that date, entered out of page order). Verbatim: 'Que siempre q.e salgan vecinos en persecucion de ladrones de caballada q.e lleven las armas y municiones necesarias.' [= whenever vecinos go out in pursuit of horse-thieves they are to carry the necessary arms and munitions.] The armed-vecino posse authorised months before Figueroas November 1833 anti-raiding bando.

# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. VERBATIM HEADING: '1827. En. 22. Mont. /

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n192** · Sav p. stamp 191 / pencil 12 · tomo p. p.p. 60-1 · 1827-01-22 · verbatim transcription · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. VERBATIM HEADING: '1827. En. 22. Mont. / Jose Castro al alc.de de S.n Jose. Terrenos.' GLOSS: lands, and to whom the Monterey ayuntamiento answers. SUBSTANCE (verbatim): 'Le dice que el comd.te g.l le aviso que no daria terrenos a ningun estrangero en lo sucesivo, y que el ayuntam.to de Mont. no reconocera otro Jefe mas q.e el comd.te gral.' Two things in one line: a declared bar on land grants to foreigners, and the Monterey ayuntamiento recognizing no chief but the comandante general, which is a jurisdictional statement worth checking against the Echeandia-era command dispute.

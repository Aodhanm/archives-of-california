# Under the year-head '1843.'; Savage writes the date as '(Sin mes) 8.' - day only, no month. 'En solicitud del extranjero

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n353** · Sav p. 351 [stamp] / 49 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 289-90 · 1843 (Sin mes) 8 - month not recorded by Savage · Savage abstract · 

Under the year-head '1843.'; Savage writes the date as '(Sin mes) 8.' - day only, no month. 'En solicitud del extranjero Juan Stoco para que venga á contestar una demanda.' (requesting the foreigner Juan Stoco to come and answer a suit). Surname read at high magnification as Sto=co, broken across lines; identification uncertain [?].

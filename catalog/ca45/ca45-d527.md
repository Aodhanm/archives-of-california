# Verbatim heading: '+ Julio 28 pp. 35-36' under the same standing block heading '1823 - Monterey / Arguello al Alcalde de

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n9** · Sav p. 9 [stamp] / 8 [ink] · tomo p. pp. 35-36 · 1823-07-28 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '+ Julio 28 pp. 35-36' under the same standing block heading '1823 - Monterey / Arguello al Alcalde de San Jose.' Gloss: 28 July 1823, Monterey, Arguello to the Alcalde of San Jose. SUBSTANCE, Savage verbatim: 'Que haga q. Ysidro Castro y Demecio Dominguez se presenten en Monterey lo mas pronto a responder de sus personas.' [= that he cause Ysidro Castro and Demecio Dominguez to present themselves in Monterey as soon as possible to answer for their persons.] The second given name is written Demecio, not Demetrio; transcribed as it stands.

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n171-n172** · Sav p. stamp 170-171 / pencil 168-169 · tomo p. pp. 715-16 · 1835-03-30 · verbatim transcription · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. VERBATIM HEADING: '1835. Mzo. 30. Los Angeles / Figueroa al Alcalde de S. Jose'. GLOSS: follow-up on the Los Angeles revolt. SUBSTANCE (verbatim): 'Que los principales motores de la revolucion q.e estallo en 7. del corriente se hallan presos y enjuiciados.' The ringleaders of the 7 March rising are imprisoned and under prosecution. Figueroa writes from Los Angeles itself.

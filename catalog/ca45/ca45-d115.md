# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1831. En.o 6 Mont. / 

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n96** · Sav p. 96 [stamp] / 94 [ink] · tomo p. pp 389. 90. · 1831-01-06 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1831. En.o 6 Mont. / Jose M.a de Echeandia, al C. Alcalde Contitucional de S. Jose' (Savage writes 'Contitucional', without the s). 'Que le acompana un egemplar del bando publicado sobre la nueva forma de Administracion en q. han de quedar en lo de adelante las Misiones del territorio.' [= forwards a copy of the published bando on the new form of administration under which the missions of the territory are henceforth to stand.] Echeandia transmitting his January 1831 mission-administration bando to San Jose. Directly pairs with the C-A 45 n105 find (Victoria ordering the suspension of the bando reducing the missions to pueblos, 1 Feb 1831).

# Same standing heading (Victoria to the Alcalde of San José). Entry verbatim: 'Mayo 12. pp 426. Que se concede licencia a

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n107** · Sav p. 107 [stamp] / 105 [ink] · tomo p. pp.426. · 1831-05-12 · Savage abstract · 

Same standing heading (Victoria to the Alcalde of San José). Entry verbatim: 'Mayo 12. pp 426. Que se concede licencia al C. Leandro Flores p.a q.e pasé á Sta. Bárb.a' (Savage writes 'pasé', with the accent, for pase). [= licence granted to citizen Leandro Flores to travel to Santa Bárbara.]

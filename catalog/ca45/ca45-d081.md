# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. New running head, verbatim: '1828 San Di

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n76** · Sav p. 76 [stamp] / 74 [ink] · tomo p. p.269 · 1828-09-30 · verbatim transcription · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. New running head, verbatim: '1828 San Diego / Gob.or Echeandia, y Agustin V. Zamorana, Secretario al Alcalde de San Jose' (Savage spells Zamorano as 'Zamorana' here and below). Entry heading: '+ Setiembre 30'. 'Lista de Electores q. han de nombrar Diputados al Congreso, y renovacion de la Diputacion de este Territorio: Por San Francisco, aun no se conoce el nombrado; San Jose, Miguel Gonzalez de Alava; Monterey, Fran.co Cota; S.ta Barbara, Manuel Dominguez; San Diego, Agustin V. Zamorana.' The full partido-elector roster for the 1828 territorial election.

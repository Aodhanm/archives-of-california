# Verbatim heading: '1823 - Monterey / Arguello al Alcalde de San Jose.' then '+ Junio 10 (2 docs.) pp.27-30'. Gloss: 10 J

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n7-n8** · Sav p. 7-8 [stamp] / 6-7 [ink] · tomo p. pp. 27-30 · 1823-06-10 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1823 - Monterey / Arguello al Alcalde de San Jose.' then '+ Junio 10 (2 docs.) pp.27-30'. Gloss: 10 June 1823, Monterey, Arguello to the Alcalde of San Jose, two documents. SUBSTANCE (Savage abstract, beginning at the foot of n7 and closing on n8): that he remit to him Manuel Pinto and Anastacio Mendoza, whom he has destined to three years in the obras publicas of these Presidios; that he likewise send him the individuals who are to enter as Militiamen. That, touching the instancias made by P. Fr. Jose Viader over the lands the vecinos of that pueblo have sown, he examine whether these occupy them without right to them; that he likewise consult with the vecinos of the pueblo whether they can give permission for the Mission ganado menor to drink water etc. in the Tular; and that he prevent caballada being pastured on the lands of the Mission. Verbatim fragments: 'a tres anos en las obras publicas de estos Presidios'; 'las instancias hechas por el P. Fr. Jose Viader sobre las tierras q. han sembrado los vecinos de ese pueblo, examine si estos las ocupan sin derecho a ellas'; 'si pueden dar permiso pa que el ganado menor de la Mision beba agua &c. en el Tular. Y que impida que se heche caballada sobre las tierras de la Mision.'

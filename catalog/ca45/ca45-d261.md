# Circular to every first juez from Santa Barbara to Sonoma announcing the new government secretary. Savage: 'Avisa qe des

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n271** · Sav p. 270 [stamp] / 91 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 390-1 · 1845-02-24 · Savage abstract · 

Circular to every first juez from Santa Barbara to Sonoma announcing the new government secretary. Savage: 'Avisa qe desde Feb. 22 ha entrado en el cargo de secretario de gobno Juan Bandini.' [= notice that since 22 Feb Juan Bandini has taken up the office of secretary of government]. Heading: 'Pio Pico a los jueces 1o del Depto, desde Sta Barbara hasta Sonoma.' Savage marks the entry with a plus sign.

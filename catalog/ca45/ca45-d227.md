# Savage writes the month as a looped abbreviation read here as '8bre' [= October]; a reading of 9bre [= November] cannot 

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n255** · Sav p. 254 [stamp] / 75 [hw] · tomo p. p. 329 · 1843-10-28 [?] · Savage abstract · 

Savage writes the month as a looped abbreviation read here as '8bre' [= October]; a reading of 9bre [= November] cannot be ruled out. Heading: 'Mis.n de Sta Clara.' 'Ordena q.e de una noticia de q.e si los terrenos de la mis.n son suficientes p.a la mantencion de sus bienes a causa de una queja de Fr.co Estrada por perjuicios q.e sufre por la caballada y borregada de dha mision' [= report whether Mission Santa Clara's lands suffice for its stock, on Francisco Estrada's complaint of damage by the mission horse herd and sheep].

# Savage heads it 'Setiembre 31' - an impossible date, left uncorrected here (verified at high magnification; it is not Oc

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n350** · Sav p. 348 [stamp] / 46 [hw] · tomo p. p. 259 · 1839-09-31 [sic, as Savage writes] · Savage abstract · 

Savage heads it 'Setiembre 31' - an impossible date, left uncorrected here (verified at high magnification; it is not Octubre). Abstract: 'De haber pasado la órden al individuo que se le recomienda.' (that he has passed the order to the individual recommended to him).

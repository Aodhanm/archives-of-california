# Revocation of the February banishment. Verbatim: 'Revocando la orn de destierro fha 20 de Feb. y ordenandole quede en co

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n380** · Sav p. 377 [stamp] / 12 [hw] · tomo p. p. 59 · 1843-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

Revocation of the February banishment. Verbatim: 'Revocando la orn de destierro fha 20 de Feb. y ordenandole quede en completa libertad la desterrada Sra. Emiliana Bernal.' Gloss: Sub-Prefect Suñol revokes the exile order of 20 February and directs that the banished Emiliana Bernal be left at complete liberty. Closes the case opened on n375-n377 (there spelled Miliana Bernal).

# New Savage block heading on the leaf: 'x 1833. En.o 29. Mont. pp 472. 73. / José Figueroa al Alcalde de S. José'. Entry 

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n115** · Sav p. 115 [stamp] / 113 [ink] · tomo p. pp.472, 73. · 1833-01-29 · Savage abstract · 

New Savage block heading on the leaf: 'x 1833. En.o 29. Mont. pp 472. 73. / José Figueroa al Alcalde de S. José'. Entry verbatim: 'Enterado de la buena disposicion de ese vecindario p.a restablecer la tranquilidad y el orden constitucional.' [= noted the good disposition of that vecindario toward re-establishing tranquillity and constitutional order.] Figueroas first recorded acknowledgement to San José from Monterey after taking the mando, closing the Victoria/Zamorano interregnum.

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n25** · Sav p. 25 [stamp] / 24 [ink] · tomo p. pp. 90-92 · 1824-07-20 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. 'Julio 20 (2 docs.) Que queda impuesto del insulto cometido por Blas Cibrian a la muger de Gongora, tratandola de prostituta 'forni retechin', y que proceda a formarle sumaria y se la remita pa proceder segun justicia.' Doc 2: 'Que aprueba que haya depositado a Maria Ygnacia Padilla en casa de su propia madre.' The quoted epithet is transcribed exactly as Savage wrote it, two words, no hyphen; its meaning is not established.

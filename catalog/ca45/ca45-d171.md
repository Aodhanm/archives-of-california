# VERBATIM HEADING: '1836. Set. 30. Mont. / N. Gutierrez al alcalde de S. Jose. Su nombram.to a gobernador.' GLOSS: his ap

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n184** · Sav p. stamp 183 / pencil 4 · tomo p. p.p. 11-12 · 1836-09-30 · verbatim transcription · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1836. Set. 30. Mont. / N. Gutierrez al alcalde de S. Jose. Su nombram.to a gobernador.' GLOSS: his appointment as governor. SUBSTANCE (verbatim): 'Le transcribe una comunicac.n de Mariano Chico a el, Gutierrez, en la cual Chico le participa que lo nombro a gobernador, a causa de querer el, Chico, ir a Mex.o para dar cuenta personalmente al Sup.mo gob.no del estado del pais.' Chico hands the governorship to Gutierrez on the stated ground that he is going to Mexico to report in person on the state of the country. This is the documentary hinge of the autumn 1836 succession that ran on into the Alvarado rising.

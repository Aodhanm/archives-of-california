**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n262** · Sav p. 261 [stamp] / 82 [hw] · tomo p. p. 370 · 1844-10-08 · Savage abstract · 

'Pide copia del informe dado por el alcalde en una solicitud hecha p[o]r José B. (Chiles) p[ar]a construir un molino en la orilla de San José.' Gloss: asks for a copy of the alcalde's report on the petition of José B. Chiles [= Joseph B. Chiles, American emigrant] to build a mill on the bank/edge of San José. Parenthetical '(Chiles)' is Savage's own identification. Doc 2 of 3 under the same heading.

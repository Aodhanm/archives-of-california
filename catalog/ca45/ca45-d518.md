# Verbatim heading: '1835. Set. 26. Mont. / Jose Castro al alc.de de S. Jose. Enfermedad del General.' Verbatim text: 'Pid

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n196** · Sav p. 195 / 16. · tomo p. p.p. 93-4. · 1835-09-26 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim heading: '1835. Set. 26. Mont. / Jose Castro al alc.de de S. Jose. Enfermedad del General.' Verbatim text: 'Pide q.e haga venir al medico James Stokes con la mayor brevedad, p.a asistir a una junta de medicos por causa de enfermedad peligrosa del General. - p.p. 93-4.' [= asks him to have the physician James Stokes come with the greatest speed, to attend a board of physicians on account of the dangerous illness of the General.] The General is not named on the leaf; that it is Jose Figueroa, Comandante General, is my inference from date and place, not something the leaf states.

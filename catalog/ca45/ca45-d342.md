# Five sailors have deserted an American whaling ship in a launch, carrying off various articles; the captain begs their a

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n321** · Sav p. 319 [stamp] / 17 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 100-1 · 1841-08-26 · Savage abstract · 

Five sailors have deserted an American whaling ship in a launch, carrying off various articles; the captain begs their apprehension, “ofreciendo una gratificacion de $20 por cada uno de ellos y $50 por la devolucion de la chalupa” (20 dollars a head, 50 for the launch's return).

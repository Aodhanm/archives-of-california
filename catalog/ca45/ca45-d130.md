# Pico sends the alcalde of San Jose the bando fixing the Asamblea's sitting days. Verbatim: 'los dias de sesiones ordinar

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n273** · Sav p. 272 [stamp] / 93 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 396-7 · 1845-03-15 · Savage abstract · 

Pico sends the alcalde of San Jose the bando fixing the Asamblea's sitting days. Verbatim: 'los dias de sesiones ordinarias pa. la asamblea departl seran lunes, miercoles y viernes de cada semana, o el inmediato si alguno de ellos fuese dia feriado' [= ordinary sessions of the departmental assembly on Monday, Wednesday and Friday each week, or the following day if one falls on a holiday].

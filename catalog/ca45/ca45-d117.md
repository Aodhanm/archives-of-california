# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1831. D.bre 21, Puebl

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n96** · Sav p. 96 [stamp] / 94 [ink] · tomo p. pp 393. 4. · 1831-12-21 · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '1831. D.bre 21, Pueblo de N.a S.a de los Angeles. / Jose M.a de Echeandia al Ilustre Ayuntam.to del Pueblo de San Jose'. 'Que el S.or D. Manuel Victoria no se halla en aptitud p.a gobernar, y ya camina a embarcarse.' [= that Sr. D. Manuel Victoria is not in a condition to govern, and is already on his way to embark.] Echeandia, writing from Los Angeles a fortnight after the Cahuenga fight, notifies the San Jose ayuntamiento of Victoria's removal and imminent departure.

# VERBATIM HEADING: '1827. Feb. 9. Mont. / Jose Castro al alc.de Mariano Castro, de San Jose', followed by three further d

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n192** · Sav p. stamp 191 / pencil 12 · tomo p. p.p. 62-3, 64-5, 66-7, 68-9 · 1827-02-09 to 1827-05-13 · verbatim transcription · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1827. Feb. 9. Mont. / Jose Castro al alc.de Mariano Castro, de San Jose', followed by three further dated sub-entries under the same running head. GLOSS: four routine items, three of them dismissed by Savage himself. SUBSTANCE (verbatim): Feb. 9, pp. 62-3, 'Incomprehensible = y sin interes.'; 'Feb. 14', pp. 64-5, 'Asunto judicial sin interes'; 'Mayo 13', pp. 66-7, 'Sobre un caballo.'; 'Marzo 20', pp. 68-9, 'Asunto judicial sin import.a'. Recorded because they are register entries not previously catalogued, not because they carry content. NOTE the sub-entries are out of date order on the leaf, as written.

# New Savage block heading on the leaf: 'x 1833. En.o 14. Bergantin Catalina. pp 470 - 71. / José Figueroa al Ayuntam.to d

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n115** · Sav p. 115 [stamp] / 113 [ink] · tomo p. pp.470 - 71. · 1833-01-14 · Savage abstract · 

New Savage block heading on the leaf: 'x 1833. En.o 14. Bergantin Catalina. pp 470 - 71. / José Figueroa al Ayuntam.to de San José = El Vice presidente me ha nombrado Gefe Politico de la Alta California'. Figueroa announces to the San José ayuntamiento, writing from aboard the brig Catalina, that the Vice President has appointed him Gefe Político of Alta California. Savage gives the heading with the announcement embedded and no separate body text on this leaf.

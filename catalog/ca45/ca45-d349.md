# Says he has administered the oath to the incoming Juez de Paz Francisco Sanchez for the administration of justice and de

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n324** · Sav p. 322 [stamp] / 20 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 116-17 · 1842-01-12 · Savage abstract · 

Says he has administered the oath to the incoming Juez de Paz Francisco Sanchez for the administration of justice and delivered him the archive with the customary formalities: “Dice haber tomado juramento al Juez de Paz entrante Francisco Sanchez para la suministracion de justicia y de haberle entregado el archivo con las formalidades de estilo.”

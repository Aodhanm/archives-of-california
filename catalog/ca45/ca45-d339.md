# Juan Copinger [?] has come before him against Cárlos Moreno for damage the latter's animals do to the former's crops: “D

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n321** · Sav p. 319 [stamp] / 17 [hw] · tomo p. pp. 94-5 · 1841-04-25 · Savage abstract · 

Juan Copinger [?] has come before him against Cárlos Moreno for damage the latter's animals do to the former's crops: “De haberse presentado Juan Copinger en contra de Cárlos Moreno por perjuicios que los Animales de éste causan á las siembras de aquel.” Savage's p is drawn like an f; reading Copinger [= John Copinger] but the hand is ambiguous.

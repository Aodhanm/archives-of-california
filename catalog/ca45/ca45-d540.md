# Same standing heading. Entry verbatim, beginning at the foot of n107 and finishing on the first line of n108: 'S.bre. 6.

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n107-n108** · Sav p. 107 [stamp] / 105 [ink] to 108 [stamp] / 106 [ink] · tomo p. pp.434, 35. · 1831-09-06 · Savage abstract · 

Same standing heading. Entry verbatim, beginning at the foot of n107 and finishing on the first line of n108: 'S.bre. 6. pp 434. 35. Que se suprime la salida de correo del | 22. de cada mes, dejando solo el del dia 11.' [= the mail departure of the 22nd of each month is suppressed, leaving only that of the 11th.] Halves the overland post out of the district to one departure a month.

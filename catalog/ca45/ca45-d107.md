# PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '+ S.bre. 6 Monterey'.

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n91** · Sav p. 91 [stamp] / 89 [ink] · tomo p. pp.348-49 · — · Savage abstract · 

PHASE 5 (2026-08-28): catalogued from a 1-to-4-leaf gap that no record covered. Heading verbatim: '+ S.bre. 6 Monterey'. 'Que remite ejemplares de decretos para su publicacion.' DATING PROBLEM, stated as such: the month is Savage's looped '?bre' glyph, and a same-scale comparison shows it is the SAME letterform he uses for 'Sbre' on n94. But the entry sits at pp.348-49, between Febrero 5 1830 (pp.344-47) and Febrero 10 1830 (pp.350-51) in a strictly page-ordered run. The year therefore cannot be fixed from this leaf and is NOT asserted here.

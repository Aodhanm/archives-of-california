# Assault on two US citizens at Yerba Buena, venue moved for partiality. Verbatim: 'Manda procesar varios individuos de Ye

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n383** · Sav p. 380 [stamp] / 15 [hw] · tomo p. p. 612-14 · 1845-10-26 · Savage abstract · 

Assault on two US citizens at Yerba Buena, venue moved for partiality. Verbatim: 'Manda procesar varios individuos de Yerba-buena, que componian una patrulla en persecucion de desertores, de los buques [interlined: balleneros] fondeados en aquella Bahia, por motivo de haber herido á Elliot Libbey.' Gloss: a Yerba Buena patrol hunting deserters from the whalers anchored in the bay wounded Elliot Libbey, captain of the bark Tasso, and knocked down Natan Spear, both US citizens; since the offenders were closely related to the local authorities, impartiality was not expected, so Buelna, at the nearest point, was to take the información.

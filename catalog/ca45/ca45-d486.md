# Vagrancy enforcement. Verbatim: 'Ordena q. pase sin demora á la Sub Prefª pª dar cta al Gobr, una lista de todos los vec

**C-A 45 (Departmental State Papers, San José), scan n377** · Sav p. 374 [stamp] / 9 [hw] · tomo p. p. 43 · 1843-02-23 · Savage abstract · 

Vagrancy enforcement. Verbatim: 'Ordena q. pase sin demora á la Sub Prefª pª dar cta al Gobr, una lista de todos los vecinos que no se ocupen de trabajar, á fin de aplicarles la ley de vagos.' Gloss: orders the judge to send the Sub-Prefecture without delay, for report to the Governor, a list of all residents not occupied in work, so the vagrancy law may be applied to them. Doc 1 of 2 under Savage's '(2 Docs.)' heading, but the pieces ARE distinguished: the second is separately dated Feb 24 with its own page cite.

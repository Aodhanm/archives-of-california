# 'Cóngruas de Ministros'. Acknowledges the order of the 3rd of that month that the pueblo, together with the Villa [de Br

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n245** · Sav p. 246/68 · tomo p. p.634 · 1834-01-25 · Savage abstract · 

'Cóngruas de Ministros'. Acknowledges the order of the 3rd of that month that the pueblo, together with the Villa [de Branciforte], is to form 'una parroquia separada de la de S.n Juan Bautista, y ambos Ministros tendran una cóngrua anual de $1.000 cada una'. ⚠ Year written 1834 by Savage but the Pueblo de Figueroa post-dates Aug 1834 — probably 1836; see issues.

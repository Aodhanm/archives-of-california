# 'Hace entrega, previo inventario, de la plata, ornamentos, vasos sagrados y alhajas de la Yglesia de dho Pueblo' [= hand

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n300** · Sav p. 301/23 · tomo p. p.288-303 · 1841-07-01 · Savage abstract · 

'Hace entrega, previo inventario, de la plata, ornamentos, vasos sagrados y alhajas de la Yglesia de dho Pueblo' [= handover, after inventory, of the silver, vestments, sacred vessels and jewels of the church of the pueblo of La Purísima]. 16 orig. tomo pp. (288-303) of itemized church goods, abstracted by Savage in a single sentence — the underlying original is a long ecclesiastical-property inventory. Shows church movables still being formally transferred between lay custodians seven years after secularization.

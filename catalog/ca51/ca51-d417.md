# Abstract in full: 'Dice: Que deste la fha. cesa de ejercer las funciones de Vicitador' [= from this date he ceases to ex

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n295** · Sav p. 296/18 · tomo p. p.186-89 · 1840-09-07 · Savage abstract · 

Abstract in full: 'Dice: Que deste la fha. cesa de ejercer las funciones de Vicitador' [= from this date he ceases to exercise the functions of Visitador]. Terminal document of Hartnell's mission-visitorship, four months after the Vallejo arrest and eleven days after the S. Buenaventura report. Month abbreviation read as 'Sbre' (Setiembre) — the glyph matches the S of 'S.ta Barbara' on the same heading line.

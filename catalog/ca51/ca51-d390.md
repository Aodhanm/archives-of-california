# 'Certifica haber prestado juramento á la independencia del Imperio Mexicano.' Gloss: Arroyo de la Cuesta certifies he ha

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n268** · Sav p. 269/10 · tomo p. p.331 · 1822-05-09 · Savage abstract · 

'Certifica haber prestado juramento á la independencia del Imperio Mexicano.' Gloss: Arroyo de la Cuesta certifies he has sworn the oath to the independence of the Mexican Empire — the documentary trace of the Franciscans' forced transfer of allegiance from Spain to Iturbide's empire at San Juan Bautista. Note this 1822 item sits out of chronological order amid 1830s–40s material in Tomo X.

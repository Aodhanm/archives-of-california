# 'Falta de esquilmos.' Answering a note ordering him to satisfy Padre Mercado's debts to private parties. Verbatim: 'qe n

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n230** · Sav p. 230/53 · tomo p. p.498-9 · 1840-11-16 · Savage abstract · 

'Falta de esquilmos.' Answering a note ordering him to satisfy Padre Mercado's debts to private parties. Verbatim: 'qe no hay mas esquilmos, qe los cueros de res, pues la mitad de la gente qe trabaja en los telares está sin frasadas, y los demas oficios suspensos.' [Gloss: nothing left to sell but cowhides; half the weavers have no blankets and the other workshops have shut down.] Destitution of the Indian workforce stated as a fiscal fact.

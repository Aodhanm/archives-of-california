# 'Herradero habido en el establecimto en aquel año' [= that year's branding at the establishment]. Tally: 'De Rodeo — 629

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n222** · Sav p. 222/45 · tomo p. p.435 · 1841-07-25 · Savage abstract · 

'Herradero habido en el establecimto en aquel año' [= that year's branding at the establishment]. Tally: 'De Rodeo — 629 cabezas / Ganado manso — 87 / Total 716.' NOTE: the 629 is written with Savage's angular 2 (629+87=716 confirms the reading). Annual branding return for Santa Clara under Mexican administration.

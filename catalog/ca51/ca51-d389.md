# Day lost: Savage's heading reads '1842 = Setiembre (por roto el papel no se ve el dia) = Sta Barbara.' Abstract in full:

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n268** · Sav p. 269/10 · tomo p. p.313-314 · 1842-09 · Savage abstract · 

Day lost: Savage's heading reads '1842 = Setiembre (por roto el papel no se ve el dia) = Sta Barbara.' Abstract in full: 'Que en el dia 24 se recibió del establecimiento de S. Buenaventura.' Gloss: Ayala reports to Gov. Alvarado that on the 24th he took receipt/charge of the San Buenaventura establishment — a mission handover under the post-secularization administrator system.

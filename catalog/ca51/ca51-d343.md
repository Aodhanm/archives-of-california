# Spans n239-n242. Fr. José de Jesús Gutiérrez repeatedly refuses to attend the dying. The neophyte 'Selso, patron de la l

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n239–n242** · Sav p. 240/62 · tomo p. p.603-6 · 1835-07-31 · Savage abstract · 

Spans n239-n242. Fr. José de Jesús Gutiérrez repeatedly refuses to attend the dying. The neophyte 'Selso, patron de la lancha y natural de esta' begged leave to denounce him to the P. Comisario, 'pues q.e p.r él habian muerto mas de ocho personas sin la administracion de Sacram.tos'; Selso had earlier gone to Monterey and got P. Altimira moved to S.n Solano. Valle refused him leave now, 'hace tanta falta la gente p.a la matanza'. Sgt. Soto (ret.) died unconfessed 8-10 days before; Admor. Gumesindo Flores was sent to remonstrate.

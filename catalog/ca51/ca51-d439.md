# Order executing the sale of San Luis Rey: 'Que entregue á Juan José Cot y José Ant.º Pico la mis.n de San Luis Rey y tod

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n330** · Sav p. 331/53 · tomo p. p.705 · 1846-07-24 · Savage abstract · 

Order executing the sale of San Luis Rey: 'Que entregue á Juan José Cot y José Ant.º Pico la mis.n de San Luis Rey y todos intereses, terrenos y demas pertenencias que le corresponden y ha tenido á su cargo, formando el correspondiente inventario.' [Savage writes 'Juan José Cot'; the SLR purchaser is usually given as Antonio José Cot — transcribed verbatim, not corrected: see issues]. Marrón had received the mission from J. J. Ortega in July 1845 (row at n336).

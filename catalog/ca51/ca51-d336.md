# Verbatim: 'Se recibió el 20 de Octe de 1838.' [Gloss: Estrada certifies that he took charge of Santa Clara on 20 October

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n234** · Sav p. 234/57 · tomo p. p.535-6 · 1839-02-08 · Savage abstract · 

Verbatim: 'Se recibió el 20 de Octe de 1838.' [Gloss: Estrada certifies that he took charge of Santa Clara on 20 October 1838.] Fixes the start of the Estrada administration, which the 1839 Apr 3 borregada letter (n234-n235) and the later Alviso letters bracket.

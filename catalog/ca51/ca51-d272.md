# Mission schoolmaster appointed: 'Haber nombrado Preceptor de la Mn. á Domingo Amado, con sueldo de $15 al mes, pagándose

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n192** · Sav p. 192/15 · tomo p. p.133-4 · 1835-01-09 · Savage abstract · 

Mission schoolmaster appointed: 'Haber nombrado Preceptor de la Mn. á Domingo Amado, con sueldo de $15 al mes, pagándoselos en efectos si no tuviere dinero la Mision.' [Teacher to be paid in goods if the mission has no cash.] The same Amado is the target of Fr. Abella's denunciation immediately following.

# Undated in the original — Savage heads it '1835 = (Sin fha).' Spans n280–n281. Received word from Mission San Fernando '

**C-A 51 (State Papers, Missions), scan n280** · Sav p. 281/3 · tomo p. p.35-36 · 1835 · Savage abstract · 

Undated in the original — Savage heads it '1835 = (Sin fha).' Spans n280–n281. Received word from Mission San Fernando 'sobre que los neofitos no querian reconocer á Carlos Carrillo, mandado por el Gobº pª la secularisacion de dicha Misn, marchó el en persona para persuadirlos, lo que no pudo conseguir.' Gloss: the San Fernando neophytes refuse to recognise the government's secularization commissioner outright, and Ybarra's personal mission to talk them round fails. Pairs directly with the 5 July accounting demand above.

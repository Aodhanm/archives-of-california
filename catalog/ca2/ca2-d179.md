# Royal Order on military *premios* (service-longevity pay) — transmitted via Gálvez (13 May): the *prest de premio* (senc

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n341–n343** · Sav p. 338–340 · tomo p. IV 4–6 (pp 5–8) · 1783-11-13 · Savage abstract · 

Royal Order on military *premios* (service-longevity pay) — transmitted via Gálvez (13 May): the *prest de premio* (sencillos reales de vellón at 25 yrs infantry / 30 cav. & dragoons; 35 reales w/ officer grade at 35 yrs común); drummers, fifers, kettledrummers & trumpeters excluded from officer grade but get 135 reales/mo at sergeant graduation; derogates prior excess grants; cites reales órdenes of 1774 & 1770. Neve orders it published in Soler's subinspección

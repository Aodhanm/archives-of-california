# Sal's first documented act in office: the late habilitado Mariano Carrillo's testamentaría — the auction (*remate*) yiel

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n29** · Sav p. 29 · tomo p. 58–60 · 1782-10-09 · Savage abstract · 

Sal's first documented act in office: the late habilitado Mariano Carrillo's testamentaría — the auction (*remate*) yields $339.5.5 + jewels appraised $118.1.1½, covering last year's company debt; the money delivered 'al habilitado Sal,' who applies the distribution, charges each individual, credits the deceased's account & gives his receipt

# Early San José horse-raiding incident: Indians killed *cabezas caballares* near Pueblo de San José; Moraga rode out w/ 8

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n358** · Sav p. 355 · tomo p. p.IV 21 (pp 38–40) · 1783-01-20 · Savage abstract · 

Early San José horse-raiding incident: Indians killed *cabezas caballares* near Pueblo de San José; Moraga rode out w/ 8 soldiers + pobladores to punish the *malhechores*, captured 2 he judged guilty, threatened the rancherías with *escarmiento* if they reoffend, and returned them to the pueblo in grilletes pending further orders → indian-raiding + SJ crossref + Moraga prosopography

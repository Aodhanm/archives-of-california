# 'Instrucciones al Comⁱˢⁱᵒ de Monterey durante su ausencia' — Neve's command-handover instruction (he departs to San Gabr

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n308–n312** · Sav p. 305–309 · tomo p. 557–563 · 1781-03-22 · Savage abstract · 

'Instrucciones al Comⁱˢⁱᵒ de Monterey durante su ausencia' — Neve's command-handover instruction (he departs to San Gabriel to meet Rivera's expedition). Clauses: ⚠ defense vs. a rumored English expedition (Admiral 'Hughes') — on any such vessel's arrival, drive all cattle inland (San Carlos Christian Indians charged w/ the removal) & hold troops ready [→ bodega/foreign-threat, 1781]; memorias/correos routed via Sgt Moraga (SF); loza y herrajes to SF & Sta Bárbara from fondos de gratificación; San Blas–vessel handling; delivery of embargoed effects of deceased soldiers (Juárez, López); arrangement of Alférez Soler's documents; hand over the *libro de filiaciones* & the *Reglamento interino de la Península*; note on the SB presidio main gate needing repair. Neve firmado

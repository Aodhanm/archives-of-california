# Passport/embarkation-control bando — invoking prior superior providencias, prohibits any inhabitant crossing to 'la otra

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n338–n339** · Sav p. 335–336 · tomo p. IV 1–2 · 1783-05-06 · Savage abstract · 

Passport/embarkation-control bando — invoking prior superior providencias, prohibits any inhabitant crossing to 'la otra banda' without the Governor's express order/pasaporte; no *arráez* or *patrón* may take anyone aboard without it, under established penalties; Comandante of Loreto & subaltern officers to enforce, offenders punished [→ bodega/movement-control]

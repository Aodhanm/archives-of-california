# 8-DOCUMENT RIVERA-Y-MONCADA ACCOUNTS/RESIDENCIA DOSSIER copied at his petition (cobro de alcances, residencia): (1) Juan

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n191–n202** · Sav p. 188–199 · tomo p. p.374–385 · 1782-04-07 (encl. 1774–86) · Savage abstract · 

8-DOCUMENT RIVERA-Y-MONCADA ACCOUNTS/RESIDENCIA DOSSIER copied at his petition (cobro de alcances, residencia): (1) Juan Soler account-settlement, Monterey 18-Apr-1780; (2) Rafael de Pedro y Gil SD guarda-almacén certification (no debt), 5-May-1780; (3) Bucareli → R.y M., México 26-Mar-1776 (pay alcances via Juan José de Echeveste); (4) Echeveste 27-May-1778; (5) liquidation of alcances — Ramón de Goya substituting Echeveste as *Factor* ($466/$472/$386; $80 to Tiburcio Chaves via Tomás Pallo de Híjar); (6) ⭐⭐⭐ Bucareli → R.y M., 18-May-1774 — recounts ANZA's 1774 reconnaissance: crossing the Colorado & Gila at San Dionisio, 'infinita Gentilidad… mansedumbre… pacíficos,' the S. Diego→Monterey road, and Anza's deduction that a NW branch of the Colorado empties toward the PORT OF SAN FRANCISCO ('la identidad del Puerto'); (7) Bucareli 22-Oct-1777 (collect $1,000 via Croix); [(8) beyond n195 — verify]. ⚠ BOUNDED dossier row; the Anza sub-doc worth a verbatim pass → founding/exploration + [[project_map_collection]] + registry (Echeveste/Goya/Soler/Pedro-y-Gil early habilitados → [[project_sal_papers]])

# The Tribunal de Cuentas orders lists of everyone paying the Reglamento pension — retroactive 1774–1780 — to be sent to S

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n15** · Sav p. 15 · tomo p. p.22–24 · 1781-09-25 · Savage abstract · 

The Tribunal de Cuentas orders lists of everyone paying the Reglamento pension — retroactive 1774–1780 — to be sent to SPAIN with the two Ramos' originals from the Caja Matriz; oficio signed Santiago Abad & Joaquín Trebuesto, el Mariscal de Castilla y Marqués de Ciria; foot-certification 'copia exacta… del original que existe en este Archivo,' Loreto 6 Apr 1782, signed Joaquín Cañete [the Loreto comandante Arrillaga later succeeded, cf. C-A 23]

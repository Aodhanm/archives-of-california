# BOUNDED CLUSTER — Moraga's 1781 MONTHLY MUSTER-EXTRACTS for the Pueblo of San José de Guadalupe (May/Jun/Aug/Oct/Nov/Dec

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n248–n250** · Sav p. 245–247 · tomo p. 431–436+ · 1781 (May–Dec) · Savage abstract · 

BOUNDED CLUSTER — Moraga's 1781 MONTHLY MUSTER-EXTRACTS for the Pueblo of San José de Guadalupe (May/Jun/Aug/Oct/Nov/Dec): consistently 5 settlers 'con sueldo y ración' + 3 'de pura ración' — the founding-era muster rolls of California's first civil pueblo (most months 'igual á la anterior'). Pairs w/ the front-matter SJ livestock census/roster (d88)

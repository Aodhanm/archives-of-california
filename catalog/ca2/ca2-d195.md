# SAN JOSÉ PUEBLO ALCALDE ELECTION + LAND-DISTRIBUTION COMMISSION — ordered to have the pobladores of San José elect 1 Alc

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n360** · Sav p. 357 · tomo p. p.IV 23 (pp 46–47) · 1783-01-20 · Savage abstract · 

SAN JOSÉ PUEBLO ALCALDE ELECTION + LAND-DISTRIBUTION COMMISSION — ordered to have the pobladores of San José elect 1 Alcalde + 2 Regidores; delayed by having to ride out against the Indians (= d191); the election fell on Ygnacio Archuleta (alcalde); the regidores' election was forgotten (to report next correo); Moraga holds the *expediente* commissioning him for the 'posesión de solares y suertes de tierras' (house-lots & field-allotments) to the San José inhabitants, to execute once *habilitado* → SJ municipal foundation + land distribution + alcalde-rule + SJ crossref

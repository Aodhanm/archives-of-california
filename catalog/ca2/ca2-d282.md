# Asserts the INDEPENDENCE of the Provincias Internas from the Viceroy of Mexico — the justices are not to comply with vic

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n422** · Sav p. 418 · tomo p. IV 84 (pp 204–205) · 1782-10-24 · Savage abstract · 

Asserts the INDEPENDENCE of the Provincias Internas from the Viceroy of Mexico — the justices are not to comply with viceregal bandos/órdenes (remit them to Croix), only observe *despachos/exhortos/requisitorias* from legitimate judges for criminal apprehension → governance/jurisdiction (Comandancia General vs. Viceroyalty) ⚠ dated 1782, bound out of order

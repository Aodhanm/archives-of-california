# Departure & escort instructions: if the frigates *Princesa* & *Favorita* haven't reached Sta Bárbara, continue to Monter

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n21** · Sav p. 21 · tomo p. 41–44 · 1782-07-13 · Savage abstract · 

Departure & escort instructions: if the frigates *Princesa* & *Favorita* haven't reached Sta Bárbara, continue to Monterey to inspect his company + San Francisco's; Lt-Comte José Fran.co Ortega [at SB] to send a barrel of aguardiente + one of wine on the two mules; instructions on the soldiers to escort Comte Pedro Fages; send the socorros de ropa/cigarros with the correos

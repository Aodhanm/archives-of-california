# THE MEASURED PLAT OF THE PUEBLO DE LOS ÁNGELES — Savage's FACSIMILE of Argüello's signed plan (folio 110, sin lugar ni f

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n56–n57** · Sav p. 55–56 · tomo p. 110 · [c.1782–86, s.f.] · Savage abstract · 

THE MEASURED PLAT OF THE PUEBLO DE LOS ÁNGELES — Savage's FACSIMILE of Argüello's signed plan (folio 110, sin lugar ni fecha): the plaza block at 50-vara scale with every lettered lot keyed to its holder — Guardia (A), Casas Reales (B), Troje del Pósito (C), solares of Felipe Rodríguez (D), José Vanegas (E), José Moreno (F), Félix Villavicencio (G), Francisco Sinoba (H), Basilio Rosas (J), Alejandro Rosas (K), Antonio Navarro (L), Manuel Camero (M), vacíos (Y), calles (N), corner callejones (O), Plaza (P); + the suertes grid at 200-vara scale (per-settler blocks, 'suertes realengas — de labor'), the zanja de riego [zanja madre], the Camino real 'para el mar y Misión de S. Gabriel,' and the river courses drawn. The founding land-distribution map of Los Ángeles with the pobladores named to their lots — cf.

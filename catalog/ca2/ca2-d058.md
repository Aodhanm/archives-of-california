# SD district report: Alférez [José] Velásquez comisionado to open the Frontera road (cabo Acebes joined; per Soler seek '

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n78–n79** · Sav p. 77–78 · tomo p. 162–164 · 1782-12-17 · Savage abstract · 

SD district report: Alférez [José] Velásquez comisionado to open the Frontera road (cabo Acebes joined; per Soler seek 'la mayor derechera y ahorro de tierra' toward the Colorado road via the paraje S. Fermín[?], after the waters); received from the Río Colorado only 6 mules & 7 aparejos, not the 14 stated [campaign livestock trickling back]; at Los Ángeles a cabo + 2 soldiers (15 with the 12 from SB); the 8 toros/8 novillos noted; the sick herrero couldn't make the pobladores' branding irons or the S. Gabriel chapa; ⭐ the order 'sobre prohibición de bailes á los Indios cristianos' communicated to the escolta cabos [dances banned to Christian Indians]; partes required when Indians kill/steal stock; soldier Serrano's $83.75 charge; ⚠⚠ 'la única novedad… la MUERTE DEL SARGENTO RAIMUNDO CARRILLO el día 4, y la del soldado Tomás Cañedo el 14; ambos murieron de dolor de costado' — ⚠ VERIFY: the famous Raymundo Carrillo (Mariano's brother-heir, later SB comandante) lived to 1809 → this dead sergeant is a DIFFERENT man of the same name or a copy error; do not conflate

# Report of the Visita del Sur de California (fiscal/mining inspection, practiced from 5 June): 3 bandos promulgated at th

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n344–n350** · Sav p. 341–347 · tomo p. IV 7–13 (pp 12–17) · 1783-07-07 · Savage abstract · 

Report of the Visita del Sur de California (fiscal/mining inspection, practiced from 5 June): 3 bandos promulgated at the reales San Antonio / Santa Cruz del Oro / Todos Santos (miners' manifest rules for the Real Casa del Rescate; embarkation-license ban; pulpería *composición* tax); silver-mining laws & annual yields; cattle-brand (fierro/marca) registration & the *real derecho* enforced via Sgt Francisco de Aguiar; merchant price-gouging ('otro arancel que el que les dicta su codicia y la lengua'); vices/vagrants imprisoned (Cayetano Guanambo; incorrigible drunks); Real Hacienda arrears — Antonio de Díez's 1,000-peso obligation (800 overdue, four *tropa* guarantors); land-grant claims (Todos Santos, San José del Cabo missions); accounting confusion & worker-wage (*trabajo personal*) abuses at the reales (Santa Ana, Real del Oro). ⚠ Antigua California (Baja) — tangential to Alta Cal focus but rich fiscal/economic-history; ends n350 (Estrada suspends resolution on ~20 fugitives from Mission Todos Santos pending Fages' decision)

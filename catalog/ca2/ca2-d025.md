# THE GOVERNORSHIP TRANSITION: on Neve's promotion to the Inspección of the Provincias-Internas veterans, 'se ha nombrado 

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n25–n26** · Sav p. 25–26 · tomo p. p.51 · 1782-09-10 · verbatim transcription · 

THE GOVERNORSHIP TRANSITION: on Neve's promotion to the Inspección of the Provincias-Internas veterans, 'se ha nombrado Gobernador interino de la Península á PEDRO FAGES' — to be recognized as such in all the presidios; everything pertaining to inspection remains with Ayudante Soler.

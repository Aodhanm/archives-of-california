# The full BANDO promoting hemp & flax cultivation in New Spain — free of duties (per the 1-Oct-1778 Reglamento), and (per

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n251–n257** · Sav p. 248–254 · tomo p. p.445–454 · 1781-04-30 · Savage abstract · 

The full BANDO promoting hemp & flax cultivation in New Spain — free of duties (per the 1-Oct-1778 Reglamento), and (per Recopilación de Indias law 20, tít.18, lib.4) all flax/hemp in the American dominions may be freely worked (looms, factories, sailcloth/*lonas*), with a processing house in the capital; framed as poor-relief. Published/read at Mission San Gabriel 14 Nov 1781 (Fr. Antonio Cruzado) — the economic-development decree behind the later Cal. hemp program

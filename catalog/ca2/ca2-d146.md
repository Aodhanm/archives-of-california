# Method for presidio-officer appointment consultations (approved by Real Orden, per Reglamento art.2 tít.8): captains of 

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n261–n263** · Sav p. 258–260 · tomo p. 473–476 · 1780-01-07 · Savage abstract · 

Method for presidio-officer appointment consultations (approved by Real Orden, per Reglamento art.2 tít.8): captains of the Provincia & California presidios propose subalterns (alféreces/sargentos/cabos) via the Inspection; Croix reserves the faculty to name presidio/compañía-volante captains without formal consulta until a Comⁿ Inspector exists.

# General instructions — the inspection/fiscal machine stood up: habilitado prevenciones on the troop's *libretas* (incl. 

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n5–n7** · Sav p. 5–7 · tomo p. p.10–13 · 1782-02-16 · verbatim transcription · 

General instructions — the inspection/fiscal machine stood up: habilitado prevenciones on the troop's *libretas* (incl. families of absentees); arms training; the revista document + the 4 *cuadros* per company (one to Soler, rest to the Comdte Gral); Relaciones de fondos de retención y gratificación per his formularios; *Papel de puntos* in quadruplicate; relaciones of horses/mules/fierros; caution with licencias (replacements scarce); horses/mules hard to get — keep them 'aunque parezcan inútiles'; revistas at each presidio with per-soldier debit/credit notebooks 'pª qᵉ cada soldado sepa el estado de su cuenta'; maestranza/sailors/embarcaciones/arsenal accounts justified & approved to the Real Hacienda; recognize the *libro de afiliaciones* against the ordenanzas

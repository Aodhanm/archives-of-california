# 'Castigo de un reo / Nombramⁱᵒ de defensor' — Auditor de Guerra condemns soldier José Antⁱᵒ Sánchez (for inducing others

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n307–n308** · Sav p. 304–305 · tomo p. p.553–556 · 1781-04-15 · Savage abstract · 

'Castigo de un reo / Nombramⁱᵒ de defensor' — Auditor de Guerra condemns soldier José Antⁱᵒ Sánchez (for inducing others to desert) to labor w/ grillete al pie in the *fábricas materiales* of the Peninsula presidios, at ración y sin sueldo; Neve to name a defensor. Sentence read & executed at Monterey 17 Ago 1781 (Carrillo formed the troops, witness alférez Pablo Antⁱᵒ Cota), prisoner sent w/ Sebastián López to Tⁱᵉ Moraga at San Francisco 19 Ago

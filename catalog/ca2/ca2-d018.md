# The founding of the POSTAL SERVICE: 'estafetillas' in every peninsula presidio from 1783 — in the charge of the HABILITA

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n20** · Sav p. 20 · tomo p. p.40 · 1782-07-12 · Savage abstract · 

The founding of the POSTAL SERVICE: 'estafetillas' in every peninsula presidio from 1783 — in the charge of the HABILITADOS (following the frontier-line system); encloses S.M.'s declarations, a postage tarifa + an instruction on managing the estafetillas; official mail franked ('será franca la correspondencia de puro oficio'); notify the other presidios. Another function loaded onto the habilitado's office

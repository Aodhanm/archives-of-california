# Comandante General resolves the permuta of the Loreto & Santa Bárbara tenientes (the first to hold the *habilitación* pe

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n359** · Sav p. 356 · tomo p. p.IV 22 (pp 42–43) · 1783-01-17 · Savage abstract · 

Comandante General resolves the permuta of the Loreto & Santa Bárbara tenientes (the first to hold the *habilitación* per his capacity, the second to become Comandante of Antigua California); Ortega's animals not to leave the establishments (buy them w/ Real Hacienda funds); soldiers transferring from California to bring ≥2 horses

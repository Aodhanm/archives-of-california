# Concesión de licencias: Loreto comandante Joaquín Cañete sent the certifications of men requesting discharge; grant them

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n16** · Sav p. 16 · tomo p. 32 · 1782-02-27 · Savage abstract · 

Concesión de licencias: Loreto comandante Joaquín Cañete sent the certifications of men requesting discharge; grant them provided the next revista proves their time is complete. ⚠ the 'La Concepción' dateline in FEB 1782 is anomalous (the Colorado site was destroyed Jul 1781; Neve was at San Gabriel) — possibly a copy-order slip; record as written

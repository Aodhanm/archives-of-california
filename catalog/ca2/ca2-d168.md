# Guard-duty / sentinel + sergeant regulation: the obligations of a *centinela* (post handover w/ arms presented; respect 

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n297–n303** · Sav p. 294–300 · tomo p. 548–549 · (no date) · Savage abstract · 

Guard-duty / sentinel + sergeant regulation: the obligations of a *centinela* (post handover w/ arms presented; respect the sentinel; never surrender the weapon; no conversation on duty; alert the cabo on fire/shots/disorder; orders only via the cabo; guard the *cuartel* door day & night) + the sergeant's rounds (night patrols, no prohibited gambling, prevent scandal, oversee the caballada/ganado) — a standard military ordenanza

# Applies the donativo to California: advise the company commanders to make the troops aware of the King's war needs (Croi

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n285** · Sav p. 282 · tomo p. p.522–525 · 1781-08-12 · Savage abstract · 

Applies the donativo to California: advise the company commanders to make the troops aware of the King's war needs (Croix thinks the troops needn't be compelled but the small sum is no burden; officers may donate directly); for the presidio *vecindarios* & adjacent missions, observe the instruction (d159), varying as convenient

# Force-strength report of his company including the San José de Guadalupe pobladores; the Indians Fages sent from Mission

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n367** · Sav p. 364 · tomo p. p.IV 30 (pp 77–78) · 1783-03-01 · Savage abstract · 

Force-strength report of his company including the San José de Guadalupe pobladores; the Indians Fages sent from Mission Santa Clara dispatched w/ alférez Sal to the pueblo (promising not to reoffend killing mares); and the Indians punished in Jan–Feb have (via Valerio) SOUGHT PEACE & FRIENDSHIP, promising moderation — closes the SF/San José/Santa Clara mare-killing → punishment → reconciliation cycle → indian-raiding

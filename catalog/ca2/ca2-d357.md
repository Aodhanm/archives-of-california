# Force/mail; the 4 imprisoned Indians RELEASED (no cause found re the disturbance); a cabo from San Juan arrested the cap

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n484** · Sav p. 480 · tomo p. IV 146 (pp 336–337) · 1784-12-21 · Savage abstract · 

Force/mail; the 4 imprisoned Indians RELEASED (no cause found re the disturbance); a cabo from San Juan arrested the capitanejo (chief) of the Río de Santa Ana + another for killing 4 horses — confirming the mission Gabrielinos were innocent and the gentiles the instigators of the *motín* → indian-raiding + a nuanced justice process

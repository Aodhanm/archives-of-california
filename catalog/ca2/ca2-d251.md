# Remits 3 *conocimientos* (bills of lading) for effects carried by the frigate *Santiago* (1777) + a receipt from Guarda-

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n397–n399** · Sav p. 394–396 · tomo p. IV 60–62 (pp 154–159) · 1783-10-14 · Savage abstract · 

Remits 3 *conocimientos* (bills of lading) for effects carried by the frigate *Santiago* (1777) + a receipt from Guarda-Almacén Juan Soler + TWO RECEIPTS BY FR. JUNÍPERO SERRA — verbatim 1775 receipt (San Carlos): Serra received 7 mules from Rivera y Moncada, 5 marked for the NEW MISSION SAN JUAN CAPISTRANO; the *Santiago*'s provisions manifest (700 fanegas maize, 150 frijol, rice/garbanzos/lentils/chile/panocha/flour/sugar/vaqueta/soap/lard + 8 boxes powder); conocimientos signed by *contramaestre* Luis Venegas. ⚠ Savage note: the only Serra document in the volume → ship registry (Santiago + crew) + Serra autograph + mission founding-supply (SJC)

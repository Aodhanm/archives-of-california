# Four items: (1) need for armeros (armorers) at the presidios; (2) the 'quietud de los gentiles del Canal' (Channel Chuma

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n388** · Sav p. 385 · tomo p. IV 51 (pp 125–128 dup.) · 1783-06-09 · Savage abstract · 

Four items: (1) need for armeros (armorers) at the presidios; (2) the 'quietud de los gentiles del Canal' (Channel Chumash peaceful) + opening of the new road frontier→Antigua Cal.; (3) ⭐⭐⭐ the SAN JOSÉ PUEBLO ↔ MISSION SANTA CLARA BOUNDARY DISPUTE — Fages wrongly refused to admit the R.R. Ministros' written petition; all parties' *instancias y recursos* must be admitted, justice to decide the division; (4) the Padre Presidente withholding the year's inventories → SJ/mission–pueblo land conflict + californio/alcalde-rule

# Transmits the Royal Resolution reforming the penalty for 2nd-time desertion — the old penalty (Art. 2 of the circular of

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n351** · Sav p. 348+ · tomo p. IV 14+ (pp 22+) · 1783-09-18 · Savage abstract · 

Transmits the Royal Resolution reforming the penalty for 2nd-time desertion — the old penalty (Art. 2 of the circular of 1 Jul 1778: 6 runs of the gauntlet [*carreras de baquetas*] through 200 men + 6 months' prison in shackles + barracks-cleaning + discharge) proved counterproductive (men courted the baquetas to win discharge); per the Supremo Consejo de la Guerra (23 May), the King substitutes a new penalty — 6 runs of the gauntlet through 200 men + 10 years' presidio service (per Ordenanzas Grales., tratado 8 tít. 7). Ends n352

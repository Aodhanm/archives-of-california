# Transmits the REGLAMENTO/ARANCEL (price schedule) for the sale of California fruits, effects, cattle, meat & seeds — the

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n494–n495** · Sav p. 490–491+ · tomo p. p.IV 156–157+ · 1784-01-15 · Savage abstract · 

Transmits the REGLAMENTO/ARANCEL (price schedule) for the sale of California fruits, effects, cattle, meat & seeds — the schedules Neve formed as Governor (per the Real Orden of 22 Feb 1781 + Croix's order to Neve of 27 Jul 1781); inhabitants of each jurisdiction to abide by the prescribed prices, varying them per the times without waiting for approval → economic history (price regulation; cf. d73/d108); ends n496 (pp 371–375)

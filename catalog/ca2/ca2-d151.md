# THE LOS ÁNGELES FOUNDING POBLADORES' ACCOUNTS + NAMES (reconstructed after loss of the originals): Rivera y Moncada deli

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n269–n271** · Sav p. 266–268 · tomo p. p.495–502 · 1781-10-31 · verbatim transcription · 

THE LOS ÁNGELES FOUNDING POBLADORES' ACCOUNTS + NAMES (reconstructed after loss of the originals): Rivera y Moncada delivered $3,146 + Lt. Zúñiga $279 (Álamos→Sta Bárbara) to the founding settlers; itemized *diarios/bagajes/raciones*; the NAMED founding pobladores of Los Ángeles — José Lara, José Vanegas, Basilio Rosas, Antⁱᵒ Mesa, Antⁱᵒ Villavicencio, Alejandro Rosas, Pablo Rodríguez, Luis Quintero, Manuel Camero, José Moreno, Antⁱᵒ [Miranda] — with their wives + 27 children, 27 bagajes over 152 leagues from la Bahía de S. Luis to San Gabriel. The founding families of LA in a financial-accounts register, complementing the 1793 Argüello plat (front-matter d48)

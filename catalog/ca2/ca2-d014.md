# Soler's 14-article Prevenciones for the Comandante of the Presidio de Loreto (⚠ Savage misdates the header '1872'): fili

**C-A 2 (Provincial State Papers), scan n7–n14** · Sav p. 7–14 · tomo p. 14–19 · 1782-02-16 · verbatim transcription · 

Soler's 14-article Prevenciones for the Comandante of the Presidio de Loreto (⚠ Savage misdates the header '1872'): filiaciones book + carpetas (1); ordenanzas/reglamento copies down to sergeants (2); discipline, serviceable vestuario/armamento (3); a presidio horse-brand + 'L' engraved on the cannons so alienated prendas are recoverable, punishment per the Comdte Gral's bando (4); sword-belts for dismounted drills 'sin la cuera… que embarazará los movimientos' (5); ready for the inspection revista under 'el nuevo Reglamento que gobierna esta Provincia' [the 1781 Reglamento], quadruplicate hojas de servicio (6); ⭐ 'Manejo de Intereses' — nothing administered without a *libreta* entry, ruled yearly columns, duplicates so the soldier AND his family each hold a copy (7); year-end libreta revision vs the libro mayor w/ visto bueno (8); death/desertion accounts settled to the day, effects listed, heirs noted 'á fin de determinar lo que se estime justo' (9); arms drill — the troop 'ha carecido de toda enseñanza,' new rules adapted 'á su carácter y naturaleza,' aim = 'manejo del arma y tirar bien al blanco' (10); instruction under alférez José Mª Estrada while the captaincy is vacant, best soldiers to the Destacamento de Sta Ana (11); ⭐ powder-and-lead economy — 3 lbs powder/soldier/year, an adobe target wall so the balls are dug out and re-melted, monthly consumed-powder/lost-balls reports (12); training days (13); detached-troop relief 'teniendo por norte la equidad y justicia,' and see the soldiers repair their houses/portales/enramadas lest they 'llegar al extremo de vivir en la calle' (14)

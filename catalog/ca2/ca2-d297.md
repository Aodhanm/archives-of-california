Royal Cédula (via Taranco) — DISINHERITANCE under the marriage Pragmática: a parent may not institute as heir a son/daughter who married against the father's will (inheritance passes to siblings / the Real Fisco); illustrated by the López v. Aranda case (Guanajuato — the Alcalde Mayor upheld father Ramón de Aranda's resistance to daughter María Manuela's marriage per the Pragmática of 23 Mar 1776 / cédula 7 Apr 1778); addressed to viceroys/audiencias/bishops → social & legal history (marriage consent + inheritance; extends d224) ⚠ faded n444–445

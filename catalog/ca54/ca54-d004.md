# muster roll, San Francisco presidial company. — Lt. José Argüello; Sgt. Pedro Amador; Cpls. Miguel Pacheco, Luis Peralta

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n7–n9** · Sav p. 8–10 · tomo p. 25–26 · 1795, Dec 31 · Savage abstract · 

muster roll, San Francisco presidial company. — Lt. José Argüello; Sgt. Pedro Amador; Cpls. Miguel Pacheco, Luis Peralta, Manuel Boronda; soldiers = the founding SF/San José families (Bernal, Higuera, Alviso, Galindo, Bojorques, Sánchez, Aceves, Valencia, Castillo). Retired alférez Ramón Lasso (de la Vega); invalids Gabriel Peralta, Ignacio Linares, Justo Altamirano, Ignacio Soto. Balance $5,751 in the company's favor.

# Pedro Cevallos (Secretary of State) → Viceroy — Royal Order on foreign whaling-contraband ships. — The companion metropo

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n190–n191** · Sav p. 191–192 · 1803, Aug 10, Madrid · Savage abstract · 

Pedro Cevallos (Secretary of State) → Viceroy — Royal Order on foreign whaling-contraband ships. — The companion metropolitan order: foreign ships 'so color de la pesca de la Ballena hacen un excedido contrabando' on the Mar del Sur coasts of the King's dominions; coastal employees to bar foreigners from landing per standing orders, 'siendo este punto convenido entre todas las potencias que tienen colonias'; ships caught smuggling — the named 'Ulises' and 'Stefan' — to be confiscated. ⭐⭐ The King is notifying the Spanish Minister to the United States of America to warn that Government that action will be taken against [ships] landing at the Spanish colonies. ⭐ Strong borderlands evidence — Spain at the metropolitan level (1803) addressing the U.S. directly over American/foreign whalers & contraband on the Pacific/California coast. → [[bay-area-borderlands project]] (American perspective / foreign incursion), [[otter-corruption-project]].

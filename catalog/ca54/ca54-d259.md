# Doc IV-69 — Romeu: Eliza's Nootka dispatches + the *Saturnina* from Nootka. — ⭐⭐ Romeu forwards (July 1791) the dispatch

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n489–n492** · Sav p. 490–492 · 1791, San Fernando / Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Doc IV-69 — Romeu: Eliza's Nootka dispatches + the *Saturnina* from Nootka. — ⭐⭐ Romeu forwards (July 1791) the dispatches of Francisco de Eliza, Comandante del Presidio de Santa Cruz de Nutka [Nootka], relayed via Loreto to the Viceroy 'en la mayor prontitud'; ⭐⭐ announces the schooner *Saturnina* arrived at Monterey 'de regreso de Nutka,' 14 Oct 1791 (again confirming the *Saturnina* as the NW-coast schooner named in the boundary letter i.119). + ornaments for the new Mission Santa Cruz; the founding of Mission Santo Tomás de Aquino (Baja, Apr 1791). → [[bodega-failure-1793]] (Nootka/Saturnina), [[ca54-bodega-vancouver-deep-read]].

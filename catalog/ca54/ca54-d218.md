# Gov. Borica → Viceroy — 'Sobre inconveniencia de construir fortalezas.' — ⭐⭐ With engineer Córdova's plans of the SF por

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n380–n381** · Sav p. 381–382 · 1796, Sep 21, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Gov. Borica → Viceroy — 'Sobre inconveniencia de construir fortalezas.' — ⭐⭐ With engineer Córdova's plans of the SF port + Santa Cruz ensenada: building fortifications capable of covering the Peninsula's main points 'erogaría crecidos gastos' — better to increase the cavalry and keep 2–3 ships always at San Francisco; '2 fragatas de guerra en San Fran.co bastarán' against single ships, and 'en aumentando la población todo hombre será miliciano.' ⭐ The governor himself declining to fortify — Spain's deliberate choice not to harden the coast. → [[presidio-decay-project]], [[bay-area-borderlands project]], [[bodega-failure-1793]].

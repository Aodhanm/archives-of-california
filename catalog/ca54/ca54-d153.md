# Solá *borradores*, pt. 3. — Promotions: Gervasio Argüello → alférez (SF), Gabriel Moraga → teniente (Sta Bárbara), Ignac

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n230–n237** · Sav p. 231–238 · 1819, Feb–Jun, Monterey/San Antonio · Savage abstract · 

Solá *borradores*, pt. 3. — Promotions: Gervasio Argüello → alférez (SF), Gabriel Moraga → teniente (Sta Bárbara), Ignacio Martínez subrogated; royal *despachos* & *cédulas* forwarded (Cabo Ignacio Alviso → sgt. 2°, etc.); militia gunners given 4 months' planting leave; a theft *sumaria* (José Antonio & Nieves Robles, father & son → 2 and 4 yrs presidio). ⭐⭐ Aftermath of the Bouchard insurgent attack on Monterey (draft to Alférez Manuel Gómez, re 'el encuentro de los insurgentes en Monterey') — names the militia artilleryman José de Jesús Vallejo and the two Monterey cannons (→ [[bouchard-raid-1818]], [[presidio-garrison-artillery-register]]). ⭐ Mass neophyte flight from Mission Santa Cruz (Padre Ramón Olbés: 'se fugó la indiada… solo quedaron tres'; Lt. Estudillo + Alférez Estrada sent to recover them) (→ [[indian-raiding-article]]).

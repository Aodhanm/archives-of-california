# Hermenegildo Sal → Gov. — soldier Flores's illicit cattle-trade with the gentiles. — Soldier Bernardino [/Hermenegildo ⚠

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n257–n258** · Sav p. 258–259 · tomo p. — · 1800, Apr 4, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Hermenegildo Sal → Gov. — soldier Flores's illicit cattle-trade with the gentiles. — Soldier Bernardino [/Hermenegildo ⚠️] Flores spent '13, 20 días y hasta un mes' slaughtering cattle on the Monterey plain 'para comerciar ilícitamente con los Gentiles' — recommends his banishment to San Blas in irons; accomplice Rafael Gómez (less culpable, 'inducido de Flores') released with admonishment. → [[sal-papers-project]] (late Sal letter), [[otter-corruption-project]] (illicit frontier trade).

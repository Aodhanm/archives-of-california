**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n46–n47** · Sav p. 47–48 · tomo p. p.197–9 · 1791–92 · Savage abstract · 

tail of the construction-funds / *gratificaciones* order (continuation of the i. 44–45 cluster). — Junta Superior de Hacienda; Habil.do Gen.l to arrange $5,700 for the Santa Barbara presidio; Ramón de Goya $4,000; Gov. Fages ampliation 11 Dec '88; pay by *bando* in the presidios so 'legítimos interesados' appear. Closes the bonus-pay cluster.

# Gov. Fages — cattle management + Monterey construction. *(out-of-sequence 1788)* — No selling cattle on the hoof; slaugh

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n178–n179** · Sav p. 179–180 · 1788, Feb 25, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Gov. Fages — cattle management + Monterey construction. *(out-of-sequence 1788)* — No selling cattle on the hoof; slaughter only as needed (hides to Real Hacienda; troops eat meat 'á precio cómodo'). Repair of the block of houses beside the Monterey church using convicts (*presidarios*) + off-duty soldiers + a craftsman; soldiers from Sta Barbara & San Diego to cut timber for the works. → [[presidio-decay-project]] (convict/soldier construction labor).

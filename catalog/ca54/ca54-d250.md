# the Bouchard deserters' *causa* + a Russian brig. — ⭐⭐ Viceroy Conde del Venadito orders a *causa* formed against the 4 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n457–n460** · Sav p. 458–460 · 1820, Monterey / Sta Bárbara · Savage abstract · 

the Bouchard deserters' *causa* + a Russian brig. — ⭐⭐ Viceroy Conde del Venadito orders a *causa* formed against the 4 men 'que se pasaron… de los buques insurgentes de Buenos Aires' (Bouchard's ships) — named Nicolás Echeverría, Mateo José Pascual, Pedro Caldívar, Juan de la Rosa — Estudillo to prosecute (→ [[bouchard-raid-1818]]); ⭐ a 'Bergantín Ruso' set sail from the Sta Bárbara roadstead, 21 Sep 1820 (→ [[russian-california]]); a customs note.

# bundle of short items. — (Jan 12) sends 4 *cédulas de inválidos*; (Jul 13) 3-yr retirement *abono* via San Blas; ⭐ (Jun 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n63–n64** · Sav p. 64–65 · tomo p. p.291–306 · 1802, México · Savage abstract · 

bundle of short items. — (Jan 12) sends 4 *cédulas de inválidos*; (Jul 13) 3-yr retirement *abono* via San Blas; ⭐ (Jun 24) encloses royal dispatches naming Raymundo Antonio Carrillo, José de la Guerra y Noriega, and Francisco M.ª Ruiz as tenientes/alféreces of the Monterey & Sta Bárbara companies; (Jul 12) on muster extracts; (Feb 5) acknowledges the gov.'s delegation to Lt.-Col. Pedro Alberni of authority to grant missionaries retirement permits; (Jan 15) rules on artillery cpl. Francisco Aguilera at Punta de Guijarros [the San Diego harbor battery] filling a sergeant vacancy.

# Arrillaga → commandants of San Diego & San Francisco — bar foreign ships from the ports. — Transmits the Viceroy's order

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n189–n190** · Sav p. 190–191 · 1803, Jun 13, Loreto · Savage abstract · 

Arrillaga → commandants of San Diego & San Francisco — bar foreign ships from the ports. — Transmits the Viceroy's order (14 Apr) + the Royal Order of 10 Aug 1802: foreign vessels in the Mar del Sur, 'so color de la pesca de ballena' (under pretext of whaling), are touching at the California coast and smuggling — they are to be denied entry to the ports per the standing Reales Prevenciones. ⭐ Early-1800s foreign-whaler/contraband problem on the CA coast. → [[bay-area-borderlands project]], [[presidio-decay-project]].

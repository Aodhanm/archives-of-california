# Hermenegildo Sal → Cabo Luis Peralta — Instructions for the Mission Santa Cruz escort (25 articles). — A detailed *instr

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n273–n280** · Sav p. 274–281 · 1791, Sep 21, San Francisco · Savage abstract · 

Hermenegildo Sal → Cabo Luis Peralta — Instructions for the Mission Santa Cruz escort (25 articles). — A detailed *instrucción* for the escort commander: rations for soldiers & the 6 mission servants; build a communal *rancho*; tobacco/soap/chocolate allowances; daily Rosary; monthly courier to Santa Clara; weekly arms/munitions/clothing review. ⭐ A Neve-style conciliatory Indian-policy code — disarm Indians before they enter the mission; 'buen trato' with the gentiles; if forced to use arms, suspend hostilities the instant they make peace and conciliate 'con la mas posible suavidad'; troops not to enter rancherías or harm Indians; watch for pasture-burning; escort the padres; no gambling. ⭐ Parallels the Carrillo Santa Inés *instrucción* (C-A 11). → [[sal-papers-project]], indigenous relations.

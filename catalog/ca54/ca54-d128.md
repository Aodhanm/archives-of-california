# Gov. Fages — maritime + moral-regulation orders. — (1) Don't detain the San Blas ships & the Manila galleon (*nao de Fil

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n180–n181** · Sav p. 181–182 · 1785, Jul 18 / Aug 5, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Gov. Fages — maritime + moral-regulation orders. — (1) Don't detain the San Blas ships & the Manila galleon (*nao de Filipinas*) at Monterey longer than necessary. (2) Heads of families to 'scrupulously correct' their children's 'excesos y desórdenes,' held responsible for resulting disturbances.

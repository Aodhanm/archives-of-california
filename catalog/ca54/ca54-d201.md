# Fr. Lasuén → Gov. Borica — roster of all California missionaries (1797). — The complete 1797 list of friars serving the 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n338–n341** · Sav p. 339–342 · 1797, Oct 20 / Nov 9, San Diego · Savage abstract · 

Fr. Lasuén → Gov. Borica — roster of all California missionaries (1797). — The complete 1797 list of friars serving the 13 old missions (2 each), for the Colegio de San Fernando — e.g. San Carlos: Lasuén, Mariano Payeras, Francisco Pujol; San Gabriel: Cruzado, M. Sánchez; SJ Capistrano: Fuster, Santiago; San Diego: Mariner, Panella; with the names of friars retiring (Diego García, J. M.ª Fernández, etc.) + Borica's certification. → mission prosopography.

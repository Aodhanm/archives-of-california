# Goycoechea → Gov. — criminal sentence (incest/adultery). — Viceroy Azanza condemned the Sta Barbara soldier José Rosalin

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n122** · Sav p. 123 · 1799, Jan 22, Santa Barbara · Savage abstract · 

Goycoechea → Gov. — criminal sentence (incest/adultery). — Viceroy Azanza condemned the Sta Barbara soldier José Rosalino Fernández, convicted & confessed of an incestuous and adulterous crime, to hard labor on the *Bóvedas*; and his daughter & accomplice María Luciana to 10 years' *recogimiento*. ⭐ Criminal / social history.

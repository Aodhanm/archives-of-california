# Goycoechea → Gov. Borica — the presidio commandants' joint petition on inadequate pay (with Borica's endorsement). — The

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n123–n124** · Sav p. 124–125 · 1799, Dec 15, Santa Barbara · Savage abstract · 

Goycoechea → Gov. Borica — the presidio commandants' joint petition on inadequate pay (with Borica's endorsement). — The Alta CA presidio commandants jointly petition the viceroy over the penury of their limited salaries (memorial carried by Capt. Grajera). ⭐⭐ Gov. Borica's endorsement (29 Dec): it is all true — their short pay keeps them 'en las mayores estrecheces,' not enough 'para comer y vestir ni medianamente'; their children often go barefoot; their wives wash other people's laundry, sew, and bake bread to help support the family; same for the alféreces of the 5 presidios and the Loreto teniente; recommends +$150/year. Closes: *'Conozco las graves atenciones del Erario, pero me juzgaría culpable si no expresase ingenuamente mi sentir.'* ⭐ Vivid evidence of the material poverty of the Spanish officer corps in California. → [[presidio-decay-project]], [[sal-papers-project]], [[bay-area-borderlands project]].

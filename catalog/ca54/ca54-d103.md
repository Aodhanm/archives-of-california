# return of the unfit stonemason Manuel Dorotea. — (1793 Dec 30) Argüello returned the apprentice stonemason Manuel Dorote

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n141–n143** · Sav p. 142–143 · 1792–93, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

return of the unfit stonemason Manuel Dorotea. — (1793 Dec 30) Argüello returned the apprentice stonemason Manuel Dorotea to San Blas as incapable; (1792 Nov 18) surgeon Pablo Soler had certified the incapacity of 'Manuel Dorotea Ruiz' as *oficial cantero*.

# Manuel Arcala[?], Habilitado General — supply/*situado* *memorias* for the 4 presidios, the Catalan Volunteers, and the 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n165–n170** · Sav p. 166–171 · 1797–98, Jan 31, México · Savage abstract · 

Manuel Arcala[?], Habilitado General — supply/*situado* *memorias* for the 4 presidios, the Catalan Volunteers, and the artillery *piquete*. — ⭐ 1797 presidio fiscal balances: Monterey déficit $6,850; San Francisco déficit $1,431; Santa Barbara existencia $3,152; San Diego existencia $3,097. Compañía de Voluntarios de Cataluña *situado* $11,315 (split into *piquetes* at SF $5,941 / Monterey $1,057 / San Diego $4,314). Plus a separate artillery *piquete* (piquete de Artillería destacado en la Nueva California) account. → [[presidio-decay-project]] (Monterey & SF running annual deficits against the *situado*).

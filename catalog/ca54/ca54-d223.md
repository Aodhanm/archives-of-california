# Borica → Viceroy — Arrillaga's skirmish with the Colorado Indians. — Forwards Arrillaga's report of his Colorado-River r

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n389–n390** · Sav p. 390–391 · 1796, Nov 19, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Borica → Viceroy — Arrillaga's skirmish with the Colorado Indians. — Forwards Arrillaga's report of his Colorado-River reconnaissance & skirmish: a numerous *indiada* attacked his party; 'prudencia y humanidad… hasta el caso forzoso de repeler la fuerza con la fuerza' — soldiers' gear hit by arrows, 1 mule killed, 8 horses wounded, a musket & lance lost; 2 enemy dead on the road + 3 in the lagoon. On opening CA–Sonora communication, 'ninguno como Arrillaga puede consultar lo que convenga.' → [[indian-raiding-article]], borderlands.

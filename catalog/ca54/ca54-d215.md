# Engineer Alberto de Córdoba → Gov. Borica — the SF presidio site. — ⭐ Córdoba advises it is not advisable to move the SF

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n361** · Sav p. 362 · 1796, Jul 20, San Francisco · Savage abstract · 

Engineer Alberto de Córdoba → Gov. Borica — the SF presidio site. — ⭐ Córdoba advises it is not advisable to move the SF presidio to the loma de San Joaquín (the hill by the fort) 'por carecer de agua' — the presidio must be repaired where it stands (corroborates Lt.-Col. Alberni's 30 Jun opinion). → [[presidio-decay-project]] (the 1796 SF-presidio relocation debate).

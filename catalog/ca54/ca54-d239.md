# Monterey 1809 deployment table + musters. — ⭐ Estudillo's 'estado de fuerza efectiva,' Monterey, 1 Nov 1809 — shows the 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n423–n426** · Sav p. 424–426 · 1809–12 + 1784 · Savage abstract · 

Monterey 1809 deployment table + musters. — ⭐ Estudillo's 'estado de fuerza efectiva,' Monterey, 1 Nov 1809 — shows the garrison dispersed: only a few at the presidio, 4 on the battery (guardia de la batería), the rest scattered as mission escorts (San Carlos, San Antonio, Soledad, San Miguel, San Luis), the Rancho del Rey (7), the horse/cattle herds, SF, etc.; ~85 total incl. 5 artillerymen. → [[presidio-garrison-artillery-register]], [[presidio-decay-project]] (garrison dispersal). + cadet service records (Estrada, Santiago Argüello); SD 1784 muster (52).

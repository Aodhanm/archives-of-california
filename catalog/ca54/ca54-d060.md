# (Jul 12) Peace with England having been made [Treaty of Amiens], withdraw the Baja Cal. militia company unless the gov. 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n60** · Sav p. 61 · tomo p. p.281–3 · 1802, México · Savage abstract · 

(Jul 12) Peace with England having been made [Treaty of Amiens], withdraw the Baja Cal. militia company unless the gov. finds it convenient to retain it. (Jun 16) Approves $3,395 to build 2 schooners (*goletas*) for the Loreto presidio.

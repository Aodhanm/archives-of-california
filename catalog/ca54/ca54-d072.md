# '(3 doc.)' *(NEW, 2026-07-02 verify pass)* — (1) Grants Habilitado Zúñiga (San Diego) leave to come to México to settle 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n105** · Sav p. 106 · tomo p. p.472–77 · 1792-05-29, México · Savage abstract · 

'(3 doc.)' *(NEW, 2026-07-02 verify pass)* — (1) Grants Habilitado Zúñiga (San Diego) leave to come to México to settle 'sus bienes patrimoniales' once relieved — Zúñiga is promoted ('ascendido á Capᵃⁿ') and Nava will designate 'un oficial idóneo y honrado' to take the habilitación (of the company only, not the other Real-Hacienda branches); Zúñiga to stay till relieved, then may pass to México 'ántes de pasar á ocupar su puesto en Tucson (Sonora).' (2) Approves the gov. having sent to Loreto Tte. J. Fco. de Ortega to replace Alfz. Joseph Mª Estrada, habilitado of that presidio, who died; measures taken to fill Ortega's vacancy. (3) Orders that Tte. de dragones José Fernández Loredo be kept in mind for California vacancies.

# Arrillaga → Argüello — routine admin bundles. — Notable items amid filler: ⭐ Viceroy's order that the habilitado Cancela

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n152–n153** · Sav p. 153–154 · 1792–93, Loreto · Savage abstract · 

Arrillaga → Argüello — routine admin bundles. — Notable items amid filler: ⭐ Viceroy's order that the habilitado Cancela hand over sums in favor of Hermenegildo Sal (i. 80–84); ⭐ provisions for a *lotería* (lottery) at Monterey (i. 90–1); arrival of the schooners *Saturnina* & *Mexicana* (i. 92–3) and the brig *Activo* & schooner *Valdesina* (i. 94+) — note *Saturnina*/*Activa* are the vessels named in the Bodega boundary letter (i.119). → [[bodega-failure-1793]] (ship identification), [[sal-papers-project]].

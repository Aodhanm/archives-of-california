# José Argüello — the death of Lt. Hermenegildo Sal. — After Sal (Comandante & Habilitado of Monterey) died [Dec 1800], th

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n353** · Sav p. 354 · 1801, Mar 7, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

José Argüello — the death of Lt. Hermenegildo Sal. — After Sal (Comandante & Habilitado of Monterey) died [Dec 1800], the commissioners inventoried the mortuary house; under the surgeon Juan de Dios Morelos, most of Sal's clothing and all his personal clothing was burned (TB quarantine), keeping only the new/unworn, 'por quitar toda escrupulosidad.' → [[sal-papers-project]] (Sal's death — confirms the tuberculosis/quarantine).

# cattle-branding & shortage of bulls/labor. — Will do the *herrado* at the appointed time; couldn't sooner because the so

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n133** · Sav p. 134 · tomo p. — · 1793, Dec 20, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

cattle-branding & shortage of bulls/labor. — Will do the *herrado* at the appointed time; couldn't sooner because the soldiers were occupied relieving the ships; notes the loss of young calves trampled in the *rodeos*; will brand 'when the Santa Barbara people arrive.' Ranching/administrative.

# Fages / Soler → Sal — bundle. — Escort for the friars only when they come to say mass at the presidio or attend the sick

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n182–n184** · Sav p. 183–185 · 1785, Oct/Sep, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Fages / Soler → Sal — bundle. — Escort for the friars only when they come to say mass at the presidio or attend the sick/baptisms; ⭐ a Royal Order rewarding the troops of the Presidio de San Agustín de Tucson for a victory over the Apaches (promotions + compensation for horses/mules lost); the documents widows of officers must present for pensions.

# convicts to CA + Goycoechea on troop pay arrears. — Branciforte sends convicts (*presidiarios*) to the CA presidios for 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n434** · Sav p. 435 · 1796, México / Santa Barbara · Savage abstract · 

convicts to CA + Goycoechea on troop pay arrears. — Branciforte sends convicts (*presidiarios*) to the CA presidios for 9-yr terms; ⭐⭐ Goycoechea (Sta Bárbara): troops paid 1½+ years in arrears per the Reglamento → 'muchos soldados han muerto careciendo de sus alcances' (died without their back-pay) and others never enjoyed it; recommends the Real Hacienda advance half the *situado*. → [[presidio-decay-project]] (the pay/*situado* crisis).

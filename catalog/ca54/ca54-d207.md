# Caballero de Croix → Gov. Neve + Junta de Guerra — the Colorado campaign vs. the Yumas. — ⭐ The military response to the

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n345–n352** · Sav p. 346–353 · tomo p. — · 1781, Nov 15, Arispe · Savage abstract · 

Caballero de Croix → Gov. Neve + Junta de Guerra — the Colorado campaign vs. the Yumas. — ⭐ The military response to the 1781 Yuma Revolt: the war council (Cmdt.-Gen., Intendant Pedro Corbalán, auditor Pedro Galindo) reads Neve's & Fages's Colorado-River dispatches; Gov. Fages to take command of the punitive expedition, 'castigar y sujetar á los pérfidos y cobardes Yumas,' conduct campaign or 'paces honrosas,' treat the allied 'Naciones Amigas' well, letting them keep the booty (horses, mules, cattle), reconnoiter the Colorado for a new presidio + settlement. ⭐ The '3.ª Resolución' requires the death or surrender of the capitanejo Salvador Palma (the Quechan leader) + the interpreter Francisco Xavier before any peace. → [[indian-raiding-article]], military history, borderlands (Colorado frontier).

# Nicolás Soler & Gov. Pedro Fages → Diego González — military discipline. — (1) Every party leaving the presidio/port to 

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n174–n175** · Sav p. 175–176 · tomo p. — · 1785, Apr 15 / Jul 1, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Nicolás Soler & Gov. Pedro Fages → Diego González — military discipline. — (1) Every party leaving the presidio/port to march well-escorted. (2) ⭐⭐ Cmdt.-Gen.'s order (via Fages): the officers & troops behave with 'una libertad vecina muy perjudicial por los desórdenes escandalosos en que incurren con las indias cristianas y gentiles' — circulate a *repressive* order with grave penalties, 'nada disimule en este punto.' (3) The settlement-of-sailors policy from the Reglamento (Art. 2, tít. 3 — fill vacancies from ship crews; admit voluntary settlers). ⭐ Spanish command's own admission of soldiers' sexual abuse of Indian women — significant for indigenous/social history. → [[indian-raiding-article]], social history.

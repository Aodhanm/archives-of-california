# Hermenegildo Sal → Arrillaga — his retirement petition. — ⭐ Sal petitions the King (in quintuplicate) for retirement wit

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n356** · Sav p. 357 · 1800, Mar 28, Monterey · Savage abstract · 

Hermenegildo Sal → Arrillaga — his retirement petition. — ⭐ Sal petitions the King (in quintuplicate) for retirement with the rank of Captain, unable to continue 'á causa de mis gravísimas enfermedades, avanzada y cansada edad' [his tuberculosis]; begs the governor's favorable report, 'esperando ha de mirarme con la caridad á que mi infeliz estado me ha conducido.' He died 8 months later. → [[sal-papers-project]] (the retirement petition; cf. the bio's 'Mar 18 1800' — this copy dated Mar 28).

# CA–New Mexico frontier/exploration report (cont.). — On the feasibility of opening overland communication between Califo

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n398–n401** · Sav p. 399–402 · ~1796–99 · Savage abstract · 

CA–New Mexico frontier/exploration report (cont.). — On the feasibility of opening overland communication between California & New Mexico: trading bulls for *abalorios* (beads) with the Colorado Indians (8 bulls = 48 pesos); the 'tierra blanca' Indians who treated the deserters well; the war-skilled enemies in between. → [[bay-area-borderlands project]] (the CA–NM road).

# Real Orden on San Blas ship freight & commerce regulation. — On collecting freight (*fletes*) per the *arancel* for good

**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n204–n207** · Sav p. 205–208 · 1803, Jun 20, Loreto · Savage abstract · 

Real Orden on San Blas ship freight & commerce regulation. — On collecting freight (*fletes*) per the *arancel* for goods the commandants, chaplains, officers & missions imported via the brig *Activo* & frigate *Concepción* on the return from California; bars Marina employees/commandants from commerce; registers of cargo + passenger lists at each port. Refs José de Gálvez's 1769 visitation + the Reales Ordenanzas de la Armada. → [[otter-corruption-project]] (officers' private trade on supply ships).

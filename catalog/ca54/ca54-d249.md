**C-A 54 (State Papers, Sacramento), scan n453–n456** · Sav p. 454–457 · 1819, Monterey · Savage abstract ·

Argüello (Baja) sends an express having heard 'los insurgentes habían atacado á Monterey' (the Bouchard news reaching Baja); Solá presses for the hot-shot furnaces (*hornillos para bala roja*) + roofing finished before the rains (→ [[presidio-decay-project]]); fiscal cost-distribution; troops arriving at the Bahía de los Ángeles.

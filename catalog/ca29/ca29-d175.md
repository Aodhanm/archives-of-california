# Frigate *California* customs = $25,000: to date the sum from the *California*'s derechos not yet delivered, delayed by s

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n239–n240** · Sav p. 242–243 · tomo p. 570–572 · 1839-01-30 · verbatim transcription · 

Frigate *California* customs = $25,000: to date the sum from the *California*'s derechos not yet delivered, delayed by scrupulous *aforo*; 'tengo noticia pr. el Sr. Noriega que… asciende á la cantidad de 25,000 ps el total producto de los dros.'; sends provisions (frijol, manteca, garbanzo + a barril de salmon, part for 'tía Bernarda'); house-construction update (carpenter left on Sr. Wilson's ship; Osio; the *salinas*)

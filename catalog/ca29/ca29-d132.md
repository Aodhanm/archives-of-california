# (filed out of order) An unsigned San Rafael Juez de Paz offers his services to the rival southern governor Cárlos Carril

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n219** · Sav p. 222 · tomo p. 496–497 · 1838-04-06 · verbatim transcription · 

(filed out of order) An unsigned San Rafael Juez de Paz offers his services to the rival southern governor Cárlos Carrillo: recognizes him 'tanto como Gobr. puesto legalmente por el Supo. Gobo., tanto por que sea V. mi primo'; 'en cuanto al mando que tengo como Juez de Paz, son las armas y gente que hay en esta frontera de mi cargo' — a Carrillista partisan in the far north (San Rafael) pledging his frontier militia

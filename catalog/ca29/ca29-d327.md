# enclosed oficio transcribing & contesting Rojo's order to suspend the matanza and relocate it 'aproximándose pª. la part

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n258–n260** · Sav p. 262–265 · tomo p. p.651–652 · 1839-06 · verbatim transcription ·

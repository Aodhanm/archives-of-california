# (English-language letter) cover for the Spear/Hinckley suit: 'With this per schooner 'Fearnought' there is sent, through

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n235** · Sav p. 238 · tomo p. p.550 · 1838-10-09 · verbatim transcription · 

(English-language letter) cover for the Spear/Hinckley suit: 'With this per schooner 'Fearnought' there is sent, through the hands of the Alcalde, a complaint to you against Mr. Leese — Mr. Spear and myself have the prospect of losing much by him, and though he says his accts. are right, he still has much property, and 3 houses which in justice are not his, as they have been procured with our property — we have therefore been obliged to apply to justice.'

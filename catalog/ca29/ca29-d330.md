# interrogatorio deposition (answers to the Gov.'s 6-question *carta oficial* of the 22nd on de la Guerra's matanza): (1) 

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n263–n264** · Sav p. 268–269 · tomo p. p.655–657 · 1839-08-01 · verbatim transcription · 

interrogatorio deposition (answers to the Gov.'s 6-question *carta oficial* of the 22nd on de la Guerra's matanza): (1) true he has slaughtered there yearly for many years; (2) never heard the vecindario complain; (3) no notable public harm — the arroyo lacks running water at matanza time & settles by 8–9 a.m.; (4) police should have warned him in time; (5) notorious the corral/tasajera were ready & cattle driven in when stopped; (6) he was unjustly harmed, ~180 pesos

# LA merchant + secularization tail: asks liquidation of 183 ps owed for supplies furnished from his shop during his absen

**C-A 29 (Departmental State Papers), scan n241** · Sav p. 244 · tomo p. p.575–576 · 1839-01-27 · verbatim transcription · 

LA merchant + secularization tail: asks liquidation of 183 ps owed for supplies furnished from his shop during his absence (agent = Sr. Gallardo); had sought cattle from San Fernando, 'mas habiéndose publicado hoy, el bando que arregla el manejo de las Misiones, veo que pr. este lado se puede dificultar mi pretensión'; hearing 'ha llegado la fragata *California*,' begs to be paid the 183 ps in effects from that ship

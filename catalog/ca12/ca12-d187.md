# Origin of the Peacock arrests + ship movements: 4 crewmen of the brig Peacock (Capt. Kimball), who came ashore at San Ju

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n173–n175** · Sav p. 171–173 · tomo p. tomo 466–467, 474–475 · 1806-05-04 San Diego · Savage abstract · 

Origin of the Peacock arrests + ship movements: 4 crewmen of the brig Peacock (Capt. Kimball), who came ashore at San Juan Capistrano on pretext of provisions, arrested by Sgt. Pico; the brig hovered off the presidio (out of cannon range) then coasted south via Todos Santos & the Carmen anchorage to the island by Santo Tomás, vanishing south.

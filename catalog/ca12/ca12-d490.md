# VERBATIM HEADING: '1810. Dic. 17. S.n Diego. / F.co Mª Ruiz - á Arrillaga. Sobre embarque de cánamo.' GLOSS: Francisco M

**C-A 12 (Provincial State Papers), scan n288–n291** · Sav p. 285-286 / 286-287 · tomo p. pp. 774-5 · 1810-12-17 · Savage abstract · 

VERBATIM HEADING: '1810. Dic. 17. S.n Diego. / F.co Mª Ruiz - á Arrillaga. Sobre embarque de cánamo.' GLOSS: Francisco María Ruiz, comandante of San Diego, to Governor Arrillaga, on the shipment of hemp. SUBSTANCE: Savage abstract with figures. Reports that the frigate 'Princesa' received at the presidio 1053 arrobas 19 lb of hemp, with Savage carrying an interlined insertion 'cosechado en el presente año'; that for payment of the same he franked to the ships contador 4213 pesos 6 reales; and that there were also embarked on the same frigate 263 arrobas 19 lb of hemp, 'que junto con 473@ 18 lb. en 82 tercios que se embarcaron en el Bergn. Activo es el total de 737@ 12 lb. que quedaron en depósito en este Presidio el año anterior de 1809.' The heading and opening stand at the foot of n288; the entry completes at the head of n291 and closes with the cite pp 774 - 5. NOT IN THE EXPORT: no ca12 record carries orig pages 774-775 or the date 1810-12-17 from San Diego. Recovered while opening the n289-n290 gap, which is itself structural.

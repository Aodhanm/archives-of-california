# Prisoner-exchange resolution: O'Cain had proposed swapping cabo Osuna + 2 soldiers for the 4 detained Americans; now Osu

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n165–n166** · Sav p. 163–164 · tomo p. tomo 438–441 · 1806-07-25 San Diego · Savage abstract · 

Prisoner-exchange resolution: O'Cain had proposed swapping cabo Osuna + 2 soldiers for the 4 detained Americans; now Osuna is freed, no exchange needed. Osuna was imprudent (could have lost his whole party); more caution in future.

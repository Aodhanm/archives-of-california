# Moraga's detailed reconnaissance/defense assessment of the Russian presidio of Ross: difficult approach (artillery can't

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n371** · Sav p. 364, 367–368 · tomo p. tomo 1215–7 · 1814-07-30 San Francisco · Savage abstract · 

Moraga's detailed reconnaissance/defense assessment of the Russian presidio of Ross: difficult approach (artillery can't reach without a better road); ~40 personas de razón + many Kodiaks; firm wooden quadrangle, two corner torreones (4 cannon each, ~26 embrasures), other two corners empty; interior galpón = barracks/warehouse; vegetable gardens; bidarkas of sea-lion hide; the ship gone to Sitka with grain & furs; ~30 cattle + horses in a separate enclosure; night sentinel at Kuskov's house. (Record recovered 2026-07-05 audit; = Russian-highlights Doc 10.)

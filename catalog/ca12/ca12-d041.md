# Royal order to CLOSE ALL PORTS (only mail-boats may sail, without funds/goods), after a British squadron in peacetime at

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n45** · Sav p. 45 · tomo p. tomo 114–115 · 1805-01-12 México · Savage abstract · 

Royal order to CLOSE ALL PORTS (only mail-boats may sail, without funds/goods), after a British squadron in peacetime attacked & seized 4 Spanish frigates en route Montevideo→Cádiz [Cape Santa María, Oct 1804 → the war]; orders reprisals vs. British ships (embargo & impound).

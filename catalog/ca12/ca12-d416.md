# Mission relocation (post-quake): on Padre Mariano Payeras's request to move the mission [La Purísima] to the paraje de l

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n349** · Sav p. 342 · tomo p. tomo 1148–50 · 1813-04-01 Sta Bárbara · Savage abstract · 

Mission relocation (post-quake): on Padre Mariano Payeras's request to move the mission [La Purísima] to the paraje de los Berros — suitable (a more direct commercial/travel road, nearer San Luis) though water-poor; the Padre would keep the *ramblas* a league off; asks the Padre for laborers.

# Cabo Francisco Soto and two soldiers seized three Russian [Aleut] fishermen in the esteros of San José; Argüello has ord

**C-A 12 (Provincial State Papers), scan n278** · Sav p. 275 · tomo p. 739–740 · 1810-09-20 San Francisco · Savage abstract · 

Cabo Francisco Soto and two soldiers seized three Russian [Aleut] fishermen in the esteros of San José; Argüello has ordered them brought to the presidio. The arrest behind the Governor's 24 Sep ration order and ca17-d238 (1 Oct 1810, manutención de presos rusos), and the prisoners begged for in the letter-on-a-pole of ca12-d332c. Published: Mathes (1990) Doc 7.

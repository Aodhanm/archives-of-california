# Mixed: inventory of the late soldier Linares's estate; public punishment of a Christian Indian (of Sta Clara) for outrag

**C-A 12 (Provincial Records / Russian California), scan n214–n215** · Sav p. 212–213 · tomo p. tomo 588–590 · 1807-08-17 La Soledad · Savage abstract · 

Mixed: inventory of the late soldier Linares's estate; public punishment of a Christian Indian (of Sta Clara) for outrages against gentiles — shackled & put in the stocks for the gentiles to see, 3–4 blows; the inválido Julián Ríos's daughter brought to Monterey; an artillery militiaman imprisoned (Tte de Navío Tamariz to judge the Sáenz/La Torre matter).
